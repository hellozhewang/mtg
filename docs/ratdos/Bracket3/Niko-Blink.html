Shards becoming copies do not enter the battlefield again. Blink Niko to grow your supply, then copy creatures whose attack triggers, combat damage triggers, or static abilities turn that supply into a win. Protect the Shards you already have rather than spending every one on drawing cards.

Build the Shard engine
Niko, Light of Hope

each real entrance makes two Shards. The activation targets a nonlegendary creature you control and transforms only the Shards present when it resolves. New Shards created afterward remain ordinary Shards.

+
Ephemerate + Niko, Light of Hope

one mana produces a fresh entrance now and another through rebound next upkeep. Blinking Niko resets summoning sickness: the new Niko cannot immediately tap without help.

++
Cloudshift + Essence Flux + Momentary Blink

cheap ways to save a nontoken creature and repeat its entrance. Momentary Blink offers another use from the graveyard. Never blink a token expecting it to return.

++
Soulherder + Thassa, Deep-Dwelling + Teleportation Circle

repeat Niko or a removal creature every end step. These engines remain useful when commander tax becomes expensive; build around the best entrance for the actual board.

Displacer Kitten

your rocks, enchantments and instants become additional blinks. Its nonland target can also be an impending Overlord that is not yet a creature.

Flickering Hound

creature CASTS blink another creature already on the battlefield. Tokens entering do not trigger it. Deploying a creature can thus both increase your board and refresh Niko or a removal entrance.

Phelia, Exuberant Shepherd

an attack temporarily exiles another nonland permanent, then returns it at the end step. It can develop your engine or remove a blocker; avoid exposing this small attacker without a useful attack.

The Mind Stone

an efficient white rock first, with a six-mana harness investment available later for recurring end-step blinks. This is a different card from Mind Stone, and the list intentionally uses both.

+
Preston, the Vanisher + Niko, Light of Hope

a blinked Niko returns without being cast, creating a 0/1 legendary Niko copy. Keep the actual commander under the legend rule; the token's entrance still makes two Shards. The token is not itself your commander.

Multiply resources without losing the rules
+
Anointed Procession + Exalted Sunborn

each doubles token creation; together they multiply it by four. Sunborn is also a legal Niko template with flying and lifelink. These replacements do not create an infinite loop on their own.

+
Exalted Sunborn + Niko, Light of Hope

turn four existing Shards into four Sunborns, then blink Niko while those copies exist. With no other modifiers, the two new Shards become 32 through four doublers. Those 32 are not part of the already-resolved copy effect and cannot attack immediately.

+
Starfield Vocalist + Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines

each adds another occurrence of your entrance triggers. Together a normal Niko entrance triggers three times, not four. Elesh Norn also suppresses opposing entrance triggers, attracting removal.

+
Starfield Vocalist + Ephemerate

warp the Vocalist for two mana, then blink it before the warp exile resolves to keep the new object permanently. The same maneuver works with Exalted Sunborn.

Thousand-Year Elixir

lets a newly cast or blinked Niko use the tap ability immediately and can untap Niko for another activation. It does not give haste for attacking. After one activation, transformed non-Shard copies will not participate in a second activation that turn.

Swiftfoot Boots

haste enables a fresh Niko activation while hexproof shields it from opponents. Blink detaches the Boots, so equip again during your main phase; this is less flexible than the Elixir after an instant-speed blink.

+
Drumbellower + Niko, Light of Hope

untaps Niko on each opponent's turn for more paid activations, such as copying defensive creatures. You still need two mana each time and a Niko allowed to tap; untapping does not fix summoning sickness.

Serra's Sanctum

every ordinary Shard is an enchantment, making this the most explosive mana source here. Tap it before converting Shards into nonenchantment creatures. Shards copied as enchantment creatures still count.

Draw without consuming the whole army
+
Entity Tracker + Niko, Light of Hope

two Shards entering mean two draw triggers before any multipliers. Merely turning existing Shards into copies causes no enchantment entrance and draws nothing.

+
Candlekeep Sage + Niko, Light of Hope

blinking your actual commander draws once for leaving and once for entering. A token copy of Niko does not get the commander's identity or this benefit.

Dour Port-Mage

draws from Niko's exile activation and from blinks. It triggers once when one or more OTHER creatures leave together without dying, not once per creature in a mass blink.

++
Rhystic Study + Mystic Remora + Esper Sentinel

early draw sources let you hold interaction instead of immediately spending Shards. Remora's upkeep cost escalates; let it go when maintaining it would prevent a meaningful turn.

+
Enduring Curiosity + Niko, Light of Hope

each Curiosity copy sees every creature you control that deals combat damage to a player. Four copies connecting together generate sixteen draw triggers. Count your library before making a huge forced draw wave; token copies cannot return after dying.

Gleaming Splendor

turns opponents' second draws each turn into Treasure without occupying a Game Changer slot. Its activated ability can give you and an opponent a card, but feeds that opponent resources; use it when the draw and potential Treasure are worth the exchange.

Mind Stone

ramp early, then cash it in when lands and Sanctum provide enough mana. Sevinne's Reclamation or Sun Titan can recover it, giving this humble rock a useful late game.

Turn Shards into lethal damage
+
Skyhunter Strike Force + Niko, Light of Hope

with Niko still under your control, every Strike Force copy grants melee to the other copies. Six attack-ready Shards become six flyers with six melee abilities each. Attack all three opponents, two copies apiece: each becomes 20/20, totaling 40 damage per opponent if unblocked and uninterrupted.

+
Hero of Bladehold + Niko, Light of Hope

multiple Heroes create Soldiers and stack battle cry across the attack. Put the Soldier-making triggers above the battle cry triggers so the new Soldiers receive those bonuses. Soldiers enter attacking but do not themselves trigger attack abilities.

+
Battle Angels of Tyr + Niko, Light of Hope

each attacking Angel copy has myriad, spreading 4/4 flyers to the other opponents. Myriad tokens do enter, but do not recursively trigger myriad because they were not declared as attackers. Its damage rewards each require the damaged player to be strictly ahead of every other player in the relevant resource.

+
Nesting Dovehawk + Niko, Light of Hope

activate Niko in your precombat MAIN PHASE so your Shards already have the Dovehawk's beginning-of-combat trigger. Populate a Dovehawk copy to make a permanent Dovehawk token; the new token copies its current copiable characteristics, not Niko's expiration time.

+
Archon of Sun's Grace + Niko, Light of Hope

normal Shards entering make flying Pegasi. Alternatively, copy the Archon first and then blink Niko so every Archon copy sees the new Shards. The Pegasi remain after the temporary Archon copies revert.

+
Overlord of the Mistmoors + Displacer Kitten

cast the Overlord for its impending cost, then blink the noncreature enchantment with Kitten to return it without time counters. Once it is a creature, Niko can copy it into 6/6 attackers that each create two flying Insects on attack.

Akroma's Will

the decisive combat spell: with your commander present, both modes provide evasion, double strike, lifelink and substantial protection. Resolve the Shard transformation first so those creatures actually receive the spell's bonuses. Colorless blockers and sacrifice effects can still matter.

The copy effect ends as the next end step begins; it does not last until cleanup. The original template returns through the delayed trigger. Do not assume copied end-step abilities will trigger, and do not assume counters on the original become counters on your Shards.

A good developed draw can threaten a turn-six-to-eight combat finish, but this is a board-building deck, not a guaranteed turn-count combo. Without Niko, the blink engines, permanent creature tokens and Akroma's Will still support a real combat plan.

Spend interaction to preserve the engine
+
Swords to Plowshares + Path to Exile

cheap exile protects your development against creature engines or removes the blocker that matters. The life or land you give away is usually cheaper than losing an entire attack.

+
Generous Gift + Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

flexible answers for permanents the creature-removal suite misses. Sink can also bounce a spell on the stack, but it does not permanently counter it; the opponent can usually cast it again.

++
Dovin's Veto + Swan Song + Mana Drain

preserve these for sweepers, opposing wins and answers to your lethal activation. Drain also pays for a larger follow-up turn, although its colorless mana does not solve double-white or double-blue costs.

+
Glen Elendra Archmage + Niko, Light of Hope

copying the Archmage gives you several potential noncreature counters at one blue mana and one sacrificed Shard apiece. Token copies do not come back through persist. Blink the original after it persists to remove its -1/-1 counter.

+
Loran of the Third Path + Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow

repeatable artifact and enchantment answers that improve every blink engine. Loran is legendary and cannot be Niko's template; Witch Enchanter is a land when you need to develop mana instead.

Skyclave Apparition

blink it to exile additional cheap nonland, nontoken permanents. Its leaving trigger makes an Illusion for the exiled card's owner rather than returning the original permanent.

Riptide Gearhulk

one entrance can tuck a nonland permanent from each opponent, providing a substantial recovery swing. Repeated blinks answer a broad range of boards; the threats are delayed, not permanently removed from the game.

+
Solitude + Ephemerate

evoke Solitude, then blink it with its sacrifice trigger still on the stack. The returned Solitude is a new object, so it stays and supplies another removal entrance. You still paid the evoke cost by exiling another white card.

Aether Channeler

repeatable choice of a card, a flying token or bouncing another nonland permanent. Use the bounce mode to clear a blocker or recover your own permanent; it is temporary interaction, not removal.

Cyclonic Rift

the third Game Changer alongside Rhystic Study and Serra's Sanctum. Overload it to clear blockers while keeping your own engine, ideally immediately before the finishing turn.

Supreme Verdict

unanimated Shards are enchantments and survive this creature sweeper. Copied creature Shards do not. Niko dies too, so leave a recast plan or protect the commander before committing.

Clever Concealment

phases out the Shards, creatures and engines you choose through most sweepers. Creature tokens can help convoke it, including newly created creatures; ordinary noncreature Shards cannot. Phased-out tokens survive, unlike exiled tokens.

+
Eerie Interlude + Parting Gust

Interlude protects selected nontoken creatures and reloads their entrances; never include creature tokens you want back. Gust is delayed blink without the gift, or permanent exile of an opposing nontoken creature when you promise the Fish.

Graveyard-exile and enchantment-exile sweepers can undo more work than creature wraths. Keep a counter or Concealment available when possible. Effects suppressing entrance triggers or creature activations attack the core engine, making the flexible removal especially important.

Find and rebuild the right piece
→
Recruiter of the Guard → Skyhunter Strike Force

get the most explosive combat template when you already have several mature Shards and a safe activation.

→
Recruiter of the Guard → Nesting Dovehawk

choose this when you need a growing permanent creature board rather than one temporary attack.

→
Recruiter of the Guard → Displacer Kitten

choose Kitten when your hand contains cheap noncreature spells and your engine needs more entrances.

→
Recruiter of the Guard → Glen Elendra Archmage

choose protection against a threatened noncreature spell. Have blue mana available; finding the Archmage does not itself stop anything.

→
Recruiter of the Guard → Loran of the Third Path

find an answer to a troublesome artifact or enchantment. Blink Recruiter later to pivot to a combat template once the obstruction is gone.

+
Sun Titan + Sevinne's Reclamation

rebuild cheap rocks, draw engines and utility permanents after removal. Titan attacks also recur permanents, making it a useful Niko template when you need recovery rather than maximum damage.

Hall of Heliod's Generosity

recovers a lost enchantment engine to the top of your library; a Shard draw can put it into your hand immediately. This spends mana and your next draw, so use it for an engine worth the tempo.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep two or three usable mana sources, an early accelerator or draw engine, and a meaningful blink or interaction spell. Prefer hands with both colors; Serra's Sanctum is not a dependable opening mana source without enchantments. The list has 35 lands plus two spell/land MDFCs.

++
Arcane Signet + Talisman of Progress + Fellwar Stone

early rocks support a turn-three Niko and leave later mana for activation plus protection. Check Fellwar Stone's actual colors; it does not promise both colors in every pod.

Knight of the White Orchid

when an opponent controls more lands, cast this before your land drop to catch up. It searches for a Plains card, including appropriate duals, not just a basic; a fetched land's own enters-tapped condition still applies.

++
Flooded Strand + Hallowed Fountain + Tundra

prioritize the colors needed for your next two turns rather than fetching automatically. Keep an untapped blue source for interaction when possible. Six basics provide straightforward fetch targets and insurance against opposing basic-land search effects.

Start with mana and draw, deploy Niko, then use an end-step blink to bank Shards for the following turn. Shards controlled continuously since your turn began can attack after becoming creatures; new Shards cannot attack without haste. An old Shard that changes card types is still the same permanent.

Before committing, count mana for the template, Niko's activation and protection. If the template is removed in response, Niko's only target is illegal and the ability does not transform your Shards. Save a counter when losing that activation would lose the game.

+
Minamo, School at Water's Edge + Thousand-Year Elixir

additional untaps are useful after rebuilding new Shards or on later turns. They do not cause already transformed non-Shard copies to be copied again. Keep the normal two-mana activation cost in your combat math.
