Godo tutors an Equipment onto the battlefield when he enters, and gives you an extra combat the first time he attacks each turn. The Equipment he fetches is Helm of the Host. Helm makes a hasty token copy of Godo at the start of every combat; that copy fetches another Equipment and grants yet another combat, so the combats never end. Two cards, and the commander finds the second one himself. A Dualcaster Mage package is the backup kill.

The commander
Godo, Bandit Warlord

six mana. When he enters, search your library for an Equipment and put it onto the battlefield. Whenever he attacks for the first time each turn, untap him and all your Samurai, and after this combat there is an additional combat phase.

The fetched Equipment arrives UNATTACHED. Helm of the Host's equip costs {5}, so the deck runs ways to attach it for free: Hammer of Nazahn, Brass Squire and Magnetic Theft.

Godo costs six. Fast mana and rituals cast him on turn three or four.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
+
Godo, Bandit Warlord + Helm of the Host

with Helm on Godo, at the beginning of combat you get a non-legendary token copy of Godo with haste. Its enters trigger fetches another Equipment; when it attacks, it grants another combat. Every new combat starts with Helm making another Godo. Unlimited combats and an army of hasty Godos.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

cast Twinflame on a creature, then flash in Dualcaster Mage and copy Twinflame targeting Dualcaster. The token copy of Dualcaster copies Twinflame again, targeting itself. Unlimited hasty Dualcasters: attack for the win.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Heat Shimmer

the same.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication

the same.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Electroduplicate

the same.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Devastating Onslaught

the same, with X = 1.

+
Hammer of Nazahn + Helm of the Host

Hammer attaches every Equipment that enters for free, so Godo fetching Helm equips it on the spot. With Hammer out first, Godo's own trigger completes the combo.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when
→
Godo, Bandit Warlord → Helm of the Host

his enters trigger. If Hammer of Nazahn is already out, Helm attaches for free. Otherwise attach it with Brass Squire or Magnetic Theft, or fetch Hammer first and Helm the next time he enters.

→
Gamble → Helm of the Host

any card, then a random discard. Cast it with few cards in hand so the discard can't hit what you fetched. If Helm gets discarded, Goblin Welder or Abstergo Entertainment gets it back.

→

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep fast mana or rituals that cast Godo by turn four, plus one way to attach Helm (Hammer of Nazahn, Brass Squire, Magnetic Theft). Godo finds Helm himself.

Combo turn: cast Godo with Hammer of Nazahn out, fetch Helm, and it attaches. Give him haste (Lightning Greaves before the Helm, or Sting) and go to combat.

If Godo is answered, the Dualcaster package is a second win: Imperial Recruiter finds Dualcaster Mage, and Gamble finds a copy spell.
