The Wise Mothman spreads rad counters across every player at the table, turning each precombat main phase into a flurry of mill and life loss. The most common mistake is forgetting that Mothman's counter trigger fires whenever nonland cards are milled from ANY library, meaning you get triggers on opponents' upkeeps, combat steps, and your own turns alike. Distribute those counters to make Mothman an evasive lethal commander, go wide with mutant tokens, or assemble clean mill combos from turn 6 onward.

Bracket 3: Game Changers are capped at three (Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor, Rhystic Study). No mass land denial. Two-card combos are included as decisive finishes but must not be fired before turn 6 under table expectations.

The radiation and mill engine

The engine runs on giving the entire table rad counters, causing each player to mill at their precombat main phase and lose life for nonland cards while feeding your counter triggers.

The Wise Mothman

enters and attacks giving each player a rad counter; distributes +1/+1 counters to up to X targets whenever X nonland cards are milled

Mesmeric Orb

forces every player to mill a card whenever their permanents untap; generates a constant stream of individual mill triggers across all turn cycles

Ruin Crab

mills each opponent for three whenever a land enters your battlefield; cracking a fetchland mills six cards per opponent

Altar of the Brood

triggers on every permanent entry including land drops and creature tokens, repeatedly milling each opponent

Psychic Corrosion

turns all of your card draw into repeatable milling across all three opponents

Screeching Scorchbeast

attacks to radiate the table for two counters each; creates 2/2 Zombie Mutant tokens whenever nonland cards are milled

Mirelurk Queen

gives target player two rad counters on entry; spawns 2/2 Mutant tokens whenever nonland cards are milled

Tato Farmer

gains you two rad counters on landfall and taps to ramp any land from any graveyard to the battlefield

Glowing One

attacks to grant four rad counters with deathtouch; gains you life equal to nonland cards milled anywhere

Infesting Radroach

an evasive flyer that adds rad counters on combat hit and recurs itself from your graveyard whenever nonland cards are milled

Vexing Radgull

Feral Ghoul

Bloatfly Swarm

Mariposa Military Base

Scaling counters and proliferate

Mothman's triggers spread counters broadly. These amplifiers multiply those counters into massive beatdown threats and card advantage.

Branching Evolution

doubles the number of +1/+1 counters placed on your creatures, accelerating Mothman into one-shot commander damage range

Corpsejack Menace

redundant counter doubling on a solid 4/4 body to rapidly overwhelm the table

Hardened Scales

adds an extra +1/+1 counter to each creature receiving counters, providing huge value for just one green mana

Winding Constrictor

adds an additional counter to permanents and players, accelerating rad counter stacks as well as creature power

Kami of Whispered Hopes

adds an additional counter whenever counters are placed and taps for mana equal to its power for massive explosive turns

Evolution Sage

Evolution Witness

turns your distributed +1/+1 counters into card draw up to once per creature each turn

Radstorm

Inexorable Tide

Game-ending combos

Under Bracket 3 table expectations, do not fire these combos before turn 6. Use them to cleanly finish long games once the table has developed.

+
Duskmantle Guildmage + Mindcrank

activate Guildmage's first ability for {1}{U}{B}; any card entering an opponent's graveyard deals 1 damage, Mindcrank mills a card, looping infinitely until life totals hit zero

+
Bloodchief Ascension + Mindcrank

with three quest counters on Ascension, any card entering an opponent's graveyard drains 2 life, Mindcrank mills two cards, creating an infinite lethal drain loop

+
Bruvac the Grandiloquent + Maddening Cacophony

kicking Cacophony for {4}{U}{U} forces opponents to mill half their libraries; Bruvac doubles the mill to their entire library, decking all opponents simultaneously

+
Syr Konrad, the Grim + Mindcrank

creature cards milled or dying deal 1 damage to each opponent, which triggers Mindcrank to mill more cards, generating a self-perpetuating mill chain

→
Demonic Tutor → Mindcrank

fetches the missing artifact piece for either Duskmantle Guildmage or Bloodchief Ascension to close the game

→
Demonic Tutor → Maddening Cacophony

finds the sorcery half of the Bruvac library wipe once six mana is available

→
Demonic Tutor → Cyclonic Rift

tutors your one-sided board wipe to clear opposing blockers and swing for lethal commander damage

Opening hands and sequencing

Prioritize hands with two to three lands, early ramp (Sol Ring, a signet, Birds of Paradise, or Delighted Halfling), and an early mill or counter engine like Ruin Crab, Hardened Scales, or Mesmeric Orb.

Cast The Wise Mothman on turn 3 or 4 to begin radiating the table. Put the first few +1/+1 counters onto Mothman to quickly reach lethal commander damage while protecting him with Stubborn Denial, Ripples of Potential, or Mutational Advantage.

If opponents deploy heavy creature boards, rely on Glowing One and Nuclear Fallout to stabilize while ticking up rad counters.

Once turn 6 arrives, tutor for or deploy Mindcrank alongside Duskmantle Guildmage or Bloodchief Ascension, or kick Maddening Cacophony with Bruvac, to close out the game cleanly.
