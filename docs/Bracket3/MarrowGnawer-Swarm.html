Count your Rats after paying Marrow-Gnawer's sacrifice cost, not before. The ability counts Rats when it resolves and includes Marrow-Gnawer if it is still a Rat on the battlefield. With five Rats including the commander, sacrifice one, then create four: you finish with eight Rats, assuming nothing else changes the board.

This is the singleton Rat-swarm version, separate from the collection's other Marrow-Gnawer deck. Every nonbasic card appears once. The deck respects Bracket 3, with Bolas's Citadel, Demonic Tutor, and The One Ring as its three Game Changers. It has no planned infinite loop, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain. It uses individual Rats, token production, finite untaps, combat boosts, and life-loss payoffs.

Establish Rats before activating the commander

Marrow-Gnawer

Turns an existing Rat population into a larger one and gives all Rats fear. Fear allows blocking by black creatures or artifact creatures, so it is not universal unblockability. The static ability also affects opposing Rats. Build at least a few bodies before investing in repeated activations, and normally sacrifice an expendable Rat rather than the commander.

Engine Rat

A cheap Rat that starts the population and discourages attackers with deathtouch. Its six-mana drain is a late mana sink, not your normal development plan. It is a useful Rat to deploy before the commander or convert into cards with Skullclamp.

Gnawing Vermin

Mills two on entry, usually targeting yourself to stock recovery, and can weaken an opposing creature when it dies. Sacrificing it to Marrow-Gnawer gives you that death trigger without spending another removal card.

Burglar Rat

Adds a Rat while each opponent discards a card. Nezumi Informant provides the same role. They are good early bodies and expendable later; do not confuse a small amount of discard with preventing opponents from drawing new answers.

Refurbished Familiar

Often becomes cheaper through mana rocks, Equipment, and Treasures. Its entry trigger makes opponents discard or gives you cards when they cannot. Flying and fear create useful opportunities to connect or exchange it through ninjutsu for another Rat.

Pack Rat

Provides an independent way to build Rats by discarding spare lands or cards you can recover. The copies retain the ability and count all your Rats for their power and toughness. Making a copy costs three mana and a card, so it supplements Marrow-Gnawer rather than replacing its efficiency.

Lord Skitter, Sewer King

Supplies a Rat at the beginning of your combat and turns each other Rat entering into optional opposing graveyard exile. Marrow-Gnawer can therefore clear several graveyard cards while building your board. Skitter's tokens cannot block; the commander's ordinary Rat tokens can.

Lord Skitter's Butcher

Usually creates a second Rat, but can sacrifice another creature for selection and a card, or give your creatures menace for a finishing attack. With Marrow-Gnawer's fear, menace requires an opponent to find two legal black or artifact blockers per attacker.

Voracious Vermin

Brings another Rat and grows when your other creatures die. It preserves some pressure after you trade or sacrifice the small bodies around it. The extra token cannot block, and the Vermin's counters do not increase the number of tokens Marrow-Gnawer creates.

Rat King, Verminister

Creates a Rat at your end step when one of your permanents left the battlefield that turn. Sacrificing a token, using a Treasure, or bouncing a creature through ninjutsu can enable it. Its other ability trades three Rats and a tap to return a creature card tapped; in this singleton list, recovering additional cards of that name is normally irrelevant.

Rat King, Pale Piper

Replaces your nontoken creatures with Rats when they leave the battlefield, including through death, exile, or ninjutsu. Sacrificing an ordinary Rat token does not make a replacement. Its two-mana token-sacrifice ability draws cards and can use noncreature tokens too.

Ogre Slumlord

Makes Rats when other nontoken creatures die, including opponents' creatures, and gives your Rats deathtouch. Sacrificing a Rat token does not trigger the creation ability. Its deathtouch improves defense while you wait to turn the swarm into a winning attack.

Piper of the Swarm

Creates Rats when the commander is unavailable, adds menace, and can sacrifice three Rats to steal a creature permanently. Its token-making and theft abilities both require tapping, so choose the one the board needs. It is naturally a Human, not a Rat.

Get additional activations without an infinite loop

Sting, the Glinting Dagger

Gives the equipped commander haste and untaps it at the beginning of every combat, including opponents' combats. Activate before a combat begins, let Sting untap Marrow-Gnawer, then activate again if sacrificing another Rat is worthwhile. Tokens made during the end of an opponent's turn are ready to attack on your next turn. Sting gives haste only to its equipped creature, not the whole swarm.

Thousand-Year Elixir

Lets Marrow-Gnawer use its tap ability immediately and supplies one additional untap for a mana and its tap. It does not grant haste for attacking. It also works with Piper of the Swarm, Ayara, and Rat King, Verminister when another activation matters more than more Rats.

Patriar's Seal

Either ramps mana or spends one mana and its tap to untap a legendary creature. That makes it useful both before and after Marrow-Gnawer arrives, but it cannot produce mana and provide the untap in the same activation.

Lightning Greaves

Gives haste and shroud for zero equip cost, allowing the commander to activate immediately. Shroud stops your own targeted untaps from Elixir or Seal and your other equip abilities too. Move Greaves at sorcery speed when planning those lines; it cannot be moved in response to removal.

Swiftfoot Boots

Gives haste and hexproof while preserving your ability to target Marrow-Gnawer with untaps and Equipment. Its equip cost is worth planning for when casting the commander, particularly if the first activation must happen immediately.

Make the Rat population lethal

Ashcoat of the Shadow Swarm

Attacking or blocking boosts your other Rats by the number of Rats you control when the trigger resolves. If Marrow-Gnawer is untapped, activating it in response can increase that count; the newly created tokens are not already attacking. Ashcoat also mills and returns up to two Rat creature cards at your end step, providing recovery even when you cannot attack safely.

Karumonix, the Rat King

Finds Rat cards in your top five and gives your other Rats toxic 1. Ten successful Rat hits can eliminate an opponent through poison, but increasing their power does not increase the poison from toxic 1. Toxic is added to ordinary combat damage rather than replacing it. Its card-finding gets better with Maskwood Nexus, which makes your other creature cards Rats too.

Coat of Arms

Turns a wide Rat board into enormous creatures. It affects every creature on the battlefield, including opposing armies that share types, so cast it when you can exploit it immediately. It counts each other qualifying creature once, not once per shared type. It also raises token toughness, preventing Skullclamp from killing many tokens merely by equipping them.

Eldrazi Monument

Adds flying, indestructible, and a power-and-toughness boost, solving many boards where fear is insufficient. Its upkeep sacrifice remains mandatory even when creatures are indestructible. Keep a continuing source of creatures available; indestructible does not protect from exile, sacrifice, or lethal toughness reduction.

Ayara, First of Locthwain

Each black Rat entering drains every opponent for one and gains you one life. A single large Marrow-Gnawer activation can therefore finish without combat. Her draw ability sacrifices another black creature. Colorless changelings from Black Market Connections and blue changelings from Maskwood Nexus are Rats but do not trigger Ayara merely by entering.

Valley Rotcaller

On attack, drains each opponent for the number of other qualifying creatures you control, including all your Rats. It gains you that number of life once, not the total lost by all opponents. The attack trigger does not require combat damage, so a blocked Rotcaller can still finish the table. It is a Squirrel naturally, not a Rat.

Mirkwood Bats

Drains each opponent whenever you create or sacrifice a token, including Rats, Treasures, and Food if any are created during play. Creating several Rats triggers it separately for each token. It also rewards sacrificing the token that pays Marrow-Gnawer's cost, giving the engine a second noncombat finish.

Maskwood Nexus

Makes all your creatures and creature cards Rats, increasing Marrow-Gnawer's count and allowing support creatures to benefit from Ashcoat and tribal recovery. It changes types, not colors. Its own token ability supplies a Rat even without the commander. With Coat of Arms, your creatures can share types with many opposing creatures, so calculate both sides of the board carefully.

Example: With ten Rats and Ashcoat attacking, the other Rats get +10/+10 when the attack trigger resolves if the count is unchanged. That is a real combat finish, but a board wipe or removing enough Rats before resolution reduces it. Against crowded black or artifact boards, prefer flying from Monument or a drain payoff rather than assuming fear gets through.

Use individual Rats for interaction and reusable value

Persistent Marshstalker

Grows in power with the swarm and can return from the graveyard tapped and attacking once threshold is satisfied and you pay. Its return triggers from declaring an attack with Rats; it does not let you return it at any arbitrary point in combat.

Nashi, Moon Sage's Scion

Fear helps an attacker go unblocked so you can use ninjutsu. Connecting reveals options from all four libraries, but you may play only one of the exiled cards from each trigger. Paying life for a spell still requires normal timing, and playing an exiled land still uses a land play.

Throat Slitter

Converts an unblocked Rat into removal for a nonblack creature controlled by the damaged player. It cannot remove a creature belonging to a different opponent through that hit. Return a nontoken entry-effect Rat through ninjutsu when replaying it will be useful.

Nezumi Prowler

Its entry trigger can give an existing large Rat lifelink and deathtouch, often recovering considerable life after an Ashcoat boost. It can target itself if that is the best attacker. The life recovery helps offset Citadel, Black Market Connections, and your lands.

Skullsnatcher

Offers cheap ninjutsu and targeted graveyard cleanup when it connects. It can exile cards only from the damaged player's graveyard. Lord Skitter and Soul-Guide Lantern provide other graveyard interaction when you cannot safely connect.

Crypt Rats

A scalable damage-based reset or final life-total push, but it damages every player and every creature, including your own Rats. Only black mana can pay X. An indestructible Crypt Rats survives its own damage, yet you must still survive the damage to yourself; do not confuse indestructibility with player protection.

Convert the swarm into cards and mana

Skullclamp

Draws two from a small Rat that dies after equipping, or from an equipped creature sacrificed to another effect. Anthem effects can stop a 1/1 from dying to the equipment immediately, so keep an outlet available. It is often the best early card to find when you already have Rat production.

Species Specialist

Choose Rat to draw for Rat deaths, including tokens and opposing Rats. You may decline draws if your library is getting low. It replaces the resources spent activating Marrow-Gnawer and makes a creature wipe much less devastating when the triggers are allowed to resolve.

Grave Venerations

Introduces the monarch for end-step draw, recovers a creature to hand at your end step while you remain monarch, and drains opponents whenever your creatures die. Its drain still works when someone steals the monarchy. Keep enough legal blockers to defend it rather than assuming every Rat token can block.

Black Market Connections

Produces cards, Treasures, and a changeling body that counts as a Rat. Taking every mode costs six life, so choose deliberately. The body is colorless, making it valid for Marrow-Gnawer but not Ayara's black-creature sacrifice cost unless another effect changes its color.

The One Ring

Independent draw when the Rat engine is disrupted, with a protective turn when cast. Its upkeep life loss is not prevented by that protection. You can sacrifice it to Deadly Dispute or Bolas's Citadel when burdens become dangerous; indestructible does not prevent sacrifice.

Bolas's Citadel

Uses life to cast spells from the top while keeping mana available for untaps, protection, and Rat abilities. Ayara, Rotcaller, and Grave Venerations replenish life. Its sacrifice-ten-nonland-permanents ability turns excess tokens into ten life loss for every opponent, with additional death or token-sacrifice triggers when the relevant payoffs are present.

Ashnod's Altar

Converts excess creatures into mana for rebuilding, artifacts, or expensive recovery. Its mana is colorless, so it cannot pay Crypt Rats' activation and does not fix black-heavy spells. Every Rat sacrificed reduces the next Marrow-Gnawer count unless another effect replaces it.

Crypt Ghast

Adds one black mana when you tap a Swamp for mana and offers extort for life recovery. Urborg makes your utility lands Swamps, but a Coffers activation still receives just one extra black from Ghast, not a second full Coffers output.

The Soul Stone

An indestructible two-mana rock that can become an upkeep reanimation engine after a substantial investment. Harnessing exiles a creature rather than sacrificing it, so death triggers do not fire from that cost. Its harnessed symbol is not an infinite-mana ability.

Three Tree City

Choose Rat and convert your population into black mana. Count the two-mana activation cost before treating it as better than a Swamp. It is especially effective after an off-turn Marrow-Gnawer activation when you untap with the enlarged army.

Cabal Coffers

Scales with your Swamps, while Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth makes nonbasics contribute. Early Coffers without enough Swamps is not a functional substitute for an ordinary black source. Nykthos offers another large-mana route, but ordinary Rat tokens have no mana cost and add no devotion.

Protect and rebuild the important pieces

Mithril Coat

Flash it in to give Marrow-Gnawer or another legendary creature indestructible. The entry attachment must target a legendary creature, and shroud from Lightning Greaves can prevent it. It does not save a creature from exile, sacrifice, or zero toughness.

Swarmyard

Establishes a regeneration shield for a Rat before it would be destroyed. Regeneration taps the creature and removes it from combat when used, and does not stop sacrifice or toughness reduction. With Maskwood Nexus, it can protect your other creatures too because they are Rats.

Imp's Mischief

Redirects a spell with a single target when a different legal target exists, protecting against some removal and counterspells. It does not answer activated abilities, triggered abilities, or an untargeted wipe. Budget the life payment.

Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire

Gives a creature a death-trigger return or acts as a tapped land in an opening hand. Leave Marrow-Gnawer in the graveyard if you want that return to work. The returned commander is a new creature and needs haste or Elixir before it can use its tap ability again that turn.

Reanimate

Recovers a lost commander or a key support creature for one mana and life equal to its mana value. The card must still be in the graveyard when it resolves, so use the command-zone option instead when leaving the commander exposed would be too risky.

Victimize

Trades one expendable creature for two creature cards from your graveyard, returned tapped. Rebuild a token engine and a payoff together when possible, but do not expect the tapped creatures to attack or use tap abilities immediately.

Patriarch's Bidding

Normally choose Rat to rebuild after a wipe. Every player returns creatures matching any chosen type, so this helps opposing graveyards too. Maskwood Nexus makes your creature cards Rats and expands your own recovery. Use graveyard interaction beforehand when an opponent would gain more than you.

Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt

Provides a land option early and a recovery spell later. It returns creatures with different mana values, each no greater than X, so it cannot return all your two-mana Rats at once. Spread the chosen cards across costs to recover multiple functions.

Remove what stops the swarm

Swarmyard Massacre

Creates two Squirrels and reduces creatures outside its protected types according to your qualifying population. Your Rats survive, but natural Humans, Elves, Ogres, Bats, and Spirits among your support creatures may not. Maskwood Nexus makes your entire creature board Rats and protects those support creatures from this spell. Opposing Rats and other listed types also escape the reduction.

Toxic Deluge

The cheaper emergency reset, including against indestructible creatures. It hits your own team too, and neither regeneration nor indestructibility prevents lethal toughness reduction. Prefer it when the threat cannot wait for the more selective tribal wipe.

Gate to Phyrexia

Trades an expendable creature for artifact destruction during your upkeep, at most once each turn. It is an important mono-black answer, but cannot stop an artifact combo immediately on an opponent's turn. Remove a known problem proactively when your upkeep arrives.

Withering Torment

Instant-speed creature or enchantment removal. Feed the Swarm supplies a second enchantment answer at sorcery speed, while Deadly Rollick exiles a creature and can be free with the commander present. Prioritize pieces shutting off creature abilities, graveyards, or combat before minor opposing value engines.

Demonic Tutor

The consistency Game Changer. Find an untap or haste enabler when the commander cannot operate, Skullclamp when your hand is empty, Ashcoat when combat can finish, or a removal spell when an opposing permanent shuts down the plan. It is not dedicated to a compact infinite combo.

Diabolic Intent

Turns a spare Rat into the same flexibility while triggering death payoffs. Sacrificing is an additional cost, so the creature is lost even if Intent is countered. Avoid sacrificing enough bodies that the tutored payoff no longer has a useful swarm behind it.

Urza's Saga

Produces Constructs and finds Sol Ring, Skullclamp, or Soul-Guide Lantern. Constructs are not naturally Rats, though Maskwood Nexus makes them count. Saga sacrifices itself after its last chapter, so preserve enough lasting black sources for the following turns.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep three functional mana sources, an early Rat or Rat producer, and acceleration or a draw engine. Two-land hands need a credible way to find more mana. This deck has 36 lands plus Malakir Rebirth and Agadeem's Awakening as land options. Colorless utility lands cannot pay the black symbols on your early Rats, and Cavern of Souls naming Rat cannot spend its colored mana on Ayara or a noncreature spell.

An example keep is three Swamps, Engine Rat, Arcane Signet, Lord Skitter, Sewer King, and Skullclamp. Develop a Rat, ramp on turn two, then establish Skitter or Clamp according to the table. The commander is strongest when it arrives to several Rats; do not sacrifice away the whole starting population merely to draw more cards.

Haste and untap effects determine when it is safe to cast Marrow-Gnawer. With Boots, Greaves, Sting, or Elixir prepared, the first activation can produce value immediately. Without one, consider whether you can protect the commander through a full turn cycle before investing five mana.

Make Rats during opponents' turns when you can, then untap with attackers ready. Track separately which tokens cannot block, which have summoning sickness, and which have received temporary boosts. Save cheap Rat bodies for activating Marrow-Gnawer rather than risking every creature in combat.

Choose a finish that matches the defense. Use Ashcoat and Coat of Arms when combat is open, Monument when you need flying, Karumonix when ten Rat connections are achievable, or Ayara, Rotcaller, and Mirkwood Bats when life loss is safer. Protect the payoff that makes the current board lethal instead of continually growing the swarm without ending the game.
