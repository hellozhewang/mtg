The single biggest mistake a new pilot makes with Killian is viewing him as a fragile casual Voltron commander rather than an oppressive Bracket 4 combo-tempo engine. Killian's static ability discounts *any* spell targeting a creature by {2} generic mana. This does not merely accelerate your Auras: it transforms premium interaction like Dismember into free removal, turns Hatred into an instant-speed three-mana one-shot that gains back all twenty-one life via Killian's natural lifelink, and allows you to tutor and storm through your entire library with Bolas's Citadel, Sensei's Divining Top, and Aetherflux Reservoir as early as turn three.

The Ink Duelist and cost reduction mechanics
Killian, Ink Duelist

Two-mana commander with lifelink and menace that discounts all spells you cast targeting a creature by {2} generic mana.

Sram, Senior Edificer

Two-mana engine that draws a card whenever you cast an Aura or Equipment spell.

Kor Spiritdancer

Two-mana engine that draws a card on every Aura cast and gains +2/+2 for each Aura attached to it.

Mesa Enchantress

Three-mana enchantress drawing cards whenever any enchantment spell is cast.

Instant-speed one-shots and lethal auras
+
Hatred + Killian, Ink Duelist

Cast Hatred targeting Killian for {1}{B}{B} thanks to Killian's {2} discount. Pay 19 life to give Killian +19/+0 until end of turn. Because Killian has menace and evasion, he connects for 21 lethal commander damage, and his natural lifelink immediately heals you for all 21 life, eliminating an opponent for three mana.

Storm combos and infinite finishers
++
Bolas's Citadel + Sensei's Divining Top + Aetherflux Reservoir

Bolas's Citadel lets you cast Sensei's Divining Top from the top of your library for 1 life. Tap Top to draw a card and place Top on top of your library. Each cast triggers Aetherflux Reservoir to gain exponentially increasing life. Repeat until you have over 150 life, then activate Aetherflux Reservoir's 50-damage cannon to blast every opponent to zero.

+
Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista

Pay {1}{W} to give Walking Ballista lifelink. Remove a +1/+1 counter from Ballista to deal 1 damage to any target. Lifelink triggers Heliod to put a +1/+1 counter back on Ballista. Repeat infinitely for lethal table burn.

Opening hand and sequencing

Prioritize opening hands with fast mana (Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond, Mana Vault, Sol Ring, Dark Ritual), at least 2 lands, and either an explosive tutor, a draw engine, or an early Aura package.

Cast Killian on turn 1 or 2 using fast mana or untapped dual lands.

On turn 2 or 3, deploy protection (Mother of Runes, Grand Abolisher, Silence) or immediately attack and hold up {1}{B}{B} for Hatred to eliminate the most dangerous player at the table.

Alternatively, assemble the Bolas's Citadel storm loop or the Heliod + Walking Ballista combo using your deep tutor suite to kill the entire pod simultaneously on turns 3 through 5.

If playing the long game, use The One Ring, Necropotence, and enchantress draws to bury opponents in card advantage while controlling the board with heavily discounted removal spells.
