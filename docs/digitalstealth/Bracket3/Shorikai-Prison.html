A prison deck that draws two cards a turn from behind a wall nobody can attack through, and then wins with a number rather than a board. The commander is an artifact, not a creature, which is the whole trick: the lock pieces that switch off everyone else's board leave Shorikai untouched. The kill is The Millennium Calendar — the same untap effects that let Shorikai draw four times a round also let the Calendar double its counters four times a round.

The engine
Shorikai, Genesis Engine

{1} and tap: draw two, discard one, and make a Pilot. Note it is a VEHICLE, so it is not a creature until you crew it — which means the symmetrical creature hate below never turns it off. Uncrewed, it is an artifact that quietly outdraws the table.

Humility

every creature becomes a vanilla 1/1. Their commanders, their engines, their blockers — all blank. Shorikai is not a creature, so it keeps drawing. This is the single most important card in the deck and the reason Humility is worth a Game Changer slot.

Unwinding Clock

untaps ALL your artifacts during every opponent's untap step. Four Shorikai activations a round instead of one, at instant speed — and four Calendar doublings.

Unstoppable Plan

untaps every nonland permanent at your end step, which is another Shorikai activation and another Calendar double each turn.

Voltaic Key

the cheap version — untap Shorikai and draw two more, or untap the Calendar.

Manifold Key

the same, and it can make a Pilot unblockable if you ever need to attack.

Bender's Waterskin

untaps on every opponent's untap step and fixes your colours.

Monument to Endurance

the discard from Shorikai stops being a cost. Every card you pitch drains an opponent or makes a Treasure.

How you actually win
The Millennium Calendar

your untap step puts a time counter on it for each permanent you untapped — on a developed board that is a dozen or more — and then {2} and a tap DOUBLES the total. At a thousand counters every opponent loses a thousand life.

Do the maths before you commit. Fifteen counters doubles to 30, 60, 120, 240, 480, 960, 1920 — seven activations. With Unwinding Clock untapping the Calendar on all three opponents' untap steps, plus Unstoppable Plan at your end step, that is five doublings per turn cycle. It kills from a standing start in under two full rounds.

Do NOT copy the Calendar with Mirrormade or Sculpting Steel. It is legendary — the copy and the original cannot coexist and you will lose all your counters. Copy Rhystic Study, Moat or Unwinding Clock instead.

Sculpting Steel

a second Unwinding Clock, Sol Ring or Thought Vessel.

Mirrormade

a second Moat, Rhystic Study, Humility or Greater Auramancy. Usually the best card in the deck by the mid-game.

Out of Time

a wrath that leaves your board alone, because your board is artifacts and enchantments.

Supreme Verdict

the same job, uncounterable, for when a creature deck gets ahead before the wall is up.

The wall
Moat

creatures without flying cannot attack you at all. Against three green and red decks this frequently reads "you cannot lose".

Propaganda

taxes what Moat does not stop. In a four-player game a tax is a deterrent, not a speed bump — they attack whoever did not play one.

Crawlspace

caps the swing at two creatures regardless of what they pay.

Energy Field

no damage to you at all, until something hits your graveyard — and Rest in Peace means your graveyard stays permanently empty, so it never gets sacrificed. Greater Auramancy protects the pair.

Maze of Ith

untaps and blanks the one attacker that gets through, every turn, from the mana base.

Blind Obedience

their creatures and artifacts arrive tapped, so no haste kills and no surprise blockers, and extort drains on every spell you cast.

Stopping the spells
Rule of Law

one spell per turn each. This is the half of the lock that most decks cannot play through.

Arcane Laboratory

the redundant copy, so you assemble it more often.

Knowledge Pool

with either of the two above, nobody casts anything again. They cast from hand, it gets exiled, and the one-spell limit stops the free replacement. Bracket 3 allows a two-card lock like this; just do not assemble it before turn 6.

Trinisphere

everything costs at least three. Brutal against the cheap interaction that would otherwise answer your lock pieces.

Narset, Parter of Veils

one draw per turn for them, while you draw two every turn. Also shuts off wheels.

Teferi's Puzzle Box

pairs with Narset — they shuffle their hand away each turn and are allowed to draw exactly one card back.

Teferi, Time Raveler

they can only act at sorcery speed, so nothing interrupts your combo turn.

Bilbo's Gambit

bounces a spell, and gifted it stops everyone casting for the rest of the turn.

Switching off their board
Cursed Totem

no activated abilities from creatures. Mana dorks, sacrifice outlets, tutor creatures — all off. Shorikai's ability is on a VEHICLE, so yours still works.

Torpor Orb

no enter-the-battlefield triggers. The most common way a table breaks out of a prison is a value creature; this removes it.

Grafdigger's Cage

nothing comes back from a graveyard or a library.

Rest in Peace

no graveyards at all — and it is what makes Energy Field permanent.

Karn, the Great Creator

their artifacts stop working, and yours become whatever you need.

Liquimetal Torque

turns any problem permanent into an artifact for Karn to shut off, or for Whir of Invention to find an answer to.

Finding the piece you need
Whir of Invention

instant speed, improvise off your own artifacts, fetches The Millennium Calendar, Cursed Totem, Torpor Orb or Trinisphere.

Fabricate

the cheaper, unconditional version.

Idyllic Tutor

the enchantment half — Humility, Moat, Rule of Law, Rest in Peace.

Tezzeret the Seeker

untaps artifacts, and the minus finds any artifact and puts it straight onto the battlefield.

Sensei's Divining Top

sets your draws, and Shorikai's draw-two turns it over constantly.

Fomori Vault

digs as deep as your artifact count, out of the mana base.

Greater Auramancy

protects Humility, Moat and Energy Field from the removal aimed at them.

Interaction
Mana Drain

counter their answer, and the mana doubles the Calendar twice.

Counterspell

the clean two-mana version.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

Dovin's Veto

uncounterable.

Swan Song

one mana for a wrath or an enchantment removal spell.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

the same for anything noncreature.

Arcane Denial

cheap, and the cards they draw arrive too late under Narset.

Swords to Plowshares

for the one creature that flies or ignores Moat.

March of Otherworldly Light

scalable exile at instant speed.

Chain of Vapor

one mana to bounce the permanent that is beating you.

Clever Concealment

usually free with convoke, and it phases your whole lock out of a wrath.

Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow

an artifact or enchantment killed on entry, and a land when you do not need the effect.

What to keep in an opening hand

Look for one wall and one draw source, in that order. A hand with Moat or Propaganda plus a rock is far better than a hand of expensive lock pieces you cannot deploy.

Shorikai does NOT need to resolve early. It is four mana of pure card advantage that gets better later; the walls are what stop you losing while you find it.

Rule of Law

deploy this BEFORE Knowledge Pool. The other order lets them empty their hand for free in response.

Humility

hold it until their board actually matters. It is symmetrical for everyone but you, and casting it into an empty board wastes the surprise.

The Millennium Calendar

land it early even when you cannot win yet. The counters accumulate every untap step for free, and every turn it sits there is one fewer doubling you need later.
