The single biggest rule a new pilot gets wrong is underestimating how Tana's token generation scales with power boosts. Tana does not create a fixed number of tokens; she creates Saprolings equal to the combat damage she actually deals to a player. Every +1/+1 counter from Ishai synergies, every anthem from Cathars' Crusade or Beastmaster Ascension, and every pump spell multiplies her token output exponentially. Trample ensures that excess damage pushes through blockers directly into a massive army of Saprolings.

The commanders: air superiority and ground swarm
Ishai, Ojutai Dragonspeaker

Deploys for {2}{W}{U} to dominate the air. Grows with a +1/+1 counter whenever an opponent casts any spell, rapidly expanding into a lethal flying commander damage threat while absorbing table removal.

Tana, the Bloodsower

Deploys for {2}{R}{G} with trample to generate a swarm of 1/1 green Saprolings equal to combat damage dealt. Benefits immensely from evasion, power buffs, and anthems.

Token multiplication and impact engines
Anointed Procession

Doubles all token production, turning a four-damage Tana connection into eight Saprolings.

Parallel Lives

Redundant green token doubler that accelerates wide boards exponentially.

Doubling Season

Doubles both tokens created and counters placed on permanents, supercharging both Tana's Saprolings and Ishai's +1/+1 counters.

Ocelot Pride

One-mana lifelink engine that achieves the city's blessing effortlessly, duplicating every token that entered this turn at your end step.

Cathars' Crusade

Whenever any creature enters, put a +1/+1 counter on every creature you control. When Tana connects for four damage, four Saprolings enter and buff Tana, Ishai, and the entire team with four +1/+1 counters each.

Beastmaster Ascension

Seven quest counters give all creatures you control +5/+5. Tana immediately hits as a 7/7 trampler that creates seven Saprolings on every swing.

Purphoros, God of the Forge

Indestructible god that deals two damage to each opponent whenever any creature enters. A wide Tana connection can easily burn the table for ten to twenty damage in one combat.

Impact Tremors

Redundant two-mana burn engine that pings every opponent whenever a Saproling or creature token enters.

Skullclamp

Equips to a 1/1 Saproling for {1} to kill it and draw two cards, turning your token swarm into relentless card velocity.

Halo Fountain

Versatile artifact that untaps tapped tokens to create Citizens or draw cards, with an alternate win condition that wins the game by untapping fifteen creatures.

Counter synergies and scaling threats
Hardened Scales

Adds an additional +1/+1 counter whenever counters are placed on your creatures, speeding up Ishai's aerial clock.

Bristly Bill, Spine Sower

Landfall places +1/+1 counters on Tana or Ishai, and his five-mana activated ability doubles all counters across your board.

Danny Pink

Draws a card whenever counters are put on a creature for the first time each turn, triggering repeatedly off Ishai's counter gains.

Forgotten Ancient

Hoards +1/+1 counters whenever any player casts a spell, then transfers them onto Tana at upkeep to multiply her Saproling generation.

Managorger Hydra

Trampling beast that gains counters on every spell cast by any player, growing alongside Ishai into an overwhelming beater.

The Ozolith

Stores all counters from dying or removed creatures and redistributes them onto Tana or Ishai at the start of combat.

All Will Be One

Converts every counter placed on Ishai, Forgotten Ancient, and your board into direct damage against any target.

Springheart Nantuko

Landfall engine that bestows onto Tana to create token copies or spawns 1/1 Insects.

The compact finish and alpha strikes
+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

Infinite hasty Dualcaster Mages. Cast Twinflame targeting any creature you control, hold priority, and cast Dualcaster Mage with flash. Copy Twinflame targeting Dualcaster Mage, creating a token copy that copies Twinflame again. Repeat for infinite attackers.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication

Redundant two-mana infinite combo line using Molten Duplication.

In accordance with Bracket 3 rules, this two-card combo is reserved for turn 6 or later. Spend early turns developing your mana, applying combat pressure with your commanders, and establishing token engines before closing out the game.

Craterhoof Behemoth

The premier combat finisher. Grants trample and enormous power buffs scaling with your total creature count to trample over any opposing defense.

Moonshaker Cavalry

White counterpart to Craterhoof that gives your entire army flying and power boosts scaling with your creature count.

Finale of Devastation

Tutors Craterhoof or Dualcaster directly onto the battlefield; with X=10, grants +X/+X and haste to your entire army for a lethal game-ending swing.

Ramp and the Cradle engine
Gaea's Cradle

Taps for green mana equal to the number of creatures you control. With Tana's Saproling swarm, Cradle routinely produces ten to twenty mana to fund massive Finale casts.

Jaheira, Friend of the Forest

Turns every Saproling and token you control into an active mana dork tapping for {G}.

Bloom Tender

Taps for up to four mana of different colors with Ishai and Tana on the battlefield.

Faeburrow Elder

Vigilant beater that taps for four mana across all four of your deck's colors.

Delighted Halfling

Uncounterable legendary mana acceleration for Ishai and Tana.

Birds of Paradise

Classic one-mana flying dork fixing all four colors.

Noble Hierarch

Exalted dork that pumps Tana's initial combat damage while fixing colors.

Sol Ring

Standard colorless fast mana.

Arcane Signet

Reliable four-color mana rock.

Fellwar Stone

Efficient two-mana rock producing multiple colors.

Nature's Lore

Searches for any land with the Forest type (Breeding Pool, Stomping Ground, Taiga, Tropical Island) to enter untapped.

Three Visits

Redundant Forest searcher entering untapped.

Farseek

Searches for Plains, Island, or Mountain dual lands to fix your mana curve.

→

→
Urza's Saga → Skullclamp

Chapter III searches Skullclamp directly into play to draw through your deck, or grabs Sol Ring.

Protection and countermagic
Grand Abolisher

Shuts off opposing interaction on your turn, ensuring your token deployment and combo lines resolve undisturbed.

Teferi, Time Raveler

Restricts opponents to sorcery-speed casting, eliminating opposing counterspells and instant-speed removal.

Heroic Intervention

Grants all your permanents hexproof and indestructible in response to opposing board wipes.

Boros Charm

Protects all your permanents with indestructible or grants double strike to Tana to double her Saprolings.

Deflecting Swat

Free redirection spell with Ishai or Tana on the battlefield.

Cyclonic Rift

Asymmetrical board wipe that bounces every nonland permanent your opponents control, clearing the way for an unblockable alpha strike.

Rhystic Study

Relentless card draw engine that taxes opponents on every spell.

Mystic Remora

Explosive early noncreature spell tax drawing cards in the opening turns.

Esper Sentinel

One-mana draw engine that scales as counters are placed on it.

Archivist of Oghma

Draws cards whenever opponents search their libraries.

Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar

Protects your turn from opponent spells and draws cards whenever modified creatures deal combat damage.

Toski, Bearer of Secrets

Indestructible squirrel that draws a card whenever any of your creatures deal combat damage to a player.

Dovin's Veto

Uncounterable noncreature counterspell.

Counterspell

Reliable two-mana hard counter.

Swords to Plowshares

Clean one-mana creature exile.

Path to Exile

Unconditional one-mana creature exile.

Opening hand and sequencing

Look for opening hands with three lands, a mana dork (Birds of Paradise, Delighted Halfling, Noble Hierarch), and either an engine piece (Skullclamp, Hardened Scales) or early interaction.

Cast Ishai on turn 3 to begin accumulating +1/+1 counters as opponents cast their ramp and setup spells.

Cast Tana on turn 4, using Ishai's aerial presence to force opponents into awkward blocking choices while Tana connects on the ground to spawn Saprolings.

On turn 5, deploy an engine multiplier (Cathars' Crusade, Purphoros, Beastmaster Ascension, or Gaea's Cradle) to convert your Saproling board into overwhelming table damage and mana.

On turn 6 or later, either drop Craterhoof Behemoth / Moonshaker Cavalry to execute an alpha strike, or resolve Dualcaster Mage and Twinflame to finish off the table.
