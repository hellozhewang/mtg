Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

The crew only comes back if its creatures had +1/+1 counters when they died and Marchesa saw those deaths. Establish a sacrifice outlet and a reliable counter source before committing to a big theft turn.

This is a mixed fictional street-crew and heist theme: eight cards keep their original Magic or official crossover characters, and the other 57 nonland cards have custom GTA character assignments below. Every custom art brief explicitly includes a visible character, even for mana rocks and spells. Lands are exempt.

The import file keeps the real Magic card names and rules. The casting sheet is a complete reskin plan, not rendered proxy artwork. What the import file does carry is a real printing for 52 cards, written as a set code and collector number after the name, so Tabletop Simulator loads the official art closest to each brief. The catalog pages still show each card's default image. Custom nicknames and pictures do not change colors, types, abilities, mana values, names used by game rules or commander identity. Token rules also stay unchanged.

CJ leads the crew
Marchesa, the Black Rose

Custom character: CJ. Display nickname: CJ, Grove Street Boss. Art: CJ rallying the crew outside his neighborhood home. Dethrone supplies counters when you attack a player tied for highest life; a creature with a counter that dies under your control returns at the next end step, even if you do not own it.

The original underworld cast
Professional Face-Breaker

Original Magic character: New Capenna's professional enforcer. Keep a character-focused original printing; combat connections produce Treasure and Treasure becomes impulsive card access.

Grim Hireling

Original Magic character: the hired rogue. Keep its character art; connecting with several players funds future turns and its Treasure-powered removal.

Body Launderer

Original Magic character: New Capenna's underworld cleaner. Its connive counters and death-trigger recursion support the crew's repeated returns.

Body Dropper

Original Magic character: New Capenna's enforcer. Sacrificing other creatures grows it, and its paid sacrifice ability supplies an additional outlet.

Dack Fayden

Original Magic character: Dack himself, the thief. His artifact theft and looting fit both the character theme and the resource-stealing plan.

Black Cat, Cunning Thief

Official crossover character: Felicia Hardy. Keep Black Cat's own artwork; each real entrance gives access to two cards from an opponent's top nine.

Satoru, the Infiltrator

Original Magic character: Satoru, the infiltrator. Keep his own artwork; Marchesa's returning creatures become fresh cards.

Fain, the Broker

Original Magic character: Fain, the dealmaker. He converts crew members into counters, counters into Treasure, or artifacts into an evasive token.

Keep the crew returning
+
Unspeakable Symbol + Marchesa, the Black Rose

pay three life to put a counter on a creature before it dies, including Marchesa herself. This works without attacking and can lower your life total enough to enable dethrone. Save life for removal and future activations rather than putting counters on everything automatically.

+
Gev, Scaled Scorch + Zulaport Cutthroat

drain each opponent before creatures enter so Gev supplies three counters in a four-player game. Life lost on an earlier turn does not count. Gev does not give itself counters, and the life loss must happen before the entrance.

+
Drana, Liberator of Malakir + Danny Pink

Drana connecting during first-strike damage puts counters on all your attackers before normal damage. Danny draws for each creature's first counter-placement event that turn, including appropriate enters-with-counters events while his ability applies.

++
Viscera Seer + Carrion Feeder + Goblin Bombardment

free, repeatable sacrifice outlets convert targeted removal or a borrowed creature into value. Sacrifice before the end step begins if you want Marchesa to return that creature during that end step. Carrion Feeder cannot block.

Yawgmoth, Thran Physician

converts other creatures and life into cards and optional -1/-1 counters. Protection from Humans means Human-source targeted abilities such as Fain's counter ability or Danny's mentor cannot target him. Marchesa's granted dethrone does not target and still works.

+
Spark Double + Marchesa, the Black Rose

make a nonlegendary second Marchesa with a counter already on it, protecting the engine's continuity. Two Marchesas give two dethrone abilities, but their death triggers cannot return the same card twice. A returning Double can choose a fresh copy target.

If Marchesa and counter-bearing creatures die together, her ability still sees their deaths. Leave Marchesa in the graveyard when relying on its own return trigger; moving it to the command zone prevents that return. Exile, bounce and graveyard removal evade the basic engine. Tokens cannot return after leaving the battlefield.

Recruit the other side
++
Mark of Mutiny + Goblin Bombardment + Marchesa, the Black Rose

Mark both steals a creature and puts the needed counter on it. Attack if useful, then sacrifice it while you still control it. Marchesa returns the card under your control without the original theft effect's expiration; an opposing commander may escape through its owner's command-zone choice.

+
Captivating Crew + Unspeakable Symbol

steal at sorcery speed, then add a counter without relying on combat. Zealous Conscripts and Molten Primordial offer entrance-based versions; borrowed creatures with no counter will not return through Marchesa.

With Agent of Treachery and Marchesa, the Black Rose, reuse the Agent's entrance to take important nonland permanents. Its three-card draw needs three permanents you do not own already under your control when your end step begins; an Agent or third stolen permanent returning later that end step does not retroactively establish the trigger.

Soul-Guide Lantern provides graveyard interaction that leaves your theft engine intact. Once a stolen card is in its owner's graveyard awaiting Marchesa, avoid exiling that graveyard yourself. Graveyard exile aimed at that card can break the delayed return.

Cash out and finish
+
Professional Face-Breaker + Grim Hireling

attacking several players can produce Treasure from each connection, while dethrone only rewards attacks at the highest-life player. Choose between maximum income and reliable counters rather than assuming every attacker receives both.

+
Warren Soultrader + Pitiless Plunderer

an eligible creature sacrificed to Soultrader costs one life and produces two Treasures with Plunderer present. This is finite unless another effect supplies more creatures; Marchesa returns them at a later end step, not immediately.

+
Juri, Master of the Revue + Uncivil Unrest

sacrifices grow Juri, and dying converts its last power into damage. With a +1/+1 counter, Unrest doubles that creature damage. Unrest also gives nontoken creatures a choice of a counter or haste on entry; choose the counter when recursion is the priority.

+
Herald of Secret Streams + Uncivil Unrest

counter-bearing attackers become unblockable and deal double damage, creating a clear combat finish. Herald does not grant haste, and removing it before blockers can reopen blocking.

+
Gray Merchant of Asphodel + Marchesa, the Black Rose

repeat Merchant entrances to drain all opponents and recover the life spent on your engine. A large life gain can turn off dethrone by putting you alone at the highest life total, so retain another counter source.

Flayer of the Hatebound

your creatures returning directly from YOUR graveyard become targeted damage. A stolen creature returning from its owner's graveyard does not qualify. Living Death returns creatures from exile, so its returns do not trigger Flayer.

+
Satoru, the Infiltrator + Grim Haruspex

dying nontoken creatures draw through Haruspex, and uncast returns draw through Satoru. Separate Marchesa return triggers usually generate separate draws; multiple creatures entering together through Living Death produce one Satoru trigger.

+
Sneak Attack + Marchesa, the Black Rose

put a powerful entrance creature onto the battlefield for one red, then give it a counter and sacrifice it before the end step starts. If you wait for Sneak Attack's end-step sacrifice, Marchesa cannot return it until the following end step. Braids sacrificing a creature during the end step has the same delay.

Find the missing role and survive interaction
→
Imperial Recruiter → Viscera Seer

find a cheap free outlet when the engine has counters but cannot sacrifice on demand. Recruiter uses power two or less, not toughness.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Herald of Secret Streams

find the combat finisher when a counter-bearing army is being stalled by blockers. Recruiter can also find Agent of Treachery for a slower value line, but does not pay the Agent's seven-mana cost.

+
Demonic Tutor + Diabolic Intent

prioritize a missing counter source, sacrifice outlet, or answer before searching for another expensive threat. Intent sacrifices a creature as a cost; use one that is already set up to return.

++
Reanimate + Victimize + Living Death

backup recursion after disruption. Reanimate costs life based on mana value. Victimize requires two graveyard targets and sacrifices as it resolves. Living Death also rebuilds opponents, so inspect every graveyard and use Lantern before committing.

++
Cyclonic Rift + Toxic Deluge + Plaguecrafter

three different resets: Rift clears opposing nonlands, Deluge uses toughness reduction, and Plaguecrafter forces sacrifices. Deluge also kills your own creatures and costs life; the counter/recursion engine can make that a favorable exchange.

+++
Swan Song + Mana Drain + An Offer You Can't Refuse + Deflecting Swat

protect the engine from exile and graveyard disruption or stop an opposing win. Offer gives away two Treasures. Swat changes targets and does not stop an untargeted sweeper.

+++
Feed the Swarm + Chaos Warp + Deadly Rollick + Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

answer troublesome permanents outside combat. Feed costs life; Sink normally delays a spell or permanent instead of disposing of it. Lightning Greaves also grants shroud, which stops your own Symbol and other targeted counter abilities until you move the Equipment.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three usable mana sources, early acceleration or a cheap creature, and either a counter source or draw engine. The list has 35 lands plus two spell/land cards; nine fetchlands, original duals, shocks and multiplayer duals establish all three colors. Four basics remain for simple fetch targets and opponents' basic-land search effects.

Develop mana and an outlet, then Marchesa and counters. Do not run your commander into removal with no way to put a counter on it. Black Market Connections and Symbol deliberately spend life, but do not spend yourself into range of an easy attack.

Before a theft turn, confirm that the target can receive a counter and that you can sacrifice it before control expires. Sacrifice returned creatures on a later turn when the timing pays off; repeated end-step returns are a grind engine, not an infinite loop.

The deck normally wins through repeated drains, a large Juri hit or unblockable doubled attacks after a developed board. The character theme takes priority over a maximum-speed combo list, and the guide preserves the real card rules even when a custom picture depicts a different species or role.
