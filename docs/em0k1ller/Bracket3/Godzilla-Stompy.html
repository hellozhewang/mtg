Godzilla changes how much damage is lethal, not your creatures' toughness. Build a draw or mana engine before committing your monsters, and protect that engine: the commander is a seven-power enabler, not a source of cards by himself.

Built for em0k1ller, Bracket 3. Godzilla, King of the Monsters is the alternate printing of Zilortha, Strength Incarnate; the list uses that rules name with the Godzilla artwork pinned. The three Game Changers are Natural Order, Survival of the Fittest and The One Ring. No mass land denial. Infinite finishes wait until turn 6.

Turn seven power into an engine
Zilortha, Strength Incarnate

seven power immediately discounts your engines and turns on your mana creatures. His ability applies to your whole team, including low-power mana creatures, but does not stop deathtouch, zero toughness, exile, sacrifice or destroy effects. If he leaves while other creatures have damage marked, they can die when normal toughness-based checks resume.

The Great Henge

costs just GG with Godzilla's seven power, immediately taps for GG, then turns each nontoken creature into a card and a counter. Develop this before emptying your hand of monsters.

The Skullspore Nexus

costs GG with Godzilla out. Its power-doubling activation strengthens attacks, draw spells and lethal-damage thresholds; its death trigger replaces a destroyed nontoken team with one large token. It does nothing for exile or bounce.

Selvala, Heart of the Wilds

Godzilla makes her tap for seven mana in any combination, after paying G. She can fund another threat plus protection. Her entry trigger can also give opponents cards if their creature is strictly the largest.

Fanatic of Rhonas

taps for four green once a four-power creature is present. Eternalize rebuilds the engine after removal; its resulting four-power token meets its own ferocious condition.

Bugenhagen, Wise Elder

fixes mana early, then draws during your upkeep if Godzilla or another seven-power creature remains present when the trigger checks and resolves. His one power means Godzilla makes even one damage lethal to him.

Goreclaw, Terror of Qal Sisma

discounts the large creature spells and grants qualifying attackers trample when she attacks. The discount does not reduce colored costs or help small utility creatures.

Ghalta the Unstoppable

costs 1G with Godzilla alone, giving you an 8/8 and team trample. Its printed mana value is still nine for tutors; cost reductions do not lower the X required by Finale of Devastation.

Ghalta, Primal Hunger

the team's combined power can reduce this to GG. Its twelve power improves every power-based engine and lets you rebuild pressure without spending eight mana on a single body.

Establish cards before committing the board
Sylvan Library

fixes early draws and lets you spend life to retain extra cards. Use a fetchland after putting unwanted cards back to shuffle them away; do not pay eight life reflexively against aggressive opponents.

The One Ring

supplies cards even after a creature wipe. Casting it buys protection until your next turn, but your permanents remain vulnerable and the burden counters keep costing life.

Garruk's Uprising

provides both entry-based draw and team trample. It checks a creature's power as it enters; later temporary boosts do not retroactively trigger it.

Outcaster Trailblazer

refunds one mana on entry and rewards subsequent four-power creatures. Godzilla's protection from damage makes its 4/2 body less fragile; it still needs ordinary protection from removal.

Bitter Work

draws once for each player attacked by one or more four-power creatures, potentially three cards per combat. Extra combats trigger it again. Its exhaust ability provides a hasty land creature when you need another attacker, at the cost of exposing a land to creature interaction.

Greater Good

turn a doomed seven-power creature into seven cards and discard three in response to removal. Sacrificing small mana creatures is card-negative, and sacrificing Godzilla may expose damaged teammates to lethal damage.

Rishkar's Expertise

draws seven with an unmodified Godzilla, then casts a spell of mana value five or less from your hand. Use that free spell to rebuild an engine or add a threat; it cannot cast your commander from the command zone.

Return of the Wildspeaker

hold up protection, then draw at an opponent's end step if nothing needs answering. It uses your greatest non-Human power; its alternative pump also misses Humans such as Outcaster Trailblazer.

Eternal Witness

returns a removed engine, tutor or protection spell. Choose the card that restores your position rather than automatically recovering the largest creature.

Make the first attack matter
Samut, Hazoret's Champion

two-mana team haste lets mana creatures work immediately and turns newly deployed monsters into immediate attacks. She also gives herself haste.

Enduring Courage

grants each other entering creature +2 power and haste. It returns as a noncreature enchantment when it dies as a creature, preserving a haste engine through destruction; exile stops that recovery.

Cactusfolk Sureshot

grants other creatures with power four or greater haste and trample at the beginning of combat. It does not grant itself haste, and unboosted Pathbreaker Ibex needs a different haste source.

Xenagos, God of Revels

doubles another creature's current power and grants haste at each of your combats. Repeated combats repeatedly increase the same monster's power, or can turn successive threats into hasty attackers.

+
Ouroboroid + Xenagos, God of Revels

put Ouroboroid's beginning-of-combat trigger on the stack first, then Xenagos's trigger targeting it. Xenagos resolves first, increasing the number of counters Ouroboroid gives the whole team. This works at each extra combat.

+
Pathbreaker Ibex + Zilortha, Strength Incarnate

when Ibex attacks alongside an unmodified Godzilla, its trigger gives your team at least +7/+7 and trample. Give Ibex haste or protect it through a turn cycle; it has no haste of its own.

Craterhoof Behemoth

turns accumulated mana creatures into a lethal attack. Count the creatures you can actually attack with: its own haste does not give the rest of your team haste.

Anzrag, the Quake-Mole

an efficient eight-power threat that makes blocking dangerous. Godzilla lets its four-toughness body survive up to seven ordinary damage; trample and protection help it keep attacking. Extra combats are conditional on becoming blocked, not guaranteed infinites.

+
Sneak Attack + Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant

pay R to deploy Ghalta, then put your other creature cards onto the battlefield from its trigger. Only the creature put in by Sneak Attack gets its delayed sacrifice and haste. The creatures Ghalta deploys stay, but need another haste source to attack immediately.

+
Sneak Attack + Greater Good

use a cheated monster's entry and attack, then sacrifice it for cards before its delayed sacrifice resolves. The Skullspore Nexus also replaces that nontoken creature with a token when it dies.

+
Terror of the Peaks + Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant

have Terror present before Ghalta enters to deal twelve from that arrival. Alternatively, put Terror in alongside your other creatures with Ghalta's deployment trigger so it sees those simultaneous arrivals; it will not see Ghalta's earlier entry. Large entries become removal or damage to players without requiring attacks. Terror's own entry does not trigger itself.

Finale of Devastation

at X of ten or more, it gives your current team +X/+X and haste as well as finding a creature. This converts Selvala's mana into a table-wide attack; smaller casts are setup or recovery, not automatic finishers.

Repeat combats from turn six
+
Aggravated Assault + Savage Ventmaw

attack with Ventmaw and let its trigger produce RRR GGG, which lasts through the turn. In the following main phase, spend five of that mana on Assault, untapping your team and creating another combat followed by another main phase. Repeat while Ventmaw can attack and survive. It needs haste or a full turn under your control before the first attack; trample helps the rest of the team finish through blockers.

+
Aggravated Assault + Bear Umbra

enchant a creature that can attack and survive. Its attack trigger untaps your lands; after combat, use lands producing at least five mana, including RR, to activate Assault. Each new attack restores that mana. Mana auras can let fewer than five lands meet the requirement. This loop requires an attacker and working lands in addition to the two enchantments.

++
Aggravated Assault + Selvala, Heart of the Wilds + Zilortha, Strength Incarnate

with Selvala able to tap and an initial G available, tap her for seven, pay five to untap the creatures with Assault, and reserve G to tap her again. Each repetition nets one mana in the same main phase. Make enough mana for a large Finale of Devastation before changing phases. This is a three-card engine; do not mistake the queued combat phases for automatic future untaps, since Assault untaps creatures when its activation resolves.

Bear Umbra

ordinary attacks also let you develop a board and untap lands to hold up protection. Umbra armor saves the enchanted creature from one destruction event, but not exile, sacrifice or zero toughness. Lightning Greaves must be moved off a creature before you can target it with this Aura.

These loops are optional finishes supporting a large-creature attack plan. Creature tutors can find Ventmaw or Selvala but cannot find Assault or Bear Umbra. Do not keep an otherwise weak hand solely because it contains one enchantment, and do not rely on unprotected infinite combat against open removal.

Tutors with a purpose
→
Survival of the Fittest → Selvala, Heart of the Wilds

exchange a redundant creature for mana when you already have a large threat. The tutor costs G and a creature card, and the fetched card still needs to be cast.

→
Survival of the Fittest → Savage Ventmaw

find the missing creature when Assault is available and you can supply haste or protect Ventmaw through a turn. Otherwise prioritize a draw engine or protection instead of a stranded combo half.

→
Survival of the Fittest → Frenzied Baloth

fetch before committing a creature finisher into counterspell mana or damage prevention. Baloth cannot be countered and shields subsequent creature spells; it does not protect Assault or your sorcery tutors.

→
Natural Order → Craterhoof Behemoth

sacrifice a small green creature when the remaining board is wide enough for lethal. Count the sacrifice before calculating the boost and damage; losing the spell to a counter also loses that sacrificed creature.

→
Natural Order → Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant

use this when your hand holds several strong creatures and you have an entry payoff or haste engine. Ghalta's deployment trigger can be answered, so avoid making this your only plan.

→
Green Sun's Zenith → Fanatic of Rhonas

X=2 builds mana when a four-power threat is already present or coming next turn. This tutor finds only green creatures; it cannot find Samut or Terror of the Peaks.

→
Finale of Devastation → Pathbreaker Ibex

retrieve or find Ibex when a haste source and a large creature are ready. At X=6 this costs eight total mana and does not itself grant haste; X=10 is the threshold for Finale's additional team boost.

→
Eldritch Evolution → Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant

sacrifice six-mana Kogla, the Titan Ape or Savage Ventmaw to reach Ghalta's mana value eight when your hand supports a deployment. A one-mana creature can instead reach Selvala, Heart of the Wilds for an engine upgrade.

Protect the engine and answer the obstacle
Frenzied Baloth

prevents combat damage prevention and makes your creature spells uncounterable. It does not bypass an effect that stops attacking or prevents damage assignment by removing the attacker, and it does not stop removal after a creature resolves.

Legolas's Quick Reflexes

protect a key creature from targeted removal, then gain a removal trigger when it next taps. Targeting Selvala can untap her for another mana activation, and a large attacker can remove a blocker before blockers are declared. Split second lasts only while this spell is on the stack.

+
Heroic Intervention + Tamiyo's Safekeeping

choose team protection or one permanent's protection according to the threat. Indestructible handles destruction, not sacrifice, mass exile or enough toughness reduction; leave mana open before committing another monster.

+
Deflecting Swat + Veil of Summer

Swat redirects targeted interaction and is free while Godzilla is present. Veil protects the critical turn against blue or black targeting and makes your spells uncounterable; neither is a general answer to every sweeper.

+
Force of Vigor + Nature's Claim

clear artifacts and enchantments that prevent attacking or disable engines. Force can trade a spare green card for two targets for no mana only on opponents' turns; Claim's life gain rarely matters once the combat engine is working.

+
Beast Within + Chaos Warp

answer permanents the narrower removal misses, including a planeswalker or a troublesome creature. Both leave the opponent something, so aim them at obstacles that actually prevent your win.

Kogla, the Titan Ape

fights a creature on entry and removes an artifact or enchantment when attacking that opponent. Returning Samut or Outcaster Trailblazer with its activation can protect Kogla and save or reuse the Human, but costs mana and may temporarily remove your haste or draw engine.

Endurance

disrupts graveyard setup at instant speed. Evoke is available when tapped out but exiles a green card and normally sacrifices Endurance, so hard-cast it when the body and card economy matter.

Chandra's Ignition

a large creature can sweep blockers and damage all three opponents. It also hits every OTHER creature you control; Godzilla does not make this one-sided, and removing the targeted creature can make the spell fail. Damage dealt to players this way is not commander combat damage.

Blasphemous Act

the emergency reset, not a free asymmetric wipe. Thirteen damage kills an unmodified Godzilla and most of your team. Protect the board with Heroic Intervention or accept the losses and rebuild using the persistent draw engines and The Skullspore Nexus.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep three usable mana sources with early green, a one- or two-mana accelerator, and either a draw engine or a coherent route to a threat. A two-land hand needs dependable acceleration and card selection; ship hands full of expensive creatures without early development. There are 35 lands plus two spell/land MDFCs, with four Forests and one Mountain keeping fetchable targets available after the typed duals are used.

Delighted Halfling

accelerates Godzilla and other legendary spells through counters. Its colored mana cannot pay for ordinary nonlegendary creatures or protection spells; count those colored sources honestly in opening hands.

+
Nature's Lore + Three Visits

find Taiga or Stomping Ground to fix both colors, or another Forest card as needed. A fetched Stomping Ground still needs its life payment to enter untapped, while Commercial District and Sheltered Thicket enter tapped regardless.

+
Utopia Sprawl + Wild Growth

create turn-one acceleration that survives creature wipes. Sprawl requires a Forest and can supply red to fund Assault later. Prefer a secure Forest target instead of relying on Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth to keep a non-Forest eligible after Yavimaya leaves.

+
Arena of Glory + Lightning Greaves

two additional haste sources. Arena needs a Mountain to enter untapped and colored mana to use its exert ability; Greaves gives shroud, so move it before targeting that creature with Xenagos, Bear Umbra or your own protection spell.

+
Turntimber Symbiosis // Turntimber, Serpentine Wood + Shatterskull Smashing // Shatterskull, the Hammer Pass

play their land faces when your opening hand needs mana; pay three life only when the untapped source matters. Later, the spell faces turn extra mana into threats or removal, but Turntimber's top-seven selection is not a guaranteed tutor.

Gemstone Caverns

early acceleration only when in the opening hand and you are not starting, at the cost of exiling another card. Without its luck counter it produces colorless, so it cannot replace your early green source.

Kessig Wolf Run

use spare mana to force damage through a blocker or increase a creature's power before a draw spell. Keep this land untapped when planning to activate it; its mana cost is separate from the tap cost.

Turns one and two establish mana; turns three through five establish draw, haste and threatening bodies while retaining protection. Do not cast Godzilla automatically if an engine gives you more immediate value. The two-card combat loops and other infinite finishes wait until turn 6 under the house rules. Before then, develop, interact and preserve your cards instead of committing everything into a sweeper.

A strong draw can threaten the table around turns six to eight, but this is an A-tier combat deck, not a commander-driven combo engine. Five creature tutors improve access to threats and creature combo pieces; none searches the crucial enchantment directly. Board wipes, mass exile and attack restrictions are the main weaknesses, so preserve a draw engine and keep an answer or a rebuild plan.
