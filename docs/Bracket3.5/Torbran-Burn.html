This deck damages everyone at the table, you included, and wins because it always hurts them more. Torbran adds 2 to every hit a red source of yours lands on an opponent or anything they control. Earthquake for 3 costs you 3 and each opponent 5; a ping of 1 becomes 3. Many small hits beat one big one, because the +2 is added to every single hit.

One rule decides every number below. When several effects change damage dealt to an opponent, THAT OPPONENT chooses the order they apply in (CR 616.1), and they will pick the smallest result: double first, then add. So a ping of 1 with Torbran and Twinflame Tyrant deals 4 (1×2+2), not 6.

The commander

Torbran, Thane of Red Fell

red sources you control deal 2 more to opponents and anything they control — spells, enchantments, creatures, and combat damage too, so every red attacker hits for 2 more. Only RED sources count: Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle and Sokenzan's Spirit tokens are colourless and get nothing from Torbran.

Lightning Greaves

shroud the moment Torbran lands, because the table knows exactly what it does. It also gives Heartless Hidetsugu the haste it needs.

Swiftfoot Boots

the second copy — hexproof and haste for Torbran or Heartless Hidetsugu.

Deflecting Swat

free with your commander out, and it can send their removal at one of their own permanents.

Stacking the damage — every one of these is one-sided

All seven of these only increase damage to OPPONENTS and their permanents. None of them makes your own Earthquake hurt you more. That is why Fiery Emancipation is not in this deck: it triples damage to you from your own sources too.

Twinflame Tyrant

doubles damage from ANY source you control, colourless ones included. With Torbran, a ping of 1 becomes 4.

Solphim, Mayhem Dominus

doubles noncombat damage, which is nearly all of this deck. With Twinflame Tyrant as well, a ping of 1 becomes 6 (1×2×2+2).

Fiendish Duo

doubles damage to your opponents from ANY source, even their own. When they burn each other, that counts double too.

Mechanized Warfare

+1 on red and artifact sources. With Torbran, a ping of 1 is 4.

Thor, Asgard's Avenger

+1 on damage from every OTHER source you control, colourless ones included, so Valakut and Sokenzan's Spirits get it even though Torbran ignores them.

Fated Firepower

flash it in at instant speed with X fire counters, and every hit gets +X. On a big mana turn it adds more than all the others combined.

Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might // Temple of Power

any red noncombat hit on an opponent for less than Ojer's power (4) becomes 4. With Torbran out, the opponent applies Torbran first (1+2=3, raised to 4), so a ping of 1 deals 4. When Ojer dies it comes back as a land.

Damage to every player

The core of the deck. Each of these hits you once, and each opponent once plus Torbran's 2.

Earthquake

X to every player and every creature without flying. X of 5 costs you 5, deals 7 to each opponent, and wipes their ground creatures.

Rolling Earthquake

the same, and it hits fliers too.

Flame Rift

two mana: 4 to you and 6 to each opponent.

Volcanic Fallout

an instant that can't be countered — 2 to you, and 4 to each opponent and each of their creatures.

Slagstorm

pick the mode: 3 to every player (5 to each opponent), or 3 to every creature as a sweeper.

Price of Progress

2 for every nonbasic land each player controls. Three-colour opponents often run fifteen or more; this deck runs six. Against a greedy mana base, one card finishes a player.

Pyrohemia

{R}: 1 to every creature and every player, which is 3 to each opponent and each of their creatures every time you activate it. It's the best mana sink in the deck. It sacrifices itself at end step if no creatures are left, and indestructible Brash Taunter keeps one around.

Sulfuric Vortex

2 to each player on their own upkeep (4 to opponents), and nobody gains life.

Roiling Vortex

1 to every player each upkeep, 5 to anyone who casts a spell without spending mana, and {R} stops opponents gaining life that turn. Your own free Deflecting Swat counts, so pay for it while the Vortex is out.

Manabarbs

1 damage every time a player taps a land for mana — 3 for opponents. An opponent who taps seven lands takes 21.

Descent into Avernus

2, then 4, then 6 damage to every player on your upkeeps, plus that many Treasures for everyone. Opponents take 2 more each time, but every opponent gets the Treasures too — cast it when you can use the mana better than they can.

Themberchaud

when it enters, it deals damage equal to your Mountain count to every other creature without flying and every player. This deck runs 28 Mountains.

Ashling the Pilgrim

{1}{R} for a counter; the third activation in one turn removes them all and deals that much to every creature and every player. Grow it two counters a turn, then detonate.

Heartless Hidetsugu

taps to deal every player half their life, rounded down. With Torbran and ONE doubler out, that kills every opponent at once — see the combos.

Arcbond

choose a creature; whenever it's dealt damage this turn, it deals that much to every other creature and every player. Cast it on Brash Taunter before an Earthquake and the Earthquake hits everything twice, with an extra hit on one opponent from Taunter.

Engines — every creature, spell or card drawn hits them

Impact Tremors

every creature you play deals 1 to each opponent, which is 3 with Torbran.

Purphoros, God of the Forge

2 to each opponent for every other creature you play, so 4. With Impact Tremors too, one creature is 7 to every opponent.

Terror of the Peaks

every other creature you play deals its power to any target.

Warstorm Surge

the same effect on an enchantment, which is harder to remove. The creature itself deals the damage, so only red creatures get Torbran's 2.

Goblin Chainwhirler

when it enters, 3 to each opponent and to EVERY creature and planeswalker they control. It wipes out most boards of small creatures.

Guttersnipe

every instant and sorcery you cast is 4 to each opponent, and this deck casts a lot of sorceries.

Razorkin Needlehead

3 to an opponent for every card they draw, including their normal draw each turn.

Goblin Bombardment

sacrifice a creature to deal 3 to an opponent or anything they control. It turns a chump blocker into burn and a combo into a kill.

The combos — this is why the deck is Bracket 3.5

Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

copy Zealous Conscripts; the copy untaps Kiki-Jiki; repeat. That makes unlimited hasty 3/3s, and each one hits for 5 with Torbran.

Zealous Conscripts

the other half of that loop, and a steal effect on its own.

Dualcaster Mage

cast Twinflame or Heat Shimmer on any creature you control in your main phase, then flash Dualcaster in with the spell still on the stack. Dualcaster copies the spell onto ITSELF, the hasty token copies it again, and so on — unlimited hasty 2/2s.

Twinflame

the two-mana sorcery Dualcaster copies.

Heat Shimmer

the backup for Twinflame.

Either loop gives you an army of hasty attackers, and it wins on the spot with any engine out: Impact Tremors is 3 per creature to every opponent, and Goblin Bombardment turns each one into 3 more.

Heartless Hidetsugu with Torbran and one doubler out — Twinflame Tyrant, Solphim or Fiendish Duo — kills every opponent at once: half their life doubled is at least their life minus one, and Torbran's 2 finishes it. You only lose half of yours. Hidetsugu has to tap, so give it haste with Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots or Hall of the Bandit Lord. The one exception: an opponent at exactly 1 life takes nothing, because half of 1 rounds down to 0.

Razorkin Needlehead with Wheel of Fortune makes every opponent draw seven cards: 21 damage each with Torbran out, 28 with Mechanized Warfare too.

Surviving your own deck

The One Ring

CAST it and you have protection from everything until your next turn, so your own Earthquake, Flame Rift and Themberchaud can't hurt you. Plan your biggest turn around it. The price is the life you lose each upkeep to its burden counters.

Brash Taunter

indestructible, and every point of damage it takes also hits an opponent, plus Torbran's 2. Your own sweepers become extra burn instead of a cost.

Your real protection is the maths. Every symmetric hit costs you X and each opponent X+2, and it costs every opponent at once. Nobody else at the table is hitting all three of their opponents every turn.

Mana

Sol Ring

turn-one acceleration toward a turn-three Torbran.

Arcane Signet

in mono-red it's just a red mana rock, and that's all you need.

Fellwar Stone

a two-mana rock. It makes whatever colours your opponents' lands can make, which still pays the generic part of every cost.

Mind Stone

ramp early, a card later.

Ruby Medallion

every red spell costs one less, which is nearly the whole deck.

Seething Song

five red for three mana, which makes a huge Earthquake.

Jeska's Will

with your commander out, you get both halves: a red mana for each card in an opponent's hand, and the top three cards of your library to play.

Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

a red mana for every spell you cast, and it doesn't empty between phases.

Neheb, the Eternal

in your postcombat main phase, a red mana for every point of life your opponents lost this turn. After a Flame Rift, that's eighteen mana for Pyrohemia.

Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx

devotion adds up fast in mono-red, and Torbran alone counts for three.

Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle

3 damage to any target for every Mountain you play once you control five others. Torbran doesn't boost it; Twinflame Tyrant, Thor and Fated Firepower do.

Castle Embereth

+1/+0 to your whole team for the combo attack.

Hall of the Bandit Lord

haste for Heartless Hidetsugu, or for Brash Taunter's fight, the turn it lands.

Sokenzan, Crucible of Defiance

channel it for two hasty 1/1s, which means two Impact Tremors triggers.

War Room

a card for one life and three mana, every turn.

Finding the pieces

Gamble

any card. The random discard hurts less in a deck with this many copies of each effect.

Imperial Recruiter

any creature with power 2 or less: Kiki-Jiki, Dualcaster Mage, Brash Taunter, Razorkin Needlehead or Guttersnipe.

Goblin Matron

any Goblin: Kiki-Jiki, Brash Taunter, Goblin Chainwhirler or Guttersnipe.

Cards

Wheel of Fortune

a fresh seven cards for everyone. With Razorkin Needlehead out, it's a finisher.

Wheel of Misfortune

another hand refill, this one with a mind game over how much damage it deals.

Chandra, Torch of Defiance

every turn, a card off the top or 2 damage to each opponent (4 with Torbran).

Light Up the Stage

two cards for {R} once an opponent has lost life this turn, which is almost always.

Faithless Looting

filtering, twice.

Valakut Awakening // Valakut Stoneforge

a land when you need one, or a new hand when you don't.

Answers

Lightning Bolt

5 to an opponent's creature or face with Torbran.

Abrade

3 damage or an artifact destroyed.

Fiery Confluence

choose three modes, repeats allowed. Pick "2 damage to each opponent" three times and each opponent takes 12 with Torbran.

Blasphemous Act

usually costs one mana, and it kills every creature. With Brash Taunter out, 13 goes at an opponent, or 15 with Torbran.

Chaos Warp

the answer to an enchantment or planeswalker.

Vandalblast

overloaded, it's a one-sided artifact wipe.

Pyroblast

one mana against the blue deck holding up a counterspell.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a cheap engine and mana. Impact Tremors or Razorkin Needlehead on turn two, Guttersnipe on turn three, then Torbran is the start that wins.

Deploy engines before sweepers. An Earthquake with nothing else out is fair. The same Earthquake after Impact Tremors, Guttersnipe and Torbran is a massacre.

Watch your own life total. You take every symmetric hit too. Cast The One Ring before your biggest turn, and don't let Descent into Avernus and Sulfuric Vortex drain you while you have nothing on the table to finish the game.

Hold each combo piece until you can win with it. Kiki-Jiki and Dualcaster Mage are just creatures until their partner lands, and they're the first thing an experienced table will kill.
