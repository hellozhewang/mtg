The single biggest rule a new pilot gets wrong is how Optimus Prime converts upon death. When Optimus Prime, Hero dies, his triggered ability returns him to the battlefield converted as Optimus Prime, Autobot Leader (the Vehicle). On opponents' turns, Living Metal is not active, meaning he is a noncreature artifact Vehicle—completely immune to creature board wipes like Supreme Verdict and Blasphemous Act. When your turn begins, Living Metal awakens him into a 6/8 Trample attacker that bolsters your board and converts straight back into Hero mode after combat.

The commander engine and Autobots team

The compact finish
+
Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista

Infinite damage combo. Cast Heliod and Walking Ballista, pay {1}{W} with Heliod to grant Walking Ballista lifelink. Remove a counter from Ballista to deal 1 damage to an opponent; lifelink triggers Heliod to put a +1/+1 counter back onto Ballista. Repeat to machine-gun the entire table.

In accordance with Bracket 3 rules, this two-card combo is reserved for turn 6 or later. Spend early turns establishing your Autobots, bolstering counters, and controlling the board, then assemble the combo to close out the game cleanly.

Opening hand and sequencing

Prioritize hands with three lands and cheap artifact ramp or cost reducers (Sol Ring, Talismans, Etherium Sculptor, Esper Sentinel) to deploy Optimus Prime on turn 3 or 4.

Cast Optimus Prime converted for {2}{U}{R}{W} using More Than Meets the Eye. Immediately begin distributing bolster counters onto your utility creatures (Esper Sentinel, Danny Pink, Steel Overseer) on each player's end step.

Use turns 4 and 5 to deploy value engines like Simulacrum Synthesizer, Inspirit, or Danny Pink while attacking with Optimus to trigger bolster 2 and convert him back to Hero mode.

From turn 6 onward, either alpha-strike through unblockable/flying effects with Herald of Secret Streams or Abzan Falconer, or tutor Heliod and Walking Ballista to finish off the table in one decisive turn.
