Sheoldred does both halves of a sacrifice deck at once: she reanimates a creature for free on your upkeep, and she makes every opponent sacrifice one on theirs. So the deck is built out of creatures that WANT to die — Kokusho, Junji, Archon of Cruelty — and outlets to kill them on your own terms. Bring one back every turn, cash it in for value, repeat.

The commander
Sheoldred, Whispering One

6/6 with swampwalk, a free reanimation every one of your upkeeps, and a forced sacrifice on every opponent's upkeep. Seven mana, and she is worth every one of them.

Lightning Greaves

shroud, because she is the single most removal-magnetic card in the deck.

Swiftfoot Boots

hexproof and haste for when you want to attack immediately.

Kaya's Ghostform

one mana that brings her back through destruction OR exile.

Imp's Mischief

redirects the removal spell aimed at her.

Creatures that pay you for dying
Kokusho, the Evening Star

five life from each opponent and fifteen back to you, every time it dies. Reanimate it on upkeep, sacrifice it, repeat — that is fifteen damage a turn cycle in a four-player pod.

Junji, the Midnight Sky

two cards discarded and two life from each opponent, or it reanimates something else on its way out.

Archon of Cruelty

a sacrifice, a discard and three life from an opponent when it ENTERS, so Sheoldred's upkeep trigger alone is a beating, and it triggers again on every attack.

Grave Titan

six power and four Zombies across entry and attack, all of them sacrifice fodder.

Massacre Wurm

a one-sided wrath on entry, and every creature they lose costs two life.

Gray Merchant of Asphodel

your devotion is high; reanimating it is six or more damage to each opponent.

Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel

draws off every sacrifice, drains on every creature death, and flips into an emblem that keeps draining for the rest of the game.

Sacrifice outlets
Ashnod's Altar

free, repeatable, and it makes mana. This is what turns Sheoldred's reanimation into a loop.

Deadly Dispute

two cards and a Treasure for a creature you were going to lose anyway.

Skullclamp

on a 2/2 Zombie, two cards.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

every end step they give up a permanent or you draw and they lose two.

Gate to Phyrexia

repeatable artifact removal that costs only a body.

Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel

a free sacrifice on entry and every attack, each one a card.

Punishing the deaths
Grave Pact

every creature you lose makes each opponent sacrifice one. Alongside Sheoldred's upkeep edict, the table simply cannot keep a board.

Syr Konrad, the Grim

damage to each opponent whenever a creature dies anywhere, whenever a creature card is milled or discarded, AND whenever a creature card leaves your graveyard — so Sheoldred's reanimation trigger itself pings the table every upkeep.

Morbid Opportunist

a card every turn creatures die.

Accursed Marauder

everyone sacrifices a nontoken creature. You have fodder; they usually do not.

Plaguecrafter

the same, and it catches planeswalkers.

Emeritus of Woe // Demonic Tutor

a 5/4 that also carries a Demonic Tutor, and it re-prepares whenever two creatures die in a turn.

Living Death

Phyrexian Reclamation

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a graveyard enabler and acceleration. Entomb plus Reanimate is a turn-two Archon of Cruelty, which is faster than casting Sheoldred and better than waiting for her.

Sheoldred is a seven-drop. Crypt Ghast, Sol Ring and Jet Medallion are what make her a turn-five play. Do not keep a hand that only works if you draw four more lands.

Sacrifice on your upkeep, not your main phase. Sheoldred's trigger reanimates first — so put Kokusho back, then sacrifice it during your turn, then do it again next upkeep. Killing it before the trigger wastes a whole turn of value.

Grave Pact

with Sheoldred out this is the lock. Their upkeep edict plus your sacrifice outlet means every opponent loses two creatures per turn cycle and cannot rebuild.
