Aesi gives you an extra land drop every turn and draws a card every time a land enters under your control. So the deck plays two, three or four lands a turn, draws that many cards, and turns land drops into Zombies, Beasts, Plants and stolen creatures. It wins by burying the table in landfall triggers, then a Craterhoof Behemoth or a Field of the Dead army.

The commander
Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait

six mana, a 5/5. You may play an additional land each turn. Landfall: whenever a land you control enters, you may draw a card.

Every land counts: land drops, fetched lands, lands from ramp spells, and lands returned and replayed. Ramp spells that put lands onto the battlefield are card draw here.

Aesi costs six. Ramp hard in the first three turns so she lands on turn four with protection up.

Key pairs
+
Scapeshift + Field of the Dead

sacrifice your lands and fetch that many, Field included. They all enter at once, and with seven differently named lands each one makes a 2/2 Zombie, plus a card each from Aesi.

+
Scapeshift + Avenger of Zendikar

every land that enters puts a +1/+1 counter on each Plant. Six lands is six counters on every Plant.

+
Ashaya, Soul of the Wild + Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait

your nontoken creatures become Forest lands, so every creature you cast is a landfall trigger and a card.

+
Oboro, Palace in the Clouds + Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait

pay {1} to return Oboro to your hand, then replay it with your extra land drop for another landfall.

+
Trade Routes + Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait

{1}: return a land to hand to replay it; {1} and discard an extra land: draw.

+
Kodama of the East Tree + Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait

whenever another permanent you control enters, put a permanent with equal or lower mana value from your hand onto the battlefield. Lands have mana value 0, so every permanent you play drops a free land.

++
Kodama of the East Tree + Simic Growth Chamber + Scute Swarm

play the Growth Chamber and return it to hand with its own trigger; every Insect the Swarm makes is a permanent entering, so Kodama puts the Chamber back onto the battlefield, which is another landfall, another Insect and another bounce. Unlimited lands entering, Insects and Aesi draws. Rampaging Baloths, Field of the Dead or Springheart Nantuko work in place of the Swarm. A three-card loop, so it fits Bracket 3.

++
Kodama of the East Tree + Oboro, Palace in the Clouds + Tireless Provisioner

each landfall makes a Treasure that pays Oboro's {1} to return it, and each Treasure entering lets Kodama put Oboro back. Unlimited landfall.

+
Cultivator Colossus + Scute Swarm

the Colossus puts every land from your hand onto the battlefield and draws for each; with Scute Swarm out and six lands, each one also copies the Swarm.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when
→
Green Sun's Zenith → Lotus Cobra

X = 2 early for mana; X = 7 later for Avenger of Zendikar.

→
Finale of Devastation → Craterhoof Behemoth

X = 8 puts Hoof onto the battlefield with haste: every creature gets +X/+X and trample. X of 10 or more pumps the team again.

→
Wood Elves → Breeding Pool

Forest-type duals arrive untapped, so this is ramp AND a dual.

→
Three Visits → Tropical Island

the same, for two mana.

→
Scapeshift → Field of the Dead

with seven lands out and seven names among them, Scapeshift for Field and six others: six or seven Zombies on the spot.

Finishing
Craterhoof Behemoth

haste, trample, +X/+X where X is the number of creatures you control. After a turn of Zombies, Beasts, Plants and Insects, that is lethal.

Avenger of Zendikar

the board that Craterhoof wants.

Field of the Dead

an army that grows with every land you play, even from the graveyard.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep three or four lands, one two-mana ramp spell, and either an extra-land-drop card or a mana creature. Lands are your best cards, so a five-land hand is fine.

Turn three Exploration or Azusa, turn four Aesi, and from then on two or three lands a turn means two or three cards.

Save Scapeshift until Field of the Dead or Avenger can make it a game-winning turn.
