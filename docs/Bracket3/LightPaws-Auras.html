Every Aura you CAST pulls a second one out of your library for free. Light-Paws searches for an Aura with mana value no greater than the one you cast and a name you don't already have, and puts it straight onto her. So a two-mana Aura is really two Auras, and a four-mana one can fetch the best four-drop in the deck. Stack protection first, then damage, then swing with a commander nobody can touch.

The commander
Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice

two mana. Whenever an Aura you control enters, if you CAST it, search for an Aura with mana value less than or equal to it and a different name from every Aura you control, and put it onto the battlefield attached to Light-Paws. The fetched Aura was not cast, so it does not trigger her again: one cast, one fetch.

The cast Aura can go anywhere. Cast Reprobation on an opponent's creature and the fetch still lands on Light-Paws. But the FETCHED Aura always goes on her, so never fetch Reprobation, Darksteel Mutation or Swift Reconfiguration: they would shut her off. On Thin Ice cannot be fetched at all; it can only enchant a snow land.

Benevolent Blessing and Flickering Ward give protection from a colour you choose. Never choose white: protection from white stops your own new Auras attaching to her.

Aura chains — what to cast, and what it fetches

Cast the most expensive Aura you can, because it sets the ceiling on the fetch.

→
Angelic Destiny → Sage's Reverie

four into four. Destiny makes her a 6/6 flying first-striker, then Reverie draws a card for every Aura on a creature and pumps her again. With three or more Auras out, this is the best card-draw turn in the deck.

→
Sage's Reverie → Angelic Destiny

the same pair the other way round, when Reverie is the one in hand.

→
Battle Mastery → Chains of Custody

double strike now, and the fetched Chains exiles an opponent's best nonland permanent until it leaves. Removal without spending a card.

→
Chains of Custody → Battle Mastery

cast Chains for the removal, fetch double strike onto her.

→
Twinblade Blessing → Timely Ward

Twinblade has flash, so cast it in their end step or mid-combat, and fetch indestructible in response to nothing at all.

→
Mantle of the Ancients → Angelic Destiny

Mantle returns every Aura and Equipment from your graveyard onto her, then fetches a four-drop. The rebuild after a wipe.

→
Reprobation → Ethereal Armor

cast the removal Aura on THEIR creature; the fetch still goes on Light-Paws.

→
Ethereal Armor → Hyena Umbra

the turn-two chain. First strike, plus umbra armor so the first removal spell misses.

Making her lethal
Battle Mastery

double strike. Every pump after this counts twice.

Twinblade Blessing

double strike with flash: the surprise version.

Ethereal Armor

+1/+1 for each enchantment you control, and first strike. With six Auras out it is +6/+6 on its own.

All That Glitters

+1/+1 for each artifact and enchantment you control.

Daybreak Coronet

needs another Aura already on her: +3/+3, first strike, vigilance and lifelink.

Idolized

when she attacks alone, +X/+X where X is your nonland permanents.

Angelic Destiny

+4/+4, flying and first strike, and it returns to your hand if she dies.

Kor Spiritdancer

the backup body: +2/+2 per Aura on IT, and a card every time you cast an Aura.

++
Stoneskin + Solid Footing + Triclopean Sight

Stoneskin gives +0/+10, Triclopean Sight gives vigilance, and Solid Footing makes a vigilant creature deal combat damage equal to its TOUGHNESS. With all three she is a 4/14 that hits for 14, or 28 with double strike.

Rogue's Passage

{4}, {T}: she can't be blocked this turn.

Skrelv, Defector Mite

gives her toxic 1 and makes her unblockable by a chosen colour; each hit is also poison.

Pariah

Removal that is also an Aura

Every one of these is a cast Aura, so each also fetches something onto Light-Paws.

Chains of Custody

exiles a nonland permanent until Chains leaves, and gives her ward {2}.

Sheltered by Ghosts

the same exile, plus lifelink and ward {2}.

Reprobation

their creature becomes a 0/1 with no abilities.

Darksteel Mutation

their creature becomes a 0/1 indestructible artifact with no abilities: good against creatures that come back.

Swift Reconfiguration

turns a creature into a Vehicle that needs crew 5. Flash.

On Thin Ice

exiles a creature. It enchants one of your snow lands, which is why the basics are Snow-Covered Plains.

Swords to Plowshares

one mana, exile a creature.

Path to Exile

the same, for a basic land.

Generous Gift

destroys any permanent, for a 3/3 Elephant.

Land Tax

Pearl Medallion

white spells cost {1} less.

the usual.

the usual.

Locking the table
Grand Abolisher

on your turn nobody can respond to the attack or the Aura chain.

Silence

the one-turn version, cast before the big chain.

Archon of Emeria

one spell per turn for everyone. Light-Paws is the least affected: one Aura a turn is still two.

Aven Mindcensor

their searches only see the top four cards.

Hushbringer

stops creature enter and death triggers. Your Auras' triggers are not creatures, so they still work.

Getting it back
Replenish

every enchantment in your graveyard returns at once. Auras that can't attach to anything stay behind.

Retether

every Aura in your graveyard returns attached to creatures.

Mantle of the Ancients

returns Auras and Equipment onto the creature it enchants.

Hall of Heliod's Generosity

puts an enchantment from your graveyard on top of your library.

Gryff's Boon

comes back from the graveyard for {3}{W}.

Sentinel's Eyes

escapes from the graveyard for {W}.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when
→
Open the Armory → Battle Mastery

any Aura to hand. Take Battle Mastery when she is already protected, a protection Aura when she is not.

→
Enlightened Tutor → Angelic Destiny

puts any enchantment on top of your library. Take the biggest Aura you can cast next turn, because it fetches the biggest second Aura.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep Light-Paws mana, one cheap Aura and one way to protect her. A hand of four-mana Auras with no early play is a mulligan.

Turn two Light-Paws, turn three cast a one-mana Aura to fetch Hyena Umbra or Shardmage's Rescue, then a damage Aura. Protection first, then damage.

Before the big chain, play Grand Abolisher or Silence so nobody can respond.

If she dies, Angelic Destiny comes back to your hand and Mantle of the Ancients or Retether rebuild the stack in one card.
