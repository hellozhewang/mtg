GOD GOD tier cEDH. Wins or locks on turns 1-4 through interaction: full fast mana, free counterspells, deep tutors and compact wins.
SS SS tier Fringe cEDH with uncapped Game Changers. Wins or locks on turns 3-6, with fast mana and many tutors aimed at redundant game-ending lines.
S S tier The ceiling of a three-Game-Changer deck. Several compact wins, enough tutors to find them, and a kill on turns 5-7.
A A tier Strong. A combo deck with less redundancy, or a top engine deck that reliably kills on turns 6-8. The table's archenemy.
B B tier Solid Bracket 3. A coherent plan with enough ramp, draw and answers; wins on turns 7-9 without combos.
C C tier Fair. Works, but slow, commander-dependent or light on interaction; wins on turn 9 or later.
D D tier Weak. Theme first or narrowly aimed; easily disrupted and struggles against a table of B decks.
