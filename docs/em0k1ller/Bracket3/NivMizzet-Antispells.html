Niv-Mizzet draws you a card whenever ANY player casts an instant or sorcery, and pings for one whenever you draw. In a four-player pod that means the rest of the table is running your engine for you, and every counterspell you hold up is a cantrip that also deals damage. The deck is built to sit behind open mana, answer whatever matters, and convert the table's own spells into a clock.

The commander
Niv-Mizzet, Parun

six mana, three blue and three red, uncounterable, and a 5/5 flier. Every instant or sorcery cast by anybody draws you a card; every card you draw deals one damage anywhere.

Harmonic Prodigy

Niv-Mizzet is a Dragon WIZARD, so both of his triggers double. Every instant or sorcery anyone casts draws you two, and each of those draws pings twice. Two mana for four times the output.

Ghyrson Starn, Kelermorph

whenever another source you control deals EXACTLY one damage, Ghyrson deals two more. Niv's ping is exactly one, so every draw becomes three damage. With Harmonic Prodigy also out, a single opposing cantrip deals twelve.

Basilisk Collar

deathtouch and lifelink on Niv. Now every one-damage ping kills a creature and gains you life. It turns the engine into a board wipe.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud, because a six-mana commander that eats a removal spell is a lost game.

Slip Out the Back

one mana to phase him out of removal, and it leaves a counter behind.

Everything you hold up is also a draw
Counterspell

the clean two-mana answer, and it draws you a card off your own cast.

Mana Drain

counter, and the mana casts Niv next turn.

Mana Sculpt

the same idea with a Wizard on the battlefield, which you almost always have.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

Fierce Guardianship

free with your commander out.

Pact of Negation

free now; the upkeep payment is easy in a deck holding this much mana.

Flusterstorm

nearly unbeatable in a counter war, and each copy is another Niv trigger.

Dispel

one mana that answers the removal spell aimed at Niv.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for any noncreature spell.

Mystical Dispute

one mana against blue decks.

Pyroblast

one mana to counter or destroy anything blue. The colour hosers are maindeck here on purpose — a four-player pod nearly always has a blue or red deck in it.

Red Elemental Blast

the second copy.

Hydroblast

the mirror, against red decks.

Blue Elemental Blast

and its partner.

Aether Gust

sends a red or green spell or permanent to the top or bottom of their library.

Turning spells into damage
Guttersnipe

two damage to each opponent for every instant or sorcery YOU cast. On a turn where you counter two things that is twelve damage across the table.

Gandalf, Goblins' Bane // Flameshape

one damage to each opponent per noncreature spell — and one damage is exactly what Ghyrson Starn wants, so it becomes three to each opponent instead.

Archmage Emeritus

a card for every instant or sorcery you cast or copy, which is another Niv ping on top of the Niv draw.

Lightning Bolt

three damage for one mana, or a removal spell.

Mizzium Mortars

kills a creature, or overloaded, kills their whole board.

Desolation of Smaug

three damage to every non-Dragon creature — Niv survives — and it refunds four mana toward a Dragon.

Card flow
Rhystic Study

in a deck already holding up mana, they rarely pay.

Mystic Remora

the best card in the deck on turns one through four.

Brainstorm

three draws with Niv out, so three pings.

Impulse

dig four, and it is an instant so it triggers Niv on your own turn.

Frantic Search

free, two draws, and it untaps three lands to keep interaction up.

Fact or Fiction

at instant speed, and every card drawn is a ping.

Memory Deluge

scales with your mana, and flashback makes it twice.

Faithless Looting

cheap filtering, twice.

Big Score

two cards and two Treasures at instant speed.

Goblin Electromancer

every instant and sorcery costs one less, which is how you double-spell on their turn.

Mana
Arcane Signet

the double-blue and double-red pips on Niv are the real cost of the deck.

Izzet Signet

fixes both halves.

Chromatic Lantern

makes every land produce whatever pip you are short.

Coldsteel Heart

cheap and colour-fixing.

Fellwar Stone

usually taps for exactly what you need in a multicolour pod.

Firemind Vessel

two different colours a turn.

High Tide

on a heavy-Island board this is a genuine explosive turn, and it costs one mana so it also triggers Niv.

Removal and resets
Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, it is the clear for a lethal Niv attack or the answer to a locked board.

Echoing Truth

bounces a permanent AND every copy of it — the answer to a token swarm.

Aetherize

returns every attacking creature to hand. In a pod that folds an entire alpha strike for four mana.

Chaos Warp

the only answer to an enchantment in these colours.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it redirects the removal spell aimed at Niv.

Charisma

on a deathtouch or pinging creature, it steals whatever it damages.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep fixing. Three blue and three red pips is a genuinely hard cost — Signets, Coldsteel Heart and Chromatic Lantern are what make Niv a turn-five or six play instead of a turn-eight one.

Play at instant speed. Every spell you cast on somebody else's turn is a Niv trigger you would have got anyway plus a card, and holding up two mana is almost always better than tapping out.

Ghyrson Starn, Kelermorph

three mana, and it triples every ping in the deck. Find it before you find another counterspell.

Basilisk Collar

the moment it is on Niv, the table's creatures stop mattering. Prioritise it against creature decks over any additional card draw.
