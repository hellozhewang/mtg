Shift counters strategically at combat with Tidus, trigger Cheer to draw cards and proliferate on damage, leverage Danny Pink for mass card draw, and overrun opponents with unblockable, counter-boosted creatures.

Counter relocation and Cheer proliferation
Tidus, Yuna's Guardian

at the beginning of combat on your turn, you may move a counter from target creature you control onto a second target creature you control. His Cheer ability dictates that whenever one or more creatures you control with counters on them deal combat damage to a player, you may draw a card and proliferate. Note that Tidus can move ANY counter type, including shield counters, indestructible counters from Arwen, Mortal Queen, or +1/+1 counters.

Danny Pink

whenever a counter is put on a creature you control, if it didn't have a counter put on it yet this turn, you draw a card. Tidus moving a counter onto a creature puts a counter on it, immediately triggering Danny Pink. Proliferating subsequently places counters on multiple creatures, drawing fresh cards for each creature modified!

Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus

whenever you proliferate, proliferate an additional time instead, doubling Tidus's Cheer payoff.

Inexorable Tide

proliferates whenever you cast any spell.

Evolution Sage

proliferates on every land entry under your control.

Sword of Truth and Justice

grants protection from white and blue, puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature on damage, and proliferates.

Evasion and counter-scaling payoffs
Herald of Secret Streams

creatures you control with +1/+1 counters on them cannot be blocked, guaranteeing that your entire army connects to trigger Cheer and combat damage effects.

Kodama of the West Tree

modified creatures you control have trample; whenever a modified creature deals combat damage to a player, search a basic land onto the battlefield tapped.

Kalonian Hydra

enters with four +1/+1 counters, trample, and doubles the number of +1/+1 counters on each creature you control whenever it attacks.

Sovereign Okinec Ahau

whenever you attack, each creature you control with power greater than its base power gets a number of +1/+1 counters equal to the difference, exponentially swelling your team.

Crystalline Crawler

enters with +1/+1 counters, gains counters on spell casts, and removes counters to add mana of any color.

Walking Ballista

scales with counters and can machine-gun opposing utility creatures or direct damage to players.

Asymmetric board wipes and counter protection
Damning Verdict

five-mana sorcery that destroys all creatures without counters on them, wiping opposing battlefields while leaving your entire modified board completely intact.

Wave Goodbye

returns each creature without a +1/+1 counter on it to its owner's hand, functioning as a one-sided board bounce.

The Ozolith

preserves all counters from creatures that leave the battlefield and transfers them onto target creature at beginning of combat.

Resourceful Defense

catches counters from permanents leaving play and lets you pay {4}{W} to move counters between any permanents at instant speed.

Arwen, Mortal Queen

enters with an indestructible counter and gives another creature an indestructible counter when it attacks.

Interaction, tutors, and opening hands
Cyclonic Rift

premier blue reset to eliminate opposing tokens and blockers.

Rhystic Study

card advantage engine taxing opponents' spellcasting.

Teferi's Protection

phases out your board in response to mass exile or global destruction.

Heroic Intervention

grants hexproof and indestructible to your permanents.

Swords to Plowshares

premier one-mana creature exile.

Keep opening hands with two to three lands, early counter producers or dorks (Birds of Paradise, Bristly Bill, Hardened Scales), and a card draw payoff (Danny Pink, Fathom Mage). Deploy Tidus on turn three, move a counter onto an unblocked attacker to guarantee Cheer combat damage, and proliferate to continuously grow your army and hand size. Deliver an unblockable alpha strike on Turn 6 or later.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, counter accumulation, and combat triggers.
