The draw engine and the threats are the same cards. Every enchantment you cast draws you one card off Sythis, another off each enchantress effect on the battlefield, and gains life on the way. With three of them out a two-mana Aura is four cards, and the deck simply never runs out. Note the land count is deliberately low — the mana comes from Auras on lands and from Sanctum Weaver, not from drawing more Forests.

The commander and the enchantress stack
Sythis, Harvest's Hand

two mana, and every enchantment spell is a card and a life. She is cheap enough to recast through removal all game.

Argothian Enchantress

the best of the redundant copies, because SHROUD means she cannot be answered by targeted removal at all.

Mesa Enchantress

another card per enchantment.

Satyr Enchanter

and another.

Enchantress's Presence

the same effect on an enchantment, so it triggers everything else when you cast it.

Eidolon of Blossoms

constellation, so it draws off enchantments that ENTER rather than are cast — which catches Replenish and every token-generated Aura.

Setessan Champion

draws and grows on every enchantment that enters.

Kor Spiritdancer

draws on Aura spells specifically, and gets +2/+2 per Aura attached to her.

Femeref Enchantress

a card whenever an enchantment hits a graveyard, which turns their removal into your card advantage.

Mana out of enchantments
Sanctum Weaver

taps for one mana per enchantment you control. On a developed board that is six or eight mana from a two-drop.

Fertile Ground

an extra mana of any colour off a land, and it is an enchantment so it draws.

Overgrowth

two extra green off one land.

Abundant Growth

fixes and replaces itself immediately.

Nylea's Presence

makes a land every basic type, and draws.

Herald of the Pantheon

enchantments cost one less and gain you a life each.

Danitha Capashen, Paragon

Auras cost one less, on a lifelinking first striker.

Land Tax

this deck is deliberately land-light, so an opponent almost always has more. Three basics a turn is free fuel.

Mirri's Guile

sets the top three every upkeep, which with this much draw means you never miss.

Protecting the board
Greater Auramancy

your other enchantments have SHROUD, and enchanted creatures have shroud too. It is the single most important protection card in the deck.

Privileged Position

hexproof for every other permanent you control.

Karmic Justice

if they destroy a noncreature permanent of yours, you destroy any permanent of theirs. It stops targeted enchantment removal before it starts.

Destiny Spinner

your creature and enchantment spells cannot be countered, and it animates a land as an X/X where X is your enchantment count.

Lightning Greaves

shroud and haste for whichever creature is carrying the Auras.

Replenish

returns EVERY enchantment from your graveyard to the battlefield. It is the answer to a wrath, and in this deck it usually returns six or more cards at once.

The Voltron kill
Ancestral Mask

+2/+2 for each other enchantment ON THE BATTLEFIELD — including your opponents'. It routinely reads +20/+20.

Ethereal Armor

+1/+1 per enchantment you control, plus first strike.

All That Glitters

the same count including artifacts.

Daybreak Coronet

+3/+3, first strike, vigilance and lifelink for two mana, as long as another Aura is already there.

Kor Spiritdancer

the natural carrier — she grows on her own and draws every time you suit her up.

Akroma's Will

with your commander out, take both halves. Double strike on an Ancestral Mask creature ends the game; protection from every colour makes it unblockable and unkillable at the same time.

The token kill
Archon of Sun's Grace

a 2/2 flying lifelinking Pegasus for every enchantment that enters. This deck plays thirty of them.

Hallowed Haunting

a Spirit for every enchantment you CAST, each one as big as your Spirit count, and at seven enchantments they all fly and have vigilance.

Luminarch Ascension

with Ghostly Prison out you rarely lose life, so the counters accumulate for free and then it makes a 4/4 flier every turn.

Destiny Spinner

turns a land into a surprise attacker sized by your enchantment count.

Removal and defence
Ghostly Prison

they pay {2} per attacker or go hit somebody else. In a deck with no early blockers this buys the turns you need.

Grasp of Fate

exiles a nonland permanent from EACH opponent, on one enchantment that also draws you cards.

Aura of Silence

taxes their artifacts and enchantments two, then eats one.

Aura Shards

every creature you play destroys an artifact or enchantment. Alongside Archon of Sun's Grace making a Pegasus per enchantment, this is a removal engine.

Kenrith's Transformation

turns their commander into a vanilla 3/3 Elk and draws you a card.

Path to Exile

one mana, exile.

Beast Within

any permanent, at instant speed.

Generous Gift

the white copy.

Tutors
Idyllic Tutor

any enchantment in the deck — usually Greater Auramancy, Grasp of Fate or Hallowed Haunting.

Open the Armory

any Aura, which usually means Ancestral Mask.

Heliod's Pilgrim

the same on a body, and it triggers constellation effects when Auras come down later.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep an enchantress and a cheap enchantment. Sythis on turn two into a one- or two-mana Aura on turn three is how the engine starts; a hand of expensive payoffs with no draw engine stalls out.

Deploy the draw engines before the payoffs. An extra enchantress on the battlefield multiplies every card you cast for the rest of the game; an extra Aura does not.

Greater Auramancy

get it down before you commit an Ancestral Mask to a creature. Without it, one removal spell in response to the Aura two-for-ones you.

Argothian Enchantress

she has shroud, so she survives everything except a wrath. If you have a choice of which enchantress to play into open mana, play her.
