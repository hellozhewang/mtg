Keep ordinary utility creatures and other planeswalker cards out of this list. Jace turns expendable tokens into one of seven major creature payoffs because those are the only creature or planeswalker cards in the ninety-nine. Adding a cheap creature changes that reliability. His +1 can put an expensive creature from your hand back into the library, but putting it on the bottom does not guarantee that the next -3 finds that particular creature.

This Bracket 3 build uses tokens, transformation spells, and blink effects to deploy and reuse large threats. The Game Changers are Consecrated Sphinx, Rhystic Study, and Smothering Tithe. It has 37 lands plus two land-option spells, ten dedicated ramp cards, additional resource engines, and no planned infinite loop, extra-turn chain, or mass land denial. The nine-mana kicked copy finisher is a late-game route after developing resources, not an early compact combo plan.

Build something disposable before casting Jace
Jace, Multiverse Architect

Start at four loyalty and turn a creature or another planeswalker you control into a library threat with -3. Tokens work, including Jace planeswalker tokens. You exile the target rather than sacrifice it, so there is no death trigger. If the only target becomes illegal, the ability does not resolve. His combat tax is one payment of two per opposing beginning-of-combat trigger, not two per attacker, and protects Jaces rather than you. Paying avoids that trigger's restriction; it does not override a restriction already created by another trigger.

Bitterblossom

A two-mana recurring source of flying blockers and transformation targets. Spend one life per upkeep to keep feeding the commander without using additional cards. Save a Faerie for defense if your next transformed threat will not stop a counterattack.

Skrelv's Hive

Another cheap recurring source, but the Mites cannot block. They are fuel, not a defensive wall. This list does not build toward poison kills; use the bodies for Jace, creature-targeting transformation spells, or a later Mass Polymorph.

Legion's Landing // Adanto, the First Fort

One mana supplies a creature without placing a small creature card in your library. If three creatures can attack safely, the transformed land becomes mana and a recurring token source. Do not sacrifice an established position merely to force that transformation.

Lingering Souls

Two flying bodies now and two more through flashback support both defense and multiple transformations. It is especially useful after a sweep because one card can rebuild several targets over two casts. Its tokens count for Mass Polymorph, unlike planeswalker tokens.

Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki

The front face is an enchantment in your library, so it does not dilute Jace's hits. Its Goblin supplies a target or attack-generated Treasure, and the second chapter filters expensive cards. The transformed Reflection copies your nonlegendary threats with haste; all seven creature payoffs qualify. Reflection enters as a new permanent and normally must wait a turn before tapping.

Black Market Connections

A single enchantment can supply mana, cards, and a creature target. Taking all modes costs six life each first main phase, so choose only what the board needs. Its Shapeshifter can block, and its Treasure helps assemble four colors.

Secure the Wastes

Produce blockers or transformation targets at the preceding opponent's end step. It scales into a wide board when Jace is unavailable and supplies multiple bodies for Divergent Transformations or Mass Polymorph. Leave mana for protection when possible.

Grand Crescendo

At X=0, two white mana makes your current creatures indestructible. With more mana it supplies tokens as well. It does not make Jace indestructible, cannot save creatures from exile or sufficient toughness reduction, and competes with your other white spells for colored mana.

Kher Keep

A land-based creature-token source that remains useful after removal clears your enchantments. Creating a token needs two mana plus tapping the land itself. Its normal mana ability produces colorless, so avoid keeping a hand whose only fifth source cannot complete Jace's colors.

Use the new Jace tokens correctly

Empower adds loyalty to a Jace planeswalker TOKEN you control, creating one if necessary. It does not add counters to your nontoken commander. If a Jace token already exists, later empower effects add to it rather than automatically making another. The predefined token has surveil and draw abilities and is not legendary, so it can coexist with your commander. Do not spend its last loyalty counter if you intend to exile it with Jace's -3 afterward.

Way of the Pyromancer

Produces a Jace token and grants your planeswalkers a +1 ability that makes red mana. The token can use that ability immediately, then be transformed by the commander later. Activating this on the commander uses his normal loyalty activation for the turn unless Oath of Teferi applies. The granted ability uses the stack; it is not a mana ability despite producing mana.

Fatehold Charm

Drawing a card while empowering supplies both selection and a future Jace target. Its alternative mode returns a spell or creature to hand, giving the same slot a defensive use. Returning a spell bypasses uncounterability, but does not permanently remove the card.

Countersculpt

Counter a spell and empower Jace. It costs two blue when you can behold a Jace you control or reveal the appropriate card from hand; otherwise pay the additional generic mana. The commander sitting in the command zone does not satisfy behold. A freshly created one-loyalty token is useful transformation fuel, but using its -1 first would put it into the graveyard.

Jace's Machinations

Creates or strengthens a Jace token and permits Jace loyalty activations at instant speed on any player's turn for that turn. The commander can use -3 on an opponent's turn to produce a surprise threat, provided he has enough loyalty and has not already activated that turn. This does not grant unlimited activations or refill the commander's loyalty. A token can also use its own draw ability before becoming transformation fuel if it retains loyalty.

Theorist's Sanctum

A fetchable Island and repeatable empower source. It enters tapped unless you can behold a Jace; the command zone alone does not help. Its three-mana-plus-tap activation can rebuild a token after Jace exiles the previous one. It produces blue normally, unlike a colorless utility land.

Deploy threats even when the commander is removed
Polymorph

Convert a creature token into one of the seven payoffs. A legal indestructible target is not destroyed but you still perform the reveal instruction, so Grand Crescendo can sometimes preserve the target as well. Opposing removal can make your sole target illegal and stop the spell. It cannot target a planeswalker token.

Transmogrify

A second four-mana creature transformation, using exile rather than destruction. Prefer an expendable token; exiling one of your real creatures permanently reduces the threats available for later plays. It does not find planeswalkers, but there are none in the ninety-nine anyway.

Divergent Transformations

Costs four mana with three opponents and transforms two creatures at instant speed. You need two different legal targets when casting it. If one later becomes illegal, it can still resolve for the other. It becomes more expensive as opponents leave the game, and using an opposing creature as a target may give that player a much stronger replacement.

Proteus Staff

Repeatable transformation from an artifact that survives creature wipes. Activation is sorcery speed, costs three mana, and only targets a creature. A token put on the bottom disappears; a nontoken creature remains available somewhere in the library. Do not casually give an opponent a free threat with it.

Mass Polymorph

Convert a board of creature tokens into several premium creatures at once. All revealed creatures enter together, so Terror of the Peaks sees the other entering creatures and triggers for each. This EXILES every creature you already control, including existing bombs; cast it before committing those creatures or when trading them away is worth the result. Jace and his planeswalker tokens stay on the battlefield, and those tokens do not increase the number of creature cards found.

Every creature hit has a job
Archon of Cruelty

The main blink and copy target. Each entry or attack pressures one opponent's creature or planeswalker and hand while draining three, drawing you a card, and gaining you three life. The target opponent chooses what to sacrifice. Reflection of Kiki-Jiki, Cursed Mirror, and Ephemerate turn one Archon into repeated advantages.

Agent of Treachery

Steal the nonland permanent that most threatens your plan or best improves your board. The theft has no duration and remains after Agent leaves. Repeated entries can reach its three-stolen-permanent draw condition, but the deck does not use repeated land theft as a mana-denial plan. Most blink effects here return cards to their owners, so blinking a stolen creature usually gives it back.

Consecrated Sphinx

The card-advantage hit replenishes the mana, protection, and tokens needed to keep operating. Each opposing draw permits two of your own draws, and you may decline them. It does not protect itself immediately, so avoid exposing it without interaction when the table can remove it before drawing.

Terror of the Peaks

Converts the other six creature payoffs and any token copies into direct damage. A simultaneous Mass Polymorph entry with other threats works because Terror sees those other creatures enter. Its targeting surcharge is three life for opposing spells, not a ward trigger, and does not stop opposing abilities or untargeted removal.

Ancient Gold Dragon

One successful combat hit creates a random one-to-twenty flying tokens. They supply future transformations or a lethal air force; each also triggers Terror of the Peaks for one damage. The result is uncertain, so do not plan as if the die always rolls twenty.

Serra's Emissary

Usually choose creature to prevent creature damage to you and your creatures, and to make your creatures unblockable by creatures. This does NOT grant protection to Jace or other noncreature permanents. Choosing instant can shield your creatures from many removal spells but also stops your own instant spells from targeting them, including Ephemerate. Select the type for the actual threats on the table.

Archfiend of Despair

Prevents opponents gaining life and repeats the life each opponent lost that turn at every end step. Combat, Terror triggers, Archon drains, and even their own life payments contribute. It does not need to have been present when the earlier loss occurred, but must be present when the end step begins to trigger. This is life loss, not additional damage.

End the game with copies and a protected attack
Rite of Replication

Four mana buys a useful creature copy; nine mana with kicker buys five. On your unmodified Terror of the Peaks, the original sees five arrivals and each new Terror sees the other four. That creates 25 triggers of five damage, or 125 total to distribute among legal targets. This requires the original Terror, nine available mana, and a resolving Rite; removing its only target before resolution stops it. It is a late-game finisher, not an infinite loop.

Cursed Mirror

A three-mana mana rock can first enter as a hasty copy of any creature on the battlefield. Copy Archon for an entry trigger and a possible attack trigger, Agent for another theft, or a strong opposing threat. It returns to its own artifact characteristics at end of turn. While copying a creature without a mana ability, it cannot also tap for its printed red-mana ability.

Reflection of Kiki-Jiki can make a hasty Archon token for both entry and attack triggers. It can also copy Archfiend: if two Archfiends trigger at the same end step, the second calculates life lost after the first resolves, potentially quadrupling the turn's earlier loss. The temporary copy is sacrificed at the end step, but its already-triggered ability still resolves. This is finite and requires the existing creatures and setup.

For a combat finish, choose creature with Serra's Emissary, attack with your flying threats and tokens, then let Archfiend repeat the life loss. Protection from creatures stops blocks but not removal spells, sacrifice effects, or a player paying Jace's tax and attacking him. Preserve interaction for the actual finishing turn.

Reset loyalty and repeat enters abilities
Flicker

This two-mana sorcery can reset your commander because it targets a nontoken permanent. Use an activation, blink Jace back at four loyalty, then use an activation of the new object. It can also repeat Archon or Agent entries. It is not instant-speed protection and cannot target a token.

Teferi's Time Twist

Protects Jace or another permanent from a removal spell and returns it at the next end step. A returning Jace is a fresh object with starting loyalty, but normal loyalty timing means you usually wait until your next main phase to activate. Leave the commander in exile if you want the delayed return; moving it to the command zone prevents that return.

Semester's End

Protects any selected creatures and planeswalkers and returns the nontoken cards at the next end step with an extra relevant counter. Jace returns with five loyalty; your large creatures repeat their enters triggers. Tokens that leave cannot return, so avoid selecting disposable tokens unless losing them is acceptable.

Ephemerate

One mana protects a creature and repeats its entry, with rebound for another use next upkeep. It is excellent on Archon or Agent, but cannot target the planeswalker commander. Blinking a token makes it disappear, and returning a creature under its owner's control can give away a creature you stole.

Teleportation Circle

Reuses one artifact or creature at each of your end steps, usually Archon, Agent, or Cursed Mirror. It cannot blink Jace. Blinking Mirror lets it become a creature copy again and trigger appropriate entry abilities, even though its copy duration ends later that turn.

Oath of Teferi

Allows two loyalty activations per planeswalker per turn. With a fresh four-loyalty Jace, +1 followed by -3 ends at two loyalty while providing cards and a creature; it does not give enough loyalty to repeat that exact pattern forever without resets. Its entry blink returns the chosen permanent at the end step, so exiling Jace immediately removes him for the remainder of that main phase. Blink a spent rock or another permanent when you need Jace to stay present.

Keep your hand and top card useful
Rhystic Study

The early engine draws into lands, protection, and token sources while you develop. It is preferable to spending the opening turns on several expensive threats that you intend to cheat into play. Opponents can pay, so do not count on every spell producing a card.

Mystic Remora

Early protection against falling behind on cards while playing your mana and token setup. Pay cumulative upkeep only while the expected cards justify slowing your own development. Sacrificing it when the cost becomes too high is normal.

Brainstorm

Put a creature stranded in hand on top, then transform a token to find that exact creature while the top remains unchanged. A fetchland can instead shuffle away unwanted cards. Do not shuffle away your deliberately arranged threat before activating Jace.

Scroll Rack

Exchanges expensive creatures from hand for fresh cards while arranging the creatures on top. This turns a random Jace activation into a selected payoff when nobody disrupts the top of the library. It is selection rather than automatic card advantage and requires cards in hand to exchange.

Caretaker's Talent

Your creature tokens, Jace tokens, and Treasures can trigger a draw, but only once each turn. Smothering Tithe can enable draws on opponents' turns. Level two copies any token, including a creature copy made by Rite of Replication; level three turns the token army into a combat threat. Leveling uses mana that might otherwise protect Jace, so do it when the copy or attack matters.

Ponder

Finds an early land, token source, or interaction and can arrange a creature on top for transformation later. It draws a card after arranging the top three, so plan which card will actually remain on top after that draw. Shuffle when the whole selection is poor.

Fund four colors and retain interaction
Sol Ring

Accelerates generic costs but supplies none of the four colored symbols in Jace's cost. The five Talismans, Arcane Signet, and Fellwar Stone supply the early fixing. Check the actual colors available rather than assuming a Sol Ring hand automatically casts the commander early; Fellwar Stone depends on opponents' lands.

Smothering Tithe

Builds the mana to cast stranded threats and pay for a kicked Rite, while enabling Caretaker's Talent on opposing turns. Treasure tokens are not creatures or planeswalkers, so Jace cannot turn an ordinary Treasure directly into a threat. You still need the separate token-fodder package.

Swords to Plowshares

Efficient exile for a threat that would stop your setup or combat finish. Path to Exile is the second version, with the drawback of giving its controller a basic land. Preserve these for creatures your blockers and temporary bounce cannot handle.

Anguished Unmaking

Exiles any threatening opposing nonland permanent at instant speed. Generous Gift covers a broader permanent range through destruction but leaves a 3/3 creature behind. Reserve these broad answers for effects that disable your engine rather than spending them on incidental creatures.

Mana Drain

Counters any spell and supplies colorless mana at the next main phase. That mana helps hard-cast a threat or fund Rite's kicker but cannot replace Jace's colored requirements. Avoid tapping out merely because a future refund is expected.

Dovin's Veto

Stops a noncreature spell uncounterably, making it useful against wipes or decisive stack interaction. Swan Song provides a one-mana answer to an instant, sorcery, or enchantment, but its gifted flying Bird can attack Jace later.

Deflecting Swat

Can be free while the planeswalker commander is under your control. Use it to redirect targeted removal or a counterspell when there is another legal target. It cannot stop a nontargeting wipe or make an illegal target legal.

Lazotep Plating

Creates or grows an Army and gives you and your current permanents hexproof for the turn. That simultaneously protects Jace from targeted opposing effects and supplies a future creature target. Hexproof does not stop a nontargeting wipe or protect a spell still on the stack.

Toxic Deluge

Adjust the toughness reduction to clear a threatening board while leaving planeswalkers intact. It affects your creatures too and can kill indestructible creatures. Supreme Verdict is the uncounterable destroy-all-creatures alternative; both may remove the tokens you were planning to transform.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Graveyard interaction without adding a weak creature to the transformation pool. It exiles one card on entry and can later sacrifice to exile all opponents' graveyards. The draw ability is a fallback, not a reason to discard important graveyard coverage early.

Recover without relying on Jace
Reanimate

Recover a dead payoff, a creature discarded to Fable, or a useful creature in an opponent's graveyard. An eight-mana creature costs eight life to return. It cannot retrieve creatures exiled by Jace, Transmogrify, or Mass Polymorph, and it cannot return the planeswalker commander.

Emeria's Call // Emeria, Shattered Skyclave

Usually a land-option card early; later it supplies two 4/4 flying bodies and protects your non-Angel creatures until your next turn. It does not protect the Angel tokens it creates, Serra's Emissary, or Jace. Sink into Stupor is the other land-option card and provides spell or opposing nonland-permanent bounce when you have enough mana.

With Jace repeatedly removed, use the five transformation cards, cast creatures normally with your rocks and Treasures, or build a token attack with Caretaker's Talent. Consecrated Sphinx, Archon, Black Market Connections, and the enchantment draw engines keep this from being entirely commander-dependent. You still need actual creature tokens for the backup transformation cards; the empower tokens only work with Jace's own -3.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep three or four lands with a credible route to all four colors, a cheap token producer, and either a two-mana rock or early card access. A counterspell or removal spell improves the keep. Mulligan hands dominated by the seven expensive creatures with no Brainstorm, Scroll Rack, token plan, or mana development.

An example keep is Flooded Strand, Blood Crypt, Command Tower, Talisman of Progress, Bitterblossom, Swan Song, and Brainstorm. Fetch Tundra for white and blue; Blood Crypt supplies black and red. Sequence the Talisman and Bitterblossom over the next turns according to pressure. With a fourth land, the rock can cast Jace on turn four and an existing Faerie can become a threat immediately, though that line may leave no protection mana.

The lands prioritize fetches, original duals, shocks, and broad fixing. Raffine's Tower and Xander's Lounge are best fetched when you can absorb the tapped entry. The two basics give Island and Plains targets for effects that specifically demand a basic. Theorist's Sanctum is a utility Island, and Kher Keep is the only normally colorless land. Choose fetch targets to establish the four colors before chasing triple white for a naturally cast Serra's Emissary.

Before each -3, count loyalty and confirm the target remains expendable. A first activation takes Jace from four to one; without resets or other bonuses, two later +1 activations reach three, and spending all three then puts Jace into the graveyard before the ability resolves. You can move him to the command zone and the ability still resolves if its target remains legal. Do not mistake the tax or empower mechanic for protection against every answer.

Use +1 when you need cards or to return an expensive creature from hand to the library. Use Brainstorm or Scroll Rack when you need a particular creature immediately. Preserve a blink spell for either a loyalty reset or a critical removal response, and remember that delayed-return blink does not give an immediate extra activation.

The usual progression is token and mana development, one protected threat, then a second threat or repeatable entry engine. Look for a protected combat finish with Archfiend, a late kicked Rite, or enough token creatures to justify Mass Polymorph. Avoid exiling a board that is already winning simply to reveal more cards, and leave mana for the answer that could otherwise stop the finishing turn.
