Yawgmoth turns every creature you own into a card. Pay 1 life and sacrifice another creature: draw, and put a -1/-1 counter where it hurts. The deck is built out of creatures that come back when they die, so that loop never runs out of fodder, and out of drain effects that make every death cost the table life. Grind with it all game; when the right three cards line up, it loops until everyone else is dead. Every loop here takes three cards, so this stays a Bracket 3 deck.

The commander
Yawgmoth, Thran Physician

four mana, protection from Humans. Pay 1 life, sacrifice another creature: put a -1/-1 counter on up to one target creature and draw a card. {B}{B}, discard a card: proliferate.

The -1/-1 counter is how undying and persist creatures loop. A +1/+1 counter and a -1/-1 counter on the same creature cancel, so aiming Yawgmoth at a creature that came back with a +1/+1 counter resets it to come back again.

The draw is not optional. Count your library before a long loop: drain fast enough that the table dies before you deck yourself.

Proliferate adds -1/-1 counters to their creatures (with Nest of Scarabs, Insects for you too) and grows Hancock.

Infinite combos — three cards each
++
Yawgmoth, Thran Physician + Butcher Ghoul + Geralf's Messenger

sacrifice the Ghoul and put the counter on the Messenger; the Ghoul returns with a +1/+1 counter. Then sacrifice the Messenger and put the counter on the Ghoul, cancelling its counter; the Messenger returns and drains an opponent for 2. Repeat: 1 life and one card per step, 2 damage every other step. Any two undying creatures loop; the Messenger is the one that also kills.

++
Yawgmoth, Thran Physician + Butcher Ghoul + Sightless Ghoul

the same loop with no damage built in. Add Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat or Vengeful Bloodwitch and every step drains.

++
Forsaken Miner + Phyrexian Altar + Blood Artist

sacrifice the Miner for {B}. Blood Artist's trigger targets an opponent, which is a crime, so the Miner's trigger lets you pay that {B} to return it. Unlimited deaths and drains. Vengeful Bloodwitch or Susurian Voidborn work in place of Blood Artist, and Warren Soultrader in place of the Altar.

++
Nether Traitor + Phyrexian Altar + Pawn of Ulamog

sacrifice the Traitor for {B}; Pawn makes a Spawn. Sacrifice the Spawn for another mana, and the Traitor's trigger brings it back for {B}. One mana of profit and two deaths per loop. Add any drain card and it wins.

++
Yawgmoth, Thran Physician + Nest of Scarabs + Blood Artist

every -1/-1 counter you place makes an Insect, and every Insect is more fodder. Not unlimited, but it usually drains the table out.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep mana, two or three fodder creatures, and a drain or draw engine. Yawgmoth on turn three or four with fodder ready draws three or four cards on the spot.

Do not loop without a drain on the battlefield: Yawgmoth's draws are mandatory, and drawing twenty cards with nothing dying on their side only costs you life and library.

Before a big loop, count: life you can pay, cards left in your library, and how much each step drains. Stop the moment the table is dead.
