Prossh makes a 0/1 Kobold for every mana you spend casting it, so a six-mana Prossh is a dragon plus six sacrifice fodder. Every Kobold dying is damage, cards or mana in this deck. Food Chain turns that into an infinite loop: exile Prossh and the Kobolds for mana, recast Prossh for more Kobolds, and make more mana each time. With a damage payoff out, the table dies to Kobolds entering.

The commander
Prossh, Skyraider of Kher

six mana, flying. When you CAST him, create X 0/1 Kobolds named Kobolds of Kher Keep, where X is the mana spent. Sacrifice another creature: Prossh gets +1/+0.

The Kobolds come from casting, not from entering, so a countered Prossh still leaves you the Kobolds. Commander tax raises X: the second cast makes eight, the third ten.

Prossh is his own sacrifice outlet. Sacrificing Kobolds to pump him also triggers every death payoff below.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
+
Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Food Chain

exile Prossh to Food Chain for 7 mana (his mana value 6, plus 1) and send him to the command zone. Recast him; the Kobolds equal everything you spent, tax included. Exile each Kobold for 1 mana. Each loop makes 5 more mana than it costs, so unlimited Kobolds and unlimited mana for creature spells. With Impact Tremors, Purphoros or Agate Instigator out, every Kobold entering damages each opponent; with Beast Whisperer, every Prossh cast draws.

+
Squee, the Immortal + Food Chain

exile Squee for 4 mana, recast him from exile for 3. One mana of profit per loop: unlimited creature mana and unlimited Squee entering.

+
Chatterfang, Squirrel General + Pitiless Plunderer

each death makes a Treasure, Chatterfang adds a Squirrel, and Chatterfang's own ability sacrifices the Squirrel for {B}. Unlimited deaths.

++
Prossh, Skyraider of Kher + Phyrexian Altar + Purphoros, God of the Forge

sacrifice Kobolds to the Altar to recast Prossh from the command zone; Purphoros deals 2 to each opponent for every Kobold that enters.

++
Warren Soultrader + Chatterfang, Squirrel General + Zulaport Cutthroat

Soultrader turns a Squirrel into a Treasure plus a Squirrel; every loop drains the table. Blood Artist works in place of the Cutthroat.

Food Chain mana only casts creatures. That is all the loop needs (Prossh and Squee are creatures), but the damage has to come from triggers, not from spells.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep mana dorks or rituals that cast Prossh by turn four, and one of Food Chain, a tutor, or a payoff. A turn-four Prossh is six Kobolds and a Gaea's Cradle that taps for seven.

Without Food Chain, Prossh plus Impact Tremors and an Altar still deals a lot: every cast is a wave of damage, and every recast after removal makes more Kobolds.

With Food Chain out, put a payoff down first, then go: the loop doesn't stop until the table is dead.
