Yuriko's trigger fires for EVERY Ninja that connects, and the damage is the mana value of the card you reveal. So the deck is built around a contradiction that only makes sense here: it runs some of the most expensive cards in Magic precisely because it never intends to cast them. Draco reveals for sixteen. Dig Through Time reveals for eight and still costs two to cast. Every Ninja that gets through is a card AND a chunk of everyone's life total.

The commander
Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow

commander ninjutsu, so she comes back from the command zone for {U}{B} every time. Every Ninja connection reveals your top card, puts it in your HAND, and drains each opponent for its mana value.

Note the changelings count. Changeling Outcast and Mothdust Changeling are every creature type, so they are Ninjas — they are both the enabler AND a trigger.

Ninjutsu goes on the stack AFTER blockers are declared. Attack with the one-drop, wait for no blocks, then swap. The Ninja arrives already unblocked.

The reveal targets
Draco

mana value SIXTEEN. Sixteen life from each opponent on a single flip. Its domain discount makes it castable in a pinch, but you are never casting it.

Dig Through Time

printed mana value eight, and delve usually makes it cost two. The best card in the deck — cheap to cast, enormous to reveal.

Murderous Cut

mana value five, delve makes it one. Removal that doubles as a flip.

Everything above roughly three mana value is a real hit in a four-player pod. The deck's curve looks clumsy on paper and is deliberate.

The unblockable bodies

These exist to attack on turn one or two and then get swapped away.

Changeling Outcast

one mana, cannot block, cannot BE blocked, and it is a Ninja in its own right.

Gudul Lurker

unblockable, and megamorph gives it a second life later.

Mothdust Changeling

every type, and it can give itself flying.

Ornithopter

zero mana, flies, a turn-one enabler.

Faerie Seer

flies and scries two, which sets up the flip you are about to take.

Bitterblossom

a flying Faerie every upkeep, forever. Endless enablers.

Nether Traitor

shadow, so it is unblockable in practice, and it returns from the graveyard.

Dauthi Voidwalker

shadow, plus it exiles everything headed for their graveyards and steals their best card.

Ophiomancer

a deathtouch Snake every upkeep — a blocker when you need one and a body to swap when you do not.

The Ninjas
Ingenious Infiltrator

a card for EVERY Ninja that connects, not just itself. With three attackers that is three cards on top of three Yuriko triggers.

Fallen Shinobi

two free cards off the defending player's library on every hit.

Higure, the Still Wind

tutors the next Ninja every connection, and makes any Ninja unblockable for {2}.

Ink-Eyes, Servant of Oni

reanimates a creature from the defending player's graveyard, and regenerates through removal.

Okiba-Gang Shinobi

two discards per hit. Against a control table it is brutal.

Dokuchi Silencer

a removal spell every hit, paid for with a spare creature card.

Mistblade Shinobi

bounces a blocker or a problem permanent every hit.

Moon-Circuit Hacker

a card per hit, with no discard on the turn it arrives.

Azra Smokeshaper

cheap ninjutsu, and it makes a creature indestructible on arrival — which saves the attack from a blocker or a sweeper.

Setting the top

Yuriko puts the revealed card in your hand, so a good top card is a card AND damage. These let you choose it.

Brainstorm

three deep, and the two you put back are the next two flips.

Ponder

orders the top three.

Preordain

scry two and draw.

Faerie Seer

a scry attached to an attacker.

Dig Through Time

two cards from the top seven, and the rest go to the bottom rather than clogging.

Interaction
Counterspell

the clean two-mana answer.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for anything noncreature.

Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Infernal Grasp

two mana, kills anything.

Murderous Cut

usually one mana, and a five on the flip.

Feed the Swarm

the only clean enchantment answer in Dimir.

Damnation

your board is cheap and recastable; theirs is not. Wrath and rebuild the same turn with ninjutsu.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, the clear for a lethal alpha strike.

Baleful Strix

a card, a flier, and a deathtouch blocker that trades with anything.

Cards and mana
Mask of Riddles

fear plus a card on every connection — it makes any body unblockable in practice.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud, and haste matters because Yuriko gets recast constantly.

Phyrexian Arena

a card a turn.

Night's Whisper

two cards for two mana.

Mind Stone

ramp early, a card later.

Dimir Signet

fixing, and ninjutsu costs add up.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a one-mana unblockable creature. Changeling Outcast, Ornithopter or Gudul Lurker on turn one means Yuriko is on the battlefield on turn two, and that is four or five extra triggers over the game.

Check the top before you swing when you can. A Brainstorm or Ponder in your first main phase turns a random flip into a sixteen-damage Draco.

Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow

ninjutsu her from the COMMAND ZONE. She does not need to be in your hand, and the commander tax applies to casting, not to ninjutsu — so she comes back for {U}{B} every single time they kill her.

Attack with everything cheap, every turn. The trigger is per Ninja, so three small connections beat one big one, and Reconnaissance-style caution costs you more than the chip damage saves.
