The single biggest rule a new pilot gets wrong is that you still control all Equipment and Auras attached to Slicer while opponents control him. When an opponent attacks another opponent with Slicer and deals combat damage, triggered abilities on your equipment—like drawing two cards from Mask of Memory or creating Treasures from Beamtown Beatstick—trigger for YOU, not the attacking player. Crucially, commander damage is tied to the physical card: all 21 lethal points can be dealt across opponents' turns, meaning your opponents will knock each other out of the game with your commander.

The engine and commander rotation
Slicer, Hired Muscle // Slicer, High-Speed Antagonist

Cast for {2}{R} via More Than Meets the Eye as a 3/4 Vehicle that attacks immediately with first strike and haste. At end of combat, it converts into a 3/4 Robot with double strike. On each opponent's upkeep, pass control: Slicer untaps, gets +2/+2, gains haste, and is goaded. It cannot attack you, and it cannot be sacrificed.

Alexios, Deimos of Kosmos

The backup Slicer in the 99. Alexios passes around the table on each player's upkeep, gains a +1/+1 counter and haste, cannot attack its owner, and cannot be sacrificed. He quickly snowballs with trample if Slicer is taxed or removed.

Homeward Path

Instant-speed insurance. If an opponent attempts to bounce, enchant, or manipulate Slicer in combat against your wishes, tap this land to reclaim control immediately.

Equipment: damage scaling and resource theft
Commander's Plate

Slicer's premier equipment. Since Slicer is mono-red, Plate grants protection from white, blue, black, and green. Slicer cannot be blocked, targeted, or damaged by four of the five colors in Magic, and the +3/+3 turns Slicer into an 8/7 double striker on opponents' turns (16 commander damage per hit, lethal in two swings).

Mask of Memory

Triggers on combat damage. Because you own and control the equipment, YOU draw two cards and discard one on your turn and on EACH opponent's combat step when Slicer connects (up to eight cards drawn per turn cycle).

Infiltration Lens

Equips for {1}. If any player chooses to block Slicer, YOU draw two cards. It forces opponents into an unwinnable dilemma: take massive double-strike commander damage, or hand you a fresh grip.

Beamtown Beatstick

Grants menace and creates a Treasure token for YOU whenever Slicer deals combat damage. Across a single four-player turn cycle, this can harvest four Treasures while opponents smash into each other.

Sword of Wealth and Power

Grants +2/+2 and protection from both instants and sorceries, rendering Slicer immune to Swords to Plowshares, Chaos Warp, and targeted removal. It also nets you a Treasure and copies your next instant or sorcery.

Tenza, Godo's Maul

Equips for {1}. Because Slicer is both legendary and red, it grants +3/+3 and trample. On opponents' turns, Slicer becomes an 8/7 double strike trampler that easily overruns blockers.

Sword of Fire and Ice

Grants protection from blue and red, draws a card, and deals two damage to any target on each hit. Double strike doubles the triggers if both first strike and regular damage connect.

Brotherhood Regalia

Equips to legendary creatures for {1}. Grants unblockable and ward {2}, bypassing any pillowfort or token shields opponents try to erect.

Lost Jitte

Accumulates charge counters on combat damage. Pay a charge counter to untap a land, make a blocker unable to block, or pump with +1/+1 counters.

Umezawa's Jitte

Double strike puts four charge counters on Jitte every combat. You control the activations, letting you machine-gun opposing utility creatures or pump Slicer at will.

O-Naginata

Costs {1} to cast and {2} to equip to creatures with power 3 or greater. Grants +3/+0 and trample, transforming Slicer into an 8/4 double-strike freight train.

Shadowspear

Grants trample and removes indestructible/hexproof from opposing permanents for {1}.

Pip-Boy 3000

Attack trigger that loots, untaps up to two lands to leave interaction up, or puts +1/+1 counters and gains life.

Goldvein Pick

Produces Treasures for you on combat damage to ramp into equipment and hold up counterspells.

Prying Blade

Additional treasure engine that stacks with Beatstick and Goldvein Pick.

Lavaspur Boots

Grants ward {1}, haste, and +1/+0 for a single mana.

Combat multipliers and value engines
Jeska, Thrice Reborn

Her 0 loyalty ability makes a target creature deal triple combat damage. On your turn, Slicer can easily deal 15 to 24 commander damage in a single attack.

Shiny Impetus

Goads and gives +2/+2, while creating a Treasure token for you whenever the enchanted creature attacks.

Monstrous Rage

Gives +2/+0 until end of turn and creates a Monster Role token (+1/+1 and trample). The Role stays attached across all opponents' turns, ensuring Slicer tramples over chump blockers forever.

Professional Face-Breaker

Turns combat damage into Treasures and sacrifices Treasures to impulse-draw through your library.

Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer

Early ramp and card advantage on turn 1 before dropping Slicer.

Treasonous Ogre

Lets you pay life for red mana to deploy multiple equipment and equip Slicer on turn 2 or 3.

The One Ring

Grindy card draw engine and protection from everything for a full turn. Keeps your hand overflowing with protection and combo pieces.

Wheel of Fortune

Refuels seven fresh cards while stripping opponents of sculpted hands and removal spells.

Underworld Breach

Escapes rituals, equipment, and combo pieces from your graveyard to rebuild instantly or execute the win.

Disruption and tax layers
Trinisphere

Forces every spell with mana value under 3 to cost at least {3}. Destroys opposing cheap interaction, cantrips, and fast mana while Slicer continues to pummel the table.

Damping Sphere

Punishes storm decks and turns lands producing multiple mana into a single colorless mana.

Vexing Bauble

Shuts off all free interaction (Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Pact of Negation) and can be sacrificed to draw a card when you are ready to combo.

Grafdigger's Cage

Shuts off creature casting from graveyards and libraries. Does not interfere with Slicer or your equipment.

Soulless Jailer

Shuts off noncreature casting from graveyards and exile.

Roiling Vortex

Prevents opponents from gaining life to survive Slicer's onslaught and shocks players who cast free spells.

Stranglehold

Completely shuts down opponent tutors and stops extra-turn chains.

Harsh Mentor

Punishes opponents for activating fetchlands, Treasures, and artifact abilities with two damage per activation.

Defense Grid

Taxes spells cast on other players' turns by {3}, protecting Slicer during combat and shielding your turn-6 combo from countermagic.

The compact finish
+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

Infinite hasty Dualcaster Mages. Cast Twinflame targeting any creature you control, hold priority, and cast Dualcaster Mage with flash. Copy Twinflame targeting Dualcaster Mage, creating a hasty token copy that copies Twinflame again. Repeat for infinite attackers.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication

Same infinite loop. Molten Duplication copies an artifact or creature; Dualcaster copies it on the stack to produce infinite hasty Dualcasters.

In accordance with Bracket 3 rules, this two-card combo is reserved for turn 6 or later. Use Slicer and your stax pieces to dismantle opponents' life totals and force out their removal during turns 2 through 5, then resolve the combo to close out the remaining players in one blow.

→

Chapter III tutors a 0 or 1-mana artifact directly into play. Grab Commander's Plate for protection, Shadowspear for trample, or Sol Ring for ramp.

→
Inventors' Fair → The One Ring

Fetches The One Ring for insurmountable card draw, or finds Sword of Wealth and Power to make Slicer untouchable.

→
Pinnacle Monk // Mystic Peak → Twinflame

Returns a countered or milled Twinflame from your graveyard back to hand.

Protection and interaction
Deflecting Swat

Free redirection spell with Slicer on the board. Redirects opposing Swords to Plowshares, Chaos Warp, or counterspells.

Bolt Bend

Costs a single red mana whenever you control a creature with power 4 or greater (which Slicer meets easily). Redirects targeted removal.

Return the Favor

Modally redirects spells and triggered abilities or copies big spells with spree.

Untimely Malfunction

Flexible modal answer that destroys an artifact, redirects a spell, or prevents two creatures from blocking Slicer.

Tibalt's Trickery

Hard counter in mono-red to stop game-ending spells or board wipes.

Pyroblast

One-mana answer to blue counterspells, Cyclonic Rift, and blue commanders.

Red Elemental Blast

Redundant one-mana blue hate and countermagic.

Welding Jar

Free artifact regeneration to protect your critical equipment from destruction.

Chaos Warp

Universal spot removal for any problematic permanent.

Abrade

Efficient removal for utility creatures or hate artifacts.

Blasphemous Act

The ultimate reset button when the board gets crowded; costs {R} when boards are wide.

Lightning Bolt

Clean spot removal for opposing hatebears and blockers.

Opening hand and sequencing

Look for hands with two or three lands and fast mana (Sol Ring, Lotus Petal, Simian Spirit Guide, Jeweled Amulet, or rituals) to cast Slicer on turn 1 or turn 2. Prioritize having at least one evasion or card-advantage equipment (Commander's Plate, Mask of Memory, Beamtown Beatstick) ready to attach on turn 2 or 3.

Cast Slicer converted for {2}{R} as Slicer, High-Speed Antagonist. Attack an open opponent immediately to convert Slicer at end of combat into Slicer, Hired Muscle.

On turns 2 through 5, pass Slicer around the table to rack up lethal commander damage while deploying disruption pieces (Trinisphere, Defense Grid, Vexing Bauble) and drawing cards through your equipment.

Hold up interaction (Deflecting Swat, Bolt Bend, Return the Favor) to protect Slicer from removal. If the game extends to turn 6 and opponents have stabilized behind blockers, assemble Dualcaster Mage and Twinflame or Molten Duplication to wipe out the table instantly.
