Build the first combat around connecting with actual power 7, not merely dealing 7 damage. Tifa creates extra combats only during your first combat phase, but her qualifying damage trigger still untaps your creatures in later combats. That distinction turns one successful attack into a lethal sequence.

This is an adult heroine and anime-art combat deck, with official printing pins on all 65 nonland cards. The characters carry the attacking plan; the remaining slots supply efficient mana, cards, and protection. Not every illustration is anime or depicts a woman. Bracket 3 uses exactly three Game Changers: Enlightened Tutor, Gaea's Cradle, and Smothering Tithe. There are no planned infinite loops, extra-turn chains, or mass land denial. A-tier is an estimate for its explosive combat engine, not a claim of cEDH power or a guaranteed kill turn.

Turn the first attack into several combats
Tifa, Martial Artist

Declare attackers against multiple opponents to grow Tifa through melee. A qualifying hit to each player produces a separate trigger; connecting with three players can create three extra combats. Double strike can generate triggers in both damage steps. The triggers untap creatures, not lands, and mana does not carry between steps or phases.

+
Tifa, Martial Artist + Aurelia, Exemplar of Justice

Mentor's separate attack trigger is a bonus; the reliable setup is Aurelia's beginning-of-combat +2/+0 targeting Tifa. Tifa also gets trample and vigilance because she is red and white. Even melee for one opponent then makes her 7 power. These temporary boosts accumulate through later combats.

+
Tifa, Martial Artist + Halana and Alena, Partners

Their beginning-of-combat counters and haste make a newly cast Tifa ready to connect. Their base 2 power supplies two counters, so a single melee bonus reaches 7. Choose a different attacker once Tifa already has enough power.

+
Tifa, Martial Artist + Tifa's Limit Break

The one-mana mode supplies +2/+2; together with one melee bonus, an otherwise unmodified Tifa reaches 7. The three-mana mode doubles current power and toughness, so resolve other boosts first.

+
Tifa, Martial Artist + Annie Joins Up

Doubles both melee triggers and Tifa's combat-damage triggers. It also doubles triggered abilities of your other legendary creatures, but not nonlegendary creatures or enchantments. Its entry damage can clear a blocker before the attack.

+
Tifa, Martial Artist + Duelist's Heritage

Give an already-7-power attacker double strike during the first combat to trigger Tifa in both damage steps if it connects twice. An untapped attacker remains attacking unless an effect removes it from combat.

+
Tifa, Martial Artist + Reconnaissance

Attack broadly for melee, then remove creatures that would die in combat. Removing them before damage forfeits their damage. Alternatively, activate in the end-of-combat step after they dealt damage to untap surviving attackers, even when no 7-power creature connected.

Adeline, Resplendent Cathar

Supplies bodies for later combats and scales with your creature count. Tokens put onto the battlefield attacking do not count as having been declared as attackers against extra opponents for melee. Without haste, new tokens cannot be declared as attackers in later combats that turn.

Myrel, Shield of Argive

Repeated attacks grow the Soldier army. During your turn she shuts off opponents' spells and activated abilities of artifacts, creatures, and enchantments; she does not stop land abilities or triggered abilities. Establish her before committing the attack where possible.

+
Champion of Lambholt + Adeline, Resplendent Cathar

Adeline's tokens grow Champion during the attack, before blockers are declared. Champion then prevents smaller creatures from blocking any of your creatures. Champion herself does not need to attack.

Make every following combat more dangerous
Unnatural Growth

Doubles power and toughness at the beginning of every combat, including opponents' combats. Your turn's successive combat boosts last until end of turn and compound, turning ordinary attackers into overwhelming threats. Its four green pips require deliberate fetching.

+
Lightning, Army of One + Duelist's Heritage

Lightning's first-strike hit establishes a damage-doubling effect before normal damage. With double strike, her second hit benefits and establishes another doubler for subsequent damage to that player or their permanents. Repeated combats multiply the pressure; the first hit does not benefit from the effect it creates.

+
Lightning, Army of One + Annie Joins Up

A single connecting hit triggers stagger twice, establishing two separate doublers for subsequent damage. Those effects last until your next turn and help damage from other players too. They increase damage, not the creature's power for Tifa's threshold.

Gisela, Blade of Goldnight

Doubles damage to opponents and their permanents while protecting your side from damage. She is an expensive finisher for an established board, not an opening-hand ramp plan. Her doubling also does not increase actual power.

+
Akroma, Vision of Ixidor + Samut, Voice of Dissent

Samut already has double strike, vigilance, and haste, so Akroma gives her at least +3/+3 at each combat's beginning. Flash is not one of Akroma's counted abilities. Akroma boosts other creatures, not herself, and each combat's bonuses persist until end of turn.

Akroma's Will

With your commander on the battlefield, both modes provide evasion, double strike, protection, and indestructible for a finishing attack. It also protects against many targeted spells and damage wipes, but not untargeted exile or sacrifice effects. Akroma can count its granted keywords if it resolves before her trigger.

Aurelia, the Warleader

Her first attack each turn untaps the team and creates another combat. She works without Tifa and makes the deck less dependent on its commander. More combats do not reset the first-attack restriction.

Karlach, Fury of Avernus

Attack with her during the first combat to untap your attackers and add another combat. The first-strike benefit excludes creatures with vigilance. She supplies an independent route to extra attacks if Tifa is removed.

+
Tifa Lockhart + Windswept Heath

Playing and then sacrificing the fetchland gives two separate landfall doublings. Pump Tifa Lockhart before those triggers resolve for larger results. She has trample, but she is not your commander and her damage does not count toward commander damage.

Embercleave

Flash it in after declaring several attackers to reduce its generic cost. The entry attachment supplies +1/+1, trample, and double strike to the creature that can connect. Two red mana are still required, and shroud from Lightning Greaves prevents the attachment trigger from targeting that creature.

+
Shadowspear + Kessig Wolf Run

Two complementary ways to force damage through chump blockers. Shadowspear also removes opponents' hexproof and indestructible with its activated ability; Wolf Run converts spare mana into a surprise qualifying attacker. Neither grants an additional combat by itself.

Get ahead without running out of cards
++
Birds of Paradise + Llanowar Elves + Delighted Halfling

One-mana acceleration makes turn-three Tifa practical. Halfling's colored mana is restricted to legendary spells and makes those spells uncounterable; use its colorless mode for ordinary spells. Birds' flying also makes it a useful later attacker once pumped.

Joraga Treespeaker

Turn one Treespeaker, turn two level up once, then tap for two green to keep developing. Level only at sorcery speed. Do not pour four more levels into her without a clear reason, and expect opponents to target the first level-up activation.

+
Bloom Tender + Tifa, Martial Artist

With Tifa on the battlefield, Tender can make red, green, and white together. Tifa's combat hits untap Tender too, but use combat-generated mana on instant-speed spells or abilities before the current step ends; it cannot wait for your second main phase.

Selvala, Heart of the Wilds

Large attackers greatly increase her mana production. Her card draw is conditional and can benefit an opponent who plays the largest creature, so do not count every creature entry as a card. She needs green mana to activate even after Tifa untaps her.

++
Nature's Lore + Three Visits + Farseek

Fetch typed dual lands to assemble all three colors without loading the deck with basics. Nature's Lore and Three Visits can find untapped Forest duals; Farseek's land always enters tapped. Fetch for the next spell, including heavy green or double-white costs.

+
Gaea's Cradle + Smothering Tithe

Creatures and Soldiers turn Cradle into large green bursts; Tithe supplies flexible mana while opponents draw. Neither replaces early reliable colored lands. Cradle with no creatures produces no mana.

+
Samut, Hazoret's Champion + Samut, Voice of Dissent

Two haste enablers keep fresh creatures and Soldier tokens useful during subsequent combats. The two-mana Samut is the efficient development play; Voice of Dissent is a flash threat, a large keyword package, and an extra creature untap when white mana is available.

+
Sylvan Library + Lightning, Army of One

Library finds missing lands or protection early, and Lightning's lifelink helps pay for extra cards later. Spend life for useful cards, not reflexively for everything you see.

+
The Great Henge + Garruk's Uprising

Growing creatures discount Henge and enable Uprising's draw triggers. Henge rewards nontoken entries and supplies mana and life; Uprising grants the team trample, which directly supports Tifa's damage trigger. Uprising's entry draw requires a creature with power at least 4 already present.

+
Guardian Project + Rishkar's Expertise

Project draws from the deck's singleton nontoken creatures. Expertise refills after a large power boost, but it is a sorcery: cast before combat after a landfall or pump setup, or in the second main phase while temporary boosts remain. It cannot be cast between consecutive combat phases.

+
Hunter's Insight + Duelist's Heritage

Insight on a creature that connects with double strike can draw twice, once for each damage event. Cast only when you can realistically get through and defend the target; a removal spell can otherwise strand the draw plan.

+
Professional Face-Breaker + Laelia, the Blade Reforged

Successful hits create Treasures per player and per damage step. Face-Breaker converts surplus Treasures into cards playable this turn, and library cards exiled this way grow Laelia. Laelia also exiles a card on each attack; neither effect permits extra land plays or changes normal spell timing.

Protect the attack and rebuild
+
Sigarda, Font of Blessings + Shalai, Voice of Plenty

Sigarda protects your other permanents and Shalai protects your other creatures, so each grants the other hexproof. Sigarda also lets you cast the many Humans and Angels from the top. This protects against targeting, not board wipes.

Saryth, the Viper's Fang

Other tapped creatures gain deathtouch and untapped creatures gain hexproof. Tifa's untap triggers can switch those benefits midcombat; do not assume deathtouch remains through both damage steps. Trample plus deathtouch can make blocking particularly ineffective.

+
Heroic Intervention + Flawless Maneuver

Keep one available when you commit several attackers. Both answer destroy effects; Intervention also stops opposing targeting with hexproof. Neither saves the board from untargeted exile or a sufficiently large toughness reduction.

+
Tamiyo's Safekeeping + Tifa, Martial Artist

One green mana gives your commander or another permanent hexproof and indestructible for the turn, letting you commit to an attack with protection held up. It can also save a combat enchantment or mana engine. The two life gained enables Aerith's end-step return to hand even without lifelink damage. This is protection, not a power boost, and it cannot stop untargeted exile or sacrifice. The cherry-blossom Secret Lair printing keeps the artwork aligned with the anime heroine theme.

+
Deflecting Swat + Boros Charm

Swat protects a critical creature by redirecting a targeted spell or ability and is free with Tifa present. Charm can defend all permanents from destruction or provide a surprise double strike; it is not an answer to exile wipes.

+
Lightning Greaves + Sejiri Shelter // Sejiri Glacier

Greaves provides repeatable haste and shroud; move it away before targeting that creature with your own pumps or counters. Shelter is a land when needed and otherwise protects one creature with protection from a chosen color. Protection can also make an attached colored Equipment fall off.

+
Swords to Plowshares + Path to Exile

Cheap exile answers for dangerous creatures or the blocker that would stop a lethal attack. Path gives its target's controller a basic land, so use Swords first when the life gain will not ruin your damage math.

++
Generous Gift + Beast Within + Wear // Tear

Answer noncreature obstacles without spending a turn developing a slow removal engine. Gift and Beast Within leave a blocker behind; account for it before attacking. Wear // Tear can remove an artifact and an enchantment together when fused from hand.

+
Boseiju, Who Endures + Eiganjo, Seat of the Empire

Lands that also provide interaction when drawn late. Your many legends discount channel costs. Boseiju can remove an obstructive artifact or enchantment; Eiganjo only damages an attacking or blocking creature.

Austere Command

Select modes based on the board you actually have. Destroying small creatures can clear Soldiers and mana dorks of your own, while destroying enchantments can erase your combat engines. There is no automatically one-sided choice.

+
Aerith, Last Ancient + Lightning, Army of One

Lightning's lifelink across multiple combats can reach seven life gained, upgrading Aerith's end-step return from hand to battlefield. Aerith checks total life gained, not net life change, and her own lifelink also contributes.

+
Eternal Witness + Bala Ged Recovery // Bala Ged Sanctuary

Recover a spent finisher, protection spell, or destroyed engine. Recovery can instead be an early tapped land; choosing that mode sacrifices the recursion spell for the game unless the land is returned to hand.

Tutor for the missing job
→
Enlightened Tutor → Reconnaissance

Find this when you have creatures but cannot profitably attack into the board. This is a setup play that costs a card and the next draw, not free card advantage.

→
Enlightened Tutor → Duelist's Heritage

Find double strike when a protected 7-power attacker can connect during the first combat. If the team cannot reach 7 yet, prioritize a power engine instead.

→
Enlightened Tutor → Unnatural Growth

Find this with enough green mana and a surviving board to convert the next combat sequence into overwhelming power. Do not search for it while unable to pay its colored cost.

→
Eladamri's Call → Myrel, Shield of Argive

Find Myrel before a decisive attack into open mana. Call puts her into your hand, so reserve another four mana to cast her and remember opponents can respond before she resolves.

→
Eladamri's Call → Halana and Alena, Partners

Find them when the missing pieces are immediate haste and enough actual power for Tifa to trigger.

→+
Defense of the Heart → Samut, Voice of Dissent + Gisela, Blade of Goldnight

With an opponent controlling at least three creatures at your upkeep and again on resolution, cheat in team haste and a damage doubler. This is a threatening setup, not a deterministic infinite combo, and the upkeep condition gives opponents a chance to prevent it.

→+
Defense of the Heart → Sigarda, Font of Blessings + Shalai, Voice of Plenty

Choose protection over damage when you already have lethal attackers but opposing targeted removal is the obstacle. Untargeted wipes remain a problem.

Finale of Devastation

Early, find the creature that supplies haste, protection, or power; late, X of at least 10 turns a developed board into a hasty finisher. It can find from the graveyard as well as the library. Large Finale is a resource-dependent finish, not a shortcut the opening hand should rely on.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep three usable lands, early acceleration, and a card that makes combat productive or draws more cards. Two lands can work with a reliable one-mana accelerator and another cheap play; mulligan hands whose only action starts at four mana, or whose expensive legends replace early colored sources. The list has 35 lands plus two spell/land cards.

A representative keep is Windswept Heath, Command Tower, Spire Garden, Llanowar Elves, Halana and Alena, Partners, Swords to Plowshares, and Guardian Project. Fetch an untapped green source, accelerate, then decide whether to deploy the draw engine or set up a protected Tifa attack. Do not spend removal just because a legal target exists.

Plan each combat before declaring attackers: verify actual power, identify which players can be hit, choose whether double strike adds a second connecting damage step, and preserve a protection spell when possible. Resolve beginning-of-combat and attack triggers in the order that maximizes the relevant creature's power. Reserve trample or removal for the block that would stop the engine.

Do not overextend every legend into a wipe. A haste enabler, Tifa, and one power source can be sufficient. Keep a follow-up threat or recursion spell in hand. Graveyard hate weakens the recovery package, while fogs and untargeted exile wipes are real vulnerabilities. Combat wins usually require a developed board; the estimated turn-six-to-eight closing window is not a guaranteed goldfish result.
