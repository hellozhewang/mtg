Nicol Bolas makes an opponent discard their entire hand whenever he deals them ANY damage. He does not need to connect in combat: equipping him with Hawkeye's Bow or Black Mage's Rod turns small pings into table-wide hand destruction. Build the mana and protection first, and remember his separate {U}{B}{R} upkeep payment. Empty hands buy a window to win; they do not remove an established battlefield.

Making one Dragon empty three hands
Nicol Bolas

The original eight-mana Elder Dragon from Legends. His damage trigger works with combat, equipment, and spells that make him deal damage. Only his combat damage counts toward the 21 commander-damage threshold. Opponents can respond before their hands are discarded.

Hawkeye's Bow

Equip Bolas for {1}. When he becomes tapped, he deals 1 to each opponent, triggering three hand discards. Attacking triggers this before blockers; Relic of Legends lets you trigger it without attacking. Simply being tapped already does nothing.

Black Mage's Rod

Its entry makes and equips a Hero token, so budget another {3} to move it to Bolas. Each noncreature spell you cast then makes Bolas ping every opponent. Even a spell that gets countered was cast and triggers the ability.

Kediss, Emberclaw Familiar

When Bolas deals combat damage to one opponent, Kediss makes Bolas deal that much damage to each other opponent. Everyone hit discards their hand. The splash is noncombat damage, so it neither loops nor adds commander damage against those other players.

Dragon Tempest

Gives your flying threats haste. When Bolas enters, the Dragon trigger makes Bolas himself deal damage to a target; aim at an opponent to strip a hand immediately. A different Dragon entering does not make Bolas deal that damage.

Chandra's Ignition

Target Bolas to make him damage every opponent and every other creature. This empties hands while clearing most creatures, including many of your own support creatures. It does not damage Bolas, but removal of Bolas in response can stop the spell.

Soul's Fire

An instant-speed way for Bolas to empty one opponent's hand without combat. Hold it for an opponent preparing a big turn or to get value in response to removal; it still needs its creature target to remain legal.

Relic of Legends

Its second ability taps an untapped legendary creature for mana, which triggers Hawkeye's Bow on Bolas. This works even if Bolas entered this turn, because it is the Relic's ability. Save an untapped Bolas to threaten a hand wipe during an opponent's draw step, but this is not a complete lock.

Minamo, School at Water's Edge

Untap Bolas after tapping him to Relic of Legends, so he can attack or tap again. Every use costs blue mana and taps Minamo itself; there is no infinite untap loop here.

Paying for the commander and keeping cheated creatures
Sol Ring

Accelerates your rocks, equipment, and generic costs. Bolas still needs two mana of each colour to cast, and Ring cannot pay any of his upkeep's three coloured pips.

Arcane Signet

Prioritize coloured rocks when choosing between development lines. The three Talismans and three Signets support both Bolas's casting cost and his ongoing upkeep.

Relic of Sauron

Produces two mana in any combination of your colours, covering much of the upkeep payment. Its draw-and-discard activation turns spare mana into cards and puts a large creature into the graveyard for reanimation.

Jeska's Will

One of the three Game Changers. Cast its mana mode before emptying opponents' hands, since its output depends on the targeted opponent's hand size. With Bolas already in play, choosing both modes can fund a decisive follow-up.

Hellkite Courser

Brings Bolas straight from the command zone with haste, avoiding a cast and any commander tax. At your end step, put the return-to-command-zone trigger on the stack first and Thassa's blink trigger above it. The blinked Bolas is a new object and stays on the battlefield.

Sneak Attack

Deploys Dragons or Archon of Cruelty from your HAND for {R}, never from the command zone. Stack Thassa's end-step blink above the delayed sacrifice to keep the new permanent. Without Thassa, exploit the attack and let the creature become a reanimation target.

Command Beacon

Moves Bolas to your hand so Sneak Attack can use him, or so Faithless Looting can discard him for reanimation. This trades away a land; use it when the saved mana or commander tax makes the exchange worthwhile.

Thassa, Deep-Dwelling

Retains creatures temporarily deployed by Hellkite Courser and Sneak Attack, while repeatedly triggering Archon of Cruelty, Smaug, Wicked Worm, or Marang River Regent. Blinking Bolas also retriggers Dragon Tempest, but detaches all his equipment. Pay to equip him again on your next main phase.

Reanimate

A cheap recovery spell for Bolas or a discarded threat; it can also steal an opponent's creature after a hand wipe. Returning Bolas costs eight life. If he dies and you want to reanimate him, leave him in the graveyard instead of moving him to the command zone.

Animate Dead

Returns a threat from any graveyard, but gives it -1/-0. An otherwise unmodified Bolas is therefore only six power, which changes your lethal calculations. Removing the Aura normally costs you the creature too.

Necromancy

Flexible reanimation from any graveyard, including at instant speed. Casting it outside normal sorcery timing makes it sacrifice itself at the next cleanup step. Use that mode for immediate impact or when Thassa can blink the creature before cleanup.

Victimize

Converts the Rod's Hero, a Zombie, or another expendable creature into two reanimated threats. They enter tapped, so plan a recovery turn unless Dragon Tempest supplies haste and another effect untaps them.

Whip of Erebos

Lifelink turns Bolas's table-wide damage into life recovery, including the Bow and Kediss splash. Its activation supplies another hasty reanimation line. Thassa can blink the returned creature before the delayed exile resolves, leaving the new permanent behind; the Whip's replacement does not prevent a move to exile.

Turning discarded hands into your next turn
Waste Not

Discarded creatures make Zombies, lands make black mana, and other nonland cards draw cards. Mana made during the declare-attackers step expires before your main phase: spend it on an instant, Necromancy, or Tinybones, Trinket Thief's activation. You cannot save it for the next upkeep.

Tinybones, Trinket Thief

Draws once at an end step when an opponent discarded that turn, not once per discarded card. After a table-wide hand wipe, its {4}{B}{B} activation makes each empty-handed opponent lose 10 life. Waste Not can help pay for it immediately.

Tinybones, Bauble Burglar

Exiles opponents' discarded cards and lets you play them during your turn, with normal timing and land-play limits. This denies graveyard recovery and supplies a second hand. It also removes creatures you might otherwise want to target with Reanimate, so consider that tradeoff.

Liliana's Caress

Converts a large hand discard into substantial life loss. Its effect is life loss, not damage, so Fiery Emancipation does not triple it. It becomes less useful once opponents are already empty-handed.

Rhystic Study

A Game Changer that supplies cards before Bolas arrives and while you hold mana for interaction. It also keeps the deck functional when Bolas is repeatedly removed.

Mystic Remora

Draws through the early setup turns without needing a creature. Let it go when its cumulative upkeep starts preventing your own development; paying both this upkeep and Bolas's can consume a whole turn.

Black Market Connections

Provides a flexible mix of cards, Treasure, and bodies. The creature token has all creature types, including Dragon, so it increases Dragon Tempest's count. Life payments add up alongside Reanimate and Stinging Study; choose modes deliberately.

Faithless Looting

Filters awkward expensive hands and deposits creatures for reanimation. It is selection rather than raw card advantage, so pair it with your ongoing draw engines.

Bitter Reunion

Discards a reanimation target while replacing itself and later gives your team haste. Sacrificing it does not cast a spell and therefore does not trigger Black Mage's Rod.

Stinging Study

Draws eight and costs eight life while Bolas is on the battlefield or in the command zone. If your only commander is instead in your hand or graveyard, it will not give you those eight cards. Whip of Erebos helps recover the life later.

Ending the game after the hand wipe
Fiery Emancipation

With an unmodified seven-power Bolas and Kediss, an unblocked attack deals 21 combat damage to the defending player. Kediss then has Bolas deal 21 to each other opponent, which this enchantment triples again to 63. This is a finite, three-permanent finishing line, assuming no damage prevention or disruption. Animate Dead's power penalty changes the numbers.

Temur Battle Rage

Gives Bolas double strike and normally trample because his power is at least four. Without other boosts, two seven-power hits total 14 commander damage, not an immediate commander kill. Kediss triggers in each combat-damage step that Bolas connects, and the first hit strips defensive cards before the second.

The Reaver Cleaver

Adds power and trample so Bolas connects, then turns his combat damage into Treasure. An otherwise unmodified Bolas makes eight Treasures on a clean hit. Kediss splash is noncombat damage and does not create extra Cleaver Treasures.

Torment of Hailfire

Spends Treasure on a finishing spell when opponents no longer have cards to discard. They can still sacrifice nonland permanents instead of losing life, so count both their boards and life totals before declaring a lethal X.

Hellkite Tyrant

Steals an opponent's artifacts through combat and offers an upkeep win when you control at least twenty artifacts. Your rocks and Treasure all count, but the threshold must still hold when the trigger resolves. This is a genuine route to victory without Bolas.

Ancient Copper Dragon

A hasty connection makes a variable number of Treasures for more threats or Torment of Hailfire. The die roll is not a guaranteed twenty, so avoid a plan that only works on a high result.

Smaug, Wicked Worm

Punishes developed opposing artifact boards with a large batch of tapped Treasures. They cannot pay for an immediate follow-up until untapped. Spending Treasure mana to cast a spell then draws one card and costs one life, regardless of how many Treasures funded that spell. Paying Bolas's upkeep is not casting a spell.

Archon of Cruelty

Provides an independent threat that generates cards, life, and sacrifice pressure on entry and attack. Reanimate, Sneak Attack, and Thassa all exploit its entry trigger when committing Bolas would be too risky.

Balefire Dragon

Clears the defending player's creatures after connecting, making subsequent Bolas attacks easier. Trample from The Reaver Cleaver can help it deal combat damage through a flying blocker.

Keeping the window open
Lightning Greaves

Free equip gives haste and protection, but shroud also stops your own equip abilities, Chandra's Ignition, Soul's Fire, and Thassa's blink from targeting Bolas. Move Greaves onto another creature before targeting him; you cannot simply detach it without another legal equip target.

Swiftfoot Boots

Costs mana to equip but uses hexproof, so you can keep targeting Bolas with your own spells and equipment. This is often the better protection piece for the Rod or Bow plan.

Mithril Coat

Flash protection against destruction, and its entry ability can attach it to Bolas. Indestructible does not stop exile, sacrifice, toughness reduction, or Bolas's unpaid upkeep sacrifice.

Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire

Protects a key creature from a death effect for one mana and two life, or serves as your thirty-seventh potential land. To return Bolas, leave him in the graveyard while the delayed trigger resolves. It does not answer exile.

Deflecting Swat

Protects your threat or engine from targeted interaction, often for free with Bolas in play. Bolt Bend provides a second redirection effect and normally costs only {R} while a large threat survives. Neither stops an untargeted sweeper.

Swan Song

Holds up cheaply against a wrath, reanimation spell, or troublesome enchantment. An Offer You Can't Refuse broadens the noncreature coverage but gives its controller two Treasures; use it when the prevented spell matters more than that resource gift.

Mana Drain

Covers threats your narrow counters cannot stop. Its delayed colourless mana helps cast Dragons and equipment but cannot pay Bolas's coloured upkeep. Holding up {U}{U} competes with your setup, so plan the mana before deploying another rock.

Cyclonic Rift

The third Game Changer. Overload it before making Bolas damage the table: opponents' nonland permanents return to their hands and are then discarded. This needs substantial mana or an already established damage outlet, and opponents can respond between the two effects.

Marang River Regent // Coil and Catch

Returns two other nonland permanents on entry. Bounce important opposing permanents before a Bolas hand wipe to remove them more durably, or reuse your own entry triggers. Its Omen side draws three and discards one, then shuffles the card into your library; it does not wait in exile to be cast later.

Chaos Warp

Answers a troublesome permanent type your other interaction might miss, although the replacement permanent can be dangerous. Feed the Swarm gives a second answer to enchantments while costing life and requiring your main phase.

Vandalblast

Removes an early problem artifact cheaply or, overloaded, sweeps opponents' artifacts. If Hellkite Tyrant can safely steal those artifacts instead, preserve them for the alternate win.

Toxic Deluge

Resets an early creature board before your Dragons take over. High values also kill Bolas regardless of indestructible, and the life payment matters alongside your other black draw and reanimation effects.

Crux of Fate

Choose to destroy non-Dragons when your threats are Dragons. This still kills your own Kediss, Tinybones creatures, and Archon of Cruelty, while opposing Dragons survive. Check the whole battlefield before choosing the mode.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Discard fuels opposing graveyards, so keep this available when that is their recovery plan. Its sacrifice can exile opponents' graveyards without touching your own reanimation targets; use its draw mode only when graveyard control is unnecessary.

Finding the useful piece and planning your mana
Fabricate

Usually finds Hawkeye's Bow when Bolas is nearly ready, Black Mage's Rod when you can follow with spells, or protection when survival is the bottleneck. Find Soul-Guide Lantern against graveyard engines or a mana rock when you cannot yet deploy your threats. Do not automatically tutor another payoff into a hand that cannot cast its commander.

Command Tower

The mana base prioritizes all three colours: Bolas needs two of each to cast, then one of each every upkeep. Fetchlands find the typed duals, shocks, or tapped utility duals. Fetch a tapped land on a quiet turn; fetch an untapped source when holding interaction matters.

Takenuma, Abandoned Mire

Recovers a creature into your hand, where Sneak Attack can deploy it or Faithless Looting can discard it again. Channeling is an activated ability, so it does not trigger Black Mage's Rod.

What to keep in an opening hand and how to sequence

Keep three lands with access to all three colours, an early mana rock, and either draw or useful interaction. A two-land hand needs unusually strong cheap development and a credible third-land plan; a hand full of Dragons and equipment is a mulligan. The commander is guaranteed access to an expensive threat, so you do not need several more in your opener.

An example keep is Command Tower, Polluted Delta, Steam Vents, Arcane Signet, Mystic Remora, Hawkeye's Bow, and Swan Song. Establish the draw engine and mana first, deploying the Bow when it does not prevent interaction. Find a second black source before trying to cast Bolas normally, and plan the second red source too.

In the opening turns, develop mana and draw while stopping an opponent's decisive engine. In the middle turns, deploy an independent threat or arrange Hellkite Courser, Sneak Attack, or reanimation. Cast Bolas when you can trigger him promptly and still afford protection; resolving an eight-mana creature and passing unprotected is the easiest way to lose tempo.

Before every Bolas turn, budget {U}{B}{R} for upkeep, any equip costs, and your interaction. If using Greaves, arrange a spare creature to carry it while you target Bolas. If using the Bow with Relic of Legends, decide whether tapping now is worth losing an attack without Minamo.

After hands are empty, prioritize lethal pressure instead of collecting more discard payoffs. Use Fiery Emancipation with Kediss, a Treasure-funded Torment of Hailfire, repeated Tinybones activations, or the Hellkite Tyrant upkeep check. Keep an answer for dangerous permanents already in play: hand destruction never substitutes for battlefield control.

This list uses exactly three Game Changers: Cyclonic Rift, Jeska's Will, and Rhystic Study. It contains no planned infinite combo, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain. Its repeated hand destruction is still severe resource pressure, so describe that plan when introducing the deck at a Bracket 3 table.
