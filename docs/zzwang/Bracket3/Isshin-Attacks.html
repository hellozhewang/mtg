Isshin doubles every attack trigger you control, and this deck is built out of nothing else. Hero of Bladehold makes four Soldiers instead of two. Adeline makes two tokens per opponent instead of one. Hellrider deals two damage per attacker. Aurelia gives you TWO extra combat phases. The whole deck is one line — attack — and Isshin makes that line twice as good.

The commander
Isshin, Two Heavens as One

if a creature ATTACKING causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers again. Read every "whenever this attacks" and "whenever you attack" in the deck as happening twice.

Note it doubles triggers from permanents YOU control caused by attacking creatures. That includes enchantments and equipment, not just the creatures themselves — Mardu Ascendancy, Curse of Opulence and Blade of Selves all double.

Token makers, doubled
Hero of Bladehold

four 1/1 Soldiers per attack, and battle cry twice for +2/+0 on the whole team.

Adeline, Resplendent Cathar

two tokens per opponent per attack. In a four-player pod that is six bodies, and her own power counts them all.

Hanweir Garrison

four attacking Humans every combat, off a three-drop.

Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon

two counters and two batches of Gnomes per attack. It snowballs faster than anything else in the deck.

Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin

two counters and two batches of Goblins, and the batches are sized after the counters land.

Myrel, Shield of Argive

doubles your Soldier count twice per attack, and opponents cannot interact on your turn at all.

Ainok Strike Leader

two Goblins per opponent whenever it or your commander attacks, and it sacrifices to make your tokens indestructible through a wrath.

Mardu Ascendancy

two Goblins for every nontoken attacker.

Blade of Selves

myriad twice — two token copies of the equipped creature attacking each other opponent.

Extra combats
Aurelia, the Warleader

her attack trigger doubles, so you untap and get TWO additional combat phases. Three combats in one turn, each one re-triggering every token maker above.

Combat Celebrant

exert it and the trigger doubles, untapping the team twice and adding two more combats.

Neyali, Suns' Vanguard

attacking tokens have double strike, and every token attack exiles a card you may play. With six tokens attacking that is six cards.

Turning attackers into damage
Hellrider

one damage per attacking creature, doubled — so two per attacker, before combat damage. On a board of ten attackers that is twenty damage from the trigger alone.

Brutal Hordechief

one life drained per attacker, doubled, and it can force their creatures to block where you choose.

Mishra, Claimed by Gix

each opponent loses life equal to your attacker count, twice.

Tectonic Giant

three damage to each opponent, twice, every attack — and again whenever an opponent targets it.

Adriana, Captain of the Guard

melee for the whole team. Attacking three players is +3/+3 on every creature, and Isshin doubles each melee instance.

Iroas, God of Victory

menace for the team and no damage to your attackers at all. Attacking becomes completely free.

Dolmen Gate

the same protection on a two-mana artifact.

Akroma's Will

with your commander out, take both halves. On a board of twenty tokens it is simply lethal.

Boros Charm

double strike on the one creature that needs it, or indestructible for the board through a wrath.

Making the tokens bigger
Cathars' Crusade

a counter on every creature for every creature that enters. With Adeline and Hero of Bladehold making six tokens a combat, the board grows by dozens of counters per attack.

Goldnight Commander

+1/+1 to the team for each creature that enters. Same trigger count, immediate damage instead of permanent counters.

Elenda, the Dusk Rose

grows on every death and leaves a pile of lifelinking Vampires behind.

Bitterblossom

a free flier every turn, which is another attacker for every "whenever you attack" trigger.

Cards and value from attacking
Chivalric Alliance

a card whenever you attack with two or more creatures — doubled to two.

Firemane Commando

the same, plus it taxes the rest of the table for attacking anyone but you.

Neyali, Suns' Vanguard

impulse draw scaling with your token count.

Curse of Opulence

a Gold token whenever the enchanted player is attacked, doubled — and the rest of the table gets one too, which points their attacks away from you.

Skullclamp

on 1/1 tokens, two cards each. This deck makes more 1/1s than any clamp can keep up with.

Midnight Reaper

a card for every nontoken creature that dies.

Bastion of Remembrance

a drain for every creature that dies, and this deck loses a lot of tokens in combat.

Necropotence

as much card advantage as your life total allows.

Phyrexian Arena

a card every turn.

Night's Whisper

two cards for two mana.

Smothering Tithe

Treasures to deploy two threats a turn.

Removal and protection
Swords to Plowshares

one mana, exile.

Path to Exile

the second copy.

Anguished Unmaking

instant-speed exile for any nonland permanent.

Bedevil

artifact, creature or planeswalker, at instant speed.

Generous Gift

anything at all.

Council's Judgment

gets around hexproof and indestructible entirely, and usually exiles more than one thing.

Chaos Warp

the answer to an enchantment.

Blasphemous Act

cheap, and this deck rebuilds a board faster than anyone else at the table.

Flawless Maneuver

free with your commander out.

Teferi's Protection

phase out through a wrath, a combo kill or a huge attack.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it redirects the removal aimed at Isshin or at Aurelia.

Sun Titan

returns Skullclamp, Bitterblossom, a Signet or a dead three-drop on entry AND on every attack — doubled, so two permanents per attack.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep two mana rocks or a rock and a cheap attacker. Isshin costs three and does nothing alone; the deck wants him down on turn three with a token maker following on turn four.

Sequence the attack triggers deliberately. Cathars' Crusade counters resolve as tokens enter, so making tokens BEFORE the pump triggers resolve means more counters — with Adeline and Hero of Bladehold, order the token makers first.

Isshin, Two Heavens as One

he is worth a removal spell to the table, so hold Flawless Maneuver or Deflecting Swat when you can. Losing him halves every card in your hand.

Aurelia, the Warleader

do not cast her into an empty board. She is a three-combat turn with a wide team and a 3/4 flier without one.
