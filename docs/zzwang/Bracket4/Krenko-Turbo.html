Krenko, Mob Boss leads this separate Bracket 4 copy of Krenko-Combo. The original Bracket 3 list is unchanged. Slow opponents' colored mana before committing the creature combo: Moon effects buy a window, while haste, fast mana and protection turn that window into a win. This is an optimized construction, not a measured win percentage or tournament result.

Make the first activation matter
Krenko, Mob Boss

Count every Goblin you control, including Krenko, before activating. A ready Krenko with two supporting Goblins produces three more immediately; an isolated Krenko is much less explosive.

Samut, Hazoret's Champion

Two-mana haste for every creature, including Krenko and the non-Goblin Marvin. Deploy it before committing Krenko when you intend to activate immediately.

Goblin Chieftain

Another Goblin haste source that also turns the fair attack into pressure. Unlike Samut, its haste applies only to Goblins.

Thousand-Year Elixir

Lets newly arrived creatures use tap abilities as though they had haste, then pays one and taps to untap Krenko. It does not actually grant haste for attacking.

Lightning Greaves

Equip for zero to protect a ready commander. Move the Greaves off before attaching Staff or Mantle or targeting Krenko with another effect; shroud stops your own targeting too.

Skirk Prospector

Converts spare Goblins into the red needed for tutors, haste and loops. Leave the required Goblin count and sacrifice outlet alive rather than cashing out the whole board early.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Thornbite Staff + Skirk Prospector

Attach Staff to Krenko, normally paying its four-mana equip; Krenko is not a Shaman that gets the automatic attachment. Activate Krenko, sacrifice one new Goblin to Prospector, and Staff's death trigger untaps Krenko. With Krenko and Prospector already present each pass makes at least two Goblins, so repeat for arbitrary bodies and red mana. Finish with Bombardment, Kreat or Mons.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Thornbite Staff + Phyrexian Altar

Altar supplies a second free sacrifice engine, making one mana of any color per creature. Start with Krenko plus another Goblin so each activation creates more bodies than the one sacrificed. Unlike Prospector, Altar can also cash in a non-Goblin token.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Staff of Domination + Skirk Prospector

Start with ready Krenko and at least FIVE Goblins including it. Tap Krenko for five tokens, sacrifice those five for five red, pay three and tap Staff to untap Krenko, then pay one to untap Staff. Each pass nets one mana while retaining the original five Goblins. Bank mana, draw with Staff for five plus one to untap it, and find Bombardment or Kreat. With only four Goblins the mana loop merely breaks even unless an entry/death payoff already wins.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Umbral Mantle + Skirk Prospector

With at least four Goblins counted by Krenko, make tokens and sacrifice three for Mantle's untap cost. Each loop leaves an extra Goblin. Exactly three counted Goblins only breaks even on bodies.

Marvin, Murderous Mimic

Copies other creatures' activated abilities, including Krenko's, but does not copy haste. Use Samut or Elixir before trying a fresh Marvin. It remains a Toy rather than another counted Goblin.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Zealous Conscripts

Kiki copies Conscripts; the token's entry untaps Kiki. Repeat for arbitrary hasty attackers, or sacrifice tokens to Bombardment. Combat needs an available combat step and a clear damage path.

General Kreat, the Boltbringer

Repeated creature entries drain every opponent, so a token loop becomes a same-turn noncombat kill instead of waiting to untap.

The One Ring

Refill after the first creature line is answered. Its protection trigger requires casting it; trading it onto the battlefield does not give protection. Account for growing burden life loss instead of treating every extra card as free.

Pashalik Mons

Goblin deaths deal one damage to any target. Prospector and Bombardment make the death trigger dependable rather than waiting for combat.

Howlsquad Heavy

Gives other Goblins haste and adds a body at combat. Its huge mana ability needs max speed; it is not immediately available just because Heavy entered.

+
Rundvelt Hordemaster + Skullclamp

Hordemaster supplies playable Goblins after deaths; Clamp turns disposable creatures into cards; a toughness-boosting lord can prevent it killing a token by itself, so use a sacrifice outlet when needed. Do not claim the Hordemaster's exiled cards go to hand.

+
Goblin Engineer + Goblin Welder

Engineer can put Staff, Mantle or another artifact in the graveyard. Welder trades a battlefield artifact for it after becoming ready; protect both targets until resolution. Engineer's own return ability is limited to mana value three or less.

+
Gamble + Underworld Breach

Gamble can find the missing piece, and Breach makes a discarded piece recoverable if enough other graveyard cards remain. Escape exiles three other cards each time; this is finite recovery, not a free permanent loop.

++
Chrome Mox + Mox Diamond + Mox Opal

Moxen accelerate the commander turn. Chrome consumes a colored nonartifact card, Diamond consumes a land, and Opal needs three artifacts. An opener whose acceleration cannot turn on is not fast.

+
Mana Vault + Jeska's Will

Vault supplies generic mana for Krenko and equipment; it does not pay red pips. With Krenko present Will can use both modes. Keep enough red sources to cast the cards it exposes.

+
Rite of Flame + Desperate Ritual

Bridge the mana gap into a haste-plus-commander or tutor-plus-combo turn. Cast these to execute a plan, not merely to add storm while losing cards.

Treasonous Ogre

Pay three life for each red mana to fund a tutor, haste and the final combo. This is repeatable acceleration rather than a one-shot ritual, but your life total is finite. Reserve life for opposing damage and the Ring instead of assuming an unlimited payment.

+
Brightstone Ritual + Battle Hymn

Bodies become burst mana. Count actual Goblins for Brightstone and all your creatures for Hymn; they are poor recovery cards on an empty battlefield.

+
Goblin Cratermaker + Gempalm Incinerator

Matron-accessible interaction. Cratermaker sacrifices to kill a small creature or a colorless nonland permanent. Cycling Incinerator draws toward a stacked pile and can remove a creature using the actual Goblin count.

Deny mana without denying your own finish
Blood Moon

All nonbasic lands become Mountains, including your own. The 21 basic Mountains keep functioning, but Tomb loses its two-mana output and Cavern, Arena, Nykthos, Three Tree City and Fair lose their special abilities. It is strongest against nonbasic-heavy multicolor hands; it does not remove artifact or creature mana. Deploy it when delaying opponents matters more than your utility lands.

Magus of the Moon

A second Moon that Imperial Recruiter can find because it has power two. It is Human rather than Goblin, so Matron, Recruiter and Muxus cannot find or deploy it. Creature removal can restore opposing mana immediately; keep that in mind before relying on the lock.

Defense Grid

Opponents pay three extra for spells during your turn. You also pay three extra for spells during their turns, and anyone with spare mana can still interact. Use it to protect the decisive turn; it does not stop abilities or make the combo unanswerable.

Recruiter piles and independent Goblin wins
→
Goblin Matron → Goblin Recruiter

Matron puts Recruiter into hand; budget the second cast. Find the engine rather than another anthem when a protected combo is available.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Goblin Recruiter

Recruiter has power one and is a legal target. Imperial also finds Snoop, Prospector, Courier or Kiki when that is the missing piece.

+
Goblin Recruiter + Goblin Ringleader

For a four-card refill, put Ringleader first, then Snoop, Courier, Prospector and Squelcher. Draw Ringleader, cast it, and take those four Goblins. Put Kiki immediately below the four if assembling Snoop next. Leave Fanatic in the library.

+
Goblin Recruiter + Muxus, Goblin Grandee

A six-hit deployment is Warchief, Kiki, Crafter, Prospector, Snoop and Fanatic. All six are Goblin creatures with mana value five or less. Crafter can champion the Recruiter already in play; Kiki/Crafter/Prospector then supplies the immediate kill. Cast Muxus from the command zone in its own deck, or have it in hand in the others.

Complete fetches before Recruiter resolves. Drawing, milling or shuffling afterward changes the planned top cards. A pile is not a guarantee if a needed piece is already in hand or the graveyard; use a different route rather than pretending it is still in the library.

+
Conspicuous Snoop + Torch Courier

The direct pile is Snoop on top to draw, followed by Courier and Kiki. After drawing and casting Snoop, cast Courier from the revealed top and sacrifice it to give Snoop haste. Kiki must now remain on top, not in hand or on the battlefield.

+
Conspicuous Snoop + Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

With Kiki revealed, tap the hasty Snoop to copy itself. Each new Snoop token has haste and the same copy ability. Use the newest untapped copy repeatedly to make as many Snoops as needed.

++
Conspicuous Snoop + Goblin Recruiter + Mogg Fanatic

Use the last Snoop's copied Kiki ability to copy the original Recruiter. The token Recruiter puts Fanatic on top. Every Snoop now gains Fanatic's sacrifice-for-one-damage ability. Sacrifice enough Snoops to kill all three opponents. Stack every needed sacrifice before resolving other effects that change the library top. Keep Recruiter on the battlefield until that final copy; having Fanatic in hand does not grant its ability.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

Copy Crafter, champion Kiki with the new hasty copy, tap that copy for three damage, then sacrifice it to Prospector. Kiki returns untapped with haste. Repeat for lethal damage and red mana. The original Crafter must champion a different Goblin or Shaman first; do not accidentally exile the only required outlet.

Goblin Warchief

Supplies haste and reduces only generic costs of Goblin spells. It makes Muxus cheaper, but cannot reduce Snoop's two red pips or Kiki's three red pips.

Hexing Squelcher

Makes spells uncounterable and taxes targeting of your other creatures with life. This does not stop removal, exile, sacrifice, triggered counters to abilities, or an opponent paying the ward cost.

Goblin Bombardment

Converts assembled tokens into damage without combat or haste. It is also a free outlet for Crafter copies, replacing Prospector's mana with an additional damage per loop.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three usable lands, functioning acceleration, and either early Goblins plus haste or a tutor-led combo. Mulligan hands made only of payoffs and equipment. City of Traitors provides a burst but sacrifices when you play another land; use it after the lands you need to keep. Cavern protects one chosen creature type, not equipment or rituals.

++
Deflecting Swat + Pyroblast + Red Elemental Blast

Save protection for the spell or removal effect that breaks the winning line. Swat changes targets; it does not counter a sweeper. A blue permanent can be destroyed by a Blast before the combo starts.

Goblin Chirurgeon

Sacrifice a spare Goblin to regenerate a creature before a destruction effect resolves. Regeneration does not stop exile or sacrifice and taps the regenerated creature; account for that if it is the only ready engine.

When the table represents removal, establish an outlet and protection before exposing every piece. Against creature-activation hate, clear the hate first or rebuild with a noncreature token payoff. This Bracket 4 version has no Bracket 3 turn-six waiting rule; agree on the faster game before shuffling.
