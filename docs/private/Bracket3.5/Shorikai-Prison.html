A defensive prison deck by zzwang. Keep Shorikai uncrewed to draw through creature hate, assemble the relevant locks, then finish with artifact abilities, Mechanized Production or Approach of the Second Sun.

Private house Bracket 3.5: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Humility, Rhystic Study, and The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale. The earlier four-Game-Changer exception has been removed. No mass land denial or extra-turn chains. Humility, Forcefield and The Millennium Calendar stay.

Draw and mana
Shorikai, Genesis Engine

pay {1} and tap to draw two, discard one and create a Pilot. Uncrewed, it is an artifact rather than a creature, so Humility and Cursed Totem leave its ability working. Once crewed, Humility removes its abilities and Totem prevents activating them. Stay uncrewed while relying on those effects.

Unwinding Clock

untaps your artifacts during each other player's untap step. With a mana rock available, this supports activations on opposing turns too. Each activation still costs mana.

Bender's Waterskin

supplies mana and untaps during every other player's untap step. It helps fund repeated activations but does not untap Shorikai itself.

Unstoppable Plan

untaps your nonland permanents at your end step, enabling another use of Shorikai, rocks, Keys or Calendar.

Voltaic Key

pay {1} and tap to untap an artifact. Budget another {1} for the Shorikai activation it enables.

Manifold Key

another untap for Shorikai or a finisher. It cannot target itself.

Rhystic Study

independent draw matters when Shorikai is removed or Confinement consumes cards. Keep this engine alongside the commander.

Mystic Remora

another independent draw source; weigh its increasing upkeep against developing your board.

There are 37 lands plus three spell/land MDFCs and eleven mana rocks. Ten rocks cost at most two mana. Maze of Ith and The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale produce no mana; the other 35 lands supply or find mana before MDFCs. The basics are four Plains and three Islands. Budget {1}{W} for each Luminarch Ascension Angel alongside development, Shorikai activations and creature upkeep payments.

Padeem and Humility
Padeem, Consul of Innovation

gives your artifacts hexproof against opponents while allowing you to target them with Keys, Clever Concealment or Mechanized Production. It protects Shorikai, mana rocks and artifact finishers, but normally not Padeem itself or the Production Aura. Hexproof does not stop untargeted removal.

Padeem draws one card on your upkeep if you control an artifact with the greatest mana value among all artifacts on the battlefield; ties count. That condition must hold both as the upkeep begins and when the trigger resolves. Solitary Confinement skips the draw step, not this draw; you can put Padeem's trigger above Confinement's upkeep trigger to draw before discarding. The draw is conditional, so keep other engines available.

Humility

removes creature abilities and sets base power/toughness to 1/1. This switches off Padeem's artifact protection and upkeep trigger regardless of which entered first. Both are deliberately retained: use Padeem while Humility is absent, then prioritize Humility when suppressing creatures matters more. Later effects granting abilities or changing stats can still matter.

Cursed Totem

stops activated abilities of creatures, including creature mana abilities. Padeem's static protection and triggered draw work through Totem alone; uncrewed Shorikai also keeps working.

Torpor Orb

creatures entering do not cause abilities to trigger. It does not stop Padeem's static protection or upkeep trigger, or suppress Rest in Peace's own entry trigger.

Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow

Torpor Orb suppresses its removal trigger, and Humility independently removes that creature ability. The land face remains useful under those effects.

Defensive layers
Moat

prevents creatures without flying from attacking. With Humility removing flying, it stops ordinary combat against you and your planeswalkers. Removal or later ability-granting effects can change this; it is not protection from every win condition.

The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale

gives each creature an upkeep trigger requiring its controller to pay {1} or destroy that creature. It pressures creature-heavy boards without casting a spell. Your Pilots, Padeem and Witch Enchanter are taxed too; let unneeded Pilots die instead of spending mana on them. Shorikai avoids the trigger when uncrewed as your upkeep begins. This destroys rather than sacrifices, so indestructible creatures can survive nonpayment. Totem and Torpor Orb do not stop these upkeep triggers.

With Humility and an ordinary, noncreature Tabernacle, entry order matters: Tabernacle entering after Humility grants the upkeep ability to the 1/1 creatures; Humility entering after Tabernacle removes that ability while both remain. New creatures entering do not change the relative order of these two effects. Prefer Humility first, then Tabernacle when you want both effects operating. Tabernacle has no mana ability and consumes your land play.

Solitary Confinement

prevents all damage to you and gives you shroud. It skips your draw step and requires a discard each upkeep. Shorikai, Rhystic and Remora still draw because only the draw step is skipped. You may activate an untapped Shorikai in response to the upkeep trigger, then discard as it resolves.

One ordinary Shorikai activation draws two and discards one; Confinement's upkeep consumes another card. That maintains the shield but nets zero cards before other plays. Clock, Keys, Plan and independent draw keep you progressing. Without a working draw source, treat Confinement as temporary. Letting it go during upkeep allows your normal draw step that turn.

Confinement does not protect your permanents, stop untargeted life loss or prevent alternate wins.

Energy Field

prevents damage to you from sources you do not control. A card entering your graveyard causes you to sacrifice it; Rest in Peace replaces those graveyard entries with exile, sustaining the shield. It does not prevent life loss.

Forcefield

retained as backup. Pay {1} to reduce the next combat damage to you from an unblocked creature of your choice to one. It does not protect your planeswalkers or cover every attacker with one activation.

Ghostly Prison

taxes each creature attacking you by {2}. It does not tax attacks on your planeswalkers.

Propaganda

a second attack tax that stacks with Prison. It likewise protects you rather than your planeswalkers.

Greater Auramancy

gives your OTHER enchantments shroud, protecting them from targeted removal while blocking your own targeting too. It does not give itself shroud or protect an unenchanted Padeem.

Clever Concealment

phases out eligible nonland permanents you control. It cannot target enchantments already protected by Auramancy's shroud. It can still protect eligible artifacts, planeswalkers, Pilots, Padeem and Auramancy itself. Padeem's hexproof does not prevent your own targeting. Phasing Auramancy does not retroactively make an illegal target selection legal.

Out of Time

phases out creatures, including your Pilots and Padeem, while preserving uncrewed Shorikai and the noncreature prison. Padeem's abilities stop applying while phased out.

Supreme Verdict

the uncounterable creature reset. It destroys your Pilots and Padeem too; uncrewed Shorikai survives.

Vanquish the Horde

destroys all creatures, with its {6}{W}{W} cost reduced by {1} for each creature on the battlefield, including your Pilots and opponents' tokens. With six or more creatures it costs {W}{W} before other cost effects. Keep Shorikai uncrewed so it survives. Padeem and Pilots are destroyed too; indestructible creatures survive. Unlike Verdict, this spell can be countered.

Luminarch Ascension
Luminarch Ascension

two mana. At each opponent's end step, if you didn't lose life that turn, it gets a quest counter — behind this prison that is usually three a round, so it is live by your second turn cycle. At four counters, {1}{W} makes a 4/4 flying Angel, as often as you can pay. It is a finisher that needs no creature on the battlefield until you choose to make one, so your sweepers never cost you anything.

Solitary Confinement prevents all damage to you, City of Brass included, so only paying life can stop the counters. While you are charging it, don't crack Flooded Strand, Marsh Flats, Polluted Delta or Windswept Heath, tap Mana Confluence, or pay for Hallowed Fountain on an opponent's turn.

Humility turns the Angels into vanilla 1/1s without flying, and then Moat stops them attacking. Make and swing with Angels while Humility is off the table, or win another way while it's on. The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale charges {1} for each Angel every upkeep.

Spell restrictions
Rule of Law

each player may cast one spell per turn. A spell on your turn consumes your allowance for protecting it with another spell that turn.

Arcane Laboratory

redundancy for the same one-spell limit.

Teferi, Time Raveler

limits opponents to casting spells during their own main phases with an empty stack. This protects your Approach casts from opposing counterspells, but not from activated or triggered abilities. His -3 can bounce an opposing artifact, creature or enchantment, or return your own Mindlock Orb to reopen searches without casting a spell.

Searches, graveyards and abilities
Mindlock Orb

stops everyone's searches, including your four fetches, Idyllic Tutor, Whir and Tezzeret's search. Fetch and tutor before deploying it. Shorikai and Fomori Vault can still draw or look at cards without searching.

Grafdigger's Cage

stops creature cards entering from graveyards/libraries and spells being cast from those zones. It does not stop all graveyard abilities or noncreature artifact/enchantment reanimation.

Rest in Peace

exiles graveyards and replaces future graveyard entries with exile, including yours. It supports Energy Field and the Helm win. Discarding still triggers Monument even when the card goes to exile.

Liquimetal Torque

primarily a mana rock. Its other ability can temporarily make one of your nonland permanents an artifact for Padeem's protection while Padeem is active.

Win conditions
Helm of Obedience

with Rest in Peace active, X=1 exiles an opponent's entire library. They normally lose on their next attempted draw, not immediately. Repeat for each opponent using paid activations and the untap engine.

The Millennium Calendar

gains counters from permanents untapped during YOUR untap step. Pay {2} and tap it to double its counters. At 1,000 or more its trigger sacrifices it and makes each opponent lose 1,000 life. Clock and Plan give extra activation opportunities, not natural counter triggers on opponents' untaps.

From fifteen counters, seven doublings reach 1,920. Those activations cost fourteen mana plus the required untaps. The legend rule puts an extra legendary permanent into the graveyard rather than sacrificing it; a fresh Calendar copy does not inherit counters.

Monument to Endurance

each discard offers a mode not chosen that turn. The drain hits EACH opponent for three once per turn. Discarding on all four turns in a normal round can drain each opponent for twelve; more discards in one turn cannot repeat that mode. Confinement's upkeep discard supplies a trigger too.

These artifact abilities can win under Rule of Law and Arcane Laboratory once deployed. Choose a finish you can fund and protect; assembling a prison alone does not guarantee the game.

Mechanized Production

enchant a nonlegendary artifact such as Monument to Endurance or Sol Ring. Each of your upkeeps creates one copy, then checks whether you control at least eight artifacts sharing a name. Those eight need not match the enchanted artifact: eight Treasure tokens from Monument also qualify. Copies of Monument each track their own modes, multiplying your draw, Treasure generation and drain when you discard.

Save Treasures when pursuing this finish; spending them can leave you below eight. The win is checked during Production's upkeep ability, not immediately when you reach eight artifacts. Shorikai's Pilots are not artifacts and do not count. Copying legendary Shorikai, Calendar or Mox Opal normally forces you to keep only one under the legend rule, so they are poor accumulation targets. This replaces Chain of Vapor's cheap interaction with a slower engine and another way to win through the one-spell limits.

Approach of the Second Sun

an additional noncombat finish. The first resolution normally gains seven life and places it seventh from the top; draw back to it with Shorikai and your other engines, then cast it from your hand again and resolve it to win. Solitary Confinement does not stop Shorikai's draw ability. Budget the full {6}{W} for each cast.

Rule of Law and Arcane Laboratory mean casting the two Approaches on different turns, and normally prevent casting a protection spell on the same turn. Establish Teferi before the winning turn to block opposing counterspells. Avoid shuffling away Approach's known position after the first resolution.

Tutors and sequencing
Whir of Invention

searches an artifact directly onto the battlefield at instant speed, with improvise helping pay generic mana. Cage permits noncreature artifacts, but Mindlock Orb prevents the search.

Idyllic Tutor

finds Humility, Moat, Confinement, Rest in Peace, Rule of Law, Mechanized Production or Luminarch Ascension, provided Orb is absent.

Tezzeret the Seeker

untaps up to two artifacts or searches an artifact onto the battlefield. Orb prevents the search. His artifact-animation ultimate exposes your board to Humility, Totem and creature removal.

Otawara, Soaring City

channel can return your own Mindlock Orb when you need to reopen searches, or bounce an opposing artifact, creature, enchantment or planeswalker. Uncrewed Shorikai does not count as a legendary creature for the discount; Padeem does, even if Humility has removed its abilities.

Keep functional mana, a draw engine and defense suited to the table. Establish sustainable draw before leaning on Confinement. Use fetches and tutors before Orb, budget one spell per turn under Rule of Law effects, and decide whether Padeem's artifact protection or Humility's creature suppression matters more at the current stage.
