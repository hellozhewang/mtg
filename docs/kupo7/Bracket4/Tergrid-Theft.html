An optimized Bracket 4 theft and lock deck led by Tergrid, God of Fright. Bracket 4 removes the Game Changer cap (running 15 Game Changers) and lifts the mass land denial ban, transforming Tergrid into an explosive stax-and-theft powerhouse. Fast mana powers Tergrid out on turn two or three, followed immediately by whole-table discard, symmetrical sacrifice, and hard locks like Contamination and Braids, Cabal Minion that prevent opponents from ever producing colored mana or maintaining permanents. Wins via stolen armies, Bloodchief Ascension + Mindcrank, or Bolas's Citadel storm.

The theft and hard locks
Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern

Steals nontoken permanents opponents sacrifice and permanent cards they discard, including lands. Every edict and wheel becomes an overwhelming board-state swing in your favor.

Contamination

Symmetrical lock that forces every land to produce {B} instead of any other type or amount. Tergrid constantly feeds its upkeep cost with stolen creatures, locking all non-black opponents completely out of colored mana.

Braids, Cabal Minion

Forces each player to sacrifice an artifact, creature, or land at upkeep. Tergrid steals each sacrificed permanent, fueling your board and feeding Braids's trigger on your own upkeep.

Pox

Each player loses a third of their life, discards a third of their hand, and sacrifices a third of their creatures and lands. With Tergrid on the board, it strips the table of resources and hands you a third of everyone's permanents.

Smallpox

Two-mana disruption that forces a discard, creature sacrifice, and land sacrifice. Steals up to nine permanents across three opponents on turn two or three.

Dark Deal

Forces each player to discard their hand and redraw minus one. Tergrid intercepts every permanent card discarded, yielding an instant army of ten to fifteen lands, rocks, and creatures.

Mindslicer

Death trigger empties all hands. Tergrid steals every permanent in opponents' hands while you untap with a full board.

Opposition Agent

Flashes in to control opponents while they search their libraries, exiling their best cards and shutting off fetchlands and tutors completely.

Orcish Bowmasters

Flashes in to punish draw steps, wheels, and cantrips, wiping out utility creatures while amassing an Orc army.

Rankle's Prank

Modular discard, sacrifice, and life loss that Tergrid turns into a massive multi-permanent theft swing.

Rankle, Master of Pranks

Repeatable combat trigger forcing discard, sacrifice, and draw on demand.

Tutors and combo finishes
++
Demonic Tutor + Vampiric Tutor + Imperial Seal

The premiere one- and two-mana tutors that assemble whatever lock piece or combo element is needed.

Beseech the Mirror

Four-mana tutor with bargain; sacrifice a stolen artifact or enchantment to tutor and cast Bolas's Citadel or Contamination directly.

Diabolic Intent

Two-mana tutor by sacrificing a creature, finding Dark Deal or a missing combo piece.

Wishclaw Talisman

Two-mana tutor to assemble the winning line on your turn.

+
Bloodchief Ascension + Mindcrank

Compact two-card infinite combo. With three quest counters on Ascension, any card put into an opponent's graveyard deals 2 damage and heals you for 2, which triggers Mindcrank to mill, repeating infinitely until all opponents are eliminated.

++
Bolas's Citadel + Sensei's Divining Top + Aetherflux Reservoir

Storm finish that draws your entire library by paying 1 life per Top activation and gaining compounding life off Reservoir, allowing you to cast your entire deck and blast the table.

Necropotence

Pay life to draw fifteen to twenty cards into hand, guaranteeing all fast mana, tutors, and protection are assembled.

The One Ring

Protection from everything for a full turn cycle while drawing an escalating stream of cards.

Protection and interaction
Defense Grid

Shuts down opposing countermagic and instant-speed interaction on your turn by taxing all off-turn spells by three generic mana.

Imp's Mischief

Deflects counterspells or targeted removal away from Tergrid or Bolas's Citadel.

+
Deadly Rollick + Snuff Out

Zero-mana instant removal to answer problematic hatebears without slowing your combo.

+
Toxic Deluge + Blasphemous Edict

Sweepers that reset the board if opponents develop faster than your edicts.

+
Feed the Swarm + Withering Torment

Targeted enchantment removal to destroy Rest in Peace, Leyline of the Void, or Stony Silence.

Lightning Greaves

Grants haste and shroud to protect Tergrid immediately upon entry.

++
Kaya's Ghostform + Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire + Not Dead After All

One-mana insurance that returns Tergrid if an opponent attempts to destroy or exile her.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep hands with fast mana (Dark Ritual, Chrome Mox, Sol Ring, Mana Vault) and at least one tutor or high-impact theft enabler. In Bracket 4, a slow hand without acceleration will be outpaced.

Deploy Tergrid early only if you have protection (Defense Grid, Imp's Mischief, Greaves, or Kaya's Ghostform) or an immediate follow-up spell (Dark Deal, Smallpox, Pox) on the same turn.

Against non-black decks, aggressively tutor for Contamination or Braids, Cabal Minion once Tergrid or a recurring creature (Bloodghast, Ophiomancer) is active. The mana lock is almost impossible for three opponents to break.

Use Urza's Saga to tutor Sensei's Divining Top (completing the Citadel combo) or Mana Vault / Sol Ring / Lotus Petal for acceleration.
