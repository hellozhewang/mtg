This is the hard-lock version of Arbiter, and the difference from a normal prison deck is that it is trying to stop the game entirely rather than slow it down. Knowledge Pool plus any one-spell-per-turn effect means nobody casts anything ever again, and Helm of Obedience with Rest in Peace exiles a library at instant speed. Every tax in the deck is one-sided because Arbiter refunds you.

The commander and the refund
Grand Arbiter Augustin IV

your white and blue spells each cost {1} less; theirs all cost {1} more. Nearly every card in the deck is white or blue, so every symmetrical tax below becomes free for you and doubled for them.

Sphere of Resistance

everything costs {1} more. With Arbiter out your spells are back at printed cost and theirs are up two.

Thalia, Guardian of Thraben

the same, aimed at noncreature spells, on a first-striking body.

Trinisphere

everything costs at least three. It is brutal against the cheap interaction that would otherwise answer the lock.

Aura of Silence

their artifacts and enchantments cost two more, and it sacrifices to destroy one.

The lock itself
Knowledge Pool

every spell cast from hand is exiled and its caster may instead cast a card exiled with the Pool. On its own that is chaos; with any of the three cards below, it is a full stop.

Rule of Law

one spell per turn each. They cast from hand, it gets exiled, and the one-spell limit means the free replacement cannot be cast. Nobody plays a spell again.

Arcane Laboratory

the second copy.

Eidolon of Rhetoric

the third, on a 1/4 that blocks.

Archon of Emeria

the fourth, on a flier, and their nonbasics arrive tapped.

Deploy the one-spell effect BEFORE Knowledge Pool. The other order gives everyone one free turn to empty their hands through the Pool.

Sanctum Prelate

name the number the table is built around. Most of the format's interaction is two or three.

Lavinia, Azorius Renegade

they cannot cast noncreature spells above their land count, and anything cast for free is countered outright.

Drannith Magistrate

no casting from graveyards, exile or the command zone — which also stops Knowledge Pool's replacement casts, if you want the Pool to be purely a prison.

Cursed Totem

no creature activated abilities.

Torpor Orb

no enters triggers, which is how a table usually escapes a lock.

Grafdigger's Cage

nothing enters from a graveyard or a library.

Containment Priest

anything entering without being cast is exiled.

Disruptor Flute

flash, name a card, and it taxes both casting and activating it.

How you actually win
Helm of Obedience

with Rest in Peace on the battlefield, activate for one and the target opponent's ENTIRE library is exiled. Repeat for each opponent. Under a full lock, nobody can respond.

Rest in Peace

the other half, and it is not a dead card on its own — it blanks every graveyard deck at the table and makes Energy Field permanent.

Energy Field

no damage to you at all from sources you do not control, and with Rest in Peace out it never gets sacrificed.

Luminarch Ascension

behind the wall you rarely lose life, so it turns on quickly and makes a 4/4 flier every turn as a backup plan.

Court of Grace

a flier every upkeep and the monarch's card, which is how a locked-down game still produces card advantage.

The wall
Moat

creatures without flying cannot attack at all.

Ghostly Prison

taxes what Moat does not stop.

Crawlspace

caps any swing at two creatures.

Blind Obedience

their creatures and artifacts enter tapped, and extort drains on every spell you cast.

Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills

another attack tax once you control three artifacts or legends, which is immediate.

Clever Concealment

usually free with convoke, and it phases the whole lock out of a wrath.

Interaction
Mana Drain

counter their answer, and the mana deploys two more lock pieces.

Dovin's Veto

uncounterable.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

Swan Song

one mana for a wrath or an enchantment.

Bilbo's Gambit

bounces a spell, and gifted it stops everyone casting for the rest of the turn.

Swords to Plowshares

one mana, exile.

Generous Gift

any permanent, at instant speed.

Supreme Verdict

uncounterable, and your board is mostly enchantments and artifacts.

Heliod's Intervention

X artifacts and enchantments — the answer to somebody else's prison.

Aven Mindcensor

flash him onto a fetchland or tutor.

Archivist of Oghma

a card and a life every time anyone searches.

Finding the pieces
Idyllic Tutor

any enchantment — Rule of Law, Rest in Peace, Moat.

Fabricate

any artifact — Knowledge Pool, Helm of Obedience, Torpor Orb.

Esper Sentinel

taxes their first noncreature spell each turn.

Rhystic Study

nobody pays the {1} while they are already paying for everything else.

Mystic Remora

the best card in the deck on turns one through four.

Gleaming Splendor

Treasures off their draws, and it can force draws itself.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a rock and a tax piece. Sol Ring or a Signet into Thalia or Sphere of Resistance on turn two is what buys the turns to assemble the real lock.

Sequence taxes first, hard locks second. Sphere of Resistance and Thalia hurt everyone a little; Rule of Law and Knowledge Pool hurt them completely — and if you land the hard lock first, they untap and remove it with mana the taxes would have eaten.

Helm of Obedience

it is the kill, but it is also a lightning rod. Hold it until Rest in Peace is already down and the lock is up, then activate all three opponents in one turn.

Remember the lock binds you too. Once Knowledge Pool and Rule of Law are both out, you get one spell a turn as well — so deploy everything you need first, and win with an activated ability rather than a spell.
