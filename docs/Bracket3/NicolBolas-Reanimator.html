Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

The commander is a useful four-mana play, not a seven-mana transformation project. Establish a graveyard, reanimate a threat, and reuse its entry effects while protecting the advantage. Transform Bolas when the immediate planeswalker activation is worth seven mana; this deck can win without ever doing that.

Bolas has a plan
Nicol Bolas, the Ravager // Nicol Bolas, the Arisen

a four-mana flying threat that makes every opponent discard once. He pressures life totals and enables the free commander-dependent interaction while reanimation does the heavy lifting. His presence is not required for the deck's main engine.

+
Nicol Bolas, the Ravager // Nicol Bolas, the Arisen + Displacer Kitten

noncreature spells can blink your creature-side Bolas to strip one card from each opponent. Blinking the transformed commander returns him front-face-up as a creature; it does not refresh his planeswalker loyalty.

Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God

repeatable cards and resource pressure at five mana, plus removal when an attacker threatens your engine. Its ultimate only defeats opponents without a legendary creature or planeswalker, so it is a conditional bonus rather than your primary win.

Nicol Bolas, God-Pharaoh

the main expensive flavor payoff: steal spells or remove a problem threat after stabilizing. Its hand-exile ability does not trigger discard effects. Reanimating it with Breach or the saga avoids paying seven.

Dark Intimations

makes all three opponents sacrifice and discard while recovering a creature or planeswalker to your hand and drawing. Its graveyard trigger adds loyalty to a Bolas planeswalker you CAST; transforming your commander or reanimating a planeswalker does not cast one.

The Eldest Reborn

a slower but coherent Bolas card: opponents sacrifice, then discard, then you steal a creature or planeswalker from any graveyard. It is a control and recovery play, not the spell to cast when you need immediate reanimation.

Deliver Unto Evil

recover four cards when you control a Bolas planeswalker. Creature-side Ravager is insufficient. Without a Bolas planeswalker, target four useful cards so the opponent cannot leave you with nothing.

Liliana, Death's Majesty

makes a Zombie and mills two while defending your Bolas planeswalkers, or immediately returns one of your own creature threats. That reanimated creature becomes a Zombie for The Scarab God. Her reanimation ability cannot return a planeswalker.

The Scarab God

the thematic reserve engine when ordinary reanimation gets disrupted. Its activation can use any graveyard at instant speed, converting a dead threat into a 4/4 Zombie copy with the original abilities. Its upkeep drain grows with the Zombies you actually control.

Develop before taking over
++
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet + Fellwar Stone

the fastest general acceleration here. Prioritize colored production for the commander and the triple-black spells; colorless mana alone will not cast Dragon-God.

++
Dimir Signet + Rakdos Signet + Izzet Signet

complete the cheap ramp package with the three Talismans. There are nine rocks, all costing one or two mana, plus Dark Ritual. Signets require a mana input and therefore cannot produce mana from an otherwise empty mana pool.

Dark Ritual

speeds out a meaningful engine or lets you reanimate while preserving interaction mana. It is a one-shot resource, so do not spend it simply to transform Bolas without a strong follow-up.

Black Market Connections

select Treasure when you need development, a card when you need resources, and a creature when you need a blocker or Victimize sacrifice. It can take six life for all modes; do not automatically select everything.

Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki

supplies an attacking Treasure-maker, then discards large threats for new cards, then becomes a reusable copying engine. The Treasure requires attacking, and the Reflection must be able to tap before copying.

Bitter Reunion

discards a creature for later reanimation while replacing itself with two cards. Save its sacrifice activation until after your new army is present, because creatures entering after the activation resolves do not gain that haste.

Overlord of the Balemurk

the two-mana impending mode mills four and can recover a non-Avatar creature or planeswalker to hand. It does not reanimate anything. Once it is a creature, attacks repeat the setup and recovery.

+
Ripples of Undeath + Frantic Search

Ripples supplies a steady graveyard while buying back a useful milled card when you can spare the mana and life. Frantic Search puts stranded threats into the graveyard while untapping lands to cast the reanimation spell.

Put the right threat in the graveyard
→
Entomb → Archon of Cruelty

the default early target when an opponent has an important creature or planeswalker and you want removal, cards, life, and an attacking threat in one play.

→
Entomb → Vilis, Broker of Blood

the draw-heavy choice when you have Reanimate, enough life to lose eight, and a plan to protect the Demon. Its mandatory draws can become dangerous with a nearly empty library.

→++
Buried Alive → Twilight Diviner + Archon of Cruelty + Gray Merchant of Asphodel

a flexible package of copy engine, disruptive threat, and drain finisher. Recover Diviner before the next reanimation if you want a copied threat; merely putting all three into the graveyard does not win.

→
Spellseeker → Reanimate

finds the cheapest way to return the threat you already placed in a graveyard. It can instead find Entomb when you have the reanimation spell but lack a target.

→
Spellseeker → Mana Drain

the defensive search when you already have pressure and must stop a sweeper or opposing win. Swan Song is preferable when you can only hold up one mana.

→
Demonic Tutor → Sneak Attack

choose this route when your threats are in hand or graveyard hate makes ordinary reanimation unreliable.

→
Demonic Tutor → Bolas's Citadel

fetch after building life and some ability to manipulate the top of your library. Searching for it with no spare life or setup can produce another expensive card stranded in hand.

→
Demonic Tutor → Saw in Half

finds immediate extra entry triggers from an established Archon or Gray Merchant. It is often a faster finish than adding another seven-mana threat.

Bring them back efficiently
+
Reanimate + Vilis, Broker of Blood

Reanimate returns Vilis before you lose eight life, so Vilis sees the loss and draws eight. You must still survive the life payment; cards in hand do not rescue you from reaching zero life.

++
Animate Dead + Dance of the Dead + Necromancy

cheap ways to steal from any graveyard or recover your own threat. Removing the Aura makes you sacrifice its creature. Dance returns it tapped and requires upkeep mana to untap it; Necromancy cast outside sorcery timing is sacrificed at the next cleanup step.

Persist

returns a nonlegendary creature from your own graveyard with a -1/-1 counter. It works on Archon, Agent, or Gray Merchant, but not on Vilis, Bolas, or The Scarab God.

Victimize

turns a spare creature into two bodies. Targets are chosen before you sacrifice, so the sacrificed creature cannot be one of the two targets. Both creatures return tapped; haste does not untap them.

+
Living Death + Soul-Guide Lantern

exile opponents' graveyards first when their rebuild would otherwise be stronger. Living Death then sacrifices the battlefield and returns the creatures that were in graveyards before it resolved. Creatures sacrificed during its resolution stay dead.

Breach the Multiverse

fills every graveyard and takes one creature or planeswalker from each player, including you. You choose after milling and the spell does not target those cards. It can return Bolas planeswalkers while building a threatening creature board.

+
Sneak Attack + Disciple of Bolas

put a large creature in for one red, get its entry effect and attack, then use Disciple before the end step to turn it into life and cards. The sacrificed creature becomes fuel for the next reanimation spell.

Reuse the threat instead of drawing another one
Twilight Diviner

surveils on entry and copies one qualifying creature entering from, or cast from, a graveyard each turn. Copies are tokens, so they do not retrigger Diviner. Living Death returns cards from exile and does NOT qualify; The Scarab God's newly created tokens do not qualify either.

+
Displacer Kitten + Archon of Cruelty

cheap noncreature spells retrigger Archon's entry ability. Each trigger affects one opponent, so distribute the pressure according to who has the most threatening board.

+
Displacer Kitten + Agent of Treachery

repeated entries take additional permanents, and the theft does not end when Agent leaves. Blink your own Agent, not the stolen creature: Kitten returns cards under their OWNER'S control. Concentrate theft on threats and engines, not a repeatable land-denial plan.

+
Displacer Kitten + The Eldest Reborn

blinking the saga restarts chapter I. To preserve a chapter III reanimation and restart the saga, cast an instant such as Frantic Search with chapter III on the stack; Kitten blinks the saga while the old chapter III still resolves independently.

+
Displacer Kitten + Liliana, Death's Majesty

after using a loyalty ability, a noncreature spell can blink Liliana into a fresh object with starting loyalty and another legal activation. The same works with Dragon-God and God-Pharaoh, but not with the transformed commander as explained above.

+
Sneak Attack + Displacer Kitten

after attacking with a creature you own that Sneak Attack put in, blink it using a noncreature spell. The returned card is a new object, so Sneak Attack's delayed sacrifice does not find it. Token copies disappear when blinked.

+
Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki + Gray Merchant of Asphodel

Reflection can copy the nonlegendary Merchant repeatedly over multiple turns. Copies retain its two black mana symbols and produce full entry triggers despite being temporary tokens.

End the game
+
Archon of Cruelty + Saw in Half

destroying a normal 6/6 Archon creates two 3/3 copies, each with the full entry and attack triggers. That supplies two immediate triggers and doubles future attack triggers. The tokens do not inherit haste granted to the old creature.

+
Gray Merchant of Asphodel + Saw in Half

both Merchant tokens enter together and contribute four black devotion before either entry trigger resolves. With Bolas's Citadel and Dragon-God also present, devotion is ten: the two triggers drain twenty life from EACH opponent and gain the total life lost across the table.

+++
Bolas's Citadel + Sensei's Divining Top + Displacer Kitten + Gray Merchant of Asphodel

the four-permanent drain engine. Tap Top to draw and put it on your library, cast it for one life through Citadel, and use Kitten's cast trigger to blink your nontoken Merchant. Drain and gain life, resolve Top, and repeat. With only these permanents your devotion is five, so eight repetitions drain forty from each opponent. You consume a library card per repetition; this is a repeatable lethal sequence with finite cards, not unlimited draws.

+
Bolas's Citadel + Sensei's Divining Top

without Kitten and Merchant, this pair only converts life into cards and helps move past lands. It is not an automatic win. Citadel still follows normal timing and land-play limits.

+
Sepulchral Primordial + Bitter Reunion

recover up to one creature from each opponent's graveyard, then grant the newly assembled army haste. Primordial is weaker early when those graveyards are empty; choose Archon for the first reanimation in that situation.

Cyclonic Rift

overload when your reanimated or stolen army can punish the open battlefield, or when an opposing engine must be reset. It also removes graveyard hate long enough to take a decisive turn.

Bolas's Citadel

the sacrifice ability drains each opponent for ten when you can spare ten nonland permanents. Use it to finish damaged opponents, not to sacrifice your entire engine for a nonlethal ten. Citadel itself can be among the ten.

Protect the advantage
++
Mana Drain + Swan Song + An Offer You Can't Refuse

hold a cheap answer once your first threat is productive. Drain can fund a later hard-cast Bolas; Offer gives the opponent Treasures, so reserve it for consequential spells.

Force of Negation

free only when it is not your turn and you can pitch another blue card. On your own reanimation turn, budget three mana or rely on your cheaper counters and Swat.

+
Deflecting Swat + Deadly Rollick

both are free while either face of your commander is on the battlefield. Swat changes targets and cannot stop a nontargeting wipe; Rollick exiles a creature that cannot safely be allowed to die and recur.

+
Feed the Swarm + Chaos Warp

preserve these for permanent types your creature engines cannot readily handle. Feed handles an enchantment at a life cost, while Warp can answer an artifact or enchantment at instant speed.

Toxic Deluge

the efficient reset when opponents get ahead. It kills your creatures too if X is large enough. With Vilis present, paying life creates a draw trigger before the sweeper resolves; count your remaining library.

+
Lightning Greaves + Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire

Greaves provides immediate attacks and targeting protection, but its shroud also prevents your own Saw in Half or blink target. Move it onto another creature before those plays; you cannot simply unequip it if no other creature is available. Rebirth returns under the owner's control, so use it on your own cards rather than opponents' creatures you stole.

+
Baleful Strix + Mystic Remora

Strix replaces itself and deters attacks against your walkers; Remora refills while opponents develop. Stop paying cumulative upkeep when it prevents your own important setup turn.

Recovery and the costs of the theme
Disciple of Bolas

the thematic draw-and-life engine makes reanimation sustainable and supplies life for Citadel. Sacrificing an opponent's stolen creature puts the card in that opponent's graveyard, where your any-graveyard reanimation can steal it again.

+
The Scarab God + Twilight Diviner

Diviner's token copies can be powerful, but a copy of a legendary creature still invokes the legend rule. Scarab God's copies are also legendary if the original was; its 4/4 Zombie modification does not remove legendary status.

+
Bolas's Citadel + Vilis, Broker of Blood

every life payment to cast off the top triggers more mandatory draws. You have excellent access to cards but can run out of life or library unexpectedly. Do not attempt a long Top loop without counting both.

The Eldest Reborn and Dark Intimations are deliberate theme commitments with useful multiplayer effects, but neither replaces one-mana reanimation. Use them once the early mana and graveyard work is done.

Soul-Guide Lantern

can clear opponents' graveyards without clearing your own, but doing so removes theft targets. Exile only the dangerous card with its entry when you want to preserve other creatures to steal.

Graveyard hate is the main strategic weakness. Remove it, play through Sneak Attack from hand, or use rocks to hard-cast your midrange threats and planeswalkers. Do not spend every reanimation spell into a visible graveyard-exile activation.

What to keep and how to sequence it

Keep two or three lands, access to black plus the other colors, an early rock or setup spell, and either a reanimation line or independent card draw. A hand of three expensive threats, two planeswalkers, and two lands is a mulligan despite its impressive late game.

+
Faithless Looting + Reanimate

a simple hand plan: put the best large creature into the graveyard, then recover it when you can protect the play or profit immediately from its entry. Looting itself loses a card overall, so use its graveyard setup rather than treating it as generic card advantage.

++
Underground Sea + Blood Crypt + Sunken Ruins

prioritize early black and full Grixis access; later, assemble triple black for Dragon-God and Citadel. There are 35 lands plus two spell/land cards. Only three of the dedicated lands always enter tapped; the two basics remain as fetchable insurance against basic-search effects.

Nicol Bolas, the Ravager // Nicol Bolas, the Arisen

turn-two ramp can give a turn-three commander, but an early reanimated threat can be better. Transform only when you can immediately draw needed cards, remove a threat, or reanimate something worth the seven mana. His ultimate leaves one card in the target player's library; it does not immediately eliminate them.

Aim to establish a meaningful threat on turns three through five and convert the advantage into a turn-six-to-eight finish. Those are planning targets, not measured guarantees. Protect your first productive engine instead of tapping out for another expensive card every turn.

This stays in Bracket 3 with Bolas's Citadel, Cyclonic Rift, and Demonic Tutor as its three Game Changers. There are no intended early two-card wins, chained extra turns, or mass land denial. The four-piece Citadel line is a late-game closer backed by ordinary reanimation, theft, and combat; the commander and the Bolas planeswalkers remain central to the deck's identity.
