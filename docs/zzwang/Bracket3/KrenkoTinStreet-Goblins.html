Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin makes Goblins equal to its POWER every time it attacks — after it gets its +1/+1 counter. So every lord, equipment and pump in this deck is also a token spell: Stoneforge Masterwork alone adds +1 for every other Goblin you control, and ten Goblins become ten more each attack. Attack with Krenko every turn, and give it extra combats.

Two rules decide most games. Krenko has to attack to do anything, so haste and protection come before payoffs. And Lightning Greaves gives shroud, which stops YOUR equip abilities too — put Swiftfoot Boots on Krenko, and save Greaves for creatures you won't need to target.

The commander
Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin

three mana. Each attack: a +1/+1 counter, then Goblins equal to its power. Order your attack triggers so Shared Animosity resolves first — its bonus counts toward the Goblins Krenko makes.

Swiftfoot Boots

hexproof and haste for Krenko. Hexproof only stops opponents, so you can still equip everything else onto it.

Mithril Coat

flash, indestructible, and it attaches itself to Krenko when it enters — the answer to a destroy spell or lethal damage in response. It does nothing against exile, bounce or sacrifice.

Commander's Plate

+3/+3 and protection from every colour except red: three more Goblins per attack, and most blockers can't touch it.

Goblin Chirurgeon

sacrifice a Goblin to regenerate Krenko through a destroy effect — and a free sacrifice outlet for the combos.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
+
Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine

the equipped creature connects, the Sword untaps your lands, and five of that mana activates Assault for another combat. Unlimited combats — and Krenko grows and makes more Goblins in every one. Put the Sword on something that will get through: Krenko wearing Commander's Plate is ideal.

++
Aggravated Assault + Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin + Skirk Prospector

no Sword needed. Each extra combat Krenko makes Goblins; in the main phase after it, sacrifice five to Skirk Prospector for {R}{R}{R}{R}{R} and activate Assault again. Once Krenko's power is 5 or more, every combat pays for the next. Phyrexian Altar works too; Ashnod's Altar can't pay the {R}{R} on its own. Combat mana empties, so sacrifice in the main phase.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Combat Celebrant

Kiki copies Celebrant; the hasty copy attacks and exerts, untapping Kiki and adding a combat; in that combat Kiki copies Celebrant again. Unlimited combats, and Krenko attacks in each.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Zealous Conscripts

Kiki copies Conscripts, and the copy untaps Kiki — target Kiki itself. Unlimited hasty 3/3s; Impact Tremors, Purphoros and General Kreat turn them into damage.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Goblin Bombardment

Kiki copies the Crafter; the copy champions Kiki, taps for 3 damage, and is sacrificed to Goblin Bombardment; Kiki returns untapped with haste and repeats. Unlimited 3-damage shots. Skirk Prospector, Goblin Chirurgeon and both Altars also work as the outlet. When the original Crafter enters you must exile another Goblin or Shaman (champion) or sacrifice it.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Thornbite Staff + Goblin Bombardment

the Staff attaches itself to Kiki when Kiki enters after it (equip costs {4} otherwise). Copy any nonlegendary creature, sacrifice a creature, and the death untaps Kiki.

→+
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Conspicuous Snoop

Recruiter puts Kiki on top of your library, and Snoop — which has every activated ability of the Goblin on top — copies itself over and over, each copy hasty. Snoop needs haste or a turn first, and the copies end up tapped, so finish with Impact Tremors, Purphoros, General Kreat or Goblin Bombardment.

++
Conspicuous Snoop + Goblin Recruiter + Mogg Fanatic

the clean finish for the Snoop loop with no payoff out. Make the last copy a copy of Goblin Recruiter; its trigger puts Mogg Fanatic on top, and now every Snoop has Fanatic's ability — sacrifice each one for 1 damage.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Thornbite Staff + Skirk Prospector

equip the Staff to Mob Boss ({4}); it taps for a Goblin per Goblin; sacrifice one to Skirk Prospector, the death untaps it, repeat.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Umbral Mantle + Skirk Prospector

Mob Boss taps for Goblins; sacrifice three to Skirk Prospector for {R}{R}{R}; the Mantle's {3} untaps it. Four Goblins to start and every lap grows.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when
→
Goblin Matron → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

when you already hold Zealous Conscripts, Combat Celebrant, Lightning Crafter or the Staff.

→
Goblin Matron → Goblin Recruiter

when you are two pieces short. Matron gets Recruiter; Recruiter stacks the rest.

→
Goblin Matron → Krenko, Mob Boss

when Thornbite Staff or Umbral Mantle is out, or you want a second token engine.

→
Goblin Matron → Skirk Prospector

when Aggravated Assault or a Kiki loop is ready but nothing sacrifices for free.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

power 2, so it finds Kiki — or Goblin Recruiter, Conspicuous Snoop, Skirk Prospector or Mogg Fanatic.

→
Gamble → Aggravated Assault

the non-Goblin pieces — Assault, the Sword or the Staff (Goblin Engineer also finds the two artifacts). Cast it with a full hand, so the random discard is less likely to hit what you fetched.

→
Goblin Engineer → Sword of Feast and Famine

puts the Sword, Staff or Mantle into your graveyard, then {R}, tap and sacrifice another artifact to put it onto the battlefield. Engineer needs a turn or haste first.

→
Urza's Saga → Stoneforge Masterwork

its last chapter puts an artifact with mana cost {0} or {1} onto the battlefield — Masterwork for a huge Krenko, or Sol Ring or Skullclamp.

→++
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

without Snoop, stack the missing pieces with the first one you need on top. Recruiter only arranges your library — unless Snoop or Muxus reaches it this turn, cast it the turn before you go off.

Muxus, Goblin Grandee

six mana: every Goblin creature costing five or less in your top six goes onto the battlefield. Arrange those six with Goblin Recruiter first and it becomes a combo.

Goblin Ringleader

every Goblin in your top four goes to your hand — the way to cash in a Recruiter stack without Snoop.

Goblin Lackey

a one-drop that, when it connects, puts a Goblin from your hand onto the battlefield — Muxus or Kiki-Jiki on turn two.

Goblin Recruiter setups — what to stack, top card first
→
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

with Conspicuous Snoop able to tap, the Snoop combo; finish it with Mogg Fanatic as described above.

→→→→
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker → Lightning Crafter → Skirk Prospector → Goblin Warchief

then cast Muxus: all four go onto the battlefield, the Crafter champions Recruiter, and Kiki starts the loop at once.

→→→
Goblin Recruiter → Skirk Prospector → Goblin Warchief → Krenko, Mob Boss

with Snoop out, cast them off the top in order — mana, then haste, then a second token engine that taps immediately.

→→→
Goblin Recruiter → Goblin Chieftain → Goblin King → Hobgoblin Bandit Lord

three lords: every one is another Goblin each time Krenko attacks.

Anything that searches your library shuffles it and wrecks the stack — Goblin Matron, Imperial Recruiter, Gamble and Goblin Engineer. Use those before Recruiter, never after.

Every pump is more Goblins
Stoneforge Masterwork

one mana: +1/+1 for every other Goblin you control. On a board of ten, Krenko attacks as an 11-power creature and makes eleven more.

Embercleave

double strike, trample and +1/+1, cheaper for every attacker, at flash speed.

Shared Animosity

+1/+0 for each other attacking Goblin — and it resolves before Krenko's trigger if you put it on the stack second.

Goblin Chieftain

other Goblins +1/+1 and haste.

Goblin King

Goblins +1/+1 and mountainwalk — opponents' Goblins too, and your own Mountains make theirs unblockable against you.

Goblin Trashmaster

other Goblins +1/+1, and a sacrifice outlet that destroys artifacts.

Hobgoblin Bandit Lord

other Goblins +1/+1; {R} and a tap deal damage equal to the Goblins that entered this turn.

Castle Embereth

{1}{R}{R} and a tap: your creatures get +1/+0, Krenko included.

Haste
Goblin Warchief

Goblins cost one less and have haste — Krenko attacks the turn it lands.

Howlsquad Heavy

your other Goblins have haste, and it makes a Goblin at the start of each of your combats.

Goro-Goro, Disciple of Ryusei

{R}: every creature you control gains haste, and with a modified attacker — Krenko, once it has attacked and has a counter — {3}{R}{R} makes a 5/5 flying Dragon.

Arena of Glory

{R}, tap and exert it for {R}{R} — one extra mana — and a creature spell cast with that mana gains haste.

Hall of the Bandit Lord

pay 3 life for colourless mana that gives a creature spell haste.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud. Shroud stops your own equip and attach abilities too, so equip everything else first or keep Greaves off Krenko. On Kiki-Jiki it blocks Zealous Conscripts' untap; the Celebrant and Crafter loops never target Kiki.

Purphoros, God of the Forge

General Kreat, the Boltbringer

Goblin Bombardment

sacrifice a creature for 1 damage — the outlet and the finisher.

Mogg Fanatic

sacrifice it for 1 damage; mostly here to finish the Snoop loop.

Protection and answers

Deflecting Swat

Pyroblast

Red Elemental Blast

Lightning Bolt

Chaos Warp

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep fast mana, a land or two and a way to give Krenko haste or protect it. A turn-two Krenko with Swiftfoot Boots, attacking every turn after, wins games by itself.

Equip before attacking: Masterwork and Plate first, then attack — the tokens use Krenko's power at the moment the trigger resolves.

Go for a combo when the board is answered or stalled. Aggravated Assault is the one to look for: with Krenko already attacking, it usually only needs Skirk Prospector or the Sword.
