Cecily needs eleven cards AFTER her attack-trigger draw, so ten cards before that draw is normally enough. Build a real draw engine before loading up on expensive spells. Elmar rewards your second spell each turn, not attacks: his untap and Clue help you reach the next big turn. Both Cecily, Haunted Mage and Elmar, Ulvenwald Informant belong in the command zone; designate both when importing this list. They share Partner—Friends forever and their combined colour identity is blue, black, red, and green.

Turning a full hand into a free spell
Cecily, Haunted Mage

Attack to draw a card and lose one life, then check whether your hand has at least eleven cards. If it does, cast an instant or sorcery from hand for free during this trigger's resolution, including a sorcery during combat. She never needs to deal combat damage, but you must still protect her from blockers if you want another attack next turn.

Elmar, Ulvenwald Informant

Your second spell each turn untaps a target creature and investigates. Untap Bloom Tender for mana or an attacking Cecily to leave her available as a blocker; untapping her does not remove her from combat. You must pay for the second spell before this untap happens. If the trigger's only target becomes illegal, you get neither the untap nor the Clue.

Harmonic Prodigy

Doubles Cecily's attack trigger because she is a Wizard. With ten cards to start, each trigger's draw can restore the eleventh card after the previous free cast; a draw spell makes this much stronger. It also doubles Archmage Emeritus, Jori En, Ruin Diver, and Storm-Kiln Artist's relevant triggers. Elmar is neither a Wizard nor a Shaman and is not doubled.

Irenicus's Vile Duplication

Makes a nonlegendary flying Cecily for another attack trigger without losing either to the legend rule. The token is not itself a commander and has summoning sickness. Other strong targets include Consecrated Sphinx and Bloom Tender, depending on whether cards or mana are the bottleneck.

Bloom Tender

With itself and Cecily on the battlefield, it produces one mana of each of your four colours. Elmar's trigger can untap it before your second spell resolves, letting you generate another four mana that turn. This is finite: the third and subsequent spells do not trigger Elmar again.

Resonating Lute

Gives every land an ability that produces two mana of one colour for casting instants and sorceries. This supports hard-casting your big spells even without Cecily. That mana cannot pay for creatures, equipment, Clue activations, or other activated abilities. The Lute itself can tap to draw when you have seven or more cards.

Storm-Kiln Artist

Creates Treasure when you cast or copy an instant or sorcery, including Cecily's free cast. Harmonic Prodigy doubles its magecraft trigger. Unlike mana floating during combat, unused Treasure can wait until your second main phase for a finisher.

Reaching eleven without giving opponents new hands
Rhystic Study

A Game Changer chosen because repeated opponent casts can fill your hand while your mana develops. Keep interaction available instead of spending every card as soon as you draw it; the eleven-card threshold is a resource to preserve.

Mystic Remora

An efficient early draw engine while opponents set up. Stop paying cumulative upkeep when it would prevent you from building your own mana or protecting an important turn.

Consecrated Sphinx

The second Game Changer supplies enough cards to switch Cecily on quickly through a normal multiplayer turn cycle. Its draws are optional, which matters when Teferi's Ageless Insight and repeated draw spells threaten to exhaust your library.

Sylvan Library

Fixes draws and converts life into extra cards when that will reach Cecily's threshold. Teferi's Ageless Insight doubles its two additional draws, but Library still asks you to choose only two cards drawn that turn to return or pay for. Watch the life bill alongside Cecily and Toxic Deluge.

Teferi's Ageless Insight

Doubles Cecily's attack draw, Clue draws, and most spell-driven draws. It does not double your first draw in each draw step. Casting it costs a card now, so do not count its future value as cards already in hand.

Archmage Emeritus

Turns each instant or sorcery cast or spell copy into a card. Together with Harmonic Prodigy, ordinary cantrips become real hand growth. Copying a card in a graveyard with Mnemonic Deluge is not itself copying a spell, but casting each resulting copy triggers magecraft.

Jori En, Ruin Diver

Adds an immediate draw to Elmar's second-spell turn, alongside the Clue you can cash later. Harmonic Prodigy makes Jori draw twice. This works on opponents' turns too, but do not waste interaction just to force a second cast.

Faerie Mastermind

Flash lets it serve as a second spell on another player's turn, and opponents' extra draws feed your hand. Its activated ability also gives opponents cards, so use it deliberately rather than automatically spending spare mana on it.

Brainstorm

Usually selection rather than card advantage, with fetchlands letting you shuffle away unwanted cards. Teferi's Ageless Insight changes draw three, put two back into draw six, put two back. Ponder and Preordain similarly help find a missing engine but do not ordinarily grow your hand by themselves.

Frantic Search

Filters two cards and untaps three lands, but ordinarily costs a card overall. With Resonating Lute, those lands can produce six mana restricted to instants and sorceries, making this a strong bridge into another spell. Discard a large sorcery when Mizzix's Mastery is your recovery plan.

Dig Through Time

Finds the two cards you actually need at instant speed. It puts cards into hand without drawing, so neither Teferi's Ageless Insight nor Psychosis Crawler amplifies that part. Delve away fetchlands and expendable cards before exiling a strong Mastery target.

Treasure Cruise

A low-mana draw-three spell once the graveyard is stocked. Like Dig Through Time, it competes with your recursion for graveyard cards. It still has mana value eight when cast with delve, which matters to Shark Typhoon.

Overflowing Insight

A straightforward seven-card refill you can hard-cast with the Lute or use as a free Cecily spell to sustain later triggers. It also gives Mnemonic Deluge a predictable draw target rather than relying on an opponent's hand size.

Sea Gate Restoration // Sea Gate, Reborn

Doubles a substantial hand while permanently removing your maximum hand size. With eleven cards before casting it, ten remain in hand and the spell draws eleven if nothing else changes. It can also be your early blue land when you need development more than a late payoff.

Choosing which expensive spell earns the free cast
Emergent Ultimatum

Finds three differently named MONOCOLOURED cards; an opponent returns one and you may cast the other two. A useful value pile is Sea Gate Restoration, Breach the Multiverse, and Brass's Bounty: whichever is denied, you gain two major resources. With a stocked graveyard, Rise of the Dark Realms or Mnemonic Deluge can make the pile more threatening. It cannot find colourless Psychosis Crawler or multicoloured Cecily.

Rishkar's Expertise

Draws according to your largest creature and can cast a spell of mana value five or less from hand. Psychosis Crawler or a large Shark makes the draw much larger than Cecily's base three power. Use the second spell for a draw engine, protection permanent, or Mizzix's Mastery; an X spell cast for free must use X equal to zero.

Breach the Multiverse

Mills every player, then takes one creature or planeswalker from each player's graveyard, not only among the newly milled cards. This supplies threats without needing your own creature-heavy deck and stocks the graveyards for Rise of the Dark Realms.

Rise of the Dark Realms

Converts accumulated trades, mills, and sweepers into a huge board under your control. Most returned creatures cannot attack that turn, and casting this from Cecily happens after attackers were declared anyway. Protect the board or win through useful entry triggers before expecting combat damage.

Mnemonic Deluge

Exiles a useful instant or sorcery from any graveyard and lets you cast up to three copies for free. Breach the Multiverse produces three rounds of mill and theft; Brass's Bounty makes three Treasure batches. Triple Sea Gate Restoration grows the hand extremely quickly, so count your library and decline copies if necessary. These are finite lines, and Deluge exiles itself.

Mizzix's Mastery

Recovers a discarded or countered bomb for four mana. Overloading it rebuilds an entire spell turn, but Cecily cannot combine her free-cast alternative cost with overload: free Mastery uses its normal single-target text. Cast useful copies in an order that leaves draw, mana, protection, and threats resolving when you need them; counterspells without useful targets can simply be declined.

Turning the resources into a win
Psychosis Crawler

Every card you actually draw makes each opponent lose one life. Teferi's Ageless Insight, a large Sea Gate Restoration, and subsequent draw spells turn the draw plan into table-wide lethal pressure. The Crawler needs to survive until the draws occur; its toughness is your current hand size, so emptying your hand can kill it.

Shark Typhoon

Each noncreature spell you CAST makes a flying Shark based on the spell's mana value, regardless of how little mana you paid. A Cecily-cast Breach the Multiverse makes a 7/7. Casting copied cards from Mastery or Deluge triggers it; merely copying a spell already on the stack would not. Cycle it when you need a surprise blocker and a replacement card instead of the six-mana engine.

Brass's Bounty

Makes one Treasure per land without caring whether those lands are tapped. Cecily lets you generate that mana without paying seven first. Save the Treasure through combat for Crackle with Power, or use it to hard-cast and protect another threat.

Crackle with Power

This is a spell to PAY for, not a useful Cecily free cast, since a free cast forces X to zero. At X equal to three it costs eleven mana and deals fifteen to each of three opponents; at X equal to eight it costs twenty-six and deals forty to each. Lute-powered lands and Treasure make those numbers realistic after several turns of development. Include existing damage and life loss when calculating the required X.

Attacking safely and defending the engine
Dolmen Gate

Prevents combat damage to your attacking creatures, letting Cecily attack into dangerous blockers to get her trigger again next turn. It does not prevent removal, noncombat damage, or damage to your blockers, and opponents can remove the Gate before combat damage.

Lightning Greaves

Haste lets Cecily attack on the turn she enters. Shroud also prevents your own Elmar untap, Irenicus's Vile Duplication, Slip Through Space, and Tamiyo's Safekeeping from targeting her. Move Greaves onto another creature first if you need those effects; a trigger already aimed at her will fail if she gains shroud before it resolves.

Swiftfoot Boots

A slower equip cost buys hexproof instead of shroud, so your own targeting effects still work. This is often the safer permanent protection when you want to copy or untap Cecily.

Slip Through Space

A one-mana cantrip that makes Cecily unblockable for the turn while contributing to the second-spell plan. It is not protection against removal. Her free-spell trigger does not require connecting, so use this for survival rather than treating combat damage as the engine.

Hexing Squelcher

Protects your spells from countering and makes opponents pay life to target your other creatures. It does not stop removal or abilities that counter Cecily's attack trigger. Deflecting Swat redirects targeted answers and is free while you control either commander, but cannot answer an untargeted sweeper.

Heroic Intervention

Covers most destruction-based sweepers and targeted removal across your board. It does not stop sacrifices, toughness reduction, or an untargeted exile effect. Keep it available when multiple engines are carrying your advantage.

Tamiyo's Safekeeping

Protects a single important permanent, including Resonating Lute or Psychosis Crawler, for one green mana. The life gain offsets a little of Cecily's attack loss and your draw costs. Its own cast can be the second spell that generates an Elmar Clue.

Cyclonic Rift

The third Game Changer is both an emergency answer and a way to clear the path for your Sharks or stolen creatures. Pay seven to overload it; Cecily's free cast cannot also use overload, so that line only bounces one permanent.

Toxic Deluge

Resets creature-heavy boards before they overwhelm your slower card engine. It also reduces your own creatures' toughness; indestructible does not save them from sufficiently large negative toughness. With a large hand, Psychosis Crawler can survive values that kill most opposing creatures.

Assassin's Trophy

Answers a problematic permanent at instant speed, at the cost of giving its controller a basic land if they have one. Abrupt Decay covers cheaper nonland permanents without being counterable; use that when its narrower range is sufficient.

Nature's Claim

Efficiently removes a graveyard-hate piece or threatening artifact or enchantment while leaving mana for development. The four life can matter to your eventual Crackle calculation, but protecting your engine takes priority.

Vandalblast

Cheap targeted artifact removal early and a one-sided artifact sweep later. As with Cyclonic Rift, Cecily cannot give you the overloaded mode for free. It leaves your Clues, Treasure, Lute, and equipment intact when overloaded normally.

Mana Drain

Stops a key spell and contributes colourless mana to your next main phase. It pays generic costs in big spells or X, but cannot replace Emergent Ultimatum's seven coloured pips. Swan Song and An Offer You Can't Refuse offer cheaper protection when holding two blue mana is awkward.

Arcane Denial

Broad, easily held-up interaction that replaces itself later, while giving the countered spell's controller extra cards. Do not claim it permanently exhausts an opponent's resources. Counter only what justifies helping their next hand.

Flame of Anor

With Cecily or another Wizard, choose two modes, commonly draw two plus destroy an artifact or deal five to a creature. The Wizard check happens as you cast the spell. It is excellent as an opponent-turn second spell because it grows your hand while interacting.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Controls opposing graveyard strategies without clearing your own Mastery targets. Its entry exiles one card; its sacrifice can exile all opponents' graveyards. That also removes potential Breach the Multiverse and Rise of the Dark Realms targets, so choose safety over theft only when the threat warrants it. Deadly Rollick provides a separate creature-exile answer, free with either commander in play.

Building four-colour mana and recovering from disruption
Delighted Halfling

Helps cast either commander without being countered. Its coloured mana is restricted to legendary spells, so it cannot fix the coloured costs of your big sorceries. Birds of Paradise is unrestricted fixing and another useful Elmar untap target.

Nature's Lore

Fetches an untapped original dual with the Forest type, such as Tropical Island or Bayou, and Three Visits does the same. This is why the mana base supports early green while still reaching Cecily's blue, black, and red cost.

Farseek

Finds typed non-Forest basics or duals and puts the land onto the battlefield tapped. Use it to cover a missing colour before the commander turn. Growth Spiral is an instant alternative when an extra land is already in hand, and can help trigger Elmar on another player's turn.

Bala Ged Recovery // Bala Ged Sanctuary

Recovers the engine, protection spell, or finisher you lost, while its reverse side supplies an early tapped green land. It does not recover cards exiled by delve, Mastery, or Deluge, so graveyard choices have lasting costs.

Reliquary Tower

Retains a hand larger than Cecily's eleven-card limit through cleanup and preserves cards if Cecily is removed. It produces no coloured mana, so do not keep a hand whose four-colour plan relies on it. Sea Gate Restoration can later provide the same hand-size freedom without a permanent remaining in play.

Boseiju, Who Endures

Channel answers a troublesome artifact, enchantment, or nonbasic land from your mana base. Otawara, Soaring City similarly provides a bounce answer. Channeling is an activated ability, not a spell cast, so neither contributes to Elmar's second-spell count.

What to keep in an opening hand and how to sequence

Keep roughly three lands with early green and blue access, one piece of acceleration, a draw engine or selection spell, and useful interaction. Fetchlands, original duals, and shocks should establish all four colours rather than simply fetching whichever land is first in the pile. Mulligan hands full of seven-mana sorceries even if they look spectacular with Cecily already active.

An example keep is Misty Rainforest, Command Tower, Blood Crypt, Nature's Lore, Mystic Remora, Ponder, and Swan Song. Fetch Tropical Island to support early blue or green, then use Nature's Lore to get Bayou if that completes the next turn's colours. You do not need either commander to be the first thing you deploy.

Elmar often comes first when you have a mana creature and two useful spells to cast. Cecily should arrive when she can start drawing safely or when your hand is approaching the free-spell threshold. Casting both automatically on curve can leave you with too few cards and no protection.

Before attacking, count the hand AFTER any land play and precombat spells. Ordinarily ten cards before Cecily's draw is enough; Teferi's Ageless Insight changes that draw to two. If necessary, crack Clues or cast a draw instant in response to her attack trigger. Nobody gets priority between the trigger's draw and its eleven-card check.

When Harmonic Prodigy gives you two attack triggers, resolve them one at a time. The spell cast through the first trigger resolves before the second trigger if nobody adds other effects, so a refill can prepare the next free cast. Drawing extra cards from Archmage Emeritus can also sustain the chain, but count the actual hand rather than assuming every trigger qualifies.

On opponents' turns, two useful instants or a flash Faerie Mastermind can trigger Elmar again. His Clues require two mana to draw; do not treat investigation as an immediate card in hand. Floating mana disappears between steps and phases, while unspent Treasure remains available later.

Win through accumulated cards and mana: Psychosis Crawler plus a large draw turn, a protected Shark army, stolen creatures, or a paid Crackle with Power. This list uses Consecrated Sphinx, Cyclonic Rift, and Rhystic Study as its three Game Changers. It has no planned infinite combo, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain; the goal is strong, interactive Bracket 3 development rather than an early deterministic kill.
