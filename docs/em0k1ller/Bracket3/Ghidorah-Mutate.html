Mutate stacks a pile of creatures into one body, and every "whenever this creature mutates" trigger you have already stacked fires AGAIN each time you add another. So the fourth mutate is not one trigger, it is four or five: draw a card, destroy an artifact, destroy a noncreature permanent, fetch a land, and put a free permanent onto the battlefield. The whole deck is about growing that pile fast and making sure one removal spell cannot eat it.

The commander and the pile
Illuna, Apex of Wishes

6/6 flying trample, and every mutate digs until it finds a nonland permanent and puts it onto the battlefield FREE. Cast her for her mutate cost, not her mana cost, whenever there is a legal base.

Mutate does not cause an enter-the-battlefield trigger. The creature was already there — it just changed. Plan around that: your payoffs here are "whenever this mutates", not "when this enters".

Auspicious Starrix

exiles until it finds X permanent cards, where X is how many times IT has mutated. The third mutate onto a Starrix pile puts three free permanents onto the battlefield.

Dreamtail Heron

a card on every subsequent mutate.

Gemrazer

an artifact or enchantment destroyed on every subsequent mutate. Repeatable Naturalize is not something green usually gets.

Sawtusk Demolisher

any noncreature permanent destroyed, every mutate.

Migratory Greathorn

a land every mutate, which pays for the next one.

Lore Drakkis

buys back an instant or sorcery each time, so your protection spells recur.

Pouncing Shoreshark

bounces a creature each mutate, and it has FLASH — mutate at instant speed in response to a blocker or a removal spell.

Cloudpiercer

a loot on each mutate, which finds the next piece.

Porcuparrot

taps to deal damage equal to the number of times it has mutated. A four-deep pile is a repeatable four-damage removal spell every turn.

Sea-Dasher Octopus

mutate it on for {1}{U} and the pile draws a card on every connection.

Bases that cannot be removed

The pile's weakness is obvious: one Swords to Plowshares answers eight cards. Start on a hexproof body and that stops being possible.

Slippery Bogle

one mana, hexproof, non-Human. The ideal base.

Gladecover Scout

the second copy.

Venomthrope

hexproof, flying and deathtouch, so the pile inherits evasion and an unblockable clock.

Swiftfoot Boots

hexproof and haste on whatever you built.

Heroic Intervention

hexproof and indestructible for the whole board through a wrath.

Tamiyo's Safekeeping

one mana for the same on the pile.

Slip Out the Back

phases the pile out in response to removal, and leaves a counter behind.

Legolas's Quick Reflexes

split second, so it cannot be responded to at all.

Veil of Summer

one mana that beats blue and black removal outright, and draws.

Frenzied Baloth

your creature spells cannot be countered — including every mutate.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it points their removal somewhere else.

If the pile does die, remember Illuna can go back to the command zone instead of the graveyard. The rest of the pile is gone, but the commander is not.

Making mutate cheap
Animar, Soul of Elements

every creature spell you cast is another counter and another discount. Mutate spells are creature spells, so a few turns in they cost nothing at all.

Pollywog Symbiote

mutate spells cost one less, and each one you cast filters a card.

Radagast of Rhosgobel

the first creature spell each turn costs {2} less and can be cast as though it had flash. Mutate on their end step.

Delighted Halfling

uncounterable mana for legendary spells, which covers Illuna.

Utopia Sprawl

a Forest that taps for two.

Skyshroud Claim

two untapped Forests, which is a four-mana turn out of nowhere.

Birds of Paradise

turn-one acceleration in a deck that wants to start mutating on turn three.

Drawing off the pile
Season of Growth

every mutate spell TARGETS a creature you control, so every single one draws a card. Two mana.

Beast Whisperer

a card for every creature spell, mutate included.

Temur Ascendancy

haste for the whole board — which matters because a freshly grown pile wants to attack immediately — plus a card for every big creature that enters.

Return of the Wildspeaker

draws cards equal to the greatest power among your non-Human creatures. On a real pile that is eight or more, at instant speed. The other mode is a team pump for the kill.

Toski, Bearer of Secrets

uncounterable, indestructible, and it draws on every connection.

Rhystic Study

their turns pay for your turns.

Mystic Remora

the first four turns, it is the best card in the deck.

Sylvan Library

with this much selection, paying life for the extra cards is nearly always right.

Scroll Rack

swaps a hand of dead cards for the top of your library, and it is exceptional alongside Sylvan Library.

Sensei's Divining Top

fixes every draw and turns spare mana into card selection.

Parcelbeast

a free land or a card every turn from the pile itself.

Removal and interaction
Pongify

one mana, kills anything, and the 3/3 they get is irrelevant next to your pile.

Beast Within

any permanent at instant speed.

Chaos Warp

the answer to an enchantment or planeswalker in these colours.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, it clears every blocker and every stax piece for the lethal swing.

Blasphemous Act

cheap in a full board — use it while the pile is hexproof and indestructible, or before you commit.

Mana Drain

counter their wrath and spend the mana mutating twice next turn.

Swan Song

one mana for the removal spell or wrath aimed at the pile.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

the same, and their two Treasures matter less than your pile surviving.

Ivy, Gleeful Spellthief

copies any single-target spell — theirs or yours — onto herself. She steals removal and doubles pumps.

Portal to Phyrexia

three creatures gone from every opponent, then a reanimation every upkeep.

Closing
Craterhoof Behemoth

even a modest board plus one enormous trampling pile is lethal to the table.

Titan of Industry

two modes on entry, and the shield counter protects the pile from the removal spell it was going to die to.

Etali, Primal Conqueror // Etali, Primal Sickness

free spells off every player's library on entry, and it flips into an 11/11 indestructible infect threat.

Koma, Cosmos Serpent

uncounterable, makes a 3/3 every upkeep, and the Coils sacrifice to tap down blockers or make Koma indestructible.

Return of the Wildspeaker

+3/+3 to every non-Human you control is often the last three points on each opponent.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a hexproof one-drop and a mutate creature. Slippery Bogle on turn one into a mutate on turn two is the start that makes the deck's engine turn on before anyone can interact.

Do not mutate onto Animar unless you have to. He is the discount engine, and putting him under a pile risks losing him to the removal spell aimed at the whole thing.

Order the pile with the TOP card in mind. The creature on top decides the name, power, toughness and — importantly — whether it flies and tramples. Illuna and Sawtusk Demolisher on top attack well; Porcuparrot on top does not.

Auspicious Starrix

mutate it EARLY and keep mutating onto that pile. Its count is per-Starrix, so a Starrix that has mutated four times puts four free permanents onto the battlefield the next time.
