Read the third line of the commander carefully, because it is the entire deck: "triggered abilities you control can't cause you to sacrifice or exile creature tokens you control." Every temporary copy effect in Magic ends with a delayed trigger that sacrifices or exiles the token. The Master turns all of them off. Myriad tokens stay. Electroduplicate's copy stays. Delina's tokens stay. And because the legend rule does not apply to your tokens, you can copy legends as many times as you like.

The commander
The Master, Multiplied

myriad on a 4/3, no legend rule for your tokens, and no trigger of yours may sacrifice or exile them. Six mana, and it converts a dozen cards in the deck from "a copy until end of turn" into "a copy, permanently".

Blade of Selves

myriad on anything. In a four-player pod that is two permanent token copies of the equipped creature every single attack.

Goldlust Triad

myriad on a flier that makes Treasures on connection.

Copy effects that stop being temporary

All of these normally end with a delayed sacrifice or exile trigger. With The Master out, the tokens simply stay on the battlefield.

Electroduplicate

a permanent copy of any creature you control, twice with flashback.

Molten Duplication

a permanent artifact copy of a creature or artifact.

Heat Shimmer

a permanent copy of ANY creature, including theirs.

Devastating Onslaught

X permanent copies of your best creature. This is usually the game.

Jaxis, the Troublemaker

a permanent copy every turn for {R} and a discarded card.

Determined Iteration

populate every combat, and the copy stays.

Delina, Wild Mage

token copies on every attack, and the roll can chain — with The Master they all stay.

Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki

the Kiki-Jiki back face copies a nonlegendary creature every turn, permanently.

Foggy Swamp Visions

copies of creature cards out of ANY graveyard, permanently.

Gogo, Mysterious Mime

becomes a copy of your best creature every combat, with haste and a pump.

Cursed Mirror

a hasty copy of the best creature on the board, and a mana rock afterwards.

Payoffs for creatures entering
Impact Tremors

one damage to each opponent per creature entering. A myriad attack in a four-player pod is two triggers.

Purphoros, God of the Forge

TWO damage to each opponent per creature entering, on an indestructible body. This is the deck's primary kill — a Devastating Onslaught for four is sixteen damage to every opponent before combat.

Enduring Courage

every creature that enters gets +2/+0 and haste, so the copies attack immediately.

Idol of Oblivion

a card every turn you made a token, which is every turn.

Grim Hireling

two Treasures per combat damage step.

Professional Face-Breaker

a Treasure per combat step, convertible into impulse draws.

Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor

a card for every creature that connects with an opponent. On a myriad attack that is one per token.

Extra combats and evasion
Karlach, Fury of Avernus

untaps all attackers, gives first strike, and grants an additional combat phase. Every myriad creature triggers again — and with The Master, that is another permanent batch of tokens.

Cover of Darkness

name the type your board is and almost nothing can block.

Dolmen Gate

your attackers take no combat damage at all, so attacking with everything is free.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud for the commander.

Interaction
Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it redirects the removal aimed at The Master.

Bolt Bend

nearly free with a big creature out, same effect.

Imp's Mischief

the black version, and it can steal their tutor.

Feed the Swarm

the only clean enchantment answer in Rakdos.

Chaos Warp

the second one, and it covers planeswalkers.

Blasphemous Act

essentially free on a board full of tokens, and you rebuild faster than anyone.

Plumb the Forbidden

sacrifice your tokens in response to a wrath and draw a card for each. It turns a board wipe into a full refill.

Animate Dead

brings back the best creature in any graveyard, which is then copyable.

Mana and cards
Dark Ritual

turn-one acceleration.

Jeska's Will

with a commander out, both halves — a huge mana burst and three impulse cards.

Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer

a Treasure and a stolen card on the first hit, and it is a fine myriad target.

Black Market Connections

three effects a turn.

Night's Whisper

two cards for two mana.

Mind Stone

ramp early, a card later.

Rakdos Signet

fixing.

Fellwar Stone

usually taps for what you need.

Firebender Ascension

builds counters off your attack triggers, then starts copying them outright.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep acceleration. The Master costs six and the deck is far weaker without him — Dark Ritual, Signets and Jeska's Will are what land him on turn four.

Cast the copy effects AFTER The Master, not before. The same Electroduplicate is a temporary body without him and a permanent second copy with him. If he is not out yet, hold them.

Purphoros, God of the Forge

this is the win condition. Count the table's life before a big copy turn — two damage per token per opponent adds up faster than the combat step does.

Karlach, Fury of Avernus

with a myriad creature on the board, her extra combat is a second full batch of permanent tokens. Attack, make tokens, untap, attack again.
