Laelia grows every time a card leaves your library or graveyard for exile, and she makes some of that exile herself when she attacks. So every impulse-draw spell in this deck is two things at once: a card you can play this turn, and a +1/+1 counter on a hasty commander. Attack every turn, play what she flips, and let Kediss copy her damage to the whole table.

The commander
Laelia, the Blade Reforged

three mana, haste. Whenever she attacks, exile the top card of your library; you may play it this turn. Whenever ONE OR MORE cards are put into exile from your library and/or graveyard, put a +1/+1 counter on her.

"One or more" means one counter per exile EVENT, not per card. Light Up the Stage exiling two cards is one counter; three separate exile effects are three counters. Cheap, repeated exile beats one big exile.

She only counts exile from YOUR library and graveyard. Ragavan and Tibalt's Trickery exile from opponents' libraries and do not grow her.

Key pairs
+
Laelia, the Blade Reforged + Kediss, Emberclaw Familiar

whenever your commander deals combat damage to an opponent, Kediss deals that much damage to each other opponent. A 10-power Laelia connecting once is 30 damage across the table.

+
Laelia, the Blade Reforged + Temur Battle Rage

double strike, and trample once she has 4 power. Two hits means two Kediss triggers.

+
Laelia, the Blade Reforged + Chandra's Ignition

she deals damage equal to her power to each other creature and each opponent. With 15 power that is 15 to everyone and a cleared board.

+
Underworld Breach + Grim Lavamancer

both exile cards from your graveyard as a cost, and every escape and every Lavamancer shot is a counter on Laelia.

++
Underworld Breach + Wheel of Fortune + Jeska's Will

the big turn. Wheel gives every opponent seven cards, so Jeska's Will makes seven red mana; escape Wheel again from the graveyard (its {2}{R} plus three other cards exiled) and do it again. Each loop refills everyone, makes mana, and puts a counter on Laelia. A three-card line, so it fits Bracket 3; save it for a turn that ends the game.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep Laelia's mana, a land, and one cheap exile spell or piece of equipment. Turn two or three Laelia, attack immediately, and play whatever she flips.

Lay out an upkeep exile engine (Outpost Siege, Tavern Brawler, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Urabrask) early: they grow her every turn before she even attacks.

Get Kediss down before the big swing, and hold Deflecting Swat for the removal.
