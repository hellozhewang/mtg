Black Cat is not merely a fun theft creature; she is an engine and an infinite win condition. While her entrance trigger steals two cards from an opponent's top nine, looping her with Nim Deathmantle, Ashnod's Altar, and a Treasure or token generator completely exiles all three opponents' libraries face down, leaving them with nothing to draw while giving you their entire decks to cast. Furthermore, Black Market converts every creature death into immense black mana pools, which Black Cat's static ability explicitly lets you spend as any color of mana to cast those stolen cards.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Demonic Tutor, The One Ring, and Vampiric Tutor. No mass land denial. Infinite combo lines (Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond/Vito, and Nim Deathmantle loops) wait until turn 6 under house rules.

Opening hand and sequencing

Keep an opening hand with two to three lands, early mana acceleration (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Dark Ritual, Wayfarer's Bauble), and a tutor, draw piece (The One Ring, Morbid Opportunist, Deadly Dispute), or combo component.

Turn 1-2: Deploy mana rocks or setup artifacts (Skullclamp, Expedition Map, Wishclaw Talisman).

Turn 3-4: Cast Black Market or a value creature (Morbid Opportunist, Vito). Cast Black Cat to exile high-value opposing cards.

Turn 5: Establish protection (Lightning Greaves) or death-triggers. Prepare your mana engines (Coffers, Black Market accumulating counters from dying creatures).

Turn 6+: Under Bracket 3 rules, deploy and execute your infinite lines: assemble Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond/Vito to drain the table, or Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar to generate infinite mana, drain with Blood Artist, and exile all opposing libraries with Black Cat.
