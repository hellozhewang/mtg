Sisay's power counts COLOURS among your other legendary permanents, not how many you control — so a single five-colour board makes her a 7/7 that tutors any legendary permanent costing six or less directly onto the battlefield. Each fetch adds colours, which grows her, which raises the ceiling on the next fetch. The whole deck is about paying for the first activation; after that it snowballs.

The commander and the maths
Sisay, Weatherlight Captain

base 2/2, +1/+1 per colour among your OTHER legendary permanents, and {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} searches out a legendary permanent with mana value strictly LESS than her power, straight onto the battlefield. Count before you activate.

Esika, God of the Tree // The Prismatic Bridge

taps for any colour AND gives every other legendary creature "{T}: add one mana of any colour". With three legends out, that alone pays Sisay's activation.

Faeburrow Elder

taps for one mana of each colour among your permanents. On a five-colour board it is a one-card Sisay activation.

Jegantha, the Wellspring

taps for exactly {W}{U}{B}{R}{G}. It is not a legal companion for this deck — Flowering of the White Tree and Mana Drain both break the rule — so it lives in the ninety-nine as the cleanest enabler you have.

Ioreth of the Healing House

untaps two other legendary creatures. Untap Faeburrow Elder and Jegantha and Sisay activates twice in a turn.

Relic of Legends

taps for any colour, and taps a legendary creature for another.

Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy

doubles every nonland mana source you tap, which turns Faeburrow Elder or Esika into two activations.

Mox Amber

free, and live from turn one in a deck this legendary.

Delighted Halfling

uncounterable mana for legendary spells, which is nearly your whole deck.

Bloom Tender

two or three colours off one tap early.

Loki's Scepter

fixes, and steals a creature for a turn on the way in.

What to fetch, and when

Fetch to fix your colours first. The correct first activation is almost always the legend that adds a missing colour — that raises Sisay's power and makes the NEXT fetch bigger.

Esika, God of the Tree // The Prismatic Bridge

the best early fetch by a distance. Mana for everything, and the back face is a free creature or planeswalker every upkeep if you drew it instead.

Jodah, the Unifier

your legends all get +X/+X for your legend count, and every legendary spell you cast from hand cascades into a cheaper legend for free.

Kethis, the Hidden Hand

legendary spells cost one less, and he turns your graveyard into a second hand.

Lavinia, Azorius Renegade

the fetch against a combo table. Nothing cast for free resolves, and their expensive noncreature spells stop working.

Jennifer Walters // The Sensational She-Hulk

opponents cannot cast spells during your turn at all — the fetch before a lethal attack.

Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar

the same lockout, and it draws off pumped attackers.

Loran of the Third Path

an artifact or enchantment destroyed on arrival.

Ertai Resurrected

flash, and it counters a spell or kills a creature or planeswalker.

Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer

a Treasure and a stolen card every hit, and it is a one-drop so Sisay can find it at almost any size.

Omnath, Locus of Creation

a card, four life, four mana and four damage to the table across a single turn's land drops.

Niv-Mizzet Reborn

ten cards deep for up to five two-colour cards. In a five-colour deck it refills your hand entirely.

Legends that draw
Reki, the History of Kamigawa

a card for every legendary spell you cast, which is most of them.

Jhoira, Weatherlight Captain

a card for every historic spell — legends, artifacts and Sagas alike.

King T'Challa // Black Panther, Hope Enduring

a card whenever anyone draws their second card each turn, and it flips into an unkillable double striker.

Oath of Nissa

digs three for a creature, land or planeswalker, and it fixes for planeswalker spells.

Rhystic Study

their turns pay for your activations.

Mystic Remora

the best card in the deck on turns one through four.

Esper Sentinel

taxes their first noncreature spell each turn.

Lotho, Corrupt Shirriff

a Treasure every time anyone casts their second spell — direct fuel for Sisay's five-colour cost.

Captain Sisay

the other Sisay, and she tutors legendary cards to HAND, including lands, which is a different axis of value.

Nature's Rhythm

any creature onto the battlefield for X, and harmonize buys it back from the graveyard later.

Dihada, Binder of Wills

digs four for legends and makes Treasures out of the misses, and the plus makes a legend indestructible.

Protecting the engine
Flowering of the White Tree

+2/+1 and ward {1} on every legend you control. The ward is what turns their cheap removal into a tax.

Hajar, Loyal Bodyguard

sacrifices to make all your legendary creatures indestructible through a wrath.

Deflecting Swat

free with your commander out.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

Mana Drain

counter, and the mana pays for a Sisay activation.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for anything noncreature.

Abrupt Decay

uncounterable, for the cheap problem permanent.

Aminatou, the Fateshifter

the minus blinks a permanent — resetting an enters trigger, or saving a creature from removal in response.

Primevals' Glorious Rebirth

returns EVERY legendary permanent from your graveyard to the battlefield. It is the rebuild after a wrath, and in this deck that is often six or seven cards.

Ramp and fixing
Arcane Signet

fixing is the deck's real cost.

Fellwar Stone

usually taps for what you are missing.

Nature's Lore

an untapped dual with a Forest type.

Farseek

fetches a shockland.

Derevi, Empyrial Tactician

untaps a permanent on entry and on every connection, and she recurs from the command zone for four mana without commander tax.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep fixing over threats. Five specific colours is the deck's entire difficulty — Bloom Tender, Faeburrow Elder, Arcane Signet and Delighted Halfling matter more than any single legend.

Count Sisay's power BEFORE you tap out. Five colours among your other legendary permanents makes her a 7/7, which fetches mana value six. Four colours caps you at five. Adding a colour is often worth more than adding a bomb.

Esika, God of the Tree // The Prismatic Bridge

this is the fetch that unlocks everything else. She fixes, she ramps, and every legend you already control starts tapping for mana the moment she resolves.

Jodah, the Unifier

cast your legends FROM HAND while he is out. Each one cascades into a free legend, and this deck's curve means the free hits are real cards.
