Turn your creatures into colossal Mutants using Jenova's combat ability, draw massive hands when sacrificing them on your turn, amplify counters with Golgari doublers, and finish games with Jarad flings or persist loops.

Mutant creation and death card draw
Jenova, Ancient Calamity

at the beginning of combat on your turn, put a number of +1/+1 counters equal to Jenova's power on up to one other target creature, turning it into a Mutant. Whenever a Mutant you control dies during your turn, you draw cards equal to its power. Because this draw triggers only on your turn, use instant-speed sacrifice outlets (Greater Good, Altars, Carrion Feeder) before your turn ends.

Greater Good

sacrifice a creature to draw cards equal to its power, then discard three cards. When sacrificing a Mutant during your turn with Jenova on the battlefield, both Jenova and Greater Good trigger, drawing DOUBLE the creature's power in cards!

Carrion Feeder

one-mana sacrifice outlet that cannot block, gaining a +1/+1 counter each time it consumes a creature.

Viscera Seer

one-mana sacrifice outlet that scrys one card on each sacrifice.

Phyrexian Altar

converts sacrificed Mutants into colored mana to cast newly drawn cards.

Ashnod's Altar

converts sacrificed creatures into two colorless mana.

Skullclamp

equips for one mana to draw two cards upon creature death, stacking with Jenova's draw trigger.

Counter doublers and amplifiers
Branching Evolution

doubles the number of +1/+1 counters put on creatures you control. With Jenova at four power, Branching Evolution places eight +1/+1 counters instead.

Corpsejack Menace

doubles all +1/+1 counters placed on creatures you control.

Hardened Scales

adds one additional +1/+1 counter whenever counters are placed on a creature you control.

Winding Constrictor

adds an additional counter of any kind whenever counters are placed on you or your permanents.

Kami of Whispered Hopes

adds an additional +1/+1 counter and taps for mana of any color equal to its power.

The Ozolith

collects all counters from permanents that leave the battlefield at combat step and moves them onto target creature at combat, preventing counter loss upon sacrifice.

Ozolith, the Shattered Spire

increases counters placed and can pay {1}{G} and tap to add +1/+1 counters.

Finisher lines and persist loop (Turn 6+)
Jarad, Golgari Lich Lord

pays {1}{B}{G} and sacrifices a creature to force each opponent to lose life equal to the sacrificed creature's power. Sacrificing a 20-power Mutant with Jarad drains each opponent for twenty life directly, bypassing combat blockers entirely.

Woodfall Primus

enters and destroys target noncreature permanent. When it dies, persist returns it to the battlefield with a -1/-1 counter. Putting +1/+1 counters on Primus (via Jenova or The Ozolith) removes the -1/-1 counter due to state-based actions, allowing infinite persist sacrifice with an Altar to destroy all opposing noncreature permanents.

Blood Artist

drains target player for one life and gains you one life whenever any creature dies.

Tutors, interaction, and opening hands
Demonic Tutor

finds Greater Good, Jarad, or a counter multiplier.

Vampiric Tutor

one-mana instant tutor placing any card on top of your library.

Ancient Tomb

provides two colorless mana for early acceleration.

Deadly Rollick

free creature exile while controlling Jenova.

Toxic Deluge

clears wide opposing boards through indestructible.

Culling Ritual

destroys all nonland permanents with mana value two or less and generates green and black mana for each destroyed permanent.

Keep opening hands with two to three lands, early mana dorks (Birds of Paradise, Llanowar Elves, Delighted Halfling), and a sacrifice outlet or counter doubler. Cast Jenova on turn three or four, immediately target an existing creature to place four +1/+1 counters and turn it into a Mutant, and sacrifice it post-combat to draw four or more fresh cards. Close out games with Jarad or massive board swings on Turn 6 or later.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, counter accumulation, and card draw.
