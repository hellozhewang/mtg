Twenty-five copies of Rat Colony. That is the deck. Rat Colony is one of the few cards with no four-of limit, so a quarter of the library is the same 2/1 that gets +1/+0 for every other Rat you control — and Marrow-Gnawer roughly DOUBLES your Rat count every time he taps. Add Thrumming Stone and one Rat Colony cast turns into a dozen for free.

The commander
Marrow-Gnawer

all Rats have fear, which means almost nothing can block them, and {T} plus a sacrificed Rat creates a token for EVERY Rat you control. Sacrificing one of six leaves five, then makes five more — ten. Then twenty. It doubles.

Thornbite Staff

equip it to Marrow-Gnawer. The Rat you sacrifice as the cost dies, which untaps him, so you activate again immediately. Each iteration doubles the board, and it keeps going as long as you want. This is the deck's engine and the reason it sits at Bracket 3.5.

Thousand-Year Elixir

haste for the activation, and an untap for a second one.

Lightning Greaves

haste so he goes off the turn he lands, and shroud against the removal that is coming for him.

Swiftfoot Boots

hexproof and haste when you need to equip him yourself.

Mithril Coat

flash and indestructible, attached automatically. Hold it as a counterspell for removal.

The Rat Colony engine
Rat Colony

twenty-five of them. Each one gets +1/+0 for every other Rat, so on a board of fifteen they are 16/1s with fear.

Thrumming Stone

every spell you cast has ripple 4 — reveal the top four and cast any card with the same name for free, and those ripple too. With a quarter of the deck named Rat Colony, one cast routinely chains into six or eight more, each of them free, each of them growing the rest.

Karumonix, the Rat King

digs five deep and puts every Rat among them into your HAND, which reloads the Thrumming Stone chain. It also gives the whole swarm toxic.

Pack Rat

copies itself for {2}{B} and a discarded card, and its size is your whole board.

Lord Skitter, Sewer King

a free Rat every combat, and it exiles a card from an opponent's graveyard for every Rat that enters.

Rat King, Pale Piper

a Rat whenever a nontoken creature leaves, and it converts spare tokens into cards.

Rat King, Verminister

grows and makes a Rat whenever a permanent leaves, and its ability returns a creature card AND every other copy of that name from your graveyard — which, with Rat Colony, is a mass reanimation.

Ashcoat of the Shadow Swarm

pumps the whole swarm by its own size when it attacks or blocks, and mills four each end step to return Rats.

Turning the swarm into a kill

The swarm has fear, so the usual plan is simply to attack. These are the outs when that is not enough.

Crypt Rats

{X} damage to each creature and each player, paid in black mana. With a big mana turn it kills the whole table — and with Eldrazi Monument out, your Rats are indestructible and only they survive.

Eldrazi Monument

+1/+1, flying and indestructible for the entire board. The upkeep sacrifice is free when you make dozens of Rats a turn.

Altar of Dementia

sacrifice a Rat to mill its power. On a board where each Rat is a 16/1, two or three sacrifices deck a player.

The Meathook Massacre

a scalable wrath your indestructible Rats survive, and every creature death anywhere drains or gains.

Karumonix, the Rat King

toxic on the whole swarm means ten poison counters is only a couple of connections.

Sacrifice value
Ashnod's Altar

free, and every Rat is two colourless mana. It is how a doubled board becomes a huge Crypt Rats activation.

Culling the Weak

four black mana for one Rat.

Deadly Dispute

two cards and a Treasure for a spare token.

Diabolic Intent

any card in the deck for a Rat.

Skullclamp

your tokens are 1/1s, so clamping is two cards each, over and over.

Gate to Phyrexia

repeatable artifact removal that costs only a Rat.

Grave Venerations

makes you the monarch for the extra card, returns a creature each end step, and drains on every creature death.

Mana and tutors
Dark Ritual

three black on turn one.

Cabal Ritual

five with threshold, which this deck reaches quickly.

Sol Ring

the fastest start.

Jet Medallion

everything costs one less.

Bontu's Monument

black creatures cost one less AND every creature spell drains the table for one. With a Thrumming Stone chain that is a lot of drain.

Necropotence

as many cards as your life total allows.

Demonic Tutor

any card.

Beseech the Mirror

bargain a token and cast the tutored card free if it costs four or less. Usually Thrumming Stone is too expensive for that, so take it to hand.

Patriarch's Bidding

name Rat. Your graveyard comes back in full; most tables get far less.

Reanimate

one mana for the best creature in any graveyard.

Interaction
Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Feed the Swarm

mono-black's only clean enchantment answer.

Imp's Mischief

redirects the removal spell aimed at Marrow-Gnawer.

Toxic Deluge

a scalable wrath. Your board rebuilds from one Marrow-Gnawer activation; theirs does not.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a ritual and a Rat. Dark Ritual into a turn-one Rat Colony or a turn-two Pack Rat means the board is already wide when Marrow-Gnawer lands on turn three or four.

Count before you activate. Marrow-Gnawer sacrifices first and then counts, so six Rats becomes ten, not twelve. The doubling is fast enough that it rarely matters — but it matters for lethal maths.

Thornbite Staff

this is the card to find. Marrow-Gnawer alone doubles once a turn; with the Staff attached he doubles as many times as you like.

Thrumming Stone

cast a Rat Colony immediately afterwards, not a tutor. Rippling into six free Rat Colonies is a bigger turn than any single card you could find.
