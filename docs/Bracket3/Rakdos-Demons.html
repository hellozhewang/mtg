Count names, not bodies. Liliana's Contract needs at least four Demons with different names already on the battlefield when your upkeep begins, and it checks that condition again when the trigger resolves. Protect that board rather than attacking or sacrificing away the fourth name for a little extra value.

The plan before the commander arrives

This is Demon tribal with substantial reanimation support. Develop mana, deploy useful smaller Demons, and cheat or discount the expensive ones. Rakdos is a disruptive threat rather than the engine you must cast on curve. The deck can win through Contract, flying combat, or amplified life loss without ever resolving its commander.

The three Game Changers are Demonic Tutor, Jeska's Will, and The One Ring. There is no intentional infinite combo, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain. The expected finishing window on a good, non-perfect draw is roughly turns seven through nine, with faster board development possible. This is a B-tier estimate under the collection's rubric, not a measured win rate: the list has strong tutors and recovery, but its wins need a developed board rather than one compact pair.

Rakdos, the Showstopper

Every creature that is not a Demon, Devil, or Imp gets a coin flip, including your own unprotected creature types. Each tails destroys that creature. Your printed Demons and Raphael are exempt, but so are opponents' qualifying creatures. Indestructible survives; a random half-wipe is not a guaranteed answer to a particular threat.

+
Herald of Slaanesh + Urza's Incubator

Name Demon with Incubator. Together they reduce Demon spells by four generic mana, letting you deploy several different names in one turn. Herald also gives your other Demons haste. Neither reduces colored pips, and Incubator does not discount Raphael, a Devil, or Roaming Throne, a Golem spell before it enters.

Rakdos, Lord of Riots

Cast him after an opponent loses life on your turn, then use that turn's total opposing life loss to discount creature spells. An early Blade of the Oni attack can supply the opening. His BBRR cost still needs actual colored mana, and his discount cannot pay colored pips. This Rakdos and your commander have different names and can coexist.

++
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet + Talisman of Indulgence

The early acceleration that lets you reach your four- and five-mana Demons while still casting interaction. Preserve black sources for the heavy-black threats; colorless acceleration alone cannot cast them.

++
Rakdos Signet + Fellwar Stone + Mind Stone

Signet fixes both colors but needs one mana to activate. Fellwar Stone depends on opposing lands for its colors. Mind Stone becomes a card later instead of remaining a poor topdeck. These permanent mana sources matter more than a hand containing only rituals and expensive Demons.

The Soul Stone

An indestructible black mana rock for early development. Its seven-mana harness activation exiles one of your creatures and only begins recurring creatures on subsequent upkeeps, so treat that as a late grind plan. Do not exile the fourth distinct Demon before a Contract upkeep.

+
Jeska's Will + Dark Ritual

Burst mana for a decisive multi-spell turn. Will produces red based on one opponent's hand and grants both modes only if you control a commander as you cast it; a different Rakdos in the ninety-nine is not your commander. Ritual supplies black. Neither burst lasts past the step or phase, and exiled Will cards are available only that turn.

Make the Contract a real win condition
Liliana's Contract

Five mana draws four and costs four life, so it remains useful before the win is ready. For the upkeep win, you need four distinct Demon names at the beginning of upkeep and again on resolution. If you start upkeep with only three, adding a fourth in response to another trigger is too late. Once the win trigger exists, removing Contract alone does not counter it; removing enough Demons can make its condition fail.

+
Blade of the Oni + Rot-Curse Rakshasa

Two cheap, differently named Demons make the four-name threshold realistic. Keep Blade unattached when you need it to count: while reconfigured onto a creature it is not a creature itself. Rakshasa cannot block and is sacrificed at end of combat if it attacks, so often leave its five power idle when it is your fourth name.

+
Black Market Connections + Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber

Connections' changeling Shapeshifter token counts as a Demon. Ritual Chamber makes a Demon token with a different name, so the two token types can supply two of Contract's names. Several tokens of the same kind still supply only one distinct name. Raphael's Devil tokens do not naturally count as Demons.

Roaming Throne

Choose Demon as it enters. It then counts as a Demon named Roaming Throne, is spared by Rakdos, and doubles triggered abilities of your other Demons. It does not double Contract's enchantment ability, activated tutors, static discounts, or Bloodletter's replacement effect.

+
Sneak Attack + Liliana's Contract

With Contract already in play, wait until the last opponent's end step has begun, then activate Sneak Attack to put missing Demons from hand onto the battlefield. The delayed sacrifice waits for the next end step, which is after your upcoming upkeep. This avoids exposing the assembled four-name board for a full round. Each activation costs red, each needs a creature in hand, and opponents can still respond.

+
Emergence Zone + Liliana's Contract

Activate Zone on the last opponent's end step to cast Contract at instant speed when you already have the four names. Budget the activation's mana and sacrificed land as well as Contract's five-mana cost. Zone also permits a late reanimation sorcery or replacement Demon, but does not make abilities such as Pyre of Heroes or reconfigure usable at instant speed.

+
Nightmare Shepherd + Liliana's Contract

When another nontoken Demon dies, Shepherd can exile its card to make a 1/1 copy retaining its name and Demon type. This preserves a distinct name and repeats entry abilities. The exile sacrifices future reanimation of that card, so choose deliberately. If a mass wipe kills Shepherd alongside other creatures, it still sees those other deaths; the returning tokens can rebuild your count.

+
Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire + Liliana's Contract

Cast Rebirth before a needed Demon dies. Its death trigger can return the creature tapped before Contract's pending win trigger resolves, restoring the count. It does not protect against exile, bounce, or a graveyard replacement effect. Leave a commander you want returned in the graveyard instead of moving it to the command zone.

For the precise upkeep condition, see Wizards' Core Set 2019 release notes: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/core-set-2019-release-notes-2018-06-22

The Demons that earn their slots
+
Doom Whisperer + Reanimate

Pay life to surveil, put a powerful Demon into the graveyard, then reanimate it. Surveil does not draw the card you leave on top. Stop digging before the life payment and Reanimate together become fatal; this deck uses life for several engines at once.

+
Vilis, Broker of Blood + Reanimate

Reanimate puts Vilis onto the battlefield before making you lose eight life, so Vilis triggers and draws eight cards afterward. You must survive that life loss. Vilis also turns fetchland life payments, mana-source life loss, and his own activated cost into cards. Life gain does not cancel the fact that life was lost earlier.

The Speed Demon

Draws on your end step immediately after deployment, rather than requiring a full turn cycle. Speed begins at one and can rise once on each of your turns when an opponent loses life, to a maximum of four. The corresponding life loss is mandatory, so lifelink and a healthy life total matter.

The One Ring

Rebuilds your hand even when your creatures are removed. Its cast-trigger protection protects you, not Contract or your Demons, and expires as your next turn begins rather than covering the upcoming upkeep. Burden-counter life loss still happens and can become dangerous. Vilis turns that loss into additional mandatory draws, so count both life and remaining library cards before activating Ring again.

Rakdos, Patron of Chaos

A six-power flying threat that demands two nonland, nontoken sacrifices from one opponent at your end step or draws you two. Choose the opponent whose resources you want pressured; an empty board normally gives you cards. It is not an edict affecting every opponent.

Archfiend of Depravity

At each opponent's end step, that player keeps at most two creatures and sacrifices the rest. This constrains rebuilding after Rakdos and helps protect your Contract setup. It does not stop an opponent attacking with a large army before their end step, so do not mistake it for immediate defense.

Kardur, Doomscourge

Its entry effect redirects opposing attacks until your next turn, even if Kardur dies in the meantime. It also drains each opponent and gains you life when any attacking creature dies. The attack requirement is not technically goad, so it does not by itself switch on Bothersome Quasit's cannot-block clause.

Bothersome Quasit

Your noncreature spells goad individual opposing creatures, and goaded creatures your opponents control cannot block. This clears selected blockers for your flying threats and gives you a useful three-mana Demon. In a two-player endgame, a goaded creature can still attack you if that is its only legal player to attack.

Curse-Marred Demon

A four-mana flying tutor on a Demon body, followed by a random discard. The newly found card can be discarded; with an otherwise empty hand, it certainly will be. Use it with a fuller hand, or to place a reanimation target into the graveyard when empty. It is not the safe tutor for your only Contract when you cannot afford to lose it.

Razaketh, the Foulblooded

Turns expendable creatures and two life per activation into exact cards. Black Market Connections and Sokenzan can provide fodder. Sacrificing a named Demon may move you away from the Contract win, so search for protection or recovery with spare tokens first. There is no self-contained infinite line here: every activation consumes a creature.

Win when the enchantment is unavailable
+
Bloodthirster + Herald of Slaanesh

Haste lets Bloodthirster start attacking immediately. Each time it deals combat damage to a player, it untaps and creates another combat, but it cannot attack a player it has already attacked that turn. With three opponents it can visit each in sequence if it connects. Only Bloodthirster untaps from its trigger; your other attackers do not automatically get to attack again.

+
Bloodletter of Aclazotz + Archfiend of Despair

Bloodletter doubles opposing life loss on your turn, while Archfiend repeats each opponent's total life loss at the end step. These multiply more than a simple doubling: seven damage becomes fourteen life lost, then Archfiend's fourteen becomes another twenty-eight, totaling forty-two. You still need the damage or another initial life-loss source; these two cards alone do not kill anyone.

+
Raphael, Fiendish Savior + Defiling Daemogoth

Raphael gives your other Demons +1/+1 and lifelink. Daemogoth gains another one life for each of your creatures dealing combat damage to a player, then makes every opponent lose life equal to all life you gained that turn. Several large lifelinking hits can therefore finish even opponents you never attacked. Raphael is a Devil and does not naturally satisfy Contract.

+
Archfiend of Despair + Defiling Daemogoth

On your end step, put Archfiend's trigger on the stack first and Daemogoth's on top. Daemogoth resolves first; Archfiend then includes that new loss in the turn's total. This sequencing turns accumulated lifelink into a much larger table-wide drain without an infinite loop.

+
Roaming Throne + Rune-Scarred Demon

Two entry triggers find two cards, turning a reanimated Rune-Scarred Demon into both a threat and a plan. Throne also doubles Bloodthirster's damage triggers, but never removes its restriction on attacking the same player twice. Extra combats alone are not an infinite.

Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber

Annex draws every end step and, while you control a Demon, drains each opponent for two while gaining you two. With Bloodletter, that opposing life loss is four on your turn. Unlocking Ritual Chamber later gives a six-power flying body and a Contract name; the two doors are not separate cards in your deck.

Rebuild rather than start over
+
Faithless Looting + Animate Dead

Looting trades card quantity for selection and puts expensive Demons where Animate Dead can reach them. Keep land drops and protection when choosing discards. Animate Dead returns a creature permanently while its Aura remains; losing the Aura normally forces you to sacrifice that creature.

Victimize

Select two creature cards already in your graveyard, then sacrifice a creature as the spell resolves to return both tapped. The creature you sacrifice cannot be one of those original targets. Trading a spare token for two differently named Demons is one of the fastest ways to approach Contract after disruption.

Patriarch's Bidding

Name Demon to return your graveyard's Demon creatures and rebuild several names at once. Every player chooses a type, and every player returns creatures of any chosen type, so opposing tribal decks can benefit greatly. Raphael is not a Demon, and Roaming Throne is only a Golem in the graveyard unless someone chooses Golem.

+
Living Death + Rakdos Charm

If one opponent has the dangerous graveyard, exile it with Charm before resolving Living Death. Living Death returns the creatures that were in graveyards before the spell began, not the creatures sacrificed during its resolution. It affects everyone, so inspect all graveyards first; your current four-Demon board can disappear if you cast it carelessly.

Necromancy

Cast it at normal sorcery timing when you want a permanent Demon for Contract. If cast when a sorcery could not be cast, it is sacrificed at the next cleanup, normally taking its creature with it. Flashing it during the last opponent's end step therefore does not normally leave that Demon alive for your upkeep. Its instant-speed mode is still useful for an emergency Kardur or tutor trigger.

+
Sneak Attack + Nightmare Shepherd

A Demon sacrificed at the next end step can become a permanent 1/1 copy through Shepherd, retaining its name and entry ability. This converts the temporary cheat into a lasting Contract body. Shepherd must see the death; if Shepherd was also cheated with Sneak Attack, resolve the other Demons' sacrifice triggers before sacrificing Shepherd.

Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt

A land when development demands one and recovery when you have ample mana. It returns creatures with different mana values, each at most X, rather than any set of differently named Demons. It cannot return two four-mana Demons in the same cast. Takenuma provides a separate land-slot creature recovery effect to hand.

Answer threats and protect the winning upkeep
+
Deflecting Swat + Imp's Mischief

Redirect targeted removal away from Contract or its supporting Demons when legal alternate targets exist. Swat also works on abilities and becomes free with your actual commander in play. Mischief only handles a spell with exactly one target and costs life equal to that spell's mana value. Neither can redirect a nontargeting sweeper.

Tibalt's Trickery

Your broad counterspell for a sweeper, a lethal spell, or removal you cannot redirect. The opponent may get a different free spell, so save it for situations where letting the original resolve is worse. It cannot counter an activated or triggered ability.

Lightning Greaves

Protects one creature from targeted answers and supplies haste for immediate attacks or Varragoth's boast setup. Shroud also prevents your own Malakir Rebirth from targeting the equipped creature. Equip is sorcery-speed, and opponents can answer before the equip resolves. Do not move the boots off your indispensable fourth Demon without considering that window.

+
Feed the Swarm + Withering Torment

Direct enchantment removal for effects that disable your creatures or replace graveyards with exile. Feed is cheaper and sorcery-speed; Torment is an instant and has a fixed two-life cost. These preserve the Contract and reanimation plans instead of leaving every noncreature problem to Chaos Warp.

++
Chaos Warp + Abrade + Vandalblast

Warp covers an arbitrary problem permanent but can give its controller another permanent. Abrade is cheap instant artifact interaction or small-creature removal. Vandalblast's overload can clear opposing mana and artifact engines while keeping your own rocks; it does not remove lands.

+
Deadly Rollick + Terminate

Precise creature answers when Rakdos's coins miss. Rollick exiles and can be free with your commander on the battlefield. Terminate destroys for two mana and prevents regeneration, but does not beat indestructible.

Toxic Deluge

The deterministic emergency sweeper. Choose X with your own Demons in mind; this can kill them and collapse your Contract count. Even indestructible creatures die from sufficient toughness reduction. Do not pay life blindly when Vilis is also about to turn that payment into many mandatory draws.

+
Rakdos, the Showstopper + Roaming Throne

Throne naming Demon gives two Rakdos triggers. With fair independent flips, an eligible creature has a 75 percent chance of being destroyed across them, assuming no other changes or protection. This still does not touch Demons, Devils, or Imps. Players act before or between resolving triggers, not between individual flips within one resolution.

Tutor for the missing piece, not always the largest Demon
→
Demonic Tutor → Liliana's Contract

Find Contract when you have the mana and several distinct Demons, or when drawing four will restart a stalled hand. If survival is the issue, find the necessary answer instead. Having the enchantment without a board is not a win.

→
Rune-Scarred Demon → Liliana's Contract

The cleanest creature-based Contract search: Rune-Scarred both finds the enchantment and contributes a six-power flying Demon with its own name. Reanimation makes its seven-mana printed cost manageable.

→
Demonic Counsel → Herald of Slaanesh

Find the discount and haste engine when your hand is heavy. Without delirium, Counsel can only find Demon cards; with four card types in your graveyard it becomes an unrestricted tutor and can find Contract. Roaming Throne is not a Demon in your library. Check actual types rather than assuming four cards mean delirium.

→
Varragoth, Bloodsky Sire → Liliana's Contract

After Varragoth attacks, pay two mana to put Contract on top. He need not deal damage or survive combat if you activate before he dies, but the card is not put into your hand. Use a subsequent draw from The One Ring or your end-step draw engines to pick it up; otherwise wait for the next draw step.

→+
Burning-Rune Demon → Vilis, Broker of Blood + Reanimate

With Burning-Rune already on the battlefield and no other relevant graveyard cards, the opponent may put Reanimate into your hand and Vilis in the graveyard for an immediate reanimation, or give you Vilis and put Reanimate into the graveyard to stop that immediate play. This is not a guaranteed two-card package. Choose this pair when either outcome helps your existing hand; never describe the opponent's choice as yours.

→+
Burning-Rune Demon → Animate Dead + Necromancy

If the right Demon is already in your graveyard, these redundant reanimation spells ensure one reaches your hand. Budget the higher cost in case the opponent gives you Necromancy. Do not offer Contract and a creature-reanimation spell as though either could recover the enchantment.

+→→→→→→
Pyre of Heroes + Blade of the Oni → Herald of Slaanesh → Kardur, Doomscourge → Doom Whisperer → Burning-Rune Demon → Rune-Scarred Demon → Vilis, Broker of Blood

This is a mana-value ladder, not one activation: sacrifice a Demon of value two to find a three, a three for a four, and so on. Each activation costs two, taps Pyre, and is sorcery-speed. The named sequence supplies haste, defense, graveyard setup, tutoring, then a draw engine. Each rung replaces a body instead of increasing your Demon count, and you can sacrifice a creature of the required value without first following the earlier rungs.

→
Razaketh, the Foulblooded → Liliana's Contract

Tutor Contract with expendable material once you can still maintain four names. When Contract is already ready, use the same tutor to find Tibalt's Trickery or other applicable protection. Keep enough life and mana to cast the card you find.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep three lands with access to both colors, early acceleration, and a cheap Demon or draw engine. A hand of several eight-mana Demons and a tutor is not a strong keep unless it also has a credible discard-and-reanimation route. The deck has 36 lands plus Agadeem's Awakening and Malakir Rebirth as optional land drops.

++
Badlands + Blood Crypt + Raucous Theater

The seven fetchlands can find these typed duals. Prioritize an untapped dual when developing, and fetch Theater when you can afford the tapped land and want to surveil a Demon into the graveyard. Three Swamps and one Mountain provide untapped fetch options without shockland life payment and targets for opposing basic-land search effects.

++
Graven Cairns + Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth + Cavern of Souls

Cairns filters into the heavy black costs, while Urborg lets utility lands also produce black. Cavern usually names Demon and protects your Demon creature spells from counterspells. Its restricted colored mana cannot cast Contract or other noncreature spells, and cannot cast Roaming Throne as a Demon. Do not keep an opening hand whose apparent colored sources only work after another land appears.

+
Arena of Glory + Bloodthirster

The exert ability can give a cast Bloodthirster haste; the next-turn untap cost is worthwhile when it creates an immediate attack sequence. It does not grant haste to a creature returned by Reanimate, because no creature spell was cast using that mana.

An illustrative keep is Badlands, Blood Crypt, Command Tower, Arcane Signet, Herald of Slaanesh, Unholy Annex // Ritual Chamber, and Terminate. Play Signet on turn two. Turn three can deploy Herald with one mana left, or Annex while developing toward a safer threat. With a fourth land, Herald lets you cast your commander for four while potentially leaving one mana available. You need not cast Rakdos just because you can: build draw or protection if opposing creature boards do not justify the trigger.

As the game develops, count distinct Demon names before every sacrifice, reconfigure, and attack. Use reanimation and discounts to deploy threats while keeping mana for protection. If Contract is unavailable, pressure life totals with flying combat and Raphael's lifelink into Daemogoth, then use Archfiend of Despair to end the game. Do not hoard all your creatures for a perfect enchantment win while an opponent assembles a faster plan.
