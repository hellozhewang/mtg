The mistake new pilots make with Terra is hesitating to Trance or treating her as a passive ramp piece. When Terra activates Trance for {4}{R}{G}, she returns transformed as Esper Terra, triggering Chapter I immediately. Her ability copies any nonlegendary enchantment you control, and if that enchantment is a Saga, she puts up to three lore counters on it. Because Sagas enter with one counter, the token copy immediately reaches four lore counters, firing Chapters I through IV simultaneously on the turn it enters. On Chapter IV, Esper Terra produces ten mana ({W}{W}{U}{U}{B}{B}{R}{R}{G}{G}) and returns to her front face, which immediately mills five cards, grabs an enchantment, and funds her own Trance to flip right back over.

Bracket 3 limits Game Changers to three: Demonic Tutor, Rhystic Study, and Smothering Tithe. Both Rhystic Study and Smothering Tithe are nonlegendary enchantments that Esper Terra can duplicate for compounding card draw and Treasure generation. Infinite mana lines via Sanctum Weaver wait until turn 6 per bracket expectations; before then, overwhelm opponents with duplicated Sagas and continuous value.

The Esper Terra copy engine
Terra, Magical Adept // Esper Terra

mills five cards on entry to put an enchantment into hand; her {4}{R}{G} Trance transforms her into Esper Terra at sorcery speed, launching a chain of duplicated Sagas

Summon: Bahamut

nine-mana Saga Dragon; when copied by Esper Terra, the token copy enters with four lore counters to immediately destroy two nonland permanents, draw two cards, and unleash Mega Flare to deal massive burn to each opponent

Summon: Knights of Round

when copied by Esper Terra, fires Chapters I through IV in one turn to generate twelve 2/2 Knight creature tokens before buffing the team with +2/+2 and indestructible

Summon: Primal Odin

copied by Esper Terra with haste, destroys a creature on Chapter I, gains Zantetsuken on Chapter II to eliminate any player it deals combat damage to, and draws two cards

Summon: Titan

copied by Esper Terra to mill five cards, return every land from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped, and pump a creature with trample scaled to your land count

Summon: Leviathan

six-mana board wipe that bounces all non-sea creatures to hand, leaving your enchantment army intact to swing and draw cards

Summon: Yojimbo

exiles an opposing threat, establishes an attack tax against opponents, and creates a flood of Treasure tokens

Summon: Valefor

bounces the highest mana-value creature each opponent controls and locks down remaining threats with stun counters

Summon: Fenrir

cheap three-mana Saga wolf that ramps a basic land, buffs your next creature, and draws a card

The Kami War // O-Kagachi Made Manifest

nonlegendary Saga that exiles a permanent on Chapter I, bounces another while forcing discards on Chapter II, and transforms into a lethal Dragon Spirit on Chapter III

Binding the Old Gods

destroys any nonland permanent, fetches a Forest onto the battlefield, and grants deathtouch to your team

Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki

produces a Goblin Shaman for Treasure generation, loots two cards, and flips into Reflection of Kiki-Jiki to copy nonlegendary creatures

Infinite mana and closing lines

Bracket 3 combo expectations require waiting until turn 6 before initiating infinite loops. Use earlier turns to develop your enchantress engine and deploy Sagas.

+
Sanctum Weaver + Freed from the Real

enchant Sanctum Weaver with Freed from the Real; tap Weaver for two or more blue mana, pay {U} to untap it, and repeat for infinite mana of all five colors to cast your entire hand and activate Trance repeatedly

++
Sanctum Weaver + Freed from the Real + Destiny Spinner

with infinite mana generated from Sanctum Weaver, activate Destiny Spinner repeatedly to animate all your lands into infinite trample power and swing for lethal

+
Terra, Magical Adept // Esper Terra + Summon: Primal Odin

copy Primal Odin with Esper Terra; the token copy gains haste and Zantetsuken, attacking immediately to eliminate a target player on combat damage

+
Terra, Magical Adept // Esper Terra + Summon: Bahamut

copy Bahamut to wipe opposing nonland permanents, draw two cards, and deal board-wide burn equal to the total mana value of your permanents via Mega Flare

Keep hands with at least 3 lands, green and white mana available, early ramp (Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, Birds of Paradise, Farseek), and a card draw engine (Sythis, Argothian Enchantress, Rhystic Study). Do not keep hands heavy on 6+ mana Sagas with no early development. Cast Terra on curve to mill and sculpt your hand, transition into Esper Terra when you control a high-value Saga or nonlegendary enchantment, and leverage your duplicated triggers to dominate the midgame.
