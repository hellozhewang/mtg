Feather recycles every instant and sorcery you aim at your own creature. Instead of going to the graveyard, the card is exiled and comes back to your hand at the end of the turn. So a one-mana cantrip on your own creature is a free card every single turn, and every spell you cast also triggers your spell payoffs (tokens, damage, counters). Fly over with Feather or the army, and pump for lethal with the same spells you replay every turn.

The commander
Feather, the Redeemed

three mana, a 3/4 flier. Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery that targets a creature you control, exile it as it resolves and return it to your hand at the beginning of the next end step.

It must TARGET a creature you control and RESOLVE. A countered spell goes to the graveyard; a spell that also targets something else still comes back.

The return happens at the next end step, yours or an opponent's. Cast a cantrip on their turn and it is back in your hand at their end step, ready again for your turn.

Copying the spells
Leyline of Resonance

whenever you cast an instant or sorcery that targets only one creature you control, copy it. Free on turn zero if it's in your opening hand.

Zada, Hedron Grinder

a spell that targets only Zada is copied for every other creature you control. Crimson Wisps on Zada draws a card per creature.

Mirrorwing Dragon

the same for its controller, as a 4/5 flier.

+
Leyline of Resonance + Crimson Wisps

the copy draws a second card, and Feather still returns the original.

+
Zada, Hedron Grinder + Defiant Strike

with eight creatures out, eight cards and +8 power spread across the team.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep Feather mana, two or three one-mana cantrips, and a payoff. Turn three Feather, then cast every cantrip on her each turn: by turn five you are drawing two or three extra cards a turn for one or two mana each.

Cast cantrips on opponents' turns when you can, and keep protection for Feather. She is the engine and the evasive clock.

Once Guttersnipe, Monastery Mentor or Stirring Hopesinger is out, every recycled spell is also damage or bodies. Win with fliers plus Temur Battle Rage, or Guttersnipe pings.
