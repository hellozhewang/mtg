A prison deck whose lock pieces are also its clock. Alela turns every artifact and enchantment you cast into a 1/1 flier, so Moat, Ghostly Prison, Propaganda, Rhystic Study and the signets are not durdle — each one is a body. You wall off the ground and win in the air, where nobody is allowed to follow.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Cyclonic Rift, Rhystic Study, and Smothering Tithe. No mass land denial.

The commander and the token engine
Alela, Artful Provocateur

flying, deathtouch, lifelink on a 2/3, other Faeries get +1/+0, and every artifact or enchantment spell makes a 1/1 flying Faerie. Read the deck as "every noncreature spell is also a creature spell".

Bitterblossom

a Faerie every upkeep for one life, forever, off a two-mana enchantment. The best early-game engine in the deck.

Bitterbloom Bearer

the second Bitterblossom, with flash. You want to see one of the two early enough that the wall has bodies behind it.

Anointed Procession

doubles every Faerie, every Bitterblossom trigger, and every Construct off Urza's Saga. With Bitterblossom out it is two free fliers a turn.

Roaming Throne

name Faerie. Alela's cast trigger doubles, and so does Bitterblossom's — the Throne is the single biggest multiplier in the deck.

Intangible Virtue

gives your Faeries +1/+1 and vigilance. Attack to draw cards through your combat engines while leaving an unassailable wall of flying blockers.

The wall, and why it is one-sided
Moat

creatures without flying cannot attack. Your entire army flies. This is not a symmetrical stax piece — it is a wall with a door only you can use.

Ghostly Prison

taxes each creature attacking you by {2}. Stacks with Propaganda to demand {4} per attacker, making attacks mathematically impossible for most boards.

Propaganda

the second attack tax. In a four-player pod the tax is a deterrent: opponents go hit whoever did not pay for one.

Sphere of Safety

taxes each creature attacking you or your planeswalkers by {X}, where X is the number of enchantments you control. With 5 to 10 enchantments out, combat against you is shut down completely.

Solitary Confinement

gives you shroud and prevents all damage dealt to you. The discard cost each upkeep is trivial behind your massive combat draw engines.

Authority of the Consuls

opponents' creatures enter tapped and gain you life. Stops haste-based combo turns and surprise blockers entirely.

Blind Obedience

artifacts and creatures enter tapped, plus extort to drain the table and offset life payments.

Cursed Totem

shuts down all activated abilities of creatures. Mana dorks, sac outlets, and activated combo lines stop cold. Your Faeries have no abilities to lose.

Torpor Orb

creature ETBs do not trigger. Shuts off opposing ETB-based engines and combos table-wide. Alela's token generation is a cast trigger, so your deck functions completely unimpeded.

Drawing off the lock
Rhystic Study

in a deck taxing opponents' mana with Prison, Propaganda, and Blind Obedience, the tax gets paid far less often. Alela creates a Faerie upon casting it.

Mystic Remora

the early-game version. Cast it turn one or two and let the cumulative upkeep run three or four turns.

Esper Sentinel

creature-based draw tax that survives Moat's world because it never needs to attack.

Smothering Tithe

the mana engine that funds everything else. Generates artifact Treasures that also trigger Mirkwood Bats.

Bident of Thassa

both an artifact and an enchantment. Draws a card for every flier connecting in combat, and can tap to force opponents' creatures to attack into each other.

Kindred Discovery

name Faerie. Draws on every token entering and every token attacking, refilling your hand in bursts of three to six cards.

Reconnaissance Mission

draws a card for every creature that deals combat damage to a player, easily triggering on three or four opponents' open boards.

Enduring Curiosity

redundant combat draw on an enchantment creature that returns as an enchantment when it dies.

Idol of Oblivion

generating tokens every turn guarantees a free card every turn; late it can crack into an emergency 10/10.

Turning Faeries into cards and kills
Skullclamp

your tokens are 1/1s that Alela makes 2/1. Clamping one kills it and draws two cards. A bottomless draw engine alongside Bitterblossom.

Grave Pact

every clamped or sacrificed Faerie forces all three opponents to sacrifice a creature. Draw two, wipe opposing boards, and repeat.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

feed her expendable Faeries or spent Seals to draw up to three cards and drain opponents for up to six life every end step.

Urza's Saga

constructs massive threats and tutors Skullclamp, Sol Ring, or Mox Opal directly into play before generating mana.

Closing the game
Approach of the Second Sun

the premier noncombat win condition. Behind your prison wall, cast it to gain 7 life, draw seven cards across a single turn cycle with your mass draw engines, and recast it to win the game.

Mirkwood Bats

drains each opponent for 1 life whenever you create or sacrifice any token. Alela's Faeries, Bitterblossom tokens, Tithe Treasures, and Skullclamp sacrifices passively burn the table down.

All That Glitters

turns Alela into a lethal 20/20+ flying, deathtouch, lifelink commander that deletes players in a single swing.

Akroma's Will

grants double strike, lifelink, vigilance, and protection from all creature colors for a lethal alpha strike across the table.

Cyclonic Rift

overloads at instant speed to bounce all opposing nonland permanents, clearing the way for an unimpeded lethal swing.

Answers and protection
Teferi, Time Raveler

forces opponents to cast spells at sorcery speed, completely shutting off counterspells and combat interaction on your turn while guaranteeing Approach of the Second Sun resolves.

Grasp of Fate

exiles the best nonland permanent from each opponent for just three mana, providing a clean 3-for-1 that also triggers Alela.

Mana Drain

premium counterspell whose stored mana deploys two or three prison pieces on your next turn.

Swan Song

one-mana counterspell. The 2/2 swan it grants is taxed by Ghostly Prison and Propaganda, and blocked cleanly by Alela.

Swords to Plowshares

unconditional spot exile for any problematic flyer or commander that bypasses Moat.

Oubliette

phases out a commander without sending it to the command zone or graveyard, removing it indefinitely.

Imprisoned in the Moon

transforms any opposing commander or troublesome land into a harmless colourless land.

Darksteel Mutation

turns a commander into an indestructible 0/1 Beetle with no abilities.

Seal of Cleansing

pre-pay for artifact or enchantment removal, crack it at instant speed, or feed it to Braids.

Aura of Silence

taxes opposing artifacts and enchantments by {2}, then sacrifices to destroy the most dangerous one.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a hand that does two of these three things: produces all three colors of mana, deploys a token engine (Bitterblossom, Bitterbloom Bearer), and drops an early stax piece (Moat, Ghostly Prison, Blind Obedience, Cursed Totem).

Alela does not need to be cast on turn four. She is a value amplifier, not the wall itself. Deploy your prison pieces and mana rocks first so opponents are already throttled before Alela hits the board.

Hold Moat until creature decks commit to the board, and prioritize finding Skullclamp or Bident of Thassa to keep your hand full. Once the fortress is secure, use Teferi, Time Raveler to protect your winning Approach of the Second Sun or close the game with an unblockable flying alpha strike.
