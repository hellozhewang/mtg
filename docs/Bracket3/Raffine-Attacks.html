Every time you attack, Raffine makes one of your attackers connive once for each attacking creature: draw that many, discard that many, and a +1/+1 counter for each nonland card discarded. So the deck goes wide with token makers that attack, and every swing is card draw that also grows an evasive threat. The discards feed a reanimation package, so a discarded Archon of Cruelty comes straight back.

The commander
Raffine, Scheming Seer

three mana, a 1/4 flier with ward {1}. Whenever you attack, target attacking creature connives X, where X is the number of attacking creatures: draw X, discard X, and put a +1/+1 counter on it for each nonland card discarded.

Raffine triggers when you attack with ANY creatures; she doesn't have to attack herself. Attack with five tokens and one of them draws and discards five.

Target the creature that is hardest to block (a flier, Fallen Shinobi, a Sword carrier), since it keeps the counters.

Key pairs
+
Raffine, Scheming Seer + Archfiend of Ifnir

every card you discard to connive puts a -1/-1 counter on each creature your opponents control. Conniving five is five counters on every blocker they have.

+
Raffine, Scheming Seer + Hero of Bladehold

Hero makes two attacking Soldiers and battle cry pumps the team; each extra attacker is one more card.

+
Raffine, Scheming Seer + Voice of Victory

mobilize 2 adds two attacking Warriors, and opponents can't cast spells during your turn, so the attack and the connive resolve undisturbed.

+
Raffine, Scheming Seer + Adeline, Resplendent Cathar

a Human token per opponent every attack: three more attackers, three more cards.

→
Entomb → Archon of Cruelty

put it in the graveyard, then Reanimate, Animate Dead, Dance of the Dead or Necromancy it back. Connive discards do the same job for free.

The reanimation package

Discarded creatures are the targets; conniving puts them in the graveyard.

Archon of Cruelty

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep three-colour mana, Raffine, and one cheap creature or token maker. Turn three Raffine, turn four attack with two creatures and connive two.

Discard creatures you can reanimate, and lands once the board is set; nonland discards grow the conniver.

When the board is wide, protect the alpha strike with Grand Abolisher or Silence, then Akroma's Will or Moonshaker Cavalry.
