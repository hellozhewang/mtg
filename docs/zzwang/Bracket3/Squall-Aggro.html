Attack alone with Squall to grant him double strike, connecting twice to return two low-cost permanents from your graveyard every combat, backed by Sun Titan recursion and the Soultrader-Gravecrawler combo.

Rough Divide and combat recursion mechanics
Squall, SeeD Mercenary

Rough Divide grants double strike to any creature you control that attacks alone until end of turn. Whenever Squall deals combat damage to a player, return target permanent card with mana value three or less from your graveyard to the battlefield. Because double strike deals damage in both first-strike and regular combat damage steps, connecting unblocked returns TWO permanents in a single turn.

Brotherhood Regalia

equips for two mana to make Squall unblockable and grant ward {2}, guaranteeing that both combat damage strikes connect unhindered.

Shadowspear

provides trample and lifelink while removing hexproof and indestructible from opposing blockers.

Sword of Feast and Famine

grants protection from black and green, forces opposing discard, and untaps all your lands twice when attacking alone with double strike.

Sword of Fire and Ice

grants protection from red and blue, deals two damage to any target, and draws a card on each strike (drawing two cards and dealing four damage per combat).

Sublime Archangel

grants exalted to all creatures you control. With a modest board of four creatures, Squall attacks alone as an 8/7 double strike threat.

Recurring utility engines (MV 3 or less)
Accursed Marauder

forces each player to sacrifice a nontoken creature on entry; recur it with Squall each combat to decimate opposing boards while preserving Squall.

Cathar Commando

flash creature that sacrifices itself for one generic mana to destroy target artifact or enchantment; recur it each turn to shatter opposing engines.

Seal of Cleansing

enchantment that sits on board and sacrifices itself for zero mana to destroy an artifact or enchantment.

Selfless Spirit

flying creature that sacrifices itself to grant all your creatures indestructible until end of turn; recur it every combat to maintain permanent board wipe immunity.

Selfless Savior

sacrifices itself to grant target creature you control indestructible until end of turn.

Boromir, Warden of the Tower

counters free spells and sacrifices itself to grant indestructible and allow the Ring to tempt you.

Esper Sentinel

taxes opposing noncreature spells with card draw.

Dauthi Voidwalker

exiles all opposing cards that would enter the graveyard and can sacrifice itself to cast an exiled card for free.

Lurrus of the Dream-Den

companion/creature allowing you to cast one permanent spell with mana value two or less from your graveyard each turn.

Lotho, Corrupt Shirriff

creates Treasures whenever any player casts their second spell each turn.

Dedicated combo finish (Turn 6+)
++
Warren Soultrader + Gravecrawler + Blood Artist

Warren Soultrader is a Zombie. With Blood Artist and Soultrader on the battlefield and Gravecrawler in hand or graveyard, sacrifice Gravecrawler to Soultrader, paying one life to create a Treasure token. Blood Artist triggers to drain target opponent for one life and gain you one life, offsetting the life payment. Spend the Treasure for {B} to cast Gravecrawler from the graveyard using Soultrader's Zombie typing. Repeat for infinite life drain to defeat all opponents.

Sun Titan

enters or attacks to return any permanent card with mana value three or less, reinforcing Squall's recursion.

Karmic Guide

returns any creature from graveyard to battlefield upon entering, providing an outlet to bring back Sun Titan or large threats.

Interaction, tutors, and opening hands
Demonic Tutor

finds Brotherhood Regalia, an equipment, or a combo piece.

Buried Alive

searches three creatures (e.g., Soultrader, Gravecrawler, Blood Artist) directly into your graveyard to set up recursion.

Smothering Tithe

generates extensive Treasure acceleration to pay for recasting commanders or large recursion turns.

Teferi's Protection

shields your entire board from mass removal or lethal opposing swings.

Deadly Rollick

free creature exile while controlling Squall.

Toxic Deluge

efficient life-based board sweeper that clears opposing blockers.

Keep opening hands with two to three lands, early ramp (Sol Ring, Signets, Talismans), and an evasive equipment or self-sacrificing utility creature (Selfless Spirit, Cathar Commando, Esper Sentinel). Cast Squall on turn three or four, equip him with protection or evasion, and swing alone to begin generating double recursion advantage every combat. Assemble combo lines or overwhelm with exalted damage on Turn 6 or later.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, card advantage, and combat pressure.
