Leverage Tifa's exponential power doubling on every landfall trigger to eliminate opponents with lethal trample commander damage, accelerated by multiple land drops and Scapeshift.

Exponential power doubling mechanics
Tifa Lockhart

possesses natural trample. Her landfall trigger doubles her power until end of turn each time a land enters the battlefield under your control. One land drop doubles her from two to four power; two lands yields eight power; three lands yields sixteen; four lands yields thirty-two; and five lands yields sixty-four trample damage.

Scale Up

sorcery that makes Tifa a 6/4 Wurm until end of turn. Landfall triggers resolving afterward double her power from six to twelve, twenty-four, forty-eight, or ninety-six power.

Berserk

one-mana green instant that grants trample and doubles target creature's current power. Cast Berserk after landfall triggers resolve to double an already massive Tifa into an unblockable one-shot kill.

Unnatural Growth

doubles the power and toughness of each creature you control at the beginning of each combat, compounding Tifa's pre-combat landfall growth.

Adventuring Gear

grants an additional +2/+2 on each landfall trigger, giving Tifa extra base stats before each doubling calculation.

Mass landfall and fetchland chains
Scapeshift

sacrifices any number of lands to search that many lands from your library directly onto the battlefield. Sacrificing four lands to put four lands into play generates four simultaneous landfall triggers, catapulting Tifa to thirty-two power.

Awaken the Woods

creates X 1/1 green Forest Dryad land creature tokens. Each token counts as a land entering the battlefield, triggering X landfall triggers in a single resolution.

Crop Rotation

one-mana instant sacrificing a land to put any land (such as Nykthos, Cradle, or a fetchland) onto the battlefield at instant speed during combat.

Harrow

sacrifices a land as an additional cost to search two basic lands directly onto the battlefield untapped at instant speed, providing two surprise combat landfall triggers.

Entish Restoration

searches two basic lands onto the battlefield tapped, or three if you control a creature with power four or greater (which Tifa easily fulfills after one land drop).

Lotus Cobra

adds one mana of any color on each landfall trigger, financing further spells and activations during explosive combo turns.

Tireless Provisioner

generates a Treasure or Food token on every land entry to accumulate ramp or life.

Extra land drops and graveyard land recursion
Azusa, Lost but Seeking

allows you to play two additional lands on each of your turns.

Exploration

allows one additional land play each turn for a single mana.

Dryad of the Ilysian Grove

allows an additional land play each turn and fixes lands to all basic land types.

Oracle of Mul Daya

reveals the top of your library, allows playing lands from top, and grants an additional land drop.

Crucible of Worlds

allows playing lands from your graveyard, turning fetchlands into repeating double-landfall engines every turn.

Ramunap Excavator

creature version of Crucible of Worlds that recurs fetchlands from the graveyard.

Conduit of Worlds

plays lands from graveyard and can cast a nonland permanent from graveyard once per turn.

Ancient Greenwarden

allows playing lands from graveyard and doubles all land-triggered abilities, causing each land to trigger Tifa TWICE for quadruple power growth per land!

Card draw, protection, and opening hands
Rishkar's Expertise

draws cards equal to the greatest power among creatures you control (drawing sixteen to thirty-two cards with Tifa) and casts a card with mana value five or less for free.

The Great Henge

taps for two green mana, gains life, and draws cards whenever nontoken creatures enter.

The One Ring

grants protection from everything upon casting and provides compounding card draw.

Sylvan Safekeeper

sacrifices a land to give target creature you control shroud at instant speed; the sacrificed land can then be replayed with Crucible or Excavator.

Legolas's Quick Reflexes

one-mana instant granting hexproof, reach, untap, and letting Tifa deal damage to attacking or blocking creatures.

Tamiyo's Safekeeping

gives target permanent hexproof and indestructible while gaining two life.

Heroic Intervention

grants all your permanents hexproof and indestructible at instant speed.

Keep opening hands with two to three lands, early exploration or ramp (Exploration, Nature's Lore, Three Visits), and a fetchland or tutor. Cast Tifa early on turn two, establish extra land drops or fetchland recursion on turns three and four, and deliver lethal commander damage on turn five or six using Scapeshift, Berserk, or multi-fetch landfall sequences.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, land drops, and ramp engines.
