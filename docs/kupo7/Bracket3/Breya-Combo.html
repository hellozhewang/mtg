Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Breya is not just an artifact value engine; she is an infinite mana sink and sacrifice payoff sitting in the command zone. The single biggest mistake a new pilot makes is casting Breya on curve without protection or an active loop ready. Her true strength is assembling Ashnod's Altar or Krark-Clan Ironworks alongside Nim Deathmantle or Eldrazi Displacer, then deploying Breya to immediately close the game in a single turn.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor, and The One Ring. No mass land denial. Infinite combo lines assemble turns 5–6 and execute from turn 6 onward.

The core loops
++
Breya, Etherium Shaper + Nim Deathmantle + Ashnod's Altar

sacrifice two Thopters to the Altar for four mana, then sacrifice Breya for two mana; pay four into Deathmantle's trigger to return Breya with two fresh Thopters, netting two colorless mana per loop. Repeat for infinite colorless mana and infinite Thopters, then spend the mana and Thopters into Breya's ability to deal infinite damage to all opponents.

++
Breya, Etherium Shaper + Nim Deathmantle + Krark-Clan Ironworks

operates identically to the Altar loop because Breya and her Thopter tokens are all artifacts, sacrificing to KCI for two mana each. Generates infinite colorless mana, infinite tokens, and infinite Breya damage.

++
Breya, Etherium Shaper + Eldrazi Displacer + Ashnod's Altar

sacrifice two Thopters to the Altar for four mana, then pay three mana to activate Displacer flickering Breya; Breya returns with two fresh Thopters, netting one colorless mana per loop. Repeat for infinite colorless mana, infinite Thopters, and infinite Breya damage.

++
Breya, Etherium Shaper + Eldrazi Displacer + Krark-Clan Ironworks

identical to the Displacer-Altar loop using KCI as the sacrifice outlet.

++
Thopter Foundry + Sword of the Meek + Ashnod's Altar

pay one to sacrifice Sword to Foundry, creating a 1/1 Thopter and gaining one life; Sword triggers on the 1/1 entering and returns attached to it; sacrifice the Thopter to Altar for two mana. Net one mana and one life per repetition for infinite mana, infinite life, and infinite Thopters.

++
Thopter Foundry + Sword of the Meek + Time Sieve

pay five mana into Thopter Foundry to create five Thopters while recurring Sword of the Meek; tap Time Sieve and sacrifice the five Thopters to take an extra turn. Repeat every turn for infinite turns.

++
Sensei's Divining Top + Mystic Forge + Etherium Sculptor

with Sculptor reducing Top's cost to zero, tap Top to draw a card and put it on top of your library; cast Top for free from your library with Mystic Forge. Repeat to draw your entire deck and assemble any winning line.

What to keep and sequencing

A functional opening hand requires three lands or two lands with a mana rock (Sol Ring, Signet, Talisman), access to blue and black mana, and either a tutor (Tribute Mage, Fabricate, Goblin Engineer) or an early draw engine (Mystic Remora, Esper Sentinel, Skullclamp). Never keep hands with four colors of lands but zero plays before turn four.

Spend turns 1 through 3 deploying mana rocks, establishing cost reducers, and accumulating Thopter fodder with Sai or Lotho.

Do not run Breya out onto an empty board without purpose; she is your finisher. Use your tutors to locate Nim Deathmantle and Ashnod's Altar (or KCI).

When ready to combo on turn 6 or later, lead with Silence or Grand Abolisher to lock opponents out of interaction. Cast your sacrifice outlet and Deathmantle first, then cast Breya and execute the infinite loop in response to her enter-the-battlefield trigger.
