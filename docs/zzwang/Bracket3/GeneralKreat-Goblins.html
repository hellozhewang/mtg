In this deck every creature is a burn spell. Kreat deals 1 damage to each opponent whenever another creature enters under your control — tokens included — so Siege-Gang Commander is four damage to every opponent before it does anything else, and a Krenko activation is as much damage as it makes Goblins. Stack more cards that do the same thing and each creature does more: with Impact Tremors, Purphoros, Agate Instigator and Molten Gatekeeper all out, one 1/1 entering is 6 damage to every opponent.

Only creatures YOU control count. Tokens you hand an opponent — from Tempt with Vengeance — trigger nothing.

That also makes every infinite-creature loop an infinite-damage loop: with Kreat out, each copy entering is 1 damage to each opponent. The deck has four of them — see the combos.

The commander
General Kreat, the Boltbringer

three mana. Whenever another creature enters under your control, 1 damage to each opponent. Whenever your Goblins attack, a 1/1 Goblin joins the attack — which is another creature entering, so another ping.

Lightning Greaves

shroud the turn Kreat lands, and haste so Krenko, Mob Boss can tap the turn it lands. Never equip it to Kiki-Jiki — shroud stops Zealous Conscripts or Restoration Angel from targeting Kiki.

Swiftfoot Boots

the second copy.

Hexing Squelcher

every other creature you control has "ward — pay 2 life," and your spells can't be countered.

Deflecting Swat

free with Kreat out; send their removal somewhere harmless.

Pyroblast

one mana to counter the blue spell aimed at your combo, or destroy a blue permanent.

Red Elemental Blast

the same, a second time.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Zealous Conscripts

Kiki copies Conscripts, and the copy's enter trigger untaps Kiki — target Kiki itself. Repeat for as many hasty 3/3s as you want, the same turn Kiki lands (it has haste). With Kreat out every copy entering is 1 damage to each opponent, so the loop wins on the spot. Never leave Lightning Greaves on Kiki: shroud stops the Conscripts copy targeting it.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Goblin Bombardment

Kiki copies the Crafter. The copy's champion trigger exiles Kiki; the hasty copy taps for 3 damage; sacrifice it to Goblin Bombardment; Kiki returns untapped — it has haste — and copies the Crafter again. Unlimited 3-damage shots, so this one kills on its own. Skirk Prospector work as the outlet too. The original Crafter must stay on the battlefield; when it enters you must exile another Goblin or Shaman (champion) or sacrifice it.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Thornbite Staff + Goblin Bombardment

the Staff attaches itself to Kiki when Kiki enters after it (otherwise equip costs {4}). Kiki copies any nonlegendary creature, you sacrifice a creature to Goblin Bombardment, and the death untaps Kiki. Unlimited loops. Every copy entering is also a Kreat ping.

→+
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Conspicuous Snoop

Recruiter puts Kiki on TOP of your library. Snoop has every activated ability of the Goblin on top, so it copies itself with Kiki's ability — and every hasty copy does the same. Snoop needs haste or a turn in play to start, and most copies end up tapped, so finish with Kreat's pings — every copy entering is 1 damage to each opponent. Rundvelt Hordemaster exiles your top card whenever a Goblin dies, so don't let one die while Kiki sits on top. Outside the combo, Snoop lets you cast Goblins straight off the top of your library.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Thornbite Staff + Skirk Prospector

equip the Staff to Krenko ({4} — he's a Warrior, so it won't attach itself). Krenko taps for a Goblin per Goblin; sacrifice one to Skirk Prospector; the death untaps Krenko; tap again. The army doubles every lap. Win with Kreat: every new Goblin is 1 damage to each opponent.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

in your main phase, cast Twinflame on any creature you control, then flash Dualcaster in with Twinflame still on the stack. Dualcaster copies Twinflame onto itself; that hasty token copies it again; repeat. Unlimited hasty 2/2s, each one a Kreat ping.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when
→
Goblin Matron → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

when you already hold Zealous Conscripts, Lightning Crafter or the Staff — Kiki is the piece that turns half a combo into a kill.

→
Goblin Matron → Goblin Recruiter

when you're missing two or more pieces. Matron gets Recruiter; Recruiter stacks the rest on top of your library.

→
Goblin Matron → Krenko, Mob Boss

when Thornbite Staff is already out, or when you simply need a board this turn.

→
Goblin Matron → Lightning Crafter

when Kiki-Jiki is on the battlefield and you have a free outlet — the Crafter is the last piece.

→
Goblin Matron → Skirk Prospector

when the combo is assembled but nothing will sacrifice for free.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

power 2, so the one non-Goblin tutor that still finds Kiki. It also gets Dualcaster Mage when you hold Twinflame, or Goblin Recruiter, Conspicuous Snoop or Skirk Prospector.

→
Gamble → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

any card — usually Kiki, the Staff or Goblin Recruiter, whichever has no backup. Cast it with a full hand: the random discard is then less likely to hit the card you just fetched.

→
Goblin Engineer → Thornbite Staff

puts the Staff into your graveyard, then {R}, tap and sacrifice another artifact puts it onto the battlefield. Engineer needs a turn or haste before it can tap.

→++
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

without Snoop, stack the missing pieces with the one you need first on top. Recruiter only arranges your library — Goblin Ringleader, Conspicuous Snoop or your draws reach it — so unless something reaches it this turn, cast it the turn before you go off, not on curve.

Goblin Ringleader

every Goblin in your top four goes to your hand — the cleanest way to cash in a Recruiter stack.

Goblin Recruiter setups — what to stack, top card first
→
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

with Conspicuous Snoop able to tap — every Snoop copy entering is a Kreat ping. Don't let a Goblin die while Kiki sits on top: Rundvelt Hordemaster would exile it.

→→→→
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker → Lightning Crafter → Skirk Prospector → Goblin Warchief

then cast Muxus: all four enter at once — four Kreat pings — and the Crafter champions Recruiter, so Kiki can start copying it immediately.

→→→→
Goblin Recruiter → Siege-Gang Commander → Krenko, Mob Boss → Goblin Matron → Goblin Chieftain

the refill for Goblin Ringleader; every one of them is several Kreat pings.

Anything that searches your library shuffles it and wrecks the stack — Goblin Matron, Imperial Recruiter and Gamble. Use those before Recruiter, never after.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep two or three lands and two-drops that make bodies. Kreat lands on turn three; from then on every creature is damage.

Deploy the pingers before the token spells. Siege-Gang Commander is 4 damage with Kreat alone and 20 with Impact Tremors, Purphoros and Agate Instigator out.

Krenko, Mob Boss needs haste to tap the turn it lands: have Goblin Warchief, Goblin Chieftain, Howlsquad Heavy, The Fire Crystal or Greaves ready. Kiki-Jiki has haste already.

Red has little protection. Against a sweeper, sacrifice to Goblin Bombardment or Skirk Prospector in response — every death still counts for Pashalik Mons.

Complete Snoop package — compact wins from turn 6
+++
Conspicuous Snoop + Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Goblin Recruiter + Mogg Fanatic

With Snoop able to tap, resolve Recruiter and put Kiki on top with Fanatic below it. Snoop gains Kiki's activated ability and can target itself because it is nonlegendary. Make more than 120 hasty Snoop copies, leaving the newest one untapped. Use that copy to copy Recruiter instead of Snoop. The new Recruiter puts Fanatic on top, so every surviving Snoop gains Fanatic's sacrifice ability. Sacrifice at least 40 Snoops for 1 damage each to each of three opponents. Most Snoops being tapped does not prevent the sacrifice ability. Kiki and Fanatic must still be in the library when searched; Recruiter cannot put a card from your hand back on top.

+
Torch Courier + Conspicuous Snoop

Cast Courier for {R} and sacrifice it targeting Snoop for haste this turn. Courier's ability has no tap cost, so it works immediately. Do this before starting the Kiki-top sequence. If Courier is your only haste source and still in the library, Recruiter can stack Courier, then Kiki, then Fanatic: cast Courier off the top through Snoop, sacrifice it to give Snoop haste, and Kiki is now revealed. Avoid extra draws, mills, exiles and shuffles that move Kiki away from the top before the copying ends. Shroud prevents Courier or Snoop's copied Kiki ability from targeting Snoop.

→
Goblin Recruiter → Goblin Ringleader

For a refill, put Ringleader on top followed by Torch Courier, Conspicuous Snoop, Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker and Mogg Fanatic. Draw Ringleader, then pay {3}{R}; its entry trigger puts the next four Goblins into your hand. If Ringleader is already in hand, omit it from the pile and cast it before another draw. This is a four-card hand refill, not an immediate Snoop kill: Kiki in hand cannot grant Snoop its ability from the library. Choose the Kiki-top pile instead when Snoop is ready to win. Additional draws require padding or a revised pile; use fetches and other shuffling searches before Recruiter.

Mogg Fanatic

The library-top finisher lets Snoop tokens sacrifice themselves for damage without another permanent payoff. An ordinary Fanatic on the battlefield can also sacrifice itself for one damage; that single card by itself is not an unlimited damage engine. Keep compact wins for turn six or later under the house rules.
