Zada-Turbo keeps the Goblin identity of Zada-Goblins and leaves that Bracket 3 list untouched. The first priority is a small army plus one targeted cantrip, not a large unsupported commander. Fast mana and rituals bring the spell-chain turn forward; the full Recruiter/Snoop package remains an independent win.

Turn one targeted spell into the next hand
Zada, Hedron Grinder

Cast an instant or sorcery that targets only Zada. Its trigger copies that spell for every other creature you control it could legally target. Copies are not cast and do not trigger cast-count engines again.

+
Expedite + Crimson Wisps

One red can draw for each legal creature target while making them ready to attack. Casting the spell with only Zada on the battlefield is just one cantrip, not a refill.

+
Renegade Tactics + Ancestral Anger

Cheap draw chains. Tactics copies target your own creatures rather than removing opposing blockers; Anger's scaling checks Anger cards in the graveyard, not the number of copies on the stack.

+
Impolite Entrance + Might of the Meek

More cheap targeted cantrips with combat utility. Apply the original and copies individually; a spell that no longer has a legal target will not provide its draw.

Fists of Flame

Each copy draws, then buffs its own target using the number of cards drawn so far that turn. Later resolutions may give larger bonuses. Spread attackers across all three opponents instead of putting every point into one player.

Temur Battle Rage

Gives the army double strike, and trample if you have a creature with power four or greater. Work out damage and remaining blockers before declaring a combat kill.

++
Reckless Ransacking + Sudden Breakthrough + Ancestors' Aid

Every resolved copy creates its own Treasure, so paying two mana can produce far more than two mana with enough bodies. Those Treasures fund the next cantrip and leave mana available for protection.

Frontline Heroism

A targeted spell creates another creature and its own extra copy. Resolve Heroism's trigger before Zada's trigger when you want the new Soldier included among Zada's eligible creatures. Copies do not trigger Heroism again.

Young Pyromancer

Original instant and sorcery casts add bodies. Resolve its token trigger before Zada's copy-making trigger to include the new Elemental when it is a legal target.

+
Goblin Instigator + Mogg War Marshal

Two-mana ways to stock the battlefield. Marshal's death also replaces itself with another Goblin, giving Prospector or Bombardment material without relying on a future attack.

+
Dragon Fodder + Krenko's Command

Unexciting two-body spells become efficient setup when every extra creature is another draw and another Treasure on the combo turn.

Empty the Warrens

Use a few spells first, then cash storm into enough Goblins to turn a single cantrip into a new hand. The new tokens lack haste until a haste effect resolves; storm copies themselves are not casts.

+
Battle Hymn + Brightstone Ritual

The burst-mana backbone. Hymn counts every creature you control, while Brightstone counts Goblins across the battlefield. Do not sacrifice away the count before resolving the ritual.

+++
Rite of Flame + Desperate Ritual + Pyretic Ritual + Seething Song

Bridge the opening mana gap before the army can pay for itself. These originals increase storm; their copied effects do not.

+
Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty + Storm-Kiln Artist

Birgi triggers once per cast. Artist triggers on both casting and copying an instant or sorcery, so a Zada spell produces multiple Treasures from Artist even though Birgi triggers only once.

Electro, Assaulting Battery

Refunds red on original instant/sorcery casts and preserves unspent red between steps. It does not refund each Zada copy. If Electro leaves, its paid damage trigger targets only one player.

++
Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty + Grinning Ignus + General Kreat, the Boltbringer

Pay three to cast Ignus and get Birgi's red. Spend that red to return Ignus, adding three mana for its next cast. Each creature entry triggers Kreat against all opponents. This requires one initial Ignus cast's mana but does not require Zada.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

Start Twinflame targeting a creature you already control, then flash in Dualcaster. Its entry copies Twinflame targeting Dualcaster; every new hasty Dualcaster repeats that copy. Finish with combat or creature-entry damage. An existing creature and the full initial mana are prerequisites.

++
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication + Heat Shimmer

Either spell also supports the Dualcaster loop. Let each entry copy the ORIGINAL spell still on the stack, targeting Dualcaster. The original spell is not cast again during the loop.

+
General Kreat, the Boltbringer + Goblin Bombardment

Same-turn noncombat outlets when a creature loop cannot safely attack. Bombardment can also cash a targeted creature out in response to exile, but that creature's cantrip copy will lose its target.

++
Chrome Mox + Mox Diamond + Mana Vault

The upgrade's mana jump. Pay the real imprint/discard costs; this creature-heavy list does not rely on turning on metalcraft for an early mox. Vault supplies generic mana for Zada; your lands still need to supply red for the chain.

Infernal Plunge

Sacrifice a disposable creature as an additional casting cost to turn one red into three. War Marshal can leave a replacement Goblin when sacrificed. Do not sacrifice Zada or the only body needed for the next draw chain; countering Plunge still costs the sacrificed creature.

++
Gamble + Underworld Breach + Past in Flames

Gamble can find a missing engine; the graveyard spells recover rituals and cantrips after a stalled chain. Escape needs three other cards per cast; flashback exiles each spell. Neither grants unlimited graveyard fuel.

Recruiter piles and independent Goblin wins
→
Goblin Matron → Goblin Recruiter

Matron puts Recruiter into hand; budget the second cast. Find the engine rather than another anthem when a protected combo is available.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Goblin Recruiter

Recruiter has power one and is a legal target. Imperial also finds Snoop, Prospector, Courier or Kiki when that is the missing piece.

+
Goblin Recruiter + Goblin Ringleader

For a four-card refill, put Ringleader first, then Snoop, Courier, Prospector and Squelcher. Draw Ringleader, cast it, and take those four Goblins. Put Kiki immediately below the four if assembling Snoop next. Leave Fanatic in the library.

+
Goblin Recruiter + Muxus, Goblin Grandee

A six-hit deployment is Warchief, Kiki, Crafter, Prospector, Snoop and Fanatic. All six are Goblin creatures with mana value five or less. Crafter can champion the Recruiter already in play; Kiki/Crafter/Prospector then supplies the immediate kill. Cast Muxus from the command zone in its own deck, or have it in hand in the others.

Complete fetches before Recruiter resolves. Drawing, milling or shuffling afterward changes the planned top cards. A pile is not a guarantee if a needed piece is already in hand or the graveyard; use a different route rather than pretending it is still in the library.

+
Conspicuous Snoop + Torch Courier

The direct pile is Snoop on top to draw, followed by Courier and Kiki. After drawing and casting Snoop, cast Courier from the revealed top and sacrifice it to give Snoop haste. Kiki must now remain on top, not in hand or on the battlefield.

+
Conspicuous Snoop + Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

With Kiki revealed, tap the hasty Snoop to copy itself. Each new Snoop token has haste and the same copy ability. Use the newest untapped copy repeatedly to make as many Snoops as needed.

++
Conspicuous Snoop + Goblin Recruiter + Mogg Fanatic

Use the last Snoop's copied Kiki ability to copy the original Recruiter. The token Recruiter puts Fanatic on top. Every Snoop now gains Fanatic's sacrifice-for-one-damage ability. Sacrifice enough Snoops to kill all three opponents. Stack every needed sacrifice before resolving other effects that change the library top. Keep Recruiter on the battlefield until that final copy; having Fanatic in hand does not grant its ability.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

Copy Crafter, champion Kiki with the new hasty copy, tap that copy for three damage, then sacrifice it to Prospector. Kiki returns untapped with haste. Repeat for lethal damage and red mana. The original Crafter must champion a different Goblin or Shaman first; do not accidentally exile the only required outlet.

Goblin Warchief

Supplies haste and reduces only generic costs of Goblin spells. It makes Muxus cheaper, but cannot reduce Snoop's two red pips or Kiki's three red pips.

Hexing Squelcher

Makes spells uncounterable and taxes targeting of your other creatures with life. This does not stop removal, exile, sacrifice, triggered counters to abilities, or an opponent paying the ward cost.

Goblin Bombardment

Converts assembled tokens into damage without combat or haste. It is also a free outlet for Crafter copies, replacing Prospector's mana with an additional damage per loop.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two durable red sources, an early way to make multiple bodies, and a cheap targeted draw spell or tutor. Mulligan isolated Zada-plus-pump hands and fast-mana hands that cannot make red. Cast Zada after developing bodies or protect it long enough to use the first spell. Aim to execute around turn four or later rather than waiting for the Bracket 3 turn-six expectation.

+
Deflecting Swat + Untimely Malfunction

Redirect targeted removal before it removes Zada. The army's copies may still resolve if Zada dies after its trigger has resolved, but losing the commander before the next cast stops the chain.

++
Pyroblast + Red Elemental Blast + Chaos Warp

Remove the specific hate piece or blue answer before going all-in. Creature-activation hate can break the Snoop finish while leaving a spell-copy combat line available; a one-spell-per-turn restriction stops the storm plan and needs answering first.

Plan combat before the chain: preserve enough attackers, do not assume newly created Goblins have haste, and keep a way to finish without combat when the board is stalled. Tutor and fetch before a Recruiter pile, and count the cards remaining before resolving a large stack of mandatory cantrip draws.
