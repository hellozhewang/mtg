Build an army before treating Jace as a cheat-into-play engine. This version wins through token combat and creature-entry damage; Jace's +1 supplies cards, and his -3 turns an expendable body into another engine or a possible finisher. Cheap token creatures are intentional inclusions, so a random -3 is not guaranteed to find an expensive threat. The deck should function when you never cast the commander.

The three Game Changers are Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, and Teferi's Protection. The list has 37 lands and two additional land-option spells, ten mana rocks, Smothering Tithe, and Professional Face-Breaker; Black Market Connections and a transformed Legion's Landing add further mana. No dedicated infinite combo, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain is included. Protect and grow an established board rather than race to an early compact combo kill.

Establish a token engine before adding multipliers
Legion's Landing // Adanto, the First Fort

One mana gets a lifelinking body, and declaring three attackers transforms the enchantment into another mana source. Creatures created already attacking do not count toward that attack trigger. Its land face can produce tokens on opponents' turns for your draw engines.

Bitterblossom

The inexpensive recurring source of flying attackers also supplies blockers for Jace. Each upkeep costs one life, regardless of how many tokens your replacement effects produce. It keeps rebuilding through creature wipes.

Skrelv's Hive

A second cheap recurring source, but its Mites cannot block. Use them to attack, pay convoke costs, or feed Jace and Skullclamp. Their toxic ability is incidental pressure; this list does not rely on poison to win, and increasing their power does not increase toxic 1.

Lingering Souls

Two flying creatures now and another two through flashback give a useful board from one card. Cast it before a multiplier if you need blockers; waiting for maximum value is not worth losing the early turns.

Charismatic Conqueror

Opponents either tap eligible artifacts and creatures as its trigger resolves or give you lifelinking tokens. They choose, so token production is not guaranteed. Tokens made on their turns can trigger Caretaker's Talent and support Bennie Bracks at their end steps.

Adeline, Resplendent Cathar

Any declared attack triggers her, even if she stays back. With three opponents, the trigger creates three Humans, allocated one to attack each opponent or their planeswalker before replacement effects multiply them. She supplies repeatable production and becomes a large vigilant attacker herself.

Ainok Strike Leader

Because Jace normally cannot attack, this creature must attack to enable its token trigger here. It creates a Goblin attacking each opponent, then can sacrifice itself to make all your creature tokens indestructible for the turn. Sacrificing it after its trigger is on the stack does not stop token creation.

Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon

Attack with any non-Gnome to grow her and create attacking Gnomes; she need not attack herself. Windcrag Siege's Mardu mode gives a second trigger, which adds another counter before calculating the second batch. Token multipliers multiply the number created, not the counters on Anim Pakal.

Hero of Bladehold

A token generator and attack pump in one slot. Put battle cry on the stack first and the token trigger above it so the new Soldiers exist when battle cry resolves. Windcrag Siege can double both triggers; resolve all token creation before the battle cry bonuses.

Myrel, Shield of Argive

During your turn she stops opposing spells and activated abilities of artifacts, creatures, and enchantments. Opposing triggered abilities and land abilities still work. Her attack creates untapped Soldiers that are not attacking, with their number counted on resolution. A second trigger from Windcrag Siege counts the Soldiers created by the first. Soldiers from either Elspeth and Hero improve that count, and the Connections Shapeshifter is a Soldier too.

Overlord of the Mistmoors

Creates two flying 2/1 tokens on entry and again when it attacks. Casting for impending still gives the entry tokens, but leaves the Overlord a noncreature enchantment until its time counters are gone. A normal Jace hit puts it in without impending counters, immediately as a creature. While impending, it is not a legal target for Jace's -3.

Angel of Invention

Usually choose two Servo tokens with fabricate to widen the board and trigger entry payoffs. Its anthem improves your other creatures, including the Servos. That toughness increase can stop Skullclamp from immediately killing a small token; the alternate fabricate counters are useful when you need a sturdier lifelinking flier.

Black Market Connections

Choose the modes you need rather than automatically paying six life. The token and Treasure support both combat and resources; multipliers increase those tokens without increasing the printed life payments. The 3/2 Shapeshifter is every creature type, including Soldier for Myrel.

Secure the Wastes

A flexible end-step army that lets you hold up interaction first. Use it before your turn to untap with attackers, or after a board wipe to recover. Warrior tokens lack haste, so casting it on your main phase normally does not add attackers that turn.

Will of the Mardu

Three mana creates Warriors equal to the chosen player's creature count. Target yourself when ahead or an opponent with a larger army when behind. With Jace present, choose both modes to make tokens and then damage a creature using your new creature count. If all its targets become illegal, the spell will not resolve.

Multiply the army and make attacks sustainable
Mondrak, Glory Dominus

Doubles all your tokens, including Treasures, and can trade two spare artifacts or creatures for an indestructible counter. Keep enough colored mana or life for the activation. Indestructible does not protect against exile, sacrifice, or sufficient toughness reduction.

Exalted Sunborn

Another all-token doubler, with flying and lifelink to offset your life payments. Warp for two mana when you can create a large token batch that turn; it will exile at the next end step and can be cast from exile on a later turn. If Jace exiles it first, that is not the warp exile and does not grant the same later-cast permission.

Ojer Taq, Deepest Foundation // Temple of Civilization

Triples creature tokens, but does not multiply Treasures. With one token doubler, a creature-token event produces six times its original quantity. If Ojer dies it returns as a land; exiling it to Jace does not trigger that recovery.

Anointed Procession

A multiplier that survives most creature wipes and doubles both your army and Treasures. It creates no tokens by itself, so develop a producer first rather than keeping a hand of multipliers without bodies.

Elspeth, Storm Slayer

Doubles all tokens while also making them, removing a large opposing creature, or giving your existing team counters and flying. Her +1 normally makes two Soldiers because of her own static ability. Her 0 only affects creatures present when it resolves; tokens created afterward will not receive that activation's counter or flying.

Elspeth, Sun's Champion

Makes three Soldiers per activation before multipliers, supporting Myrel and rebuilding after removal. Her -3 removes creatures with power four or greater, including your own large creatures or buffed tokens. Her emblem is an eventual combat payoff, not something the deck must assemble to function.

Windcrag Siege

Usually choose Mardu to double your attack triggers from Adeline, Anim Pakal, Hero, Myrel, Ainok, and Overlord. It does not double combat-damage triggers from Curiosity Crafter or Professional Face-Breaker. Jeskai gives a modest recurring token when you have no attack engine, and the choice cannot be changed afterward.

Reconnaissance

Lets you attack for triggers, then remove an endangered attacker from combat before damage. You can also use it during the end-of-combat step to untap survivors after they dealt damage. This makes attacking with your engines safer and leaves blockers; it does not stop targeted removal or protect a creature after lethal damage has already killed it.

Turn the army into an actual win
Purphoros, God of the Forge

Each other creature entering deals two damage to every opponent. For example, Secure the Wastes with X=5 and one token doubler creates ten creatures, dealing twenty to each opponent. His pump ability turns spare mana into stronger attacks. He works as an enchantment below five red devotion and still counts as a creature card in the library for Jace, but cannot be exiled by Jace while he is a noncreature on the battlefield.

Impact Tremors

The cheaper, smaller entry-damage engine. It deals one to each opponent per creature entering, making doubled token batches useful even through clogged combat. It also triggers for your nontoken creatures; neither this nor Purphoros requires attacking or sacrificing anything.

Akroma's Will

The main combat finisher: flying and double strike make a developed army dangerous. With Jace under your control you can choose both modes for protection, lifelink, and indestructible as well. Without him, choose one mode. Resolve it after attack-trigger tokens enter if you want those new attackers to gain the abilities too.

Moonshaker Cavalry

An eight-mana payoff for a board already full of creatures, granting flying and +X/+X based on your creature count when the trigger resolves. Jace can find it as a backup finish, especially after Brainstorm arranges it on top. It does not grant haste, and creatures created later do not get the resolved pump. Cast or transform into it before declaring your established army's attack.

Caretaker's Talent

Draws once each turn from any token entering, including opposing-turn Treasures from Smothering Tithe. Level two copies a token; level three gives all your creature tokens +2/+2, turning a small army into a real threat. Upgrading reduces Skullclamp's ability to immediately kill tokens, so draw the cards you need first.

Keep cards flowing without relying on the commander
Skullclamp

Equipping an unbuffed 1/1 usually kills it and draws two cards. It turns surplus tokens into recovery after removal. Counters and toughness bonuses can prevent that immediate death; equipping remains legal but no longer guarantees a draw. Jace exiles rather than kills, so transforming an equipped token does not trigger Clamp.

Bennie Bracks, Zoologist

Your tokens can convoke this onto the battlefield, even if they have summoning sickness. At each end step, it draws if you created a token that turn, including a Treasure. Smothering Tithe, Conqueror, and instant token spells can enable draws on opposing turns. Simply having an old token is not enough.

Curiosity Crafter

Each creature token that deals combat damage to a player draws a card, with no once-per-turn limit. Flying tokens and Elspeth's flying effect help you connect. With double strike, a token connecting in both damage steps can trigger twice. Its hand-size ability lets you keep the resulting cards.

Tocasia's Welcome

Draws once each turn when a creature of mana value three or less enters. The ordinary creature tokens in this list have mana value zero regardless of their size, so they qualify even after anthem effects. It also rewards several cheap nontoken engines and avoids depending on combat damage.

Rhystic Study

Keeps resources flowing while you develop mana and bodies. Opponents can pay for the tax, so do not rely on it to fix a hand with too few lands. It is one of the three Game Changers rather than a reason to delay the token plan indefinitely.

Brainstorm

Draws into early setup or protection and can put Moonshaker or another creature/planeswalker from hand on top for Jace's -3. Do not fetch and shuffle after arranging the intended hit. Without that setup, use a fetchland to clear unwanted cards instead.

Mana should support spells plus protection
Sol Ring

Accelerates generic costs, but supplies none of Jace's four colored symbols. The five Talismans and Arcane Signet provide reliable colored mana; Fellwar Stone depends on opposing lands. The white-heavy token plan takes priority over forcing Jace onto the battlefield at the earliest possible moment.

Coalition Relic

Provides any color and can bank a charge counter for the next first main phase. If charged beforehand, the counter mana plus a normal tap gives two mana that main phase, helping cast an engine while retaining interaction. The stored mana does not persist into combat unless another effect says so.

Smothering Tithe

Treasure funds your expensive token payoffs and supports the once-per-turn draw engines on other players' turns. Mondrak, Procession, Sunborn, and Storm Slayer multiply its Treasures; Ojer Taq does not. Opponents may pay, so it is not guaranteed ramp.

Professional Face-Breaker

Wide attacks can make one Treasure for each player you damage in a combat-damage step, not one per connecting creature. Double strike can produce another set in the second damage step. Sacrifice surplus Treasures for temporary access to cards only when you can use them before the turn ends.

Cursed Mirror

Can first act as a hasty copy of a creature before becoming a mana rock at end of turn. Copy Moonshaker for its entry pump, Overlord for tokens, or a useful opposing creature. Copying a legendary creature you already control invokes the legend rule. It is not a token, and while copying a creature it lacks its normal mana ability unless the copied creature has one.

Protect the board and remove what matters
Teferi's Protection

The third Game Changer protects your board and life total through a decisive opposing turn or a wipe. Phased-out tokens return; this is not exile. You also phase out your lands, so treat it as committing to sit out until your next turn rather than ordinary spot protection.

Clever Concealment

Convoke lets untapped tokens protect your nonland permanents, including Jace and your enchantments. Summoning sickness does not prevent paying convoke. It saves tokens through phasing, but does not protect you from damage and cannot phase out lands. Tapping attackers to convoke before combat reduces that turn's attack.

Flawless Maneuver

Free with Jace present even though he is a planeswalker, and three mana otherwise. It protects your creatures from destruction and lethal damage, not Jace or enchantments. It does not stop exile, sacrifice, or Toxic Deluge.

Grand Crescendo

Supplies bodies and gives your creatures indestructible; at X=0 it is two-mana protection. Its own new tokens receive the protection, but creatures entering later do not automatically inherit it. It has the same exile and toughness-reduction limits as Flawless Maneuver.

Swords to Plowshares

Efficient exile for a creature that blocks your plan or threatens a win. Path to Exile is the second one-mana option, with an opposing basic-land ramp drawback. Save them for threats the army cannot simply attack past.

Anguished Unmaking

Broad nonland exile covers restrictive enchantments, artifacts, and planeswalkers. Generous Gift offers broader permanent targeting through destruction but gives a 3/3 back. Their role is breaking a specific obstacle while your army supplies the clock.

Dovin's Veto

Stops a noncreature spell uncounterably, particularly a wipe or a decisive combo piece. Swan Song and Flusterstorm provide one-mana backup, but have narrower targets; Flusterstorm only counters instants and sorceries and can be paid for. Hold the answer that matches the threat you expect.

Mana Drain

Broad countermagic whose later colorless mana can fund a large token spell, Caretaker upgrade, or Moonshaker's generic cost. The refund does not pay Jace's colored symbols or Moonshaker's three white mana. Do not spend your last protection merely to chase a mana bonus.

Toxic Deluge

The emergency reset can shrink indestructible creatures and leave planeswalkers standing. It kills your tokens too, so use it when the alternative is losing, or after phasing your own nonland board out with Clever Concealment. Indestructibility alone does not save creatures from sufficient toughness reduction.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Graveyard interaction without relying on creature combat. The entry trigger removes a key card, and sacrificing it can clear opponents' graveyards. Use the draw mode only when that coverage is not needed.

Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow

A land when developing mana or an artifact/enchantment answer later. It is also a possible Jace hit, entering as the creature front face rather than the land. That flexibility is useful but is another reason the backup transformation is not a guaranteed enormous threat.

Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

A land option or temporary answer to an opposing spell or nonland permanent. Returning a spell to hand can answer an uncounterable spell, but lets its owner cast it again later. The mana base's two MDFCs improve land access without making every late draw a basic land.

Use Jace as value and an opportunistic finish
Jace, Multiverse Architect

His +1 is the default when you need cards: draw two, then bottom a card from hand. His -3 exiles another creature or planeswalker you control and finds the next creature or planeswalker card. Here it can find a cheap engine, either Elspeth, or a finisher; it is not a tutor without top-deck setup. Exile an expendable token, not a working multiplier or attack engine. His combat trigger taxes opponents two once per trigger to allow attacks against Jaces that turn; it does not protect you or either Elspeth.

A fresh Jace has four loyalty. After one -3 he has one, requiring two later +1 activations before another -3 without outside help. If the last three loyalty are spent, he leaves the battlefield but the activated ability still resolves if its target remains legal. This list does not dedicate slots to repeatedly resetting that loyalty.

Keep Brainstorm plus an expendable token in mind when Moonshaker is stranded in hand and you already have an army ready to attack. Putting Moonshaker on top before the -3 finds it, and its entry pumps the remaining board. If the sole target becomes illegal before the -3 resolves, the ability does nothing. Merely putting Moonshaker on the bottom with Jace's +1 does not guarantee the next hit.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep three usable lands, a cheap token producer, and either ramp or a draw engine, with interaction when possible. For example: Command Tower, Flooded Strand, Sacred Foundry, Arcane Signet, Adeline, Skullclamp, and Swan Song. Fetch for the colors that support your hand; the deck needs early white and often double white, plus blue available when holding countermagic. A hand of multipliers and expensive finishers with no producer should usually be shipped.

Develop mana or an inexpensive producer on the first two turns, then a repeatable generator or draw engine. Deploy a multiplier when it will immediately amplify the next token batch. Casting Jace on turn five is optional if an engine plus protection is a stronger play. The deck has enough creatures and token engines to keep operating through commander removal.

Before attacking, identify which triggers need a declared attacker. Adeline and Anim Pakal can trigger while staying back; Myrel, Ainok, Hero, and Overlord need to attack themselves. Resolve token creation before casting Akroma's Will if those new attackers need its benefits. Tokens created by Myrel are not attacking and cannot join that combat merely because a later effect grants haste.

Leave bodies untapped for convoke when an exile wipe is plausible. Reconnaissance can untap surviving attackers after damage, making them available for protection on later turns. Avoid committing every producer and multiplier into the same wipe; recurring enchantments and flashback provide a rebuild plan.

In the late game, choose a protected combat finish, repeated creature-entry damage, or Brainstorm into a decisive Jace hit. Count actual damage rather than equating a large token count with a win. The chief costs of this build are life payments from lands and engines, vulnerability to sweepers, and occasional Jace hits that improve resources rather than immediately ending the game.
