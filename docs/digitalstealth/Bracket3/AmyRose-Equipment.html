The biggest mistake: over-committing to Amy without protection or secondary threats

The most common trap with Amy Rose is treating her as a solo Voltron commander and pouring every resource into suiting her up without holding up protection. While Amy has built-in haste and circumvents massive equip costs when attacking, she possesses zero innate hexproof, ward, or indestructible. Opponents will gladly let you cast Colossus Hammer or Excalibur, Sword of Eden, wait for Amy to declare an attack, and remove her before or during combat, stranding massive unequipped artifacts on an empty board.

This deck solves that failure mode with two pillars: instant-speed resilience and lethal secondary threats. You should never swing Amy without either an active protection piece on board (Robe of Stars, Mithril Coat, Hammer of Nazahn), open mana for interactive spells (Flawless Maneuver, Boros Charm, Galadriel's Dismissal, Loran's Escape, Teferi's Protection), or an unattached Sunforger ready to tutor answers. Furthermore, Amy's second ability explicitly rewards attacking alongside other threats by granting another attacker +X/+0 equal to her boosted power. Diversifying your board with double-strikers (Kellan, the Fae-Blooded, Blade Historian, Reyav, Master Smith) and army generators (Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin, Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon) ensures that an answered Amy does not stall your game plan.

The commander: timing, attack trigger, and equip cheat

Amy Rose costs {2}{R}{W} and enters with haste. Her attack trigger fires during the Declare Attackers step and has two sequential clauses:

1. "Attach up to one target Equipment to her."

2. "Then up to one other target attacking creature gets +X/+0 until end of turn, where X is Amy Rose's power."

Because the second clause begins with "Then," the game checks Amy's power *after* the targeted Equipment attaches. For example:

* When Amy attacks and targets Colossus Hammer ({8} equip cost bypassed), Amy immediately becomes a 13/13.

* Amy's trigger then grants the other targeted attacker +13/+0 until end of turn.

* If Amy attacks and attaches Excalibur, Sword of Eden, Amy becomes a 13/3 with vigilance, and the second attacker gets +13/+0.

* If she attaches Kaldra Compleat, she becomes an 8/8 with first strike, trample, indestructible, and haste, granting another attacker +8/+0.

You bypass all timing and mana costs associated with equipping. Even if an Equipment was just cast that turn, or was previously equipped to another creature, Amy moves it directly to herself for free upon declaring an attack.

Secondary threats and going wide: lethal pump recipients

The deck runs an aggressive suite of autonomous threats that function independently of Amy and turn her power buff into lethal strikes:

* **Double Strikers (Kellan, the Fae-Blooded, Blade Historian, Reyav, Master Smith, Halvar, God of Battle):** A creature with double strike deals both first strike and regular combat damage. When Amy grants Kellan +13/+0 off a Colossus Hammer attach, Kellan swings as a 15/3 double striker, dealing 30 damage in one combat step. Reyav, Master Smith grants double strike to any equipped or enchanted creature, while Blade Historian grants double strike to all attacking creatures.

* **Token Swarms (Krenko, Tin Street Kingpin & Anim Pakal, Thousandth Moon):** When Amy and Krenko attack together, order the triggers so Amy's trigger resolves first. Krenko receives +X/+0 (e.g. +13/+0), boosting his power to 14. Krenko's trigger then resolves, adding a +1/+1 counter and creating 15 Goblin tokens in a single swing. Anim Pakal rapidly scales Gnome tokens on every attack step.

* **Autonomous Living Weapon & For Mirrodin Threats (Kaldra Compleat, Hexplate Wallbreaker, Nettlecyst):** Kaldra Compleat enters as a 5/5 first strike, trample, indestructible creature that exiles blockers. Hexplate Wallbreaker enters with a 2/2 Rebel token and grants an extra combat phase. Nettlecyst creates a massive Germ token scaled to your artifact count. These cards provide immediate board presence without needing Amy on the field.

* **Combat Multiplier (Aurelia, the Warleader):** Aurelia attacks with flying and vigilance, untaps all creatures you control, and generates an additional combat phase. In the second combat, Amy attacks again, attaches a second Equipment, and distributes another massive power buff.

The removal resilience package: phased shields and Sunforger toolbox

This deck is built to shrug off single-target removal, mass bounce, and destructive sweepers (such as our own Blasphemous Act and Vanquish the Horde):

* **Phasing Effects (Teferi's Protection, Galadriel's Dismissal, Clever Concealment, Robe of Stars):** Phasing is the supreme defensive mechanic in Commander. Permanents that phase out do not leave the battlefield, meaning all attached Equipment stays attached and triggers do not reset. Teferi's Protection phases your entire board and life total until your next turn. Galadriel's Dismissal can phase out target creature for {W} or overload to save your entire team (or phase out all blockers from an opponent). Clever Concealment can be convoked for {0} mana. Robe of Stars phases out the equipped creature and all attached equipment for {2} at instant speed.

* **Indestructible & Hexproof (Flawless Maneuver, Boros Charm, Loran's Escape, Mithril Coat, Hammer of Nazahn):** Flawless Maneuver is completely free while Amy is on the board. Boros Charm gives all permanents you control indestructible (or gives double strike to finish an opponent). Mithril Coat flashes in at instant speed and auto-attaches to Amy, granting indestructible. Hammer of Nazahn grants indestructible to itself and equipped creatures while auto-attaching every newly cast equipment.

* **The Sunforger Package:** Unattaching Sunforger for {R}{W} allows you to search your library for any red or white instant with mana value 4 or less and cast it for free. Sunforger acts as an on-demand silver bullet:

* For table wipes / exile: fetch Teferi's Protection or Boros Charm.

* For spot removal: fetch Loran's Escape or Galadriel's Dismissal.

* For stack interaction / counterspells: fetch Tibalt's Trickery.

* For spot removal: fetch Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Stroke of Midnight, Chaos Warp, or Wear // Tear.

* For lethal combat blowouts: fetch Deflecting Palm.

Card advantage and continuous draw engines

Boros equipment decks often gas out if wiped; this list packs redundant card advantage:

* **Equipment-Cast / ETB Draw (Puresteel Paladin, Sram, Senior Edificer):** Refills your hand for every Equipment and Aura cast or entering the battlefield. Puresteel also grants Equip {0} via Metalcraft.

* **Combat & Attack Draw (Akiri, Fearless Voyager, Wyleth, Soul of Steel, Nahiri, Forged in Fury, Mask of Memory, Sword of Fire and Ice):** Akiri draws a card whenever you attack with equipped creatures and can detach an equipment for {W} to give a creature indestructible. Wyleth draws cards equal to the number of attached equipment. Nahiri impulses cards on attack. Mask of Memory draws two cards and loots on combat damage.

* **Staple & Punishment Draw (The One Ring, Esper Sentinel, Trouble in Pairs):** The One Ring draws exponential cards while granting protection from everything for a full turn. Esper Sentinel taxes opponents' noncreature spells (and its tax scales with Amy's power pump). Trouble in Pairs punishes opponents for drawing extra cards, casting multiple spells, or attacking you.

Key interactions and rules quirks

* **Colossus Hammer flying loss:** Colossus Hammer removes flying from the equipped creature. If you attach it to Aurelia, the Warleader or Armored Skyhunter, they lose flying. Keep this in mind when planning aerial combat.

* **Forge Anew instant equip:** Forge Anew lets you activate equip abilities at instant speed during any player's turn, and the first equip ability each turn costs {0}. It also recurs an Equipment from your graveyard directly to the battlefield on ETB.

* **Sigarda's Aid:** Gives all Equipment and Aura spells flash, and whenever an Equipment enters the battlefield, you may attach it to a creature you control for free.

* **Bruenor Battlehammer & Astor, Bearer of Blades:** Bruenor makes the first equip each turn cost {0} and gives each creature +2/+0 per attached equipment. Astor gives all equipment equip {1}.

* **Witch Enchanter MDFC:** In your hand, Witch Enchanter can be played as an untapped white land (Witch-Blessed Meadow) for 3 life, or cast as a 2/2 creature to destroy an opposing artifact or enchantment.

Mulligans and opening hands

Look for hands with:

* 2–3 lands with both colors (Sacred Foundry, Plateau, Battlefield Forge, Sunbaked Canyon, Arid Mesa, etc.).

* Early ramp (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Boros Signet, Talisman of Conviction).

* At least one cheap Equipment tutor or card draw engine (Stoneforge Mystic, Steelshaper's Gift, Open the Armory, Sram, Senior Edificer, Puresteel Paladin, Esper Sentinel).

* One bomb Equipment or protection spell (Colossus Hammer, Excalibur, Sword of Eden, Flawless Maneuver, Mithril Coat).

Ship hands with multiple 5+ mana cards and no acceleration, or hands with expensive equipment but no creatures or equip-cost reduction.

Combos and deterministic loops

* **Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine (Infinite Combats & Infinite Mana):**

* *Prerequisites:* Aggravated Assault on the battlefield. A creature equipped with Sword of Feast and Famine (e.g. Amy Rose, Aurelia, or an unblockable attacker via Rogue's Passage). At least five lands that can produce {3}{R}{R}.

* *Loop:* Attack with the equipped creature. Deal combat damage to an opponent. Sword of Feast and Famine triggers, untapping all lands you control. In the postcombat main phase, tap five lands to activate Aggravated Assault for {3}{R}{R}. All creatures untap, and you receive an additional combat phase followed by an additional main phase.

* *Result:* Infinite combat phases, infinite untaps, infinite mana (if you control more than five lands). As a Bracket 3 deck, do not execute this combo before turn 6.

Card-specific notes

* **Excalibur, Sword of Eden:** Reduces its own casting cost by the mana value of historic permanents you control (legendaries, artifacts, sagas). In this deck, Excalibur frequently casts for {2} to {4} mana, and Amy attaches it for free to immediately gain +10/+0 and vigilance.

* **Armored Skyhunter:** When it attacks, dig six cards deep to cheat an Equipment onto the battlefield and attach it for free to Amy or any secondary attacker.

* **Tibalt's Trickery:** A red hard counterspell that can counter an opposing game-ending spell, board wipe, or extra turn. Easily tutored directly onto the stack with Sunforger.

* **Deflecting Palm:** Does not target the creature or permanent, only the damage source. It bypasses hexproof and protection, preventing lethal damage and reflecting it straight back to the attacker's face.

* **Inventors' Fair & Buried Ruin:** Inventors' Fair tutors any Equipment or The One Ring directly to hand once you have three artifacts. Buried Ruin recovers destroyed equipment from the graveyard.
