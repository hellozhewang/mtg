This is a draw-go control deck that wins with fliers. Baral makes every instant and sorcery one mana cheaper and loots whenever you counter something, so each counterspell digs you toward the next one. Hold up mana on their turns, answer only what matters, and let Talrand, Murmuring Mystic and Shark Typhoon turn the spells you were casting anyway into an air force.

The commander
Baral, Chief of Compliance

two mana. Instants and sorceries cost {1} less. Whenever a spell or ability you control counters a spell, you may draw a card, then discard one. The loot is optional, and it triggers on every successful counter.

Baral's discount stacks with Archmage of Runes and Sapphire Medallion, but it only cuts GENERIC mana. Arcane Denial and Snap drop to {U}; Counterspell stays {U}{U} and Swan Song stays {U}, because they have no generic mana to remove.

Baral is a 1/3. Protect him by countering the removal aimed at him; he is the engine, not the clock.

How the deck wins

Nothing here goes infinite. The win is a flying army made out of the spells you cast to stay alive.

Talrand, Sky Summoner

a 2/2 flying Drake for every instant or sorcery you cast. Two counterspells on their turns is two Drakes.

Murmuring Mystic

a 1/1 flying Bird for every instant or sorcery. With Talrand out, one spell is two fliers.

Shark Typhoon

a flying Shark the size of each noncreature spell's mana value. Or cycle it at instant speed for an X/X Shark and a card, which dodges sorcery-speed removal.

Thing in the Ice // Awoken Horror

each instant or sorcery removes an ice counter. After four, it flips into a 7/8 and returns every non-Horror creature to its owner's hand: their board and your Drake tokens alike, so flip it on THEIR turn and attack with what is left.

Tolarian Terror

a 5/5 with ward {2} that costs {1} less per instant and sorcery in your graveyard. Often two or three mana by turn five.

Ledger Shredder

a flier that connives whenever any player casts their second spell in a turn, which in this deck is most turns.

Jace, the Mind Sculptor

card selection with 0, bounce with −1. His −12 exiles a player's whole library: a win against one opponent.

Archmage of Runes

another discount, and a card for every instant and sorcery.

Counter what matters

You will not counter everything at a four-player table. Save hard counters for wincons, tutors for combo pieces, and board wipes that hit you. Let creature beats pass while the fliers grow.

Counterspell

the baseline.

Mana Drain

a counter that pays you back next main phase. Cast a fatty off it.

Fierce Guardianship

free while Baral is out: counters any noncreature spell.

Force of Negation

free on their turn for a noncreature spell.

Pact of Negation

free now. Pay {3}{U}{U} next upkeep or lose, so use it on the turn you win or when you can pay.

Arcane Denial

two mana, and each player who gets countered draws later.

Mana Leak

cheap early, weaker late.

Memory Lapse

puts the spell back on top of their library: they redraw it instead of a new card.

Delay

exiles the spell for three turns, which is often the rest of the game.

Disallow

counters spells, activated abilities AND triggered abilities.

Rewind

counters, then untaps up to four lands. With Baral it is often mana-positive.

Unwind

the same for noncreature spells, three lands.

Negate

noncreature spells.

Dispel

one mana against an instant, usually the removal spell aimed at your threat.

Spell Pierce

cheap tax on a noncreature spell.

Flusterstorm

unbeatable in a counter war.

Mental Misstep

free against a one-drop.

Swan Song

one mana against enchantments, instants and sorceries; they get a 2/2 bird.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana against any noncreature spell; they get two Treasures.

Strix Serenade

one mana against an artifact, creature or planeswalker spell; they get a 2/2 bird.

Muddle the Mixture

counters instants and sorceries, or transmutes for a two-drop.

Tale's End

counters an ability or a legendary spell, usually a commander.

Mindbreak Trap

free against a player who cast three spells this turn; exiles a whole storm.

Tishana's Tidebinder

flash; counters an activated or triggered ability. If it was a creature's, artifact's or planeswalker's, that permanent loses all abilities while the Tidebinder stays.

Narset's Reversal

copies their instant or sorcery for you and bounces the original.

Cryptic Command

pick two: counter, bounce, tap their whole team, or draw. Counter-plus-draw is the default; tap-plus-draw ends a game on your swing turn.

Answers that are not counters
Cyclonic Rift

overloaded at seven mana it returns every nonland permanent they control to hand. Cast it in the end step before your fliers swing.

Pongify

one mana, destroy a creature for a 3/3 Ape.

Snap

bounce a creature and untap two lands. With Baral it costs one and untaps two.

Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

bounce a spell or permanent, or play it as an Island.

Otawara, Soaring City

a land that channels into a bounce.

Drawing and digging
Rhystic Study

their spells pay you.

Mystic Remora

the early-game version; drop it when the upkeep gets expensive.

Archmage Emeritus

draws for every instant and sorcery you cast or copy.

Faerie Mastermind

flash flier that draws when opponents draw extra.

Wavebreak Hippocamp

draws for the first spell you cast on each opponent's turn: every counterspell is a cantrip.

Teferi's Ageless Insight

every draw outside your draw step becomes two. Baral's loot becomes draw two, discard one.

Fact or Fiction

five cards, one pile.

Stock Up

the top five, keep two.

Lórien Revealed

draw three, or cycle it for an Island early.

Frantic Search

draw two, discard two, untap three lands. With Baral it costs two and untaps three, so it makes mana.

Windfall

refill everyone, but you get to counter what they draw.

Brainstorm

best with a shuffle effect or Sensei's Divining Top.

Ponder

the cheap dig.

Preordain

the same.

Opt

the same.

Gitaxian Probe

see their hand before you commit counters.

Sensei's Divining Top

arranges your top three every turn.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep Baral, three or four lands and two cheap interaction spells. Card draw without interaction loses to early threats; interaction without draw runs out.

Early game, develop: Baral, a rock, a draw engine. Counter only what would end the game or swing it hard.

Mid game, land a flier engine (Talrand, Murmuring Mystic or Shark Typhoon) with counter mana held up, then cast at least one spell on every opponent's turn.

Hold Cyclonic Rift for the turn your fliers are big enough to finish.
