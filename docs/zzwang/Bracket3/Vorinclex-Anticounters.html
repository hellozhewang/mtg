Built to beat counters. Vorinclex's second line reads "if an opponent would put one or more counters on a permanent or player, they put half that many, rounded down" — and half of ONE is zero. Every proliferate trigger, every poison counter, every planeswalker tick, every charge counter on a mana rock that goes up by one simply does not happen. Then the deck adds a second anti-counter layer and a pile of artifact destruction, because that is where counter decks keep their engines.

The commander
Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider

6/6 trample haste for six. YOUR counters double; THEIRS halve, rounded down. A one-counter effect from an opponent is blanked entirely, and even a four-counter proliferate becomes two.

He is a Praetor, so he also just attacks for six with haste the turn he lands. Against a non-counter table he is a beater; against a proliferate table he is the lock.

Melira, Sylvok Outcast

the second layer, and a different one. You cannot get poison counters at all, your creatures cannot have -1/-1 counters, and their creatures cannot have +1/+1 counters. Infect, toxic and -1/-1 strategies stop dead.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud, because Vorinclex is a six-drop that every counter deck must answer.

Heroic Intervention

hexproof and indestructible for the whole board through a wrath.

Where counter decks actually live

Counters need permanents to sit on, and in mono-green the answer to a permanent is to destroy it.

Bane of Progress

destroys EVERY artifact and enchantment on the board and grows for each one. Against an artifact proliferate deck this is a one-sided reset.

Creeping Corrosion

destroys all artifacts, full stop.

Collector Ouphe

artifact activated abilities cannot be activated. Astral Cornucopia, Everflowing Chalice, Contagion Clasp and every mana rock stop working while it is out.

Manglehorn

an artifact on entry, and theirs enter tapped.

Caustic Caterpillar

a one-drop that eats an artifact or enchantment whenever you need it.

Acidic Slime

artifact, enchantment OR land, on a deathtouch body.

Gemrazer

mutate it onto a creature and destroy an artifact or enchantment every time you mutate again.

Force of Vigor

free on their turn, destroying two artifacts or enchantments at once.

Krosan Grip

split second, so it beats the response.

Bramblecrush

any noncreature permanent, including a land or a planeswalker.

Beast Within

the instant-speed catch-all.

Freyalise, Llanowar's Fury

a mana Elf every turn and an artifact or enchantment on the minus.

Your own counters, doubled
Hardened Scales

+1 to every batch of +1/+1 counters, then Vorinclex doubles the result. The two of them together turn a single counter into four.

Inspiring Call

draws for every creature with a counter and makes them all indestructible — a blowout in combat and through a wrath.

Avenger of Zendikar

a Plant for every land, then counters on all of them per landfall, doubled by Vorinclex.

Garruk's Uprising

trample for the whole board and a card when a big creature lands.

How you actually win
Craterhoof Behemoth

the standard ending. A board of dorks and hatebears is wide enough that it is lethal to more than one player.

End-Raze Forerunners

the second copy, with vigilance and haste for the whole team.

Hornet Queen

five flying deathtouch bodies, which both blocks everything and makes Craterhoof lethal.

Kogla, the Titan Ape

fights on entry and destroys an artifact or enchantment on every attack.

Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider

six trampling haste damage a turn is a real clock on its own.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a mana dork. Turn-one acceleration is what makes a turn-four or five Vorinclex possible, and he is the card the whole deck is named for.

Read the table before spending the artifact hate. Bane of Progress and Creeping Corrosion are close to blank against a creature deck and game-winning against an artifact one — hold them rather than firing on the first Signet.

Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider

remember the halving is rounded DOWN. A proliferate that would add one counter adds none. Against a deck built on single-counter triggers he is not a tax, he is an off switch.

Melira, Sylvok Outcast

two mana, and she answers a different set of decks than Vorinclex does. Against infect or a -1/-1 deck she is the more important card of the two.
