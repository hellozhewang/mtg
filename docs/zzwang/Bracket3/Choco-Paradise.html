Attack with an aerial flock of Birds to trigger Choco's digging ability, putting lands directly onto the battlefield while drawing cards, untapping them with Amulet of Vigor, and protecting the flock with Sephara.

Bird combat dig and mass land acceleration
Choco, Seeker of Paradise

whenever one or more Birds you control attack, look at that many cards from the top of your library. You may put one of them into your hand. Then put any number of land cards from among them onto the battlefield tapped and the rest into your graveyard. Choco gets +1/+0 until end of turn whenever a land enters under your control.

Amulet of Vigor

whenever a permanent enters the battlefield tapped under your control, untap it. Combined with Choco, every land retrieved from your attack enters UNTAPPED, giving you immense mana in your second main phase to cast game-ending spells!

Lotus Cobra

adds one mana of any color on each landfall trigger, creating massive mana bursts during combat.

Tireless Provisioner

creates a Treasure or Food token on each land entry.

Emeria Angel

creates a 1/1 white Bird creature token with flying whenever a land enters under your control. Lands put into play by Choco create fresh Birds, which swell your flock for subsequent turns!

Murmuration

bird storm enchantment that grants flock abilities and produces Bird tokens on end step.

Flurry of Wings

three-mana instant creating an X/X flock of 1/1 flying Bird tokens, where X is the number of attacking creatures.

Scute Swarm

landfall token engine that duplicates itself exponentially on land entries.

Bird tribal lords, card draw, and evasion
Sephara, Sky's Blade

7/7 flying lifelink Angel that costs only {W} and tapping four untapped flying creatures to cast. Grants indestructible to all other creatures you control with flying, shielding your entire flock from removal and combat damage.

Kastral, the Windcrested

whenever a Bird you control deals combat damage to a player, choose one: draw a card, put a +1/+1 counter on each Bird you control, or return a Bird card from your graveyard to the battlefield.

Kindred Discovery

choose Birds on entry to draw a card whenever a Bird you control enters the battlefield or attacks, drawing dozens of cards in combat.

Airborne Aid

draws a card for each Bird on the battlefield for four mana.

Empyrean Eagle

gives other creatures you control with flying +1/+1.

Soulcatchers' Aerie

gains feather counters whenever Birds die and gives all Birds +1/+1 for each feather counter on it.

Jackdaw Savior

flying Bird that returns lower-mana-value creatures from your graveyard to the battlefield whenever another Bird you control dies.

Derevi, Empyrial Tactician

untaps lands or taps opposing blockers whenever combat damage connects.

Tutors, interaction, and opening hands
Cyclonic Rift

premier blue reset to clear opposing blockers and flying defenders.

Rhystic Study

continuous card draw engine taxing opposing spells.

Teferi's Protection

phases out all your permanents in response to board wipes.

Heroic Intervention

grants hexproof and indestructible at instant speed.

Raise the Palisade

bounces all non-Bird creatures to clear opposing boards for an unblockable alpha strike.

Keep opening hands with two to three lands, early low-cost Birds (Birds of Paradise, Judge's Familiar, Esior, Gilded Goose), and an acceleration or dig engine (Amulet of Vigor, Emeria Angel, Herald's Horn). Cast Choco on turn three, attack with multiple flying Birds on turn four to dig deep, dump multiple lands onto the battlefield, and overwhelm opponents with flying anthems and Sephara protection on Turn 6 or later.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, Bird swarm development, and landfall ramp.
