Brudiclad changes tokens already on the battlefield into copies; those tokens do NOT enter again. Build a wide collection of inexpensive tokens, make one strong token to copy, and use the next combat to convert the whole collection. Treasure, Food, Clues, and Maps all count, so converting them also spends your future mana and utility. You cannot choose to transform only some of your other tokens.

What the commander actually copies

Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer

At the beginning of combat on your turn, create a Myr, then optionally choose one token for every other token to copy. You may choose the newly created Myr. No player gets priority between creating it and making that choice. The copy effect has no end-of-turn expiry and remains if Brudiclad leaves, but creature tokens lose the haste he was granting.

Changing a token does not untap it, erase its counters or damage, or make it a new object. A Treasure tapped to Urza remains tapped after becoming a creature. Conversely, a token you have controlled since the beginning of your turn can attack after changing types even if Brudiclad is removed, provided it is otherwise able to attack. Tokens created this turn still need haste.

Urza, Lord High Artificer

Supplies a Construct token whose power and toughness scale with your artifact count, immediately giving Brudiclad a strong template without another copy spell. Urza turns untapped artifacts into blue mana without sacrificing them, including newly created artifact creatures. Reserve untapped tokens if you want those same objects to attack after conversion.

Simulacrum Synthesizer

Creates another scaling Construct when an artifact of mana value three or more enters under your control. Brudiclad entering triggers it; Brudiclad changing existing tokens into expensive artifacts does not. A newly created copy token of Wurmcoil Engine has its copied mana value six and does qualify.

Master of Etherium

A powerful copy target because each copy has size equal to your artifact count and pumps every other artifact creature. These ongoing abilities work immediately after conversion without entry triggers. Cyberdrive Awakener supplies flying so the huge army is harder to chump-block.

Hellrider

Each Hellrider triggers for every creature you declare as an attacker, dealing damage to whatever that creature attacks. For example, ten token Hellriders plus the original Hellrider and Brudiclad make twelve attackers and eleven damage triggers per attacker. Sending four attackers at each opponent deals forty-four to each before blockers, assuming no prevention or interference. This is finite attack damage, not an infinite loop.

Wurmcoil Engine

Creates an army with lifelink and deathtouch that is difficult to race. When those copies die, each produces two smaller artifact Wurms, rebuilding token quantity after a destruction wipe. Exiling or bouncing them does not produce death tokens. The two smaller Wurm types have different abilities even though they share a name.

Cyberdrive Awakener

Grants flying to your other artifact creatures, including an army of Constructs. Its entry also animates noncreature artifacts as 4/4s for the turn, giving a route to victory without the commander. Without Brudiclad, artifacts that entered this turn cannot attack merely because they became creatures. Converting tokens into copies of Cyberdrive does not repeat its entry animation.

Mechanized Production

Enchant a stable nontoken artifact, often a mana rock, to keep the Aura legal while token forms change. At your upkeep it makes a copy of the enchanted artifact, then checks whether you control eight artifacts sharing a name. Those eight do not need to share the enchanted artifact's name. Brudiclad can provide eight matching artifact tokens on the previous turn, but nonartifact Hellriders would not qualify.

Goldspan Dragon

A strong independent threat that makes Treasure on attack or when a spell targets it. A copy spell aimed at the original therefore helps pay for your plan. Multiple Goldspans do not multiply each Treasure's output beyond two mana: each grants the same two-mana ability. Newly made attack Treasures arrive after Brudiclad's beginning-of-combat conversion and remain Treasure until a later conversion.

Producing more tokens than you spend

Academy Manufactor

Turns each Treasure, Food, or Clue creation into one of each, so Big Score normally makes six tokens instead of two. It does not replace Myr, Servo, Drone, or Thopter creation. Multiple Manufactors multiply the replacement output further; this is finite, but avoid enormous mandatory draw bursts with Fateful Discovery.

Worldwalker Helm

Adds one Map to each event creating one or more artifact tokens, not one Map per token. Its activated ability copies an existing artifact token, useful for another Construct or a permanent copy of a temporary template. It cannot target a nonartifact creature token unless another effect made that token an artifact.

Stridehangar Automaton

Adds one flying Thopter to an artifact-token creation event and pumps your Thopters. It stacks with Worldwalker Helm, supplying more material without needing to cast extra spells. Neither replacement effect repeats itself indefinitely on the tokens it just added.

Third Path Iconoclast

Turns every noncreature spell into an artifact Soldier, including mana rocks, interaction, and copy spells. These inexpensive bodies feed Skullclamp early and become your finishing army later.

Sai, Master Thopterist

Makes a flying token when you CAST an artifact spell, not when an artifact land is played or a token is created. Its activated sacrifice of two artifacts can turn surplus material into a card or recover value before removal. Do not sacrifice your only useful copy template if you still need it for combat.

Saheeli, Sublime Artificer

Makes Servos from noncreature spells and can temporarily turn an artifact token into a copy of another artifact or creature you control. Use a Treasure or Servo as the target and a powerful creature as the model, then let Brudiclad copy the altered token. The other tokens retain their copied form after Saheeli's original temporary effect expires.

Loyal Apprentice

With Brudiclad in play, put Brudiclad's combat trigger on the stack first and Apprentice's above it. Resolve the Apprentice first so its new Thopter joins the conversion. If Brudiclad leaves before the Apprentice trigger resolves, its lieutenant condition can fail.

The Misty Mountains Cold

Makes Treasure on its chapters. Once a chapter leaves you with at least four Treasures, sacrifice the Saga to make a 6/6 flying Dragon token. That is a ready-made combat template, while the earlier Treasures also supported development. Keep enough Treasures through the relevant chapter if you want the Dragon rather than spending them prematurely.

Myr Battlesphere

One entry supplies the large body plus four artifact tokens. Copying it with a spell or activated ability makes another real entry and four more Myr; simply converting existing tokens does not. Its attack ability needs untapped Myr, so Myr already tapped from attacking cannot also pay for it. Choose between a broad attack and holding Myr back to fuel one Battlesphere.

Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki

Supplies an attack-Treasure Goblin, filters cards into a better hand, and becomes a repeatable token-copy engine. Reflection can copy Hellrider, Wurmcoil Engine, Myr Battlesphere, or Cyberdrive Awakener, but cannot target legendary creatures such as Brudiclad or Urza. The temporary copy can still become the template for your other tokens.

Making the first copy and keeping the army

Saheeli, the Sun's Brilliance

For two mana and a tap, creates an artifact copy of another creature or artifact you control. Her original token is sacrificed at the next end step, but the other tokens Brudiclad converted into copies are not subject to that delayed sacrifice. Saheeli herself needs haste or a turn under your control to tap.

Molten Duplication

The cheap spell version of that plan. Its artifact-copy exception is copied by Brudiclad, so a Hellrider copied this way produces artifact Hellriders that still count for Master of Etherium and Mechanized Production. Only the original token made by Duplication is tied to its delayed sacrifice.

Irenicus's Vile Duplication

Makes a permanent flying token copy that is explicitly not legendary. This is the safe way to copy Brudiclad or Urza and then spread that form without losing the army to the legend rule. A normal legendary token remains legendary when copied, which is a serious trap with your other copy effects.

Several nonlegendary Brudiclads each trigger at the next beginning of combat. Resolve those triggers one at a time, potentially growing the token count and choosing new templates between separate resolutions. A Brudiclad created during the current beginning-of-combat step does not retroactively trigger for that same beginning. This growth is finite, not an infinite combat engine.

Quasiduplicate

A low-cost permanent creature-token copy, with jump-start for another use by discarding a card. It only targets your own creatures and does not remove legendary. Discard a creature that Feldon can use later when that improves both halves of the plan.

Rite of Replication

Copies any creature, including an opponent's best nonlegendary threat. Four mana is enough to get the one template Brudiclad needs; the nine-mana kicked version makes five real entries and is a late standalone payoff. Do not hold it for kicker if one copy already wins.

Three Steps Ahead

Flexible counterspell, copy spell, or filtering spell depending on the additional costs you choose. The copy mode costs four mana total and can copy your artifact or creature. Holding it up lets you defend the board or make a template on the last opponent's turn.

See Double

Can copy an opposing creature or a spell on the stack, and can choose both modes when an opponent has at least eight graveyard cards. A copy of a permanent spell resolves as a token, which can become Brudiclad's template. It does not counter the original spell, and See Double itself cannot be copied.

Feldon of the Third Path

Reuses a creature card from your graveyard as a hasty artifact token, including after a sweeper. Its delayed sacrifice only applies to Feldon's original token; converted tokens remain. It cannot recover a dead token, because tokens do not remain creature cards in the graveyard.

Drawing without waiting for a successful attack

Rhystic Study

One of the three Game Changers, supplying cards while you develop mana and hold interaction. It keeps the deck moving when the commander costs too much to redeploy immediately.

Mystic Remora

An efficient early draw engine that can refill before Brudiclad arrives. Let it go when cumulative upkeep would prevent a meaningful development or protection play; you do not have to keep paying forever.

Skullclamp

Converts 1/1 Soldiers, Servos, Thopters, and small Myr into two cards each. Equip them before converting the army into larger creatures if you need cards more than attackers. The 2/1 Myr Brudiclad creates also dies to the toughness reduction when equipped unless another effect increases its toughness.

Idol of Oblivion

Draws once you have created any token that turn, including a Treasure. Brudiclad supplies that condition every combat. Its eight-mana sacrifice creates a 10/10 Eldrazi token when you need a big template and have no better one, but drawing cards is its main early job.

Curiosity Crafter

Turns successful token attacks into cards and removes your hand-size limit. Its draws are mandatory. Converting the whole army into Crafter copies multiplies draw triggers dramatically, so count your library before choosing that template.

Fateful Discovery

Draws whenever any artifact enters under your control, including tokens and artifact lands. It does not draw merely because existing tokens become artifacts through Brudiclad. Its draw is mandatory; do not combine a massive Manufactor burst with too few library cards remaining.

Big Score

Discards an excess land or a Feldon target, draws two, and makes two Treasures at instant speed. Manufactor, Helm, and Automaton increase the token return. It can set up a next-turn attack while you keep mana available for interaction.

Paying six mana without sacrificing your whole army

Jeska's Will

The second Game Changer provides a burst to deploy Brudiclad or a finishing sequence. With Brudiclad already present you can choose both modes. Exiled cards must be played that turn with their normal costs and timing, and choosing a nearly empty-handed opponent produces little mana.

Arcane Signet

Alongside Sol Ring, Izzet Signet, Talisman of Creativity, Fellwar Stone, and Mind Stone, gives early acceleration that also raises your artifact count. Mox Opal becomes active at three artifacts, including itself; do not keep a hand that assumes it works before you can establish metalcraft. Mind Stone can become a card when mana is no longer the bottleneck.

Enthusiastic Mechanaut

Reduces the generic cost of artifact spells, including Brudiclad and expensive artifact threats. It does not reduce coloured costs, activated abilities, or most copy sorceries. Its flying body is a useful blocker while developing.

Storm the Vault // Vault of Catlacan

Makes Treasure from combat connections and transforms at your end step if you still control at least five artifacts. Its land side produces blue mana equal to your artifact count, supporting big copy turns without sacrificing tokens. Converting artifact tokens into nonartifact creatures can reduce that output sharply.

Professional Face-Breaker

Makes Treasure when your creatures connect with different players, and lets you sacrifice Treasure for temporary card access. Those tokens are also potential attackers after conversion, so decide whether you need mana, cards, or bodies before spending them.

Clearing a path and stopping a losing turn

Cyclonic Rift

The third Game Changer, either a cheap emergency bounce or a seven-mana one-sided reset that clears blockers and opposing engines. It is a strong way to turn a developed token board into a decisive attack without needing the perfect template.

Perplexing Test

The nontoken mode preserves your token army while bouncing many opposing blockers, but also returns Brudiclad and your other nontoken creatures. Newly created tokens may lose the haste they need when he leaves. The token mode can answer an opposing token army, but also removes your creature tokens permanently when they leave the battlefield.

Blasphemous Act

An efficient reset when the board gets out of control. A Wurmcoil army leaves smaller tokens after dying, while most other templates do not. Brudiclad also dies unless protected, so evaluate your follow-up mana and copy access before casting it.

Vandalblast

Overload clears opponents' artifacts while preserving your own engine. The one-mana mode is often enough for a key hate piece. Do not delay answering a dangerous artifact merely to extract maximum value from overload.

Mana Drain

Counters a critical spell and helps fund the generic part of your next threat or copy spell. The resulting mana cannot pay Whir of Invention's three blue pips. Arcane Denial is the easier-to-hold-up backup that replaces itself later while also giving the opponent cards.

Swan Song

Cheap protection from a dangerous enchantment, instant, or sorcery. An Offer You Can't Refuse covers any noncreature spell, at the cost of giving its controller two Treasures. Keep one of these available when committing the only useful template.

Pongify

A one-mana creature answer when you cannot afford to stop developing. Resculpt exiles an artifact or creature for two mana, providing an answer to indestructible threats as well. Account for the replacement token as a possible blocker.

Chaos Warp

Covers permanent types your other removal may miss, particularly troublesome enchantments. The replacement permanent can be dangerous, but protecting your engine or preventing a win is worth that uncertainty.

Keeping the template and commander alive

Deflecting Swat

Free with Brudiclad present and useful against targeted removal aimed at him or your one valuable token. It cannot answer an untargeted sweeper. Brudiclad's own transformation ability does not target, so opponents cannot redirect its chosen template with a target-changing effect.

Spellskite

Redirects a spell or ability to itself only when it is a legal target. It can absorb many artifact or creature removal effects, but cannot take a spell that specifically targets a legendary creature or an enchantment. Paying life instead of blue mana helps when you tapped out to deploy Brudiclad.

Lightning Greaves

Gives Brudiclad haste and shroud or enables Saheeli and Feldon to tap immediately. Its shroud also stops your own targeted copy spells from targeting the equipped creature. Move it to another creature before making that copy, then re-equip if needed. The other creature tokens already get haste from Brudiclad.

Padeem, Consul of Innovation

Gives your artifacts hexproof, including Brudiclad and artifact copy templates, while often drawing in upkeep because of your expensive artifacts. It does not protect nonartifact creatures such as a normal Hellrider token, and hexproof does not stop an overloaded Vandalblast or another untargeted wipe.

Finding the missing piece and using the land slots

Fabricate

Find Skullclamp or Idol when cards are the bottleneck, a rock when mana is missing, or Manufactor, Helm, Synthesizer, or Wurmcoil when the board is ready for an engine or template. It cannot find Hellrider, Mechanized Production, or an ordinary copy sorcery.

Whir of Invention

Finds an artifact directly onto the battlefield at instant speed. Improvise can tap expendable artifacts to pay generic mana, but you still need three blue mana. If you tap tokens during your turn, they remain tapped after conversion and cannot attack that turn without another untap effect.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Graveyard interaction that is searchable with your artifact tutors and Saga. Its sacrifice can clear only opponents' graveyards, preserving Feldon's targets, or draw a card when graveyard control is unnecessary. Do not sacrifice it for a card if an opponent's graveyard is their most credible winning line.

Urza's Saga

Makes scaling Construct tokens that are already ideal templates, then finds Sol Ring, Skullclamp, Mox Opal, or Soul-Guide Lantern. It does not find two-mana rocks. Remember it sacrifices itself after its third chapter; count that land loss when planning a six-mana commander turn.

Inventors' Fair

Turns a land into the artifact you need once you control at least three artifacts. Activating it costs four mana, tapping it, and sacrificing the land, so it is a late-game tutor rather than early acceleration.

Academy Ruins

Recovers a destroyed artifact to the top of your library. Brudiclad and most artifact threats are eligible, but dead tokens are not cards you can return. This costs your next draw unless another draw engine immediately collects the recovered card.

Uthros, Titanic Godcore

Tapping creatures to station twelve charge counters turns this land into substantial artifact-based blue mana. You can station with creatures that entered this turn, but tapping them spends potential attackers. Charge counters persist, so build toward twelve over quiet turns rather than sacrificing a lethal attack for extra mana.

Treasure Vault

Converts spare mana into X Treasures by paying two times X and sacrificing the land. Create them on the last opponent's end step or before Brudiclad's combat trigger resolves. Manufactor and the other artifact-token modifiers can make the activation produce many more objects than the printed X.

What to keep in an opening hand and how to sequence

Keep three lands with both colours, an early rock, and either a token engine or a draw engine. A hand of copy spells without a worthwhile creature or token source is a mulligan. So is a hand that relies on inactive Mox Opal as its only extra mana. The commander is six mana, and repeated land drops matter more than a spectacular but uncastable opener.

An example keep is Command Tower, Steam Vents, Island, Arcane Signet, Third Path Iconoclast, Skullclamp, and Swan Song. Develop Iconoclast when the next noncreature spells can make tokens, then use Clamp to find lands and a template while preserving protection. Fetchlands find Volcanic Island, Steam Vents, or Thundering Falls as needed; avoid fetching a tapped source when it prevents an important response.

Build tokens before casting Brudiclad unless you need his immediate Myr and haste to stabilize. Your ideal commander turn starts with several inexpensive tokens already on the battlefield and a template ready before combat. Urza's Construct, a Synthesizer Construct, or the Saga's Dragon avoids spending another spell solely to make a template.

At beginning of combat, resolve Loyal Apprentice before Brudiclad when you want the extra token converted. Activate Saheeli or Feldon in response to Brudiclad's trigger if necessary. Once that trigger starts resolving, there is no pause between creating the Myr and choosing the template, so finish all preparation beforehand.

Before converting, count the mana you need afterward, the untapped attackers you will actually have, and any artifact-based protection or size that would change if the new form is not an artifact. Choosing no conversion is often correct when you need Treasure for interaction or already have a mixed board that works better than one uniform army.

The primary finish is a developed board converted into lethal attackers or Hellrider attack triggers. Mechanized Production provides an upkeep-based alternative, while Cyberdrive and ordinary token combat let you play without Brudiclad. Do not turn every token into a legendary copy unless the template explicitly removed legendary.

This deck uses Rhystic Study, Cyclonic Rift, and Jeska's Will as its three Game Changers. Its intended finishes are finite board conversions, artifact-token growth, and combat after normal Bracket 3 development. It contains no planned infinite combo, extra-turn chain, or mass land denial.
