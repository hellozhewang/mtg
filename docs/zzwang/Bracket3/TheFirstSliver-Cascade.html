Every Sliver you cast cascades — and a cascade cast is still a CAST, so if the free spell is also a Sliver, it cascades too. One five-drop Sliver chains down through the whole curve, and each link is another body, another anthem, and another enters-the-battlefield trigger shared by the entire board. The deck is not really a tribal aggro deck. It is a combo deck whose combo is "cast a Sliver".

The commander and the chain
The First Sliver

five colours, a 7/7, and every Sliver spell you cast has cascade. Once he is out, the correct play is almost always "cast the most expensive Sliver in hand" — the chain does the rest.

Morophon, the Boundless

name Sliver and every Sliver spell costs {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} less. Most of the deck becomes free, and each free cast still cascades.

Maelstrom Nexus

the first spell you cast each turn cascades, Sliver or not.

Quandrix, the Proof

your instants and sorceries from hand cascade too.

Maelstrom Wanderer

two more cascades, and permanent haste for the whole board — which matters because the chain deploys creatures mid-turn.

Apex Devastator

four cascades on one card.

Call Forth the Tempest

two cascades plus a sweeper sized by everything the chain has already cast.

Averna, the Chaos Bloom

every cascade also puts a land onto the battlefield, so a long chain ramps you at the same time.

Getting paid for the free casts
Nalfeshnee

every spell cast from exile is COPIED. A cascade chain with Nalfeshnee out doubles every Sliver in it.

Quintorius Kand

two damage to each opponent and two life to you for EVERY spell cast from exile. A six-link chain is twelve damage to the table before combat.

Lavabelly Sliver

every Sliver that enters pings a player for one and gains you one. During a chain, that is one trigger per link, on top of everything else.

Harmonic Sliver

every Sliver that enters destroys an artifact or enchantment. A cascade chain becomes a one-sided Vandalblast on the table's whole board.

Guardian Project

a card for every differently named nontoken creature — and every Sliver in the deck has a different name.

Kindred Discovery

name Sliver. A card for every Sliver that enters AND every one that attacks.

The mana that makes it possible
Gemhide Sliver

all Slivers tap for any colour. Two Slivers later this is a five-colour mana engine.

Manaweft Sliver

the second copy.

Basal Sliver

every Sliver can sacrifice for {B}{B}, which is how you convert a huge board into a huge spell.

Birds of Paradise

turn-one fixing.

Bloom Tender

scales with your colours.

Delighted Halfling

uncounterable mana for legendary spells — The First Sliver himself.

Nature's Lore

an untapped dual.

Farseek

a shockland.

Fellwar Stone

usually taps for exactly what you are missing.

Lotus Bloom

suspend it on turn one and it is three mana of any colour on turn four.

The suspend package

Suspended cards are cast for free from EXILE when their last counter is removed, which triggers Nalfeshnee and Quintorius Kand just like a cascade does.

Ancestral Vision

three cards for one mana, four turns later.

Inevitable Betrayal

the best creature out of an opponent's library, onto your battlefield.

Profane Tutor

any card in the deck for two mana on a delay.

Lotus Bloom

acceleration with no card cost.

Anthems and evasion
Predatory Sliver

+1/+1 to the whole team.

Cloudshredder Sliver

flying AND haste for every Sliver. It is what turns a cascade chain into a lethal attack the same turn.

Galerider Sliver

the one-mana flying anthem.

Bonescythe Sliver

double strike for the whole board.

Leeching Sliver

a life off the defending player for every Sliver that attacks.

Akroma's Will

with your commander out, take both halves. On a wide Sliver board it simply ends the game.

Protection and removal
Crystalline Sliver

all Slivers have shroud. Nothing they play can target your board — but note it stops your own targeting too.

Diffusion Sliver

the gentler version, taxing their removal two.

Hibernation Sliver

every Sliver can bounce itself for two life, saving it from removal or resetting an enters trigger.

Quick Sliver

Slivers gain flash, so you can build the board at the end of their turn.

Necrotic Sliver

every Sliver can sacrifice to destroy any permanent. On a wide board that is unlimited removal.

Heroic Intervention

hexproof and indestructible through a wrath.

Assassin's Trophy

two mana for any permanent.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, the clear for a lethal attack.

Raise the Palisade

name Sliver and every other creature at the table goes back to hand. A one-sided bounce wrath.

Patriarch's Bidding

name Sliver. You rebuild your entire graveyard; most tables get far less back.

Homing Sliver

gives every Sliver in hand slivercycling, so dead cards become the exact Sliver you need.

Mystic Remora

the best card in the deck on turns one through four.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep fixing and a cheap Sliver. Gemhide Sliver or Manaweft Sliver on turn two solves the five-colour problem by itself, because every Sliver you play afterwards is also a mana source.

The First Sliver costs five specific colours. Prioritise Morophon, the mana Slivers and Bloom Tender over another anthem — the deck does nothing until he resolves, and everything once he does.

Cast the most expensive Sliver first. Cascade finds a cheaper card, so leading with a five-drop chains through four-, three-, two- and one-drops; leading with a one-drop finds nothing.

Cloudshredder Sliver

keep it in mind before you start the chain. Flying and haste means the board you just built attacks this turn, which is usually the difference between a big turn and a lethal one.
