Sauron turns "the Ring tempts you" into a free wheel: discard your hand, draw four. In almost any other deck that is a cost. Here it is the point — the cards you throw away are seven-drops you were never going to cast, and the graveyard is where this deck keeps its best creatures. Every temptation is card advantage AND a loaded graveyard at the same time.

The commander

Sauron, the Dark Lord

a 7/6 with a ward that eats a legendary permanent, an Orc Army that grows every time an opponent casts anything, and — the important line — discard your hand and draw four whenever the Ring tempts you.

Call of the Ring

tempts you at the beginning of EVERY upkeep. With Sauron out that is a free wheel each turn, and it draws an extra card when you name a Ring-bearer.

Ringsight

tempts you and tutors any card sharing a colour with a legend you control.

Sauron's Ransom

tempts you, and either pile is fine — cards in hand are cards, cards in the graveyard are reanimation targets.

Orcish Bowmasters

amasses alongside Sauron's own Orcs, and punishes every extra card the table draws.

The Army is a real clock. Once it connects, Sauron tempts you again, which wheels you again.

Filling the graveyard on purpose

Entomb

one mana, instant speed, exactly the creature you want.

Buried Alive

three at once.

Careful Study

two down for one mana.

Faithless Looting

the same, twice with flashback.

Frantic Search

free, and it untaps three lands.

Ledger Shredder

connives on everyone's second spell each turn, filtering constantly.

Grave Researcher // Reanimate

surveils every upkeep and prepares a free Reanimate once three creatures are down there.

Archfiend of Ifnir

every discard puts a -1/-1 counter on each of their creatures. Discarding a full hand to Sauron's trigger is a one-sided board wipe.

Monument to Endurance

every discard becomes a card, a Treasure or three life from each opponent. On a wheel turn it is all three.

Bringing them back

Reanimate

one mana, any graveyard.

Animate Dead

two, and it works on theirs.

Dance of the Dead

the third copy, and the untap clause rarely matters.

Necromancy

flash speed, so you can reanimate at the end of their turn or in response to a wrath.

Persist

two mana for any nonlegendary creature.

Chainer, Nightmare Adept

discard a card to cast a creature from your graveyard each turn, and anything not cast from hand gets haste.

Living Death

your graveyard is deliberately the deepest at the table.

Ancient Brass Dragon

on connection, reanimate creatures with total mana value up to a d20 roll, from ANY graveyard.

Anger

sitting in the graveyard with a Mountain out, everything you reanimate has haste.

What you are reanimating

Archon of Cruelty

the best target. On entry and on every attack, an opponent sacrifices, discards and loses three while you draw and gain three.

Burning-Rune Demon

a two-card tutor where the "wrong" one goes to your graveyard, which is exactly where you want it.

Marang River Regent // Coil and Catch

a 6/7 flier that bounces two of their permanents, and an Omen half that draws three when you would rather have cards.

Smaug, Wicked Worm

Treasures scaled to their artifact count, and a card every time you spend Treasure mana.

Archfiend of Ifnir

a 5/4 flier that turns your own wheels into sweepers.

Interaction

Mana Drain

counter, and the mana casts a reanimation spell.

Counterspell

the clean two-mana answer.

Arcane Denial

cheap, and the cards they get arrive too late.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for anything noncreature.

Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it redirects the removal aimed at Sauron.

Bitter Triumph

a creature or planeswalker, and the discard cost is upside here.

Feed the Swarm

the only clean enchantment answer in Grixis.

Chaos Warp

the second one, and it covers planeswalkers.

Blasphemous Act

cheap, and your best creatures are in the graveyard where a wrath cannot reach them.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, the clear for a lethal reanimated board.

Dauthi Voidwalker

unblockable, blanks their graveyards, and steals their best card.

Closing the game

Chandra's Ignition

point it at Archon of Cruelty or Sauron. Six or seven damage to every other creature on the board and to each opponent, all at once.

Shadowspear

trample and lifelink on a fatty, and it strips indestructible and hexproof from their blockers.

Lightning Greaves

haste for the thing you just reanimated, and shroud on Sauron.

Living Death

after a couple of wheels, this is usually lethal on the spot.

Card flow and mana

Rhystic Study

their turns pay for yours.

Mystic Remora

the best card in the deck on turns one through four.

Black Market Connections

three effects a turn, and this deck's life total is not its bottleneck.

Relic of Sauron

two coloured mana and a repeatable loot.

Fellwar Stone

usually taps for exactly the pip you need.

Arcane Signet

the same.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep an enabler and a reanimation spell. Entomb plus Reanimate with two lands beats any hand with an Archon of Cruelty in it, because the Archon is better in your graveyard than in your hand.

Do not be precious about your hand. Sauron's wheel discards everything — so cast what matters first, then take the temptation. Cards you were holding "just in case" become Reanimate targets.

Call of the Ring

two mana, and with Sauron out it is a wheel every single upkeep. Prioritise it over almost any single fatty.

Sauron, the Dark Lord

his ward costs you a legendary permanent, which is real. Lightning Greaves is often the better protection, because it means they cannot target him at all.
