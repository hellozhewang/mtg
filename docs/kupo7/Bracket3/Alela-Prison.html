A prison deck whose lock pieces are also its clock. Alela turns every artifact and enchantment you cast into a 1/1 flier, so Moat, Propaganda, Rhystic Study and the signets are not durdle — each one is a body. You wall off the ground and win in the air, where nobody is allowed to follow.

The commander and the token engine
Alela, Artful Provocateur

flying, deathtouch, lifelink on a 2/3, other Faeries get +1/+0, and every artifact or enchantment spell makes a 1/1 flying Faerie. Read the deck as "every noncreature spell is also a creature spell".

Bitterblossom

a Faerie every upkeep for one life, forever, off a one-mana enchantment. The best turn-one play in the deck.

Bitterbloom Bearer

the second Bitterblossom. You want to see one of the two early enough that the wall has bodies behind it.

Anointed Procession

doubles every Faerie, every Bitterblossom trigger, every Construct off Urza's Saga. With Bitterblossom out it is two free fliers a turn.

Roaming Throne

name Faerie. Alela's cast trigger doubles, and so does Bitterblossom's — the Throne is the single biggest multiplier in the deck.

Court of Grace

a flier every upkeep, and a 4/4 Angel instead while you hold the monarchy. The card draw from being monarch is what you were short on.

The wall, and why it is one-sided
Moat

creatures without flying cannot attack. Your entire board flies. This is not a symmetrical stax piece — it is a wall with a door only you can use.

Propaganda

taxes the fliers and the trample that Moat does not stop. In a four-player pod the tax is a deterrent: they go hit whoever did not pay for one.

No Mercy

anything that does connect with you dies. Between this and Moat, attacking you is a losing trade even before the tax.

Authority of the Consuls

everything they play arrives tapped, so no surprise blocker and no haste kill. It is also an enchantment, so it makes a Faerie.

Blind Obedience

the same effect plus extort, and it turns off haste-based combo turns.

Cursed Totem

no creature activated abilities. Mana dorks, sac outlets and tutor creatures all stop. Your Faeries have no abilities to lose.

Drawing off the lock
Rhystic Study

in a deck already taxing their mana with Propaganda and Blind Obedience, the "unless they pay" cards get paid far less often. Alela makes a Faerie when you cast it.

Mystic Remora

the early-game version. Cast it turn one or two and let the cumulative upkeep run three or four turns.

Esper Sentinel

same tax, on a body, and it survives Moat's world because it never needs to attack.

Smothering Tithe

the mana that pays for everything else. Nobody pays the two when they are already paying Propaganda.

Kindred Discovery

name Faerie. Draw on every token that enters and every one that attacks. With Bitterblossom and Anointed Procession this is two to four cards a turn.

Reconnaissance Mission

a card for every Faerie that connects, and under Moat they all connect.

Enduring Curiosity

the same job on an enchantment creature that comes back once when it dies.

Idol of Oblivion

a token every turn already means a card every turn; late it makes a 10/10.

Turning Faeries into cards and kills
Skullclamp

your tokens are 1/1s that Alela makes 2/1. Clamping one kills it and draws two. This is a mana sink that never runs out while Bitterblossom is on the table.

Grave Pact

now every clamped Faerie makes all three opponents sacrifice a creature. Draw two, wipe the pod's board, repeat. This is the deck's real engine and the reason to prioritize Skullclamp.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

feed her the same expendable Faeries, plus the Seals you have already used. Each one is draw a card and drain two, or they give up a permanent.

Urza's Saga

makes its own Construct, then fetches Skullclamp, Sol Ring or Mox Opal. Hall of Heliod's Generosity and Academy Ruins buy back what gets destroyed.

Closing the game
Gravitational Shift

fliers get +2/+0, everything on the ground gets -2/-0. Your Faeries become 4/1s and half the table's creatures shrink out of relevance. Combined with Moat this ends games in two swings.

Sephara, Sky's Blade

costs almost nothing with a board of fliers and gives them all lifelink and indestructible — the anti-wrath button and a damage doubler at once.

Akroma's Will

the finisher. On a wide flying board, protection from creatures for the alpha strike or double strike for the kill, and both halves are on one card.

Cyclonic Rift

the other way games end. Overload it once the wall is up and swing into an empty table.

Answers
Mana Drain

the counterspell you want most, because the floating mana deploys two prison pieces the following turn.

Swan Song

it gives them a 2/2 flier, which Moat does not stop — but Gravitational Shift shrinks it and No Mercy kills it if it ever attacks.

Swords to Plowshares

for the one creature that flies or ignores Moat.

Oubliette

the cleanest answer to a commander, since it is not destruction, exile or targeting once it resolves.

Imprisoned in the Moon

turns a commander into a land they cannot recast from the command zone.

Darksteel Mutation

same idea for one mana, and it is an enchantment, so it makes a Faerie.

Seal of Cleansing

pre-pay for the artifact or enchantment removal, then crack it in response — and it is Braids food afterwards.

Aura of Silence

taxes their artifacts and enchantments two while it sits there, then eats one.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a hand that does two of these three things: makes mana, makes a Faerie every turn, and stops the fastest deck at the table. Bitterblossom plus any two lands is a keep on its own.

Alela does NOT need to come down on turn four. She is a value multiplier, not the plan — deploying Moat or Bitterblossom on curve matters more, and she is much safer cast into an untapped-mana table that is busy paying Propaganda.

Moat

hold it until a creature deck actually commits. Cast into an empty board it just eats a Krosan Grip for nothing.

Skullclamp

find this before you find a finisher. Clamp plus Bitterblossom plus Grave Pact is a better win condition than any single card in the deck.
