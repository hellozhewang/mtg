Teysa doubles every death trigger you control. Blood Artist drains twice, afterlife makes twice the Spirits, Grave Pact makes each opponent sacrifice twice, and Liliana draws two. Then the token doublers multiply the Spirits AGAIN. The deck's job is to get a free sacrifice outlet and one drain payoff onto the battlefield; after that, every creature that dies — yours or theirs — is several points of life off everyone at the table.

The commander and the second doubler
Teysa Karlov

if a creature DYING triggers an ability of a permanent you control, it triggers an additional time. Your tokens also get vigilance and lifelink, which quietly turns a wide board into a life-swing every combat.

Drivnod, Carnage Dominus

the exact same doubling on a second body. With both out, every death trigger fires FOUR times.

Anointed Procession

doubles every token created. Afterlife doubled by Teysa and then doubled again by Procession is four Spirits from one 2/1.

Mondrak, Glory Dominus

the second token doubler, and it can become indestructible.

Free sacrifice outlets
Ashnod's Altar

free, repeatable, and it makes the mana for the next spell.

Phyrexian Altar

the same with colour fixing.

Carrion Feeder

a one-drop that eats everything and grows.

Bartolomé del Presidio

creatures AND artifacts, so Treasures are fodder too.

Priest of Forgotten Gods

two creatures becomes two life from every opponent, a forced sacrifice from every opponent, two black mana and a card. Doubled by Teysa it is the single most brutal activation in the deck.

Deadly Dispute

two cards and a Treasure for a creature that was going to die anyway.

Diabolic Intent

any card in the deck for a spare Spirit.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

every end step, they give up a permanent or you draw and they lose two.

Bodies that leave bodies
Ministrant of Obligation

afterlife 2, doubled to four Spirits, doubled again to eight with a Procession out.

Orzhov Enforcer

afterlife 1 on a deathtouch blocker.

Doomed Traveler

one mana, two bodies.

Hunted Witness

the same, with lifelink attached.

Hallowed Spiritkeeper

dies into a Spirit for every creature card in your graveyard — which by the mid-game is a dozen.

Elenda, the Dusk Rose

grows on every death and dies into a pile of lifelinking Vampires as big as she got.

Bitterblossom

a free Faerie every upkeep, forever. It is the most reliable fodder in the deck.

Ophiomancer

a deathtouch Snake every upkeep, which both blocks and dies well.

Pawn of Ulamog

a mana Spawn for every nontoken creature that dies, doubled to two.

Pitiless Plunderer

a Treasure per death, doubled to two, which pays for everything else.

Luminous Broodmoth

your ground creatures come back with flying when they die, so every death is a two-for-one.

Payoffs for the deaths
Blood Artist

a drain for every creature that dies ANYWHERE, doubled by Teysa. Three opponents' creatures dying is six life swung.

Cruel Celebrant

the same, restricted to your own board, and it catches planeswalkers.

Bastion of Remembrance

the enchantment version, so removal cannot touch it easily.

Elas il-Kor, Sadistic Pilgrim

drains on your deaths and gains on your creatures entering — both halves fire constantly here.

Funeral Room // Awakening Hall

another drain per death, on a Room you can later unlock to return every creature from your graveyard at once.

Marionette Apprentice

a drain for every artifact OR creature you lose, so Treasures count.

Mirkwood Bats

a drain when you CREATE a token and again when you sacrifice it. With Teysa and Anointed Procession, one afterlife death is eight separate drains.

Grave Pact

every creature you lose makes each opponent sacrifice one — twice, with Teysa. Two turns of this empties the table's board.

Dictate of Erebos

the same effect with flash, so you can land it in response to a wrath.

Liliana, Dreadhorde General

two cards per creature death, plus a Zombie every turn and a symmetric edict on the minus.

Midnight Reaper

two cards per nontoken creature death.

Grim Haruspex

the same, without the life loss.

Athreos, God of Passage

every creature that dies comes back to your hand unless they pay three — doubled, that is six life or they hand it back.

Removal and closing
Anguished Unmaking

instant-speed exile for any nonland permanent.

Path to Exile

one mana.

Damn

a removal spell early, a wrath late — and your board rebuilds from a wrath better than anyone's.

Orcish Bowmasters

flash, punishes their draws, and the Orc Army is fodder.

Bolas's Citadel

casts your deck off the top for life, then sacrifices ten permanents to deal ten to each opponent. On a board of Spirits and Treasures, ten permanents is one turn.

Black Market Connections

three effects a turn including a body, all paid in life you are gaining back through lifelink tokens.

Esper Sentinel

taxes their first noncreature spell each turn.

Dark Ritual

turn-one acceleration into a turn-two Bitterblossom or turn-three Teysa.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a sacrifice outlet or a token generator. Bitterblossom on turn two, Carrion Feeder on turn one, or Ashnod's Altar all mean Teysa has something to do the moment she lands.

Get the drain payoff down BEFORE you start sacrificing. Blood Artist or Cruel Celebrant first, then the outlet, then the fodder — killing six creatures with no payoff on board is six wasted cards.

Teysa Karlov

four mana, and she doubles everything already on the battlefield. She is a multiplier, not a starter, so develop the engine first and cast her into a turn where you can immediately convert.

Priest of Forgotten Gods

with Teysa out this is usually lethal over two activations. Prioritise finding her, and hold spare tokens so you can activate on their end step and again on your turn.
