Eminence discounts OTHER Sphinx spells while The Ur-Sphinx is in the command zone or on the battlefield; it does not discount the commander itself. The mill-and-cast attack ability works only while the commander is on the battlefield. Develop discounted Sphinxes and card engines first, then deploy the commander when existing attackers can immediately turn it into free spells.

Develop without waiting for nine mana

The Ur-Sphinx

A 10/10 flying finisher and a command-zone discount for the rest of the tribe. Its attack ability triggers once when one or more of your Sphinxes attack, not once per Sphinx. It need not attack itself, so casting it before attacking with an older Sphinx can immediately generate value.

For each resolving attack trigger, each player mills cards equal to the number of Sphinxes that attacked. You may cast up to one card milled this way from EACH player, including yourself, without paying its mana cost. In a four-player game that is up to four spells per trigger. More Sphinxes improve the choice of cards, rather than increasing the one-card-per-player allowance.

Cast those spells during the trigger's resolution; you cannot save the permission for later. Normal timing based on card type is ignored, but other restrictions and mandatory additional costs still apply. Lands cannot be cast, and an X in a free spell's mana cost is zero. New creatures from those spells do not join a combat whose attackers were already declared.

Sol Ring

Accelerates the generic costs of your large creatures and artifacts. Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, and the three Talismans provide early color fixing; protect access to blue and do not mistake colorless acceleration for the white-blue-black needed to cast Raffine.

Urza's Incubator

Choose Sphinx. It reduces Sphinx creature spells by two generic mana, including the commander. With Eminence, most other Sphinxes receive a combined three-mana generic discount.

Herald's Horn

Another generic discount and a possible Sphinx card at each upkeep. The card goes to hand rather than being drawn. It is less explosive than Incubator but stays useful across a long game.

Sapphire Medallion

Reduces generic costs of blue spells, including your commander, most Sphinxes, and several protective spells. It cannot reduce a colored mana requirement.

Watcher of the Spheres

Reduces the generic cost of flying creature spells, including The Ur-Sphinx. It is not a Sphinx, so Eminence does not discount Watcher; Mirror Entity also normally lacks flying and gets no Watcher discount.

Smothering Tithe

A Game Changer that builds mana from opponents' draws when they decline to pay. It helps reach the commander while leaving mana for interaction and supports large Mirror Entity activations.

Black Market Connections

Supplies cards, Treasure, and a 3/2 changeling that counts as a Sphinx. Each mode has a separate life cost: one for Treasure, two for drawing, three for the token. Choose only the modes you need; taking all three every turn costs six life.

Tivit, Seller of Secrets

Discounted to five mana by Eminence, it produces five voting outcomes in a normal four-player game because you vote twice. Your two votes can guarantee two Treasure tokens if you choose bribery; the opponents determine the other votes. Entry and combat-damage triggers supply mana or cards while building a flying threat.

Make every Sphinx repay its cost

Unesh, Criosphinx Sovereign

Another two-mana generic reduction, plus a four-card split whenever it or another Sphinx you control enters. You choose one pile for your hand and put the other into your graveyard, stocking reanimation and spell reuse. This is not drawing cards.

Defiler of Dreams

You may pay two life to reduce a blue permanent spell's blue requirement by one, and each blue permanent cast draws a card. It stacks with generic reductions but can consume significant life alongside your lands, Reanimate, and Black Market Connections.

Consecrated Sphinx

The second Game Changer gives optional draws whenever opponents draw. Resolve and track each trigger rather than drawing automatically; with Roaming Throne, each opposing draw can offer four cards.

Sphinx of Enlightenment

Gives one opponent a card and gives you three. With Consecrated Sphinx, that opposing draw can give you two more cards; Smothering Tithe can also turn it into Treasure if the opponent declines payment.

Kindred Discovery

Choose Sphinx. Draws from your Sphinx entries and attacks, including changeling tokens. Roaming Throne does not double this enchantment's triggers. The draws are mandatory, so track library size on large reanimation turns.

Roaming Throne

Choose Sphinx as it enters. It becomes a Sphinx on the battlefield and doubles triggered abilities of your OTHER Sphinx creatures: Unesh, Tivit, Consecrated Sphinx, and the commander's attack ability are premium choices. It is a Golem spell on the stack, so Eminence does not discount casting it.

Spark Double

Copies a creature you control without being legendary. A second Unesh adds another discount and entry engine; a second Ur-Sphinx gives another attack trigger; copying a utility Sphinx repeats its entry ability where applicable. It is not a Sphinx spell while being cast and receives no Eminence discount.

Mystic Remora

Early card access and a tax on opposing noncreature spells while you develop mana. Let it go when cumulative upkeep would prevent a necessary engine or protection play.

Esper Sentinel

Early noncreature-spell draw pressure. Counters from Denzilore can increase its tax later, but it does not become a Sphinx unless Mirror Entity changes its types.

Curator of Destinies

An uncounterable threat that divides five cards into a hidden and visible pile for an opponent to choose. Either outcome advances this deck because discarded access to the other pile becomes graveyard material.

Quantum Riddler

Warp costs only one blue with Eminence, drawing a card on entry and offering a second cast on a later turn after the warp exile. A warped Riddler leaves at the next end step, so it is temporary development rather than a lasting blocker. Its low-hand draw replacement helps rebuild when you have one or fewer cards in hand.

Sphinx of False Conclusions

A three-mana flash Sphinx with Eminence, attack-based looting, and a token replacement when the nontoken original dies. It is a resilient threat and useful Victimize sacrifice. Its token copy does not make another copy when that token dies.

Turn selection into a lethal board

Raffine, Scheming Seer

Its attack trigger counts ALL your attacking creatures and makes one targeted attacker connive that many times as one connive instruction. Draw and discard that many cards, then put counters on the targeted attacker for the nonland cards discarded. It seeds the graveyard while improving an attacker. Eminence cannot discount its three colored mana symbols.

Curator of Mysteries

A cheap flying body under Eminence, with a scry trigger for each other card you discard or cycle. Raffine discarding three cards generates three separate scry triggers. Curator can cycle for blue when you need lands or a reanimation target in the graveyard.

Denzilore Fatehold

Each separate scry or surveil event puts a +1/+1 counter on every creature you control. With Raffine and Curator of Mysteries, multiple discarded cards can produce multiple team-wide pumps before combat damage. Scry 3 or surveil 4 is still only ONE event and therefore one Denzilore trigger.

Sphinx of Foresight

Its opening-hand reveal improves your first upkeep, and its recurring upkeep scry works with Denzilore. On the battlefield it is a four-power flier for three mana with Eminence.

Prognostic Sphinx

Scry 3 on attack improves upcoming draws or your own commander mill. Put its attack trigger above the commander's trigger if you want the scry to resolve first. Discarding a card protects Prognostic through hexproof and can feed the graveyard, but taps it; use that before attackers are declared only if you are willing to lose its attack.

Dream Eater

Flash, surveil 4, and optional nonland bounce in one Sphinx. Surveil improves your graveyard and top card while triggering Denzilore once. The bounce is a separate reflexive trigger, so opponents can respond after the surveil.

The Water Crystal

Reduces blue spell costs and makes EACH opponent mill four extra cards whenever they would mill. This improves the selection from the commander's attack trigger without increasing the number of spells you may cast. Your own mill is unchanged. Its activated mill is a late-game option, not the deck's primary win condition.

When Raffine, Prognostic, Kindred Discovery, and The Ur-Sphinx all trigger from an attack, choose the order deliberately. Drawing the top card before milling can remove the card you meant to cast for free; resolving a scry first can place a useful card into your next mill. Targets for triggered abilities are chosen when those abilities are put on the stack.

Reuse what you discard and mill

Reanimate

Returns a creature from any graveyard for one black mana, at a life cost equal to its mana value. It can recover your expensive Sphinx or steal an opponent's creature that was milled. Reanimating The Ur-Sphinx costs nine life and only works if the commander is actually in the graveyard.

Animate Dead

Two-mana reanimation from any graveyard. The returned creature depends on the Aura remaining on the battlefield and gets -1/-0. Giving the creature protection from black with Akroma's Will causes the Aura to fall off, which makes you sacrifice the creature; indestructible does not prevent that sacrifice.

Victimize

Requires two creature cards already in your graveyard and a creature to sacrifice when it resolves. Exchange a changeling token or Sphinx of False Conclusions for two major threats. Both returns enter tapped, so they cannot attack or serve as untapped blockers immediately.

Bloodline Bidding

Choose Sphinx to rebuild your entire Sphinx graveyard, including Mirror Entity. Convoke lets surviving creatures help pay the eight-mana cost; this is not automatically cheap after a complete board wipe. Roaming Throne and Spark Double are not Sphinx cards in the graveyard and are not returned by choosing Sphinx.

Scholar of the Lost Trove

Its entry ability casts an instant, sorcery, or artifact from your graveyard for free. Bloodline Bidding can rebuild the tribe, while a spent removal spell can answer the obstacle stopping combat. Instants and sorceries cast this way are exiled afterward, so this is not a repeatable two-card reanimation loop.

Sphinx of Forgotten Lore

Flash lets you deploy it while preserving interaction. Attacking grants flashback to an instant or sorcery already in your graveyard. You must choose that target when its trigger goes on the stack, so you cannot select a card that the commander's pending trigger has not milled yet.

Uldaros Theorix

Its entry ability requires that you CAST it. Reanimating it does not provide the spell-copy payoff; casting it free through the commander does. It exiles up to one nonland card of each card type from your graveyard, then lets you cast copies with total mana value at most six. A copied Reanimate plus Arcane Signet fits; you cannot simply select several sorceries.

Conspiracy Unraveler

Turns expendable graveyard cards with total mana value ten into an alternative payment for a spell. It can help cast the commander from the command zone, but additional costs such as commander tax still apply. Exiling a precious reanimation target may be worse than paying mana normally.

Takenuma, Abandoned Mire

An untapped black land early, or late recovery of a creature to hand after milling three. Returning Uldaros to hand preserves the chance to cast it for its payoff.

Normally leave the commander in the command zone until its cost is manageable. If it dies while you have reanimation ready, you may leave it in the graveyard instead of moving it to the command zone. That exposes it to graveyard interaction, so make the choice based on the table and your available recovery.

End the game with pressure and combat

Magister Sphinx

Sets a target player's life total to ten, turning your established fliers into an immediate lethal threat. This is particularly effective against life-gain decks. Roaming Throne doubles its entry trigger, potentially setting two opponents to ten; it is not an automatic kill without follow-up.

Mirror Entity

Changeling makes it a Sphinx in every zone, so Eminence reduces it to one generic and one white. Its activation changes your team's base power and toughness to X/X and gives all creature types. Use large mana reserves to turn small bodies into attackers; a small X can shrink your existing large Sphinxes.

Mirror Entity can make your non-Sphinx support creatures count toward the commander's attack trigger if activated BEFORE attackers are declared. It does not grant flying by itself. Later boosts from counters and Chronicle of Victory still apply on top of its new base power and toughness. Do not activate for zero casually.

Chronicle of Victory

Choose Sphinx for a +2/+2 team bonus, first strike, trample, and a draw trigger whenever you cast a Sphinx spell. This is a finishing investment that helps fliers punch through token blockers, not an early ramp play. Its cast trigger does not draw from reanimated creatures.

Akroma's Will

Double strike turns a developed flying board into a fast finish, while its defensive mode supplies lifelink, indestructible, and protection from each color. With the commander actually under your control, choose both modes. Remember the Animate Dead interaction and that protection does not stop sacrifice or untargeted exile.

The commander's printed ten power with double strike is only twenty commander damage, not lethal from an untouched commander-damage total. One +1/+1 counter or Chronicle's anthem raises that above twenty-one. Ordinary life-total damage from several Sphinxes is usually the main route to victory.

Dream Trawler

Lifelink repairs the costs of your lands and black engines. Draws increase its power that turn, so its own attack draw and Kindred Discovery can make it a substantial attacker. Its discard-for-hexproof ability taps it; activating after it has attacked does not remove it from combat.

Sphinx of the Second Sun

The extra beginning phase untaps, gives an upkeep, and draws after your postcombat main phase. It is not an extra turn or an extra combat, and it does not create another main phase afterward. Use the untapped mana for protection and flash creatures as the turn moves toward its end.

Protect your advantage and remove blockers

Reconnaissance

Lets you attack for triggers, then remove a threatened attacker from combat and untap it before combat damage. It can also untap surviving attackers during the end-of-combat step after damage. The already-triggered commander ability remains on the stack, but a creature removed before damage does not deal combat damage.

Lightning Greaves

Haste can add an immediate commander attack or let a newly cast Sphinx contribute to the attack count. Shroud blocks your own targeted abilities too, including Raffine's connive target, Reconnaissance, and Clever Concealment. Move Greaves first when your own targeting matters.

Clever Concealment

Phases selected nonland permanents out through destruction or exile wipes, including your enchantment engines. Attached Auras and Equipment phase with their creatures rather than falling off. Convoke uses untapped creatures; your attackers usually cannot help pay after attacking.

Flawless Maneuver

Protects creatures from destruction and damage, and is free only when you control a commander on the battlefield. Eminence from the command zone does not satisfy that condition. It does not stop exile, bounce, sacrifice, or lethal toughness reduction.

Swords to Plowshares

Efficient exile for a must-answer creature. Anguished Unmaking covers nonland permanents more broadly at a three-life cost, which matters when you are already paying for cards and reanimation.

Mana Drain

Answers any counterable spell and supplies generic mana later for a major Sphinx or artifact. Swan Song, Dovin's Veto, and An Offer You Can't Refuse provide cheaper protection against noncreature spells; their coverage and drawbacks differ.

Cyclonic Rift

The third Game Changer clears opponents' nonland boards for a finishing attack or answers a single obstacle for two mana. Overload preserves your board but does not remove lands.

Toxic Deluge

The reset when opponents develop faster than you. Choose X carefully: it can kill your own Sphinxes and costs life, but can also clear smaller creatures while preserving large fliers. Indestructible does not stop the toughness reduction.

Enigma Thief

If one of your Sphinxes or Rogues dealt combat damage to a player this turn, prowl becomes available. Eminence reduces that alternative cost to two generic and one blue. Its entry bounces up to one opposing nonland permanent per opponent, helping you keep pressure while interacting.

Archive Arbiter

An on-theme answer to a noncreature, nonland permanent, or life recovery when no such target matters. Cost reductions make its printed six mana manageable, and reanimation repeats its entry effect.

Sphinx Summoner

Searches an artifact creature to hand: Magister Sphinx for a lethal setup, Archive Arbiter for removal, or Roaming Throne for doubled triggers. It cannot search every Sphinx merely because they share a creature type.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Controls opponents' graveyards without exiling your own, which is important when the commander is feeding graveyards. Hold it against a recursion response or sacrifice for a card when graveyard hate is irrelevant. You cannot activate it in the middle of the commander's resolving trigger.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep three lands with early blue and a route to the other colors, plus a rock or reducer and a useful early Sphinx or draw engine. A two-land hand needs reliable cheap card access and acceleration. Mulligan hands full of six- to eight-mana Sphinxes with no development, even with Eminence.

Your first goal is a working mana and card engine, usually a rock into a discounted Sphinx, Unesh, or another repeatable source of cards. You do not need to cast the commander early for the deck to function; its command-zone reduction is already doing work.

The 37 lands include nine fetchlands, original duals, shocklands, and typed surveil lands. Fetch for the next two spells rather than only the current spell. Most Sphinxes need blue; Dream Trawler also demands two white. Four basic lands provide a modest fallback, while the typed duals handle most fixing.

Cavern of Souls

Choose Sphinx to make actual Sphinx creature spells uncounterable, including Mirror Entity. It cannot provide colored mana for Spark Double or Roaming Throne merely because they will become Sphinxes later.

Three Tree City

Choose Sphinx. Its colored-mana ability needs two mana and its tap, so calculate the net gain from the number of Sphinxes you control. It is not an early three-color land. Otawara provides a channel answer when its land slot is no longer needed.

Reanimate and Animate Dead can use opponents' creatures too, but maintain enough life and interaction to survive the turn cycle. Hold a recovery spell when a wipe is likely instead of committing every threat.

On the finishing turn, resolve relevant pumps or protection before relying on them, order attack triggers deliberately, and remember that opponents may respond after the commander trigger has finished casting its free spells. Free does not mean uncounterable, and extra milling is also extra graveyard fuel for your opponents.
