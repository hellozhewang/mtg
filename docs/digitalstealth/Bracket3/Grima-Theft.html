Gríma cannot be blocked. That one line is the deck: a guaranteed connection every turn, and every connection digs through an opponent's library until it finds an instant or sorcery and casts it for free. The build is then about multiplying that trigger — copy Gríma, give him extra combats, and double his ability — until you are casting three or four of the table's best spells a turn without paying for any of them.

The commander
Gríma, Saruman's Footman

unblockable, and each hit exiles down to an instant or sorcery from that player's deck and casts it FREE. Four mana, and it never misses.

Lightning Greaves

haste so he connects the turn he lands, and shroud so the removal aimed at him fizzles.

Swiftfoot Boots

hexproof and haste, for when you need to target him yourself.

Slip Out the Back

one mana in response to removal. He phases out and comes back bigger.

Octopus Form

hexproof, +1/+1, and it untaps him — relevant after he taps for an ability or gets tapped down.

Conqueror's Flail

opponents cannot cast spells during your turn at all, so nothing interrupts the attack.

Multiplying the trigger
Roaming Throne

name Advisor. Gríma's damage trigger fires TWICE, so every connection is two free spells.

Sakashima of a Thousand Faces

copy Gríma. The legend rule does not apply to your permanents, so you attack with two unblockable Grímas.

Spark Double

another nonlegendary Gríma, with a counter on it.

Irenicus's Vile Duplication

a flying copy of anything, usually Gríma.

Quantum Misalignment

a copy now and a second one next upkeep from rebound.

Auton Soldier

a copy of Gríma with MYRIAD. It attacks one opponent and makes tapped attacking copies at the other two — all unblockable, all triggering. This is the single biggest turn the deck has.

Impostor Syndrome

every nontoken creature that connects makes a permanent copy of itself. Gríma connects, you get a second Gríma, and it compounds every combat.

Irma, Part-Time Mutant

becomes a copy of Gríma at the beginning of combat, every turn, growing each time.

Taskmaster, Mercenary Mimic

copies the best creature on the board OR in any graveyard, each of your turns.

Genji Glove

double strike plus a whole extra combat phase. Two Gríma triggers per combat, twice.

Getting paid for the free spells
Tasha, the Witch Queen

a 3/3 Demon every time you cast a spell you do not own — which is every Gríma trigger. Her minus recasts their best instant or sorcery out of exile for free too.

Lord of the Nazgûl

a 3/3 menace Wraith for every instant or sorcery you cast, stolen ones included, and at nine Wraiths they all become 9/9s.

Sword of Wealth and Power

a Treasure on damage, and it COPIES the next instant or sorcery you cast that turn — which is the spell Gríma just handed you.

Sword of Feast and Famine

they discard and all your lands untap, so the free spell you were given can be followed by one you pay for.

Buster Sword

draws on damage and lets you cast a spell from hand free, priced by the damage dealt.

Gonti, Night Minister

Treasures whenever anyone casts a spell they do not own, and it exiles a card off an opponent whenever ANY creature damages them.

Grim Hireling

two Treasures on every combat damage step, and the Treasures convert into removal.

Eagle Vision

freerunning costs {1}{U} whenever your commander connected this turn — which is always. Three cards for two mana.

Taking things outright
Dauthi Voidwalker

unblockable, exiles everything headed for their graveyards, and cashes in for a free cast of any of it.

Opposition Agent

flash him onto a fetchland or a tutor. You control the search and keep what they find.

Gilded Drake

two mana to swap a 3/3 flier for their best creature.

Breach the Multiverse

mills everyone ten and takes the best creature or planeswalker from EACH graveyard, onto your battlefield.

Dream Harvest

five mana value worth of free spells off every opponent at once.

Reanimate

one mana for the best creature in any graveyard, including the ones Breach the Multiverse just filled.

Interaction
Mana Drain

counter their answer and spend the mana on a copy effect.

Counterspell

the clean two-mana version.

Arcane Denial

cheap, and the cards they draw arrive too late to matter.

Memory Lapse

a Time Walk against a tapped-out player.

Swan Song

one mana for a wrath or a removal spell.

Strix Serenade

the creature and planeswalker half of the same effect.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for anything noncreature.

Misdirection

free, and it redirects the removal aimed at Gríma.

Tale's End

counters a triggered or activated ability, which is what most combo decks actually win with.

Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Pongify

one mana, kills anything.

Reality Shift

exile plus a face-down 2/2 they cannot use well.

Feed the Swarm

the only clean enchantment answer in Dimir.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, it clears the board for a lethal alpha strike.

March of Swirling Mist

phases your team out of a wrath, or phases their blockers away.

Propaganda

nobody attacks the player with a 1/4 commander and a wall of blue mana up.

Card flow
Rhystic Study

they will not pay while they are already paying for everything else.

Mystic Remora

the strongest card in the deck on turns one through four.

Faerie Mastermind

flash, flies, and draws whenever anyone doubles up on draws.

Baleful Strix

a card, a deathtouch blocker, and it trades with anything.

Enduring Curiosity

a card for every creature that connects — and your creatures are unblockable copies of Gríma.

Sheoldred, the Apocalypse

two life per card you draw and two lost per card they draw.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a rock and a haste or protection piece. Gríma on turn three with Lightning Greaves is three free spells before anyone has stabilised.

Attack the player whose deck you most want to cast from. The trigger digs through THEIR library, so hitting the storm deck gets you a ritual and a tutor, while hitting the creature deck may whiff for several cards.

Auton Soldier

hold it until Gríma is already on the battlefield and unblocked. Copying him with myriad is usually three free spells and three Tasha Demons in a single combat.

Roaming Throne

name Advisor, not Rogue. Gríma is the trigger that matters; the Rogues in the deck are incidental.
