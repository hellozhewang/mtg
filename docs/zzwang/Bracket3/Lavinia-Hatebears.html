This deck was built to beat the decks that cheat. Lavinia stops opponents casting noncreature spells above their land count and hard-counters anything cast for free — but she does not stop a permanent being PUT onto the battlefield, and that is the gap the rest of the deck closes. Containment Priest exiles it. Three separate creatures stop its enters trigger. And the counterspells hold the line while the hatebears lock the door.

The commander, and her blind spot
Lavinia, Azorius Renegade

two mana. Opponents cannot cast noncreature spells with mana value greater than their land count — so a six-drop enchantment needs six lands — and anything cast for NO mana is countered outright. Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, evoked Elementals and ritual-free combo turns all stop.

She does nothing about a permanent that is put onto the battlefield rather than cast. Against The Prismatic Bridge, Natural Order or a reanimation spell, she buys time; the cards below are what actually answer them.

Containment Priest

any nontoken creature entering WITHOUT being cast is exiled instead. This is the hard answer — it turns a cheat effect into a blank.

Hushbringer

creatures entering or dying trigger nothing at all.

Hushwing Gryff

the same for entering, at flash speed, so you can hold it up.

Doorkeeper Thrull

flash, and it covers artifacts as well as creatures.

Be aware these three also blank Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines and your own enters triggers. This deck has very few, which is why it can afford them — but sequence deliberately.

The rest of the lock
Drannith Magistrate

opponents cannot cast from graveyards, exile or the command zone. It turns off every other commander at the table.

Grafdigger's Cage

nothing enters from a graveyard or a library, and nothing is cast from either.

Clarion Conqueror

no activated abilities of artifacts, creatures or planeswalkers, for anyone. It is a 3/3 flier that shuts off mana rocks, dorks and combo pieces at once.

Linvala, Keeper of Silence

the same, restricted to their creatures, and it leaves your own artifacts working.

Leonin Arbiter

nobody searches without paying two. Fetchlands, tutors and ramp all get taxed.

Aven Mindcensor

flash him on top of that and the search finds four cards.

Archivist of Oghma

a card and a life every time anyone searches anyway.

Meddling Mage

name the card the table is built around. Against a known combo it is a one-card answer.

Deafening Silence

one noncreature spell per turn each, for one mana.

Grand Abolisher

they cannot act during your turn at all.

Myrel, Shield of Argive

the same lockout on a body that builds an army every attack.

Aura of Silence

taxes their artifacts and enchantments two, then destroys one.

Karn, the Great Creator

every activated ability on their artifacts stops.

Esper Sentinel

taxes their first noncreature spell each turn.

Counterspells
Counterspell

the clean two-mana answer.

Mana Drain

counter, and the mana deploys two hatebears next turn.

Dovin's Veto

uncounterable, for the spell you cannot afford to lose a fight over.

Force of Negation

free on their turn, which matters when you have tapped out to develop.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for anything noncreature.

Consign to Memory

counters a triggered or activated ABILITY — which is what most combo decks actually win with, and what The Prismatic Bridge's upkeep trigger is.

Removal
Get Lost

two mana for a creature, enchantment or planeswalker, at instant speed.

March of Otherworldly Light

scalable exile, cheap when you pitch white cards.

Grasp of Fate

exiles a nonland permanent from EVERY opponent on one card.

Heliod's Intervention

X artifacts and enchantments at instant speed. It is the answer to a board full of enchantments, including a resolved Bridge.

Austere Command

pick the two halves that miss your own board.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, it resets the table and clears the way.

Enlightened Tutor

finds Grafdigger's Cage, Grasp of Fate or Greater Auramancy.

Idyllic Tutor

the enchantment half.

Greater Auramancy

shroud for your other enchantments, so the lock cannot be picked apart.

Card flow
Mystic Remora

the strongest card in the deck on turns one through four.

Consult the Star Charts

scales with your land count, at instant speed.

Fact or Fiction

whichever pile they give you.

Memory Deluge

scales with mana, and flashback makes it twice.

How you actually win
Approach of the Second Sun

the natural finisher for a deck that stops the game. Cast it, gain seven, then cast it again seven cards later. Both casts must be FROM YOUR HAND.

Luminarch Ascension

behind this much interaction you rarely lose life, so it turns on and makes a 4/4 flier every turn.

Myrel, Shield of Argive

her attack trigger doubles your Soldier count every combat.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep two lands, a rock or a two-drop hatebear, and one piece of interaction. The deck wins by never letting anything resolve, which means it needs to be doing something on turns two, three and four.

Lavinia is the tempo card, not the lock. Cast her on turn two and let her tax; the hard answers to cheating are Containment Priest and the ETB blankers, so hold those for the moment they matter.

Consign to Memory is the card people forget. It counters The Prismatic Bridge's upkeep trigger, a Craterhoof trigger, a storm payoff, or a commander's enters ability — one mana, at instant speed.

Against a known cheat deck, deploy Containment Priest BEFORE their upkeep, not in response to the trigger. The Priest replaces the creature entering; it does not undo a trigger that has already resolved.
