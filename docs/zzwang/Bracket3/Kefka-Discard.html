Do not spend eight mana transforming Kefka just because you can. His front face turns blink spells, attacks and discarded cards into resources; the compact wins work without him. His back face draws obligatorily and can make a large discard-and-damage turn kill YOU by drawing your library out.

Strip resources while building your own hand
Kefka, Court Mage // Kefka, Ruler of Ruin

each entry or attack makes every player, including you, discard one card, then draws for the DISTINCT card types across those discarded cards. Two discarded creatures count once; an artifact creature can supply two types. Legendary and Human are not card types. Choose your discard to add a missing type, preserve both combo pieces, and use reanimation to recover valuable creatures.

+
Essence Flux + Kefka, Court Mage // Kefka, Ruler of Ruin

for {U}, blink Kefka to dodge targeted removal and trigger another discard-and-draw. A transformed Kefka returns front face up. Blinking an attacker removes it from combat. This is value, not an infinite loop.

+
Displacer Kitten + Kefka, Court Mage // Kefka, Ruler of Ruin

each noncreature spell can blink Kefka for another trigger. The blink resolves before the spell that triggered Kitten. Decline the optional target when you need Kefka to remain attacking or transformed. Creature spells do not trigger Kitten.

Waste Not

opponents' discarded lands make black mana, creatures make Zombies, and other cards draw. Kefka and the wheels supply the discards; spend the mana in the current step or phase. Your OWN discards do not trigger this enchantment. Zombies can be an initial Duplication target or Intent sacrifice.

Tinybones, Trinket Thief

Kefka's entry or attack turns on the end-step draw. Flux on another player's turn can turn on that turn's end step too. Each trigger costs one life. Its six-mana activation is a late-game outlet against empty hands, not the main plan.

Tinybones, Pocket Nuisance

the entry trigger pressures all opposing hands. Each player discarding one or more cards gives a separate damage trigger, so a normal four-player Kefka trigger can deal four to each opponent. A wheel discarding seven from one player still creates only ONE Tinybones trigger for that player. With transformed Kefka, each opponent's life loss creates mandatory draws: count them before committing.

Monument to Endurance

your own Kefka, wheel or Shredder discards can provide draw, Treasure and table-wide life loss, each mode only once PER TURN. Discarding on other players' turns refreshes that choice. A wheel is not seven drains. With transformed Kefka, the drain can force nine draws in a four-player game; make sure you can survive them.

Ledger Shredder

each player's second spell can connive this creature, improving hand quality and triggering Monument. It counts creature spells too. Connive is mandatory, so keep its triggers in mind around an emptied library.

Faerie Mastermind

rewards opponents drawing their second card each turn and works independently of Kefka. Its activated draw helps everyone; do not activate it with your library empty. Opponents can also force a second-card trigger at an awkward moment.

Rhystic Study

the third Game Changer slot buys sustained cards while you wait for the permitted combo turn. Its draw is optional: decline it when the library is empty or you need to preserve cards.

Windfall

converts a depleted hand into the largest discarded hand size while powering Waste Not and Monument. It also refills opponents. Count YOUR library and how much help you give the next player; it is not a one-sided wheel.

Wheel of Misfortune

seven fresh cards require choosing above the lowest number, and the highest chooser takes that much damage. You cannot guarantee who wheels. Life is also needed for lands and removal, so do not bid recklessly.

Two compact ways to finish
+
Thassa's Oracle + Demonic Consultation

with at least {U}{U}{B}, cast Oracle and let it resolve. With its entry trigger on the stack, cast Consultation naming a card absent from the deck, exiling your library. Resolve Oracle's trigger to win. Do not resolve Consultation and then pass the turn hoping to cast Oracle later. A zero-card library satisfies the check even if Oracle is removed, but opponents can counter the trigger or force a draw before it resolves.

+
Thassa's Oracle + Tainted Pact

spend {U}{U} plus {1}{B}, using the same Oracle-trigger timing. Decline every card Pact offers to exile the entire library. All 100 card names are unique, including the snow and ordinary basics. Adding a second copy of a basic breaks this guarantee. Neither line needs the commander or the graveyard.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication

before combat, target an artifact or creature you already control with Duplication for {1}{R}. Hold priority and cast Dualcaster for {1}{R}{R}. Its entry copies Duplication; target Dualcaster with that copy. Each new hasty Mage token copies the still-pending ORIGINAL Duplication and targets a Mage, producing any finite number of hasty 2/2s. End the chain by pointing the last copy at a suitable non-Mage permanent, then resolve the original and attack. This needs five mana, including three red, plus the initial target. Fogs, attack taxes and effects preventing attacks can stop the combat kill.

Dualcaster's copying ability is mandatory when it has a legal target, but choosing targets lets you stop the token chain. An opponent removing the Mage you need to copy while its entry trigger is pending, or countering the original Duplication before the first copy exists, can disrupt the setup. Merely removing the original spell's initial target does not prevent the Mage's entry trigger from copying that still-pending spell with a new target. Do not promise lethal until the stack and combat actually resolve.

Find the missing piece
→
Imperial Recruiter → Thassa's Oracle

finds the creature half of the noncombat win. It also finds Dualcaster, Spellseeker and Kitten, so retain the body for another blink or an Intent sacrifice.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Dualcaster Mage

choose this when Duplication and the red mana are ready, especially if graveyard hate is active. Do not deploy the Mage without a spell to copy just because Recruiter found it.

→
Spellseeker → Demonic Consultation

pairs with an Oracle already in hand. Seeker can also get Pact or Duplication; it cannot find Oracle itself because it searches only instants and sorceries.

→
Spellseeker → Essence Flux

recover a card engine or protect Kefka when the table is not yet ready for the win. Flux can blink Seeker on a later occasion for another search.

Demonic Tutor

unrestricted two-mana access to whichever half, answer or land the hand lacks. Search for interaction instead of a win when a hate permanent makes the win nonfunctional.

Wishclaw Talisman

pay {1} and tap it during your turn to find any card. Normally an opponent receives it afterward, so use it on the winning turn rather than casually handing out a free tutor. It does not require haste because it is normally not a creature.

+
Displacer Kitten + Wishclaw Talisman

with your Talisman activation on the stack, cast a noncreature instant, such as Flux targeting a creature, and use Kitten to blink the Talisman BEFORE its activation resolves. The returning Talisman is a new object with three counters; the old activation still searches, but cannot hand over that new object. Once the opponent already controls Talisman, Kitten cannot target it, since it requires a permanent you control.

Diabolic Intent

sacrifice a Zombie, spent Recruiter or other expendable creature to tutor. The sacrifice is a cost and is lost if Intent is countered. Do not sacrifice your only prospective initial Duplication target without planning another.

→
Beseech the Mirror → Thassa's Oracle

bargain an expendable artifact, enchantment or token to tutor and cast Oracle without its mana cost. Keep {B} for Consultation, or {1}{B} for Pact, after paying {1}{B}{B}{B}. A sacrificed Defense Grid stops protecting you immediately. Without bargain the searched card simply goes to hand.

→
Shred Memory → Thassa's Oracle

its {1}{B}{B} transmute finds a mana-value-two card: Oracle, Pact, Duplication, Intent or Wishclaw. Transmute is a sorcery-speed activated ability and does not trigger Kitten. Its ordinary spell mode is useful graveyard interaction instead.

→
Dizzy Spell → Demonic Consultation

transmute for {1}{U}{U} to find a mana-value-one card such as Consultation or Flux. It cannot find two-mana Oracle. This is reliable setup for turn six, not cheap same-turn tutoring.

Lim-Dûl's Vault

trades life for selecting a five-card packet and stacking it on top. It does not put a card in hand; follow with a draw spell or Kefka trigger. Preserve enough life for the lands and removal suite.

Spend mana efficiently and recover
Sol Ring

accelerates Kefka's generic mana and the eight-mana transformation, but does not pay his colored costs, Oracle's {U}{U} or Dualcaster's {R}{R}.

Arcane Signet

one of seven permanent mana rocks, alongside Sol Ring, Fellwar Stone, Dimir Signet and the three Talismans. Prioritize the colors the intended win needs. Dimir Signet requires another mana to activate.

Lotus Petal

temporary fixing for a protected win or early engine. Its artifact type is useful to discard for Kefka, or to sacrifice to bargain, but those uses consume the same card. Dark Ritual provides a separate burst of black, not blue or red.

Geist of Saint Thalia

reduces the generic portion of your noncreature spells, including rocks, wheels and tutors. It cannot reduce colored requirements, does not discount the creature commander, and does not reduce Kefka's activated ability.

Cavern of Souls

normally name Wizard to support Kefka, Oracle, Spellseeker, Snapcaster and Dualcaster. Its colored mana cannot cast Consultation or other noncreature spells. It protects the creature SPELL from counters, not its subsequent triggered ability.

Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

flexible spell-or-land slot. The other two MDFCs are Fell the Profane and Malakir Rebirth. There are 33 ordinary lands plus these three options; early land drops usually matter more than keeping every spell face. All basics have distinct names for Pact.

Reanimate

recover your discarded value creatures or steal an opponent's discarded creature for one mana and life equal to its mana value. Returning Kefka gets the front face and a fresh trigger; leaving him in the graveyard risks exile, so decide whether to use the command-zone option carefully.

Animate Dead

a second recovery spell, but removal of its Aura normally makes you sacrifice the creature. Kitten blinking the Aura can reanimate another graveyard creature while the old creature's sacrifice trigger remains pending.

Snapcaster Mage

recasts a used Consultation, tutor, protection spell or Flux for its normal mana cost plus Snapcaster's {1}{U}. Discarding a spell to Kefka can preserve it for this later use. Flashback exiles the spell afterward.

Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire

protects a creature from dying that turn, rather than exile or bounce, at the cost of two life. A returning Kefka triggers again, but returns tapped and front face up. Do not assume it protects an Oracle trigger from being countered.

Brainstorm

put redundant lands or a combo half safely on top before a Kefka discard, then fetch away unwanted cards. Ponder and Preordain smooth the opening. Gitaxian Probe checks one opponent's hand before choosing protection; it cannot certify the whole table is safe.

Clear the path without disabling yourself
Defense Grid

taxes opponents' spells on your turn by three, including alternate-cost spells. It also taxes YOUR interaction on other players' turns. Opponents can still pay; Grid does not stop activated or triggered abilities.

Pact of Negation

protects a win with no immediate mana, but requires {3}{U}{U} next upkeep if the game continues. Daze trades an Island, including an appropriate dual, for a conditional counter; that land setback matters before Kefka.

Flusterstorm

protects the instant/sorcery exchanges around your wins, but cannot directly counter a creature, enchantment or triggered ability. Dispel, Swan Song, Offer, Misstep and the red blasts cover different pieces; keep the one your opponent's colors and likely response require.

Chain of Vapor

clear a nonland hate permanent or save your own engine. Its controller may sacrifice a land to copy it, potentially bouncing one of your pieces. Into the Flood Maw can answer any opposing nonland permanent if you promise the Fish gift, without that copy risk.

Abrade

kills a small disruptive creature or an artifact. Feed the Swarm covers enchantments at sorcery speed and costs life. Use these before committing an Oracle library-exile spell into a known hate effect.

Fire Covenant

distribute paid life as damage among threatening creatures at instant speed while preserving your own board. It does not kill an indestructible creature merely by assigning lethal damage. Toxic Deluge is the broader answer and can kill your own engines too.

Dress Down

temporarily removes creature abilities and draws a card. It can disable an opposing creature-based hate effect, but ALSO disables Kefka, Kitten, Dualcaster, Recruiter and Oracle. Remove it or wait for its sacrifice before attempting your own entry-trigger win. Its mandatory entry draw makes it particularly dangerous after Consultation.

Soul-Guide Lantern

attack opposing graveyards without exiling your own reanimation or Snapcaster resources. Kitten can blink it to pick off more cards, provided it has not been sacrificed as an activation cost.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three usable mana sources, access to blue and black, and either early ramp or a repeatable draw engine. A hand containing only a combo and expensive tutors is a mulligan. Plan a red source before Kefka, and multiple red sources if targeting the Dualcaster line. Cavern is not a blue source for your cantrips or counters.

Develop rocks and cards, then use front-face Kefka to reduce hands and find protection. Do not empty your own hand without a way to rebuild, and do not feed opponents a wheel while they are better placed to win. Reserve a counter or bounce spell for the critical turn.

This uses the repository's Bracket 3 HOUSE RULES: three Game Changers and no mass land denial. Compact and two-card wins wait until TURN SIX or later. Before then, build resources and interact; the deck's speed is a construction target, not a claim of measured win rates. Even after turn six, confirm the required colors, removal for hate, and protection before starting a line.

Transform only when the life-loss draws produce a controlled advantage and you can afford eight mana without abandoning defense. Count every draw from multiple opponents separately; a discard-damage engine can generate more mandatory draws than your remaining library. Keeping Kefka front face up is usually the stronger default in this build.
