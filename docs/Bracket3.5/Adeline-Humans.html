Adeline does not need to attack. Her trigger is "whenever you attack" — send any one creature and she makes a 1/1 Human attacking EACH opponent, three tokens a turn at a four-player table. Her power equals your creature count and she has vigilance, so she can swing and still stand guard. Everything else here either makes that trigger bigger, pumps the tokens, or turns them into cards.

There's also a two-card kill: Heliod and Walking Ballista — see the combo.

Her tokens are Humans. That is what the Human cards here are for — Greymond, Thalia's Lieutenant, Coppercoat Vanguard and Beregond all count them.

The commander

Adeline, Resplendent Cathar

three mana, vigilance, power equal to your creature count, and three attacking tokens every time you attack. Attack every turn, even with one token, to keep the tokens coming.

Lightning Greaves

shroud the turn Adeline lands, and haste so she attacks straight away. Never equip it to Walking Ballista — shroud stops Heliod giving it lifelink or counters.

Mother of Runes

taps to give Adeline protection from a colour — the colour of the removal pointed at her.

Giver of Runes

the same, and it can also give protection from colourless.

The combo — why this is Bracket 3.5

Heliod, Sun-Crowned

whenever you gain life, a +1/+1 counter on a creature or enchantment you control; {1}{W} gives another creature lifelink. It also puts a counter on a token every time Ocelot Pride or Belladonna Took gains you life.

Walking Ballista

give it lifelink with Heliod, then remove a counter: 1 damage, 1 life, and Heliod puts the counter straight back. Unlimited damage — the whole table dies. Cast it for X of 2 or more: with only one counter it dies the moment you remove it, before Heliod puts one back — unless Flowering of the White Tree or Wedding Festivity is giving it +1/+1.

Idyllic Tutor

finds Heliod, which is an enchantment.

Recruiter of the Guard

finds any creature with toughness 2 or less — Walking Ballista has 0, so it's the second way to find the combo, along with Mother of Runes and Giver of Runes.

Search for Glory

finds any legendary card — Heliod.

Ranger of Eos

finds two creatures with mana value 1 or less — Walking Ballista and Mother of Runes to protect the combo.

More tokens per attack

Roaming Throne

name Human. Adeline is a Human, so her trigger happens twice: six tokens every attack. It doubles Hero of Bladehold, Myrel, Thalia's Lieutenant, Beregond, Goldnight Commander and Mentor of the Meek the same way.

Anointed Procession

every token effect makes twice as many.

Mondrak, Glory Dominus

the same, on a 4/4.

Elspeth, Storm Slayer

the same again, plus two Soldiers every turn (she doubles her own) or a +1/+1 counter and flying for your whole team. Adeline with Roaming Throne and one doubler is twelve tokens an attack.

Hero of Bladehold

two more attacking Soldiers, and +1/+0 to every other attacker.

Myrel, Shield of Argive

when it attacks, a 1/1 Soldier for every Soldier you control — Hero's tokens count. Your opponents can't cast spells or use artifact, creature or enchantment abilities during your turn.

Voice of Victory

two attacking Warriors each time it attacks (sacrificed at the next end step), and your opponents can't cast spells during your turn.

Castle Ardenvale

spare mana into another Human every turn.

Ocelot Pride

a Cat at your end step if you gained life. Once you control ten permanents, it copies every token that entered this turn and is still yours.

Elspeth, Sun's Champion

three Soldiers a turn. Careful with −3: it destroys every creature with power 4 or more, which includes Adeline and your own pumped tokens.

Pumping the army

Cathars' Crusade

every creature that enters puts a +1/+1 counter on EACH creature you control. Six tokens entering is +6/+6 on everything, before combat damage.

Intangible Virtue

tokens get +1/+1 and vigilance.

Flowering of the White Tree

nonlegendary creatures +1/+1; legendary ones +2/+1 and ward {1}.

Greymond, Avacyn's Stalwart

pick two of first strike, vigilance and lifelink for every Human; with four Humans out, Humans also get +2/+2.

Coppercoat Vanguard

other Humans +1/+0 and ward {1}.

Thalia's Lieutenant

a +1/+1 counter on every other Human when it enters, and it grows with every Human after.

Beregond of the Guard

whenever a Human enters, your creatures get +1/+1 and vigilance until end of turn — and Adeline's tokens are Humans entering mid-combat.

Goldnight Commander

whenever a creature enters, your creatures get +1/+1 until end of turn.

Wedding Announcement // Wedding Festivity

a card or a Human each end step; after three turns it flips into +1/+1 for your team.

Abandoned Air Temple

{3}{W} and a tap: a +1/+1 counter on each creature.

Finishers

Mirror Entity

{X}: your creatures become X/X. With a wide board, five mana makes every token a 5/5.

Akroma's Will

with Adeline out, both modes: flying, vigilance and double strike, plus lifelink, indestructible and protection from every colour. Protection from white also stops Heliod targeting your creatures, so finish the Ballista combo before casting it.

Emeria's Call // Emeria, Shattered Skyclave

a land early; late, two 4/4 flying Angels and indestructibility for your non-Angel creatures until your next turn.

Rogue's Passage

{4}: Adeline can't be blocked.

Cards — white's weakness, handled

The One Ring

cast it for protection from everything until your next turn, then draw more cards every turn.

Caretaker's Talent

a card the first time tokens enter each turn. Level 2 ({W}) copies a token once; level 3 ({3}{W}) gives all your creature tokens +2/+2.

Enduring Innocence

a card once a turn when small creatures enter. When it dies it comes back as an enchantment and keeps drawing.

Tocasia's Welcome

a card once a turn when a creature with mana value 3 or less enters — tokens count.

Welcoming Vampire

a card once a turn when small creatures enter.

Mentor of the Meek

pay {1} to draw for each small creature that enters, as many as you can pay for.

Idol of Oblivion

taps to draw on any turn you made a token.

Minas Tirith

{1}{W}: a card on any turn you declared two or more attackers.

Belladonna Took

each turn, the first token gains you a life, the second draws a card, the third puts a +1/+1 counter on every creature you control.

Trouble in Pairs

draws when an opponent attacks you with two or more creatures, draws their second card, or casts their second spell in a turn.

Esper Sentinel

taxes each opponent's first noncreature spell each turn or draws you a card.

Skullclamp

equip a 1/1 token and it dies: two cards for one mana.

War Room

a card for one life and three mana.

Land Tax

three basic lands to hand each of your upkeeps an opponent has more lands than you — thinning the deck and feeding the draws.

Mana

Sol Ring

the fastest start.

Arcane Signet

a second mana rock.

Mind Stone

ramp early, a card later.

Pearl Medallion

every white spell costs one less, nearly the whole deck — not Walking Ballista or activated abilities.

Knight of the White Orchid

a first-striker that fetches a Plains when you're behind on lands.

Smothering Tithe

every card an opponent draws is two mana from them or a Treasure for you.

Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx

mono-white devotion adds up fast.

Three Tree City

name Human: {2} and a tap makes a white mana for every Human you control.

Springleaf Drum

tap it and an untapped creature — a token made this turn works — for one mana of any colour.

Protection

Teferi's Protection

the whole board phases out until your next turn.

Flawless Maneuver

free with Adeline out; your creatures become indestructible.

Clever Concealment

phases out any of your nonland permanents; convoke means your tokens pay for it.

Galadriel's Dismissal

phase out one creature for {W} — or kicked, every creature one player controls. Point it at yourself to dodge a sweeper, or at an opponent to remove their blockers for a turn.

Flare of Fortitude

free by sacrificing a nontoken white creature: your life total can't change and your permanents gain hexproof and indestructible. Your life can't change that turn, which also switches off the Heliod combo — don't use it on the turn you go off.

Boromir, Warden of the Tower

counters every spell an opponent casts without paying mana, and sacrifices itself to make your creatures indestructible.

Grand Abolisher

during your turn, opponents can't cast spells or use artifact, creature or enchantment abilities.

Ranger-Captain of Eos

finds a creature with mana value 1 or less — Walking Ballista counts, at 0 — and sacrificing it means opponents can't cast noncreature spells this turn, which protects the combo.

Silence

one mana and nobody else casts spells this turn. Cast it before you start the combo.

Aven Interrupter

flash: exile the spell aimed at your combo — even one that can't be countered. Its owner can recast it later, by which point the game is over.

Answers

Swords to Plowshares

one mana, any creature.

Path to Exile

the second one.

Winds of Abandon

one creature for two mana, or overloaded for six: every creature your opponents control is exiled.

Council's Judgment

every player votes, and the most-voted nonland permanents are exiled — it doesn't target, so hexproof doesn't help them.

Generous Gift

any permanent; they get a 3/3.

Loran of the Third Path

destroys an artifact or enchantment, then trades cards with an opponent.

Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow

destroys an artifact or enchantment, or plays as a land.

Eiganjo, Seat of the Empire

a land that deals 4 to an attacking or blocking creature.

Charismatic Conqueror

whenever an opponent's creature or artifact enters untapped, they tap it or you get a lifelink Vampire.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep three lands and something to cast on turn two. Adeline comes down on turn three and attacks from turn four.

Attack every turn, even with a single token — the trigger is what matters, not the damage.

The deck's enemy is a board wipe. Past eight or so creatures, keep Teferi's Protection, Flawless Maneuver, Clever Concealment or Flare of Fortitude ready before adding more.

Draw is where mono-white usually falls behind. Get one of Caretaker's Talent, Enduring Innocence, Tocasia's Welcome or The One Ring out early and you won't.
