Kaalia puts an Angel, Demon or Dragon straight onto the battlefield attacking, skipping the seven or eight mana it would have cost. The whole deck is built to make that attack happen on turn four and to survive the removal spell that is coming for her. One thing decides how much value you get, and it is easy to get wrong: creatures put onto the battlefield attacking were never DECLARED as attackers, so their "whenever this attacks" triggers do not fire. Enter-the-battlefield and combat-damage triggers do.

The commander
Kaalia of the Vast

attacking an opponent puts an Angel, Demon or Dragon from your hand onto the battlefield tapped and attacking that player. Four mana, and the fatty is free.

Isshin, Two Heavens as One

Kaalia's attack trigger DOUBLES. Two fatties per attack, every combat. This is the best possible thing to have on the battlefield alongside her.

Aurelia, the Warleader

untaps everything and gives an extra combat, so Kaalia attacks twice. With Isshin, two extra combats and four cheated creatures in a turn. Cast her from your hand — her attack trigger does not work if Kaalia puts her in.

Kaalia, Zenith Seeker

not the commander, but she digs six deep for an Angel, a Demon AND a Dragon on entry, which refills the hand Kaalia of the Vast empties.

Dragon Tempest

haste for every flier that enters, which turns a hard-cast Dragon into an immediate attacker, plus damage per Dragon whenever a Dragon arrives.

Keeping Kaalia alive

The deck does nothing if she never connects, and every opponent knows it. Half these cards exist purely to get one attack through.

Lightning Greaves

haste so she attacks the turn she lands, and shroud so the removal spell has no target. The best card in the deck alongside her.

Swiftfoot Boots

hexproof and haste when you need to target her yourself.

Mother of Runes

protection from any colour, every turn, for one mana. She beats almost every removal spell in the format.

Flawless Maneuver

free with a commander out, and it saves the whole board from a wrath.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it points their removal at something of theirs instead.

Grand Abolisher

they cannot interact at all during your turn. Deploy it before you attack.

Silence

one mana for the same effect on the turn that matters.

Avacyn, Angel of Hope

every permanent you control is indestructible, Kaalia included.

Serra's Emissary

name instant or creature and you AND your board are untouchable by that card type.

Sephara, Sky's Blade

nearly free with a board of fliers, and it makes every other flier indestructible.

What to cheat in, and why

These all trigger on ENTERING or on dealing COMBAT DAMAGE, so they work perfectly when Kaalia drops them in attacking.

Angel of Despair

destroys any permanent on arrival.

Angel of Serenity

exiles three creatures — from the battlefield or from graveyards — on arrival.

Rune-Scarred Demon

a Demonic Tutor stapled to a 6/6 flier.

Lord of the Void

seven cards off an opponent's library and a free creature from among them, every hit.

Balefire Dragon

one opponent's entire board dies when it connects.

Hellkite Tyrant

takes every artifact that player controls, and wins the game outright at twenty artifacts.

Ancient Copper Dragon

an average of ten Treasures per connection, which hard-casts the rest of your hand.

Ancient Gold Dragon

an average of ten 1/1 fliers, which is a board out of nowhere.

Ancient Brass Dragon

reanimates creatures with total mana value up to the roll — from ANY graveyard.

Archfiend of Despair

they cannot gain life, and every point they lost this turn is dealt again at end step. It doubles a combat step that already happened.

Rakdos, Patron of Chaos

they give up two permanents or you draw two, every single end step.

Liesa, Forgotten Archangel

your nontoken creatures come back to hand when they die, and THEIR creatures get exiled instead of dying.

Drana and Linvala

turns off every activated ability your opponents have, and steals them all for herself.

The one that does NOT work that way
Master of Cruelties

its trigger requires it to be DECLARED as an attacker, unblocked and alone. Kaalia cannot set that up — she is attacking too, and a creature put onto the battlefield attacking never triggers it. Cast it, then attack with it by itself on a later turn, ideally with an evasion or protection effect. When it does connect, that player is on one life.

Doubling the damage
Gisela, Blade of Goldnight

all damage you deal to opponents is doubled and all damage to you is halved. A single connected Dragon becomes lethal.

Bloodletter of Aclazotz

opponents lose twice as much life during YOUR turn. Stacked with Gisela, damage on your turn is quadrupled.

Archfiend of Despair

then repeats the whole total at end step.

Boros Charm

double strike on the creature that is about to connect, or indestructible for the board.

Isshin, Two Heavens as One

doubles every attack trigger in the deck, not just Kaalia's.

Getting there
Sol Ring

the fastest route to a turn-three Kaalia.

Dark Ritual

three black mana on turn one.

Lotus Petal

free acceleration and fixing.

Necropotence

as many cards as your life total will pay for, and this deck's life total is well protected by Gisela.

Phyrexian Arena

a card a turn.

Faithless Looting

filters, and putting a fatty in the graveyard is upside.

Smothering Tithe

the Treasures hard-cast the fatties Kaalia has not cheated in yet.

Esper Sentinel

taxes their first noncreature spell each turn.

Demonic Counsel

a Demon for two mana, or any card at all with delirium on.

Grim Tutor

any card, straight to hand.

Razaketh, the Foulblooded

a tutor for every creature you sacrifice, repeatedly, in one turn.

Reanimate

one mana for whatever you looted away or whatever they killed.

Animate Dead

the second copy.

Necromancy

flash speed, so you can rebuild in response to a wrath.

Removal
Swords to Plowshares

one mana, exile.

Path to Exile

the second copy.

Anguished Unmaking

instant-speed exile for any nonland permanent.

Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Angel of Despair

removal that Kaalia can cheat in for free.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep acceleration and a protection piece over a hand of fatties. Sol Ring or Dark Ritual plus Lightning Greaves is a far better opener than three Dragons, because the Dragons cost nothing once Kaalia attacks.

Do not attack into open mana without protection. A Kaalia that eats a removal spell mid-combat has cost you a turn and a card; a Kaalia with Greaves or Mother of Runes has cost them a removal spell for nothing.

Cheat in the ENTER and DAMAGE triggers, hard-cast the attack triggers. Angel of Despair and Rune-Scarred Demon are perfect Kaalia targets; Aurelia and Master of Cruelties want to be cast normally.

Isshin, Two Heavens as One

three mana, and he doubles the single most important trigger in your deck. Prioritise him over any individual fatty.
