Kilo-Turbo is the Bracket 4 copy of Kilo-Infinite; the original is unchanged. Tapping Kilo is the trigger, not attacking. Build a repeatable tap/untap loop, seed a real winning counter first, and protect the conversion. Urza makes the artifacts into blue sources and lets Winter Orb restrict opponents without restricting your own untap when timed correctly.

Turn taps into a win
Kilo, Apogee Mind

Haste makes tap abilities ready immediately. Each time Kilo becomes tapped, proliferate. It cannot put a first counter on a permanent or player; seed poison, a charge counter or a Ballista counter before claiming a win.

++
Kilo, Apogee Mind + Freed from the Real + Paradise Mantle

Equip Mantle to Kilo, tap for blue, resolve proliferate, and spend the blue on Freed to untap Kilo. Repeat with no net mana. The equipment needs its one-mana equip payment first.

++
Kilo, Apogee Mind + Freed from the Real + Relic of Legends

Relic's second ability taps Kilo for blue without tapping Relic. Use that blue to untap Kilo with Freed and repeat. Kilo's haste is unnecessary for Relic's externally supplied tap cost, but remains useful elsewhere.

++
Kilo, Apogee Mind + Freed from the Real + Urza, Lord High Artificer

Urza's mana ability taps the artifact Kilo for blue. Proliferate, spend the blue to untap Kilo with Freed, then repeat. This is mana-neutral until another seeded permanent converts the proliferates to mana or damage; Prologue's poison counters also win. Urza's ability supplies the tap, so summoning sickness on the tapped artifact is not a restriction.

++
Kilo, Apogee Mind + Pemmin's Aura + Paradise Mantle

Pemmin supplies the same one-blue untap. Relic can replace Mantle. Giving Kilo shroud before attaching other pieces prevents your own targeting, so sequence attachments first.

++
Kilo, Apogee Mind + Aura of Dominion + Pentad Prism

Enchant Kilo and start with at least TWO Prism counters. Remove one for mana and tap Kilo to pay Aura's untap ability. The proliferate trigger resolves first, replacing the counter while Prism still has one. Resolve the untap and repeat. Starting Prism with one and spending it to zero cannot self-seed.

Prologue to Phyresis

Give each opponent one poison counter, then nine proliferates reach ten and eliminate them. It draws a card and can be found by Spellseeker; no combat or creature damage is needed.

Walking Ballista

With at least one counter left, a proliferate loop replenishes repeated damage shots. Never remove its last counter before replenishing it; it normally dies at zero toughness.

+
Everflowing Chalice + Astral Cornucopia

Cast with counters before proliferating. A large counter count yields arbitrarily large mana from a later tap. A zero-counter version helps artifact count but produces no counter growth until seeded.

Crystalline Crawler

Keep a +1/+1 counter on it while Kilo loops, then remove newly added counters for mana of any color. Do not strip its last seed counter before the next proliferate. This turns a mana-neutral Kilo loop into a mana-positive one.

+
Kilo, Apogee Mind + Springleaf Drum

Drum supplies a low-cost tap and colored mana on a normal turn. Drum taps too, so this is not the repeatable Mantle/Relic loop by itself.

Tezzeret, Cruel Captain

The zero ability untaps Kilo and adds a +1/+1 counter to it because it is an artifact creature. The minus-three finds Mantle, a Key, Chalice, Cornucopia or Ballista to HAND. Loyalty abilities are normally usable only once per turn; proliferating Tezzeret does not change that.

Independent wins when Kilo is answered
+
Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista

Put at least two counters on Ballista and pay Heliod's one-white-plus-one ability to grant lifelink. Remove one counter to deal one damage, gain one life, and return the counter with Heliod's trigger. Repeat for lethal damage. A one-counter Ballista normally dies before the first life-gain trigger can restore it.

+
Isochron Scepter + Dramatic Reversal

Imprint Reversal and tap nonland mana sources before paying two to activate Scepter. Reversal untaps those sources and Scepter. Two mana sustains repeated activations; at least three yields net mana. Add Ballista for a mana win, or a ready Kilo tap engine and a seeded counter for proliferate.

++
Sol Ring + Mana Vault + Grim Monolith

These make Scepter mana-positive. Vault and Monolith do not normally untap, but Reversal untaps them. Monolith's untap cost is four when you do not have that loop.

→
Whir of Invention → Isochron Scepter

Find Scepter directly onto the battlefield only when Reversal is already in hand to imprint. Improvise helps generic costs, not the three blue pips; Kilo can be tapped for improvise to trigger proliferate.

→
Enlightened Tutor → Paradise Mantle

Finds either an artifact or an enchantment on top. Choose the absent half of a loop, or the winning counter source. Finish fetches and other searches before the top-deck tutor.

→
Mystical Tutor → Enlightened Tutor

Turns the new instant tutor into access to an artifact/enchantment engine, with another draw required. It can instead find Reversal, Prologue, removal or protection directly.

→
Muddle the Mixture → Aura of Dominion

Transmute finds mana value TWO: Aura, Pentad Prism or Scepter. It cannot find the three-mana Freed; Drift reaches that instead.

→
Drift of Phantasms → Freed from the Real

Transmute at sorcery speed for a three-mana piece: Freed, Relic, Pemmin's Aura or Teferi. Casting Drift as a blocker does not also tutor.

→
Spellseeker → Prologue to Phyresis

Finds the poison seed, Reversal, Silence or another qualifying instant/sorcery to hand. It does not find an Aura or Ballista.

→
Recruiter of the Guard → Walking Ballista

Ballista has toughness zero in the library. Ranger-Captain also finds it by mana value zero, and neither gives a Ballista put directly onto the battlefield any X counters.

→
Transmute Artifact → Isochron Scepter

Pay two blue, then on resolution sacrifice an artifact and pay any increase in mana value to put Scepter onto the battlefield. Reversal must already be in hand to imprint. A failed search or unpaid difference does not refund the sacrificed artifact, and the sacrifice happens on resolution rather than as a casting cost.

+
Emry, Lurker of the Loch + Sevinne's Reclamation

Emry recasts an artifact from the graveyard once per tap; Reclamation returns a permanent of mana value three or less. Ballista returns without X counters and normally dies. Use recursion to restore mana or a loop piece instead.

Make the protected turn affordable
+++
Chrome Mox + Mox Diamond + Mox Amber + Mox Opal

Amber uses Kilo's three colors once the commander is present; Opal needs metalcraft. Chrome consumes a colored nonartifact card and Diamond consumes a land. Do not count these as unconditional colored sources in an opener.

+
Ancient Tomb + Mana Vault

Cover generic costs of artifacts and tutors. They cannot cast the three-colored commander by themselves; retain blue, red and white sources.

+
Rhystic Study + Mystic Remora

Draw while developing the actual loop. Remora's upkeep payment escalates. Study triggers on opposing spells, not on your own artifact casts, and opponents can pay.

The One Ring

A protected refill when a game stalls. Proliferating its burden counters also increases your next upkeep's life loss, so normally exclude it from proliferate selections.

Urza, Lord High Artificer

Tap otherwise unused artifacts for blue, including Kilo to trigger proliferate and zero-counter mana artifacts that produce nothing themselves. His Construct is another artifact source. The five-mana ability shuffles, so do not use it after stacking an Enlightened or Mystical Tutor unless you accept losing the known top card.

+
Winter Orb + Urza, Lord High Artificer

Keep Orb UNTAPPED through opponents' untap steps to limit land untaps. In the last opponent's end step, tap Orb to Urza for blue. Because Orb is tapped as your untap step begins, all your lands untap normally, along with Orb; it then restricts the next opponent again. Without Urza, Orb restricts your lands too. Deploy it after establishing nonland mana, not into a hand needing several colored land untaps.

Drannith Magistrate

Opponents cannot cast commanders from the command zone, escaped graveyard spells or other spells from exile. Their spells from hand and activated/triggered abilities still work. Your own spells and recursion are unaffected.

Voice of Victory

A second creature that stops opponents casting spells during your turn. It does not stop their activated abilities, unlike Abolisher's specified restrictions. Deploy it before exposing the final Aura or Scepter, not after the opponent has already answered it.

++
Grand Abolisher + Silence + Teferi, Time Raveler

Create an opening before deploying the final pieces. Silence and Teferi restrict spells, while Abolisher also restricts opposing artifact, creature and enchantment activations on your turn. They do not erase abilities already on the stack.

Deflecting Swat

Protect the particular permanent or stack item the loop needs. A redirected target must still be legal; this cannot turn an exile sweeper into harmless targeted removal.

+
Flusterstorm + Mental Misstep

Cheap stack interaction saves mana for a multicolor commander and the actual loop. Flusterstorm covers instants/sorceries and Misstep covers mana value one; neither is a universal answer to a resolved hate permanent.

++
Force of Will + Fierce Guardianship + Pact of Negation

Free protection leaves mana for assembly. Guardianship needs a commander, Force needs a blue pitch card, and Pact leaves a five-mana next-upkeep bill if the attempt fails.

++
Cyclonic Rift + Swords to Plowshares + Wear // Tear

Remove activation hate or spell limits before committing. Artifact-activation hate turns off Ballista and the mana rocks; creature-activation hate can stop Mantle's granted ability. Prologue plus a working proliferation engine wins without Ballista damage.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep functional three-color mana, acceleration, and either a loop component plus a tutor or a genuine draw engine. Mulligan hands of expensive payoffs without blue access. A natural ready-to-attack Kilo does not excuse casting an unseeded Prism and expecting it to work.

Deploy mana, identify the outlet, establish protection, then expose the final Aura or Scepter. With a top-deck tutor, perform shuffling searches first. Target a protected turn-four-or-later attempt; this separate Bracket 4 build does not inherit the original's turn-six waiting expectation. No win-rate percentage has been measured.
