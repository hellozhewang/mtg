A Dragon found by Ureni's attack trigger is not attacking. Even if it gains haste, the declare-attackers moment has already passed. Choose an immediate enter ability or a static damage effect for the current combat; choose an attack-trigger Dragon when preparing a later combat.

This Bracket 3 list has 37 lands and 28 Dragon creature cards in the ninety-nine, with early ramp, haste, draw, and interaction. The three Game Changers are Rhystic Study, Fierce Guardianship, and Cyclonic Rift. It uses combat and Dragon-entry damage without built-in infinite combat loops, extra-turn chains, or mass land denial.

Get Ureni online with mana left to matter

Ureni of the Unwritten

Both entering and attacking can find a Dragon among the top eight cards and put it directly onto the battlefield. It is not cast, so you do not get cast triggers or pay its mana cost. You may decline a Dragon, and the unchosen cards go to the bottom in a random order.

Birds of Paradise

Early fixing helps assemble all three colors and reach seven mana. Keep a green source in an opening hand that depends on it, and remember creature wipes remove this acceleration.

Delighted Halfling

Makes a legendary creature spell such as Ureni harder to counter. Its colored mana can only cast a legendary spell, so it cannot supply colored mana for an ordinary Dragon or a protection spell.

Utopia Sprawl

Enchant a Forest, including a Forest dual, and choose the missing color. Wild Growth offers another one-mana land enchantment; these preserve acceleration through most creature wipes.

Nature's Lore

Along with Three Visits, finds typed Forest duals that can enter untapped. Farseek finds the appropriate Island or Mountain dual instead and always puts it in tapped. Search for colors needed by both Ureni and the interaction in your hand.

Skyshroud Claim

Finds two Forest cards, including original duals and shocks. Untapped targets can immediately pay for another spell, so it is an efficient bridge from early ramp to seven mana.

Sol Ring accelerates the generic part of Ureni and your large Dragons. Arcane Signet provides color fixing; neither replaces the need to make land drops.

Bloomvine Regent // Claim Territory

Cast its Omen early to find two basic Forests, one tapped onto the battlefield and one into hand, then shuffle this card into the library. It remains a future Ureni hit. The creature side gains life as Dragons enter, offsetting fetches, shocks, and other life costs.

Urza's Incubator

Choose Dragon to reduce their generic casting costs by two, including Ureni's. It also helps cast Dragons drawn into your hand; it does nothing to the cost of your protection and removal.

Sarkhan, Soul Aflame

Discounts Dragon spells and can temporarily copy a Dragon that enters while retaining Sarkhan's name. Becoming a copy is not a new entry, so it does not repeat enter abilities. Copy Ureni before attackers are declared to get an additional attack trigger, and decline later optional copy triggers if you want to remain Ureni.

Make the first turn with Ureni count

Dragon Tempest

Gives an entering flyer haste and turns each Dragon entry into damage based on your Dragon count. Haste lets a freshly cast Ureni find one Dragon on entry and another on attack. Tokens count for the damage engine too.

Temur Ascendancy

Team haste plus cards from creatures entering with power four or greater. It works with large Dragon tokens as well as nontoken Dragons, and helps the deck function when Ureni is unavailable.

Rhythm of the Wild

Makes creature spells uncounterable and gives nontoken creatures a choice of haste or a counter. Choose haste for a new Ureni when you lack another haste source. Its riot ability does not give your Dragon tokens haste.

Arena of Glory

A Mountain lets this enter untapped. Spending mana from its exert ability on a creature spell gives that spell haste; paying one red to make two red is filtering rather than net acceleration across your mana sources. Exert also skips this land's next untap.

Hellkite Courser

Cast it to put Ureni from the command zone onto the battlefield with haste, bypassing commander tax. Ureni can trigger on entry and then attack before returning at the next end step. Courser does little if Ureni is already on the battlefield when you find it.

Thassa, Deep-Dwelling

Blink Ureni at your end step for another top-eight trigger, or repeat a removal Dragon's entry. If Courser brought Ureni in, resolve Thassa's blink before the delayed return trigger to keep the returned new object on the battlefield. Its tap ability can remove a blocker when you have spare mana.

Essence Flux

One mana saves a creature from targeted removal and repeats its entry. Ureni is a Spirit, so it returns with a counter. Blinking an attacker removes it from combat, and the returned object needs haste to attack again that turn.

Roaming Throne

Choose Dragon to double Ureni's entry and attack triggers and your other Dragons' triggered abilities. It is not a Dragon card in the library, so Ureni cannot find it. It does not double Dragon Tempest's triggers because that source is an enchantment, not a Dragon creature.

Choose the Dragon that solves this board

Marang River Regent // Coil and Catch

Bounce up to two other nonland permanents on entry, whether deployed by Ureni or cast normally. Its Omen is instant-speed draw three, discard one, then shuffle this card into the library; it is a useful early play when you do not yet need a six-mana creature.

Dragonlord Atarka

Immediate divided damage removes small utility creatures or a planeswalker before combat damage. Blink it when another five damage would open the board.

Ureni, the Song Unending

A large attacker with an entry-damage budget equal to your land count. It can coexist with your commander because they have different names. Protection from white and black does not stop an untargeted board wipe.

Summon: Bahamut

A Dragon hit that destroys a nonland permanent with each of its first two chapters, draws later, and eventually damages every opponent. Its final damage counts the mana values of your other permanents, not their power. Ordinary generated Dragon tokens generally contribute zero; tokens copying a card normally retain that card's mana value.

Balefire Dragon

If it connects with a player, it deals that much damage to each creature that player controls. It must deal combat damage to the player first, so flying blockers can prevent the sweep.

Parapet Thrasher

Spread combat damage across opponents to access different modes: artifact removal, damage to the other opponents, or a card to play that turn. Each mode can be chosen only once each turn, even with additional combats.

Broodcaller Scourge

Combat damage from your Dragons can deploy a permanent from hand, helping when your large threats were drawn instead of found by Ureni. Multiple damaged players give separate triggers, and the mana-value limit uses the Dragon damage dealt to that particular player.

Thundermane Dragon

Lets you inspect the top card and cast a qualifying large creature from there, with haste. You still pay for it and follow timing rules. This is independent card access when you cannot rely on the commander.

Deceptive Frostkite

A two-mana copy of one of your creatures with power at least four, while also remaining a flying Dragon. Copy a nonlegendary engine or a nonlegendary Miirym token when possible. It does not remove legendary status, so copying a normal legend invokes the legend rule.

Multiply entries and build a lethal army

Miirym, Sentinel Wyrm

Copies another nontoken Dragon when it enters, with a nonlegendary token exception. A Dragon put in by Ureni qualifies. If Ureni's first entry finds Miirym, Miirym does not retroactively copy the Ureni that already entered.

Lathliss, Dragon Queen

Every other nontoken Dragon entering adds a 5/5 Dragon token, raising damage triggers and combat pressure. Neither those tokens nor Miirym's tokens trigger Lathliss again.

Utvara Hellkite

Each Dragon declared as an attacker produces a 6/6 Dragon token. The new tokens are not attacking, but their entry damage may matter immediately. Finding Utvara after declaring attackers will not create tokens for that attack.

Astral Dragon

Copy a useful noncreature permanent into two flying Dragon creatures. Dragon Tempest, Rhystic Study, and Urza's Incubator are strong targets in this list. It cannot copy another creature, and the resulting tokens are vulnerable to creature removal.

Goldlust Triad

Myriad creates temporary attacking Dragon copies against the other opponents. Their entries trigger your Dragon damage engines, and successful combat damage creates Treasures. The copies are exiled at end of combat and do not themselves trigger attack abilities.

Terror of the Peaks

Large creatures and copied Dragons entering become direct damage. It triggers for other creatures, not itself, and uses the entering creature's power as its trigger resolves.

Scourge of Valkas

Another Dragon-entry damage engine that includes its own entry and scales with Dragon count. Resolve token-creating triggers first when that will increase the Dragon count before damage is calculated.

Twinflame Tyrant

Doubles your damage to opponents and their permanents, including combat damage and entry-trigger damage. Unlike an attack trigger, its static effect helps the current combat if Ureni finds it after attackers are declared.

Atarka, World Render

Dragons declared as attackers while Atarka is present get double strike. If Ureni finds Atarka during the attack, existing attackers do not retroactively get that trigger. Establish it before combat or use a later combat.

Scourge of the Throne

Its first attack each turn can give an extra combat if it attacks a player with the most life or tied for most life. Keep that player at the top until the trigger resolves; premature damage can turn the trigger off. It untaps attacking creatures when the trigger resolves, not at the start of the extra combat.

If Roaming Throne produces multiple extra-combat triggers, their untaps happen during the original combat. Do not assume creatures untap between every additional combat. Ureni can find another Dragon each time it actually attacks, but Dragons without vigilance still need to be untapped to attack again.

Turn combat into cards and mana

Old Gnawbone

Each of your creatures connecting with a player produces Treasures equal to its combat damage. Those Treasures let you keep interacting or rebuild after a wipe; they do not require you to empty your hand immediately.

Ancient Copper Dragon

Combat damage to a player produces a d20 worth of Treasures. Double strike can trigger it twice, but the amount is variable, so do not plan a necessary follow-up around a guaranteed high roll.

Goldspan Dragon

Attacking creates a Treasure, and all your Treasures can produce two mana of one color. This upgrades Ganax and Old Gnawbone dramatically. Being targeted by an ability alone does not trigger Goldspan's Treasure ability; it specifies a spell.

Ganax, Astral Hunter

Treasures from itself and every other Dragon entry work even when opponents prevent combat. Token Dragons count, letting Miirym, Lathliss, and Utvara help fund protection.

Klauth, Unrivaled Ancient

Its attack generates mana equal to total attacking power, and the mana lasts until end of turn. It can only cast spells, so it cannot pay for Thassa's tap ability or Lathliss's pump activation. Ureni can find it early to support a later hasty attack.

Smaug the Magnificent

A four-mana hasty Dragon that adds a Treasure at your upkeep and converts your current Treasure count into attack-trigger damage. Keep enough Treasures unspent if you need its damage to remove a blocker or finish an opponent.

Ancient Silver Dragon

Combat damage draws a d20 worth of cards and removes your maximum hand size for the rest of the game. These draws are mandatory, so count your library if copies, double strike, or Roaming Throne create many triggers.

Rhystic Study

The draw Game Changer provides resources before seven mana and through creature wipes. It lets you keep developing without depending on Ureni's survival.

Garruk's Uprising

Draws from most large Dragons entering and gives trample to convert large power into player damage. That helps your combat-damage Dragons connect through small blockers.

The Great Henge

Large power reduces its casting cost, then it supplies mana, life, counters, and cards from nontoken creature entries. Ureni's seven power makes it cost two green mana. It does not draw from your token Dragons.

Greater Good

Sacrifice a doomed large creature to preserve cards through exile or destruction. You draw its power and discard three, so a small creature is a poor sacrifice. Keep a Dragon in reserve rather than committing every card you draw.

Protect the turn that matters

Fierce Guardianship

Protects a resolved Ureni or a developed board from a noncreature spell for no mana. It is not free while Ureni is still on the stack and cannot stop creature spells.

Deflecting Swat

Redirects targeted removal, a counterspell, or another targeted ability, often free with Ureni present. It cannot redirect a targetless board wipe.

Swan Song

Cheap protection against instants, sorceries, and enchantments. The replacement flyer can block a small attacker, so account for it when arranging a combat-damage trigger.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

One mana stops a noncreature spell, at the cost of giving its controller two Treasures. Reserve it for an important threat or protection exchange.

Heroic Intervention

Protects your permanents from targeted removal and destruction. It does not stop untargeted exile or sacrifice effects, and indestructible creatures can still die to sufficiently reduced toughness.

Cyclonic Rift

Single-target tempo early, or a seven-mana one-sided nonland reset before a decisive attack. It is an answer to noncreature obstacles that damage Dragons cannot remove.

Beast Within

Flexible permanent removal, including a troublesome enchantment or utility land. The replacement ground creature is often less threatening to your flying army.

Chaos Warp

Another broad answer, with the risk of revealing a replacement permanent. Use it when an obstacle needs a permanent answer rather than temporary bounce.

Pongify

One mana removes a dangerous creature while leaving room for protection. It does not remove indestructible creatures; use bounce or Chaos Warp when that matters.

Desolation of Smaug

Three damage to each non-Dragon creature can clear small opposing engines while preserving your Dragons. It also kills your Birds of Paradise and Delighted Halfling. Spend the four mana it provides during the same phase on Dragon spells; it cannot pay for instants or activated abilities.

Blasphemous Act

Emergency creature reset when opponents are ahead. Without protection, it also kills most of your Dragons; it is not a one-sided wipe merely because you play large creatures.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Low-cost graveyard interaction that preserves your own recovery targets. Use its mass-exile activation when an opponent commits to a graveyard line, or draw a card when graveyards are not relevant.

Haven of the Spirit Dragon

Returns a destroyed Dragon to hand when you need to rebuild, at the cost of sacrificing a land. Its colored mana can only cast Dragon creature spells, so keep unrestricted colored lands for interaction.

What to keep and how to sequence it

Keep three lands with access to green, at least one early ramp play, and a way to develop cards, haste, or interaction. Two expensive Dragons are not a reason to keep a hand that cannot reach Ureni. Two lands are a risk unless cheap acceleration and the rest of the hand strongly support it.

An example keep is Misty Rainforest, Taiga, Island, Nature's Lore, Dragon Tempest, Rhystic Study, and Heroic Intervention. It supplies colors, early acceleration, haste, draw, and protection; use early draws to make land drops and find the next acceleration or Dragon.

There are 37 lands, including nine fetchlands and five basics. The three basic Forests give Bloomvine Regent's Omen targets; use typed duals for Nature's Lore, Three Visits, and Skyshroud Claim when possible. Fetch an untapped land when you need a response, and use tapped surveil lands or Ketria Triome when you can afford the delay.

Cavern of Souls normally names Dragon and helps resolve Ureni, but its restricted colored mana cannot pay for a counterspell or blink effect. Arena of Glory needs a Mountain already under your control to enter untapped. Boseiju, Who Endures and Otawara, Soaring City offer interaction through channel when their land drops are no longer necessary.

The 28 Dragon cards provide about a 93.8 percent chance of at least one Dragon in eight cards in the simplified case of all 28 remaining in a randomized 99-card library. Actual odds change with what has been drawn, deployed, or put on the bottom. Roaming Throne and Sarkhan are not extra Dragon hits in the library.

Try to establish haste or a draw engine before committing Ureni, while keeping interaction available when possible. On the entry trigger, choose the Dragon that makes the coming attack or the next round stronger. On the attack trigger, favor immediate removal, static damage multiplication, or value over an attack trigger that has already missed its opportunity.

Do not blink a Dragon token expecting it to return; the exiled token ceases to exist. A blinked nontoken creature is a new object, so temporary haste, counters, and attack status may be lost. With Ureni, a surviving static haste source makes repeated entries much easier to convert into attacks.

Against repeated removal, cast your midrange Dragons and draw engines instead of paying an ever-growing commander tax into open interaction. Keep enough lands and a recovery card to restart after a wipe. The strongest finish is the one you can protect, not necessarily the one that puts the most Dragons onto the battlefield.
