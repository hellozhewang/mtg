Venser has flash and bounces a SPELL, so he is a hard counter that leaves a 2/2 Wizard behind. Everything else in the deck exists to make that trigger happen again — blink him, copy him, double his trigger — until the table cannot resolve anything. And because he is a Wizard, the tribal payoffs that tap Wizards for value are all live at the same time.

The commander
Venser, Shaper Savant

flash, and his enters trigger returns a target spell OR permanent to its owner's hand. Hold four mana and he answers a wrath, a combo piece or a commander at instant speed.

Naban, Dean of Iteration

if a WIZARD entering triggers an ability of a permanent you control, it triggers again. Venser is a Wizard, so every arrival bounces two things.

Panharmonicon

the same doubling for any creature or artifact.

Roaming Throne

name Wizard. A third doubler, on a ward {2} body.

Archmage of Echoes

copies every Faerie or Wizard PERMANENT spell you cast. Casting Venser makes two Vensers and two bounces.

Blinking him, over and over
Displacer Kitten

every noncreature spell you cast blinks a permanent you control. Point it at Venser and each counterspell, each cantrip, each removal spell also bounces something of theirs. This is the deck's engine.

Conjurer's Closet

a free blink every end step, forever.

Essence Flux

one mana.

Displace

two creatures at once, at instant speed.

Blur

a blink and a card.

Planar Incision

a blink that leaves a +1/+1 counter, and it works on artifacts too.

Dour Port-Mage

bounces one of your own creatures every turn for {1}{U}, and draws whenever your creatures leave without dying.

Barrin, Tolarian Archmage

bounces a creature or planeswalker on arrival, and draws at end step whenever a permanent went to your hand from the battlefield — which this deck does every single turn.

Aether Channeler

three modes, all of which are fine, and all of which double under Naban.

The Wizard engines
Azami, Lady of Scrolls

tap any untapped Wizard to draw a card. On a board of five Wizards that is five cards a turn.

Patron Wizard

tap a Wizard to tax every spell they cast. Alongside Azami it makes your board a soft counterspell wall.

Galecaster Colossus

tap a Wizard to bounce any nonland permanent they control. With enough Wizards it is a one-sided board wipe every turn.

Sage of Fables

every other Wizard arrives with a +1/+1 counter, and counters convert into cards.

Docent of Perfection // Final Iteration

a Wizard token per instant or sorcery, then it flips into an anthem that keeps making them.

Kindred Discovery

name Wizard. A card for every Wizard that enters AND every one that attacks — and this deck flickers Wizards constantly.

Herald's Horn

Wizards cost one less and it digs for them each upkeep.

Patchwork Banner

an anthem plus fixing.

Psychosis Crawler

each opponent loses a life for every card you draw. With Azami running that is lethal quickly.

Interaction
Counterspell

the clean two-mana answer.

Mana Drain

counter, and the mana casts Venser at instant speed.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for anything noncreature.

Glen Elendra Archmage

a repeatable counterspell for noncreature spells, twice thanks to persist.

Glen Elendra Guardian

flash, flies, and counters a noncreature spell while drawing them a card they do not need.

Pongify

one mana, kills anything.

Rapid Hybridization

the second copy.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, the clear for a lethal Wizard attack.

Raise the Palisade

name Wizard and every other creature at the table goes back to hand.

Cyclone Summoner

cast from hand, it returns ALL permanents except Giants, Wizards and lands. Your board survives; nobody else's does.

Master of Waves

protection from red, an anthem, and a pile of Elemental tokens scaled to your devotion.

Card flow
Rhystic Study

their turns pay for your instant-speed turns.

Mystic Remora

the best card in the deck on turns one through four.

Archaeomancer

returns an instant or sorcery on entry, and blinks for another every turn.

Quistis Trepe

casts an instant or sorcery from ANY graveyard on entry, and blinks to do it again.

Emeritus of Ideation // Ancestral Recall

a 5/5 flier that also carries an Ancestral Recall.

Candlekeep Sage

your commander draws a card whenever he enters OR leaves — so every blink is two cards.

Sapphire Medallion

everything costs one less.

Mind Stone

ramp early, a card later.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep mana and a cheap Wizard. The deck wants to be holding up four mana from turn four onwards, and Venser is only a counterspell if you can actually cast him at instant speed.

Cast Venser on THEIR turn whenever possible. Bouncing a spell off the stack is strictly better than bouncing a permanent, and holding him up costs you nothing because he is also your best blocker-answer.

Displacer Kitten

prioritise her over any single Wizard. With Venser on the battlefield she turns every cheap blue spell in the deck into a two-for-one.

Naban, Dean of Iteration

two mana, and he doubles the most important trigger in the deck. Play him before you start blinking, not after.
