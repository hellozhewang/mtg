Assemble a coven of black Wizard tokens that burn opponents on every noncreature spell, transform Kuja into Trance Kuja to double all Wizard damage, chain spells via Bolas's Citadel, and finish with the Dualcaster-Twinflame combo.

Wizard token creation and transforming into Trance Kuja
Kuja, Genome Sorcerer // Trance Kuja, Fate Defied

creates a tapped 0/1 black Wizard creature token at the beginning of your end step with "Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, this token deals 1 damage to each opponent." Then, if you control four or more Wizards, Kuja transforms into Trance Kuja, Fate Defied. Trance Kuja's static ability Flare Star dictates that if a Wizard you control would deal damage to a permanent or player, it deals double that damage instead, turning each 0/1 Wizard token into a 2-damage burn cannon per spell.

Harmonic Prodigy

possesses prowess and states that if an ability of another Shaman or Wizard you control triggers, that ability triggers an additional time. This doubles Kuja's end step token generation (yielding two Wizard tokens per turn) and doubles each token's noncreature damage trigger.

Coruscation Mage

Wizard with offspring that deals one damage to each opponent whenever you cast a noncreature spell.

Guttersnipe

deals two damage to each opponent on each instant or sorcery cast; under Trance Kuja, Guttersnipe deals four damage per spell.

Electrostatic Field

one-mana 0/4 defender that deals one damage to each opponent on each instant or sorcery.

Roaming Throne

choose Wizard on entry to double all triggered abilities of Wizards, doubling Kuja's token generation and all burn triggers.

Spellslinger mana and storm acceleration
Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

adds {R} whenever you cast any spell, keeping mana flowing during long cantrip turns.

Storm-Kiln Artist

creates a Treasure token whenever you cast or copy an instant or sorcery spell.

Jeska's Will

premium ritual adding red mana equal to cards in target opponent's hand and exiling the top three cards to play.

Mana Geyser

adds {R} for each tapped land your opponents control, frequently producing 15 to 25 red mana in mid-to-late game turns.

Bolas's Citadel

lets you look at the top card of your library and cast spells from the top by paying life equal to their mana values, stringing together long noncreature spell sequences that trigger repeated Wizard burn.

Mizzix's Mastery

overloads from your graveyard to cast copies of all instant and sorcery cards in your graveyard for free, triggering massive bursts of noncreature casts.

Dedicated combo win lines (Turn 6+)
+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

cast Twinflame targeting any creature you control, leaving priority with Twinflame on the stack. Cast Dualcaster Mage with flash. Dualcaster's entry trigger targets Twinflame, creating a copy of Twinflame that targets Dualcaster Mage. The copy resolves to create a token copy of Dualcaster with haste, which triggers to copy Twinflame again. Repeat for infinite hasty 2/2 Wizards to attack for the win.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication

functions identically to Twinflame; produces infinite hasty creature/artifact tokens while triggering all Wizard noncreature entry and cast synergies.

Solphim, Mayhem Dominus

doubles all noncombat damage dealt by red sources you control, stacking with Trance Kuja's damage doubling.

City on Fire

triples all damage dealt by sources you control.

Tutors, interaction, and opening hands
Demonic Tutor

finds Bolas's Citadel, Twinflame, or a critical board answer.

Deflecting Swat

zero-mana redirection spell while controlling Kuja to protect him from targeted removal.

Deadly Rollick

free creature exile while controlling your commander.

Blasphemous Act

one-mana board wipe to clear opposing boards before deploying Kuja and your Wizards.

Vandalblast

overloads to destroy all opposing artifacts.

Keep opening hands with two to three lands, early acceleration (Sol Ring, Rakdos Signet, Talisman), and cheap noncreature cantrips or rituals (Faithless Looting, Night's Whisper, Deadly Dispute). Deploy Kuja early to start generating Wizard tokens on end steps, transform into Trance Kuja once four Wizards are on board, and unleash a flurry of spells with Bolas's Citadel or the Dualcaster combo on Turn 6 or later.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, Wizard accumulation, and card advantage.
