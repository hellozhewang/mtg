Ral-Storm is the owner's separate Bracket 4 storm list. Discounted red draw and Tavern Scoundrel turn early casts into more resources, then a deliberate transformation supplies another hand or a stable discount. Lost front-side coin flips still cost life: neither the Treasure engine nor flashback makes a finite life total unlimited. The Bracket 3 decks remain unchanged.

Cost reduction, then a deliberate transformation
Ral, Monsoon Mage // Ral, Leyline Prodigy

Reduces only generic instant/sorcery costs. Every such cast on YOUR turn flips a coin; losses deal one damage to you, wins optionally transform him. You can decline a winning transformation to retain the front's discount, but cannot decline the flip or loss damage.

Ral, Monsoon Mage // Ral, Leyline Prodigy

The back starts with two loyalty plus the number of instant/sorcery spells you have cast that turn, so six such spells give eight loyalty on transformation. Resolve the current spell and clear the stack before activating a loyalty ability.

Ral, Monsoon Mage // Ral, Leyline Prodigy

After transforming, the plus-one establishes a one-generic discount until your next turn without further creature-side coin losses. The minus-eight instead grants permission to cast instant/sorcery cards among the top eight for free that turn. Choose the mode the hand actually needs; you cannot normally activate both that turn.

Geist of Saint Thalia

A second cost reducer that survives Ral's transformation. Its generic reduction covers noncreature spells and makes ritual/cantrip chains more reliable without requiring repeated coin-flip wins.

Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun

Gives your instant/sorcery spells split second. It restricts responses while one is on the stack, but does not erase triggered abilities or protect non-instant/sorcery spells. You also cannot cast your own counterspell while a split-second spell remains on the stack.

Hexing Squelcher

Spells cannot be countered while Squelcher survives. The ward tax on other creatures is a cost, not immunity to removal. It does not suppress opponents' activated abilities.

+++
Ponder + Preordain + Brainstorm + Consider

Cheap filtering finds land drops and protection before the chain. Blue one-mana cantrips do not benefit from a generic cost reduction, while a one-red-plus-one draw spell becomes one red with Ral.

Faithless Looting

Prepare the graveyard and keep the spells the next turn needs. Do not rely on a discarded card being recoverable without Breach or flashback; this is selection at the cost of cards, not raw card advantage.

Tavern Scoundrel

Each WON coin flip makes two Treasures, including a Ral flip whose optional transformation you decline. Lost flips produce no Treasures and still cost life through Ral. Once Ral transforms he no longer supplies those flips. This is a useful early engine, not a guaranteed infinite-mana combo; Scoundrel's own sacrifice ability has additional costs.

Highway Robbery

Ral reduces its normal cost to one red. Discard a card or sacrifice an expendable land to draw two, stocking the graveyard without refilling three opponents. If you plot it, the separate plot payment is not reduced by Ral, and the free cast waits until a later turn.

Rob the Archives

With Ral it costs one red to expose two cards for THIS turn. Casualty can sacrifice a spare power-one creature to copy it. Ral's discount is determined before paying the sacrifice, but sacrificing Ral means he is absent when casting finishes and will not trigger a coin flip. Copies do not add casts, and exposed cards still need ordinary timing permission.

Rituals need an outlet, not just a large mana pool
Rite of Flame

A one-red start to the chain. Cost reduction cannot reduce that red pip; its extra mana counts cards with the same name in ALL graveyards, not storm copies.

+
Desperate Ritual + Pyretic Ritual

Each normally costs two and makes three red; with one generic reduction it costs one red. Desperate's splice needs another Arcane spell and the appropriate additional cost, not merely any instant.

Seething Song

With a one-generic reduction it costs one-red-plus-one and makes five red. Spend the burst on an actual draw or recovery engine; a large mana pool alone is not a finish.

Jeska's Will

With a commander present, including a transformed commander, it may use both modes. Mana depends on the targeted hand. The exiled cards are temporary resources; check land-play limits and the mana needed to cast them.

+
Hidden Strings + Snap

Untap real mana sources after their other effects. Snap needs a legal creature target; if the target is illegal on resolution you do not untap lands. Strings can untap two useful permanents but its cipher requires later combat damage for more copies.

+
Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty + Electro, Assaulting Battery

Birgi refunds red per spell and Electro per instant/sorcery cast. Electro's mana does not empty between steps. The refunds trigger on casts, not storm copies or copied rituals.

Storm-Kiln Artist

Creates a Treasure for each instant/sorcery cast OR copy, including storm copies. Resolve the triggers in a controlled order rather than overlooking the original casting payment. Artist does not make additional cards to cast and does not turn a finite chain into an automatic infinite loop.

Grapeshot

The immediate storm-count outlet. Count opponents' actual life totals and assign enough copies to each. Infinite red mana without casts is not enough, and copying Grapeshot does not create another storm trigger.

Breach is a funded engine, not a three-card wish
++
Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze

Resolve Breach before discarding the hand to Diamond. Escape Diamond for three other cards, make three blue, then escape Freeze for one-blue-plus-one and three other cards. Use enough mill copies on yourself to feed the next two escapes, then send surplus at opponents. Watch the shrinking library and actual fuel.

Brain Freeze

Milling libraries does not immediately make players lose. Follow with a draw effect when safe, a Grapeshot finish, or a controlled turn pass. Keep enough of your own library for any draw spell you intend to resolve.

→++
Intuition → Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze + Past in Flames

With Breach available and sufficient fodder, either the chosen hand card or the graveyard cards can be used. With no recovery engine, the opponent's choice means this is NOT a guaranteed one-card combo. Never hide that prerequisite.

+
Past in Flames + Flashback

Recover rituals and selection after a stalled attempt. Flashback the one-red instant can give a chosen instant/sorcery in the graveyard its own flashback permission. These permissions do not recover artifacts or enchantments and spells cast through flashback are exiled.

+
Echo of Eons + Wheel of Fortune

Eons refills and shuffles graveyards; Wheel discards and preserves graveyard fuel. Eons can undo a mill finish or remove the Breach resources you intended to use. Choose intentionally rather than taking the first available wheel.

Will of the Jeskai

With a commander present, including planeswalker Ral, choose both modes: each player MAY discard and draw five, then your graveyard instants and sorceries gain flashback for their mana costs. Opponents can decline, so this is not forced lethal after milling them. It cannot recover Breach or Diamond and flashback still exiles each spell. With Ral's discount, two generic and one red buys the combined recovery spell.

Tutors, protection and functional mana
→
Mystical Tutor → Intuition

Top-deck access to a setup or answer, with a draw still needed. Tutor after fetches and other shuffling effects.

→
Personal Tutor → Past in Flames

Finds a sorcery on top: recovery, a wheel, Gamble, Grapeshot or a ritual. It cannot directly find Breach, Diamond or an instant.

→
Merchant Scroll → Brain Freeze

Blue-instant access to the mill piece, Intuition or protection in hand. It does not find the red ritual or an arbitrary enchantment.

+
Gamble + Solve the Equation

Gamble finds any card with a random-discard risk; Equation finds an instant or sorcery directly to hand. Equation does not find Breach or Diamond.

+++
Chrome Mox + Mox Diamond + Mox Amber + Mox Opal

Use functioning moxen, not imagined zero-cost mana. Amber sees a legendary creature OR planeswalker, so the transformed Ral still enables it. Diamond requires a land discard and Opal requires three artifacts.

++
Mana Vault + Sol Ring + Lotus Petal

Supply the initial mana for Ral and an engine. Vault and Ring are colorless; Petal can repair a missing color but is spent afterward. Do not discard Breach to Diamond before casting it.

Springleaf Drum

Pays one instead of a two-mana rock, then taps a creature you control for colored mana. A fresh Ral can pay Drum's external tap cost despite summoning sickness. Transformed Ral is a planeswalker, so without another creature Drum cannot work. Plan that transition before counting Drum as permanent colored mana.

++
Ancient Tomb + City of Traitors + Gemstone Caverns

Fast lands help a protected attempt. Traitors sacrifices when you play another land, and Caverns needs an opening-hand, nonstarting-player condition plus an exiled card for its special start.

++
Force of Will + Fierce Guardianship + Pact of Negation

Keep the prerequisites and Pact's next-upkeep bill in mind. A transformed commander still satisfies Guardianship's commander requirement.

++
Flusterstorm + Pyroblast + Red Elemental Blast

Win the key counter fight or remove a blue permanent before committing. These do not cover every creature spell or activated ability. Split second can prevent responding while your own protected spell is on the stack.

++
Cyclonic Rift + Chain of Vapor + Otawara, Soaring City

Clear resolved hate before the chain. Overloading Rift costs seven before applicable reductions; Chain may be copied by its target's controller. Otawara is a channel ability and survives many spell-counter restrictions.

Opening hands and sequencing

The finished list has 26 true lands plus five modal spell/lands, rather than the seed's 17 true lands. Keep two functional colored sources, Ral or a reliable setup plan, and a draw/tutor or ritual chain with protection. A one-land hand whose only help is conditional Opal is a mulligan. Treat a modal land used as mana as a spell you no longer have.

Develop early, use the front discount and Scoundrel for efficient spells, then transform when the loyalty or life total makes that correct. Preserve graveyard resources for Breach or Will rather than automatically shuffling them away. The former buyback/fork package is gone: wins come from the funded storm chain or Breach, not a loop the list no longer contains. No goldfish percentage or tournament result has been measured.
