Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Yuna is the combo engine, not a reason to spend five mana on counter doublers. The primary plan is to pair Grand Summon with a repeatable untapper, then cast Walking Ballista with lethal entry counters. Independent Heliod and Devoted Druid lines keep the deck functional when Yuna is removed. The three Game Changers are Enlightened Tutor, Worldly Tutor and Rhystic Study. Hold compact wins and infinite loops until turn six under the house rules. This is an optimized construction, not a measured turn-six win-rate claim.

Mulligans and the first five turns

Keep two or three usable mana sources, a turn-one accelerator or early draw engine, and a tutor or a real route toward a finish. A hand of expensive tutors with no green source or no acceleration is not a keep just because it contains combo names. Count Bala Ged Recovery as either a tapped land or a spell, never both.

A strong ordinary start is a green source and a one-mana creature on turn one, then a draw engine, another accelerator or a tutor on turn two. Develop enough white and blue to cast Yuna; do not spend every source on optional value if that prevents protecting her. Yuna has a tap ability and normally needs to survive a turn before Grand Summon is available.

Use turns three through five to stage Yuna, a mana creature or an independent combo, while retaining interaction. On turn six choose the cheapest complete line your actual hand supports. Do not tutor another engine when the only missing card is the outlet or a protection spell.

Primary finish — Grand Summon into a lethal Ballista
++
Yuna, Grand Summoner + Freed from the Real + Walking Ballista

Start with Yuna able to tap and Freed attached to her. Tap Yuna for {U}, creating a delayed Grand Summon effect; spend that {U} to untap her with Freed. Repeat 60 times, then cast Ballista with X=0. Every delayed effect applies to that next creature spell, so Ballista enters with at least 120 +1/+1 counters. Shoot each of three opponents at 40 life 40 times. Choose a larger finite number if opponents have more life or relevant prevention. This loop needs no net mana and no counter doubler; it needs a castable Ballista in hand or another explicit way to access it.

++
Yuna, Grand Summoner + Pemmin's Aura + Walking Ballista

The same counter-entry loop, using the Aura's {U} untap ability. Once the Aura is attached, granting Yuna shroud with it does not stop its own untap ability, which does not target. Giving her shroud before casting the Aura would prevent attaching it. The Aura's power/toughness modes do not produce the entry counters.

Yuna, Grand Summoner

All accumulated Grand Summon effects are spent on the NEXT creature spell you cast this turn. Do not insert a mana creature or a creature tutor between setting up lethal counters and casting Ballista. Unused effects expire with the turn. A creature put onto the battlefield by a tutor was not cast and does not receive these cast-based bonuses.

+
Yuna, Grand Summoner + Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy

Kinnan makes Yuna's {U} activation produce an additional {U}. With either untap Aura, each cycle now also banks one blue mana. Bank blue before making other colors and paying blue untaps. Kinnan does not untap Yuna by itself.

+
Yuna, Grand Summoner + Badgermole Cub

With an untap Aura attached to Yuna, Cub adds {G} whenever you tap Yuna for mana. Each {U}-for-untap cycle also banks {G}. The extra mana is useful, but Cub is not required for the zero-mana Ballista entry-counter kill.

Independent finishes and mana engines
+
Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista

With Heliod out, normally cast Ballista for X=2, costing four mana, then pay {1}{W} to give it lifelink. Remove one counter to deal one damage; lifelink gains one life, and Heliod's trigger replaces the counter. Resolve that trigger before shooting again. Repeat for the whole pod. A normal X=1 Ballista dies when its last counter is spent, before the shot and lifegain can reload it. Heliod need not be a creature for these abilities to work.

++
Yuna, Grand Summoner + Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista

An ordinary Grand Summon activation lets a zero-mana Ballista enter with two counters. With Heliod already out, only the {1}{W} lifelink activation remains to start the loop. Grand Summon's mana can help pay that activation. This is a cheap finish without an untap Aura, provided no other creature spell consumes the bonus first.

+
Devoted Druid + Swift Reconfiguration

Enchant Druid with Swift. It keeps its abilities but becomes a noncreature Vehicle: tap for {G}, put a -1/-1 counter on it to untap, and repeat. Noncreatures are not put into the graveyard for having zero toughness, and the noncreature Druid can tap even if it entered this turn. Do not crew it while it has lethal negative counters. Generate enough green to cast Ballista for X at least 120, or use a real converter below. Swift does not remove an opposing commander's abilities; it is not a universal ability lock.

+
Devoted Druid + Vizier of Remedies

Vizier replaces the one -1/-1 counter with zero, allowing repeated untaps and green mana. Here Druid remains a creature and needs tap readiness or haste. A fresh Vizier is fine because its replacement effect works immediately. Unlimited green still requires Ballista, a converter or an accessible tutor to win.

+
Incubation Druid + Freed from the Real

Druid needs a +1/+1 counter and a land you control that can produce blue. Tap for {U}{U}{U}, spend {U} to untap, and bank two blue. Bank untap mana before tapping for other available colors. Pemmin's Aura supplies the same untap. Grand Summon supplies entry counters if Druid was cast; otherwise adapt costs {3}{G}{G}.

+
Incubation Druid + Vigean Graftmage

Put a counter on Druid, by grafting when it enters after Graftmage or through Grand Summon. Druid taps for three blue; Graftmage's untap costs {1}{U}, leaving one blue per cycle. Graftmage itself need not tap. The target must have a +1/+1 counter. Grafting away Graftmage's last counter kills the 0/0, so keep it alive when you need its untap ability.

+
Kami of Whispered Hopes + Freed from the Real

A ready Kami with power at least two taps for at least two blue, pays one blue to untap and nets mana. Its printed power is only one; supply counters first. Grand Summon makes it enter with two counters and power three. Kami's counter replacement does NOT add another counter to its own entry. With Vigean Graftmage instead of an Aura, Kami needs a +1/+1 counter and power at least three to net mana after the two-mana untap.

+
Bloom Tender + Freed from the Real

Tender and the blue Aura already provide green and blue permanents, producing {G}{U}; spend the blue to untap and bank green. With Yuna out, Tender also produces white, so each cycle banks {G}{W}. This does not produce unlimited blue: its one blue pays the untap. The green/white is enough for Ballista or generic-cost converters. Pemmin's Aura works the same way.

+
Faeburrow Elder + Vigean Graftmage

Elder's green/white and Graftmage's blue give all three Bant colors. A ready Elder with a +1/+1 counter taps for {G}{W}{U}; pay {G}{U} for Graftmage and bank {W}. The counter must come from graft, Grand Summon or another actual effect. Elder's printed power bonus is not a counter. Either untap Aura also works, banking green/white.

Converting mana and surviving missing pieces
Walking Ballista

Unlimited unrestricted mana casts X=120 for 240 mana against three 40-life opponents. X appears twice in the cost. Existing Ballistas add counters for four mana each. The damage ability has no tap cost, so a fresh Ballista can shoot. If all counters are spent and Ballista dies with none, Yuna does not redistribute counters that no longer exist.

Thrasios, Triton Hero

Each activation costs four generic mana. With unlimited green or white, put revealed lands onto the battlefield tapped and draw nonlands until you find Ballista or a usable tutor. Do not activate after the library is empty and assume you are safe: revealing no land leads to a mandatory draw and a loss. Scrying is optional selection, not a free draw.

Staff of Domination

With unlimited mana, pay five and tap to draw a card, then one to untap Staff. Repeat only as far as needed. A full draw cycle costs six, and Staff is a converter here rather than an assumption that any three-mana dork already goes infinite. Its creature untap costs three plus a tap, then another one to reset Staff.

→
Finale of Devastation → Walking Ballista

Finale finds ONE creature from library and/or graveyard, not the whole creature suite. At X at least ten, its temporary toughness bonus keeps a zero-counter Ballista alive; use unlimited mana to add at least 120 counters before the bonus expires, then shoot. At small X, tutoring a normal 0/0 Ballista directly onto the battlefield kills it. Grand Summon does not apply because Finale does not cast the creature.

+
Agatha's Soul Cauldron + Walking Ballista

If Ballista is in a graveyard, Cauldron can exile it and put a +1/+1 counter on a creature you control. Your creatures with +1/+1 counters then gain Ballista's add-counter and damage abilities. Unlimited mana can load a surviving carrier and shoot. Resolve Cauldron's counter trigger and preserve a carrier with counters; an empty carrier loses the granted abilities. Cauldron also supplies graveyard interaction, but it cannot retrieve an already exiled Ballista unless that card was exiled specifically with Cauldron.

Concordant Crossroads

One green mana gives all creatures haste, including opposing creatures. Hold it until a winning turn needs immediate Yuna, Druid, Tender or Kami activations. Do not hand the table haste on turn one. Swift-reconfigured Druid is a noncreature and does not need Crossroads.

Bala Ged Recovery // Bala Ged Sanctuary

Return a lost Aura, Ballista, tutor or protection card to hand; the other face is a tapped green source. Endurance can instead recycle your graveyard into the library for future tutors, or disrupt an opponent's graveyard, but it does not put a card directly into your hand.

Tutor map — choose the missing job
→
Enlightened Tutor → Freed from the Real

Finds either untap Aura, Swift Reconfiguration, Heliod, Ballista, Cauldron, Staff or a draw engine. It puts the card on TOP: use it before a draw or with a paid draw available, not as though it placed the card in hand.

→
Worldly Tutor → Walking Ballista

Top-deck creature access to the outlet, a mana creature, Heliod or a protective creature. Arrange the actual draw before attempting the line. Do not shuffle the fetched card away with a fetch land.

→
Eladamri's Call → Heliod, Sun-Crowned

Two-mana instant to hand for any missing creature: Heliod, Ballista, Druid, Vizier, Thrasios or a tutor creature. Prefer this over a more expensive tutor chain when it finds the card directly.

→
Pia, Aether Ascetic → Freed from the Real

Its entry trigger requires discarding a card, then puts an enchantment into hand. Finds either untap Aura, Swift, Heliod, Crossroads or Rhystic Study. It cannot find Ballista or Staff, which are artifacts, not enchantments.

→
Formidable Speaker → Walking Ballista

Discard a card on entry to find any creature directly to hand. Its separate {1}, tap ability can untap another permanent, but requires readiness and does not create a repeatable mana-positive loop by itself.

→
Fauna Shaman → Heliod, Sun-Crowned

Pay green, tap and discard a CREATURE card to find the missing creature. A spare land is not valid fodder. Stage it early so the tap is available; Crossroads can enable a new Shaman on the winning turn.

→
Recruiter of the Guard → Walking Ballista

Toughness two or less finds Ballista, Incubation Druid, Devoted Druid, Vizier, Tender, Kami, Fauna or Grand Abolisher. It cannot find Heliod or Thrasios, whose toughness is too high. Ranger-Captain finds Ballista through its mana value of zero, then can sacrifice to protect the attempt.

→
Drift of Phantasms → Freed from the Real

Transmute for {1}{U}{U} at sorcery speed to find a card of mana value exactly three: either Aura, Heliod, Graftmage, Kami, Staff, Pia or Formidable Speaker. Discarding Drift is part of the transmute cost; it is not a spell and does not produce a blocker.

→
Green Sun's Zenith → Devoted Druid

X=2 finds a green two-drop directly onto the battlefield. X=3 finds a green three-drop such as Kami or Formidable Speaker. It cannot find the white Heliod or colorless Ballista. The creature is put in, not cast, so it does not get Grand Summon's delayed entry counters.

→
Nature's Rhythm → Heliod, Sun-Crowned

Searches for any creature directly onto the battlefield. Use X=3 for Heliod or X=2 for a mana creature. Harmonize from the graveyard retains the four green pips; tapping a creature reduces only the generic X portion. An ordinary X=0 Ballista has no entry counters and dies.

→
Eldritch Evolution → Heliod, Sun-Crowned

Sacrificing a one-mana creature can fetch a creature with mana value up to three. Plan for the real sacrifice and the {1}{G}{G} payment; countering Evolution still costs you the sacrificed creature. Chord can find a creature at instant speed with convoke, including using summoning-sick creatures to help pay, but a creature already tapped for mana cannot also convoke.

→
Idyllic Tutor → Pemmin's Aura

Straight-to-hand enchantment access for three mana. It can also get Freed, Swift, Heliod, Crossroads or a draw engine. If Yuna is ready and Ballista is held, prioritize the untapper over an unrelated value enchantment.

Mana, protection and real weaknesses
Birds of Paradise

Prioritize green on turn one, then plan blue for untappers and white for protective creatures. Birds, Noble Hierarch and the dual/fetch suite supply flexible colors. The three basics are intentional recovery against nonbasic hate; fetch a basic when that protection matters. Mistrise Village can make the next spell uncounterable, but activating it spends its tap and another blue mana.

Delighted Halfling

Colored mana from its second ability only casts a LEGENDARY spell. It can protect Yuna or Heliod; it cannot pay for an Aura or an activation. Its first ability makes unrestricted colorless. Mox Amber needs an appropriate legendary creature to produce mana, so it is not an automatic colored source in an opening hand.

Grand Abolisher

On your turn, prevents opposing spells and activated abilities of artifacts, creatures and enchantments. It does not stop triggered abilities, land abilities or everything a permanent might do. Voice of Victory stops opposing spells on your turn but does not shut down their activated abilities.

+
Silence + Ranger-Captain of Eos

Silence stops opposing spells after it resolves, not abilities or spells already on the stack. Ranger-Captain stops only NONCREATURE spells. Resolve protection before exposing the decisive Aura or Ballista when possible; opponents can respond to the protection itself.

Pact of Negation

Free protection for a turn you expect to win. If the attempt fails, the next-upkeep payment is {3}{U}{U}; unlimited green from an earlier turn does not carry through cleanup to pay it. Force of Negation is free only on opponents' turns, so reserve three mana if you want it during your own attempt.

Veil of Summer

Protects against countering and blue/black targeting; it does not stop every removal color or activated ability. Flusterstorm only counters instants and sorceries. Dovin's Veto, Swan Song and An Offer You Can't Refuse cover different spell types; check the actual threat rather than counting any counterspell as universal protection.

+
Nature's Claim + Boseiju, Who Endures

Remove activation hate, graveyard hate or an Aura answer before attempting a vulnerable line. Creature-activation hate stops Yuna, ordinary mana creatures and Ballista. Artifact-activation hate also stops Ballista, Cauldron and Staff. Swift makes Druid an artifact, so artifact hate matters there too. An available answer is part of the line, not something to hope to draw afterward.

Heroic Intervention

Helps against targeted removal and destruction, not sacrifice effects, a toughness reduction or most exile sweepers. Against removal aimed at Yuna, pivot to Heliod/Ballista or Druid when that is cheaper than paying commander tax again. Against graveyard exile, keep a direct hand/library route rather than relying entirely on Cauldron or recovery.

+
Rhystic Study + Sylvan Library

Play draw engines early enough to repay the tempo. Opponents may pay for Study, Remora and Sentinel, so no opening line assumes free cards from them. Library can keep two extra cards for eight life; preserve enough life to survive the table rather than buying every optional card automatically.
