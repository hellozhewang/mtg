Your experience counters belong to YOU, not to Meren, and they are not spent when she reanimates something. The important early play is to build mana and arrange useful creature deaths while she is present. Once established, keep turning the same cards into resources and answers until a mass reanimation or drain sequence ends the game.

Get value from the commander immediately
Meren of Clan Nel Toth

each OTHER creature of yours that dies gives you an experience counter, including tokens. At your end step, target a creature already in your graveyard; its mana value and your experience total determine whether it returns to the battlefield or to your hand.

Meren checks your experience when her end-step ability RESOLVES, so you can sacrifice creatures in response to reach the needed number. You must choose the target when the trigger goes on the stack; sacrificing a creature afterward does not let you change the target to that new card.

Meren still sees other creatures die alongside her in a sweeper. Removing her does not erase experience. Her recursion is once on YOUR end step, not once on each player's turn, so the independent recursion cards matter.

+
Sakura-Tribe Elder + Meren of Clan Nel Toth

sacrifice Elder after Meren resolves to gain experience and a tapped basic. Once you have two experience, the end-step return puts Elder straight back into play; before that, returning it to hand is still value.

+
Accursed Marauder + Meren of Clan Nel Toth

choose Marauder for your own required nontoken sacrifice. You gain experience and can bring it back to make opponents sacrifice again. Opponents choose what they lose, but their tokens cannot satisfy this particular edict.

+
Cankerbloom + Meren of Clan Nel Toth

convert two experience into recurring artifact or enchantment removal. The proliferate mode can increase experience too: its sacrifice creates Meren's experience trigger above the activated ability, so that first counter can exist before proliferation resolves.

Build mana with expendable creatures
+++
Birds of Paradise + Elves of Deep Shadow + Llanowar Elves + Elvish Mystic

turn-one acceleration makes a turn-three Meren realistic. Preserve mana creatures until your lands or other engines can replace their output; experience alone is not worth missing your next play.

Deathrite Shaman

fetchlands supply early mana targets, while its other modes provide selective graveyard disruption or life swings. It cannot make mana without a land card to exile and is not a reliable turn-one ramp source in every opening hand.

+
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet

acceleration that survives most creature wipes and helps recast Meren after removal. Signet fixes the black and green pips that a colorless Ring cannot cover.

Simulacrum Shaper

a three-mana creature that ramps a basic on entering and draws when it dies. It is particularly efficient to return with Meren once you have three experience. The deck keeps six basics to support repeated searches by Shaper and Elder.

Primal Witchstalker

mills four, then returns a land from ANYWHERE in your graveyard tapped. A fetchland already there supplies a target even if the four milled cards contain no land. Reanimating it repeats both setup and ramp.

Wight of the Reliquary

trades another creature for any land, tapped, while adding experience if Meren is out. Its tap ability needs to be free of summoning sickness; returning it with Meren does not immediately enable another activation.

+
Gaea's Cradle + Phyrexian Tower

Cradle rewards keeping a real board before sacrificing it; Tower turns one creature into two black mana. Tap Cradle before shrinking your army when planning a large turn.

+
Warren Soultrader + Ashnod's Altar

Soultrader converts another creature and one life into a Treasure for colored mana; it cannot sacrifice itself. Altar sacrifices any of your creatures for two colorless mana without costing life. Use both to fund reanimation, but distinguish generic costs from the black pips you still need to pay.

Always have a way to sacrifice
+
Viscera Seer + Carrion Feeder

cheap, repeatable sacrifice outlets allow you to respond to exile removal, stock the graveyard before Living Death, or turn removal aimed at an expendable creature into experience. Feeder cannot block, so Seer's smaller body is sometimes more useful defensively.

Umbral Collar Zealot

sacrifices OTHER creatures or artifacts to surveil, improving both draws and graveyard setup. It can dispose of a Treasure without spending it for mana; that does not count as a creature death.

Yawgmoth, Thran Physician

turns another creature and one life into a card and an optional -1/-1 counter. Token fodder becomes removal and draw. His protection from Humans does not apply to his card in the graveyard, so Meren can reanimate him normally.

Ophiomancer

a Snake every upkeep is possible only if you control no Snakes when the upkeep begins AND when the trigger resolves. Sacrifice the existing Snake before the next upkeep if you want a replacement. Four players can give you four bodies over a full round.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

at your end step, sacrifice a disposable permanent to pressure opponents' resources or draw cards and drain life. They choose whether to sacrifice, so three cards are possible, not guaranteed. You can order Braids and Meren's triggers, but Meren's target must already be in the graveyard.

Stitcher's Supplier

mill three on entering and three on dying while adding experience and supplying sacrifice material. Blind milling can lose access to a useful spell, so use targeted graveyard setup when you need a specific answer immediately.

Draw cards and recover without Meren
++
Skullclamp + Ophiomancer + Sylvan Library

Clamp turns the 1/1 Snake into two cards and clears the Snake condition before the next upkeep. Library provides early selection without requiring a creature; its optional life payments can later be offset by your drain creatures. Clamp also cashes in one-toughness mana creatures once you no longer need their mana.

Grim Haruspex

other NONTOKEN creatures dying draw cards without spending life. Snake and Army tokens do not trigger it, and Haruspex does not draw for its own death.

Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel

entering or attacking can sacrifice another creature for a card, while creature deaths drain one targeted opponent. On the fourth resolution of his death trigger in a turn, he transforms and grants an emblem that persists after he leaves. Each trigger or emblem drains one opponent, not all opponents.

Six

gives nonland permanent cards in your graveyard retrace during your turn, letting you discard a land and pay their normal costs to cast them. It also recovers noncreature engines that Meren cannot return. Its attack mills and may return a land from those milled cards to your hand.

Eternal Witness

recovers a protection spell, tutor, Living Death, or a destroyed engine. Returning Witness to recover the right spell can be stronger than reanimating a large creature.

++
Reanimate + Animate Dead + Necromancy

efficient independent reanimation makes Meren optional on an important turn. All three can take creatures from opponents' graveyards. Reanimate costs life equal to mana value; the enchantments remain linked to their reanimated creatures. Necromancy can be cast at instant speed, but doing so outside sorcery timing makes it temporary through the next cleanup.

Victimize

sacrifice one creature to return two creatures already in your graveyard tapped. Choose the targets before sacrificing, so the sacrificed creature cannot be one of those targets.

Dread Return

four-mana reanimation from hand or a flashback that sacrifices three creatures instead of paying mana. The flashback can generate three experience counters, but it consumes actual board resources and exiles the spell afterward.

Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt

a land early and a late multi-creature recovery spell. Each target must have a different mana value. It cannot return Gray Merchant and Syr Konrad together because both cost five.

Cauldron of Essence

drains EACH opponent when your creature dies and also trades a creature for a reanimation at sorcery speed. Its activated ability costs three mana and a tap; you cannot use it as a free instant-speed sacrifice outlet or target the creature you are about to sacrifice.

Avacyn, Angel of Horror

the high-end reanimation target that returns herself and your other nontoken creatures after they die. Deaths before an end step return at its beginning; deaths after that beginning wait until the NEXT end step. Tokens and exiled creatures do not return through this ability.

Twilight Diviner

surveils on entry and copies one other creature entering from, or cast from, a graveyard once EACH turn. A mass return still gives just one copy. A copy of a legendary creature invokes the legend rule, which can be useful with Kokusho.

Recycle answers while advancing the board
Plaguecrafter

pressures creatures or planeswalkers, and makes an opponent discard if they cannot sacrifice either. You also sacrifice, usually Plaguecrafter itself. Opponents can sacrifice a token to this one.

Grave Pact

converts every death of one of your creatures into another sacrifice for each opponent. Feed it expendable bodies to clear blockers or attack protected creatures indirectly. This demands fodder and does not prevent opponents from casting spells or rebuilding.

Haywire Mite

a one-mana body that sacrifices to EXILE a noncreature artifact or enchantment. It gains life when it dies and is easy for Meren to recur. It cannot target an artifact creature or an enchantment currently functioning as a creature.

Orcish Bowmasters

flash removal for small utility creatures, pressure on extra draws, and an Army you can sacrifice. Sacrificing the Army before a later amass resolution creates a fresh body; simply adding counters to an existing Army is not another creature entering.

+
Endurance + Bojuka Bog

creature-based and land-based graveyard interaction prepare a safer Living Death or stop an opposing graveyard plan. Endurance puts a chosen graveyard under its owner's library; Bog exiles an opponent's graveyard when you target them.

+
Assassin's Trophy + Abrupt Decay

preserve instant-speed answers for graveyard hate and other effects that switch off recursion. Trophy covers any permanent but can give a basic; Decay is uncounterable but limited to nonland permanents costing three or less.

Boseiju, Who Endures

channel removes a troublesome artifact, enchantment, or nonbasic land without casting a spell. The opponent can receive a land with a basic land type. This is targeted interaction, not a repeated land-denial plan.

+
Deadly Rollick + Toxic Deluge

Rollick exiles a creature and is free with Meren on the battlefield; Deluge handles large or indestructible creature boards by reducing toughness. Deluge can kill your team too, and indestructible will not prevent that.

Spore Frog

a reusable emergency combat shield. One sacrifice prevents combat damage for the current turn only. Meren's single end-step return does not protect you through all three opponents' turns by itself, and Frog does not stop noncombat damage.

+
Heroic Intervention + Veil of Summer

protect the engine against destruction and targeted interference. Veil also prevents your spells being countered for the turn, but its hexproof applies only against blue and black sources; neither card is a universal answer to graveyard exile.

Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire

let the spell resolve before sacrificing its target, then leave the creature card in the graveyard so it can return. It also supplies a tapped black land when needed. This does not protect a creature from being exiled instead of dying.

Turn accumulated deaths into a win
+
Zulaport Cutthroat + Cauldron of Essence

make every creature death drain all opponents while replenishing your life. Cutthroat sees itself die; Cauldron must be on the battlefield when the deaths happen.

+
Gray Merchant of Asphodel + Twilight Diviner

reanimating Merchant can create a second Merchant, adding both another drain trigger and two more black devotion. Resolve the Diviner copy first when that improves the original Merchant trigger's devotion count.

+
Kokusho, the Evening Star + Twilight Diviner

a reanimated Kokusho produces a legendary copy; keep the original and let the token die to the legend rule for a table drain of five. Sacrifice the original afterward for another five, then recur it later.

Massacre Wurm

punishes token boards, and repeated entrances can finish larger creatures by stacking -2/-2 effects during the SAME turn. Each opposing creature death costs its controller two life while Wurm sees it die.

Syr Konrad, the Grim

rewards creature deaths, creature cards being milled, and creature cards leaving YOUR graveyard. His mill ability is a useful mana sink. He does not count himself dying, and ordinary opposing reanimation does not trigger the own-graveyard clause.

Living Death

sacrifice creatures you want back BEFORE this resolves, because the spell exiles graveyards first and only then sacrifices the battlefield. Creatures sacrificed by the spell itself are not in its return batch. Clear a dangerous opposing graveyard first when necessary.

+
Syr Konrad, the Grim + Living Death

Konrad already on the battlefield sees creature cards leave your graveyard during the first instruction and other creatures die during the sacrifice instruction. A Konrad returned by the spell was absent for those events and does not trigger retroactively.

+
Living Death + Massacre Wurm

Wurm returning alongside opposing creatures can shrink that returned army after the spell resolves. A Wurm returned this way did not see the earlier sacrifice instruction; count only the deaths it actually witnesses.

+
Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest + Warren Soultrader

sacrificing a creature puts counters on your surviving team; sacrificing the resulting Treasure triggers Mazirek again. Opponents' sacrifices and everyone's fetchland sacrifices also grow your army. Creatures that leave and return lose their old counters.

Finale of Devastation

a flexible creature tutor early and a combat finish at X=10 or more, costing at least twelve total mana. The large mode gives the team haste and +X/+X but NOT trample, so clear blockers with edicts or a sweeper and count actual lethal attacks.

+
Avacyn, Angel of Horror + Kokusho, the Evening Star

sacrifice Kokusho before an end step, return it through Avacyn, then sacrifice it again. Its new death schedules the next end step's return, letting a developed engine drain across players' turns without an infinite loop.

Tutor for the missing role
→
Survival of the Fittest → Sakura-Tribe Elder

exchange an expensive creature card for early ramp when your mana is behind. The discarded creature becomes a later reanimation target.

→
Survival of the Fittest → Haywire Mite

fetch an answer when a noncreature artifact or enchantment is preventing your graveyard plan. The search puts it in hand, so you still need mana to cast and activate it.

→
Entomb → Kokusho, the Evening Star

put a drain finisher directly in the graveyard only when a reanimation spell or sufficient Meren experience can use it. Entomb can instead find a small utility creature when that is what the position needs.

→++
Buried Alive → Gray Merchant of Asphodel + Kokusho, the Evening Star + Massacre Wurm

stock a late-game Living Death with complementary drain and creature-removal threats. Against graveyard exile, avoid investing all your threats into one unprotected graveyard.

→
Diabolic Intent → Living Death

cash in an expendable creature for the mass recovery spell when your graveyard is already stronger than opponents'. If the graveyard is not ready, search for the missing engine or an immediate answer.

→
Eldritch Evolution → Avacyn, Angel of Horror

sacrifice a six-mana creature such as Kokusho to search for Avacyn and put her onto the battlefield. Kokusho's death pays you immediately; Avacyn did not witness that earlier death, so use another recursion effect to recover Kokusho.

→
Birthing Pod → Twilight Diviner

sacrifice a two-mana creature to find Diviner. Pod requires EXACTLY one greater mana value, only activates at sorcery speed, and cannot turn any small body directly into a finisher.

→
Wight of the Reliquary → Gaea's Cradle

find Cradle when you have enough creatures left after paying Wight's sacrifice cost to make it profitable. Find Bojuka Bog instead when a graveyard needs answering immediately.

What to keep in an opening hand and how to sequence

Keep three mana sources or a sound two-land-plus-ramp hand, a cheap creature, and either draw, a sacrifice outlet, or a relevant answer. A hand of large reanimation targets without a way to put them in the graveyard needs a mulligan. Cradle alone is not a functional starting mana source without creatures.

Aim to develop mana on turns one and two, then cast Meren around turn three with something useful ready to die. Sacrificing your only mana creature just to gain experience can leave you unable to use the cards Meren recovers; prefer Elder, a token, or a creature already threatened.

Fetch the colors needed for your next plays. The typed duals support your seven fetchlands; six basics support recurring Elder and Shaper. A tapped surveil land is useful when it does not cost you the turn's play. Keep black access for the later double- and triple-black spells.

Before ending your turn, choose the exact creature you want Meren to target and make sure it is already in the graveyard. If you need more experience, get the remaining deaths before her trigger resolves. Order Braids and Meren deliberately rather than treating the whole end step as simultaneous.

Keep a sacrifice outlet and an independent recovery card available when possible. Exile-based graveyard hate is the main structural weakness: save an answer, avoid dumping every threat into the graveyard at once, and use normal creature casting and Mazirek combat when recursion is shut off.

The deck is built to establish control and finish a developed game, approximately turns six through eight on a good draw. It has no designed infinite combo, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain. Once you can win with a drain or a large attack, take that line instead of spending several more turns repeating removal.
