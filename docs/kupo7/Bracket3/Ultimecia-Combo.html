A Bracket 3 combo deck led by Ultimecia, Time Sorceress. The single biggest mistake is paying eight mana to transform her too early before your combo lines or protection are in place. Ultimecia's real strength is acting as a surveil-fueled graveyard engine from the command zone, sculpting your hand and yard to assemble deterministic infinite extra turns, infinite mana blinks, or instant-speed mill locks. Bracket 3 rules: 3/3 Game Changers (Demonic Tutor, Cyclonic Rift, The One Ring), no mass land denial, and two-card combos are held until turn 6.

Infinite turns and time loops
++
Archaeomancer + Time Warp + Thassa, Deep-Dwelling

cast Time Warp for an extra turn; at end step, Thassa flickers Archaeomancer to return Time Warp from your graveyard to your hand. Repeat on every extra turn for infinite turns, winning through combat damage with Ultimecia, Omnipotent.

++
Shipwreck Dowser + Temporal Manipulation + Thassa, Deep-Dwelling

identical infinite turn loop with Dowser and Manipulation; Thassa flickers Dowser at end step to return Manipulation for the next turn.

+
Peregrine Drake + Deadeye Navigator

soulbond Navigator and Drake, pay {1}{U} to flicker Drake, untapping five lands. Floats three mana per loop for infinite mana and blinks. Soulbond to Archaeomancer to recur all spells, or to Ultimecia to surveil your entire library into your graveyard.

++
Peregrine Drake + Ghostly Flicker + Archaeomancer

cast Ghostly Flicker targeting Drake and Archaeomancer; Drake untaps five lands and Archaeomancer returns Flicker to hand. Loops for infinite colored mana, then targets Ultimecia to mill your deck.

The mill and drain locks
+
Duskmantle Guildmage + Mindcrank

activate Guildmage's first ability for {1}{U}{B}; whenever a card enters an opponent's graveyard or an opponent loses life, Mindcrank mills that many cards and Guildmage drains that player for 1 life, creating an unstoppable damage and mill loop.

+
Bloodchief Ascension + Mindcrank

with three quest counters on Ascension, any card put into an opponent's graveyard causes them to lose 2 life and you gain 2 life; Mindcrank mills 2 cards, looping until every opponent is dead.

Duskmantle Guildmage

its second ability {2}{U}{B} mills two cards on demand, serving as an instant self-contained starter for the Mindcrank loop with no outside combat required.

Commander transformation and Final Fantasy engines
Ultimecia, Time Sorceress // Ultimecia, Omnipotent

surveils 2 on entry and attack to fuel the graveyard with spells and combo pieces; at end step, pay {4}{U}{U}{B}{B} and exile eight cards to transform into Ultimecia, Omnipotent for an extra turn.

Thassa, Deep-Dwelling

flickers Ultimecia, Omnipotent at end step to reset her to her front face, re-triggering surveil 2 and enabling future transform triggers.

Y'shtola Rhul

flickers a creature and creates an additional end step, allowing Ultimecia to enter on her front face in the first end step and trigger her transform in the second.

Matoya, Archon Elder

turns every Ultimecia entry and attack trigger (surveil 2) into raw card draw.

Emet-Selch, Unsundered // Hades, Sorcerer of Eld

loots on entry and attack, then transforms into Hades, Sorcerer of Eld to cast spells directly from your graveyard.

Dreams of Laguna

surveils and draws for two mana, with flashback to double-dip on graveyard velocity.

Displacer Kitten

blinks nonland permanents whenever you cast a noncreature spell, resetting The One Ring burden counters or flickering Archaeomancer.

Protecting the combo
The One Ring

indestructible protection from everything for a full turn cycle, buying time to assemble combo pieces while drawing deep into interaction.

Force of Negation

free interaction against opposing disruption during opponents' turns.

Pact of Negation

zero-mana counterspell to guarantee your combo turn resolves without interruption.

Mana Drain

counters a key threat and funnels colorless mana into casting Deadeye Navigator, extra turn spells, or activating Ultimecia.

Swan Song

one-mana hard counter for instants, sorceries, and enchantments targeting your combo pieces.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one-mana noncreature counterspell to defend against board wipes and removal.

Deadly Rollick

free instant exile with Ultimecia on the board.

Cyclonic Rift

clears the entire opposing board overloaded at instant speed before untapping for the win.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two to three lands, early mana rocks, and selection or a tutor. Ultimecia does not need to be rushed out on turn 5; prioritize establishing mana and sculpting your graveyard.

Do not cast two-card combo lines before turn 6 under Bracket 3 table expectations. Use turns 1 through 5 to set up mana rocks, cast The One Ring, deploy Ultimecia to surveil, and hold up countermagic.

If a combo piece gets milled by Ultimecia's surveil, do not panic: Archaeomancer, Shipwreck Dowser, Reanimate, and Animate Dead are specifically built to pull pieces back from the graveyard.

Always hold at least one counterspell (Pact of Negation, Swan Song, or Mana Drain) when attempting to resolve Deadeye Navigator, Peregrine Drake, or Mindcrank.
