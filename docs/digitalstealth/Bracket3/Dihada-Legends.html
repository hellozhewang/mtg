The biggest mistake: greedy hoarding with Dihada's -3

The single most common mistake with Dihada is putting every revealed legendary card into your hand off her -3 ability. Her ability reads: "Reveal the top four cards of your library. Put any number of legendary cards from among them into your hand and the rest into your graveyard. Create a Treasure token for each card put into your graveyard this way."

Inexperienced pilots treat this as a pure card-draw ability and hoard every revealed legend. But Dihada is equally a burst-mana ritual and graveyard setup engine. If you reveal heavy bombs like Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite or Gisela, Blade of Goldnight when you lack the mana to hardcast them, keeping them in hand starves you of Treasures, leaves Dihada vulnerable at 2 loyalty, and strands expensive cards in your grip. Binning them generates up to four immediate Treasures, giving you the exact mana required to cast Reanimate, Animate Dead, or follow-up spells on the very turn Dihada lands. Treat her -3 as an on-demand ritual: keep only what you can cast immediately or need to assemble a win, and feed the rest to the graveyard for mana and future mass reanimation.

The commander: loyalty sequencing and role

Dihada costs {1}{R}{W}{B} and enters with 5 loyalty:

* **The +2 ability:** Targets up to one legendary creature, granting it vigilance, lifelink, and indestructible until your next turn. Use this to turn your utility legends (such as Shanid, Sleepers' Scourge, Sheoldred, the Apocalypse, or Drana and Linvala) into brick walls that attack freely, gain life, and survive combat and sweepers.

* **The -3 ability:** Mills four cards, selectively draws legendary cards, and generates 0 to 4 Treasures. On an empty or developing board, you will almost always activate the -3 first to generate Treasures, ramp into your next play, and fill your graveyard.

* **The -11 ultimate:** Steals all nonland permanents, untaps them, and grants them haste. In grindy games where Dihada ticks up repeatedly behind indestructible blockers, this ultimate immediately wins the game on resolution.

The primary win condition: Underworld Breach infinite loops

The deck's primary compact combo utilizes Underworld Breach alongside Dihada and a reset outlet (Flicker or Heart of Kiran) to generate infinite Treasures, infinite mill, and infinite storm:

1. **The Flicker Loop:**

* *Setup:* Dihada and Underworld Breach on the battlefield. Flicker in hand or graveyard.

* *Loop:* Activate Dihada's -3, sending all 4 revealed cards to the graveyard to create 4 Treasure tokens. Spend 2 Treasures for {1}{W} to cast Flicker from your graveyard via escape, exiling 3 other cards. Flicker targets Dihada; she exiles and returns to the battlefield as a new object with 5 fresh loyalty.

* *Result:* Each iteration mills 4 cards and consumes 3 cards for escape, netting +1 card in your graveyard and +2 Treasures. Repeat until your entire library is in your graveyard with dozens of Treasures.

* *The Finish:* Escape Mayhem Devil into play. Continue looping Flicker or sacrificing Treasures to machine-gun all opponents down with direct damage triggers.

2. **The Heart of Kiran Recast Loop:**

* *Setup:* Dihada, Underworld Breach, and Heart of Kiran on the battlefield.

* *Loop:* Activate Dihada's -3, milling 4 cards and creating 4 Treasures. Activate Heart of Kiran's loyalty-crew ability by removing 1 loyalty counter from Dihada, dropping her to 0 loyalty and sending her to the graveyard. Use the 4 Treasures to recast Dihada from your graveyard via escape (exiling 3 milled cards).

* *Result:* Infinite casts, infinite mill, infinite storm, and an infinite graveyard. Win via Mayhem Devil pings or mass legendary reanimation.

The backup win condition: legendary reanimation and combat beats

If Underworld Breach is exiled or answered, the deck effortlessly pivots to a devastating Mardu Legends reanimator plan:

* **Primevals' Glorious Rebirth:** At {5}{W}{B}, this legendary sorcery returns all legendary permanent cards from your graveyard to the battlefield at once. Resolving this after a few Dihada -3 activations dumps 10+ legendary creatures, planeswalkers, and artifacts (The One Ring, Mox Amber, Heroes' Podium, Shadowspear) onto the board simultaneously.

* **Urza's Ruinous Blast:** Cast for {4}{W} while controlling Dihada or any legend to exile all nonland permanents that aren't legendary. Because nearly your entire creature base is legendary, this functions as an asymmetrical board wipe that strips opponents of all creatures, tokens, mana rocks, and enchantments while leaving your board completely untouched.

* **Overwhelming Combat Output:** Gisela, Blade of Goldnight doubles your damage and halves incoming damage. Aurelia, the Warleader and Karlach, Fury of Avernus grant additional combat phases. Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite buffs your army while wiping out small opposing creatures. Combined with Shanid, Sleepers' Scourge granting menace and Dihada granting indestructible and lifelink, your combat attacks are lethal and unblockable.

Key interactions and rules quirks

* **Cadric, Soul Kindler:** Whenever another nontoken legendary permanent enters under your control, you may pay {1}. If you do, create a token copy that gains haste and is NOT legendary, then sacrifice it at end of turn. When Dihada enters with Cadric out, pay {1} to make a nonlegendary Dihada token! The token has 5 loyalty, can immediately -3 to mill four more cards and make four more Treasures, generating explosive mana and card advantage on turn four.

* **Ratadrabik of Urborg:** Whenever another legendary creature you control dies, Ratadrabik creates a 2/2 black Zombie token copy that is not legendary. This protects your legendary creatures from board wipes and lets you trigger enter-the-battlefield abilities twice.

* **Relic of Legends:** Taps for any color, and allows you to tap any untapped legendary creature you control to add one mana of any color. Turns every legendary threat into a mana dork, generating massive mana bursts on the turn you reanimate multiple legends.

* **Drana and Linvala:** Shuts off all activated abilities of creatures your opponents control while granting all of those abilities to Drana and Linvala.

* **Chandra, Acolyte of Flame:** Her -2 ability allows you to recast an instant or sorcery with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard (such as Flicker, Demonic Tutor, Reanimate, or Boros Charm).

* **Plaza of Heroes:** Legendary land that produces mana of any color among legendary permanents you control. Its activated ability can exile Plaza of Heroes to give target legendary permanent hexproof and indestructible until end of turn, protecting Dihada from removal.

Mulligans and opening hands

Look for hands with:

* 2–3 lands with all three Mardu colors (Savai Triome, Blood Crypt, Godless Shrine, Sacred Foundry, Badlands, Plateau, Scrubland, or fetchlands).

* Early acceleration (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Talisman of Hierarchy, Talisman of Conviction, Talisman of Indulgence, Mox Amber) to guarantee a Turn 3 Dihada.

* At least one cheap utility legend or draw engine (Esper Sentinel, Skrelv, Defector Mite, Loran of the Third Path, Kambal, Consul of Allocation, Shanid, Sleepers' Scourge).

* One reanimation piece or tutor (Reanimate, Animate Dead, Demonic Tutor).

Ship hands with multiple 6+ mana bombs (Elesh Norn, Gisela, Kaervek) and no fast mana or reanimation enablers. You want Dihada on the board by turn 3 or 4 to start generating Treasures and filling your graveyard.

Combos and deterministic loops

1. **Dihada + Underworld Breach + Flicker:**

* *Prerequisites:* Dihada, Binder of Wills and Underworld Breach on the battlefield. Flicker in hand or graveyard.

* *Loop:* Activate Dihada's -3, sending all 4 cards to the graveyard and creating 4 Treasures. Spend 2 Treasures to escape Flicker targeting Dihada, exiling 3 cards from your graveyard. Dihada flickers and resets to 5 loyalty.

* *Result:* Infinite Treasures, infinite mill, infinite storm count. Execute after turn 6 in Bracket 3.

2. **Dihada + Underworld Breach + Heart of Kiran:**

* *Prerequisites:* Dihada, Binder of Wills, Underworld Breach, and Heart of Kiran on the battlefield.

* *Loop:* Activate Dihada's -3 for 4 Treasures. Remove 1 loyalty counter from Dihada to crew Heart of Kiran, sending Dihada to the graveyard. Spend 4 Treasures to escape Dihada from your graveyard, exiling 3 cards.

* *Result:* Infinite storm, infinite mill, infinite death triggers. Win with Mayhem Devil triggers.

Card-specific notes

* **Mox Amber:** Costs {0} and taps for Red, White, or Black as long as you control a legendary creature or planeswalker. Dihada can put Mox Amber directly into your hand off her -3 ability for immediate free mana.

* **The One Ring:** Can be put into hand off Dihada's -3. Gives you protection from everything until your next turn and draws massive cards, easily kept alive by Dihada's lifelink buffs.

* **Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose:** Whenever you gain life, target opponent loses that much life. When Dihada gives a huge attacker (Gisela, Elesh Norn, Sheoldred) lifelink and indestructible, Vito mirrors that combat damage as direct life loss to an opponent.

* **Urza's Ruinous Blast & Yawgmoth's Vile Offering:** Both are legendary sorceries and can be put into your hand off Dihada's -3. They require a legendary creature or planeswalker on your battlefield to cast, which Dihada naturally satisfies.

* **Kardur, Doomscourge:** Goads all opponents' creatures on ETB. In multiplayer games, this forces opponents to attack each other and tap out, keeping Dihada completely safe from attacks.

* **Tibalt's Trickery:** Hard counterspell in Red that stops opposing game-ending spells, board wipes, or counter-wars.

* **Shizo, Death's Storehouse:** Gives target legendary creature fear for {B}, letting your biggest legend swing past nonblack blockers with Dihada's lifelink and vigilance.
