Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Your goal is to keep mana open and turn useful spells on opponents' turns into an evasive army. Alela rewards your first spell on each opponent's turn, not every spell. Do not spend a needed counter just to make a token: use a cantrip or flash creature when there is nothing important to answer.

This Bracket 3 build uses Rhystic Study, Cyclonic Rift, and Fierce Guardianship as its three Game Changers. It wins through combat, a single Notorious Throng extra turn, and Obyra's drain. There are no built-in infinite combos, extra-turn recursion engines, or mass land denial.

Build a board while leaving answers available

Alela, Cunning Conqueror

Each first spell during an opponent's turn gives a black Faerie Rogue, even if that spell is later countered. With three opponents, that is up to three tokens per round. Alela must be present when you cast that turn's first spell; entering later does not make your next spell count as the first.

Bender's Waterskin

Untaps during each other player's untap step, giving you a fresh colored mana for interaction or a cantrip on every opponent's turn. It supports the commander's rhythm especially well.

Arcane Signet

Along with Talisman of Dominance and Dimir Signet, helps cast Alela while still leaving colored mana for interaction on a later turn. Dimir Signet needs another mana source to activate.

Sol Ring

Pays generic costs on Alela, your engines, and finishers. It cannot pay the blue requirements on Counterspell or Mana Drain, so a Sol Ring hand still needs real colored sources.

Mind Stone

Early acceleration that can be cashed in for a card later. Its draw activation is not a spell and therefore cannot make an Alela token.

Patchwork Banner

Choose Faerie for both a mana source and a one-sided anthem. It is a better developmental play than committing your large finishing artifact before you can attack.

Bitterblossom

Makes black Faerie Rogues even when Alela is unavailable, sustaining the combat and draw engines. Its life loss is compulsory; do not ignore accumulated costs from the rest of the deck.

Bitterbloom Bearer

Flash gives you a creature to deploy when no interaction is needed, then upkeep tokens provide another independent source of Faeries. Its blue-and-black tokens are not Rogues, unlike Alela's and Bitterblossom's.

High Fae Trickster

Lets you deploy your slower engines and Coat of Arms on opponents' turns. Playing it on the final opponent's end step lets you untap with it, but remember to leave mana for protection if the table is holding removal.

Oona's Blackguard

Future Alela tokens enter with counters because they are Rogues, making them larger and forcing discards when they connect. It does not add counters to existing tokens, to Alela, or to Bitterbloom Bearer's non-Rogue tokens.

Refill faster than you trade cards

Rhystic Study

Keeps the answers flowing while opponents develop. Deploy it when the mana spent will not expose you to an immediate loss; it is the dedicated card-advantage Game Changer.

Mystic Remora

Efficient early draw against noncreature spells. Let it expire when cumulative upkeep would stop you casting Alela or interacting; keeping it forever is not the goal.

Voracious Tome-Skimmer

Every spell you cast on an opponent's turn can replace itself for one life, including second and third spells that no longer trigger Alela. Being a Faerie also lets it attack, benefit from anthems, and count toward your tribal draw.

Wavebreak Hippocamp

Adds a card to your first spell on each opponent's turn without a life payment. It is not a Faerie, so it misses tribal bonuses and is bounced by Raise the Palisade when you choose Faerie.

Nymris, Oona's Trickster

A flash blocker and selection engine for the same first-spell pattern. It does not trigger on its own casting. Choose carefully before putting a finisher into the graveyard; the deck cannot recover its spent sorceries.

Faerie Mastermind

Draws when an opponent draws their second card in a turn. Arcane Denial's two-card compensation can trigger it. The four-mana group-draw activation is a late mana sink, not a spell for Alela.

Talion, the Kindly Lord

Draw and drain without requiring combat or the commander. Choose a number based on visible decks; two is often useful but is not automatically correct for every table.

Enduring Curiosity

Flash it in before combat damage when your attackers are getting through, then draw for each creature that connects. After it dies as a creature, its enchantment form survives future creature-only wipes.

Reconnaissance Mission

Turns several small evasive hits into several cards. Cycling can rescue a poor hand, but cycling is an activated ability and does not trigger Alela.

Kindred Discovery

Name Faerie to draw from both creature entries and attacks, without needing combat damage. A large Notorious Throng makes a draw trigger for every new token; count your remaining library before committing.

Harmonized Crescendo

Instant-speed mass draw that counts Faerie permanents, including Bitterblossom. Black Alela tokens can convoke its generic cost, but cannot pay its two blue symbols; blue creatures and blue-and-black tokens can.

Consult the Star Charts

Uses your land count to find the answer or finisher you need. Kick it late for two cards when you can still preserve necessary interaction.

Brainstorm

A cheap opponent-turn spell that can put excess lands or situational cards back, then a fetchland can shuffle them away. Consider and Opt provide additional low-cost plays when the table gives you nothing worth countering.

Frantic Search

Filters your hand and untaps lands to keep playing later in the round. On its own it costs a card overall; your Alela and draw triggers supply the additional value that makes it worthwhile.

Counter the threats that matter

Spellstutter Sprite

If it is your first opponent-turn spell, Alela's token arrives before the Sprite resolves. The Sprite then enters and counts your Faeries when its counter ability resolves, including itself and the new token. Bitterblossom also counts as a Faerie permanent.

Fierce Guardianship

Protects a resolved Alela and your board from noncreature answers without spending mana. It is not free while Alela is still on the stack, and it cannot stop creature spells.

Force of Negation

Another way to protect the table during opponents' turns while tapped out, at the cost of a blue card. Its alternate cost is unavailable on your own turn, so budget mana to protect your finish.

Counterspell

The clean unconditional answer. Save it for threats that your more restricted counters cannot handle rather than countering ordinary value plays.

Mana Drain

Counters a critical spell and funds your next main phase. Its colorless mana can help cast Coat of Arms, Kindred Discovery, or a prowled Notorious Throng, but disappears if you leave the phase without using it.

Arcane Denial

Easier on colors and replaces itself, but gives the opponent cards. Prefer it when protecting your engine or stopping a serious threat justifies the compensation.

Swan Song

One mana stops many removal spells, wipes, and dangerous enchantments. The Bird can block a small Faerie, so plan your next attack rather than treating the replacement as irrelevant.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

Stops a noncreature threat cheaply but gives its controller two Treasures. Avoid using it on harmless setup spells and accelerating the next dangerous play.

Spider-Sense

Counters an instant, sorcery, or triggered ability. Web-slinging can return a tapped nontoken attacker for a one-mana cast and later reuse its enter ability; returning a token makes the token disappear. Returning a creature this way does not target, so shroud does not stop the cost.

Unwind

Answers a noncreature spell and restores three lands if it resolves, allowing another response later in the round. You still need the initial three mana.

Glen Elendra's Answer

An uncounterable answer to the opponents' spells and abilities currently on the stack, including triggered abilities that ordinary counters miss. It does not stop future spells that turn and does not counter your own Alela trigger.

Glen Elendra Archmage

Presents two potential noncreature counters through persist, with one blue mana needed for each activation. The activations do not make Alela tokens. Ordinary anthem bonuses do not remove its -1/-1 counter or reset persist.

Glen Elendra Guardian

Flash develops a Faerie while holding an answer for later. Removing its initial counter pays for one noncreature counter activation, which also gives the spell's controller a card. It can remove other kinds of counters too.

Remove the obstacle and keep attacking

Deadly Rollick

Free creature exile while Alela is present, preserving mana for further interaction. Unlike destruction, exile handles indestructible creatures and many death-value engines cleanly.

Pongify

One-mana creature removal with a ground token that your flyers can often ignore. It does not answer indestructible creatures; Reality Shift supplies another exile option.

Resculpt

Exiles an artifact or creature, giving Dimir a permanent answer to an artifact that bounce would only delay. The 4/4 replacement is meaningful; goad it after connecting with its controller if that helps.

Withering Torment

Destroys a creature or enchantment at instant speed, covering one of Dimir's usual gaps. Hold it for an enchantment if your hand already has plenty of creature answers.

Lethal Scheme

Lets untapped black Faerie tokens pay for removal and connive, conserving lands for counters. Newly created creatures can convoke, but the token from casting this spell arrives too late to pay for this same spell. A +1/+1 counter from connive can cancel the Archmage's -1/-1 counter and restore a future persist.

Nightmare Sower

Each opponent-turn spell can shrink a creature permanently, while lifelink offsets your draw engines' life costs. If it is worth the cost, put a -1/-1 counter on Glen Elendra Guardian to supply another counter activation; Scion's shroud prevents targeting the Guardian while Scion is present.

Snap

Removes a blocker or dangerous creature temporarily, then untaps two lands. It can reuse your own enter abilities when legal, but Scion of Oona prevents targeting your other Faeries.

Brazen Borrower // Petty Theft

Its Adventure can bounce an opposing nonland permanent on one opponent's turn, and the creature can be cast on a later opponent's turn for another Alela trigger. The creature can only block flyers.

Cling to Dust

One-mana graveyard interaction that either gains life from a creature card or draws from another card type. Escape makes it reusable when enough cards have accumulated in your graveyard, without recurring your extra-turn spell.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Exiles a graveyard card on entry and can clear all opponents' graveyards when one-card removal is insufficient. Its sacrifice abilities do not trigger Alela; cash it in for a card if graveyards are not relevant.

Cyclonic Rift

Overload on the last opponent's end step to clear nonland blockers and engines before your attack. Early, it can answer one immediate problem; do not refuse a necessary two-mana use just because the seven-mana mode is stronger.

Raise the Palisade

Choose Faerie to preserve your tribal creatures and clear most blockers. Opposing Faeries remain, and your Wavebreak Hippocamp and creature-form Enduring Curiosity return to hand.

Perplexing Test

Usually return nontoken creatures to preserve your token army. This also returns Alela and your creature lords, so recalculate token power. Its token mode clears opposing token boards but destroys your own tokens as well.

Turn the advantage into a finish

Coat of Arms

A finishing play, usually cast immediately before a decisive attack or with High Fae Trickster just before your turn. It boosts opposing creatures too, and sharing multiple types with one creature still counts that creature only once. Clear an opposing tribal board before relying on it.

Notorious Throng

Connect with a Rogue to unlock prowl, then spend six mana for tokens and one extra turn. Alela herself is a Warlock, not a Rogue; her tokens are Rogues. The new tokens can attack on that extra turn because you controlled them as that turn began. The four-mana normal cast creates tokens but grants no extra turn.

Obyra, Dreaming Duelist

Each new Faerie drains all three opponents, making Notorious Throng threaten life totals without another attack. Throng counts damage dealt to opponents that turn, not Obyra's life loss. If casting Obyra triggers Alela, the resulting token normally enters before Obyra and is not seen by her.

Scion of Oona

Flash anthem and protection for your other Faeries, including the Faerie enchantment Bitterblossom. It does not protect itself, and shroud stops your own targeted effects too. It cannot protect the board from an untargeted wipe.

Tegwyll, Duke of Splendor

Anthem and recovery insurance: other Faeries that die, including tokens, become cards at a life cost. It sees other Faeries dying simultaneously with it. A mass bounce or exile effect does not produce those death triggers, and a huge token wipe can cost dangerous amounts of life.

Alela's combat trigger rewards hitting different opponents: each damaged player can supply a goad trigger, rather than every individual Faerie supplying one. Use goad to send their biggest attackers elsewhere and make future attacks easier. With only one opponent remaining, goad no longer keeps their creatures from attacking you.

A clean finish is a protected attack, then prowled Notorious Throng, then an untap and enlarged second attack. Coat of Arms can accelerate that line, while Obyra can convert the token burst directly into life loss. This deck does not repeatedly recur Throng.

Find the right resource

Faerie Harbinger

Flash tutor to the top of your library for a Faerie card: Scion for protection, Tegwyll for recovery, Spellstutter Sprite for interaction, or Bitterblossom for rebuilding. With Kindred Discovery already present, resolve the Harbinger's tutor trigger before its enter-draw trigger to draw the searched card.

Waterlogged Teachings // Inundated Archive

A land when you need mana; otherwise an instant tutor for an instant or a card that actually has flash. High Fae Trickster's permission does not let it find every card. Notorious Throng and Coat of Arms are not valid targets.

Three Tree City

Name Faerie. Its activated mana scales with the army and can fund a finisher while preserving colored lands for counters, but requires two other mana to activate and is only a colorless land when your board is small.

Minamo, School at Water's Edge provides untapped blue mana and can untap Three Tree City for a second activation. With five or more Faeries, this produces more mana than simply using Minamo for mana alongside one City activation, provided you can fund the initial costs. Minamo can also untap a legendary attacker to block, but Scion of Oona's shroud prevents targeting your other Faeries. Neither land activation triggers Alela.

Cavern of Souls

Usually name Faerie to protect Alela and support creatures from counters. Its restricted colored mana cannot cast your instant answers, so it is not a substitute for an unrestricted blue source.

Takenuma, Abandoned Mire

Channel can recover a destroyed Faerie or another creature engine, but cannot recover Notorious Throng. Channel is an ability, so it neither triggers Alela nor uses normal spell-countering rules.

What to keep and how to sequence it

Keep three lands, access to blue and black, and either a two-mana rock or a cheap draw engine, plus useful interaction. A hand of expensive engines and finishers without early plays is a mulligan. Two lands are defensible with cheap selection and acceleration, but missing Alela's fourth mana is costly.

An example keep is Polluted Delta, Underground River, Island, Talisman of Dominance, Consider, Counterspell, and Voracious Tome-Skimmer. Establish mana, use the first draw or interaction opportunity that matters, then deploy Alela when you can afford the table's likely response.

The mana base has 35 dedicated lands plus Sink into Stupor and Waterlogged Teachings as optional lands. Four basics provide fetchable basics and targets for opposing basic-land search effects; the remaining sources prioritize duals and fixing. River of Tears normally makes blue on opponents' turns, but makes black after your land drop on your own turn. Mana Confluence and City of Brass provide either color immediately and independently; use painless sources first when possible because their life costs add to your draw engines and token makers. City of Brass also damages you when another effect taps it.

Underground Sea, Watery Grave, and Undercity Sewers are the three fetchable duals. Fetch Undercity Sewers when you can afford a tapped land, often late in the final opponent's turn; prioritize an untapped source when you need to respond. Preserve fetchlands when your colors are already secure to shuffle away cards from Brainstorm. This mana base emphasizes untapped interaction over extra graveyard utility; keep Cling to Dust or Soul-Guide Lantern available when graveyards matter.

In multiplayer, trade one-for-one only when the threat affects your engine, creates an immediate loss, or prevents your planned finish. Let opponents spend their own removal when possible. Use a cantrip at an opponent's end step if you still want your first-spell trigger and no useful response was needed.

Try to hold enough mana for one useful spell on each opponent's turn, with Bender's Waterskin, Snap, and Unwind stretching the budget. Keep some Faeries untapped for blocking or convoke when tapping out the entire army would expose you.

Before committing to Coat of Arms or Notorious Throng, count opposing flyers, available interaction, your remaining library under Kindred Discovery, and whether a Rogue actually connected. Protect the window to finish rather than spending the whole hand to gain another small increment of value.
