Bello does not merely attack; he animates non-Equipment artifacts and non-Aura enchantments with mana value 4 or greater into 4/4 indestructible, hasty Elementals that draw a card whenever they connect with an opponent's face. Crucially, they are only creatures on your turn, dodging opposing sorcery-speed removal entirely while surviving your own symmetrical sweepers like Jokulhaups, Obliterate, and Blasphemous Act because Bello grants them indestructible. Because they are creatures during your turn, untap effects like Aggravated Assault untap animated mana rocks like Thran Dynamo, Gilded Lotus, and Chromatic Orrery to trigger infinite combat loops.

The infinite combat engines
+
Aggravated Assault + Chromatic Orrery

on your turn, Chromatic Orrery is a 4/4 creature that taps for {C}{C}{C}{C}{C} (spendable as any color); pay {3}{R}{R} to activate Aggravated Assault, untapping all creatures including Orrery for infinite combat phases, infinite mana, and infinite card draw

+
Aggravated Assault + Nature's Will

Nature's Will attacks as a 4/4 indestructible Elemental; when it or any creature deals combat damage to an opponent, untap all your lands, floating mana to activate Aggravated Assault for infinite combats

+
Aggravated Assault + Old Gnawbone

connecting with two 4/4 Elementals produces 8 Treasures, easily paying {3}{R}{R} for Aggravated Assault with surplus Treasures to spare

+
Aggravated Assault + Savage Ventmaw

attacking with Savage Ventmaw generates {R}{R}{R}{G}{G}{G}, paying {3}{R}{R} for Aggravated Assault with {R}{G} mana floating each combat

++
Aggravated Assault + Thran Dynamo + Gilded Lotus

both rocks animate into hasty creatures on your turn, tapping together for {C}{C}{C}{R}{R}{R} (6 mana) to pay for Aggravated Assault ({3}{R}{R}) and net infinite combat phases and mana

Asymmetrical mass land denial and sweepers
Jokulhaups

destroys all artifacts, creatures, and lands; your 4+ mana value artifacts and enchantments have indestructible on your turn, so your entire animated army survives while opponents lose everything

Obliterate

an uncounterable sweeper that wipes all artifacts, creatures, and lands; your 4+ mana value artifacts and enchantments remain intact to swing with haste and close the game on the spot

Blasphemous Act

one-mana board wipe that deals 13 damage to all creatures; your animated 4/4 Elementals ignore the damage thanks to Bello's indestructible aura

Opening hands and sequencing

Look for aggressive hands featuring 0-1 mana fast acceleration (Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox, Sol Ring, Mana Vault, or dorks) and at least one 4-drop artifact or enchantment. The ideal curve deploys fast mana on turn 1, casts Bello on turn 2, and drops a 4+ mana value artifact or enchantment on turn 3, immediately swinging for 4 indestructible damage and drawing a card. In Bracket 4, you do not need to wait until turn 6: assemble Aggravated Assault with Chromatic Orrery or Nature's Will as quickly as possible, or wipe the board with Jokulhaups or Obliterate while your animated army stands tall.
