Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Deploy cheap artifacts to continuously surveil your library, assemble artifact token swarms, and leverage Golbez's end step recursion to drain opponents for massive damage using high-power creatures like Phyrexian Dreadnought.

Surveil and end-step drain engine
Golbez, Crystal Collector

surveils one whenever an artifact you control enters. At your end step, if you control four or more artifacts, return target creature card from your graveyard to your hand. If you control eight or more artifacts, each opponent loses life equal to that card's power.

Phyrexian Dreadnought

one-mana 12/12 that sacrifices itself on entry unless you sacrifice twelve power. Put it into the graveyard via surveil, self-sacrifice, or Entomb; returning it with Golbez drains each opponent for twelve life every turn.

Metalwork Colossus

10/10 with an active cost reduction based on the total mana value of noncreature artifacts you control. Drains for ten life when returned via Golbez.

Triplicate Titan

9/9 flying, trample, vigilance threat that breaks into three 3/3 Golems upon death. Drains nine life when returned via Golbez.

Wurmcoil Engine

6/6 deathtouch and lifelink threat that splits into two 3/3 tokens. Reusable recursion target that drains six life on return.

Noxious Gearhulk

destroys target creature on entry and gains life equal to its toughness while draining five life on end step return.

Artifact token producers and cost reduction
Sai, Master Thopterist

creates a 1/1 flying Thopter token whenever you cast an artifact spell, triggering Golbez's surveil and rapidly achieving the eight-artifact drain threshold.

Sharding Sphinx

creates a 1/1 Thopter token whenever an artifact creature you control deals combat damage to a player. These tokens trigger surveil and double your artifact count.

Etherium Sculptor

reduces the mana cost of artifact spells by one generic mana.

Foundry Inspector

secondary cost reducer that enables multi-spell storm turns with Sensei's Divining Top and Mystic Forge.

Cloud Key

names artifacts to provide unconditional cost reduction across all artifact cards.

Forensic Gadgeteer

creates Clue tokens whenever you cast artifact spells and reduces artifact activated ability costs by one mana.

Combat finishes and infinite turns (Turn 6+)
Cyberdrive Awakener

animates all noncreature artifacts into 4/4 flying creatures until end of turn, converting an army of Clues, Thopters, and mana rocks into lethal evasive damage.

Kappa Cannoneer

enters with improvise and ward 4, gaining +1/+1 counters and becoming unblockable whenever artifacts enter your battlefield.

Tezzeret, Master of the Bridge

grants affinity for artifacts to creature and planeswalker spells; his +2 ability drains each opponent and gains life equal to the number of artifacts you control.

+
Time Sieve + Sai, Master Thopterist

sacrifice five Thopter tokens to Time Sieve to take an extra turn. With Sai or Sharding Sphinx generating five or more artifact tokens per turn cycle, this generates unbounded extra turns.

++
Aetherflux Reservoir + Sensei's Divining Top + Mystic Forge

with cost reduction reducing Top to zero mana, tap Top to draw and recast it from top of library with Forge, accumulating massive life gain to eliminate opponents with 50-damage lasers.

Tutors, interaction, and protection
Demonic Tutor

finds Phyrexian Dreadnought, Time Sieve, or Cyberdrive Awakener.

Transmute Artifact

sacrifices a cheap artifact token to tutor any artifact directly into play.

Entomb

puts Phyrexian Dreadnought or Triplicate Titan directly into your graveyard to enable immediate end-step drain triggers.

Cyclonic Rift

clears opposing blockers and disruptive permanents before initiating an alpha strike.

Mana Drain

counters opposing key spells and converts their mana value into acceleration for high-cost artifacts.

Deadly Rollick

free creature exile while controlling Golbez.

Opening hands and sequencing

Look for opening hands with two to three lands, early mana rocks (Sol Ring, Signets, Talismans), and cheap artifacts or surveil enablers. Cast Golbez on turn two to start sculpting your draws with surveil on every artifact entry. Aim to hit four artifacts early for creature recursion, then push to eight artifacts to turn your graveyard into a repeating burn engine. Save combo finishes for Turn 6 or later.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, card advantage, and graveyard setup.
