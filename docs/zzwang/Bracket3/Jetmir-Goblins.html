Jetmir counts your creatures. With three, every creature you control gets +1/+0 and vigilance; with six, +2/+0 and trample as well; with nine, +3/+0 and double strike too. At nine, a 1/1 Goblin token is a 4/1 with double strike and trample — 8 damage — and ten of them are 80. Vigilance keeps your attackers untapped to block afterwards, so attack every turn.

And any infinite loop of hasty creatures with Jetmir out is an unlimited army of double strikers — the deck has several — see the combos.

Mana creatures count toward the thresholds, and so do tokens made at instant speed. Secure the Wastes or March of the Multitudes after blockers are declared can take you from six creatures to nine before damage. The bonuses also drop the moment you fall below a threshold, so don't trade creatures away mid-combat when you're sitting on exactly nine.

The commander
Jetmir, Nexus of Revels

four mana. Three creatures: +1/+0 and vigilance. Six: also +1/+0 and trample. Nine: also +1/+0 and double strike. Every creature you control, tokens included.

Lightning Greaves

shroud the turn Jetmir lands — the table knows what it does. Never equip it to Kiki-Jiki — shroud stops Zealous Conscripts or Restoration Angel from targeting Kiki.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Zealous Conscripts

Kiki copies Conscripts, and the copy's enter trigger untaps Kiki — target Kiki itself. Repeat for as many hasty 3/3s as you want, the same turn Kiki lands (it has haste). With Jetmir out every copy is a double striker; Impact Tremors and General Kreat turn them into damage before they even attack. Never leave Lightning Greaves on Kiki: shroud stops the Conscripts copy targeting it.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Restoration Angel

Kiki copies the Angel; the copy blinks Kiki, which comes back untapped with haste. Flash: start it in an opponent's end step and the copies last until the end of your next turn.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Village Bell-Ringer

the copy untaps every creature you control, Kiki included. Flash, so this one works in an opponent's end step too.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Goblin Bombardment

Kiki copies the Crafter. The copy's champion trigger exiles Kiki; the hasty copy taps for 3 damage; sacrifice it to Goblin Bombardment; Kiki returns untapped — it has haste — and copies the Crafter again. Unlimited 3-damage shots, so this one kills on its own. The original Crafter must stay on the battlefield; when it enters you must exile another Goblin or Shaman (champion) or sacrifice it.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Thornbite Staff + Goblin Bombardment

the Staff attaches itself to Kiki when Kiki enters after it (otherwise equip costs {4}). Kiki copies any nonlegendary creature, you sacrifice a creature to Goblin Bombardment, and the death untaps Kiki. Unlimited loops. This loop deals damage rather than building an army.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Thornbite Staff + Goblin Bombardment

equip the Staff to Krenko ({4} — he's a Warrior, so it won't attach itself). Krenko taps for a Goblin per Goblin; sacrifice one to Goblin Bombardment; the death untaps Krenko; tap again. The army doubles every lap. Win with Impact Tremors, General Kreat or a double-striking attack.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when
→
Goblin Matron → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

when you already hold Zealous Conscripts, Restoration Angel, Village Bell-Ringer, Lightning Crafter or the Staff — Kiki is the piece that turns half a combo into a kill.

→
Goblin Matron → Goblin Recruiter

when you're missing two or more pieces. Matron gets Recruiter; Recruiter stacks the rest on top of your library.

→
Goblin Matron → Krenko, Mob Boss

when Thornbite Staff is already out, or when you simply need a board this turn.

→
Goblin Matron → Lightning Crafter

when Kiki-Jiki is on the battlefield and you have a free outlet — the Crafter is the last piece.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

power 2, so the one non-Goblin tutor that still finds Kiki. It also gets Village Bell-Ringer or Goblin Recruiter.

→
Eladamri's Call → Zealous Conscripts

instant speed, for the non-Goblin half that Matron can't find.

→
Chord of Calling → Restoration Angel

with Kiki already out, Chord the Angel in at the end of an opponent's turn and go off right there. Convoke lets your army pay the generic part.

→
Formidable Speaker → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

discard a spare card when it enters to find any creature — Kiki, or whichever partner you lack.

→+
Goblin Recruiter → Goblin Ringleader + Muxus, Goblin Grandee

choose a four-card hand refill or a six-creature deployment. The exact piles and draw sequencing are below; either payoff works without the commander.

Goblin Recruiter setups — what to stack, top card first
→
Goblin Recruiter → Goblin Ringleader

TOP to BOTTOM: Goblin Ringleader; Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker; Lightning Crafter; Skirk Prospector; Goblin Warchief. Draw or otherwise reach Ringleader, then cast it: its entry trigger puts the next four selected Goblins into your hand. Those creatures still need to be cast; Ringleader does not deploy them. If Ringleader is already in hand, start the pile with the four targets instead.

→
Goblin Recruiter → Muxus, Goblin Grandee

TOP to BOTTOM: Muxus, Goblin Grandee; Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker; Lightning Crafter; Skirk Prospector; Goblin Warchief; Goblin Ringleader; Krenko, Mob Boss. Reach and cast Muxus; all six following cards are Goblin creatures with mana value five or less, so its entry trigger puts them onto the battlefield. If Muxus is already in hand, omit it from the pile and reveal the six immediately.

Goblin Ringleader

Put these four cards immediately UNDER the six-card Muxus package: Goblin Matron; Siege-Gang Commander; Goblin Chieftain; Howlsquad Heavy. When Muxus deploys Ringleader, the entry trigger puts this backup package into your hand. Resolve Ringleader before any other draw triggers and before the original Lightning Crafter's champion trigger. Only stack cards still in your library; substitute useful Goblins for pieces already in hand or on the battlefield.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

The original Crafter champions the Recruiter that made the pile, or the Ringleader AFTER its refill resolves. Keep Kiki and Prospector on the battlefield. Tap Kiki to copy Crafter; the hasty copy champions Kiki, taps for 3 damage, then is sacrificed to Prospector for {R}. Kiki returns untapped with haste. Repeat for lethal damage and red mana. No combat or commander is required, but removal or effects that stop the entry triggers/activated abilities can interrupt it.

Goblin Warchief

Included on the Muxus board to give your other Goblins haste and reduce the generic cost of backup Goblin spells. Kiki and its copies already have haste; Warchief is for Krenko and the wider recovery plan.

Use the next natural draw to reach the payoff, or arrange for an existing draw engine to take exactly the intended cards. If Skullclamp will draw two, put an extra useful Goblin above the payoff so the four/six-card package stays intact.

Guardian Project

Order the Muxus entry trigger ABOVE Project's draw trigger so Muxus reveals its intended six first. When those creatures enter, put Ringleader's trigger above their Project draws so it gets the intended four. Mandatory draws also limit how many Kiki loops you can safely repeat; finish before your library empties.

Searches that shuffle undo the pile: use fetch lands and other tutors BEFORE Recruiter. Any extra draw, mill or exile also changes the positions; account for it before committing. Muxus only puts eligible Goblin CREATURES onto the battlefield: Goblin-typed instants/enchantments and Muxus itself are not hits. Recruiter does not put the payoff in your hand for free.

March of the Multitudes

Akroma's Will

Kessig Wolf Run

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a mana creature and something that makes two bodies. Cast Jetmir on turn three or four with three creatures already out and your team attacks for real at once.

Attack every turn — vigilance means the army is still there to block.

Count before combat. Eight creatures and an instant token spell in hand is nine with double strike.
