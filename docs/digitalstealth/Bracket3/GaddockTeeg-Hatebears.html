The single biggest mistake a new pilot makes with Gaddock Teeg is assuming his restriction only hurts your opponents. Gaddock Teeg stops *all* players—including you—from casting noncreature spells with mana value 4 or greater, or spells with {X} in their mana costs. Running staples like Smothering Tithe, Chord of Calling, Finale of Devastation, or The One Ring will leave dead cards stranded in your hand. Instead, this list ensures that *every* noncreature spell in the ninety-nine costs 3 or less with no {X}, while all your high-end finishers (Elesh Norn, Craterhoof Behemoth, Moonshaker Cavalry) are creature spells that bypass Teeg's restriction completely.

The Kithkin Advisor and the stax lockdown
Gaddock Teeg

Cast for {G}{W}. Shuts off all noncreature spells with mana value 4 or greater and any noncreature spells with {X} in their mana costs, locking out board wipes, extra turns, and planeswalkers.

Drannith Magistrate

Locks out commanders from the command zone, flashback spells from graveyards, and cascade, discover, or exile casts. Combined with Teeg, opponents cannot play commanders or sweepers.

Archon of Emeria

Restricts each player to casting only one spell each turn while forcing opponents' nonbasic lands to enter tapped, completely suffocating storm and high-tempo decks.

Thalia, Guardian of Thraben

Taxes all noncreature spells by {1}, punishing early cantrips, mana rocks, and cheap removal.

Vryn Wingmare

Flying tax hatebear that taxes all noncreature spells by an additional {1}.

Aven Interrupter

Flash flying bird that exiles a spell on the stack into plot with a {2} mana tax, and taxes all spells cast from graveyards and exile by {2}.

Blind Obedience

Two-mana enchantment that forces all opposing creatures and artifacts to enter tapped, turning off hasty attackers and instant mana rocks.

Deafening Silence

One-mana enchantment allowing each player to cast only one noncreature spell each turn.

Search suppression and graveyard denial
Aven Mindcensor

Flash flyer that restricts opposing searches to only the top four cards of their library, neutering fetchlands and tutors.

Leonin Arbiter

Prevents any player from searching libraries unless they pay {2} per search, crippling early mana development.

Archivist of Oghma

Flash hatebear that draws a card and gains 1 life whenever an opponent searches their library.

Rest in Peace

Two-mana enchantment that exiles all graveyards on entry and permanently exiles any card that would be put into a graveyard.

Spirit of the Labyrinth

Restricts each player to drawing at most one card each turn, completely shutting down wheel spells, Rhystic Study, and brainstorms.

Scavenging Ooze

Repeatable graveyard hate on a green creature that exiles cards from graveyards, gains life, and grows in size.

Yasharn, Implacable Earth

Prevents players from paying life or sacrificing nonland permanents to cast spells or activate abilities; searches a basic Forest and Plains on entry.

Ability and artifact suppression
Collector Ouphe

Shuts down all activated abilities of artifacts across the entire table, neutralizing Treasures, Signets, and combo pieces.

Stony Silence

Two-mana enchantment that shuts down all artifact activated abilities.

Kataki, War's Wage

Forces opponents to pay {1} during each upkeep for every artifact they control or sacrifice it.

Manglehorn

Enters to destroy an artifact, and forces all opposing artifacts to enter the battlefield tapped.

Linvala, Keeper of Silence

Shuts off all activated abilities of creatures opponents control, blanking mana dorks and creature combo pieces.

Sanctum Prelate

Name a number (typically 1, 2, or 3) to prevent opponents from casting noncreature spells with that mana value.

The creature toolbox and tutor lines
Survival of the Fittest

The premier creature engine. Pay {G} and discard a creature to search your library for any creature card and put it directly into your hand.

Fauna Shaman

Repeatable Survival of the Fittest on a creature, tapping for {G} to swap any creature for your best silver bullet.

Eladamri's Call

Instant-speed two-mana tutor putting any creature directly into your hand.

Recruiter of the Guard

Enters to tutor any creature with toughness 2 or less directly into hand (fetches almost every hatebear in the deck, plus Walking Ballista).

Ranger-Captain of Eos

Tutors any creature with mana value 1 or less (Esper Sentinel, Mother of Runes, Walking Ballista), and sacrifices to silence noncreature spells for a full turn.

Yisan, the Wanderer Bard

Taps to search creatures directly onto the battlefield with increasing verse counters, tutoring on curve every turn cycle.

Eldritch Evolution

Sacrifices a small hatebear or dork to put a creature with mana value up to two greater directly onto the battlefield.

Protection and anti-sweeper defense
Mother of Runes

Taps to give any creature you control protection from any color, shielding your lock pieces from spot removal.

Giver of Runes

Taps to give another creature protection from any color or colorless, protecting against Eldrazi and artifact answers.

Grand Abolisher

Shuts off all opponent spells and abilities during your turn, guaranteeing that your tutors and combat phases resolve uninterrupted.

Selfless Spirit

Flying creature that sacrifices at instant speed to grant all your creatures indestructible until end of turn.

Boromir, Warden of the Tower

Shuts off all free spells and cascades, and sacrifices to grant your board indestructible and tempt you with the Ring.

Sylvan Safekeeper

Sacrifices a land to give any target creature shroud at instant speed.

Flawless Maneuver

Free instant-speed spell granting all your creatures indestructible while Gaddock Teeg is on the battlefield.

Heroic Intervention

Two-mana instant granting all your permanents hexproof and indestructible.

Shalai, Voice of Plenty

Grants hexproof to you and all your other creatures and planeswalkers, and provides a repeatable +1/+1 counter pump.

Compact win and aggro finishers
+
Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista

Heliod grants lifelink to Walking Ballista for {1}{W}. Remove a +1/+1 counter from Ballista to deal 1 damage to an opponent. Lifelink gains 1 life, triggering Heliod to put a +1/+1 counter back on Ballista. Repeat infinitely for lethal table burn. (Note: Collector Ouphe or Stony Silence shuts off Ballista's activated ability, so sacrifice or remove them before executing).

In accordance with Bracket 3 rules, this two-card combo is reserved for turn 6 or later. Early turns focus on establishing hatebears, locking opponent mana, and applying combat pressure.

Craterhoof Behemoth

Eight-mana creature with haste and trample that gives your entire hatebear swarm trample and massive +X/+X to close out games in one swing.

Moonshaker Cavalry

Eight-mana creature that gives your entire team flying and +X/+X on entry, launching an unblockable aerial alpha strike.

Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite

Seven-mana creature that gives all opposing creatures -2/-2, wiping enemy dorks while buffing your hatebears by +2/+2.

Archon of Valor's Reach

5/6 flying trampler that chooses Instant or Sorcery on entry, locking out whatever remaining answers opponents could possibly have.

Beastmaster Ascension

Three-mana enchantment that triggers on attacks. Once seven creatures have attacked, grants your entire team +5/+5 permanently.

Mana dorks, acceleration, and card advantage
Gaea's Cradle

Legendary land tapping for green mana equal to the number of creatures you control, producing huge bursts of mana to cast Craterhoof or activate Yisan.

Esper Sentinel

One-mana creature taxing noncreature spells and drawing cards.

Sylvan Library

Two-mana enchantment providing card selection and drawing extra cards each turn.

Kutzil, Malamet Exemplar

Shuts down opponent interaction on your turn and draws cards when modified or pumped creatures connect for combat damage.

Beast Whisperer

Draws a card whenever you cast any creature spell, turning your hatebears into continuous cantrips.

Birds of Paradise

Taps for any color of mana on turn 1.

Delighted Halfling

Taps for any color to cast uncounterable legendary spells.

Avacyn's Pilgrim

One-drop mana dork producing white mana.

Classic one-drop green mana dork.

One-drop green mana dork.

One-drop green mana dork.

Spot removal and noncreature answers
Swords to Plowshares

Premier one-mana instant creature exile.

Path to Exile

One-mana instant creature exile.

Beast Within

Instant-speed universal destruction for any permanent.

Reprieve

Two-mana instant tempo answer that returns a spell to hand and draws a card, bypassing uncounterable clauses.

Winds of Abandon

Two-mana sorcery exiling an opposing creature, or six-mana overloaded one-sided creature exile wipe.

Aura of Silence

Taxes opposing artifacts and enchantments by {2}, and sacrifices to destroy target artifact or enchantment.

Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow

MDFC creature that destroys target artifact or enchantment on entry, or enters tapped as a land.

Bala Ged Recovery // Bala Ged Sanctuary

MDFC sorcery returning any card from graveyard to hand, or enters tapped as a land.

Disciple of Freyalise // Garden of Freyalise

MDFC creature that sacrifices a creature to draw cards and gain life, or enters tapped as a land.

Boseiju, Who Endures

Channel ability that provides uncounterable artifact, enchantment, or nonbasic land destruction.

Eiganjo, Seat of the Empire

Channel ability that deals 4 damage to an attacking or blocking creature.

Wirewood Lodge

Taps to untap an Elf like Priest of Titania or Yisan.

Gavony Township

Taps to put a +1/+1 counter on every creature you control, steadily turning small hatebears into lethal attackers.

Opening hand and sequencing

Look for opening hands with 2 to 3 lands, a 1-drop mana dork (Birds of Paradise, Llanowar Elves, Avacyn's Pilgrim, Delighted Halfling), and at least one impactful turn-2 hatebear or tutor (Gaddock Teeg, Thalia, Drannith Magistrate, Eladamri's Call).

On turn 1, play a land and deploy a mana dork.

On turn 2, deploy Gaddock Teeg or a targeted hatebear based on what your opponents are playing (Drannith Magistrate against commander-centric pods, Collector Ouphe against artifact ramp, Thalia against spellslinger).

On turns 3 through 5, assemble your lock using Survival of the Fittest, Recruiter of the Guard, or Yisan to tutor the exact silver bullet needed to shut down the table, while holding up protection (Mother of Runes, Selfless Spirit, Heroic Intervention).

On turn 6 or later, execute your finish by dropping Craterhoof Behemoth, Moonshaker Cavalry, or triggering Beastmaster Ascension for an overwhelming combat alpha strike, or tutor up the Heliod + Walking Ballista combo to burn out the table.
