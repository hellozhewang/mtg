Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Keep Banner available as a draw engine until you know which win you are taking. Transforming him gives you Hulk's combat engine but removes your command-zone outlet for infinite mana; there is no built-in transformation back.

Optimized Bracket 3 for ratdos. The three Game Changers are Rhystic Study, The One Ring and Fierce Guardianship. No mass land denial. The plan is to assemble and protect a compact win for turn 6 or later, with ordinary Hulk attacks as pressure.

Choosing the face and the moment
Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk

cast the front for U to enable draw and commander-dependent protection. His draw ability costs twice X, taps him and is sorcery speed. Alternatively cast the Hulk face directly for 2RRGG; commander tax applies to either face when cast from the command zone. Transforming a permanent is not casting it and does not incur tax.

+
Moonmist + Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk

transform the Human front for 1G, ideally during the last opponent's end step. Moonmist also prevents combat damage from non-Wolves and non-Werewolves for that entire turn, including Hulk's. Transforming preserves tapped status, counters and how long you have controlled him; it does not grant haste or untap him.

+
Training Grounds + Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk

reduces the draw activation's total generic cost by two, to a minimum of one: X=1 costs one and X=2 costs two. It reduces the transformation to RRGG, not to two mana. It does not reduce Basalt Monolith's artifact ability.

+
Shang-Chi, Master of Kung Fu + Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk

lets a newly cast Banner activate his tap ability immediately, and supplies two mana for creature abilities, including drawing or transforming. It does not let Hulk attack immediately. Its restricted mana cannot cast spells or untap Basalt Monolith.

Samut, Hazoret's Champion

grants actual haste, so either a newly cast Hulk can attack or a newly cast Banner can draw. It also turns freshly cast mana creatures on immediately.

Swiftfoot Boots

haste and hexproof protect the commander while allowing your own targeted protection and Minamo activation. Budget one mana to equip after casting or recasting him.

Lightning Greaves

zero-cost equip supports an immediate Hulk attack. Shroud also stops your own targeting, including Minamo, Legolas's Quick Reflexes and equipping Hawkeye's Bow; move Greaves away first if needed. Caltrops does not target.

Hulk's repeatable combat finish
+
Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk + Caltrops

with Hulk face up and able to attack, Caltrops deals him one damage on each attack. Enrage puts a counter on him, untaps him and adds another combat. Repeat attacks to eliminate the table through trample and commander damage, provided he survives and can keep attacking. No additional main phases are created.

Caltrops

its damage stays marked until cleanup, but each successful enrage trigger adds one toughness to offset the next one-point ping. Blocker damage also accumulates and deathtouch can kill Hulk; the counter arrives only after damage and cannot rescue him from already-lethal damage. Your other one-toughness attackers die to Caltrops, so usually send Hulk alone.

++
Hawkeye's Bow + Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk + Caltrops

equip the Bow to Hulk before combat. Each attack taps him and deals one damage to each opponent; Caltrops untaps him for the next attack. This wins through combat-damage prevention, including Moonmist on your own turn, if attacks remain possible and the loop survives. Bow damage is not commander damage. Effects that prevent attacking still stop this route.

+
Walking Ballista + Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk

spare counters can ping an attacking Hulk for extra combats and untaps without drawing Caltrops. Each counter buys one activation, not an infinite loop by itself. Outside combat the ping grows Hulk but creates no extra combat.

Legolas's Quick Reflexes

protects Hulk with hexproof and can remove a blocker when he next taps to attack. Each extra attack can trigger the granted damage ability again that turn. Do not target Hulk with his own power-sized damage: an unmodified Hulk kills himself before enrage can save him.

Heroic Intervention

indestructible lets Hulk survive accumulated blocker damage while hexproof protects the combo permanents. Damage still happens, so enrage continues to trigger. It does not stop sacrifice, untargeted exile or toughness reduction.

The infinite-mana backup
+
Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy + Basalt Monolith

tap Monolith for four colorless mana with Kinnan's extra mana, spend three to untap it, and repeat for arbitrarily much colorless mana. Kinnan also improves ordinary mana creatures and rocks; the pair does not itself deal damage or produce colored mana.

+
Power Artifact + Basalt Monolith

enchant Monolith so its untap costs one. Tap for three and spend one to untap, repeatedly netting two colorless mana. This second compact mana engine works without Kinnan or the commander. Power Artifact is otherwise a narrow card, so avoid exposing it without the Monolith.

+
Basalt Monolith + Walking Ballista

after either infinite-mana engine is active, cast Ballista with enough counters to remove every opponent. This requires no commander or combat. Ballista's damage ability does not tap, so summoning sickness does not stop it. An already-deployed Ballista can instead receive counters with its four-mana ability.

+
Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk + Basalt Monolith

once either mana loop is active, an untapped Banner who can use tap abilities can draw as many remaining library cards as needed at sorcery speed. Choose X deliberately; never draw more cards than remain. Find Walking Ballista and cast it using the floating colorless mana. Hulk face up cannot use Banner's draw ability.

Finale of Devastation

with infinite colorless and GG available, cast for a very large X to give the team haste and lethal power. This is a backup to Ballista when combat is available. The search puts creatures straight onto the battlefield; fetching Ballista this way gives it zero counters and normally kills it immediately, so fetch a different creature.

Minamo, School at Water's Edge

pays U and taps to untap Banner for another draw activation, or The One Ring for another activation. Infinite colorless does not pay that U. It cannot target Banner through Lightning Greaves.

Find the missing piece
→
Fabricate → Caltrops

find the commander-plus-one combat finish when Hulk is ready to attack; allow another three mana to cast the artifact.

→
Fabricate → Walking Ballista

with infinite mana already available, put the payoff in hand so you can actually pay X while casting it.

→
Trophy Mage → Basalt Monolith

when Kinnan or Power Artifact is ready, find the shared mana engine. Trophy Mage also finds Caltrops; both cost exactly three, whereas Walking Ballista has mana value zero in the library.

→
Whir of Invention → Caltrops

at X=3, put the combat engine directly onto the battlefield, often on the preceding end step. Improvise pays only generic costs; keep UUU available. Basalt Monolith is the other primary target.

→
Transmute Artifact → Basalt Monolith

turn a spare artifact into the engine. Sacrificing a two-mana artifact requires one extra mana to put Monolith onto the battlefield. The sacrifice happens during resolution, and you must budget the difference; declining that payment sends the fetched artifact to the graveyard.

→
Muddle the Mixture → Power Artifact

transmute at sorcery speed for the missing Monolith partner. It can also find Kinnan, whose mana value is two. As a spell, retain it to counter a critical instant or sorcery instead when protection matters more.

→
Green Sun's Zenith → Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy

X=2 finds Kinnan because he is green as well as blue. It cannot find the red Samut, the blue Trophy Mage or the colorless Ballista.

→
Chord of Calling → Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy

use spare creatures to convoke a missing combo piece, preferably on an opponent's end step. This also reaches Trophy Mage when you need an artifact tutor, with X=3 instead.

→
Nature's Rhythm → Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy

another direct route to the mana partner. Later, harmonize can turn a large Hulk's tap into a discount on the generic X portion, but never the four green mana; tapping Hulk may cost your attack.

→
Eldritch Evolution → Trophy Mage

sacrifice an expendable one-mana creature to fetch the three-mana artifact tutor. You lose the sacrificed creature even if Evolution is countered. Fetch Kinnan directly instead when Monolith is already available.

→
Spellseeker → Transmute Artifact

turns a creature tutor into access to either three-mana artifact, provided you have an artifact to sacrifice and sufficient blue mana.

→
Spellseeker → Moonmist

fetch the cheap transformation when the combat route is ready. For defense, the same tutor can instead find Veil of Summer or Tamiyo's Safekeeping.

→
Finale of Devastation → Samut, Hazoret's Champion

X=2 finds haste when a newly cast Hulk is otherwise waiting a turn. Unlike most of these tutors, Finale can also recover a creature from the graveyard.

Develop while keeping cards flowing
+
Birds of Paradise + Kinnan, Bonder Prodigy

Birds supplies colored mana for Hulk, tutors and protection, and Kinnan adds a second mana of the chosen type. Elvish Mystic and Llanowar Elves are additional one-mana accelerants; they principally help pay green and generic costs.

Delighted Halfling

its colored mana casts legendary spells and makes them uncounterable, including either commander face and The One Ring. Its colorless mode can pay generic costs elsewhere; its colored mode cannot pay for a transformation or a nonlegendary spell.

Bloom Tender

Banner supplies blue and Tender supplies green, so the pair produces GU. With Hulk face up it produces RG instead; add a separate blue permanent to reach GUR.

Badgermole Cub

earthbend an untapped land to gain a hasty creature mana source; tapping it or another creature for mana adds an extra G. With Kinnan, a mana creature receives both bonuses, but an earthbent land remains a land and does not receive Kinnan's nonland bonus. The earthbent land also becomes vulnerable to creature interaction.

+
Nature's Lore + Three Visits

find untapped Tropical Island or Taiga, or a suitable shockland by paying life. These search for Forest cards, not basic Forests, and are reliable fixing for Hulk's RRGG activation.

+
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet

Ring pays the generic half of tutors, artifact setup and Banner draws, while Signet fixes colors. Keep colored sources available instead of assuming a big colorless pool can pay Hulk's transformation.

+
Mox Amber + Lotus Petal

Amber works with the cheap commander and other legends; Banner enables blue, Hulk enables red and green. Petal is one-shot fixing for the turn you need to develop and hold protection together. Neither replaces a stable colored land hand.

+
Rhystic Study + Mystic Remora

independent draw finds the tutors while leaving Banner free to transform. Remora is strongest while opponents are developing; stop paying cumulative upkeep when it prevents your own meaningful development.

The One Ring

provides a separate refill engine if the commander is removed or already Hulk. Its protection is for you, not your permanents. Extra Minamo activations accelerate both cards and future burden damage; plan a finish before that life loss becomes decisive.

+
Sylvan Library + Faerie Mastermind

Library selects combo pieces and can turn life into cards. Mastermind rewards opponents' extra draws without needing Banner's tap. Preserve life when repeated Library payments and Ring burden overlap.

+
Eternal Witness + Noxious Revival

recover a removed engine or tutor after interaction. Witness returns a card to hand; Revival puts it on top, so account for the draw needed to access it. Graveyard exile can shut either recovery plan down.

Make the winning turn survive
+
Fierce Guardianship + Deflecting Swat

a one-mana Banner enables both free spells early. Guardianship stops a noncreature spell; Swat redirects a targeted spell or ability. Neither is a universal answer to a creature spell or untargeted board wipe.

Hexing Squelcher

makes your spells uncounterable, including the artifact engine and Ballista. Its ward asks only two life, so it is not reliable removal immunity. It does not protect activated or triggered abilities from being countered.

+
Veil of Summer + Tamiyo's Safekeeping

Veil fights blue and black interaction and protects your spells from counters for the turn. Safekeeping protects a single permanent, including Caltrops, Monolith or Hulk, with hexproof and indestructible. Save the relevant color before committing the engine.

Spellskite

can absorb removal only if it is a legal target of the spell or ability after redirection. It cannot redirect a spell restricted to a legendary creature onto itself, and cannot stop a sweeper.

+
Force of Negation + Pact of Negation

Force is free only on another player's turn and only with a blue card to pitch; normally pay three to use it on your own winning turn. Pact needs 3UU at your next upkeep if the win fails, so do not use it casually to defend early development.

+
Swan Song + Flusterstorm

cheap ways to win a stack fight while spending most of your mana on the combo. Song covers enchantments as well as instants and sorceries; Flusterstorm only covers instants and sorceries and is a tax that can be paid.

+
An Offer You Can't Refuse + Delay

Offer counters a noncreature spell but gives its controller two Treasures; reserve it for threats worth that acceleration. Delay can hit a creature spell and buys three upkeep steps, rather than removing the threat permanently.

+
Nature's Claim + Force of Vigor

clear artifact and enchantment restrictions that prevent searches, activated abilities or attacking. Force can remove two obstacles and is free only on opponents' turns with a green card to pitch. Do not destroy your own Caltrops or Monolith while choosing targets.

+
Pongify + Resculpt

remove creature-based restrictions or a blocker Hulk cannot safely fight. Resculpt also exiles artifacts and handles indestructible threats. Both leave a body, so account for the new blocker before attacking.

+
Abrade + Beast Within

Abrade answers a small creature or artifact; Beast Within reaches any troublesome permanent. They support the combo rather than trying to trade one-for-one with every threat at a four-player table.

Chain of Vapor

temporarily removes a blocker or restriction, or rescues one of your pieces. Its controller may sacrifice a land to copy it, potentially bouncing your engine in return. Use it before committing the vulnerable permanent when possible.

Endurance

answers a graveyard win at instant speed and can recycle your own graveyard when needed. Evoke costs a green card and normally sacrifices Endurance, so use the free mode only when tempo or survival demands it.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two to three lands with early green and blue, an accelerator, and either a draw engine or tutor plus interaction. A one-land hand with several expensive tutors is not reliable. There are 34 lands, including nine fetchlands and three basics; the original duals, shocks and multiplayer duals carry most fixing. The basics leave targets for opponents' basic-land replacement effects. Wild Growth is additional one-mana acceleration; it is less flexible than a creature with Kinnan but survives creature sweepers.

Fetch for what the hand needs: early GU supports Banner and green development, UU supports Muddle and Power Artifact, UUU supports Whir, and RRGG supports Hulk. Ketria Triome, Hedge Maze and Thundering Falls always enter tapped, so fetch them when spending that turn's mana is unnecessary. Gemstone Caverns accelerates an opening hand only when you are not the starting player and exile a card; without its luck counter it produces only colorless.

On turns 1-3, build colored mana, cast Banner when useful and establish a draw engine. On turns 4-5, tutor for the missing engine, hold protection, and use the last opponent's end step for Moonmist or Whir where appropriate. Do not tap Banner for cards and then expect an immediate untapped Hulk attack after transforming him.

All two-card infinite engines and commander-plus-one finishes wait until turn 6 under the house rules: Hulk plus Caltrops, Kinnan plus Basalt Monolith, and Power Artifact plus Basalt Monolith. Before then, develop resources and interact without starting those loops. Ordinary attacks are available, but do not assemble Caltrops with an attacking Hulk and describe its repeated combats as ordinary pressure.

At the winning turn, choose between Hulk combat and Banner drawing into Ballista before transforming. Preserve colored mana and a relevant answer. If attacking would expose Hulk to lethal blockers or attack restrictions, take the mana route; if your artifact abilities are shut off, remove that restriction or use the combat route. Expect a good supported hand to threaten a protected finish around turns 6-7, not a guaranteed goldfish win through every board.
