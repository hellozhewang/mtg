Protect Zur's first attack, then turn each attack into the enchantment the board actually calls for. Equip the pieces you want BEFORE giving him shroud: Zur can attach fetched Auras through shroud, but your Aura spells and equip abilities still target. This is a Bracket 3 Voltron-control list built around combat damage, with Luminarch Ascension as its independent backup.

Get Zur onto the battlefield with protection available

The deck has 34 lands, one additional spell/land card, and ten mana rocks costing one or two. The rocks accelerate Zur and leave mana for interaction and Vanishing on later turns. A turn-two rock can enable turn-three Zur, but an exposed commander into open removal is often worse than waiting a turn.

Arcane Signet

fixes all three commander colors. Sol Ring alone cannot supply Zur's white, blue and black requirements.

Azorius Signet

converts one mana into white and blue; with a black source and another mana, this gets Zur onto the battlefield. Its activation needs an input mana.

Dimir Signet

supplies blue and black together, complementing the white lands and spells in this list.

Orzhov Signet

supplies white and black together, leaving blue sources available for interaction when the mana permits.

Thought Vessel

early acceleration that later preserves a large Necropotence hand for Empyrial Armor. Its mana is colorless, so still check all three commander colors.

Cavern of Souls

choose Human to cover Zur and the other Humans in this list. Its colored mana is restricted to creatures of that type; it cannot pay for Auras or counters.

Hall of the Bandit Lord

can give a newly cast Zur haste, but enters tapped and charges three life for its mana. Develop it before the turn you need it.

Sea of Clouds

joins Morphic Pool and Vault of Champions as an untapped dual while you have at least two opponents. They enter tapped once the game becomes heads-up.

Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

an additional blue land when needed, or a three-mana answer to an opposing spell or nonland permanent. The land side can enter untapped for three life.

Protect the attack and keep the Auras attached

Lightning Greaves

immediate haste and shroud with zero equip cost. Equip Wizard's Staff and any other desired Equipment first; moving Greaves off Zur later needs another legal creature to equip.

Swiftfoot Boots

haste and hexproof while preserving your ability to cast Auras targeting Zur. Unlike shroud, your own spells and abilities can still target him.

Lavaspur Boots

a cheap haste option with a small power boost. Ward one is only a tax, so do not treat it as complete removal protection.

Diplomatic Immunity

protects Zur from targeted effects and has shroud itself. You can still fetch Auras onto Zur, but cannot cast them targeting him or attach new Equipment with equip.

Greater Auramancy

gives other enchantments shroud and gives enchanted creatures shroud. Cast and equip deliberately before deploying it. It does not grant itself shroud unless another effect does so, and does not stop sweepers.

Vanishing

keep two blue mana available to phase Zur and his attached Auras and Equipment out of removal, exile sweepers, or sacrifice effects that would catch him. The ability does not target. Phasing him out during combat preserves the investment but ends his participation in that attack.

Hyena Umbra

adds first strike and absorbs one destruction event, preserving Zur and his other attachments. Umbra armor does not answer exile, sacrifice, or toughness reduced to zero.

Kaya's Ghostform

recovers Zur after death or exile if you leave him in that zone for the trigger to return him. Moving him to the command zone prevents that recovery. His old Auras still go away, and the returned Zur needs haste or a later turn to attack.

Flawless Maneuver

free while you control Zur and useful against destruction sweepers. Indestructible does not protect against exile, sacrifice, or enough negative toughness.

Grand Abolisher

shuts off opposing spells and activated abilities of artifacts, creatures and enchantments during your turn. Opposing triggered abilities and land abilities can still matter.

Choose the right attack trigger

Zur the Enchanter

searches for an enchantment of mana value three or less and puts it directly onto the battlefield. This does not cast a spell, so it works through Rule of Law. An Aura entering this way does not target, but must still be legally attachable; protection and enchant restrictions still apply.

Wizard's Staff

costs two to cast and one to equip to Zur, a Wizard. While attached, it makes his attack ability trigger twice. Resolve those triggers separately: protection first and a draw engine second is a common line. It must already be attached when he attacks, and shroud blocks the equip ability.

Choose protection if removal threatens Zur, a draw engine if your hand is running out, an answer if another player is about to win, and a damage Aura if it creates a useful clock. There is no compulsory first fetch. Moat is mana value four and cannot be found by Zur; Wizard's Staff is an artifact and also cannot be found by him.

Rhystic Study

the general-purpose engine when several opponents are casting spells. It helps find interaction while Zur spends subsequent attacks on defense or damage.

Necropotence

converts life into cards at your next end step and skips your draw step. Activate before that end step, keep enough life to survive, and plan the cards for later turns. Paying life in combat does not immediately grow Empyrial Armor.

Entity Tracker

draws when an enchantment enters under your control, including one fetched by Zur. This engine works with the commander's actual action. Its draw is mandatory, so watch your remaining library late in the game.

Combat Research

adds power and ward to legendary Zur and draws when he connects. With double strike, it can trigger in each combat-damage step; Wizard's Staff doubles each of these abilities granted to Zur as well.

Black Market Connections

choose the resources you can afford each first main phase. Treasure helps fund protection, cards replenish your hand, and a Shapeshifter can absorb an ordinary sacrifice effect. Choosing all modes costs six life; lifelink helps, but do not automatically take all three.

Brainstorm

puts Auras drawn into your hand back in the library for Zur to fetch. Cast it before the attack trigger resolves and keep Rule of Law's spell limit in mind.

Copy Enchantment

usually copies a useful draw engine, attack tax, or Greater Auramancy. Copying an Aura as it enters can also attach it without targeting. An extra Necropotence adds little because one already lets you activate repeatedly.

Make combat damage lethal

Empyrial Armor

turns the size of your existing hand into power and toughness. Seven cards make otherwise unmodified Zur an 8/11; ten cards make him an 11/14. Thought Vessel or Reliquary Tower lets you keep a larger hand through cleanup. Casting or discarding cards reduces the bonus immediately.

All That Glitters

counts both enchantments and artifacts, so the ten rocks and Equipment contribute to the same lethal attack. An artifact enchantment counts only once.

Ethereal Armor

scales with your enchantments and supplies first strike. Recount after every new enchantment, including itself; first strike and double strike do not create a third damage step.

Twinblade Blessing

gives double strike. Eleven power connecting in both damage steps deals twenty-two commander damage, enough to eliminate one opponent from zero. It can be fetched before blockers, or cast with flash if Zur can legally be targeted. Double strike gives no extra attack trigger.

Steel of the Godhead

Zur is both white and blue, so he gets +2/+2, lifelink, and cannot be blocked. This is the main route past flying blockers and the main way to repay life spent on cards.

Unquestioned Authority

another route past creature blockers, with an immediate card from its entry trigger. Protection from creatures does not strip the ordinary enchantment Auras or Equipment in this list.

Daybreak Coronet

adds +3/+3, vigilance and lifelink, but Zur must already have another Aura. If his last other Aura disappears, Coronet becomes illegally attached and goes to the graveyard. Multiple instances of lifelink do not multiply life gained.

Track commander damage separately for each opponent. An eliminated player takes their cards out of the game, so reassess blockers, removal and resources before the next attack. Lifelink does not reduce commander damage already marked against someone.

Stay alive while you work through three opponents

Moat

prevents creatures without flying from attacking anyone. Zur and Luminarch Ascension's Angels can still attack; opposing flyers can too. This is cast from hand, not fetched by Zur.

Ghostly Prison

makes each creature attacking you cost two mana. It discourages attacks but does not stop an opponent who can pay, and does not protect you from noncombat wins.

Blind Obedience

opposing creatures and artifacts enter tapped, slowing haste attackers, fresh blockers and many mana rocks. Extort requires an actual spell cast and an additional payment; Zur's fetched enchantments do not trigger it.

Rule of Law

limits every player, including you, to one spell each turn. Zur's triggers, Equipment activations and Angel creation remain available. Develop your rocks before deploying it, and do not spend your one spell on a cantrip if you need a counter later in the same turn. It cannot undo spells already on the stack.

Solitary Confinement

prevents damage to you and gives you shroud, but skips your draw step and requires a discard each upkeep. Establish Necropotence or another working engine first. Life loss, life payments and effects that say you lose the game still work. This is a defense you must maintain, not invulnerability.

Luminarch Ascension

the backup win if Zur is repeatedly removed. Once it has four quest counters, each two mana makes another flying 4/4, with no once-per-turn limit. An opponent's end step only adds a counter if you lost no life that turn; fetchlands, painlands, Talismans and Necropotence activations can spoil that condition even when no attack connects.

Answer the problem without disabling your own engine

Grasp of Fate

one fetched enchantment can exile a nonland permanent from each opponent. Its entry ability targets even though Zur's search ability does not; opposing hexproof can stop those targets. Removing Grasp returns the exiled permanents.

Darksteel Mutation

can be fetched onto a troublesome creature without targeting. The creature remains on the battlefield, so an opposing commander cannot simply choose the command zone, but its controller can still find ways to sacrifice it.

Imprisoned in the Moon

Zur can fetch this onto a creature, land or planeswalker without targeting it. The permanent stays on the battlefield as a colorless land with a colorless-mana ability and loses its other types and abilities. An opposing commander cannot simply move to the command zone, but its controller may still find a way to sacrifice or remove it.

Oubliette

phases out one creature and its attachments until Oubliette leaves. Its entry ability targets. It is useful against commanders and does not count as making the creature leave the battlefield.

Sheltered by Ghosts

combines removal of an opposing nonland permanent with lifelink, a power boost and ward for Zur. The removal trigger targets. If the Aura leaves, including because Zur dies, the exiled permanent returns; phasing is not leaving.

Aura of Silence

taxes opposing artifact and enchantment spells and can sacrifice itself for removal. Choose between preserving the tax and answering the immediate threat.

Swords to Plowshares

efficient creature exile when waiting for an attack is unsafe. Solitude supplies another answer that can be cast by pitching a white card when necessary.

Mana Drain

counters any spell, with delayed colorless mana for rocks, a commander recast, or other generic costs. The mana cannot pay Zur's three colored requirements.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

protects a key play or stops a noncreature win attempt for one blue mana. Its two Treasures are real resources for the opponent, so choose the exchange deliberately.

Bilbo's Gambit

returns a spell to its owner's hand, including a spell that cannot be countered. Promising the Treasure also prevents EVERY player, including you, from casting more spells that turn once it resolves. It needs a spell to target and does not remove other spells or abilities already on the stack.

Dovin's Veto

an uncounterable answer to a noncreature spell. Swan Song is a cheaper option for an instant, sorcery or enchantment, but gives the opponent a flying blocker.

Cyclonic Rift

its seven-mana overload clears opposing nonland permanents while preserving your board, opening a combat finish or buying a recovery turn.

Toxic Deluge

clears creatures with negative toughness, including indestructible ones. An enlarged Zur can survive a carefully chosen X, but Hyena Umbra and Flawless Maneuver do not save him if his toughness reaches zero.

Out of Time

a fetchable board reset that phases out every creature present, including Zur, until it leaves. Cast it before deploying Zur, or activate the Vanishing Aura for two blue in response to Out of Time's entry trigger. If Zur phases out first, that trigger misses him and he returns at your next untap while the other creatures remain phased out. Fetching this during an attack and then phasing Zur out ends his participation in that combat; plan it as a reset, not a lethal swing.

Winds of Rath

destroys unenchanted creatures while an Aura-covered Zur survives. Equipment alone does not qualify, and opponents' enchanted creatures survive too.

Soul-Guide Lantern

inexpensive graveyard interaction that can exile every opponent's graveyard at instant speed once on the battlefield. Its mass-exile mode preserves your own graveyard; drawing a card is a separate sacrifice mode.

Replenish

rebuilds enchantments lost to destruction or sacrificed, but cannot recover exiled cards. Have a legal creature available for the Auras. Daybreak Coronet cannot use another Aura entering at the same time to satisfy its attachment requirement.

Necropotence exiles discarded cards from your graveyard. While it is on the battlefield, discarding enchantments during cleanup or to Solitary Confinement is not a reliable way to stock Replenish. Use Hall of Heliod's Generosity for enchantments that actually remain in the graveyard.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep roughly three usable lands, a cheap rock, and interaction, haste, or an early draw engine. Check the actual white, blue and black sources; several utility lands plus a colorless rock can still fail to cast Zur. Two-land hands need a credible draw and mana plan. A hand of damage Auras without development is a mulligan.

Develop mana, then land Zur with protection or haste when possible. Attach Wizard's Staff and other desired Equipment before shroud. On early attacks, stabilize the board and secure cards; then assemble a power Aura, evasion and double strike. Save two blue for Vanishing when a sweeper would otherwise erase several turns of investment.

If Rule of Law is in play, choose your one spell per turn before spending it. Equip, activate Necropotence, create Angels, and fetch with Zur without consuming that spell. Under Solitary Confinement, verify that your next upkeep has a discard and your draw engine will still work.

This list has three Game Changers: Necropotence, Rhystic Study and Cyclonic Rift. It contains no intended infinite combo, mass land denial, extra-turn chain, or paired spell lock. Its Bracket 3 plan is to establish resources and defenses before closing through combat; a strong draw is not a guarantee against three opponents' interaction.
