# Competitive Brawl update — October 12, 2026

Recorded October 22, 2026 from the owner's supplied list and checked against
[Wizards' announcement](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/announcements-october-12-2026).

This update applies to **Competitive Brawl on MTG Arena**. It does not itself
change this project's Commander rules or Scryfall's `legalities.commander`.
Applying it to our Commander catalog would require a separate house-rule decision.
Do not treat a Competitive Brawl permission to play a card in the 99 as an unban
in Commander.

## Rebalance

Nashi, Illusion Gadgeteer is rebalanced in Competitive Brawl: its previous addition
of flash is reverted. This is not a commander ban and does not instruct us to
remove a Nashi deck or alter the paper card's rules text.

## Project decision and catalog audit

On October 22 the owner withdrew the removal request after the Competitive Brawl
format distinction was identified. **Keep the Commander decks and the existing
Commander rules.** No house ban or unban is adopted from this announcement.

A complete audit of the 164 current deck commanders found two matching lists:
`zzwang/LightPaws-Auras` and `zzwang/Yuriko-Ninjas`. Both remain in the catalog and
tournament pool. No current deck is led by Spider-Man 2099 or the other named
cards. Other decks using these cards in their 99 remain unchanged. No deck uses
Nashi, Illusion Gadgeteer.

Yuriko, Light-Paws and Spider-Man 2099 are still Commander-legal legendary creatures.
Nadu remains banned from Commander entirely. Oko and Wrenn and Six being legal
cards in Commander does not make them eligible commanders under the normal rules.
See [the official format-specific ban lists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/banned-restricted-list).

No validation policy, decklist, guide, tournament entry or generated catalog was
changed. Saved Games and Steam Workshop remain v64.
