Ghave turns +1/+1 counters into Saprolings and sacrificed creatures back into counters, so with any counter or token doubler on the battlefield the two halves feed each other forever. The deck is a pile of those doublers, sacrifice outlets and drain payoffs, plus four separate two-card combos built around Basking Broodscale. Assemble any loop and drain the table.

The commander
Ghave, Guru of Spores

five mana; enters with five +1/+1 counters. {1}, remove a +1/+1 counter from ANY creature you control: create a 1/1 Saproling. {1}, sacrifice a creature: put a +1/+1 counter on target creature.

Ghave is a sacrifice outlet and a counter-mover on his own, and five counters means five Saprolings even with nothing else out.

His abilities cost {1} each, so a loop with him needs a mana source in it: Ashnod's Altar or Phyrexian Altar.

Infinite combos — why this is Bracket 3.5
+
Basking Broodscale + Cathars' Crusade

whenever counters land on Broodscale it makes an Eldrazi Spawn. The Spawn entering makes Cathars' Crusade put a counter on every creature, Broodscale included, which makes another Spawn. Unlimited Spawns: sacrifice them for unlimited colourless mana.

+
Basking Broodscale + Rosie Cotton of South Lane

each Spawn is a token you created, so Rosie puts a +1/+1 counter on Broodscale, which makes another Spawn. The same unlimited Spawns and mana.

+
Basking Broodscale + Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest

sacrifice a Spawn for {C}; Mazirek puts a counter on every creature, so Broodscale makes another Spawn. Unlimited mana and sacrifices.

+
Chatterfang, Squirrel General + Pitiless Plunderer

a creature dies, Plunderer makes a Treasure, Chatterfang adds a Squirrel. Sacrifice the Squirrel to Chatterfang's own ability ({B} from the Treasure). Unlimited Treasures, Squirrels and deaths.

++
Ghave, Guru of Spores + Ashnod's Altar + Doubling Season

remove a counter for two Saprolings ({1}), sacrifice one to the Altar ({C}{C}), and pay {1} to sacrifice the other and put two counters on Ghave. That breaks even on mana and gains a counter. Spend the spare counters on more Saprolings for the Altar: unlimited mana, Saprolings and deaths.

++
Ghave, Guru of Spores + Ashnod's Altar + Cathars' Crusade

each new Saproling puts a counter back on Ghave, so remove one ({1}), sacrifice the Saproling ({C}{C}), and net {C} a loop. Ivy Lane Denizen works in place of the Crusade.

++
Slimefoot, the Stowaway + Ashnod's Altar + Doubling Season

Slimefoot's {4} makes two Saprolings; sacrificing both to the Altar pays the {4} back, and each Saproling dying drains every opponent 1. Unlimited damage, with or without Ghave.

Every two-card line here is exactly what Bracket 3 forbids and this house Bracket 3.5 allows.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when

Count what you have, then fetch the missing half of the cheapest loop.

→
Demonic Tutor → Basking Broodscale

with Cathars' Crusade, Rosie or Mazirek already out, Broodscale finishes it for two mana.

→
Vampiric Tutor → Ashnod's Altar

instant speed, on top: with Ghave and a doubler out, the Altar is the last piece.

→
Green Sun's Zenith → Ivy Lane Denizen

X = 4, straight onto the battlefield. Basking Broodscale is devoid (colourless), so Green Sun's Zenith cannot find it.

→
Fauna Shaman → Rosie Cotton of South Lane

discard any creature to tutor any creature, every turn.

→
Eladamri's Call → Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest

instant-speed creature tutor to hand.

→
Finale of Devastation → Pitiless Plunderer

X = 4 onto the battlefield; with Chatterfang out, that's the combo.

→
Diabolic Intent → Cathars' Crusade

sacrifice a Saproling for any card.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep mana for a turn-four Ghave (dorks help), a doubler or outlet, and a tutor. Broodscale with Cathars' Crusade wins without Ghave at all.

With Ghave out, every creature is a sacrifice outlet's fuel and every doubler is half a combo; take the line with the fewest pieces left.

Protect the combo turn with Grand Abolisher or a free Flawless Maneuver against a wipe.
