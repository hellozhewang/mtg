Selvala taps for mana equal to the greatest power among your creatures, so one big body turns her into a mana engine. The deck ramps with elves into a single huge creature, untaps Selvala to double the mana, and spends it on more huge creatures, each of which also draws a card off her. It ends with Craterhoof Behemoth or a team-wide pump the turn after everything lands.

The commander
Selvala, Heart of the Wilds

three mana. {G}, {T}: add X mana in any combination of colours, where X is the greatest power among creatures you control. Whenever another creature enters, its controller may draw a card if its power is greater than every other creature's.

The draw is symmetric. Your 9/9 entering draws you a card; an opponent's creature that becomes the biggest on the battlefield draws THEM one. Keep the biggest creature on your side.

With only Selvala out, X is 2: she needs a fatty or a pump to do anything big.

Big-mana lines
+
Selvala, Heart of the Wilds + Bygone Colossus

warp the Colossus for {3} and Selvala taps for 9 that turn. You draw a card as it enters as the biggest creature. Warp exiles it at end of turn, but you can cast it again later.

+
Selvala, Heart of the Wilds + Quirion Ranger

return a Forest to untap Selvala once a turn: the same big X twice.

+
Selvala, Heart of the Wilds + Scryb Ranger

the same untap, with flash and protection from blue.

+
Selvala, Heart of the Wilds + Wirewood Symbiote

return an Elf (Selvala is one) to untap a creature. Bounce a mana elf to untap Selvala, then replay the elf.

+
Selvala, Heart of the Wilds + Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus

Zopandrel doubles every creature's power at the beginning of each combat. Tap Selvala after combat starts and X doubles.

Hyrax Tower Scout

enters and untaps Selvala.

++
Temur Sabertooth + Hyrax Tower Scout + Selvala, Heart of the Wilds

pay {1}{G} to return the Scout and recast it for {2}{G} to untap Selvala; with her own {G}, a loop costs six. Once she taps for seven or more (one 7-power creature), every loop makes mana, as much as you like. Each recast is also a creature entering, which can draw off Selvala. A three-card loop, so it fits Bracket 3.

Great Oak Guardian

flash; your whole team gets +2/+2 and untaps. X goes up by 2 and Selvala taps again.

Legolas's Quick Reflexes

split second: untap Selvala with hexproof.

Concordant Crossroads

everything has haste, so this turn's fatty can attack and Selvala can tap the turn she arrives. Opponents' creatures get haste too.

Finishers
Craterhoof Behemoth

haste, and every creature gets trample and +X/+X, where X is the number of creatures you control. With eight creatures out that is usually lethal.

Pathbreaker Ibex

Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus

doubles every creature's power and toughness at each combat.

Finale of Devastation

Ghalta, Primal Hunger

costs {X} less for your creatures' total power: often two mana. A 12/12 trampler and a Selvala draw.

Kogla, the Titan Ape

fights a creature on entering, and destroys an artifact or enchantment on each attack.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep two lands, a mana elf or two, and Selvala. Turn two Selvala off a turn-one elf is the strongest start.

Turn three or four, land one fatty (Bygone Colossus, Ghalta, a tutored six-drop) and Selvala's X jumps to five or more.

Then find an untap creature, double the mana, and find Craterhoof Behemoth with Natural Order or Eldritch Evolution.
