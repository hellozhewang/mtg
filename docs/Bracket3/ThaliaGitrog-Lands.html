Before attacking, decide what you will sacrifice. Thalia and The Gitrog Monster demands a creature or land even if losing it is inconvenient. Feed it a token or a land you can replay; keep the engine pieces that make the next attack profitable.

This is a Bracket 3 lands-and-creatures midrange deck for four-player games. Its three Game Changers are Aura Shards, Field of the Dead, and Gaea's Cradle. It wins through combat after building resources and restricting opposing options, with no planned infinite loop or mass land denial.

Make every land do more than one job

Thalia and The Gitrog Monster

Extra land plays accelerate the engine, opposing creatures and nonbasic lands enter tapped, and attacking converts expendable material into a card. First strike plus deathtouch makes ordinary blockers poor trades, but does not make the commander unblockable or immune to removal.

Exploration

A cheap extra land play becomes repeatable ramp with a fetchland and Ramunap Excavator. It needs lands in hand or permission to play them from the graveyard; it supplies neither by itself.

Azusa, Lost but Seeking

Adds two land plays on top of the commander's additional play. Deploy her when you can actually use those plays, particularly after Life from the Loam.

Lotus Cobra

A fetchland entering and its fetched land entering produce separate mana triggers. Use that mana during the current step or phase rather than expecting it to survive until combat.

Tireless Provisioner

Turns land drops into Treasures that can bank mana across turns. Those Treasures cannot pay the commander's sacrifice requirement, which specifically asks for a creature or land.

Nissa, Resurgent Animist

Produces mana from each landfall and finds an Elf or Elemental on the second resolution each turn. Fetchlands enable that second trigger; possible hits include Aftermath Analyst, Titania, Protector of Argoth, and Lumra, Bellow of the Woods, but you do not choose the hit.

Spelunking

Makes your lands enter untapped, including those returned by Splendid Reclamation or found with Scapeshift. That lets a mass land return finance a follow-up play when Archon of Emeria is absent.

Delighted Halfling

Makes the commander and other legendary spells uncounterable when its colored mana pays for them. Its colored mana cannot pay for nonlegendary spells; use its colorless mode for those.

Deathrite Shaman

Offers acceleration and selective graveyard interaction. Its mana ability needs a land card in a graveyard, and exiling your own fetchland spends a resource you might otherwise replay.

Gaea's Cradle

Converts the token army into enough mana for a large Finale of Devastation. Without creatures it produces no mana through its printed ability, so do not treat it as a dependable opening green source.

Reuse the lands without depending entirely on the commander

Life from the Loam

Restocks fetchlands and utility lands, while dredge fills the graveyard for later recovery. Dredging replaces a fresh draw, so stop doing it when you need an answer that is not already accessible.

Ramunap Excavator

Replays fetchlands and lands sacrificed to the commander. Playing a land from the graveyard still consumes one of your available land plays.

Crucible of Worlds

Provides the same land access through a permanent that survives creature sweepers. It does not grant additional land plays or make a land's sacrifice ability free to repeat without limits.

Icetill Explorer

Combines an extra land play, graveyard land access, and milling from landfall. Its milling is mandatory, so monitor library size during large landfall sequences.

Mole Man, Moloid Master

Replays lands and makes a Moloid on every landfall. Those bodies feed the commander, Skullclamp, Aura Shards, and Gaea's Cradle; their optional attack milling can stock the graveyard further.

The Necrobloom

Supplies Plants, then Zombies once seven differently named lands are present. Its land dredge can return the very land sacrificed to the commander in place of that attack trigger's draw, trading fresh cards for reliable land access.

Six

Gives nonland permanent cards in your graveyard retrace during your turn. Discard a land and pay the spell's normal costs to rebuild an engine; it cannot retrace Finale of Devastation or other nonpermanent spells, and Archon of Emeria still limits casting.

Aftermath Analyst

Mills early and later sacrifices itself to return all your graveyard lands. Its activated ability can operate on an opponent's turn and does not count as casting a spell under Archon of Emeria.

Splendid Reclamation

Cashes in a graveyard full of fetchlands and sacrificed lands for a large landfall turn. The lands normally return tapped; Spelunking changes that. Check for graveyard interaction before committing.

Lumra, Bellow of the Woods

Combines mass land recovery with a large vigilant reach creature. It can rebuild after a board wipe without needing the commander to survive.

Ancient Greenwarden

Grants land replay and doubles triggered abilities caused by lands entering, including Field of the Dead and Lotus Cobra. It does not double the commander's attack trigger or Titania's land-death trigger.

Eternal Witness

Retrieves the answer, protection spell, or finisher that land-only recursion cannot recover. Use creature tutors to find it when the most important card is already in the graveyard.

Draw actual cards as well as recycling lands

Esper Sentinel

Offers inexpensive draw pressure while opponents develop. Opponents can pay, so it supplements the draw plan rather than guaranteeing cards every turn.

Sylvan Library

Finds the right mix of lands, interaction, and engines. Pay life selectively in faster games; repeated dredging should not prevent you from finding a missing defensive spell.

Skullclamp

Kills an unboosted Moloid, Scute Swarm, or Plant through its toughness reduction and draws two cards. A normal 2/2 Zombie survives equipping; sacrifice that equipped creature to the commander to draw three cards across the two abilities.

Tymna the Weaver

Rewards combat damage to different opponents, rather than the number of creatures connecting. Spread safe attacks using flying creatures, Dauthi Voidwalker, or excess tokens, and budget the life payment.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

Turns an expendable creature, artifact, or land into either opposing sacrifices or cards and life loss. Each opponent chooses, so three draws are not guaranteed. Voice of Victory's Warriors can feed Braids if you stack the end-step triggers to resolve Braids before their delayed sacrifice.

The Gitrog Monster

Adds another land play and draws when one or more lands enter your graveyard from anywhere. Sacrificing a land to the commander yields its own draw plus this trigger; sacrificing many lands simultaneously to Scapeshift produces only one Gitrog trigger.

Archivist of Oghma

Converts opposing library searches into cards and life. Aven Mindcensor restricting a search does not stop it from being a search, even when the opponent fails to find anything.

Disrupt the table while preserving your land engine

Archon of Emeria

Restricts every player to one spell per turn, including you. Land plays and activated abilities remain available, and Finale of Devastation puts its creature directly onto the battlefield. After casting your spell, you cannot cast a protection spell that turn while Archon remains, even if that spell would be free.

Aven Mindcensor

Flash it in before an opposing search resolves to restrict the search to the top four cards. This also weakens the replacement searches on Path to Exile and Assassin's Trophy, but does not guarantee that they miss.

Drana and Linvala

Shuts down activated abilities of opposing creatures, including creature mana abilities, while leaving yours usable. It does not stop their triggered or static abilities, or activated abilities of noncreature permanents.

Dauthi Voidwalker

Prevents opposing cards from reaching their graveyards and offers an evasive attacker for Tymna. Its own sacrifice ability and the commander's sacrifice are different costs; one sacrifice cannot pay for both.

Aura Shards

Turns each creature entering under your control into optional artifact or enchantment removal. Field of the Dead, Mole Man, Moloid Master, and the other token engines can dismantle several pieces in a turn. Lands themselves do not trigger it without a creature entering as a consequence.

Phyrexian Tower

Sacrifice Archon of Emeria before combat when you need to cast several spells for a finishing turn. Sacrificing Archon to the commander's attack trigger also removes its restriction, but happens too late to add new attackers to that combat.

Bojuka Bog

Clears a graveyard that was already stocked before Dauthi Voidwalker arrived. Knight of the Reliquary can find it at instant speed once the Knight can tap; playing it as a normal land follows normal land-play timing.

Boseiju, Who Endures

Channels an answer to a dangerous artifact or enchantment without casting a spell. Recover it with Life from the Loam when repeated removal matters, remembering that each channel activation consumes mana and the land card.

Toxic Deluge

Resets a creature board that has outgrown yours. It also kills your own small creatures and tokens; indestructible does not stop toughness reduction. Keep land-based resources available to rebuild.

Keep the decisive turn intact

Voice of Victory

Stops opponents from casting during your turn and creates attack fodder. When it attacks alongside the commander, put the commander's trigger on the stack first and mobilize above it; the Warriors enter before you must sacrifice. Opponents can still activate abilities.

Grand Abolisher

Protects your turn from opposing spells and activated abilities of their artifacts, creatures, and enchantments. Opposing land abilities and triggered abilities remain available, so assess those before committing everything.

Sylvan Safekeeper

Trades lands for shroud on your creatures, protecting crucial engines without casting another spell. Shroud also prevents your own targeting, and losing too many lands can stall you if the graveyard engine is disrupted.

Heroic Intervention

Protects your permanents from most targeted removal and destruction. It does not stop untargeted exile or toughness reduction, and Archon of Emeria can prevent you from casting it after another spell.

Flawless Maneuver

Protects the creature board from destruction while leaving mana available if the commander is present. It does not protect Aura Shards or your other noncreature engines, and its free cost does not bypass Archon of Emeria.

Clever Concealment

Uses your creatures to help phase out important nonland permanents before a wipe, including many exile wipes. Unlike indestructible, phasing also avoids Toxic Deluge; lands cannot be chosen.

Turn the engine into a lethal attack

Field of the Dead

With seven differently named lands, every land entering creates a Zombie. All 38 lands in this list have different names. Lands entering simultaneously see one another for the condition, making Scapeshift and mass land recovery particularly strong.

Scapeshift

Converts your existing land count into a tailored land package and a burst of landfall. With at least seven lands, find Field of the Dead plus enough differently named lands to switch it on. It searches the library, so it cannot immediately retrieve a Field or Cradle you sacrificed from the battlefield.

Scute Swarm

Becomes an escalating token engine once you control six lands. Copies created by a batch of simultaneous land entries do not retroactively trigger for those same entries; subsequent land drops are what multiply the new Swarms.

Felidar Retreat

Makes an army when you lack bodies or puts counters and vigilance on the team when you already have them. If several triggers are pending, order them so creature creation resolves before the counter-granting triggers when appropriate.

Titania, Protector of Argoth

Returns a land on entry and converts each of your lands dying into a 5/3 Elemental. Fetchlands, the commander's sacrifice, and Scapeshift all provide fuel; unlike The Gitrog Monster, Titania triggers separately for each land sacrificed together.

Avenger of Zendikar

Supplies an immediate army whose size depends on your land count, then turns subsequent landfall into Plant counters. Save a land play or an uncracked fetchland to begin growing those Plants after the Avenger resolves.

Craterhoof Behemoth

Gives the established army trample and a large power boost. It has haste itself but does not give haste to other creatures, so a newly created token army normally needs to survive until your next turn before attacking.

Finale of Devastation

Finds a creature from the library or graveyard for utility at low values and lethal combat at X of at least 10. At that threshold it gives your creatures haste and +X/+X, so finding Craterhoof Behemoth can immediately convert fresh tokens into attackers. Budget at least twelve mana for X equals 10.

Akroma's Will

With the commander present as you cast it, both modes provide flying, double strike, vigilance, lifelink, indestructible, and protection from all colors. It converts an established board into a dangerous attack or protects it, but does not grant haste to fresh tokens.

Find the piece the board actually needs

Elvish Reclaimer

Turns an expendable land into Field of the Dead, Gaea's Cradle, or a defensive utility land. It requires two mana, tapping, and sacrificing a land, so plan a turn ahead when summoning sickness matters.

Knight of the Reliquary

Tutors a land while growing from the lands already in your graveyard. Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth makes all your lands Forests and therefore eligible for its sacrifice cost; Yavimaya also helps opponents produce green mana.

Green Sun's Zenith

At X equals zero, finds Dryad Arbor as early creature-based acceleration. Higher values find green engines or Craterhoof Behemoth; it cannot find white-only protection creatures. Dryad Arbor cannot tap for mana the turn it enters unless it has haste.

Chord of Calling

Finds a creature directly onto the battlefield, including Aven Mindcensor in response to a search or a missing engine at an opponent's end step. Summoning-sick creatures can help convoke because you are not activating their own tap abilities.

Eladamri's Call

Finds any creature but puts it into your hand, so reserve mana and a legal casting window for that creature. Under Archon of Emeria, cast Call on an opponent's turn to leave your next turn's spell available.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep three functional lands, an early accelerator, and either a draw engine or a useful interaction piece. A two-land hand needs credible early acceleration and a way to keep finding resources; Gaea's Cradle without a creature does not count as a reliable land. Reject hands loaded with expensive payoffs but no early development.

An example keep is Windswept Heath, Bayou, Command Tower, Birds of Paradise, Sylvan Library, Swords to Plowshares, and Ramunap Excavator. Start with an untapped green source and Birds, then choose Library or Excavator according to whether you need fresh cards or land reuse. A turn-three commander is possible, but do not abandon an urgent removal window merely to curve out.

Preserve fetchlands when an upcoming landfall engine will make them worth more, but spend them when necessary to cast your spells. Fetch colored sources for Drana and Linvala, Dauthi Voidwalker, or Grand Abolisher according to the hand you actually have, rather than automatically finding more green.

Establish one source of continuing material and one source of cards before piling on expensive payoffs. The deck functions without its commander through its land-recursion creatures, token engines, and independent draw, although repeated commander removal slows its extra land plays and attack-based draw.

Against graveyard hate, prioritize removing the hate with Aura Shards, Boseiju, Who Endures, or your flexible removal. Meanwhile, normal land drops still trigger Field of the Dead and the token creatures. Do not mill or dredge aggressively into an exile effect merely because recursion is your usual plan.

Before a finishing turn, count available attackers, haste, colored mana, and your spell allowance under Archon of Emeria. Find protection if the board is already lethal; find a payoff if the board is safe but cannot close. An established army plus Akroma's Will or Craterhoof Behemoth needs less setup than building an enormous fresh army, while Finale of Devastation at X of at least 10 supplies the haste needed to finish immediately.
