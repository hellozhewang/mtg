Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Azami can tap herself and other Wizards for cards immediately, even on the turn they enter. Her activation uses the words "Tap an untapped Wizard," not the tap symbol. Build a useful Wizard board before casting her, and leave enough of that board untapped to threaten interaction rather than automatically drawing everything at once.

This is Bracket 3 Wizard control with a three-card blink combo, draw-based life loss, and token combat finishes. The three Game Changers are Rhystic Study, Cyclonic Rift, and Force of Will. The list contains 24 Wizards including the commander, 35 lands plus two spell/land cards, eight mana-producing artifacts, and Sapphire Medallion. It omits fast two-card combo engines, mass land denial, and extra turns. A-tier reflects a strong draw engine with a more demanding finish than a compact two-card combo deck; expect setup and interaction rather than a guaranteed goldfish turn.

Turn Wizards into a working engine
Azami, Lady of Scrolls

Every untapped Wizard becomes an immediate card without spending mana. Activate before passing priority after she resolves if you need value immediately. Drawing is optional, so stop before an empty library unless the winning replacement effect is ready.

+
Faerie Seer + Siren Stormtamer

Cheap early Wizards make the five-mana commander productive on arrival. Seer improves your next draws; Stormtamer protects against a spell or ability targeting you or a creature you control, but cannot stop an untargeted wipe.

+
Baral, Chief of Compliance + Stonybrook Banneret

Baral discounts instants and sorceries; Banneret discounts Wizards and Merfolk, including Azami. A Merfolk Wizard receives only one Banneret reduction. Neither reduces colored pips, so preserve three blue sources for the commander.

Aphetto Alchemist

Once it can use its tap-symbol ability, untap a mana rock for acceleration or another Wizard for an extra card. With Azami it can instead be tapped immediately to draw. One activation cannot do both jobs.

+
Fatestitcher + Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx

A developed Wizard board makes Nykthos a powerful blue-mana engine, and Fatestitcher can untap it. Unearth gives Fatestitcher haste for a one-turn burst. It still costs two mana to activate Nykthos each time; count net production before committing.

Unstoppable Plan

Untaps your nonland permanents at your own end step. Spend Wizards and rocks during your turn, then recover them for opponents' turns. It does not trigger at every opponent's end step and does not untap your lands.

+
Intruder Alarm + Azami, Lady of Scrolls

Every creature entering, including an opponent's, untaps all creatures. Draw with available Wizards before the untap trigger resolves to turn entries into repeated refills. Alarm also prevents normal creature untaps for everyone; it helps opposing mana creatures and can strand your own board if no creatures enter. Deploy it with follow-up creatures or another untap effect ready.

+
Intruder Alarm + Docent of Perfection // Final Iteration

Casting an instant or sorcery creates a Wizard token; its entry untaps the team, and that new token can immediately draw through Azami. This is a repeatable value engine, not an infinite loop by itself. The transformed side keeps producing Wizards.

+
Rhystic Study + Mystic Remora

Independent draw engines let you develop while Azami is absent or too expensive. Ask for each tax consistently. Let Remora go when its cumulative upkeep would prevent development or protection; it mainly punishes noncreature-heavy tables.

+
Archmage Emeritus + Teferi's Ageless Insight

Emeritus converts your interaction and blink spells into cards; Insight doubles most draws, including Azami activations. Both increase the risk of drawing too far during the combo. Insight does not double the first normal draw of your draw step.

Make the mana support the commander
++
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet + Mind Stone

Cheap acceleration is how the deck reaches five mana while still developing Wizards. Mind Stone is a later card when mana is no longer the bottleneck. Sol Ring pays generic costs but cannot supply any of Azami's three blue pips.

++
Coldsteel Heart + Sky Diamond + Fellwar Stone

Heart and Diamond enter tapped, so deploy them before the turn you need the mana. Choose blue for Heart. Fellwar Stone only produces blue if an opponent's land can; otherwise its mana is useful for generic costs, and an opponent must control a land producing a color for it to work at all.

+
Sapphire Medallion + Thought Vessel

Medallion helps you cast several blue spells in a turn and reduces the blink loop's generic cost. Vessel makes mana and keeps the large hand. Do not confuse Medallion's discount with mana production, and remember colorless artifacts do not get its discount.

+
Thran Dynamo + Aphetto Alchemist

Dynamo supplies three mana toward a blink spell, a large draw spell, or several creatures. Alchemist can reuse it after summoning sickness wears off. This is finite acceleration, not an infinite combination.

+
High Tide + Turnabout

With several Islands, High Tide turns each into at least two blue mana. Float mana before Turnabout untaps your lands for another pass. High Tide helps every player's Islands, so opponents may gain interaction mana too. Turnabout can instead untap creatures for cards or tap an opponent's creatures to open combat.

Dramatic Reversal

A single spell that reuses Wizards and mana rocks together. Tap the relevant permanents before it resolves. It does not untap lands, and the deck has no artifact imprinted with it for an automatic loop.

+
Minamo, School at Water's Edge + Riptide Laboratory

Minamo converts spare blue mana into one more Azami activation. Riptide protects a Wizard from removal or reuses an entry ability, at the cost of recasting it. Riptide itself normally makes colorless mana.

+
Cavern of Souls + Mystic Sanctuary

Name Wizard on Cavern and use its restricted colored mana to make that creature spell uncounterable. It cannot supply blue for counterspells. Fetch Sanctuary once you have three other Islands to put a spent instant or sorcery on top, then draw it with Azami. Early Sanctuary is a tapped land with no recursion trigger.

+
Sea Gate Restoration // Sea Gate, Reborn + Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

Usually prioritize their land faces when you would otherwise miss a land drop. Late Restoration refills a large hand and removes your hand-size limit; Stupor is instant-speed interaction. Casting a spell face gives up that land drop, and paying three life is required for either land face to enter untapped.

The decisive blink combo
++
Peregrine Drake + Archaeomancer + Ghostly Flicker

With both creatures on the battlefield and lands that collectively produce more than the blink spell costs, cast Flicker targeting both. They return; Drake untaps up to five lands and Archaeomancer returns Flicker from the graveyard. Flicker has finished resolving before Archaeomancer's trigger is put on the stack, so it is a legal target. Five ordinary Islands produce five mana for a three-mana spell: each repeat nets two blue mana. Repeat a chosen finite number of times for as much mana as needed. Opponents can interrupt the spell or the entry triggers.

++
Peregrine Drake + Archaeomancer + Displace

Redundant three-card version with the same mana math. Displace only blinks creatures and returns them to their owners, so use creatures you own. Both blink spells can also protect creatures from targeted removal or reuse entry abilities without the full combo.

++
Azami, Lady of Scrolls + Archaeomancer + Ghostly Flicker

During the mana-positive Drake loop, tap Archaeomancer to Azami between iterations. Each blink returns Archaeomancer untapped, and summoning sickness does not prevent this activation. This adds controlled card draw to the mana loop; Azami herself need not be untapped.

+
Psychosis Crawler + Azami, Lady of Scrolls

Each card drawn makes every opponent lose one life, allowing the blink-and-draw loop to finish the table without combat. Ordinary draw bursts also become substantial pressure. Check opponents' life totals against the cards left in your library; extra mana does not create extra library cards. Crawler dies if your hand becomes empty and nothing boosts its toughness.

+
Laboratory Maniac + Azami, Lady of Scrolls

After drawing down the library, resolve Maniac with protection available and draw from an empty library to win. Maniac is itself a Wizard, so it can immediately supply that final activation. Having an empty library alone does not win; you must attempt to draw, with Maniac still present.

Blue Sun's Zenith

A scalable refill or outlet for the loop's large blue-mana supply. Target an opponent for more cards than remain in their library to eliminate them. To reuse Zenith against all three players, it must be drawn again after shuffling into your library; with an empty library and Azami's repeatable draw from the blink loop, it becomes the only card to draw. Keep Laboratory Maniac off the battlefield if you specifically want to execute that line instead of winning on an empty draw.

+
Archmage Emeritus + Ghostly Flicker

Emeritus forces a draw when you cast each Flicker, so you cannot repeat the loop arbitrarily with a finite library and no winning replacement effect. Stop in time to deploy Maniac or Crawler. This deck normally sets up the kill in its own main phase so it can cast those creatures; infinite mana during an opponent's turn does not grant them flash.

++
Archaeomancer + Ghostly Flicker + Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx

An alternate mana loop can blink Archaeomancer and Nykthos. With blue devotion at least six, Nykthos makes at least six while its activation and Flicker cost five total, so each repeat gains mana. At devotion five the loop is mana-neutral but can repeatedly reset Archaeomancer for Azami draws. Start with enough mana to activate Nykthos, account for discounts separately, and do not count blue symbols on lands toward devotion.

Win through a stalled or disrupted combo
Minn, Wily Illusionist

Drawing the second card of each turn makes an Illusion. On your turn the normal draw counts as the first; on opponents' turns you usually need two Azami activations. Illusions grow with their number and can put permanents from your hand onto the battlefield when they die. They are not Wizards, so Azami cannot tap them for cards.

Lyra, Tolarian Archangel

Drawing at least three cards before an end step begins produces a 3/3 flying Angel at that end step. Her condition must already be satisfied as the step starts. This makes a real evasive clock from the draw engine, though one board of tapped Wizards cannot automatically supply three draws on every player's turn without untapping them. Angel tokens are not Wizards.

Docent of Perfection // Final Iteration

Three Wizards are easy to establish; its spell trigger then transforms it into a flying anthem that gives your Wizards +2/+1 and flying. This turns draw creatures and spell-generated Wizard tokens into a combat finish. Neither face of Docent itself is a Wizard.

+
Cyclonic Rift + Docent of Perfection // Final Iteration

Overload Rift at the last opponent's end step to clear opposing nonland permanents, then attack with the flying Wizard army. Alternatively clear hate pieces and attempt the protected combo. Seven mana is substantial: retain development rather than sitting idle with Rift stranded in hand.

+
Perplexing Test + Minn, Wily Illusionist

Returning nontoken creatures preserves your Illusions and Angels for an attack but bounces your own Wizards and Azami. The token mode instead clears opposing armies while preserving your nontoken engine. Choose the mode based on the actual board, not a presumed one-sided wipe.

Defend the engine without countering everything
Patron Wizard

Every untapped Wizard can tax any spell by one. Activate once, let the opponent decide whether to pay, then tax again while their spell is still on the stack if necessary. Wizards used for this cannot also pay Azami's cost until they untap. It is pressure and protection, not a guaranteed hard lock.

+
Glen Elendra Archmage + Glen Elendra Guardian

Archmage can normally counter two noncreature spells across its persist return; Guardian flashes in and can trade its starting counter for one noncreature counterspell, giving that spell's controller a card. Neither ability has a tap cost, so these Wizards can draw for Azami while still threatening their counter abilities if mana remains.

Malevolent Hermit // Benevolent Geist

Its front face taxes a noncreature spell for three, then disturb supplies a flying Wizard that makes your noncreature spells uncounterable. That does not protect your creatures from removal or stop an opponent from exiling a spell from the stack. If the disturbed side would go to the graveyard, exile it instead.

+
Tishana's Tidebinder + Venser, Shaper Savant

Tidebinder stops a crucial activated or triggered ability and can blank its artifact, creature, or planeswalker source while she remains. Venser returns a spell or permanent to hand, including a spell that cannot be countered. Neither is a substitute for removing every threat; save them for pivotal effects.

Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun

Gives your instants and sorceries split second while they are spells on the stack. This makes the blink spell or a decisive answer harder to contest, but does not protect creature spells or the entry triggers after a blink resolves. You also cannot activate Azami while your own split-second spell is on the stack. Mana abilities and triggered abilities still work.

++
Force of Will + Force of Negation + Flare of Denial

Free interaction protects a five-mana commander while your mana is committed. Will costs a blue card and one life; Negation's alternate cost works only on opponents' turns and only targets noncreature spells; Flare requires sacrificing a nontoken blue creature. Generated Wizard tokens cannot pay Flare's sacrifice cost.

+++
Pact of Negation + Mana Drain + Counterspell + Swan Song

Reserve hard counters for wins, sweepers, and effects that stop your own engine. Pact creates a mandatory three-generic-two-blue payment next upkeep or you lose, so it is a finishing-turn safeguard rather than casual early interaction. Drain's later mana is colorless. Swan Song cannot counter creatures, artifacts, or planeswalkers.

++
Pongify + Resculpt + Otawara, Soaring City

Pongify answers a creature cheaply; Resculpt exiles an artifact or creature, including indestructible threats, while leaving a 4/4 token. Otawara's channel answers several permanent types from a land slot and is discounted by your legendary creatures. Bounce enchantment hate and be ready for it to be recast.

+
Snapcaster Mage + Archaeomancer

Snapcaster immediately reuses interaction, while Archaeomancer returns it to hand. Flashback exiles the spell afterward, so flashing back your only available blink spell does not sustain the recursion loop. Use the redundant blink spell if the first has been exiled.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Its sacrifice activation exiles opponents' graveyards while preserving your own recursion. Deploy it before a graveyard deck reaches its decisive turn. If no graveyard matters, it can instead become a card for one mana.

Tutor for the missing resource
→
Trinket Mage → Sol Ring

Choose acceleration when the bottleneck is paying for Azami and protecting her. The tutor leaves behind a Wizard, unlike a generic artifact-search spell.

→
Trinket Mage → Soul-Guide Lantern

Find graveyard interaction against a recursion or reanimation threat instead of automatically taking mana.

→
Tribute Mage → Sapphire Medallion

Best when several blue spells in your hand have generic costs. If you instead need actual blue mana for Azami's pips, take Arcane Signet or a blue-producing two-mana rock.

→
Spellseeker → High Tide

Find a mana burst once several Islands are in play. The same tutor can find Dramatic Reversal, inexpensive removal, or a cheap counterspell according to the board. It cannot directly find either three-mana blink spell.

→
Step Through → Archaeomancer

Wizardcycling for two finds the missing recursion creature. It is an activated ability, not casting the five-mana sorcery. When already prepared to empty the library, the same search can find Laboratory Maniac.

→
Vedalken Aethermage → Patron Wizard

Wizardcycle for three at instant speed when spell control is missing. It can instead find a specific protective Wizard or Archaeomancer. Casting it as a flash Wizard is sometimes better than spending the turn tutoring.

→
Merchant Scroll → Ghostly Flicker

Find the blink spell when both creatures are ready. Scroll also reaches Displace, Blue Sun's Zenith, and Long-Term Plans, but it searches only blue instants.

→
Solve the Equation → Displace

Find the redundant blink spell if Flicker is unavailable. It can also find a recovery spell or interaction, but it cannot search for creatures or enchantments.

→
Long-Term Plans → Peregrine Drake

Universal search fills the gap left by Wizard tutors: Drake is not a Wizard. The card goes third from the top, so keep three draws available and avoid shuffling it away before drawing it. This also finds an engine or finisher when that is the missing piece.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep three usable lands, a cheap mana artifact, an early Wizard, and some protection or independent card draw. A representative keep is three Islands, Arcane Signet, Stonybrook Banneret, Counterspell, and Rhystic Study. Lead on the rock, then decide whether development or holding up the counter matters more; Azami is available from the command zone and need not be in the hand.

Two-land hands need credible access to more mana or cards, not simply several expensive Wizards. Mulligan hands of combo pieces with no ramp, and hands with several colorless utility lands that cannot pay three blue. The two spell/land cards are included to reduce missed land drops, not to justify routinely keeping one land.

Aim to establish useful Wizards and mana during turns one through three, then resolve Azami when she can immediately refill and survive. Spend your draws deliberately across turns to support Minn and Lyra, and reserve untapped Wizards for Patron when an opponent is threatening a decisive spell. Azami improves dramatically with a board; casting her into an empty battlefield simply to use all your mana is often poor sequencing.

For the combo, verify sufficient land output, graveyard access, the correct targets, and protection before starting. Removal of Archaeomancer, graveyard exile in response to its trigger, or a counter on the blink spell interrupts the loop. Activated-ability hate shuts off Azami and Patron; entry-trigger hate shuts off the Drake loop; draw limits punish the entire plan. Answer the piece that actually stops your line instead of trying to counter all three opponents' normal development.

Keep a fallback draw source or creature in reserve against sweepers. The deck can still play through lost access to Azami with Study, Remora, Emeritus, the token makers, and the independent three-card mana loop, but repeated commander removal makes it substantially weaker. No maximum hand size is convenient, not mandatory; discard surplus lands before using a tutor solely to avoid discarding.
