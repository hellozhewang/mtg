Zada turns one spell into twenty. Any instant or sorcery that targets ONLY Zada is copied once for every other creature you control, each copy targeting a different one. So a one-mana cantrip with twenty creatures out draws twenty cards, and a two-mana pump spell pumps the whole army. The main engine is cheap spells that target your own creature, and Goblins to copy them onto. Recruiter into Ringleader or Muxus is the backup when Zada cannot stick. Muxus is a six-mana setup payoff; keep the cheap bodies, cantrips and rituals for the normal Zada plan.

Two rules matter. Copies are not CAST: Storm-Kiln Artist triggers on copies, but Young Pyromancer, Birgi, Electro and Runaway Steam-Kin only see the original spell. And Zada must be targetable by you — Swiftfoot Boots is fine, because hexproof only stops opponents, but never give Zada shroud.

The commander
Zada, Hedron Grinder

four mana. Cast a spell that targets only Zada and it is copied for each other creature you control. Cast your creatures first, then the spells.

Swiftfoot Boots

haste and hexproof for Zada — opponents can't target it, you still can.

Hexing Squelcher

your spells can't be countered, so the spell Zada copies resolves — though removing Zada in response still fizzles it.

The engine — one spell, every creature
+
Zada, Hedron Grinder + Expedite

one red mana: every creature gains haste and you draw a card per creature. Crimson Wisps, Impolite Entrance, Might of the Meek, Renegade Tactics, Ancestral Anger and Accelerate do the same draw.

++
Zada, Hedron Grinder + Storm-Kiln Artist + Reckless Ransacking

each copy makes a Treasure, and Storm-Kiln makes another for the cast and for every copy — about two Treasures per creature for two mana, and the whole team gets +3/+2. Sudden Breakthrough and Ancestors' Aid do the same with first strike.

+
Zada, Hedron Grinder + Fists of Flame

cast it after a cantrip turn. Each copy draws first, then gives its creature +1/+0 for every card drawn so far this turn, plus trample — after twenty draws each creature gets +21/+0 or more.

+
Zada, Hedron Grinder + Haste Magic

every creature gets +3/+1 and haste, and each copy exiles a card you can play until your next end step.

+
Zada, Hedron Grinder + Twinflame

two mana: a hasty copy of every creature you control. With Impact Tremors or General Kreat out, each copy entering is damage to each opponent. Molten Duplication does the same. Copies of legendary creatures still enter and trigger your payoffs, but you keep only one of each name.

+
Zada, Hedron Grinder + Temur Battle Rage

the finisher: double strike for the whole army, plus trample if anything has 4 power.

Frontline Heroism

whenever you cast a spell that targets one of your creatures, a hasty 1/1 and a copy of the spell for it. Resolve its trigger before Zada's, so the new Soldier gets a Zada copy too.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

cast Twinflame on any creature you control in your main phase, then flash Dualcaster in with Twinflame still on the stack. Dualcaster copies Twinflame onto itself; the hasty token copies it again; repeat. Unlimited hasty 2/2s. Molten Duplication also works when its token copies Dualcaster.

+
Grinning Ignus + Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

{R} returns Ignus to your hand for {C}{C}{R}; recast it for that; Birgi refunds {R}. Unlimited casts and enters at sorcery speed — damage with Impact Tremors or General Kreat. Ruby Medallion makes every lap a mana richer.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Zealous Conscripts

Kiki copies Conscripts and the copy untaps Kiki — target Kiki itself. Unlimited hasty 3/3s.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Goblin Bombardment

Kiki copies the Crafter; the copy champions Kiki, taps for 3 damage, and is sacrificed; Kiki returns untapped with haste. Unlimited 3-damage shots; Skirk Prospector works as the outlet too. When the original Crafter enters you must exile another Goblin or Shaman (champion) or sacrifice it.

→+
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Conspicuous Snoop

Recruiter puts Kiki on top of your library, and Snoop copies itself with Kiki's ability, every copy hasty. Snoop needs haste or a turn first; all but the last copy end up tapped, so finish with Impact Tremors, General Kreat or Goblin Bombardment.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when
→
Goblin Matron → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

when you already hold Zealous Conscripts or Lightning Crafter.

→
Goblin Matron → Goblin Recruiter

when you need two or more pieces — Recruiter stacks them.

→
Goblin Matron → Krenko, Mob Boss

when you need bodies for Zada to copy onto.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Storm-Kiln Artist

power 2: the Treasure engine — or Dualcaster Mage when you hold a copy spell, Kiki-Jiki, Conspicuous Snoop or Young Pyromancer.

→
Gamble → Twinflame

any card: Twinflame when Dualcaster is in hand, Storm-Kiln Artist when you have spells but no mana, Underworld Breach for a big turn. Cast it with few cards in hand.

→
Underworld Breach → Expedite

escape cantrips from your graveyard by exiling three other cards each time. Not infinite, and Breach is sacrificed at the beginning of the next end step, but with Zada and Storm-Kiln each recast that turn is a pile of cards and Treasures.

Past in Flames

every instant and sorcery in your graveyard can be cast again this turn — a second Zada turn in one card.

Pinnacle Monk // Mystic Peak

returns an instant or sorcery to your hand, or plays as a land.

Goblin Recruiter setups — what to stack, top card first
→
Goblin Recruiter → Goblin Ringleader

TOP to BOTTOM: Goblin Ringleader; Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker; Lightning Crafter; Skirk Prospector; Hexing Squelcher. Draw or otherwise reach Ringleader, then cast it: its entry trigger puts the next four selected Goblins into your hand. Those creatures still need to be cast; Ringleader does not deploy them. If Ringleader is already in hand, start the pile with the four targets instead.

→
Goblin Recruiter → Muxus, Goblin Grandee

TOP to BOTTOM: Muxus, Goblin Grandee; Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker; Lightning Crafter; Skirk Prospector; Hexing Squelcher; Goblin Ringleader; Krenko, Mob Boss. Reach and cast Muxus; all six following cards are Goblin creatures with mana value five or less, so its entry trigger puts them onto the battlefield. If Muxus is already in hand, omit it from the pile and reveal the six immediately.

Goblin Ringleader

Put these four cards immediately UNDER the six-card Muxus package: Goblin Matron; General Kreat, the Boltbringer; Conspicuous Snoop; Goblin Instigator. When Muxus deploys Ringleader, the entry trigger puts this backup package into your hand. Resolve Ringleader before any other draw triggers and before the original Lightning Crafter's champion trigger. Only stack cards still in your library; substitute useful Goblins for pieces already in hand or on the battlefield.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

The original Crafter champions the Recruiter that made the pile, or the Ringleader AFTER its refill resolves. Keep Kiki and Prospector on the battlefield. Tap Kiki to copy Crafter; the hasty copy champions Kiki, taps for 3 damage, then is sacrificed to Prospector for {R}. Kiki returns untapped with haste. Repeat for lethal damage and red mana. No combat or commander is required, but removal or effects that stop the entry triggers/activated abilities can interrupt it.

Hexing Squelcher

The Muxus pile supplies ward for the other creatures and makes later spells uncounterable. Entering with Muxus does not protect the Muxus spell retroactively; when you need that protection, cast Squelcher before Muxus.

Conspicuous Snoop can cast the top Goblin payoff directly. A Zada cantrip normally draws several cards: account for every copy, putting spare cards above the payoff so the payoff is the LAST card drawn and the package stays below it. The next natural draw or casting through Snoop is simpler.

→
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

The existing Snoop line remains: with Conspicuous Snoop able to tap and a damage/sacrifice payoff available, leave Kiki on top to grant Snoop the copy ability. Choose this pile instead of the Muxus/Ringleader pile when Snoop already has the win.

Searches that shuffle undo the pile: use fetch lands and other tutors BEFORE Recruiter. Any extra draw, mill or exile also changes the positions; account for it before committing. Muxus only puts eligible Goblin CREATURES onto the battlefield: Goblin-typed instants/enchantments and Muxus itself are not hits. Recruiter does not put the payoff in your hand for free.

Protection and answers
Defense Grid

every spell costs {3} more except on its caster's turn, so answering Zada on your turn costs opponents three extra. They can still pay, and activated abilities aren't taxed. Your own Pyroblast and Deflecting Swat cost more on their turns too.

Deflecting Swat

free with Zada out; redirect the removal aimed at it — or the counterspell aimed at your big spell.

Untimely Malfunction

change the target of a single-target spell or ability — the removal pointed at Zada mid-turn.

Pyroblast

one mana against the blue answer.

Red Elemental Blast

the second copy.

Chaos Warp

the red answer to anything.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep mana, two or three creatures and a cheap spell. Zada on turn three or four with four bodies out already draws four off one Expedite.

Build the board first, then spend the turn: a Treasure spell to make mana, cantrips to draw, Twinflame or a pump to finish.

Nothing here stops a sweeper that doesn't target. Equip Swiftfoot Boots before the big turn (equipping is sorcery speed), keep Deflecting Swat or Untimely Malfunction for targeted removal, and after a wipe rebuild with Kher Keep and the token spells.
