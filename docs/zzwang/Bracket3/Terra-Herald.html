Self-mill and gain flying with Terra's Trance, connect for combat damage to reanimate power 3 or less utility and combo creatures for two mana, and double triggers with Delney to overwhelm the table.

Trance self-mill and combat reanimation
Terra, Herald of Hope

at the beginning of combat on your turn, Trance mills two cards and grants Terra flying until end of turn. Whenever Terra deals combat damage to a player, you may pay {2} to return target creature card with power three or less from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped.

Delney, Streetwise Lookout

power 2 creature that causes triggered abilities of creatures you control with power two or less to trigger an additional time. While controlling Delney, Terra's reanimation ability triggers twice upon dealing combat damage, allowing you to pay {2} twice to return TWO creatures in a single combat!

Master of Cruelties

power 1 demon with first strike and deathtouch. Can only attack alone and cannot be blocked except by one or more creatures. If Master of Cruelties attacks and is unblocked, the defending player's life total becomes one, setting up an immediate elimination.

Karmic Guide

power 2 flying Angel that returns ANY creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield upon entering. Reanimating Karmic Guide with Terra bypasses the power 3 restriction, allowing Karmic Guide to bring back Aurelia, the Warleader or Sun Titan.

Gray Merchant of Asphodel

power 2 Zombie that enters the battlefield and forces each opponent to lose life equal to your devotion to black while gaining you that much life.

Priest of Fell Rites

power 2 creature that pays {T}, sacrifices itself, and loses three life to reanimate any creature card from your graveyard, with unearth {2}{B}.

Loran of the Third Path

power 2 creature destroying target artifact or enchantment on entry.

Accursed Marauder

power 2 creature forcing each player to sacrifice a nontoken creature.

Graveyard stocking and haste enablers
Anger

gives all creatures you control haste while in your graveyard as long as you control a Mountain. Pitch it via Trance or looting to attack immediately with Terra and reanimated creatures.

Buried Alive

searches three creature cards (e.g., Delney, Master of Cruelties, Anger, or Karmic Guide) directly into your graveyard for three mana.

Entomb

instant-speed one-mana search putting any reanimation target into your graveyard.

Stitcher's Supplier

mills three cards on entry and three on death to fuel Terra's recursion targets.

Faithless Looting

draws two cards and discards two, filtering hands while putting large recursion targets into the graveyard.

Ripples of Undeath

surveils two on upkeep and pays one mana and life to draw a card while loading the graveyard.

Late-game bombs and combat multipliers (Turn 6+)
Aurelia, the Warleader

attacks to untap all creatures and grant an additional combat phase, allowing Terra to attack, fly, and trigger reanimation a second time in the same turn.

Sun Titan

enters and attacks to return any permanent card with mana value three or less from your graveyard to the battlefield.

Rise of the Dark Realms

puts all creature cards from all graveyards onto the battlefield under your control.

Ruinous Ultimatum

asymmetric seven-mana board wipe that destroys all nonland permanents target opponent controls.

Tutors, interaction, and opening hands
Demonic Tutor

finds Buried Alive, Delney, or protection.

Smothering Tithe

generates massive Treasure reserves off opposing card draws to easily fund Terra's {2} reanimation cost.

Teferi's Protection

phases out all your permanents in response to graveyard hate or board wipes.

Deflecting Swat

zero-mana redirection spell while controlling Terra to protect her during combat.

Deadly Rollick

free creature exile while controlling your commander.

Keep opening hands with two to three lands, early mana rocks (Sol Ring, Signets, Talismans), and a self-mill or discard enabler (Faithless Looting, Stitcher's Supplier, Ripples of Undeath). Cast Terra on turn three, trigger Trance to mill and take flight on turn four, and spend two mana to reanimate Delney, Karmic Guide, or Master of Cruelties. Close out games with Aurelia extra combats or Rise of the Dark Realms on Turn 6 or later.

House rules apply: infinite combo loops and compact wins are restricted to Turn 6 or later. Earlier turns focus on establishing board presence, graveyard stocking, and combat recursion.
