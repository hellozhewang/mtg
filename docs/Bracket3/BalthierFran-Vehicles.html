Reserve {1}{R}{G} before you attack. Balthier and Fran's extra-combat payment happens when their attack trigger resolves, before combat damage. They do not untap anything or give you a main phase between combats. Your Vehicles normally stay ready for the next attack because the commander gives them vigilance.

This Bracket 3 list emphasizes early development, haste, a small number of threatening attackers, and short combat turns. Its fifteen Vehicles supply pressure and resources. Ancient Tomb, Jeska's Will, and The One Ring are the three Game Changers. There is no planned infinite combo, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain. The usual plan is one additional combat, with more available when an established board makes them decisive.

The turn you want to repeat

Balthier and Fran

Their four power can crew every Vehicle in this list alone. The Vehicle must actually have been crewed by them that turn and must attack in the first combat phase to trigger the payment. Other pilots crewing a Vehicle do not qualify it. Once crewed, a Vehicle stays a creature until end of turn, covering all your combats. The commander can crew immediately after entering because crew is an ability of the Vehicle, not the commander's own tap ability.

Declare your intended attacks, pay for the additional combat if it advances the game, resolve the attack and damage effects, then attack again with the available creatures. A Vehicle already animated by another effect may still be crewed by the commander to meet the extra-combat condition. Returning the commander after it was removed creates a new object; crew with the new commander if you want its ability to recognize that crew action.

Rocketeer Boostbuggy

A cheap Vehicle that makes a Treasure on every attack. Put the commander's extra-combat trigger on the stack first and Boostbuggy's Treasure trigger above it, so the Treasure exists when the payment is due. One Treasure pays only one of the three required mana. Its exhaust ability can permanently animate it, but the commander still needs to crew it that turn for the additional-combat trigger.

Smuggler's Copter

A two-mana flying attacker that loots on each attack or block. Two attacks offer two opportunities to replace unwanted cards. Discard expensive artifacts when Goblin Welder can recover them, or spare lands when your mana is already developed.

Mighty Servant of Leuk-o

Crew it for the first time that turn with exactly two creatures to earn its combat-damage draw ability. Use Balthier and Fran plus a spare mana creature: exceeding the minimum crew power is legal. It can then draw two cards on each successful combat-damage event that turn. Crewing with only the commander first and adding another crew activation later does not earn the ability.

Gastal Thrillroller

Arrives animated with haste, making it a straightforward early attacker. Crew it with Balthier and Fran anyway when you want the extra-combat trigger. Its graveyard activation provides recovery at the cost of mana and a discarded card; the finality counter means it is exiled if it would subsequently die.

Thunderous Velocipede

A large, trampling three-mana Vehicle that improves later creatures and Vehicles with counters. The bonus is one counter for mana value four or less and three counters above that threshold. It does not put counters on creatures that merely become animated later or on itself from its own ability.

Make two attacks produce more than damage

The Regalia

Haste and crew one make it useful with or without the commander. Every attack finds the next land and puts it onto the battlefield tapped, so two attacks build future mana quickly. That tapped land normally cannot fund the current additional-combat payment.

Hedge Shredder

Its attack milling can turn lands from the library into tapped lands on your battlefield, while stocking artifact recursion. The land ability watches lands going from library to graveyard, not lands discarded from your hand or sacrificed from the battlefield. Its milling is optional.

Hoard Hauler

Trample helps it connect, then the damaged player's artifact count determines your Treasures. Those Treasures arrive after the commander's attack-trigger payment, so they cannot retroactively pay for that extra combat. Use them for later instant-speed plays, your postcombat main phase, or subsequent turns.

Esika's Chariot

Provides its own two Cats for crew and makes another token when it attacks. Copy a Cat for pressure or a Treasure for mana when that is more useful. The token is not created attacking and has no granted haste. The Cats lack the commander's Vehicle vigilance, so attacking with them normally taps them for the later combat too.

Skysovereign, Consul Flagship

Deals three to an opposing creature or planeswalker on entry and on each attack. Across two combats it can remove several small blockers or utility creatures while applying flying damage. Its triggered damage cannot target players.

N'Yami-Class Mother Ship

A flying, vigilant, hasty threat that turns each connection into a permanent from the top entering or a card in hand. It already has haste when cast without a support piece. Its ability can put a land onto the battlefield without using your normal land play.

The Thanos-Copter

Animates all Vehicles you control when its entry trigger resolves and rewards Vehicle combat damage with cards. It has haste itself, but its animation does not grant haste to the other Vehicles or animate ones that enter later. Still crew one of the attackers with Balthier and Fran to qualify for the additional combat.

Reaver Titan

Its attack trigger deals five to every opponent each combat, while its large body attacks one defender. Two attacks therefore deal ten to each opponent before any combat damage. It has protection from mana value three or less, which also prevents your own Temur Battle Rage or Shadowspear from targeting or attaching to it. Crew does not target, so Balthier and Fran can crew it legally.

Unlicensed Hearse

Provides cheap graveyard interaction and grows as it exiles cards. If you tap it to exile cards before combat, it is not available to attack unless something untaps it. Without cards exiled, it is naturally 0/0 as a creature; the commander's bonus can keep it alive, but losing that bonus may kill it.

Develop mana early and keep the payment available

Birds of Paradise

Supplies either commander color on the next turn or helps crew a Vehicle when mana is less important. It has zero power naturally, so it can be the second creature for Mighty Servant only when the other creature already supplies enough crew power.

Llanowar Elves

One-mana acceleration that enables a turn-two commander after two land drops. Elvish Mystic provides redundancy. Decide whether each mana creature is producing mana or crewing this turn; a tapped creature cannot do both without being untapped.

Delighted Halfling

Its colored mana casts legendary spells uncounterably, including the commander, The Regalia, or The One Ring. Use its colorless mode for ordinary Vehicles. Its colored mana cannot pay the commander's extra-combat ability because that is not casting a legendary spell.

Intrepid Stablemaster

Can produce two mana of one color for Vehicle spells, or one green mana without that spending restriction. The restricted two-mana mode cannot pay for extra combats. It is a useful early accelerator and later crew body.

Nature's Lore

Finds a Forest card, including Taiga or Stomping Ground, to establish permanent colored mana. Three Visits does the same. Choosing Stomping Ground still requires paying two life if you want it untapped; Commercial District enters tapped regardless.

Cultivator's Caravan

Ramps early and becomes an attacker later. Tapping it for mana leaves it tapped, even if it subsequently gains vigilance; vigilance prevents tapping to attack and does not untap an already tapped creature. Do not count the same Caravan as both a mana source and an attacker for the additional combat.

Ancient Tomb

The acceleration Game Changer is particularly effective for colorless Vehicles and the generic portion of spells. It does not supply red or green for the commander or its trigger. Its life cost accumulates, so use Shadowspear or other life recovery when the game runs longer.

Jeska's Will

With the commander present, supplies red mana and three cards to play that turn. Spend that mana during your main phase and preserve lands or Treasures for the combat payment. Unspent mana from Will does not normally carry out of the main phase into combat. Exiled sorceries cannot be cast between the back-to-back combat phases.

Sol Ring

Accelerates your artifacts without supplying either commander color. Arcane Signet and Talisman of Impulse help fix those colors. Keep a hand with actual green access for early development rather than relying on colorless acceleration alone.

Add haste and get value without the commander

Samut, Hazoret's Champion

Gives your creatures haste continuously, so a Vehicle gains the benefit as soon as it becomes a creature. At two mana, it lets newly cast Vehicles attack without a large setup investment. Samut also lets your tap-ability creatures operate immediately.

Fervor

Another continuous source of haste that survives creature-only wipes. Unlike an ability that grants haste only when a creature enters, it works on Vehicles that entered as noncreature artifacts and were crewed afterward.

Bello, Bard of the Brambles

During your turn, makes qualifying non-Equipment artifacts and non-Aura enchantments into hasty, indestructible 4/4 creatures with combat-damage draw. Your qualifying Vehicles keep their abilities and receive the commander's +1/+1, but their printed larger bodies may become smaller because their base stats are set to 4/4. This includes Reaver Titan. The animation ends outside your turn, and you must still crew an attacker with Balthier and Fran for their trigger.

Oviya, Automech Artisan

Places a creature or Vehicle from hand onto the battlefield for one green mana and a tap, adding two counters if it is an artifact. It does not grant haste, so prepare Samut, Fervor, or another applicable haste source. Her trample ability applies to any creature attacking one of your opponents, including another opponent's creatures.

Kona, Rescue Beastie

Crew with Kona to tap it before your second main phase, then put a permanent from hand onto the battlefield through survival. The extra combats occur before that second main phase, so a Vehicle put in with Kona normally arrives too late to attack that turn. Treat Kona as development for the next turn rather than fuel for the combats already scheduled.

Goblin Engineer

Finds an artifact for your graveyard and can exchange another artifact for a target of mana value three or less. It can retrieve an early Vehicle or mana rock, but cannot directly return Reaver Titan through its own activated ability. Goblin Welder is the unrestricted-mana-value recovery partner.

Goblin Welder

Exchanges a controlled artifact and an artifact card in the same player's graveyard. A Treasure or spare rock can become a lost Vehicle, provided both targets remain legal. The recovered Vehicle has no automatic haste. Do not sacrifice the targeted Treasure for mana before Welder resolves, because that would invalidate one of its targets.

Keep the hand stocked with simple draw engines

The One Ring

Refills your hand and buys a turn of protection when cast. Returning it through Welder does not grant the cast-dependent protection, but returns a fresh Ring without the old burden counters. Bello can animate the Ring during your turn, so decide whether its tap is being used for cards or an attack.

Sylvan Library

Improves early draws and offers extra cards for life when you need development. Fetchlands can shuffle away unwanted cards after using Library, but repeated four-life payments alongside Ancient Tomb can leave you vulnerable to counterattacks.

Toski, Bearer of Secrets

Rewards each creature connecting in each combat, helping the deck keep deploying threats. It must attack if able and lacks vigilance naturally, so it does not automatically attack twice. A Vehicle army can supply the repeated damage triggers even while Toski stays tapped after its first attack.

Return of the Wildspeaker

Draws from your largest non-Human creature or pumps your non-Humans for combat. Crew first if you want a Vehicle counted. Balthier and Fran are Human and cannot set the draw amount, while your ordinary Vehicle creatures are non-Human.

Hunter's Insight

Cast on a creature likely to connect to turn its combat damage that turn into cards, including damage in the additional combat. It targets, so Reaver Titan's protection prevents you from targeting it with this three-mana spell. A large trampling Vehicle such as Hoard Hauler is a suitable alternative.

Garruk's Uprising

Gives your creatures trample and can draw on entry if the commander or another qualifying creature is present. Vehicles entering as noncreature artifacts do not trigger its later draw ability merely by being crewed afterward. Bello can make qualifying Vehicles enter as creatures during your turn, allowing that draw trigger.

Faithless Looting

Filters awkward opening hands and puts artifacts into the graveyard for recovery. It is not net card advantage. Reckless Impulse supplies two playable cards through the end of your next turn when you need additional resources; leave mana and a land play available when possible.

Close with a few large attacks

Unnatural Growth

Doubles your creatures' power and toughness at the beginning of each combat. Crew the Vehicles before the trigger resolves so they are included. The first boost lasts through the turn, so the second combat doubles the already boosted values again. It does not grant trample, and four green symbols make it a late payoff rather than an automatic on-curve play.

Overwhelming Stampede

Crew the team in your main phase before casting it, then give those creatures a large boost and trample for the whole turn. Creatures that become animated only after it resolves miss the effect. Its boost continues through the additional combat, so one spell can support two attacks.

Temur Battle Rage

Gives one attacker double strike and, with sufficient power on your board, trample for the entire turn. This applies in both combats and is useful on a large Vehicle without protection from the spell. It does not target Reaver Titan legally while that protection is active.

Shadowspear

Adds trample and lifelink to a crewed Vehicle, helping damage get through while recovering life. Equip during your main phase after crewing. When the Vehicle stops being a creature, the Equipment becomes unattached; it must be equipped again later. Its activated ability removes opposing hexproof and indestructible so your removal can work.

Sting, the Glinting Dagger

Equip the commander, crew one Vehicle in your main phase, then let Sting untap the commander at the beginning of combat and crew a second Vehicle before declaring attackers. If both attack in that first combat, each produces a separate payment opportunity. Six mana buys two additional combats. The ability cannot generate fresh commander combat triggers in later combat phases, so this line is finite.

Combat Celebrant

Exert it as it attacks to untap all your other creatures and create an additional combat. This readies pilots and other tapped attackers, but does not untap Celebrant itself. It can be exerted only once that turn; untapping it by another method does not reset that restriction. Exert also skips its untap in your next untap step.

A simple finishing example: an 11/11 Reaver Titan under the commander, without Bello changing its base stats, becomes 22/22 in the first combat and 44/44 in the additional combat with Unnatural Growth. It also deals five to every opponent on each attack. That is substantial pressure, but blockers can still stop its combat damage unless you give it trample through a nontargeting effect such as Garruk's Uprising.

Protect the attack and clear the obstacle

Heroic Intervention

Protects your permanents from destruction and targeted opposing effects. It does not stop mass exile or toughness reduction. With Vehicles already crewed, it can preserve them through your own Blasphemous Act.

Defend the Rider

One-mana protection for a key permanent, including an uncrewed Vehicle. The Pilot mode is useful when a blocker or extra crew body matters more than protection, but one Pilot cannot independently reproduce Balthier and Fran's extra-combat ability.

Tamiyo's Safekeeping

Protects one permanent and gains a little life. Keeping the commander alive maintains vigilance through later attacks. If it is removed after the extra combat is created, the extra combat still exists, but your Vehicles no longer have its granted vigilance or +1/+1.

Deflecting Swat

Protects against targeted spells or abilities and can be free with the commander present. It does not stop untargeted sweepers or a spell whose targets cannot be changed usefully.

Bolt Bend

Usually costs one red while the commander is present because its power is four. It handles a spell or ability with exactly one target; it is not a universal counterspell. Losing your last four-power creature before casting means paying the full cost.

Krosan Grip

Removes an artifact or enchantment with split second, limiting most responses while it is on the stack. It does not prevent triggered abilities or mana abilities. Use it on an obstacle that would otherwise stop a finishing combat rather than a minor value piece.

Beast Within

Answers any permanent through destruction, while Chaos Warp can handle an indestructible permanent by shuffling it away. Nature's Claim and Abrade provide cheaper artifact or enchantment interaction where applicable. Save broad answers for effects the narrower spells cannot solve.

Vandalblast

Removes a key artifact for one mana or clears opposing artifacts with overload. Your Vehicles survive the overloaded mode. It cannot destroy indestructible artifacts unless you first remove that protection, for example through Shadowspear's activated ability.

Blasphemous Act

The emergency creature wipe. Cast it before crewing to preserve normal noncreature Vehicles, but remember that Bello makes qualifying artifacts creatures during your turn. Bello's indestructibility protects those objects from damage destruction while its effect applies; if Bello dies, check the remaining board and continuous effects carefully.

Recover and keep the sequencing short

Eternal Witness

Retrieves the protection spell, finisher, land, or threat the board currently needs. Bala Ged Recovery offers the same broad recovery as a spell or a tapped land early. Pinnacle Monk is another land option whose creature face recovers an instant or sorcery.

Mech Hangar

Its colored mana can cast Vehicle spells, but Balthier and Fran are not Pilots, so that mana cannot pay their colored casting costs. It also cannot pay the extra-combat trigger. Its animation ability offers a backup when pilots are scarce, but does not grant haste or replace the requirement that the commander actually crew a qualifying attacker.

Kessig Wolf Run

Converts spare mana into power and trample on a creature, including a crewed Vehicle. A land has mana value zero, so Reaver Titan's protection also prevents this ability from targeting it. Boseiju, Who Endures adds interaction from the mana base, and Buried Ruin can recover an artifact for a later turn.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep three functional lands, a cheap accelerator, and an affordable Vehicle or card-selection spell. Two-land hands need dependable early acceleration and a way to find more lands. The deck has 36 lands plus three land-option cards: Bala Ged Recovery, Pinnacle Monk, and Shatterskull Smashing. Prioritize an untapped green source for the one-mana mana creatures, and enough red and green to pay the commander and its combat trigger.

An example keep is Forest, Stomping Ground, Command Tower, Llanowar Elves, Rocketeer Boostbuggy, Smuggler's Copter, and Defend the Rider. Turn one deploy Elves. Turn two can develop a Vehicle or cast the commander; choose according to whether you can protect the setup and attack usefully next turn. Spend the following turns establishing the remaining pieces while reserving protection or the combat payment, rather than emptying your hand indiscriminately.

For a quick default turn: identify the Vehicle Balthier and Fran will crew, reserve {1}{R}{G}, crew other attackers, then attack. Resolve resource triggers that can pay for the commander first. Handle the second combat before moving to your second main phase. You do not need the multi-Vehicle Sting line every turn; use it when the additional combat is likely to close the game.

At the beginning of combat, remember three checks: which Vehicles are creatures, which have been under your control since the turn began or have haste, and which were crewed by the commander. Vigilance does not fix a Vehicle you already tapped for mana. Animation does not supply haste, and haste does not satisfy the commander's crew requirement.

The deck can keep attacking without Balthier and Fran through Bello, haste sources, ordinary pilots, and the Vehicles' printed abilities. It loses the commander's easy second combat and vigilance, so stop assuming tapped attackers can swing again. Rebuild the commander when the second attack is worth the mana, or finish through an already established boost and evasive threats.
