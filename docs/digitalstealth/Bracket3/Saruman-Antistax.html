Two things are happening at once. Saruman turns every noncreature spell you cast into +1/+1 counters on a single Army equal to that spell's mana value — so the interaction you were holding up anyway assembles a lethal creature for free. And the spell list itself is pointed squarely at the decks Grixis usually loses to: white prison and green ramp. Gloom, Perish, Dystopia, Hibernation and Filter Out are not filler, they are the reason the deck exists.

The commander
Saruman, the White Hand

every noncreature spell amasses Orcs equal to its mana value, and your Orcs and Goblins have ward {2}. A Cyclonic Rift is seven counters. A Mana Drain is two. The Army is your clock and your blocker and it never needed a card slot of its own.

Orcish Siegemaster

your Orcs and Goblins gain trample, and it attacks for the greatest power on your board. It is what stops the Army being chump-blocked forever.

Lazotep Plating

amass one AND hexproof for you and everything you control. It is a counterspell for removal, a fog for a targeted wrath, and a Saruman trigger.

Slip Out the Back

one mana to phase the Army out of a wrath, plus a counter.

Deflecting Swat

free with your commander out.

The other spell payoffs
Ovika, Enigma Goliath

X hasty Goblins for every noncreature spell, where X is its mana value. Alongside Saruman, one big spell is a board and a lethal Army in the same breath — and the Goblins get ward from Saruman and trample from Orcish Siegemaster.

Sedgemoor Witch

a Pest for every instant and sorcery, with menace and ward on the body.

Archmage Emeritus

a card for every instant and sorcery you cast or copy.

Guttersnipe

two damage to each opponent per instant and sorcery.

Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

red mana on every spell you cast, which is how the big turns get bigger. The back face turns dead cards into two impulse draws.

Beating white
Gloom

white spells cost {3} more, and activated abilities of white enchantments cost {3} more. Against a prison or Voltron deck this is close to a lock, and the second clause specifically answers the enchantments Grixis cannot destroy.

Dystopia

every player sacrifices a green or white permanent every upkeep. You have none, so it is entirely one-sided, and the cumulative upkeep is paid in life.

Deathmark

one mana for a green or white creature.

Debt to the Kami

they exile a creature OR an enchantment of their choice. Edicts are how Grixis answers a hexproof enchantment.

Pharika's Libation

the same, at instant speed, as a sacrifice.

Extract the Truth

strips a creature, enchantment or planeswalker from their hand, or forces an enchantment sacrifice.

Beating green
Perish

destroys every green creature on the board. Against an elf or ramp deck it is a one-sided wrath for three mana.

Hibernation

returns every green PERMANENT to hand — creatures, enchantments, mana auras and all. It undoes an entire green deck's turn.

Ashiok, Dream Render

opponents cannot search their libraries at all. Fetchlands, tutors, Cultivate and every green ramp spell stop working.

Opposition Agent

flash him in and you control their search. Everything they find is yours.

Beating stax itself
Filter Out

returns ALL noncreature, nonland permanents to their owners' hands. Every prison enchantment, every mana rock, every Equipment at the table, gone at instant speed — and it is a four-counter Saruman trigger on top.

Rushing River

bounce one permanent, or two if you kick it. Instant speed answers to a lock piece.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, the same effect aimed only at them, and it amasses seven.

Brotherhood's End

three damage to every creature and planeswalker, or every cheap artifact destroyed. Pick whichever half the table is built on.

Imprisoned in the Moon

turns a commander, a land or a planeswalker into a colourless land they cannot recast.

Reality Shift

exile a creature and leave them a face-down 2/2.

Feed the Swarm

the rare black spell that kills an enchantment outright.

Chaos Warp

the answer to anything at all, once.

Counterspells that build the Army
Counterspell

two counters and the answer.

Mana Drain

two counters, and the mana casts the next spell.

Mana Sculpt

three counters, and with a Wizard out — which is nearly always — it refunds their mana to you.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana for anything noncreature.

Muddle the Mixture

a counterspell, or transmute for any three-drop — Sedgemoor Witch, Ovika's setup, or Dimir Machinations.

Baral, Chief of Compliance

cheaper spells, and a loot every time you counter something.

Flame of Anor

with a Wizard out, take two of three modes. Draw two and kill a creature, usually.

Mana and tutors
Dark Ritual

three black on turn one.

Seething Song

five red for three.

Rite of Flame

cheap acceleration that is also a Saruman trigger.

Goblin Electromancer

instants and sorceries cost one less.

Nightscape Familiar

the same for blue and red, on a regenerating body.

Demonic Tutor

any card.

Grim Tutor

the second copy.

Merchant Scroll

any blue instant, which usually means a counterspell or Filter Out.

Dimir Machinations

transmute for a three-drop, or strip their top three cards.

Ringsight

any card sharing a colour with a legend you control.

Impulse

dig four at instant speed.

Pieces of the Puzzle

two instants or sorceries from the top five.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep interaction and mana, not threats. The Army IS the threat, and it only exists because you cast spells — a hand of counterspells and removal is a hand of Saruman triggers.

Read the table before you keep the hosers. Gloom, Perish, Dystopia and Hibernation are dead cards against a mono-black or artifact table and game-winning against a green or white one. Against the wrong pod they are your worst four cards, so spend them as Saruman fodder rather than holding them.

Saruman, the White Hand

four mana, and every turn he is out is counters you are not getting. He is a 2/5 that blocks well, so he is safer to deploy early than most commanders of his cost.

Ovika, Enigma Goliath

hold her until you have a big spell to follow with. Landing her and immediately overloading Cyclonic Rift is seven Goblins with haste, seven counters on the Army, and usually the game.
