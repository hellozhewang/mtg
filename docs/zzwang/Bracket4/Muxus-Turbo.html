Muxus-Turbo is an independent Bracket 4 upgrade of Muxus-Goblins. Muxus is a deployment engine, not a promise that six random cards win. Use Recruiter for deterministic piles, and use fast mana to cast the six-mana commander while keeping red mana and protection available. The Bracket 3 file stays intact.

Pay for the deployment, then protect the result
Muxus, Goblin Grandee

Reveals six cards and puts only Goblin creature cards with mana value five or less onto the battlefield. Non-Goblins and six-mana Goblins are misses. Its combat size bonus is useful pressure but is not the main combo plan.

Goblin Recruiter

Stack exactly the creatures needed for the next Muxus trigger. Do not make a blind top-six reveal your only line when Recruiter is available.

+
Goblin Lackey + Warren Instigator

Connecting can put a Goblin from hand onto the battlefield, including another Muxus cast from hand after a bounce. They cannot put a commander directly from the command zone into play. Instigator must actually connect to trigger.

Skirk Prospector

Bank small Goblins as mana for Muxus, then preserve Prospector for the Crafter loop. Sacrificing every body to reach six mana can leave the resolved commander unsupported if its trigger is stopped.

++
Chrome Mox + Mox Diamond + Mox Opal

Use real imprint, land-discard and metalcraft resources in the keep decision. The moxen help reach Muxus while leaving colored lands untapped for interaction.

+
Sol Ring + Mana Vault

Large generic bursts cover Muxus's four generic mana. Still reserve two red. Mana Vault does not normally untap and is not a repeatable ritual every turn.

++
Ancient Tomb + City of Traitors + Crystal Vein

Lands that produce a one-turn jump into the commander. Traitors sacrifices on your next land play; Vein's burst sacrifices itself. Keep enough durable lands for recovery after a counter or wipe.

++
Rite of Flame + Desperate Ritual + Pyretic Ritual

Reliable small ritual steps; the two-mana rituals net one mana when paid normally. Do not assume they replace a missing red land indefinitely.

+
Seething Song + Simian Spirit Guide

Song turns three mana into five and Guide supplies one emergency red. Their job is to make a six-mana threat arrive on the pivotal turn rather than over several fair turns.

+
Brightstone Ritual + Battle Hymn

Goblin and creature counts matter at resolution. Brightstone counts Goblins on the battlefield, including opponents'; Hymn counts only your creatures. Wipes can strand both.

Jeska's Will

With Muxus present, use both modes to turn a successful deployment into more action. Without a commander, choose one mode and count the targeted opponent's actual hand.

+
Wily Goblin + Impulsive Pilferer

Low-curve Goblins leave Treasures for the Muxus turn. Pilferer must die to make its Treasure; Wily supplies one on entry and costs two red.

Scuzzback Scrounger

Can blight one creature at the first main phase for a Treasure. Do not put the counter on the one-toughness creature you must retain for the combo unless you intend it to die.

+
Cursed Mirror + Molten Duplication

Copy an existing Muxus or Recruiter to get another entry trigger. The legend rule makes you keep only one Muxus, but does not remove its already-triggered ability. The copy must have a legal target when created.

Goblin Welder

Recovers a used mana artifact or Mirror by trading another artifact. It needs a tap, two legal targets and time to become ready; it is not a turn-one unrestricted reanimation spell.

+
Goblin Chieftain + Howlsquad Heavy

Extra haste providers reduce reliance on a single Warchief. Heavy's mana ability still requires max speed; its haste for other Goblins works immediately.

Krenko, Mob Boss

A resolved Muxus can supply enough Goblins and haste for Krenko to rebuild the army or fund a second burst with Prospector. Count the actual board before sacrificing the protection creatures.

+
Rundvelt Hordemaster + Skullclamp

Use expendable Goblins to reload after a failed attempt. A toughness-boosting lord can prevent Clamp killing a token by itself, so use an outlet when needed. Clamp's draw and Hordemaster's temporary exile permission are different resources; sequence plays before that permission expires.

+
Goblin Trashmaster + Goblin Cratermaker

Goblin-searchable answers to artifact hate. Trashmaster consumes a Goblin per artifact; Cratermaker cannot destroy a colored nonland permanent through its colorless-permanent mode.

+
Goblin Sharpshooter + Pashalik Mons

Creature deaths untap Sharpshooter and trigger Mons. These clear small blockers and mana creatures or turn a token sacrifice chain into damage; neither is an infinite combo by itself.

+
General Kreat, the Boltbringer + Impact Tremors

Either converts arbitrary creature entries from the Crafter loop into damage to every opponent. Tremors does not rely on a surviving creature's activated ability.

+
Gamble + Wheel of Fortune

Gamble finds a missing engine at the cost of a random discard. Wheel can draw into a Recruiter pile or refill after dumping mana, but also gives three opponents new answers; protect the winning turn first.

Recruiter piles and independent Goblin wins
→
Goblin Matron → Goblin Recruiter

Matron puts Recruiter into hand; budget the second cast. Find the engine rather than another anthem when a protected combo is available.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Goblin Recruiter

Recruiter has power one and is a legal target. Imperial also finds Snoop, Prospector, Courier or Kiki when that is the missing piece.

+
Goblin Recruiter + Goblin Ringleader

For a four-card refill, put Ringleader first, then Snoop, Courier, Prospector and Squelcher. Draw Ringleader, cast it, and take those four Goblins. Put Kiki immediately below the four if assembling Snoop next. Leave Fanatic in the library.

+
Goblin Recruiter + Muxus, Goblin Grandee

A six-hit deployment is Warchief, Kiki, Crafter, Prospector, Snoop and Fanatic. All six are Goblin creatures with mana value five or less. Crafter can champion the Recruiter already in play; Kiki/Crafter/Prospector then supplies the immediate kill. Cast Muxus from the command zone in its own deck, or have it in hand in the others.

Complete fetches before Recruiter resolves. Drawing, milling or shuffling afterward changes the planned top cards. A pile is not a guarantee if a needed piece is already in hand or the graveyard; use a different route rather than pretending it is still in the library.

+
Conspicuous Snoop + Torch Courier

The direct pile is Snoop on top to draw, followed by Courier and Kiki. After drawing and casting Snoop, cast Courier from the revealed top and sacrifice it to give Snoop haste. Kiki must now remain on top, not in hand or on the battlefield.

+
Conspicuous Snoop + Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

With Kiki revealed, tap the hasty Snoop to copy itself. Each new Snoop token has haste and the same copy ability. Use the newest untapped copy repeatedly to make as many Snoops as needed.

++
Conspicuous Snoop + Goblin Recruiter + Mogg Fanatic

Use the last Snoop's copied Kiki ability to copy the original Recruiter. The token Recruiter puts Fanatic on top. Every Snoop now gains Fanatic's sacrifice-for-one-damage ability. Sacrifice enough Snoops to kill all three opponents. Stack every needed sacrifice before resolving other effects that change the library top. Keep Recruiter on the battlefield until that final copy; having Fanatic in hand does not grant its ability.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

Copy Crafter, champion Kiki with the new hasty copy, tap that copy for three damage, then sacrifice it to Prospector. Kiki returns untapped with haste. Repeat for lethal damage and red mana. The original Crafter must champion a different Goblin or Shaman first; do not accidentally exile the only required outlet.

Goblin Warchief

Supplies haste and reduces only generic costs of Goblin spells. It makes Muxus cheaper, but cannot reduce Snoop's two red pips or Kiki's three red pips.

Hexing Squelcher

Makes spells uncounterable and taxes targeting of your other creatures with life. This does not stop removal, exile, sacrifice, triggered counters to abilities, or an opponent paying the ward cost.

Goblin Bombardment

Converts assembled tokens into damage without combat or haste. It is also a free outlet for Crafter copies, replacing Prospector's mana with an additional damage per loop.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep durable red sources, early bodies or a real fast-mana start, and a tutor or recovery engine. A six-mana commander plus no acceleration is a mulligan. Hands that can deploy Muxus but cannot pay two red are also traps. Aim for a protected turn-four-or-later deployment and preserve a fallback if its trigger is countered.

++
Deflecting Swat + Pyroblast + Red Elemental Blast

Protect the decisive deployment and kill blue interaction permanents before trying to combo. Swat cannot retarget an ability whose targets would become illegal, and does not stop untargeted removal.

Goblin Chirurgeon

Protects key creatures from destruction by sacrificing other Goblins. It does not rescue a whole board from exile, and regeneration taps a creature that may need to activate.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Hold up its graveyard exile when an opponent threatens recursion, or sacrifice it for a card when that threat has passed. Do not spend the only usable artifact needed to turn on Opal without checking metalcraft.

After a wipe, the commander is the refill: develop mana and Recruiter before committing all remaining creatures. If Kiki or Fanatic was drawn, use the Crafter/Bombardment route or a fair attack rather than claiming the original Snoop pile still works. This is the faster Bracket 4 version, not the turn-six Bracket 3 copy.
