The plan is the same every turn: attack with a one-mana creature nobody can block, wait for blockers to be declared, then swap it out for something enormous that is already through. Ninjutsu and sneak both do this, and the commander pays you a card and a permanent +1/+1 counter every time any of it connects. The cheap unblockable bodies are not filler — they are the engine.

The commander
Heroes in a Half Shell

5/5 vigilance, menace, trample, haste, and every combat damage step where your Mutants, Ninjas or Turtles connect puts a counter on ALL of them and draws you a card. Five colours is the cost; it is worth it.

Roaming Throne

name Ninja. Every Ninja damage trigger in the deck fires twice — two cards off Ingenious Infiltrator, two off Ninja of the Deep Hours, two commander triggers.

Silver-Fur Master

ninjutsu costs {1} less, and every other Ninja and Rogue gets +1/+1. It makes the cheap enablers into real threats.

Splinter, Hamato Yoshi

another Ninja anthem on a menacing body that can itself be sneaked in.

The unblockable bodies
Changeling Outcast

one mana, cannot block, cannot BE blocked, and it is every creature type — so it triggers the commander too.

Mist-Cloaked Herald

one mana, unblockable.

Slither Blade

the third copy.

April O'Neil, Kunoichi Trainee

scries two on entry and cannot be blocked by anything with power three or more.

Prehistoric Pet

unblockable by anything bigger, and it taps to bounce another creature you control — a free ninjutsu enabler that does not need combat.

Faerie Seer

flies, scries two.

Mothdust Changeling

every type, and it can give itself flying by tapping a spare creature.

Inkrise Infiltrator

flies, and pumps when you have mana spare.

Ornithopter

free, flies, and it is a perfectly good ninjutsu enabler on turn one.

Smoke Shroud

flying on anything, and it climbs back out of the graveyard every time a Ninja enters.

Cover of Darkness

name Ninja. Fear on the whole team means almost nothing can block them.

The Ninjas you swap in
Fallen Shinobi

two free cards off their library on every hit. The best raw payoff in the deck.

Ingenious Infiltrator

a card for EVERY Ninja that connects, not just itself. With three attackers that is three cards.

Ninja of the Deep Hours

a card per hit for two mana.

Moon-Circuit Hacker

a card per hit, with no discard on the turn it arrives.

Higure, the Still Wind

tutors the next Ninja on every connection, and makes any Ninja unblockable for {2}.

Nashi, Moon Sage's Scion

exiles the top card of every library and lets you cast one, paying life instead of mana.

Prosperous Thief

a Treasure whenever any Ninja or Rogue connects — which pays for the next ninjutsu.

Mistblade Shinobi

bounces a blocker or a problem permanent every hit.

Kappa Tech-Wrecker

exiles an artifact or enchantment on connection.

Sakashima's Student

a copy of the best creature on the board, and it is a Ninja on top.

Satoru, the Infiltrator

draws whenever creatures enter WITHOUT being cast. Ninjutsu does not use the stack as a cast, so every single ninjutsu activation draws a card.

The Turtles you sneak in

Sneak works like ninjutsu but is a discounted CAST — the creature enters tapped and attacking after blockers.

Raphael, the Nightwatcher

attacking creatures have double strike. Every damage trigger in the deck fires twice.

Michelangelo, Improviser

puts a creature AND a land from your hand onto the battlefield on every hit.

Donatello, Gadget Master

copies an artifact on every hit.

Leonardo, Cutting Edge

lifelink, and it grows on every point of life you gain.

Dark Leo & Shredder

attacking Ninjas gain deathtouch, and with five Ninjas out its damage halves an opponent's life.

Mikey & Don, Party Planners

lets you play lands and cast Mutants, Ninjas and Turtles straight off the top of your library, with a bonus counter.

Don & Raph, Hard Science

their attack makes your next noncreature spell cost one less per artifact.

Raph & Leo, Sibling Rivals

untaps attackers and grants an ADDITIONAL COMBAT PHASE. Two rounds of ninjutsu triggers per turn.

Mikey & Leo, Chaos & Order

a card the first time each turn you put a counter on a creature — which the commander does every combat.

Turning the connections into a kill
Shredder, Shadow Master

attacks one player and makes a tapped attacking copy at each other opponent. Every copy that connects halves that player's life. Two swings ends the table.

Dark Leo & Shredder

the second halving effect, live as soon as you have five Ninjas.

Sphere Grid

a counter on every creature that connects, and everything with a counter gets reach and trample.

Bident of Thassa

a card for every creature that connects, and it can force the table to attack each other.

Dolmen Gate

your attackers take no combat damage at all. Attack with everything, every turn, for free.

Heroic Intervention

hexproof and indestructible through the wrath this deck is most afraid of.

Continue?

returns up to four creatures that died this turn straight back to the battlefield. It is the answer to a blocked alpha strike or a wrath in combat.

Interaction
Path to Exile

one mana, exile.

Assassin's Trophy

two mana for any permanent.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded, it clears every blocker for a lethal ninjutsu turn.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

Deflecting Swat

free with your commander out.

Kappa Tech-Wrecker

repeatable artifact and enchantment removal on a body.

Rhystic Study

their turns pay for yours.

Mana in five colours
Birds of Paradise

turn-one fixing, and it flies, so it is also a ninjutsu enabler.

Bloom Tender

with a five-colour commander down it taps for a lot.

Chromatic Lantern

makes every land produce every colour, which is what a WUBRG commander needs.

Farseek

fetches any shockland with a basic type.

Nature's Lore

an untapped Forest-typed dual.

Ninja Pizza

a Food every turn that taps for any colour.

Prosperous Thief

Treasures off combat, which is the deck's most reliable ramp.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a one-mana unblockable creature. Changeling Outcast, Mist-Cloaked Herald or Ornithopter on turn one means a real Ninja is on the battlefield by turn two, and the deck simply does not function without that opener.

Ninjutsu AFTER blockers are declared, not before. The whole point is that the creature you return was already unblocked, so the Ninja arrives already through. Sneak works the same way.

Heroes in a Half Shell

five specific colours is a real cost, so do not hold the whole hand waiting for her. The Ninja package wins games on its own; she makes it draw twice as many cards when she lands.

Roaming Throne

name Ninja, not Turtle. The Ninjas carry the repeatable damage triggers; the Turtles are mostly static effects that the Throne would not double anyway.
