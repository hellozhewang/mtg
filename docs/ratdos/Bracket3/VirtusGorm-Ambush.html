Put both Virtus the Veiled and Gorm the Great in the command zone; the remaining 98 cards are the deck. Gorm pulls blockers away, but he does not make Virtus automatically unblockable. Build a reliable route through combat, then multiply Virtus's hits and finish the reduced life totals before the table can rebuild.

Understand the partners before attacking
Virtus the Veiled

a normal unblocked one-damage hit takes a player from 40 to 39, then its trigger makes them lose 20, leaving 19. The trigger uses their life total when it resolves. The lost life is not damage and does not count as commander damage; only the actual combat damage does.

Gorm the Great

must be blocked if able and by two or more creatures if able. An opponent with one eligible blocker must block him with it; an opponent with two must use both. With three or more, they can still have blockers for Virtus. Attack the SAME player with both partners when relying on this interaction.

The partners each have their own commander tax and commander-damage total. Their partner-with search abilities cannot retrieve a partner from the command zone. Do not treat Gorm as compulsory setup when an equipment or land already lets Virtus connect; spend that turn protecting Virtus or developing a backup threat instead.

Make connecting reliable
Brotherhood Regalia

the best early equipment target: two mana to cast, one to equip either legendary partner, unblockability, and ward two. Ward is a tax opponents may pay, not hexproof. Regalia can also make a backup creature an Assassin for Roaming Throne.

Silver Shroud Costume

flash it in to attach to your threatened creature and grant temporary shroud, then retain unblockability afterward. The attach trigger targets, so an opponent can respond before the shroud is granted. Shroud stops your own targeting for the rest of that turn too.

My Precious // Allure of Power

permanent hexproof plus unblockability without shroud's restrictions on your own targeting. Its Adventure can sacrifice a creature to draw two before you later cast the equipment, but do not sacrifice a critical attacker just because the option is available. Equip costs two mana AND two life.

Manifold Key

a repeatable unblockable activation from a one-mana artifact. Its other mode untaps another artifact, letting it reuse Sol Ring, The One Ring, or Strionic Resonator. Each use still costs mana and tapping; this list does not loop those activations infinitely.

+
Access Tunnel + Rogue's Passage

evasion from land slots survives most creature wipes. Tunnel targets power three or less when activated and when the ability resolves; after it resolves, later power increases do not remove unblockability. Passage has no power restriction but costs more to activate.

+
Shizo, Death's Storehouse + The Black Gate

Shizo grants a legend fear, so black and artifact creatures can still block. The Gate stops a selected player with the most life, or tied for most including you, from blocking the targeted creature. It cannot simply pick any opponent after life totals change.

Champion of Lambholt

each other creature entering grows the Champion and makes more opposing creatures unable to block your entire team. Shredder's copies and Pest tokens grow it before blockers are declared if created before that step. The restriction depends on its current power; removing Champion restores normal blocking options.

Rancor

power plus trample makes Virtus's deathtouch relevant even when blocked. One assigned damage is lethal to an ordinary blocker from a deathtouch source, so assign one per blocker and send excess to the player. A single point reaching the player triggers Virtus. Rancor returns only when it goes from battlefield to graveyard, not when its spell fails to resolve.

+
Nemesis Mask + Gorm the Great

upgrades Gorm's two-blocker requirement into a demand for every creature able to block him. This clears a route for the other attackers against that defender. Restrictions still matter: creatures that cannot legally block Gorm cannot be forced to do so.

Dolmen Gate

keeps your attacking creatures alive through ordinary combat damage, especially Gorm drawing several blockers. It does not stop destruction spells, exile, sacrifice, or damage to your creatures while they are blocking.

+
Infiltration Lens + Gorm the Great

each creature blocking Gorm gives you a separate optional draw-two trigger. Two blockers can mean four cards; with Mask the total can be much larger. If you make Gorm unblockable, the Lens produces no cards that combat.

Multiply the hits without an early automatic kill
+
Fireshrieker + Virtus the Veiled

a one-power double-striking Virtus takes an unprotected player from 40 to 19 after first strike, then from 19 to 9 after normal damage and the second trigger. This is two damage events and two life-halving triggers. A blocked double-striker still needs trample or another way to reach the player after its blocker dies.

+
Quietus Spike + Virtus the Veiled

a hit triggers both the Spike and Virtus, taking a player from 40 to 19 to 9 after the initial one damage. The Spike's trigger belongs to the equipment, so an Assassin-naming Roaming Throne does not double that trigger.

Roaming Throne

normally choose Assassin to double Virtus and Unstoppable Slasher's combat-damage triggers. Gorm's blocking requirements are static abilities, not triggers, and are not doubled. Throne can instead name Ninja for a Shredder-focused endgame, or Regalia can make Shredder an Assassin while the equipment remains attached.

Strionic Resonator

copy Virtus's life-halving trigger after he connects, or copy a valuable combat draw or Shredder attack trigger. The copied Virtus trigger still affects the same damaged player because it does not target; changing targets cannot redirect it to a different opponent.

Each halving uses the life total left by the previous effect. Ordinary damage doublers would not double Virtus's triggered life loss. This list excludes cheap life-loss-doubling kills and cheap drain loops; its major one-hit finishing effect costs eight mana and belongs to the developed endgame.

Other threats make the plan survive removal
Unstoppable Slasher

a second deathtouch life-halver that carries your evasion equipment well. It returns after dying only if it had no counters, and then has two stun counters. Adding a +1/+1 counter with Repast also disables that death condition while the counter remains.

Shredder, Shadow Master

attacks one opponent and creates a nonlegendary attacking copy for each other opponent, each attacking that other player. Those copies also halve life when they connect and are sacrificed at end of combat. They do not copy attached equipment, its bonuses, or its unblockability. Their entry does not create more attack triggers.

Shredder's copy and attack-trigger details follow the official release notes: https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/TMT_Release_Notes_eM8Yplm3UV/u17aHk8iV1_EN_MTGTMT_ReleaseNotes_20251001.pdf

Glissa Sunslayer

first strike plus deathtouch discourages blocks, while evasion turns her into draw, enchantment removal, or counter removal. Removing Slasher's stun counters can let it resume attacking sooner. Use the mode that solves the board instead of automatically drawing.

Questing Beast

a hasty threat that small creatures cannot block and that stops prevention of combat damage from your creatures. It helps against damage-prevention defenses; it does not remove blocking restrictions, grant unblockability, or stop effects that prevent an opponent's life total changing.

Hooded Blightfang

each attacking deathtouch creature drains every opponent for one, closing the low totals left by halving attacks. The trigger happens on declaring attackers, before combat damage. Creatures entering already attacking, including Shredder's copies, do not create extra attack triggers.

+
Ohran Frostfang + Hooded Blightfang

Frostfang gives your attacking creatures deathtouch, letting normal declared attackers contribute Blightfang triggers. Frostfang also draws for each creature connecting with a player. Deathtouch lets Gorm trade efficiently, but two power only assigns one lethal damage to two ordinary blockers unless you increase his power.

Vorpal Sword

equips deathtouch and extra power to Gorm or another creature, then offers an eight-mana endgame activation that makes a connecting hit cause the damaged player to lose the game. Activate before combat damage. This is an expensive mana sink, not an early combo to rush.

+
Archfiend of Despair + Virtus the Veiled

the eight-mana endgame closer. With Archfiend present for the end step, a player hit by ordinary Virtus from 40 falls to 19 and then loses the 21 life already lost that turn, which is lethal. Its no-lifegain ability prevents a simple healing response. Keep this as a late-game conversion of combat pressure, not a plan to cheat out an early two-card kill.

+
Archfiend of Despair + Shredder, Shadow Master

a potential table finish if the original and copies each connect with their respective opponents and Archfiend reaches the end step. Blockers and removal can still interrupt it. At a normal 40 life, a five-damage Shredder hit leaves 17, and the later 23-life loss is lethal.

Exsanguinate

finish several weakened players at once, bypassing blockers. Choose X based on the life totals that remain rather than waiting for enough mana to drain three players from 40. Its life gain also offsets fetchlands, Library, and Ring costs.

Draw and develop without depending on Virtus
++
Birds of Paradise + Delighted Halfling + Elves of Deep Shadow

early fixing gets the partners onto the battlefield ahead of curve. Halfling's colored mana can cast your legendary partners through counters, but cannot pay equipment abilities. Deep Shadow's black mana costs a life each activation.

+
Llanowar Elves + Elvish Mystic

turn-one acceleration makes room for equipment and protection alongside your commanders. Late, they can draw through Toski when Gorm clears blockers or Champion makes the whole team evasive.

++
Nature's Lore + Three Visits + Farseek

find typed dual lands to supply both colors. Lore and Visits can get an untapped Bayou or an Overgrown Tomb if you pay the life; Farseek always puts its target in tapped. Underground Mortuary enters tapped regardless of which search found it.

++
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet + Talisman of Resilience

mana for the repeated cast-and-equip costs is as important as mana for creatures. Do not commit every resource to equipment if it leaves your only connecting attacker exposed to removal.

+
Sylvan Library + The One Ring

independent draw engines let the deck recover when combat is unavailable. Library's extra cards cost life, and the Ring's burden counters add recurring life loss. The Ring's cast-entry protection protects you, not your creatures, and does not prevent life-loss effects such as its upkeep trigger.

+
Toski, Bearer of Secrets + Oakhame Adversary

draw from successful attacks. Toski rewards every creature that connects and survives destruction; Adversary is often a two-mana deathtouch attacker against green permanents. Neither needs Virtus on the battlefield.

Tireless Tracker

land drops make Clues, and sacrificing those Clues grows a backup attacker. A fetchland provides two landfall events, but you still need two mana for each Clue you sacrifice to draw.

Gonti, Night Minister

successful hits against your opponents provide cards from their libraries, with flexible mana to cast those spells later. Opponents attacking each other also benefit, and any player casting a spell they do not own gets a Treasure. This supplies options, not guaranteed cards of the type you need.

Buster Sword

gives Virtus four power, draws when he connects, and may cast a spell from your hand with mana value at most the combat damage dealt. Virtus's subsequent life-loss trigger does not increase that damage number. The free spell is optional; retain an answer when its timing matters more than casting it free.

Protect both partners and answer the actual problem
Guardian Augmenter

flash protection gives BOTH commanders hexproof and +2/+2. Virtus becomes three power and still qualifies for Access Tunnel until another bonus raises him further. The Augmenter itself is not protected by its own ability.

Saryth, the Viper's Fang

your other untapped creatures have hexproof, while your other tapped creatures have deathtouch. Gorm's vigilance keeps him untapped and protected, but means he does not gain deathtouch from this effect while attacking. The untap ability can protect another creature by making it untapped before targeted removal resolves.

+
Lightning Greaves + Swiftfoot Boots

haste and protection allow recast commanders to contribute immediately. Greaves' shroud blocks your own equipment and targeting; move it elsewhere before attaching another equipment, then move it back. Boots' hexproof avoids that problem but costs mana to equip.

Mithril Coat

flash it in to attach to a legendary creature and protect it from destruction. Indestructibility keeps Gorm alive through most combat, but cannot stop exile, sacrifice, or lethal toughness reduction. Its attach trigger must resolve before that protection is present.

++
Heroic Intervention + Tamiyo's Safekeeping + Veil of Summer

keep a crucial attacker or board alive without tapping out for another threat. Safekeeping protects one permanent; Intervention protects your permanents broadly; Veil addresses counters and targeted blue or black interaction. None of these universally answers every board wipe.

+
Revitalizing Repast // Old-Growth Grove + Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire

land slots double as protection. Repast grants indestructible but also adds a counter, which matters to Slasher. Rebirth works only when the creature dies; leave a commander in the graveyard rather than moving it to the command zone if you want that trigger to return it.

++
Assassin's Trophy + Abrupt Decay + Beast Within

remove the permanent blocking your plan, whether a creature, equipment, enchantment, or other engine. Decay is restricted to nonland permanents of mana value three or less. Trophy and Beast Within are broader but compensate the opponent.

Deadly Rollick

controlling either partner enables the free cost. Exile the blocker that Gorm cannot draw away or the creature threatening to win immediately. It cannot target your own creature.

+
Force of Vigor + Pest Infestation

handle multiple artifacts and enchantments. Force's alternative cost works only on an opponent's turn and requires another green card. Infestation costs 2X+G and supplies twice X Pests, which can grow Champion and support combat draws even when no target needs destroying.

+
Scavenging Ooze + Bojuka Bog

graveyard interaction without dedicating your plan to recursion. Ooze can answer a specific card in response; Bog exiles an entire targeted graveyard when its entry trigger resolves. Preserve green mana for Ooze against an imminent graveyard win.

Toxic Deluge

reset a creature board that evasion and targeted removal cannot solve. It ignores indestructibility, including your own Coat and Gate defenses. Your small commanders and mana creatures are vulnerable, so use the scalable X carefully and rebuild with surviving equipment and draw engines.

Bala Ged Recovery // Bala Ged Sanctuary

recover a destroyed engine or finisher when needed, or use its tapped land face early to make a land drop. This is insurance; the deck does not need a self-mill or reanimation package to function.

Tutor for the missing part of the attack
→
Demonic Tutor → Brotherhood Regalia

the default when Virtus is ready but cannot connect safely. If evasion is already solved, find card draw, protection, or a finishing tool instead of a redundant unblockable effect.

→
Vampiric Tutor → The One Ring

find a refill before your next draw when opponents have stopped combat. Tutor costs life and puts the card on top; it does not put it into your hand immediately. Avoid a fetchland shuffle after resolving the tutor unless you intend to lose the setup.

→
Varragoth, Bloodsky Sire → Fireshrieker

attack, then pay the boast cost to put double strike on top for your next draw. Varragoth need not connect, and you may activate after attackers are declared before blockers. Boast is only once each turn and only if Varragoth attacked that turn.

→
Formidable Speaker → Ohran Frostfang

find a draw engine that also improves your combat trades. Speaker needs a discarded card to tutor. Its later one-mana tap activation can untap another permanent, such as Ring, a mana source, or an equipped attacker that needs to block.

→
Formidable Speaker → Champion of Lambholt

find team evasion when multiple attackers need to connect and more creatures are ready to enter. For a single immediate attack, Regalia or a land activation is often simpler than waiting for Champion to grow.

→
Urza's Saga → Manifold Key

find repeatable evasion from a land slot. Saga can instead find Sol Ring for mana or Infiltration Lens for Gorm's card-draw attack. It cannot find Regalia, whose mana cost is two, or Vorpal Sword, whose mana cost is B rather than generic zero or one.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three lands with both colors available, an early accelerator, and a route to combat or a draw engine. A good example is Command Tower, Bayou, Access Tunnel, Birds of Paradise, Brotherhood Regalia, Heroic Intervention, and Sylvan Library. A hand of several equipment and no mana development is usually too slow even though the commanders are guaranteed access.

Deploy turn-one acceleration when possible, then Virtus or the appropriate setup card. Attack exposed opponents early to make later hits more threatening. Cast Gorm when he actually changes blocking decisions, rather than waiting to assemble both partners before taking any action.

Before combat, identify the defender, count legal blockers, decide whether Gorm or unblockability is doing the work, and reserve protection mana. Give Gorm the Mask or Lens and Virtus the evasion equipment. Do not make Gorm unblockable when the plan requires opponents to block him instead of Virtus.

Halving one player repeatedly can eliminate them faster, but distributing early hits can make Exsanguinate or a late Archfiend turn much stronger. Evaluate who is closest to winning rather than automatically attacking the highest life total. The life loss attracts attention, so expect the table to treat an exposed Virtus as a removal priority.

Once blockers are declared, removing a blocker does not make an already blocked creature unblocked. Trample can still carry excess damage through, and evasion established BEFORE blockers prevents the block in the first place. Gorm's forced-block requirements never override restrictions on what may block him.

The expensive Archfiend and Vorpal lines are intended to close developed games around turn six or later; the deck omits cheap automatic two-card kills. There is no intentional infinite combo. Repeated commander exile, effects that stop attacking, and spell-based combo decks are the main weaknesses: keep independent threats, card draw, and targeted answers available instead of investing everything into one fragile hit.
