Giada rewards building an Angel count, not waiting to cast the biggest Angel in your hand. Establish cheap Angels, use Giada and cost reducers to cast multiple threats, then protect the board and finish through the air. A one-mana Angel can add more to your next three creatures than another expensive standalone threat would.

Start small and make every Angel matter
Giada, Font of Hope

her counter ability modifies how other Angels enter; it is not a triggered ability. Count Angels already on the battlefield, including Giada. Angels entering simultaneously do not count each other. Giada herself receives no counters from this ability, and removing her afterward does not remove counters already placed.

Giada's white mana pays only for Angel spells. It can pay for an Angel's warp cost or a changeling creature, but not an equipment activation, protection spell, token-making ability, or non-Angel support creature. Vigilance lets her attack and still tap for mana afterward, once she is no longer summoning sick.

+
Lightstall Inquisitor + Segovian Angel

inexpensive bodies establish the count before your larger Angels enter. Inquisitor makes opponents temporarily commit a card to a more expensive spell or tapped land; it does not permanently remove those cards. Segovian's flying and vigilance make it a useful attacker and blocker once the counter engines are active.

Youthful Valkyrie

grows whenever another Angel enters, including a token. Deploy it early so subsequent plays grow both the newcomer through Giada and Valkyrie through its own trigger.

Inspiring Overseer

an Angel that replaces itself and gains a life. The life event activates the counter engines, while the additional body increases the size of later Angels.

Lyra, Archangel of Dawn

each life-gain event puts a counter on every Angel you control. Ten life gained at once is one trigger; life gained separately from several sources creates several triggers. This is a different card from Lyra Dawnbringer, and both can coexist.

Archangel of Thune

gives counters to every creature when you gain life, including your non-Angel support creatures. Its own lifelink provides an independent trigger when it deals damage.

Bishop of Wings

each Angel entering gains four life, and each Angel dying leaves a flying Spirit. Spirits are not Angels and receive no Giada entry counters, but they preserve blockers after a creature wipe.

Righteous Valkyrie

gains life equal to each other entering Angel or Cleric's toughness, including its entry counters. At 47 or more life in a normal 40-life game, its +2/+2 anthem strengthens all your creatures. The anthem switches off immediately if your life falls below that threshold.

Angel of Vitality

turns each life-gain event into one additional life, helping meet Resplendent Angel's five-life threshold. Four life from Bishop becomes five. Larger life gains remain a single event for Lyra and Archangel of Thune.

Lyra Dawnbringer

gives your other Angels +1/+1 and lifelink, while already having lifelink herself. Several lifelink creatures dealing damage together produce separate life-gain events, rapidly multiplying the team's counters through the other Lyra and Archangel of Thune.

Turn the extra bodies into an engine
Resplendent Angel

checks at the beginning of EACH end step whether you gained at least five life that turn. Meeting that threshold makes a vigilant 4/4 Angel, enlarged by Giada. The token's own entry can gain more life but does not restart the same end-step trigger indefinitely.

Luminarch Ascension

once it has four quest counters, spare mana becomes Angels at instant speed. Its end-step condition is that you did not lose life during that opponent's turn; gaining back lost life does not repair the condition. Do not crack a life-paying fetchland during an otherwise safe opponent's turn when you need the counter.

Exalted Sunborn

doubles your Angel tokens, Bishop's Spirits, Treasures, Clues, and Constructs. It is also a lifelink Angel that can be warped for two mana. Angel tokens created together receive Giada counters based on the Angels already present, not on the other new tokens entering alongside them.

Battle Angels of Tyr

myriad supplies temporary Angel copies attacking the other opponents, and those copies receive fresh Giada counters. They do not copy counters on the original. Its combat-damage bonuses require the damaged player to have strictly more cards, lands, or life than EVERY other player for the corresponding reward; ties fail.

+
Exalted Sunborn + Battle Angels of Tyr

doubles the number of myriad tokens. That means more attacking Angels and more entry life-gain triggers, but the myriad tokens are still exiled at end of combat. They are not a permanent army.

Roaming Throne

choose Angel. It doubles useful triggered abilities of your other Angels, such as Righteous Valkyrie's life gain, Battle Angels' myriad, and Inspiring Overseer's entry trigger. Giada's entry-counter effect is not triggered and is NOT doubled. Abilities restricted to triggering only once per turn still respect that restriction. Throne is only a Golem spell while on the stack, so Giada's mana and Angel-specific cost reductions cannot pay for or discount it.

The Ozolith

records counters when your creatures leave, including when counter-bearing myriad copies are exiled. On your next combat, move all its counters onto a suitable creature. This preserves pressure after removal, but does not itself replace lost Angels or produce new bodies.

Pay for threats and keep cards coming
+
Starnheim Aspirant + Urza's Incubator

each reduces an Angel spell's generic cost by two. They do not remove white pips, so a hand containing several white-intensive Angels still needs real white mana. Giada cannot supply the mana to cast either of these non-Angel cards.

+
Pearl Medallion + Herald's Horn

each removes another generic mana from qualifying spells. Medallion also helps white protection and removal; Horn names Angel and can put a qualifying top card into your hand during upkeep. Horn is not a guaranteed extra card every turn.

++
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet + Mox Amber

cheap acceleration helps develop alongside Giada. Amber makes white once Giada or another white legendary creature is present, and its mana has no Angel-only restriction. Without a suitable legend, Amber produces nothing.

Sword of the Animist

equip an evasive creature and turn attacks into basic Plains. Giada's vigilance is especially useful here because she can attack, trigger the Sword, and later provide mana for an Angel.

+
Land Tax + Deep Gnome Terramancer

Tax keeps land drops in hand when an opponent is ahead; it does not put those lands onto the battlefield. Gnome ramps when opponents put lands onto the battlefield without playing them, such as through fetchlands, at most once each turn. The large Plains count supports both and the late-game Emeria plan.

Smothering Tithe

funds the expensive Angels and lets you leave mana for protection while developing. Opponents can pay to deny Treasures, so do not keep an otherwise nonfunctional hand based on guaranteed future Tithe mana.

+
Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx + Three Tree City

the finishing mana lands. Nykthos uses white symbols on your permanents; City names Angel and counts Angel creatures, including tokens. Both need setup and activation mana. They can produce white for spells and abilities Giada cannot pay for.

+
Esper Sentinel + Folk Hero

Sentinel supplies early cards when opponents decline its tax; Folk Hero makes the first qualifying Angel cast each turn draw while your commander has the granted ability. Folk Hero does not draw from token creation and does nothing without a suitable commander creature on the battlefield.

Exemplar of Light

entering with Giada's counters can draw immediately, and later life gain grows it. Its draw ability works only once per turn, so it is best with life-gain opportunities spread across the turn cycle.

+
Wojek Investigator + Firemane Commando

Investigator leaves Clues when opponents have larger hands, and Commando draws from your attacks with at least two creatures. Budget two mana to turn each Clue into a card. Commando also rewards opposing two-creature attacks aimed entirely away from you.

The One Ring

the primary independent refill after emptying your hand. The cast-entry protection shields you, not your creatures. Its upkeep life loss is manageable with lifelink, but do not activate blindly at a low life total or assume protection stops that life loss.

+
War Room + Minas Tirith

land slots become draw engines when you have spare mana. War Room costs one life in this mono-white deck; Minas Tirith needs you to have attacked with at least two creatures that turn. Neither requires Giada to survive.

New Angels offer plays before turn five
→
Starfield Shepherd → Esper Sentinel

warp Shepherd for two mana to find the early draw piece, then cast Shepherd from exile on a later turn for another search. Its other practical targets are a basic Plains, Lightstall Inquisitor, or Segovian Angel. These go to hand, not onto the battlefield.

Astelli Reclaimer

restores an artifact or enchantment from your graveyard based on the actual mana spent casting it. Warping for three can recover Horn or Incubator; paying five can recover Tithe or the Ring. Cost reductions shrink that maximum, and returning the Ring this way does not grant its cast-only protection.

+
Personify + Starfield Shepherd

blink a warped Shepherd before the delayed exile resolves to keep the returned creature, repeat its search, and create a changeling token that is also an Angel. The returned Shepherd is a new object and is not exiled by the old warp trigger. The same technique can keep a warped Sunborn or Reclaimer, although blinking Reclaimer spends no mana to cast the returned object and its new X is zero.

Personify

also saves a creature from targeted removal and adds an Angel body. Blinking removes its existing counters; a returning Angel gets a fresh Giada calculation if Giada is present. Blinking Giada herself gives her no entry counters. Choose phasing instead when preserving a huge stack of counters matters.

Flock Impostor

a flash changeling that counts as an Angel for Giada, Horn, Incubator, and Folk Hero. Its optional bounce can save another creature, return an entry-effect creature for reuse, or pick up a warped Angel before exile. The bounced creature loses its counters and must be cast again.

Remove the obstacle, then attack
+
Swords to Plowshares + Path to Exile

efficient exile for a creature that threatens to stop your board or end the game. The life or land compensation is usually less important than preserving your engine. Leave real white mana available; Giada cannot cast either spell.

+
Generous Gift + Stroke of Midnight

answer noncreature problems that Angels alone cannot solve. Gift can hit any permanent; Stroke cannot target a land. The small token left behind rarely blocks a developed flying army effectively.

Reprieve

buy a critical turn against a spell, including one that cannot be countered. The spell returns to hand, so its owner may recast it. Use that window to finish or establish protection rather than treating it as permanent removal.

Linvala, Keeper of Silence

shuts off activated abilities of opposing creatures, including mana abilities and many creature-combo pieces. It does not stop their triggered abilities or artifact activations. Your own Giada remains active.

Metropolis Reformer

protects you from targeting and gains life when it is dealt damage. It does not give your other permanents hexproof, and sacrifice effects or damage that do not target you can still matter.

Angel of Finality

graveyard exile attached to a growing Angel. Personify can reuse it if a graveyard rebuilds. Preserve it for a threatening graveyard rather than automatically deploying it to exile two irrelevant cards.

Archangel of Tithes

taxes opposing attacks while untapped and opposing blocks while attacking. It has no built-in vigilance, so attacking normally turns off the defensive tax until it untaps. Your mana-intensive white spells make keeping enough Plains important.

Winds of Abandon

overload it to exile opposing creatures while preserving your board, then attack before opponents untap with the replacement basics. It is also a two-mana single-creature answer. It does not remove artifacts or enchantments unless they are creatures.

Austere Command

choose the pair of modes that removes the threats while costing you the least. Small-creature destruction kills Giada and several engines; large-creature destruction kills much of your army. With Avacyn present, destruction modes can become strongly one-sided, but exile and sacrifice remain threats.

Finish through the air
Akroma's Will

with Giada present as you cast it, both modes give the army double strike, evasion, protection, indestructible, vigilance, and lifelink. The multiple life-gain events can generate counters between first-strike and normal combat damage, increasing the second wave. Count actual blockers and damage before committing.

Angel of Destiny

at your end step, with at least 55 life in a normal 40-life game, each player it attacked that turn loses. It must survive until the trigger happens, and the life condition is checked again on resolution. It need not connect, but its combat-damage life-gain ability also heals opponents and can undermine an ordinary damage plan.

Serra's Emissary

often choose creature to make your creatures unblockable by creatures and protect you and them from creature damage. Choose another card type when that type of targeted removal is the immediate danger. Protection from sorceries does not stop an untargeted destruction or exile spell merely because it is a sorcery.

Avacyn, Angel of Hope

the expensive capstone protects all your permanents from destruction and attacks as a huge vigilant flyer. It does not protect against exile, sacrifice, or toughness reduction. You do not need to wait for eight mana to win; the cheaper Angels and Will are the primary clock.

Gisela, the Broken Blade

a four-mana first-strike lifelink attacker that grows substantially through Giada. It is here for combat efficiency and reliable life gain; this list is not built around its meld ability.

Emeria's Call // Emeria, Shattered Skyclave

normally an early land when you need mana, or two substantial Angel bodies late. Its indestructible clause protects NON-Angels, so it is not protection for the main Angel army. With Sunborn present, it creates four Angels instead of two.

Keep the board and rebuild when needed
Swiftfoot Boots

protects Giada from targeted removal and lets her tap the turn she enters, after paying the equip cost. Hexproof still allows your own spells to target her. It does not protect against an untargeted wipe.

+
Flawless Maneuver + Dawn's Truce

preserve the board through destruction. Maneuver can be free with Giada present; Truce requires a promised card gift to grant indestructible and also grants hexproof. Neither handles sacrifice or toughness reduction.

+
Guardian of Faith + Galadriel's Dismissal

phasing preserves creatures and their counters through exile and other resets that indestructibility cannot answer. Guardian cannot phase itself with its own trigger. A kicked Dismissal can protect your whole creature board or remove an opponent's blockers temporarily for a lethal attack.

Teferi's Protection

protects both your life total and all your permanents through a decisive turn. Phased-out creatures have not left the battlefield, so Ozolith does not collect their counters and Bishop does not create Spirits.

+
Serra Paragon + Sevinne's Reclamation

recover low-cost engines, protection, mana, and lands. Paragon allows only one such play during each of your turns and does not add a land play. Permanents played using it gain the later graveyard-exile ability, so it is not unlimited recursion of the same card.

Emeria, the Sky Ruin

rewards the high basic Plains count with a creature returned during upkeep once you have seven other Plains. It is slow insurance, not early ramp. Prioritize a card that restores draw or protection over the biggest body when recovering from a wipe.

→
Urza's Saga → Sol Ring

the default chapter-three target when more mana is needed. Saga sacrifices itself as its final chapter finishes, so account for losing a land. Its earlier Constructs are incidental artifact bodies, not Angels.

→
Urza's Saga → The Ozolith

find counter insurance when the board is already large and removal is likely. Mox Amber is the alternative when you need unrestricted white mana and have a white legend. Saga cannot find two-mana rocks or Esper Sentinel, whose mana cost is white rather than generic zero or one.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three lands with reliable white mana, Giada access through the command zone, and an early Angel, accelerator, or draw engine. A two-land hand needs a credible next land or cheap plays; a hand of Avacyn, Emissary, and several five-drops is a mulligan despite its late power. Avoid opening hands overloaded with colorless utility lands.

A good start is a one-mana Angel or Sentinel on turn one, Giada on turn two, then a three- or four-mana Angel on turn three. Giada's mana is available only once she can tap. With extra mana, deploying a cheap Angel before a larger one increases the latter's entry counters; do the arithmetic before spending Giada's restricted mana.

Keep one protection spell available once you have a threatening board. Spreading all resources into a fourth large Angel can be worse than keeping the first three safe. Use draw engines before the hand empties, and reserve the most expensive threats for when you can cast them without abandoning interaction.

Life thresholds care about different things: Righteous Valkyrie wants a current total of 47, Angel of Destiny wants 55, Resplendent Angel wants five life gained during that turn, and Luminarch Ascension wants no life lost that opponent's turn. Track them separately. Cast a flash Angel early enough to gain life BEFORE an end step begins if you want Resplendent to trigger there.

The deck wins through accumulated flying pressure, usually with a protection spell or Will backing the decisive attack. There is no intentional infinite combo. Its main weaknesses are repeated commander removal, exile wipes, and spell-based combo decks that can ignore combat; apply pressure and preserve the appropriate answer instead of trying to gain an arbitrary amount of life.
