The single biggest rule a new pilot gets wrong is timing Be'lakor's two abilities. Be'lakor's card-draw trigger (Prince of Chaos) triggers only when Be'lakor enters the battlefield, counting all Demons you control (including himself). However, his burn trigger (Lord of Torment) triggers whenever ANOTHER Demon you control enters, dealing damage equal to that Demon's power to ANY target. Crucially, mass reanimation spells like Living Death and Patriarch's Bidding bring all your Demons back simultaneously: Be'lakor sees every single returning Demon enter, triggering massive, lethal burn that can wipe out an entire table before combat even begins.

Opening hand and sequencing

Aim for opening hands with three lands, early mana rocks (Sol Ring, Talismans, Signets), and either a cost reducer (Herald of Slaanesh, Heartless Summoning) or an early Demon.

Spend turns 1 through 3 establishing mana acceleration and dropping cost reducers or draw engines.

Cast Be'lakor on turn 4 or 5 once you have at least one or two Demons on board or in hand to maximize his draw trigger.

On subsequent turns, deploy high-impact Demons (Lord of Change, Bloodthirster, Valgavoth) to trigger Lord of Torment and burn opposing utility creatures and life totals.

On turn 6 or later, execute a table-wide knockout with Patriarch's Bidding, Living Death, or the Dualcaster Mage combo to wipe out the remaining players in one turn.
