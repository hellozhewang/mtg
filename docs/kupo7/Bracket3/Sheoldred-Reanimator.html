Sheoldred does both halves of a sacrifice deck at once: she reanimates a creature for free on your upkeep, and she makes every opponent sacrifice one on theirs. So the deck is built out of high-impact creatures that want to die or enter—Archon of Cruelty, Vilis, Razaketh, Kokusho—and outlets to recycle them on your own terms. Early on, cheap reanimation spells cheat monsters into play on turns 1 to 3; later, Sheoldred and Mikaeus take over to lock out or kill the entire table.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Demonic Tutor, Opposition Agent, and The One Ring. No mass land denial. Assemble infinite combo lines from turn 6 onward.

The infinite combo
+
Mikaeus, the Unhallowed + Triskelion

Triskelion enters with three +1/+1 counters and gains +1/+1 from Mikaeus. Remove one counter to deal 1 damage to an opponent, then remove the other two counters to deal 2 damage to Triskelion itself. With zero counters, Triskelion dies as a 2/2 with two damage marked. Undying triggers and returns Triskelion with an additional +1/+1 counter (now four counters total). Repeat the loop removing two counters to ping opponents and two to kill itself, dealing infinite damage to all opponents.

→+
Buried Alive → Mikaeus, the Unhallowed + Triskelion

the primary winning pile. Entomb both pieces alongside a third threat (like Gray Merchant or Archon of Cruelty); return them together with Victimize, Living Death, or Agadeem's Awakening to end the game on the spot.

Heavy reanimation targets
Vilis, Broker of Blood

an 8/8 flying demon that draws cards whenever you lose life. Cheating him out with Reanimate loses eight life, immediately drawing eight fresh cards for a single black mana.

Razaketh, the Foulblooded

an 8/8 flying trample demon that sacrifices any creature for two life to tutor any card to hand at instant speed, effortlessly chaining tutors into your winning combo.

Archon of Cruelty

forces an opponent to sacrifice a creature, discard a card, and lose three life while drawing you a card and gaining three life on both entry and attack.

Kokusho, the Evening Star

drains five life from each opponent and heals you for fifteen whenever it dies. Bringing it back on upkeep and sacrificing it drains the table for fifteen life every turn cycle.

Junji, the Midnight Sky

forces discards and drains life when it dies, or reanimates another creature from your graveyard.

Grave Titan

generates four 2/2 Zombies across entry and attack to feed sacrifice outlets or protect your life total.

Massacre Wurm

a one-sided sweeper on entry that drains opponents for two life per dead creature.

Gray Merchant of Asphodel

leverages heavy black devotion to drain the table for lethal chunks of life when reanimated.

Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel

draws cards off sacrifices and flips into an emblem that permanently drains opponents.

Sacrifice outlets and payoffs
Ashnod's Altar

free, repeatable sacrifice outlet that produces two colorless mana per creature, accelerating plays and enabling loops.

Grave Pact

every creature you lose forces each opponent to sacrifice one. Alongside Sheoldred's upkeep edict, opposing creature boards are wiped clean.

Syr Konrad, the Grim

deals damage to all opponents whenever creatures die anywhere, cards are milled or discarded, or creature cards leave your graveyard—triggering every time Sheoldred reanimates.

Skullclamp

attach to 1/1 tokens or expendable creatures to draw two cards for one mana.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

sacrifices expendable bodies on your end step to draw cards and drain opponents.

Deadly Dispute

draws two cards and creates a Treasure while sending a big target to the graveyard.

Morbid Opportunist

draws a card every turn a creature dies.

+
Accursed Marauder + Plaguecrafter

forced sacrifices that clear opposing boards and put themselves in the graveyard to trigger your death engines.

Graveyard setup and reanimation spells
Entomb

one black mana at instant speed to put your best creature directly into the graveyard.

Buried Alive

searches three creatures straight into your graveyard, immediately setting up the Mikaeus + Triskelion win or an Archon + Vilis value pile.

Stitcher's Supplier

mills three cards on entry and three on death, loading six cards into your graveyard for one mana.

Ripples of Undeath

mills three cards each turn and allows you to pay one mana and three life to draw the best card among them.

Grave Researcher // Reanimate

surveils on upkeep and grants a free prepared Reanimate once three creatures are in your graveyard.

Reanimate

one mana to reanimate any creature from any graveyard.

Animate Dead

two mana enchantment that returns any creature.

Necromancy

three mana reanimation that can be cast at flash speed in response to removal or sweepers.

Victimize

sacrifices an expendable creature to return two creatures to the battlefield tapped, assembling two-card creature combos in one cast.

Living Death

mass creature wipe that simultaneously empties all graveyards onto the battlefield, breaking stalemates completely.

Phyrexian Reclamation

two mana and two life to return creature cards from your graveyard to your hand to bypass graveyard hate.

Tutors and mana acceleration
Demonic Tutor

unconditional two-mana tutor for whatever missing piece completes your plan.

Dark Ritual

provides three black mana for one, enabling turn 1 or turn 2 reanimation of Archon of Cruelty or Vilis.

++
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet + Mind Stone

early mana rocks that help ramp toward Sheoldred or big reanimation spells.

Jet Medallion

discounts all black spells in the deck.

Crypt Ghast

doubles mana from every Swamp and extorts on every cast.

Expedition Map

finds Cabal Coffers or Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth to unlock massive black mana.

The One Ring

grants protection for a turn while drawing deep into your combo pieces and reanimation spells.

Black Market Connections

yields Treasures, card draw, and Changeling fodder every turn.

Removal and protection
Deadly Rollick

free exile removal while Sheoldred is on the battlefield.

Bitter Triumph

two-mana instant removal for creatures or planeswalkers whose discard cost helps pitch big reanimation targets from your hand.

+
Feed the Swarm + Withering Torment

answers troublesome enchantments or creatures at low mana costs.

Toxic Deluge

low-cost sweeper to reset aggressive creature boards.

Opposition Agent

flashes in on opposing fetchlands and tutors to steal their searches.

+
Lightning Greaves + Swiftfoot Boots

grants shroud or hexproof and haste to protect Sheoldred and let reanimated fatties attack immediately.

Kaya's Ghostform

one-mana enchantment that brings Sheoldred back through destruction or exile.

Imp's Mischief

redirects opposing targeted removal spells or counterspells.

What to keep and sequencing

A playable opening hand requires two or three mana sources, a discard outlet or self-mill enabler (Entomb, Stitcher's Supplier, Buried Alive, Bitter Triumph), and a reanimation spell (Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Victimize). Hands with big 8-drops but no way to discard or reanimate them are unplayable.

In the early turns, use Dark Ritual or cheap reanimation to get Archon of Cruelty or Vilis into play on turn 2 or 3 to bury the table in card advantage.

When preparing to win on turn 6 or later, cast Buried Alive to bin Mikaeus, the Unhallowed and Triskelion, then return them with Victimize or Living Death to execute the infinite damage loop. If a combo finish is not available, Sheoldred and Grave Pact will starve the table of creatures while Kokusho and Gray Merchant drain opponents out.
