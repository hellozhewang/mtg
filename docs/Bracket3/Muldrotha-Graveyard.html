Muldrotha turns your graveyard into a second hand. Every turn you may play a land and cast one permanent of EACH type (artifact, creature, enchantment, planeswalker) from your graveyard. So the deck mills itself on purpose, fills the yard with permanents that do something every time they enter, and replays them turn after turn. Edicts, removal Seals and value creatures come back every turn until the table runs out of answers; Jarad and Golgari Grave-Troll close it.

The commander
Muldrotha, the Gravetide

six mana, a 6/6. During each of your turns, you may play a land and cast a permanent spell of each permanent type from your graveyard.

An artifact creature uses EITHER the artifact or the creature slot, your choice. An enchantment creature the same.

Muldrotha costs six. Ramp and fill the graveyard first; when she lands, she casts two or three spells the same turn.

The allowance belongs to that Muldrotha. If she leaves and comes back (a blink, or Kaya's Ghostform returning her), the new one has a fresh set.

Infinite loops — three or four cards, never two
++
Muldrotha, the Gravetide + Displacer Kitten + Lotus Petal

cast Lotus Petal from your graveyard (the artifact slot). It's a noncreature spell, so the Kitten blinks Muldrotha, and the new Muldrotha has a fresh artifact slot. Sacrifice the Petal for mana and cast it again. Each loop nets one mana of any colour, and every cast is a spell for storm and Secrets of the Dead. Unlimited mana and draws: then Hydroid Krasis for a huge X, or Golgari Grave-Troll and Jarad.

+++
Muldrotha, the Gravetide + Kaya's Ghostform + Altar of Dementia + Lotus Petal

sacrifice Muldrotha to the Altar to mill a player six; Ghostform returns her with fresh slots. Cast Lotus Petal from the graveyard for {B}, recast Ghostform on her with it, and repeat. Mills the whole table out.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep three lands in three colours, a ramp piece and a self-mill creature. Turn two Stitcher's Supplier or Satyr Wayfinder, turn three a two-drop ramp, turn five Muldrotha.

The first Muldrotha turn should cast a land and at least one permanent from the graveyard: Sakura-Tribe Elder for mana, or a removal creature.

Recast the cheap removal (Seals, Chupacabra, Plaguecrafter) every turn: the table runs out of permanents before you run out of graveyard.
