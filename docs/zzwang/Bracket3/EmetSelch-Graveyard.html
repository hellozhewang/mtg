The key to piloting Emet-Selch, Unsundered is understanding its core engine: Emet-Selch filters hands on enter and attack. With 14 cards in your graveyard, he transforms into Hades, Sorcerer of Eld, allowing you to cast any card from your graveyard during your turn with total freedom.

This is a Bracket 3 build running three Game Changers: Rhystic Study, Cyclonic Rift, Demonic Tutor. It contains zero mass land denial and focuses on high-synergy board development, interactive answers, and an inevitable endgame clock.
