The mistake new pilots make with Sephiroth is treating him as a creature you need to protect forever, or rushing out an infinite combo before the board is prepared. Sephiroth's fourth resolution each turn creates an emblem that grants "Whenever a creature dies, target opponent loses 1 life and you gain 1 life." Emblems cannot be destroyed, exiled, or interacted with by any card in Magic. In a four-player game, a single symmetrical edict like Accursed Marauder forces four creature deaths simultaneously, transforming Sephiroth immediately and permanently locking in the emblem drain for the rest of the game.

This is a Bracket 3 build operating with three Game Changers: Bolas's Citadel, Demonic Tutor, and Vampiric Tutor. It contains no mass land denial. Infinite sacrifice loops are designed to close games from turn 6 onward; before then, establish your mana, control the board with edicts, and transform Sephiroth to accumulate emblems.

The emblem and edict engines
Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel

draws a card on enter or attack by sacrificing a creature, and drains 1 life whenever any other creature dies; on the fourth death resolution each turn, he transforms into Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel and grants an emblem that drains on any creature death

Accursed Marauder

two mana to force each player to sacrifice a nontoken creature; at a full table this represents four simultaneous deaths, instantly transforming Sephiroth and granting the emblem

Fleshbag Marauder

three mana to force each player to sacrifice a creature; provides another four simultaneous creature deaths while serving as a Zombie for Gravecrawler lines

Plaguecrafter

forces each player to sacrifice a creature or planeswalker, or discard; another clean four-death trigger for Sephiroth's transformation

Flare of Malice

free instant-speed removal by sacrificing a nontoken black creature; makes each opponent sacrifice their highest-mana threat, generating four creature deaths for zero mana

The Masamune

equips to Sephiroth to grant first strike and force blocks, while doubling every death trigger from Sephiroth and doubling every trigger from his emblems; transforms Sephiroth in just two creature deaths instead of four

Grave Pact

turns every creature you sacrifice into a one-sided board wipe, forcing three opposing sacrifices and accelerating Sephiroth triggers

Sac outlets and recursion
Warren Soultrader

free sacrifice outlet that produces a Treasure for 1 life; as a Zombie Goblin, it enables Gravecrawler recursion and forms the core of the Forsaken Miner crime loop

Carrion Feeder

one mana sacrifice outlet that grows with +1/+1 counters; its Zombie typing allows casting Gravecrawler from the graveyard

Viscera Seer

one mana sacrifice outlet providing card selection via scry 1 on every death

Phyrexian Altar

sacrifices creatures for colored mana; generates infinite mana, casts, and death triggers with Gravecrawler

Ashnod's Altar

produces two generic mana per sacrifice, converting tokens into rapid mana for Bolas's Citadel or Gray Merchant

Yawgmoth, Thran Physician

free sacrifice outlet that draws cards, spreads -1/-1 counters to remove opposing threats, and triggers Sephiroth's drain

Forsaken Miner

recurs from the graveyard for {B} whenever you commit a crime; targeting an opponent with Sephiroth's drain ability is a crime, enabling the infinite loop with Warren Soultrader

Gravecrawler

casts itself from the graveyard for {B} whenever you control a Zombie such as Carrion Feeder, Warren Soultrader, Accursed Marauder, or Fleshbag Marauder

Nether Traitor

recurring shadow attacker that returns to the battlefield for {B} whenever another creature dies; loops with Phyrexian Altar and mana generators

Bloodghast

returns from the graveyard automatically on any land drop, providing zero-mana fodder every turn

Nine-Lives Familiar

provides nine distinct lives, returning automatically up to eight times whenever it dies

Ophiomancer

creates a 1/1 deathtouch Snake on every player's upkeep if you control no snakes, providing four free sacrifices per turn cycle

Bitterblossom

creates a 1/1 flying Faerie Rogue on your upkeep to feed Sephiroth and Skullclamp

Bitterbloom Bearer

flash flying threat that creates a 1/1 Faerie every upkeep, providing immediate fodder and aerial defense

Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia

generates a 2/2 decayed Zombie token on your end step, triggering Gravecrawler legality and dying automatically in combat

Black Market Connections

yields card advantage, Treasure mana, and a 3/2 Shapeshifter every upkeep

Chthonian Nightmare

repeatable energy engine that sacrifices a creature to recur low-mana threats from the graveyard

Aristocrat payoffs and finishers
Blood Artist

drains target player for 1 life whenever any creature dies, including opposing creatures

Zulaport Cutthroat

drains each opponent for 1 life whenever any creature you control dies, dodging targeting restrictions

Marionette Apprentice

fabricates a token or counter, and drains each opponent whenever an artifact or creature you control dies; triggers off Treasures as well as creatures

Ayara, First of Locthwain

drains each opponent for 1 life on black creature entry, provides three devotion pips, and taps to sacrifice and draw

Bastion of Remembrance

enchantment drain piece that enters with a 1/1 Soldier and survives creature sweepers

The Meathook Massacre

scalable -X/-X sweeper that permanently stays on board to drain on your creature deaths and gain life on opponents' creature deaths

Syr Konrad, the Grim

drains each opponent for 1 life whenever a creature dies, is milled, or leaves your graveyard

Sheoldred, the Apocalypse

gains 2 life on your draws to offset life payments, while draining opponents for 2 life on each of their draws

Gray Merchant of Asphodel

massive burst drain and lifegain scaled to your devotion to black, often draining the table for 10 to 14 life upon entry

Pitiless Plunderer

creates a Treasure token whenever another creature you control dies, converting every death into mana

Pawn of Ulamog

creates an Eldrazi Spawn token whenever a nontoken creature dies, generating two deaths and one mana per body

Infinite combos and lines

Remember that in Bracket 3, infinite loops are reserved for turn 6 and later. Assemble your pieces or hold them until the table has reached the midgame.

++
Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel + Warren Soultrader + Forsaken Miner

Soultrader sacrifices Miner to make a Treasure, Sephiroth targets an opponent with the drain ability which counts as committing a crime, crack the Treasure for {B} to return Miner, and repeat infinitely to drain the entire table

++
Phyrexian Altar + Gravecrawler + Carrion Feeder

with Carrion Feeder providing Zombie typing, sacrifice Gravecrawler to Phyrexian Altar for {B}, drain with Sephiroth or Blood Artist, and recast Gravecrawler from the graveyard for {B} infinitely

+++
Warren Soultrader + Gravecrawler + Carrion Feeder + Blood Artist

sacrifice Gravecrawler to Soultrader for a Treasure and 1 life loss, Blood Artist drains an opponent and gains 1 life back, crack Treasure for {B} to recast Gravecrawler, repeating infinitely

++
Phyrexian Altar + Nether Traitor + Pawn of Ulamog

sacrifice Nether Traitor to Phyrexian Altar for {B}, Pawn creates an Eldrazi Spawn, sacrifice Spawn for {1}, Nether Traitor triggers on the Spawn's death and returns for {B}, netting infinite colorless mana and infinite death triggers with Sephiroth

+
Bolas's Citadel + Ayara, First of Locthwain

cast spells directly off the top of your library using your life total, replenishing your life with Sephiroth and Ayara triggers on entry and death, and tap Citadel to sacrifice ten permanents to deal 10 damage to each opponent

Tutors and Game Changers

The three Game Changers in this list are Bolas's Citadel, Demonic Tutor, and Vampiric Tutor. Use the tutors to locate the missing piece of your engine or an immediate answer to a hostile stax piece.

→
Demonic Tutor → Warren Soultrader

when you hold Forsaken Miner or Gravecrawler and need the primary sacrifice engine to close the game

→
Demonic Tutor → Phyrexian Altar

to establish an infinite mana and death loop with Gravecrawler

→
Demonic Tutor → Toxic Deluge

when facing an overwhelming wide board or untargetable threats

→
Vampiric Tutor → Bolas's Citadel

cast on the previous opponent's end step to untap and begin churning through your deck

→
Vampiric Tutor → Withering Torment

instant-speed search for your premier enchantment and creature answer

→
Diabolic Intent → Warren Soultrader

sacrifices a fodder creature to trigger Sephiroth while fetching your combo piece

→
Beseech the Mirror → Warren Soultrader

bargain by sacrificing a Treasure, Blood token, or artifact to cast Soultrader directly onto the battlefield

→++
Buried Alive → Gravecrawler + Forsaken Miner + Nether Traitor

bins all three primary recursive creatures in one cast, ready to be revived

→
Entomb → Gravecrawler

one-mana instant-speed graveyard setup for immediate recast with a Zombie present

→
Wishclaw Talisman → Demonic Tutor

two-mana artifact tutor; bargain it away with Beseech the Mirror or sacrifice it to Braids before giving it to an opponent

→
Grim Tutor → Toxic Deluge

three-mana unconditional search for a board wipe or combo piece

Feed the Swarm

Sheoldred's Edict

Imp's Mischief

Toxic Deluge

Skullclamp

attach to 1/1 tokens or 1-toughness recurring creatures for {1} to draw two cards per death

Morbid Opportunist

draws a card on the first creature death each turn, yielding up to four cards per round at a four-player table

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

sacrifices a creature, enchantment, or artifact at end step to force opponents to match or hand you three cards and lose 2 life

Liliana, Dreadhorde General

Reanimate

one-mana resurrection of your strongest creature or a key threat from an opponent's graveyard

Animate Dead

two-mana aura reanimation for Gary, Warren Soultrader, or high-impact targets

Deadly Dispute

instant-speed sacrifice of an outmatched creature or Treasure to draw two cards and create a fresh Treasure

Dark Ritual

Culling the Weak

Sol Ring

colorless staple acceleration

Arcane Signet

clean two-mana colored rock

Jet Medallion

discounts all black spells in the deck

Keep opening hands with 2 to 3 lands, at least one piece of early ramp (Sol Ring, Dark Ritual, Jet Medallion) or fodder generation (Bitterblossom, Jadar, Ophiomancer), and either an edict or a tutor. Do not keep hands reliant purely on 4+ mana spells with no early plays. Cast Sephiroth on turn 2 or 3, follow up with an edict to immediately transform him and gain the emblem, and control the pace of combat until turn 6 when you can safely tutor and execute your winning drain loop.
