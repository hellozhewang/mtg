Lathril's drain needs eleven untapped Elves: Lathril herself plus ten other Elves. New tokens can pay the tap-ten part immediately, but Lathril needs to have been under your control since your turn began, have haste, or have Tyvar's permission to activate as though she did. Build the board and the draw engine before spending cards on a finisher.

Establish the Elf engine
+++
Llanowar Elves + Fyndhorn Elves + Elvish Mystic + Elves of Deep Shadow

These are the clean turn-one accelerators. Green starts the Elf chain, while Deep Shadow provides black for the commander, removal and Necropotence.

+
Arbor Elf + Overgrown Tomb

Untap a Forest-typed dual to make green or black as needed. Fetch a Forest before casting Arbor Elf; a hand containing only non-Forest lands does not give it a working target.

Deathrite Shaman

Seven fetchlands help supply its mana ability, but it still needs a land card in a graveyard. Its creature-exile ability disrupts reanimation, and its instant/sorcery-exile mode drains every opponent when spare black mana is available.

+
Heritage Druid + Birchlore Rangers

Convert freshly made Elf tokens into mana without waiting a turn. Heritage needs three untapped Elves for three green; Birchlore needs two for one mana of any color. Neither activation uses the tap symbol, so the creatures tapped for it can be summoning sick.

++
Priest of Titania + Elvish Archdruid + Circle of Dreams Druid

These are the big mana engines. Priest counts all Elves on the battlefield, Archdruid only yours, and Circle all your creatures. Protect one rather than deploying every mana source into an obvious sweeper.

+
Marwyn, the Nurturer + Woodland Weavemaster

Elf entries increase their mana output. Marwyn keeps counters; Weavemaster's entry bonuses expire at end of turn, and its mana can only cast Elf spells or activate abilities of Elf sources. It cannot pay for Craterhoof, Finale or protection instants.

++
Gaea's Cradle + Growing Rites of Itlimoc // Itlimoc, Cradle of the Sun + Three Tree City

Creature count becomes land-based mana. Rites needs four creatures at the beginning of your end step and again on resolution; Three Tree City chooses Elf and costs two mana to activate its scaling ability.

Tyvar, Jubilant Brawler

Lets new mana creatures and a fresh Lathril activate as though they had haste. It does not let them attack immediately. The plus untaps one creature; the minus can recover a two-mana engine after a removal spell.

+
Dwynen's Elite + Elvish Warmaster

The Elite makes a token if another Elf is present, while Warmaster adds one token from another Elf entering, once each turn. Multiple simultaneous Elf entries still produce only one Warmaster trigger.

+
Lys Alana Huntmaster + Elvish Promenade

Promenade is an Elf spell, so it triggers Huntmaster and Leaf-Crowned Visionary. The Huntmaster token enters before Promenade resolves and increases the number of tokens it makes. Promenade is not a creature spell.

Elven Ambush

Doubles your Elf count at instant speed. Cast it before your turn for attackers that will be ready to attack, or during a developed turn to reach ten other untapped Elves for Lathril. It does little on an empty board.

Turn tapping into more mana and damage
+
Dionus, Elvish Archdruid + Lathril, Blade of the Elves

On your turn, the first time each Elf taps it untaps and gets a counter. With all eleven first-tap triggers unused, one drain untaps the entire payment and allows a second drain. It is not infinite, does not work on opponents' turns, and Elves already tapped for mana that turn have spent their trigger.

+
Dionus, Elvish Archdruid + Heritage Druid

Tap three Elves for mana, resolve their individual untap triggers, then use them again. Each Elf only receives one such untap each turn, so count unused triggers before planning the turn.

+
Vitalize + Mobilize

Each costs one green and untaps your whole creature board for another mana burst or another Lathril activation. Vitalize is instant-speed; Mobilize is sorcery-speed. They do not untap lands and do not reset Dionus's once-per-turn limit.

+++
Lathril, Blade of the Elves + Dionus, Elvish Archdruid + Vitalize + Mobilize

With eleven ready Elves including Dionus, all relevant Dionus triggers unused and two green mana available, drain twice using Dionus, then once after each untap spell. Four activations cost each opponent 40 life. This is a finite, board-dependent line with multiple response windows, not an infinite combo.

+
Seedborn Muse + Lathril, Blade of the Elves

Untap everything on each opponent's untap step. With the full Elf board intact, Lathril can drain once on your turn and once during each opponent's turn for 40 life lost per opponent across that cycle. Activate after the untap step when you receive priority, not during it.

Quest for Renewal

Four creatures becoming tapped create four separate quest triggers, so mana development can charge it quickly. Once active it supplies the creature untaps needed for Lathril on opponents' turns, but unlike Seedborn Muse it does not refresh lands.

+
Quirion Ranger + Priest of Titania

Return a Forest, including a typed dual, to untap a large mana producer. The ability is once each turn. Returning and replaying a land can help after missing a land drop, but consumes that turn's land play.

+
Wirewood Symbiote + Elvish Visionary

Return the Visionary to untap a creature, then recast it to draw again. Symbiote is an Insect, not an Elf, and its ability is once each turn; it cannot return itself. Returning an Elf can also rescue it from targeted removal.

+
Formidable Speaker + Gaea's Cradle

Speaker turns an expendable card into the creature needed next. Its separate one-mana tap ability untaps any other permanent, including Cradle, Three Tree City or a mana Elf. Without haste permission, that tap ability must wait.

Wirewood Lodge

A land-based untap for an Elf that produces enough mana to justify the cost. It can untap Lathril, but that alone does not untap the other ten Elves needed for another drain.

Draw through the cheap creatures
+
Leaf-Crowned Visionary + Beast Whisperer

Visionary rewards Elf spells with a paid green draw; Whisperer rewards every creature spell without an extra payment. Neither draws for tokens entering or creatures returned directly from the graveyard.

Champions of the Perfect

A second creature-cast draw engine on an Elf body. Exile a spare Elf from hand, or an expendable Elf permanent, as its additional cost. If Champions is countered, it never enters and its leave trigger will not return that card, so avoid risking an essential piece.

+
Realmwalker + Eladamri, Korvecdal

Realmwalker names Elf and lets you cast Elf creature spells from the top; Eladamri permits all creature spells. A land on top stops the chain, so use a draw effect or an available fetchland to change the top card.

+
Eladamri, Korvecdal + Craterhoof Behemoth

With Eladamri able to tap, one green and two other untapped creatures put Craterhoof from hand or the revealed top of your library onto the battlefield during your turn. The tapped creatures still count for Craterhoof's bonus but need untapping to attack.

Necropotence

Trade life for a new hand at the beginning of your next end step, then repay the life through Lathril. It skips your draw step and normally exiles discarded cards, so it does not stock the graveyard for mass reanimation.

Skullclamp

Equip an unboosted 1/1 token to kill it and draw two cards. Anthems and counters can raise its toughness enough that equipping no longer kills it. Plan draws before increasing the entire team's toughness.

Pact of the Serpent

Name Elf to turn a developed board into a large hand. The life loss equals the number drawn, so check the total first; an opponent with many creatures of a shared type can also be a lethal target.

Return of the Wildspeaker

Draw from your largest non-Human creature, often Marwyn, or choose the team pump when it creates lethal combat damage. It remains useful when you have one large creature but no wide board.

Finish without relying on the commander
+
Craterhoof Behemoth + Finale of Devastation

Finale with X eight can find Craterhoof but only the Behemoth itself inherently has haste. At X ten or more, Finale also gives the entire team haste and a large additional pump. Have enough bodies left untapped to attack.

+
Ezuri, Renegade Leader + Allosaurus Shepherd

Two scalable combat outlets for excess green mana. Ezuri grants a repeatable +3/+3 and trample; Shepherd sets your Elves' base size to 5/5, with counters and other bonuses applied afterward. Repeating Shepherd does not stack the base-size setting.

+
Elvish Champion + Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth

Yavimaya makes opposing lands Forests in addition to their other types, so Champion gives your other Elves forestwalk against those opponents. Champion also helps opposing Elves, and does not give itself forestwalk.

+
Joraga Warcaller + Dionus, Elvish Archdruid

A counter gained from tapping Warcaller increases every other Elf's bonus, even if Warcaller was cast without kicker. Counters from High Perfect Morcant's proliferate ability can increase that bonus further.

+
Shaman of the Pack + Wirewood Symbiote

Shaman drains one targeted opponent according to your Elf count on resolution. Returning and recasting it provides another trigger with additional mana. This is finite; Symbiote's once-per-turn limit remains.

Skemfar Shadowsage

Usually counts your Elves and drains every opponent without targeting. It provides an immediate finish through combat stalls; the alternate lifegain mode can replenish a depleted life total before another Necropotence refill.

This list develops mana and cards before converting a wide board into combat damage or repeated drains. A turn-six-to-eight finish is an estimate for a strong developed game, not a playtest result. There are no intentional infinite loops, extra-turn chains or mass land denial effects.

Remove the obstacle and preserve your board
High Perfect Morcant

Each Elf entry forces every opponent to put a -1/-1 counter on one creature they control. They choose the creature, so this is gradual board suppression rather than targeted removal. Its sorcery-speed proliferate activation can add your +1/+1 counters and their -1/-1 counters without adding unwanted counters elsewhere.

+
Reclamation Sage + Wirewood Symbiote

An Elf body with artifact/enchantment removal becomes reusable when you return and recast it. The bounce also untaps another important creature, but shroud can prevent that creature from being a legal target.

++
Assassin's Trophy + Abrupt Decay + Beast Within

Trophy and Beast Within cover almost any permanent; Decay handles cheap nonland problems without being countered. Prioritize effects that shut down creature abilities or prevent your next finishing turn.

+
Tear Asunder + Boseiju, Who Endures

Flexible artifact/enchantment answers supplement the Sage. Kicked Tear exiles any nonland permanent; Boseiju's channel also answers a nonbasic land and is an ability, not a spell.

+
Deathrite Shaman + Bojuka Bog

Graveyard interaction occupies a mana creature and a land slot. Deathrite needs the right card type, mana and a ready tap to interrupt a line; Bog is a one-time sorcery-speed land play.

+
Heroic Intervention + Selfless Safewright

Intervention protects all your permanents from targeting and destruction. Safewright names Elf and can be paid for with convoke, but protects only your other Elf permanents: it does not protect itself, Seedborn Muse, Craterhoof or Wirewood Symbiote.

+
Tyvar's Stand + Revitalizing Repast // Old-Growth Grove

Stand at X zero is cheap hexproof and indestructible; larger X can also make Lathril connect for more tokens. Repast provides indestructible and a counter, or becomes a tapped dual land when needed. Neither indestructibility effect defeats exile or lethal toughness reduction.

+
Veil of Summer + Allosaurus Shepherd

Veil protects spells from counters and shields you from blue/black targeting for the turn; Shepherd keeps your green spells uncounterable while it remains on the battlefield. Neither makes every possible interaction harmless.

Lightning Greaves

Gives immediate haste and shroud, useful for Lathril or a new mana engine. Shroud also blocks your own targeted pumps and untaps. Move Greaves during your main phase before using those effects; Vitalize and Mobilize do not target and work through shroud.

Toxic Deluge

An emergency answer to a creature board you cannot race. It can kill your own Elves and ignores indestructible, so use the smallest effective X or plan a recovery spell afterward. Once a Lathril drain is activated, killing her does not remove that ability from the stack.

Rebuild after removal
Patriarch's Bidding

Choose Elf to recover the tribe after a destroy-based wipe. It is symmetrical: every player returns creatures of every type chosen, so inspect opposing graveyards before casting. Shaman and Shadowsage can deliver immediate life-loss triggers from the rebuilt board.

Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt

Recovers creatures with different mana values. At X four, choose at most one each at zero, one, two, three and four; it cannot return several one-mana Elves together. The land face is a black source that can enter untapped for three life.

+
Tyvar, Jubilant Brawler + Priest of Titania

Tyvar's minus can recover Priest after milling, and its static ability allows Priest to tap immediately. This is a small, efficient rebuild when a full mass-reanimation spell is unnecessary.

Champions of the Perfect

When it leaves the battlefield after resolving, its exiled card returns to hand, preserving an Elf for rebuilding. If you exiled an Elf token, that token ceased to exist and cannot be returned.

Tutor for what the board is missing
→
Green Sun's Zenith → Dryad Arbor

X zero turns Zenith into one-mana acceleration. Arbor is a creature land, so it normally cannot tap on the turn it enters and is vulnerable to creature removal.

→
Green Sun's Zenith → Priest of Titania

X two finds scalable mana when enough Elves are already present. For a missing card engine, X two can instead find Leaf-Crowned Visionary.

→
Formidable Speaker → Selfless Safewright

Find protection before committing more creatures. Speaker can also find either drain Elf; Green Sun's Zenith can find the green Shaman of the Pack, but not the mono-black Skemfar Shadowsage.

→
Chord of Calling → Seedborn Muse

With eleven ready Elves and the mana/convoke resources available, find Muse before an opponent's untap step to begin repeated drains. Creatures tapped for convoke are temporarily unavailable for paying Lathril's cost until untapped.

→
Chord of Calling → Skemfar Shadowsage

Fetch the all-opponent drain at instant speed when your Elf count makes it lethal, including in response to a destroy spell that would otherwise remove the board.

→
Finale of Devastation → Craterhoof Behemoth

Use Finale as a battlefield tutor when the army is ready, or as recovery from the graveyard when the target has already died. The X-ten pump is a separate finishing bonus, not a requirement for the search.

→
Diabolic Intent → Patriarch's Bidding

Sacrifice an expendable token to find recovery. Intent can also reach Necropotence, a protection spell or Gaea's Cradle when the missing resource is not a creature; the sacrifice is still paid if Intent is countered.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three reliable lands, an early accelerator, and a draw engine or a path to one. Three lands plus a one-mana Elf and Leaf-Crowned Visionary is much better than a hand of finishers with no way to keep drawing. Mulligan hands whose only mana depends on already controlling creatures.

Do not count Gaea's Cradle as opening mana on an empty board, Dryad Arbor as immediately usable mana, or Cavern of Souls as black mana for Necropotence. The two land MDFCs help avoid missed land drops, but their spell faces compete with using them as lands.

Fetch an untapped Forest-typed source early for Arbor Elf and Quirion Ranger. Secure black for Lathril and interaction, then build toward triple black only if Necropotence or Agadeem's Awakening is part of the plan. Two basic Forests and a Swamp provide replacement-effect targets alongside the fetchable duals.

Sequence draw engines before the cheap Elf chain. With Dionus out, track each Elf's first tap carefully: spending its untap on mana means it will not untap again when used for Lathril later that turn.

Lathril is not always the correct turn-three play. If she cannot connect or drain soon, a draw engine or untap engine can be more useful. When preparing a finishing turn, retain the mana and untapped bodies needed for protection rather than turning every resource into another attacker.
