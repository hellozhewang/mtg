Flubs checks your hand when its trigger resolves, not when you play the card. Your best resource is often an empty hand with cards available in exile or your graveyard. This is a finite storm deck: build mana and access to cards, then turn a long sequence of plays into a lethal payoff.

Getting the chain started
Flubs, the Fool

casting your last card normally draws the next card BEFORE the spell resolves. Playing a land also triggers Flubs, but a fetchland putting its target onto the battlefield does not. Track your remaining land plays separately from your available mana.

Eruth, Tormented Prophet

replaces each draw with two cards in exile that you may play this turn. This keeps your hand empty for subsequent Flubs triggers. You must still pay costs, obey timing, and have land plays available; unused cards stay stranded in exile after the turn.

Containment Construct

lets you exile a discarded card from your graveyard and play it this turn. A forced discard can therefore preserve access to the card without filling your hand. Decline the exile when saving the card for later graveyard recursion is better.

+
Wild Mongrel + Containment Construct

discard unwanted cards at will, then preserve access through Construct. You can empty your hand in response to a Flubs trigger so it draws instead of discarding. This does not create mana or extra land plays.

Inti, Seneschal of the Sun

each discard event provides an impulse card until your next end step. Flubs discarding one card repeatedly produces repeated triggers; discarding a whole hand at once produces only one Inti trigger.

Song of Creation

adds a land play and two cards per spell, including creatures. With an empty hand and no replacement effects, resolve Flubs before Song to draw three total; resolve Song first to draw two and then discard one. Choose based on available mana and your discard payoffs.

+
Song of Creation + Eruth, Tormented Prophet

each spell can provide six exiled cards across the two triggers while your hand stays empty. This is powerful access, not six free spells. It can consume your library quickly, so find a finisher instead of continuing without a plan.

Abundance

replaces a draw with a land or nonland selection, helping avoid drawing an unusable land after all land plays are spent. It puts the selected card in hand. With Eruth or dredge also applicable, choose the replacement you need; you do not get all three benefits from one draw.

Wrenn's Resolve

two cards available through the end of your next turn can bridge a stalled turn without loading your hand. Casting it as your last card also generates a Flubs draw before it resolves.

Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

the front converts every spell into red mana. Casting the reverse face instead gives you a repeatable discard outlet that accesses two exiled cards per activation. These are alternative uses of this one card, not two permanents you automatically control together.

Turning land plays and cheap spells into mana
Birds of Paradise

fixes every commander color on turn one. Prefer it over a green-only dork when your lands cannot supply all three colors on turn two.

Delighted Halfling

helps cast Flubs through counters and fixes its colored requirement. Its colored mana is restricted to legendary spells, so it cannot fix every spell in the deck.

+
Llanowar Elves + Elvish Mystic

cheap permanent acceleration improves opening hands and stays useful when later drawn through Flubs. They are not immediate mana on the turn they enter.

+
Utopia Sprawl + Wild Growth

accelerate without summoning sickness and increase the value of untapping the enchanted land. Sprawl needs a Forest; the typed duals make it reliable without filling the deck with basics.

Mox Amber

a free cast that can replace itself through an empty-handed Flubs trigger, then produces any of your three colors with Flubs in play. It makes no mana without a suitable legendary creature or planeswalker.

+
Exploration + Azusa, Lost but Seeking

additional land plays stack with Flubs. These effects prevent a hand full of lands from ending the turn before the spell engine gets moving.

Dryad of the Ilysian Grove

another extra land play plus reliable colored mana, particularly useful when green ramp must fund a sequence of red and blue spells.

+
Lotus Cobra + Tireless Provisioner

every land entry supplies mana or a Treasure. A fetchland gives two landfall events but only one Flubs trigger. Resolve the mana triggers before spending the next spell's costs.

Summer Bloom

a burst of three additional land plays for the current turn. Cast it when lands are accessible and landfall mana can fund the next spells; it does not find those lands itself.

+
Simic Growth Chamber + Gruul Turf

return an already-used land to replay with your additional land allowance. They enter tapped and can slow an opening hand. Returning a land and resolving Flubs in different orders changes whether Flubs draws or discards; neither land creates an unlimited loop here.

Jeska's Will

usually save it until Flubs is on the battlefield so you can choose both modes. Target a player with a large hand and use the red mana to convert exiled cards into progress this turn.

+
Rite of Flame + Seething Song

short bursts of red mana to deploy a payoff and keep casting. Do not burn a ritual just to cycle through Flubs when you have nothing useful to spend its mana on.

Storm-Kiln Artist

creates a Treasure for each instant or sorcery cast or copied, including spells cast from your graveyard. Creatures and enchantments do not trigger it. Manamorphose becomes especially useful with this engine active.

Make discarded cards available again
Ramunap Excavator

replays fetchlands and lands discarded to Flubs. It supplies access, not additional land plays, so pair it with the land-play engines.

Icetill Explorer

combines an extra land play with graveyard land access, while landfall mills fuel for later recursion. The milling is mandatory; keep track of a shrinking library and where your finishers go.

Six

on your turn, nonland permanent cards in your graveyard have retrace. Pay their mana costs AND discard a land to cast them. It recovers discarded engines and finishers, but does not grant retrace to your instants and sorceries.

Life from the Loam

recovers up to three lands for additional land plays, retrace costs, or discard damage. Dredging replaces a draw and refills the graveyard; taking back three lands is not automatically good when your land plays are already spent.

Underworld Breach

the explosive rebuild and finishing turn. Escaping a spell still costs its mana cost and three other graveyard cards. Recast cheap mana and the missing payoff while tracking remaining fuel; this list does not contain a self-sustaining two-card Breach loop.

Past in Flames

recasts the rituals, interaction, and tutors already in your graveyard when it resolves. Newly discarded spells do not retroactively gain flashback, and cards cast with flashback are exiled afterward.

+
Eternal Witness + Bala Ged Recovery // Bala Ged Sanctuary

recover an engine or payoff that the narrower recursion cannot conveniently reach. Putting a card into your hand can change the next Flubs trigger from a draw to a discard, so sequence its next use deliberately.

Noxious Revival

rescues a key card cheaply or arranges your next draw. Its own Flubs trigger resolves BEFORE Revival puts the target on top, so that trigger will not draw the card you are recovering.

Shifting Woodland

with delirium, temporarily copies a permanent in your graveyard, including a discarded Reservoir. Copying Breach can provide another escape turn, but if Woodland is still a Breach copy as your end step begins, its sacrifice trigger will cost you the land.

Convert the engine into a win
Aetherflux Reservoir

the main storm payoff. Each spell cast after it enters gains life equal to the number of spells you have cast that turn, including earlier spells. Land plays do not count. You need more than 50 life to fire without losing to state-based actions, and more than 150 for three shots without intervening life gain.

+
Magmakin Artillerist + Wild Mongrel

turns every card you can discard into damage to all opponents. Mongrel supplies the free outlet; the draw, exile, and recursion engines supply the cards. This is finite damage, not an infinite two-card combo.

Glint-Horn Buccaneer

a second discard payoff that triggers once for EACH discarded card. Its activated discard-and-draw ability costs mana and works only while it is attacking; its triggered damage works whenever you discard.

+
Artist's Talent + Glint-Horn Buccaneer

at level three, each one-damage Buccaneer event becomes three damage to each opponent. Fourteen discarded cards deal 42 to each opponent if nothing prevents the damage. Reaching level three requires paying both level-up costs first.

+
Artist's Talent + Magmakin Artillerist

boosts each damage event by two. Fourteen separate single-card discards deal 42 each, but discarding fourteen cards simultaneously produces one Artillerist event for sixteen each, not 42. The Talent's optional noncreature-spell rummage also helps clear your hand.

+
Song of Creation + Glint-Horn Buccaneer

Song's end-step hand discard can finish the table through Buccaneer's separate triggers. Cards exiled by Eruth are not in your hand and will not be discarded. Construct permissions at the end step still do not let you cast sorceries or play lands then.

Monument to Endurance

turns discards into a card, a Treasure, and three life lost by each opponent. Each mode is available only ONCE per turn; this is efficient support, not unlimited drain. Artist's Talent does not increase loss of life.

Valakut Exploration

landfall supplies temporary card access; the unplayed cards go to your graveyard at your end step and deal damage to every opponent. It advances both the storm turn and the recursion plan even without Flubs.

Faldorn, Dread Wolf Herald

creates Wolves from spells cast from exile and lands entering from exile. Eruth and Construct enable it; casting a spell from your graveyard does not. Its activated ability also restarts card access once Faldorn can tap.

+
Scute Swarm + Finale of Devastation

develop six lands, multiply Scute through subsequent landfall triggers, then cast Finale with X at least ten to give the whole army haste and +X/+X. That costs at least twelve mana. Wolves and mana creatures join the same finishing attack.

Protect the turn and remove roadblocks
Hexing Squelcher

makes your spells uncounterable and gives your other creatures ward requiring two life. Opponents can still pay ward to remove Flubs, and removal that does not target bypasses it.

Sylvan Safekeeper

converts a land into shroud for Flubs or a crucial engine. Graveyard land access helps replace the sacrificed land later. Shroud also stops your own targeted effects from targeting that creature for the turn.

+
Deflecting Swat + Veil of Summer

protect a committed engine turn. Swat is free only while you control your commander; Veil still needs green mana and does not answer every type of removal.

+
Swan Song + Pact of Negation

reserve counters for a decisive opposing spell or removal that would stop your win. Pact's next-upkeep payment is real; casting it without a win or a plan to pay can lose the game. Neither card is good to strand as your only card with no legal spell to counter.

+
Nature's Claim + Force of Vigor

remove spell limits and graveyard hate that switch off your engine. Vigor's alternative cost is available only on an opponent's turn and requires another green card in hand; often you must pay four mana on your own turn.

+
Pongify + Snap

remove a hostile creature or bounce your own value creature. Snap can untap aura-enhanced lands to recover mana, but it must resolve with a legal creature target to untap anything.

+
Chain of Vapor + Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

flexible answers to troublesome nonland permanents. Sink can also return an opposing spell. A Chain opponent may sacrifice a land to copy it back at your engine, so consider that risk before choosing it as protection.

Endurance

disrupts an opposing graveyard line or recycles your own graveyard when you need resources back in your library. Using it on yourself removes the fuel currently available to Breach, Six, and land recursion.

+
Boseiju, Who Endures + Otawara, Soaring City

interaction from land slots; their channel abilities are not spells and do not trigger Flubs. Channel can empty your hand before a pending Flubs trigger, but you lose that card as a land play.

Tutors should solve the current bottleneck
→
Gamble → Underworld Breach

find Breach for a graveyard-rich finishing turn. Gamble can discard the card it finds, so have Construct or another recovery route when losing that card would stop you. Flubs resolves before Gamble, changing how many cards are in hand for the random discard.

→
Fabricate → Aetherflux Reservoir

find the kill when you already have mana and repeatable card access. Tutoring the payoff first does little if the next few spells leave you unable to continue.

→
Fabricate → Containment Construct

find access to discarded cards when forced discards are wasting your best resources. It can be the better early target than a finisher.

→
Formidable Speaker → Eruth, Tormented Prophet

find the draw replacement when keeping your hand empty is the missing engine piece. Speaker needs you to discard a card on entry to tutor; ensure you will actually have one after the cast trigger resolves.

→
Formidable Speaker → Magmakin Artillerist

find a payoff when the engine already works and you have plenty of cards to discard. Speaker's later untap ability can also reuse a mana-producing permanent once it can tap.

→
Finale of Devastation → Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

with X equal to three, fetch Birgi when mana is the limiting resource. The same X can find Eruth for card access or Scute Swarm for the combat plan; Finale can search your graveyard as well as your library.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three lands with access to all three colors, an early accelerator, and either a way to extend the chain or protect the commander. A green source plus Birds, a second land, an exile engine, and cheap plays is much stronger than five lands and two expensive payoffs. Two lands plus fast mana can be enough; one-land hands need exceptional support and are usually a mulligan.

Deploy turn-one acceleration, then aim for an early Flubs with another useful play available. Do not automatically cast Flubs first if developing Construct or another support piece makes the following turn much stronger. This is built for developed Bracket 3 games, with an estimated finishing window around turns six through eight when the engine survives, not a measured goldfish guarantee.

Resolve each trigger deliberately. Flubs can discard a card you intended to cast next. Cast or play from exile when doing so preserves a useful hand card, or discard that hand card with Mongrel before the trigger resolves when you instead need a fresh draw. Keep a visible count of land plays, spells cast, floating mana, and Monument modes used.

If the chain stalls, use the reverse face of Birgi, Faldorn's activation, Valakut Exploration, or graveyard access to restart it when available. Removing Flubs sharply reduces the engine's efficiency, so preserve resources for commander tax rather than treating every discarded protection spell as expendable. Empty-handed reactive play is a genuine weakness of this commander.

Before the big turn, identify the payoff and the resource that will fund it. Save removal for effects that stop multiple spells, graveyard access, or your commander. Once a deterministic lethal amount of damage or a lethal attack is available, demonstrate the count and finish instead of extending the sequence unnecessarily.
