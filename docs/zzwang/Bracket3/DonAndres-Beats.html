A theft deck that barely draws cards — it plays off the top of everyone else's library instead. Almost every creature here exiles an opponent's card when it connects, and almost every one of those effects says "mana of any type can be spent", so colour is never the problem. Don Andres then does two things at once: the creatures you steal become +2/+2 deathtouch menace Pirates, and the SPELLS you steal pay you two Treasures each.

The commander, read carefully
Don Andres, the Renegade

creatures you control but do not OWN get +2/+2, menace and deathtouch, and become Pirates. Separately, casting a NONCREATURE spell you do not own makes two tapped Treasures. Stolen creatures get the buff; stolen spells get the mana. Not both.

The Pirate clause is the hidden half. Every creature you take off an opponent turns into a Pirate, which switches on Breeches, Malcolm, Ramirez DePietro and The Indomitable using their stuff.

Nathan Drake, Treasure Hunter

mana of any type for every spell you do not own AND for abilities of permanents you control but do not own. He fixes the entire theft plan, and his attack exiles a card off each player.

Hitting them to steal from them
Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer

a Treasure and their top card on the first connection, on turn one.

Breeches, Brazen Plunderer

whenever ANY Pirates you control damage opponents, exile the top card of each of those players' libraries. With Don Andres out, your stolen creatures are Pirates too, so a single alpha strike exiles a card off everyone.

Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator

a Treasure per opponent damaged. He is what turns a wide attack into the mana to cast everything Breeches just exiled.

Ramirez DePietro, Pillager

two Treasures on entry and another exiled card per Pirate connection, and the cards stay castable indefinitely.

Vaan, Street Thief

exile or a Treasure on every Rogue, Scout and Pirate hit, and +1/+1 counters on the whole crew whenever you cast something you do not own.

Grenzo, Havoc Raiser

two mana, and every connection either steals a card or goads a creature into attacking someone else.

Locke, Treasure Hunter

unblockable by anything bigger, mills everyone a card and lets you cast from among them.

Klaw, Master of Sound

deathtouch, steals their top card on damage, and becomes indestructible every time you play a card from exile — which in this deck is constantly.

Black Widow, Super Spy

menace, and each hit digs past their lands to a real card.

Thief of Sanity

three cards deep every hit, and the two you do not take go to their graveyard.

Gríma, Saruman's Footman

unblockable, and it digs specifically for an instant or sorcery and casts it FREE.

Zara, Renegade Recruiter

reads their hand and puts a creature from it onto the battlefield attacking. Under Don Andres it arrives as a +2/+2 deathtouch menace Pirate.

Professional Face-Breaker

a Treasure on every combat damage step, and Treasures convert into impulse draws.

Gonti, Night Minister

Treasures whenever anyone casts a stolen spell, and it fires whenever ANY creature damages one of your opponents — including their attacks on each other.

Taking things outright
Hostage Taker

exiles a creature or artifact and lets you cast it with any mana. The single most efficient theft card in the deck.

Gonti, Lord of Luxury

deathtouch blocker that keeps a card from their library forever.

Bribery

their library is full of better creatures than yours, and under Don Andres whatever you find gains deathtouch and menace.

Praetor's Grasp

takes the combo piece out of their deck permanently and lets you cast it yourself.

Hurl Through Hell

instant-speed exile that doubles as a two-turn loan of their best creature.

Opposition Agent

flash him in response to a fetchland or a tutor. You control the search, and everything they find is yours to cast.

Siphon Insight

two mana, twice, for the best of their top two.

Dauthi Voidwalker

unblockable, exiles everything headed for their graveyards, and cashes itself in for a free cast of any of it.

Gisa, Glorious Resurrector

every creature that dies under an opponent's control is exiled and comes back on YOUR upkeep. Combine with Blasphemous Act — the wrath kills the table and hands you their entire board the following turn.

Blasphemous Act

usually one or two mana in a four-player game. Cast it while Gisa is out and it stops being a reset and becomes a heist.

Casting off the top of their decks
Etali, Primal Storm

attacks and casts a free spell from every player's library. The best raw payout in the deck.

Plargg and Nassari

up to two free spells every upkeep, and an opponent only gets to protect one card.

Stolen Strategy

a card off each opponent every upkeep, castable with any mana.

Laughing Jasper Flint

exiles X cards off one opponent per upkeep, where X is your outlaw count — and Don Andres makes your stolen creatures Mercenaries too, so the count climbs fast.

Brainstealer Dragon

a card off each opponent every end step, and every stolen permanent that lands drains its owner for its mana value.

Extract Power

three cards, one from each opponent, playable indefinitely.

Inside Information

X cards deep on one player, paid for in life instead of mana.

Ensnared by the Mara

either a free spell for you or a large chunk of damage. Both outcomes are fine.

Arcane Heist

a free instant or sorcery out of their graveyard, and the cipher makes it repeatable on an unblockable body.

Tasha, the Witch Queen

a 3/3 Demon every time you cast something you do not own, plus a minus that casts their best removal or wrath for free.

Borrowing the whole table
Insurrection

untap and steal EVERY creature in play. With Don Andres, all of them are +2/+2 deathtouch menace. This is the deck's finisher, and it usually kills two players at once.

Mass Mutiny

the cheaper version — one creature from each opponent, hasted.

Seize the Spotlight

they choose whether to give you creatures or cards and Treasures. Both halves advance you.

Captivating Crew

repeatable theft every turn for four mana, and the creature you borrow is a deathtouch menace attacker for the turn.

Reanimate

one mana for the best creature in ANY graveyard.

Animate Dead

the second copy, and under Don Andres a reanimated opponent's creature keeps the buff.

Interaction
Mana Drain

counter their wrath and spend the mana on Insurrection.

Force of Negation

free on their turn.

An Offer You Can't Refuse

one mana. The two Treasures they get are worth far less than the spell you stopped.

Deflecting Swat

free with your commander out, and redirecting a targeted removal spell is often a two-for-one.

Cyclonic Rift

overloaded it clears every blocker before the Insurrection turn.

Chaos Warp

the only clean answer to an enchantment or planeswalker in Grixis.

Feed the Swarm

creature or enchantment, on a sorcery.

Blasphemous Act

cheap, and much better than it looks once Gisa is on the table.

Mana and card flow
Rhystic Study

this deck spends its whole turn on combat, so their turns are when you get paid.

Black Market Connections

three modes a turn, and the Treasures cast the cards you exiled.

Nightscape Familiar

makes your blue and red spells cheaper and regenerates through wraths.

Lightning Greaves

haste matters enormously — most of your creatures only do anything the turn they connect.

The Indomitable

a 6/6 trampler that draws on every creature's connection, and it can be cast straight out of the graveyard once you have three tapped Pirates.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a cheap evasive creature and a way to give it haste or protection. Ragavan, Grenzo or Thief of Sanity connecting on turn two or three starts the engine three turns earlier than a hand of five-drops does.

Don Andres, the Renegade

four mana, and he is a multiplier, not a starter. If you have no stolen permanents and no attackers, a cheap thief first is better — he doubles the value of everything you steal afterwards.

Cast the stolen noncreature spells, even bad ones. Two Treasures per cast adds up, and Tasha and Vaan both trigger on the cast, not on the effect.

Insurrection

hold it until the table has creatures worth taking and you have a sacrifice-proof way to connect. Eight mana is a lot, but the Treasures from a single Malcolm attack usually cover the last three.
