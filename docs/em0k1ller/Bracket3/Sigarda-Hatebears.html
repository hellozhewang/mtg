Sigarda cannot be targeted and cannot be edicted — the two ways a table normally answers a commander are both closed. The deck builds outward from that: more hexproof, until the whole board is untargetable, and a stack of hate bears that shut off the things a hexproof creature cannot block. Once the lock is up, one Equipment on an unanswerable 5/5 flier finishes the job.

The commander and the hexproof shell
Sigarda, Host of Herons

5/5, flies, hexproof, and opponents' spells and abilities cannot make you sacrifice anything. Edicts, Grave Pact, Braids and every "each player sacrifices" effect simply do not work on you.

Sigarda, Font of Blessings

your other PERMANENTS get hexproof. Between the two of them the only removal left is a board wipe.

Shalai, Voice of Plenty

hexproof for you, your planeswalkers and your other creatures, plus a mana sink that grows the whole team.

Heroic Intervention

hexproof AND indestructible for everything, which is the answer to the wrath that is your only real weakness.

Guardian of Faith

flash, and it phases your board out of a wrath at instant speed.

Galadriel's Dismissal

phases out one creature, or kicked, a whole player's creatures — a fog or a clear for a lethal swing.

Akroma's Will

with your commander out you take both halves. Indestructible and protection from every colour beats every wrath and every blocker at once.

Hate bears
Grand Abolisher

opponents cannot cast spells or activate abilities during your turn.

Linvala, Keeper of Silence

their creatures' activated abilities all stop. Mana dorks, sacrifice outlets and combo creatures alike.

Containment Priest

any nontoken creature entering without being cast is exiled instead. It blanks reanimation, blink and cheating effects entirely.

Hallowed Moonlight

the instant-speed version, and it cantrips.

Aven Mindcensor

flash him onto the fetchland or the tutor.

Archon of Valor's Reach

name a card type and NOBODY can cast it. Against a spell deck name instant; against an artifact deck name artifact. Your own deck is almost entirely creatures, so the cost to you is near zero.

Sanctifier en-Vec

protection from black and red, and every black or red card that would hit a graveyard is exiled instead. Against a Rakdos or reanimator table it is close to unbeatable.

Rest in Peace

no graveyards at all, and this deck never uses its own.

Karn, the Great Creator

every activated ability on their artifacts stops working.

Esper Sentinel

taxes their first noncreature spell each turn.

Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines

your enter triggers happen twice and theirs do not happen at all. It doubles Aura Shards, Loran, Manglehorn and Guardian Project simultaneously.

Removal without targeting problems
Aura Shards

every creature you play destroys an artifact or enchantment. With Elesh Norn out that is two per creature. It is the reason this deck beats stax.

Loran of the Third Path

an artifact or enchantment on entry, and a repeatable draw afterwards.

Manglehorn

the same for artifacts, and theirs enter tapped.

Path to Exile

one mana, exile.

Solitude

free, at instant speed.

Generous Gift

any permanent.

March of Otherworldly Light

scalable instant-speed exile, cheap when you pitch white cards.

Grasp of Fate

exiles one nonland permanent from EACH opponent, on one card.

Darksteel Mutation

turns a commander into a 0/1 Insect that cannot be recast from the command zone.

Krosan Grip

split second, so it beats the response.

Reprieve

not a counterspell, so it works under Archon of Valor's Reach, and it draws.

Cards
Guardian Project

a card for every nontoken creature you play, doubled by Elesh Norn.

Beast Whisperer

the same trigger without the name restriction.

Eladamri's Call

instant-speed tutor for whichever hate bear the table is weak to.

Enlightened Tutor

finds Aura Shards, Rest in Peace or Grasp of Fate.

Ohran Frostfang

deathtouch on all your attackers and a card for every one that connects.

Loran of the Third Path

taps for a symmetrical draw when you have nothing better to do.

Mana
Birds of Paradise

turn-one fixing.

Avacyn's Pilgrim

the white half.

Llanowar Elves

the green half.

Bloom Tender

two colours off one tap.

Delighted Halfling

uncounterable mana for legendary spells, which covers both Sigardas and Shalai.

Nature's Lore

an untapped dual.

Farseek

the same for shocklands.

Sol Ring

the fastest possible start.

Destiny Spinner

your creature and enchantment spells cannot be countered, and it animates a land as a surprise attacker.

Frenzied Baloth

the same protection, and its combat damage cannot be prevented, so fogs stop working.

Closing
Blackblade Reforged

+1/+1 per land on a hexproof, un-sacrificeable 5/5 flier. Equip for three to a legendary creature and there is genuinely no answer left but a wrath.

Lion Sash

grows by eating graveyards, and it reconfigures onto Sigarda.

Duelist's Heritage

double strike on an attacker every combat — on a Blackblade-equipped Sigarda that is lethal commander damage in one swing.

Ohran Frostfang

nobody can block profitably, and every connection is a card.

Akroma's Will

the finisher for a wide board, and the save button for a narrow one.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a mana dork and a cheap hate bear. Turn-one acceleration into a turn-two Grand Abolisher or Containment Priest is what stops the table before it starts.

Deploy the hate piece the table is weakest to, not the strongest card in hand. Containment Priest against reanimator, Linvala against combo, Archon of Valor's Reach against a spell deck — reading the pod is most of the skill here.

Sigarda, Host of Herons

five mana, and she is safe the moment she lands. Cast her into open mana without fear; the only thing that answers her is a wrath, and Heroic Intervention beats that.

Aura Shards

prioritise it against any table with artifacts or enchantments. It turns every creature in the deck into a removal spell for the rest of the game.
