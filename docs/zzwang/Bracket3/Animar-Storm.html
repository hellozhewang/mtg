Animar gets a +1/+1 counter for every creature spell you cast, and every counter makes your creature spells one mana cheaper. Ten creature spells in, your four-drops are free and Animar is a 12/12. Once creatures cost nothing, Ancestral Statue or Hullbreaker Horror can return them to hand and recast them forever: unlimited casts, unlimited counters, and a kill through Aetherflux Reservoir, Walking Ballista or a giant hasty Animar.

The commander
Animar, Soul of Elements

three mana, protection from white and from black. Whenever you cast a creature spell, put a +1/+1 counter on Animar. Creature spells you cast cost {1} less for each +1/+1 counter on him.

The discount only cuts GENERIC mana. Coloured pips still have to be paid, so Peregrine Drake always costs at least {U}.

Protection from white and black stops targeted removal and damage from those colours: Swords, Path, Doom Blade. It does NOT stop effects that hit everything (Toxic Deluge, Farewell), and blue bounce, red burn and green fights still reach him.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
+
Animar, Soul of Elements + Ancestral Statue

once Animar has four counters, the Statue costs {0}. Cast it (another counter), and its enters trigger returns itself to your hand. Recast, repeat: unlimited casts, unlimited counters on Animar, unlimited creature entries.

+
Animar, Soul of Elements + Hullbreaker Horror

with two free creatures (Ornithopter of Paradise, Spellskite, Cloud of Faeries), every creature you cast lets Hullbreaker return the other one to hand. Cast them back and forth forever.

++
Hullbreaker Horror + Peregrine Drake + Cloud of Faeries

casting each one with Hullbreaker out returns the other; the Drake untaps five lands and the Faeries two. Unlimited mana.

Most lines here need Animar to have four or more counters first. That is what the first half of the game is for.

Turning the loop into a win
Aetherflux Reservoir

every spell you cast gains life equal to the number of spells cast this turn. Fifty-odd casts later, pay 50 life to deal 50 damage, once per opponent.

Walking Ballista

Ballista's cost is all generic, so after a Statue loop Animar makes it free at ANY X. Cast it for X = 60 and shoot the table.

Rhythm of the Wild

your creature spells can't be countered, and each creature you cast enters with haste or a +1/+1 counter. Animar was out before the loop, so he can already swing at loop size.

Beast Whisperer

a card for every creature spell. Careful: its draw is mandatory, so during a Statue loop stop before you draw your whole library.

Kozilek, Butcher of Truth

free at ten counters (all generic cost): draw four on cast, annihilator 4.

Cloudstone Curio

whenever a nonARTIFACT creature enters, return another creature you control. At ten counters Ulamog and Nulldrifter are both free, so they can bounce each other, but Nulldrifter's draw is mandatory, so stop before decking.

Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger

free with enough counters: exile two permanents on cast.

Nulldrifter

draw two on cast, annihilator 1; evoke it cheaply when you just need the cards.

The Great Henge

costs {X} less for your biggest creature's power: often free next to a big Animar. Taps for {G}{G}, and draws on every creature entering.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep Animar on turn two or three (dorks help) and a handful of cheap creatures. Every creature cast is a counter, so cheap creatures early ARE the ramp.

Count counters. At four, Ancestral Statue is free and the combo is live; tutor the Statue (Transit Mage, Survival) and a win condition (Aetherflux Reservoir, Walking Ballista, Rhythm of the Wild).

Fierce Guardianship and Pact of Negation are free, so hold one up on the combo turn.
