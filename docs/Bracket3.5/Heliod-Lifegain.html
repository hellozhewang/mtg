Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Heliod puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature every time you gain life, and for {1}{W} gives another creature lifelink. Give Walking Ballista lifelink and every ping gains a life, which puts the counter right back on Ballista: unlimited damage from two cards, one of which is your commander. The rest of the deck finds Ballista or Triskelion and stops the table from doing anything about it.

The commander
Heliod, Sun-Crowned

three mana, indestructible. Whenever you gain life, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature or enchantment you control. {1}{W}: another target creature gains lifelink until end of turn.

Heliod is only a creature with five devotion to white, and the combo does not need him to be one. He just needs to be on the battlefield.

He is indestructible, so exile, bounce and tuck effects are what stop him. Hold Mother of Runes, Giver of Runes or a counterspell-style stax piece for those.

Infinite combos — why this is Bracket 3.5
+
Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Walking Ballista

cast Ballista with X of at least 1, pay {1}{W} to give it lifelink, then remove a counter to ping. You gain 1, Heliod puts a counter back on Ballista, repeat. Unlimited damage and life. Seven mana across two turns: Heliod (3) one turn, then Ballista with X = 1 (2) and the activation (2).

+
Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Triskelion

the same loop with Triskelion, which enters with three counters already.

+
Walking Ballista + Agatha's Soul Cauldron

if Ballista is in your graveyard, exile it with the Cauldron. Every creature you control with a +1/+1 counter gains its abilities, so give one of them lifelink with Heliod and loop the same way.

Two cards, with the commander as one of them, is exactly what Bracket 3 forbids and this Bracket 3.5 deck keeps.

Tutor lines — what to fetch, and when

Every tutor in the deck can find Ballista. Pick the one that leaves mana for the activation the same turn.

→
Ranger-Captain of Eos → Walking Ballista

Ballista has mana value 0 in your library, so it counts as a one-drop. Three mana for the Captain, and you can sacrifice it to stop opponents casting noncreature spells on the combo turn.

→
Recruiter of the Guard → Walking Ballista

any creature with toughness 2 or less: Ballista (0) or Triskelion (1).

→
Ranger of Eos → Walking Ballista

two creatures of mana value 1 or less: Ballista plus Mother of Runes or Esper Sentinel.

→
Enlightened Tutor → Walking Ballista

it's an artifact, so Enlightened Tutor puts it on top. Cast it at an opponent's end step and draw it next turn.

→
Scrapyard Recombiner → Triskelion

sacrifice an artifact to tutor any Construct: Ballista, Triskelion, or the Recombiner itself.

→
Inventors' Fair → Walking Ballista

with three artifacts out, a land that tutors any artifact.

→
Buried Ruin → Walking Ballista

returns an artifact from your graveyard to hand.

The stax shell — use it, but know it cuts both ways

These slow the table down while you assemble two cards. Most are symmetric, so play them when they hurt the opponents more than you.

Rule of Law

one spell per turn for everyone. Your combo is one spell (Ballista) and an activation, so it still works.

Eidolon of Rhetoric

the same, on a creature.

Archon of Emeria

the same, and their nonbasic lands enter tapped.

Phyrexian Censor

one non-Phyrexian spell each per turn, and non-Phyrexian creatures enter tapped.

Deafening Silence

one noncreature spell per turn each.

Ethersworn Canonist

artifact spells are exempt, so Ballista still resolves after another spell.

Thalia, Guardian of Thraben

Chalice of the Void

counters every spell with a chosen mana value. Pick a number your opponents use more than you do; set to 1, it also counters your Swords and Path.

Vexing Bauble

counters spells cast with no mana. It also counters your own free Flawless Maneuver and an evoked Solitude.

Boromir, Warden of the Tower

counters opponents' free spells, and sacrifices to protect your board.

Grand Abolisher

nobody responds on your turn. The best card to have out on the combo turn.

Voice of Victory

Silence

Blind Obedience

Authority of the Consuls

Dauntless Dismantler

Aven Mindcensor

Rest in Peace

Grafdigger's Cage

Weathered Runestone

Soulless Jailer

no permanents from graveyards, and no noncreature spells from graveyard or exile.

Containment Priest

Calamity's Wake

exile every graveyard and stop noncreature spells for the rest of the turn. Your Ballista is a creature spell, so cast it after.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a land count of three, some acceleration, and either a combo piece or a tutor for it. Turn three Heliod, turn four or five Ballista with {1}{W} up.

Land one lock piece early (Rule of Law, Archon of Emeria, Grand Abolisher) to slow the table, but don't play two that also strangle you.

On the combo turn: Grand Abolisher or Silence first, then Ballista, then the activation.
