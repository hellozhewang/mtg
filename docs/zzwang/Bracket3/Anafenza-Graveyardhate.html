A deck built for one job: making graveyards not exist. Anafenza's static ability exiles every creature card an opponent would put in a graveyard from ANY zone — milled, discarded, countered, wrathed — and behind her sit four more layers of redundant hate, each of which catches something the others miss. Against a reanimator or a Underworld Breach table this is close to unwinnable for them; against a fair table it is a hatebear deck with a Craterhoof.

The commander and the layers
Anafenza, the Foremost

a 4/4 for three that exiles opponents' creature cards headed for a graveyard from anywhere. One-sided, permanent, and it costs them nothing to play around only if they never use creatures.

Leyline of the Void

everything of theirs is exiled instead, not just creatures — and it can start the game on the battlefield.

Relic of Progenitus

chips away at one graveyard a turn, then exiles them all and draws you a card.

Grafdigger's Cage

the second layer. Even if a card reaches their graveyard, it cannot come back — and nothing enters from a library either, which turns off tutor-to-battlefield effects.

Containment Priest

the third layer, and the one that catches what the others miss: anything entering without being CAST is exiled, including reanimation from exile, blink effects and cheat-into-play.

Dauthi Voidwalker

unblockable, exiles what they would bury, and cashes itself in to cast their best card for free.

Farewell

pick "exile all graveyards" alone if that is all you need, or add creatures and artifacts for a full reset that your enchantment-light board survives.

Deploy in this order against a graveyard deck: Anafenza or Leyline first, then Cage, then Priest. Each one closes a different escape route, and a reanimator pilot who is stopped by only one of them will find the other.

The two Elesh Norns
Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines

your enter triggers happen twice and theirs do not happen at all. It doubles Knight of Autumn, Acidic Slime, Reclamation Sage and Duplicant while blanking every value creature they play.

Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite

+2/+2 to yours and -2/-2 to theirs, which is a one-sided wrath against token and hatebear boards that then stays on the battlefield.

How you actually win
Craterhoof Behemoth

the ending. A board of hatebears that has been holding the table hostage is wide enough that this is lethal to one player and usually two.

Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite

the +2/+2 turns the same board into a real clock without needing a finisher.

Anafenza, the Foremost

a 4/4 that grows your tapped attackers is a genuine beater in a deck full of two-drops.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep Leyline of the Void if you have it. It is free in your opening hand and it is the single most effective card in the deck against the matchup it is built for.

Read the pod before committing the hate. Against a table with no graveyard decks, Grafdigger's Cage and Relic of Progenitus are close to blank — deploy the taxing creatures instead and hold the hate for whoever reveals a fetchland or a looter.

Anafenza, the Foremost

cast her on turn three whenever you can. Her exile clause only matters if it is already on the battlefield when they try, and she is a 4/4 body regardless.

Containment Priest

hold her up rather than casting her on curve when you can afford to. Flashing her in response to a reanimation spell is a two-for-one; casting her precombat is just a 2/2.
