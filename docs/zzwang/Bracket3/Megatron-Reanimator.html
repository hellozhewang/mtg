Megatron is a damage cannon that fires artifacts. On the Vehicle side, every attack sacrifices an artifact and deals damage equal to its MANA VALUE to a creature — and any excess goes to that creature's controller. Sacrifice an eleven-drop, point it at a 1/1, and eleven damage hits a face. The deck is built to have enormous artifacts in the graveyard, get them onto the battlefield cheaply, and feed them to the cannon.

The commander
Megatron, Tyrant // Megatron, Destructive Force

cast him CONVERTED for {1}{R}{W}{B} — four mana instead of his printed cost — and he arrives as the Vehicle with living metal, so he is a creature on your turn without needing crew.

On the Tyrant side, opponents cannot cast spells during combat at all, and converting in your postcombat main adds {C} for every point of life your opponents lost this turn. A big cannon shot pays for the next fatty.

Basilisk Collar

this is the multiplier. Deathtouch means one damage is lethal to ANY creature, so every point past the first is excess and goes to the face. Sacrificing a mana value twelve artifact deals eleven to a player regardless of what you pointed it at.

Isshin, Two Heavens as One

Megatron's attack trigger is an attack trigger, so it happens twice. Two artifacts sacrificed, two bursts of damage, in one combat.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud.

The ammunition

Every one of these is a huge mana value, which is the only stat the cannon reads.

Excalibur, Sword of Eden

mana value twelve, and its cost falls by your historic permanents, so it is castable as well as loadable.

Metalwork Colossus

mana value eleven, nearly free to cast with noncreature artifacts out, and it returns itself from the graveyard for two artifacts.

Mycosynth Golem

mana value eleven, and it gives every artifact creature spell affinity so the rest of the ammunition costs almost nothing.

Panther Robot

mana value ten with affinity.

Ancient Stone Idol

mana value ten, flash, and it leaves a 6/12 behind when it dies.

Phyrexian Triniform

mana value nine, and it dies into three 3/3s.

Bygone Colossus

mana value nine, and warp lets you play it for three now and cast it properly later.

Cityscape Leveler

mana value eight, destroys a permanent on cast and on every attack, and unearths.

Imskir Iron-Eater

a second cannon — sacrifice an artifact to deal its mana value to ANY target, no creature required.

Getting them into the graveyard and back
Buried Alive

three fatties straight into the yard.

Faithless Looting

cheap filtering, twice.

Goblin Engineer

tutors an artifact directly into the graveyard, then recurs cheap ones.

Goblin Welder

swaps an artifact you control for a better one in the graveyard, every turn, free.

Mishra, Tamer of Mak Fawa

every artifact card in your graveyard gains unearth {1}{B}{R}. That is three mana to put a mana-value-eleven cannon shell onto the battlefield, and it also gives your whole board a ward.

Daretti, Rocketeer Engineer

returns an artifact from the graveyard on entry and on every attack.

Daretti, Scrap Savant

rummages, reanimates an artifact on the minus, and the ultimate returns every artifact that dies from then on.

Kuldotha Forgemaster

three artifacts becomes any artifact in the deck, straight onto the battlefield.

Animate Dead

cheap reanimation for the artifact creatures.

Necromancy

at flash speed, so you can rebuild in response to a wrath.

Persist

two mana for any nonlegendary creature.

Morbid Curiosity

sacrifice a fatty and draw cards equal to its mana value. Eleven cards is not unusual.

Making the attack happen
Krang, Utrom Warlord

every other artifact creature gains flying, trample, indestructible and haste. It turns a board of ammunition into an army.

Anger

sitting in the graveyard with a Mountain out, everything you reanimate has haste.

Grand Abolisher

nobody interacts on your turn.

Megatron, Tyrant // Megatron, Destructive Force

the Tyrant side already stops opponents casting during combat, which covers the window that matters most.

Boros Charm

indestructible for the board through a wrath, or double strike, or four to the face.

Clever Concealment

usually free with convoke, and it phases the board out of a sweeper.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it redirects the removal aimed at Megatron mid-combat.

Removal
Demonic Junker

destroys a creature from every player at once, and grows if one of yours died.

Organic Extinction

improvise makes it cheap, and it destroys every NONARTIFACT creature — a one-sided wrath here.

Cityscape Leveler

a permanent destroyed on cast and on every attack.

Combustible Gearhulk

three cards, or three milled cards' worth of damage — and milling yourself loads the cannon.

Imskir Iron-Eater

draws off your artifact count and throws artifacts at anything.

Mana and tutors
Mox Opal

metalcraft is trivial here.

Arcane Signet

fixing three colours.

Fellwar Stone

usually taps for what you need.

Mycosynth Golem

affinity on every artifact creature spell — the single biggest cost reducer.

Metalwork Colossus

effectively free with a few rocks down.

Demonic Tutor

any card.

Enlightened Tutor

any artifact, to the top.

Esper Sentinel

taxes their first noncreature spell each turn.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a graveyard enabler and acceleration. Buried Alive or Goblin Engineer plus a rock means Megatron converts on turn four with a mana-value-eleven shell already waiting in the yard.

Cast Megatron CONVERTED. Four mana for the Vehicle side is the whole point; the Tyrant side is where you flip TO after the damage, not where you start.

Basilisk Collar

find it before you find more ammunition. Without deathtouch you need a small creature to point at; with it, every shot dumps almost its full mana value into a player.

Convert in your postcombat main phase. The mana it adds scales with the life your opponents lost that turn, so a big cannon shot funds a second fatty the same turn.
