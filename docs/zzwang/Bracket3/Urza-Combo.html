Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Unlimited mana is not automatically a win, and Urza is not required for every engine. Assemble a mana-positive loop plus a damage outlet, protecting the window before committing. The two critical counts: Scepter needs more recurring nonland mana than its activation costs, and Ballista cast free through Urza has X=0 and dies.

Develop mana without depending on Urza
Urza, Lord High Artificer

turns each untapped artifact into one blue mana, including a fresh Construct or an artifact creature with summoning sickness. This is Urza's mana ability, not a tap ability granted to that artifact. A tapped Sol Ring cannot also pay for it. Neither the mana ability nor the {5} ability requires Urza himself to tap or have haste.

Mox Amber

starts producing blue once Urza, Emry, Samut or Geist is out. Before then it is a free artifact for metalcraft; with Urza it can be tapped through Urza's ability regardless of Amber's own condition. Mox Opal still needs three artifacts for its own ability.

Moonsnare Prototype

before Urza, tap this and ANOTHER untapped artifact or creature to produce one colorless. It helps deploy Urza, but does not turn two tapped permanents into two mana. After Urza arrives, compare tapping each artifact separately for blue instead.

+
Voltaic Key + Manifold Key

pay one to untap a Monolith and reuse its three-mana output. This is useful acceleration before finding a combo. A Key and Monolith alone do not repeat indefinitely because the Key remains tapped; the second Key also does not make this mana-positive without another engine.

The mana base now has sixteen Islands instead of the three rarely assembled Tron lands. Prioritize two real blue sources for Urza and Power Artifact; a hand of colorless lands and artifacts is not automatically a functional Urza hand.

The compact mana engines: execute from turn 6
+
Basalt Monolith + Power Artifact

enchant Basalt, tap it for three colorless, pay one to untap it, and repeat. Each repetition nets two colorless. Neither Urza nor a creature activation is needed; this works through restrictions on creature abilities, but not restrictions on artifact abilities.

+
Grim Monolith + Power Artifact

Grim costs two to cast and produces three. The Aura reduces its four-mana untap cost to two, so each repetition nets one colorless. Start with Grim untapped, or reserve mana for its first untap if you already spent its output. This is the cheaper redundant Monolith pair.

+
Basalt Monolith + Forensic Gadgeteer

Gadgeteer reduces Basalt's untap cost from THREE to TWO, not one. Tap for three, pay two to untap, net one each cycle. Gadgeteer's reduction is one mana and cannot reduce a cost below one; Grim plus Gadgeteer alone merely breaks even and is NOT infinite.

+
Basalt Monolith + Rings of Brighthearth

start with Basalt untapped and two additional mana. Tap Basalt for three; spend three activating its untap, then pay two for Rings to copy that ability. Let the copy untap Basalt, tap Basalt for three while the original untap is still on the stack, then resolve the original untap. You finish with Basalt untapped and three floating, one more than the starting two. Repeat. Copy the UNTAP ability, not the mana ability, which Rings cannot copy.

+
Isochron Scepter + Dramatic Reversal

imprint Reversal from hand. Produce at least THREE mana from reusable nonland sources OTHER THAN SCEPTER, pay two and tap Scepter, then cast its Reversal copy. Reversal untaps Scepter and those sources, netting at least one mana per cycle. Sol Ring plus Mind Stone works without Urza. With Urza, three other nonland artifacts also suffice. Do not count an artifact land, a sacrificed Petal, or Scepter's own tap toward that recurring output.

+
Forensic Gadgeteer + Isochron Scepter

with Reversal imprinted, Gadgeteer reduces the activation to one, so two recurring nonland mana suffices. It does not reduce the cost to zero. Removing Gadgeteer changes the threshold immediately: retain enough output to continue or stop and rebuild.

With a Monolith loop already producing unlimited colorless and Urza on the battlefield, tap the Monolith through Urza for blue, then spend floating colorless on its untap ability. Repeat to make unlimited blue too. Reserve enough mana and a legal outlet before exposing your engine. All compact infinite finishes wait until turn 6 under our house rules.

Convert the mana into a win
Goblin Cannon

a noncreature artifact outlet usable immediately, including when cast free through Urza. With unlimited mana, HOLD PRIORITY and activate its two-mana damage ability enough times for lethal, assigning targets before any shot resolves. The first resolution sacrifices Cannon; the remaining abilities still resolve and deal their damage. Do not let the first shot resolve before queuing the rest. Creature-activation restrictions that disable Urza and Ballista do not disable this noncreature artifact, but artifact-activation restrictions do.

Walking Ballista

cast from HAND with a sufficiently large X, then spend the counters on lethal damage. If it is already alive with counters, unlimited mana also pays its four-mana counter ability repeatedly. Casting it free with Urza forces X=0; it normally dies before you can activate anything. Neither haste nor indestructible fixes a 0/0.

→
Staff of Domination → Walking Ballista

with unlimited colorless, spend five and tap Staff to draw, then one to untap it. Repeat until Ballista or Cannon reaches your hand, STOP before drawing from an empty library, then cast the outlet with mana. Staff is a searchable converter that works without Urza; drawing or gaining arbitrary life alone is not the win.

+
Urza, Lord High Artificer + Goblin Cannon

with unlimited mana, use Urza's five-mana ability to find and cast Cannon free, then queue lethal activations. Each activation shuffles before exiling. You cannot use this to put Ballista into your hand, and exiling the entire library blindly can strand cards you wanted to draw. Prefer fetching the outlet or Staff directly when available. If Cannon is not in the library, recover it or draw another outlet instead.

Borne Upon a Wind

opens an opponent's end step or another response window for your spells and Urza's exiled cards that turn. Without this, Urza's ability does NOT override normal casting timing or land-play limits. Flash does not override a one-spell restriction either. It draws a card on resolution, so do not cast it with an empty library.

Tutor for the missing function
→
Muddle the Mixture → Power Artifact

transmute for {1}{U}{U} at sorcery speed, finding a card of EXACT mana value two. This reaches the Aura, Grim, Scepter or Defense Grid. It cannot find Basalt, which costs THREE. Keep Muddle as an instant/sorcery counter when you already have a complete line.

→
Tribute Mage → Grim Monolith

a three-mana body that finds a two-mana artifact to hand: Grim for Power Artifact, Scepter when Reversal is ready, or Grid before exposing the finish. It cannot find the Aura, which is not an artifact.

→
Trophy Mage → Basalt Monolith

finds artifacts of EXACT value three: Basalt for any of its partners, Rings when Basalt is ready, or Staff when you have mana but no outlet. It does not find the four-mana Cannon.

→
Fabricate → Goblin Cannon

when an engine is already assembled, find the actual kill instead of another redundant rock. Otherwise find the missing Monolith, Scepter or Staff. This puts the artifact into hand and still requires casting it.

→
Merchant Scroll → Dramatic Reversal

finds a blue instant to HAND. It also reaches Whir, protection or Borne when timing is the missing piece. It does not find Power Artifact or Scepter directly.

→
Solve the Equation → Transmute Artifact

finds an instant or sorcery to hand, setting up a battlefield tutor. Find Reversal directly when Scepter is ready, or find Whir when an instant-speed artifact search is worth the extra mana.

→
Reshape → Staff of Domination

choose X=3 and sacrifice an artifact as an ADDITIONAL cost; Staff enters directly for five mana total before reductions. X=2 reaches Grim or Scepter; X=4 reaches Cannon. If Reshape is countered, the sacrificed artifact is already gone, so prefer expendable artifacts rather than your only engine.

→
Transmute Artifact → Goblin Cannon

sacrifice an artifact as the spell resolves, then search. If the target's mana value exceeds the sacrificed artifact's, pay the difference or the target goes to your graveyard. A two-mana artifact into Cannon needs two additional mana after the spell's {U}{U}; a three-mana Basalt into Cannon needs one, but sacrifices that engine. Choose expendable material and preserve the mana source you need to win.

→
Whir of Invention → Goblin Cannon

X=4 puts Cannon straight onto the battlefield at instant speed, while X=3 finds Staff or Basalt. Improvise helps pay generic mana only. An artifact tapped to Urza for blue cannot ALSO be tapped for improvise; make the color/generic plan first.

→
Inventors' Fair → Staff of Domination

with three artifacts, pay four, tap and sacrifice Fair for an artifact to hand. This is an activated land tutor, useful when spells are restricted. Count the artifact threshold before sacrificing Treasures or other artifacts, and budget the artifact's casting cost too.

→
Urza's Saga → Sensei's Divining Top

Chapter III puts an artifact with printed mana cost {0} or {1} directly onto the battlefield. Find Top for selection, Sol Ring for recurring Scepter output, or Welding Jar for destruction protection. It does not find Ballista with its {X}{X} cost or any two-mana combo piece.

Protect the attempt before committing
Force of Will

the Game Changer slot replacing the slower reset spell. Exile another blue card and pay one life to protect a critical cast even after tapping out and even when Urza is absent. Reserve the blue card; a nominally free counter without fuel is not protection.

Misdirection

exile another blue card to redirect a SPELL with a single target to another legal target. It cannot stop an untargeted wipe, change ability targets, or redirect removal to something that spell cannot target. In a counter war, a targeted counterspell can be redirected to Misdirection while it is still resolving on the stack; never target the counterspell at itself.

Defense Grid

on your turn opponents' spells cost three more, making your combo window harder to contest. It is not a hard lock and does not stop abilities. On opposing turns YOUR spells cost three more too, including alternative-cost counters; budget that tax before planning free interaction or Borne.

Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun

your instants and sorceries have split second, including the Reversal copies you cast from Scepter. While one is on the stack, players cannot cast spells or activate nonmana abilities; mana abilities and triggered abilities remain possible. This protects those SPELLS, not Scepter's preceding activation or your artifact/enchantment casts, and it does not remove static hate. It also makes your counterspells harder to fight over.

Spellskite

can redirect eligible spells OR abilities to itself for blue mana or two life. It must be a legal new target: it can absorb removal that targets an artifact or creature, but cannot absorb enchantment-only removal aimed at Power Artifact. An illegal target change does nothing.

Welding Jar

free artifact development that can be sacrificed to regenerate an artifact against destruction. It does not stop exile, bounce, sacrifice or zero toughness. Regeneration taps the saved permanent, so you may need a Key or a resolving Reversal before continuing that turn.

Force of Negation

free by pitching blue ONLY when it is not your turn. On your own finishing turn it normally costs {1}{U}{U}; do not count it as tapped-out protection there. Grid adds its off-turn tax even when using the pitch option.

Pact of Negation

costs no mana now, but requires {3}{U}{U} next upkeep or you lose. Use it when the current attempt genuinely ends the game or that payment is secure, not merely because your first combo piece resolved.

Flusterstorm

efficient in an instant/sorcery counter war, but does not counter a creature, artifact or enchantment spell. Counterspell and Mana Drain handle broader spells; Swan Song and An Offer You Can't Refuse have their own target restrictions. Leave the right answer available for the disruption you expect.

Clear hate and rebuild after removal
Chain of Vapor

bounces a nonland permanent, including an obstacle that disables your engine. An opponent may sacrifice a land to copy it and bounce one of yours, so consider that retaliation before exposing Power Artifact or a critical rock.

Into the Flood Maw

one blue bounces an opposing creature; promise the tapped Fish gift to bounce ANY opposing nonland permanent instead. This covers artifact and enchantment hate rather than only creature removal. A temporary clear is enough if you finish before it can be replayed.

Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs

answers an opposing spell or nonland permanent, with the blue land face available when development is more urgent. Choose the mode at play time; a card already played as a land is no longer a removal spell in hand.

Otawara, Soaring City

channel gives a land-based answer to an artifact, creature, enchantment or planeswalker. It still uses the stack and can be answered as an ability; spell counters do not directly counter it. Your legends reduce its channel cost, making it useful against hate that has already resolved.

Emry, Lurker of the Loch

mills four on entry and lets you CAST an artifact from your graveyard after tapping. You still pay its normal costs and obey timing restrictions; unlike Urza, this lets you choose a large Ballista X. Summoning sickness delays a freshly cast Emry's tap ability. Graveyard exile can shut off this recovery route.

→
Buried Ruin → Goblin Cannon

pay two, tap and sacrifice the land to return an artifact to hand for recasting. This is faster recovery than waiting for a draw step, but costs a land and cannot recover exiled cards.

→
Academy Ruins → Isochron Scepter

puts a destroyed artifact on TOP for {1}{U} and tapping the land; it does not return it to hand immediately. A rebuilt Scepter needs a fresh instant in hand to imprint; its old Reversal stays exiled with the old object. If that happens, use a Monolith line rather than assuming recovery restores the imprint.

+
Copy Artifact + Phyrexian Metamorph

copy a useful rock or artifact engine, not the effect of an Aura attached to it. Copies do not inherit counters or imprinted cards. A copied Scepter gets a new imprint trigger and needs an instant in hand. Copying a legendary Mox does not let you keep both under the legend rule. Metamorph can also copy a creature such as Gadgeteer; Copy Artifact cannot copy a nonartifact creature.

All routes remain vulnerable to broad artifact-activation hate. Bounce or remove that obstacle before starting; a second Monolith does not bypass it. Creature-only activation hate is different: the noncreature Monolith engines, Staff and Cannon can work without Urza, while Ballista cannot shoot through it.

Draw efficiently instead of waiting on expensive engines
++
Mystic Forge + Sensei's Divining Top + Geist of Saint Thalia

Forge permits casting Top from the library; Geist removes its one generic mana cost. Tap Top to draw a card and put Top on the library, recast it free, and repeat as needed. This draws through a FINITE library, not unlimited mana or an automatic kill. Stop before an empty draw. Etherium Sculptor substitutes for Geist's reduction of Top's artifact cost.

Forensic Gadgeteer

each artifact CAST investigates, giving another artifact for Urza or an inexpensive Clue draw. A free Top recast still triggers it. It does not investigate merely because a tutor puts an artifact onto the battlefield, and drawing through Clues still requires mana and sacrificing them.

Thoughtcast

four artifacts reduce this to one blue for two cards. It refills immediately rather than requiring another turn cycle. Count artifacts still on the battlefield after paying any other costs.

Thought Monitor

six artifacts reduce this to one blue for a flying artifact creature and two cards on entry. It has mana value seven regardless of the discount; artifact cost reducers can help further with generic costs. Its draw is mandatory, so do not cast it with fewer than two cards left after drawing through Staff.

Rhystic Study

retained as the repeatable draw Game Changer. It supplies both combo access and blue pitch cards across the table's turns; do not replace actual development with waiting for opponents to feed it.

Mystic Remora

establish early for cheap cards, but weigh cumulative upkeep against deploying a protected engine. Let it go when maintaining it prevents the decisive turn.

Timetwister

reload after deploying cheap artifacts, but remember it gives every opponent seven cards too and shuffles away your graveyard recovery targets. Prefer resolving it before finding the final piece, not gifting fresh interaction immediately before an unprotected win attempt.

Windfall

refill an emptied hand when an opponent is holding cards. It is not guaranteed to draw seven and stocks opposing graveyards. Grid or an existing Samut can improve the spell window, but neither guarantees those newly drawn answers cannot matter afterward.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two or three usable mana sources, at least one blue source with a realistic path to the second, cheap acceleration and either draw or a tutor. A one-land keep needs a concrete castable acceleration/cantrip sequence, not just several zero-mana artifacts that do nothing before Urza. Avoid treating an unsupported colorless land plus a pile of expensive cards as a fast hand.

Before turn 6, deploy mana, draw and find complementary pieces. With Power Artifact in hand, a cheap Grim can be better than committing four mana to Urza. With Scepter and Reversal, count recurring mana BEFORE imprinting. Hold a blue card for Force or Misdirection and preserve an answer for the hate permanent that would stop your line.

On the finishing turn, clear existing hate, establish the protection you can afford, then assemble the engine. Produce enough mana, find or cast Cannon or Ballista, and execute the actual lethal sequence. Do not spend every resource on unlimited mana with no outlet accessible. Keep compact infinite wins until turn 6 under our house rules.

After disruption, reassess the card zones. Lost Urza does not stop the Monolith engines or Staff/Cannon. An exiled Reversal makes the original Scepter plan unavailable; pivot to a Monolith pair. A destroyed artifact may be recovered with Emry or a land, while an exiled artifact needs another route. The aim is a protected, redundant finish, not pretending any one line is disruption-proof.
