This is the Bracket 4 version of Sisay for one reason: it destroys everybody's lands. Living Plane turns every land on the table into a creature, and Elesh Norn gives your creatures +2/+2 while giving theirs -2/-2 — so their entire mana base dies and yours grows. Mass land denial is not legal below Bracket 4, and it is the whole point of the build. Everything else is a tutor chain that assembles those two cards.

The lock
Living Plane

all lands are 1/1 creatures. On its own it does nothing; it is half of a one-sided Armageddon.

Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite

your other creatures get +2/+2 and theirs get -2/-2. With any land animation out, every land your opponents control is a 1/1 or 2/2 taking -2/-2 — it dies immediately, and yours become 3/3s.

Nature's Revolt

the second copy of the animation, at 2/2.

Natural Affinity

the INSTANT-speed version. Hold it with Elesh Norn already on the battlefield and wipe the table's mana at the end of somebody's turn, or in response to removal aimed at Norn.

Kudo, King Among Bears

sets other creatures to base 2/2, which turns on the same interaction and shrinks every fatty at the table on the way.

Be deliberate about this. It ends games and it is miserable to sit through. Deploy it when you can actually close, not on turn five for value.

The commander, and untapping her
Captain Sisay

{T} to search out ANY legendary card — creature, land, artifact, enchantment or planeswalker — straight to your hand. Elesh Norn is legendary; most of the lock's protection is too.

Instill Energy

{0} to untap her once per turn, and it grants haste. Two tutors a turn from a one-mana Aura.

Thousand-Year Elixir

haste for the activation plus a separate untap.

Sting, the Glinting Dagger

haste and a free untap at the beginning of each combat.

Seedborn Muse

untaps everything on all three opponents' turns. Four Sisay activations per round.

Lightning Greaves

haste the turn she lands, and shroud against the removal that follows.

Sigarda, Font of Blessings

your other permanents get hexproof, which covers Sisay and the enchantments.

Sterling Grove

shroud for your other enchantments, and it sacrifices to tutor one.

Closing after the lock
Kamahl, Fist of Krosa

animates lands and pumps the team +3/+3 with trample. With Living Plane already out, your lands are the army.

Concordant Crossroads

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a mana dork and a tutor. Turn-one acceleration into a turn-two or three Sisay is what makes the lock arrive before anyone can set up.

Assemble the animation and Elesh Norn in the SAME turn where possible. Living Plane on its own telegraphs everything and invites the enchantment removal that ruins the plan.

Natural Affinity is the surprise version. With Elesh Norn already down, holding three mana means you can wipe the table's lands at instant speed — usually at the end of the turn before yours.

Sisay finds lands too. Do not forget the legendary lands in your own mana base; against a table with no pressure, fetching a utility land is often better than a threat.
