Aloy does not need to attack to trigger Discover. She is a Human Warrior rather than an artifact creature, so keep her safe behind your lines with reach and vigilance while your giant robotic beasts do the attacking. Discover X checks the greatest power among attacking artifact creatures at the moment attackers are declared, casting cards with mana value X or less for free or drawing them.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Cyclonic Rift, Fierce Guardianship, and The One Ring. No mass land denial. Basalt Monolith, Forsaken Monument, and Walking Ballista provide a compact infinite finish that waits until turn 6 under house rules.

The engine: Aloy and Discover
Aloy, Savior of Meridian

whenever one or more artifact creatures attack, discover X, where X is greatest power among them. You can cast the discovered spell without paying its mana cost or put it into your hand.

Roaming Throne

name Human or Warrior. Roaming Throne is an artifact creature that can attack, and it doubles Aloy's triggered ability so you discover twice every combat.

Genji Glove

equips for {3} to grant double strike and an extra combat phase each turn. Attack with the equipped machine to discover once in the first combat, untap it, and attack again to discover a second time.

Cyberdrive Awakener

turns all your noncreature mana rocks and utility artifacts into 4/4 flying artifact creatures for the turn, swinging over blockers and triggering Discover 4.

Simulacrum Synthesizer

crafts Construct tokens whose power equals the number of artifacts you control whenever an artifact with mana value 3 or greater enters, generating 8/8 or 10/10 attackers that scale Discover into massive numbers.

Master of Etherium

an artifact creature whose power equals the number of artifacts you control while buffing other artifact creatures by +1/+1.

Kappa Cannoneer

ward {4} and unblockable when an artifact enters, growing each time to deliver huge unblockable Discover triggers.

Traxos, Scourge of Kroog

a 7/7 trample artifact creature for four mana that untaps whenever you cast a historic spell, guaranteeing Discover 7 on every swing.

Ultron, Artificial Malevolence

pay {2} whenever another nontoken artifact enters to create a copy of it, doubling up on beaters, mana rocks, or game-ending bombs.

Shuri, Wakandan Inventor

discounts artifact spells by {1} and taps to make one artifact become a copy of your biggest artifact creature until end of turn.

The giant machines
Blightsteel Colossus

an 11/11 indestructible trampler with infect that discovers for 11 on attack and threatens to eliminate an opponent in a single swing.

Darksteel Colossus

an 11/11 indestructible trampler that triggers Discover 11 and survives almost every damage-based or destroy sweeper.

Cityscape Leveler

an 8/8 trampler that destroys a nonland permanent on cast and on attack while triggering Discover 8; unearth lets it return from the graveyard for a surprise swing.

Triplicate Titan

a 9/9 flying, vigilance, trample Golem that discovers for 9 and leaves three 3/3 keyworded Golems behind if removed.

Krang, Utrom Warlord

a 9/9 flying, trample, indestructible, haste threat that gives all your other artifact creatures flying, trample, indestructible, and haste.

Metalwork Colossus

a 10/10 that costs {X} less where X is the noncreature artifact mana value on board, coming down for near-zero mana to trigger Discover 10.

Wurmcoil Engine

a 6/6 deathtouch lifelink Wurm that stabilizes your life total, discovers for 6, and splits into two tokens on death.

Darksteel Forge

makes all your artifacts indestructible, shielding your machines, lands, and mana rocks from sweeps and shatter effects.

Portal to Phyrexia

forces each opponent to sacrifice three creatures on entry and reanimates a creature from any graveyard every upkeep; discovering into this completely crushes opposing boards.

Infinite mana and the compact finish
+
Basalt Monolith + Forsaken Monument

Forsaken Monument adds an extra {C} when Basalt taps for {C}{C}{C}, netting 4 mana; pay {3} to untap Basalt, netting infinite colorless mana

++
Basalt Monolith + Forsaken Monument + Walking Ballista

infinite colorless mana into Walking Ballista shoots the entire table for lethal damage

++
Basalt Monolith + Forsaken Monument + Urza, Lord High Artificer

infinite colorless mana into Urza activates his ability infinitely, exiling your library and casting every nonland card

Forsaken Monument

doubles mana from colorless sources, gives colorless creatures +2/+2 to boost Discover triggers, and gains two life whenever you cast a colorless spell.

Basalt Monolith

acts as early explosive ramp to accelerate Aloy or a big machine, and serves as half of the infinite mana engine.

Walking Ballista

an artifact creature that removes problem creatures or planeswalkers early, can be tutored by Urza's Saga or Tezzeret, and acts as the game-ending outlet for infinite mana.

Urza, Lord High Artificer

taps artifacts for blue mana, creates a Karn-struct token, and serves as an infinite mana sink to cast your entire library.

Under Bracket 3 rules, this infinite combo must wait until turn 6 or later. Spend turns 1 through 5 establishing ramp, casting Aloy, and discovering giant threats.

Opening hand and sequencing

A winning hand needs two to three lands, early ramp (Sol Ring, Mox Opal, Arcane Signet, Nature's Lore, or Three Visits), and either an early engine (Simulacrum Synthesizer, Kappa Cannoneer, Traxos) or card draw (Mystic Remora, The One Ring).

Turn 1-2: Deploy mana rocks or land ramp to reach four to five mana quickly. Drop a cost reducer like Etherium Sculptor or Shuri if in hand.

Turn 3-4: Cast Aloy or a large artifact creature. If Aloy is already out, deploying Traxos, Kappa Cannoneer, or an affinity beater lets you declare attackers on the following turn and start Discovering immediately.

Turn 5+: Swing with your artifact machines. Discover chains into additional free threats, mana rocks, or card advantage. Hold up Fierce Guardianship, Heroic Intervention, or Cyclonic Rift to protect your board.

Turn 6+: If combat is locked down or you need to close immediately, tutor and assemble Basalt Monolith + Forsaken Monument for infinite mana into Walking Ballista or Urza.
