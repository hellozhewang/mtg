Omega does not just tap creatures for a single turn. Its Wave Cannon trigger scales directly with the number of nonbasic lands you control and fires whenever Omega enters the battlefield. With thirty-six nonbasic lands in the ninety-nine, each entrance slaps eight to twelve stun counters onto each opponent's most critical nonland permanent while gaining you eight to twelve life. When paired with flicker engines and trigger doublers, Omega systematically freezes out opposing commanders, engines, and blockers for the remainder of the game while fueling game-ending Aetherflux Reservoir blasts.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Cyclonic Rift, Field of the Dead, and Rhystic Study. No mass land denial. Infinite flicker combos (Deadeye Navigator plus Peregrine Drake, and Ghostly Flicker loops) wait until turn 6 under house rules.

The Wave Cannon lock
Omega, Heartless Evolution

an 8/8 legendary artifact Robot. When it enters, tap up to one target nonland permanent each opponent controls, put X stun counters on each, and gain X life, where X is your nonbasic land count.

Fear of Sleep Paralysis

an enchantment creature that prevents opponents from removing stun counters from their permanents; permanents stunned by Omega can never untap as long as this remains on board.

Verity Circle

draws a card whenever an opponent's creature becomes tapped without attacking, turning every Omega trigger into multiple free card draws.

Summon: Shiva

taps down creatures with stun counters on early chapters, and Chapter III draws a card for each tapped creature your opponents control.

Evolution Sage

landfall proliferates, adding extra stun counters onto every tapped opposing threat whenever you play or ramp a land.

Karn's Bastion

utility nonbasic land that proliferates for {4} and {T} to keep opposing boards locked down.

Infinite flicker and the lockdown finish
+
Deadeye Navigator + Peregrine Drake

soulbond Deadeye Navigator to Peregrine Drake; pay {1}{U} to flicker Drake, untapping five lands to generate infinite mana. Then soulbond Deadeye to Omega and flicker it infinitely to tap down every opposing nonland permanent with infinite stun counters and gain infinite life.

++
Ghostly Flicker + Archaeomancer + Peregrine Drake

cast Ghostly Flicker targeting Archaeomancer and Drake; Drake untaps five lands and Archaeomancer returns Ghostly Flicker to hand, netting infinite mana. Swap Drake for Omega to tap and freeze all opposing nonland permanents.

+
Aetherflux Reservoir + Omega, Heartless Evolution

repeated Omega Wave Cannon triggers generate enormous life totals, enabling Aetherflux Reservoir to fire 50-damage laser blasts to eliminate all opponents.

Craterhoof Behemoth

gives your board trample and massive power boosts based on creature count, easily swinging past frozen opposing blockers for a lethal combat finish.

Under Bracket 3 rules, the Deadeye Navigator and Peregrine Drake loop must wait until turn 6 or later. Use turns 1 through 5 to develop your nonbasic land base, cast Omega, and lock down the table's primary threats.

Nonbasic land ramp engine
Skyshroud Claim

searches for up to two Forest cards (such as Tropical Island, Breeding Pool, Hedge Maze, or Tangled Islet) and puts them onto the battlefield untapped.

Open the Way

reveals cards until you hit up to four lands and puts them onto the battlefield tapped, heavily inflating your nonbasic land count for Omega.

Farseek

fetches a nonbasic dual land (Breeding Pool, Tropical Island, Hedge Maze, Rimewood Falls) to fix colors and count toward Wave Cannon.

Overlord of the Hauntwoods

Spelunking

draws a card and allows your lands to enter the battlefield untapped, letting lands from Open the Way or bounce lands enter active immediately.

Dryad of the Ilysian Grove

grants an extra land play each turn and lets your lands tap for all colors.

Field of the Dead

Shifting Woodland

Talon Gates of Madara

nonbasic land that phases out a creature upon entering, protecting your pieces or removing opposing blockers.

Flicker engines and trigger multipliers
Panharmonicon

doubles Omega's Wave Cannon entrance trigger, tapping two permanents per opponent, doubling the stun counters, and doubling the life gain.

Starfield Vocalist

Roaming Throne

Spark Double

enters as a nonlegendary copy of Omega, triggering Wave Cannon and providing a second massive 9/9 body that does not die to the legend rule.

Thassa, Deep-Dwelling

Y'shtola Rhul

Conjurer's Closet

repeatable end-step flicker to lock down new threats.

Displacer Kitten

Opening hand and sequencing

A winning hand contains two to three lands (preferably nonbasics), early mana acceleration (Sol Ring, Birds of Paradise, Delighted Halfling, Farseek, Nature's Lore), and an engine piece (Rhystic Study, Spelunking, Lotus Cobra, Sylvan Library).

Turn 1-2: Develop mana rocks and cast land ramp to build your nonbasic count.

Turn 3-4: Deploy value engines or trigger doublers (Panharmonicon, Starfield Vocalist, Roaming Throne). Ramp into six to seven mana with Skyshroud Claim or Open the Way.

Turn 5-6: Cast Omega, Heartless Evolution. Target each opponent's key threat, commander, or engine, burying them under six to ten stun counters and gaining six to ten life.

Turn 7+: Establish flicker engines (Thassa, Displacer Kitten, Y'shtola) or lock pieces (Fear of Sleep Paralysis). Assemble Deadeye Navigator + Peregrine Drake to freeze the entire table and fire Aetherflux Reservoir laser blasts, or overrun with Craterhoof Behemoth.
