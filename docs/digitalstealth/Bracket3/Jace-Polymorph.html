The single biggest mistake a new pilot makes with Jace, Multiverse Architect is putting creature cards into the ninety-nine. Jace's −3 ability exiles a creature or planeswalker you control to reveal cards until hitting a creature or planeswalker card. By running *zero* creature cards in the ninety-nine, every single Jace activation is mathematically guaranteed to hit one of your fifteen Giant Planeswalkers (mana value 5 to 8). Furthermore, if you ever draw a high-cost planeswalker into your hand, do not panic: Jace's +1 allows you to draw two cards and tuck that giant planeswalker directly onto the bottom of your library, perfectly setting up your next −3 activation.

The Multiverse Architect and the walker cascade
Jace, Multiverse Architect

Cast for {1}{W}{U}{B}{R}. Taxes opponents {2} to attack your Jaces, draws cards while tucking big walkers with +1, and turns any token into a Giant Planeswalker with −3.

Reality Scramble

Four-mana sorcery with retrace. Targets any planeswalker or token to reveal until hitting another card sharing that card type, flipping another Giant Planeswalker directly onto the battlefield.

Reweave

Six-mana instant that sacrifices a permanent to flip into another permanent sharing its card type, turning any planeswalker into another titan.

Opening hand and sequencing

Prioritize opening hands with 3 lands, at least two colors of early ramp (Sol Ring, Talismans), and a cheap token producer (Bitterblossom, Skrelv's Hive, Hard Evidence, or Jace's Machinations).

On turns 1 through 3, develop your mana rocks and deploy a token generator so a creature or Jace token is already waiting on the battlefield.

On turn 4 or 5, cast Jace, Multiverse Architect and immediately activate his −3 targeting your token, flipping directly into a game-ending Giant Planeswalker.

If you happen to draw a high-mana walker in your hand, activate Jace's +1 to draw two cards and tuck that walker onto the bottom of your library for future Polymorphs.

Once multiple Giant Planeswalkers are deployed, use Oath of Teferi, The Chain Veil, and All Will Be One to double your activations and burn out the table, or activate Sarkhan the Masterless to swing for lethal Dragon damage.
