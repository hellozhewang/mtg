Valgavoth draws you a card and grows whenever an opponent loses life for the first time during THEIR OWN turn — once per opponent, per turn cycle. That is the design constraint the whole deck answers: every punisher here is a passive that fires on somebody else's turn. Play a land, take two. Cast a spell, take two. Draw a card, take one. Each of those is a card for you and a counter on a flier that quickly gets out of reach.

The commander
Valgavoth, Harrower of Souls

4/4 flier with ward—pay 2 life, and a +1/+1 counter plus a CARD every time an opponent loses life for the first time on their own turn. In a four-player pod that is three cards a round.

The trigger is once per opponent per turn, and only on their turn. Damage you deal on your own turn does nothing for it — so the punishers below are not just a clock, they are the draw engine.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud, because a ward of two life stops almost nothing.

Mithril Coat

flash, indestructible, attaches itself. Hold it as a counterspell for removal.

Punishers that fire on their turn
Polluted Bonds

two life every time an opponent plays a land. It triggers on the first land drop of their turn, every turn, from every opponent. This is the best Valgavoth enabler in the deck.

Roiling Vortex

a point at each player's upkeep — guaranteed, from every opponent, before they do anything — plus five damage to anyone casting something for free.

Spellshock

two damage per spell, from every player.

Pyrostatic Pillar

two damage on every cheap spell, which is most of what a table casts.

The Frightful Four

their first noncreature spell each turn costs its mana value in life.

Gleeful Arsonist

damage equal to its power on every noncreature spell they cast, and undying brings it back bigger.

Harsh Mentor

two damage for every nonmana activated ability they use.

Fate Unraveler

a damage per card they draw — including their draw step.

Razorkin Needlehead

the same, and it first-strikes on your turn.

Kederekt Parasite

another copy while you control a red permanent.

Scrawling Crawler

forces everyone to draw at your upkeep and taxes every draw they take.

Sheoldred, the Apocalypse

two life per card they draw, and two GAINED per card you draw.

Blood Seeker

a life for every creature they play.

Tunnel Ignus

three damage for every extra land a ramp deck plays in a turn.

Revenge of Ravens

attacking you costs a life per creature and gains you one.

The loop
Exquisite Blood

whenever an opponent loses life, you gain that much.

Enduring Tenacity

whenever you gain life, an opponent loses that much.

The two together feed each other until the table is dead. It is two cards and needs no combat: allowed in Bracket 3, used from turn 6 on.

Bloodthirsty Conqueror

the second copy of the Exquisite Blood half, on a 5/5 flying deathtoucher.

Bloodchief Ascension

three quest counters comes fast when the table is losing life every turn, and then every card into any opponent's graveyard drains two more.

Multiplying the damage
Solphim, Mayhem Dominus

doubles all noncombat damage you deal to opponents. Every punisher above fires twice as hard.

Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might // Temple of Power

any red source you control dealing noncombat damage below its power deals FOUR instead. Pyrostatic Pillar's two becomes four; Fate Unraveler's one becomes four.

Lasting Tarfire

two damage to each opponent at every end step you put a counter on a creature — and Valgavoth puts counters on himself constantly.

Basilisk Collar

deathtouch and lifelink on a pinger turns every ping into a dead creature and a life gain, which Enduring Tenacity converts straight back into their life loss.

Chandra's Ignition

point it at Valgavoth once he has grown. His power hits every other creature on the board and every opponent in the face.

Not dying while it happens
Crawlspace

no more than two creatures can attack you each combat.

Silent Arbiter

no more than one attacks, and no more than one blocks.

Koskun Falls

tap a creature at upkeep and they need three creatures to attack you at all.

No Mercy

anything that damages you dies.

Forcefield

reduces the one big attacker that does get through to a single point.

Revenge of Ravens

attacking you is a losing proposition even before the above.

Kardur, Doomscourge

forces their board to attack each other instead of you, and drains for every creature that dies.

Ophiomancer

a deathtouch Snake every upkeep, which blocks anything.

Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it redirects the removal aimed at Valgavoth.

Toxic Deluge

a scalable wrath your enchantments ignore.

Blasphemous Act

cheap, and this deck's threats are mostly enchantments.

Removal and mana
Chaos Warp

Rakdos's only clean answer to an enchantment or planeswalker.

Rakdos Charm

graveyard hate, an artifact, or a symmetric ping that finishes a token board.

Shadowspear

strips hexproof and indestructible, and gives Valgavoth trample and lifelink.

Hexing Squelcher

your spells cannot be countered and your creatures get ward.

Liquimetal Torque

fixes, and turns a problem permanent into an artifact you can destroy.

Jeska's Will

with a commander out, both halves.

Black Market Connections

three effects a turn, paid in life you are gaining back.

Demonic Tutor

any card — usually Polluted Bonds or a combo half.

Reanimate

one mana for the best creature in any graveyard.

The One Ring

a turn of protection and then an escalating draw engine.

The Soul Stone

once harnessed, a creature back every upkeep.

Boggart Trawler // Boggart Bog

graveyard hate on a body, or a land when you need one.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a rock and a cheap punisher. Pyrostatic Pillar or Roiling Vortex on turn two means Valgavoth is drawing you three cards a round from the moment he lands.

Prioritise the punishers that trigger on THEIR turn over the ones that trigger on yours. Polluted Bonds and Roiling Vortex are worth more here than any amount of damage you can deal in your own main phase.

Valgavoth, Harrower of Souls

four mana and a flier, so he is safe to deploy on curve. He is also the deck's card advantage — losing him costs you three cards a turn, so hold Mithril Coat or Deflecting Swat when you can.

Watch your own life total. Roiling Vortex and Spellshock hit you too, and Black Market Connections, Toxic Deluge and Reanimate all cost life. Enduring Tenacity and Bloodthirsty Conqueror are what make that sustainable — get one down before you start spending.
