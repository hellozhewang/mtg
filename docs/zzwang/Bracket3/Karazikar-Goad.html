Goad does two things at once: it forces a creature to attack, and it forbids it from attacking you. So the deck's job is to point the entire table's board at each other, sit behind a couple of cheap defensive artifacts, and collect the pieces afterwards. The single most important upgrade is Bothersome Quasit, because a goaded creature that also cannot BLOCK means your opponents are simultaneously forced to attack and unable to defend.

The commander
Karazikar, the Eye Tyrant

every time you attack a player, tap AND goad a creature that player controls. Attacking three opponents taps and goads three creatures, and the goad lasts until your next turn — so they spend their whole turn cycle attacking each other.

Attack with anything, even a 1/1. The trigger is on attacking a PLAYER, not on damage, so a chump attacker still goads their best blocker.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud for a five-drop the whole table wants dead.

Blackblade Reforged

+1/+1 per land on a legendary creature for three. It turns Karazikar into a real clock while the table fights.

Making goad lethal
Bothersome Quasit

goaded creatures your opponents control CANNOT BLOCK, and it goads another creature on every noncreature spell you cast. This is the card that turns goad from an annoyance into a lock.

Baeloth Barrityl, Entertainer

everything smaller than him is permanently goaded, with no work from you.

Disrupt Decorum

goad every creature you do not control, all at once.

Fast Forward

the same, and it costs one less per opponent you attacked this turn — which Karazikar encourages anyway.

Kardur, Doomscourge

everything of theirs attacks, and it drains them for each creature that dies afterwards.

Geode Rager

goads a whole player's board on every landfall.

Grenzo, Havoc Raiser

goads on every connection, or steals a card off their library instead.

Coercive Impetus

a permanent goad on one creature, with a counter.

Bloodthirsty Blade

a movable permanent goad — attach it to whatever is most dangerous each turn.

Hot Pursuit

suspects a creature, so it cannot block at all while the enchantment stays out.

Not being attacked yourself

Goad says "a player other than you if able", but the table still has creatures that were never goaded. These cover the gap.

Crawlspace

no more than two creatures can attack you each combat. Against a goaded board this is close to immunity.

Forcefield

reduces the one big attacker that does get through to a single point.

Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out, for the creature that would actually kill you.

Deflecting Swat

free, and it redirects a removal spell or a trigger.

Fire Covenant

pay life to spread damage across their board at instant speed — usually during their combat, after blockers.

Blasphemous Act

on a table full of forced attackers, this is one or two mana and resets everything.

Profiting from the war
Grim Hireling

two Treasures on every combat damage step. In a deck attacking three players, that is a lot of mana.

Black Market Connections

three effects a turn, paid in life.

Bitterblossom

a free flier every upkeep, which is both a chump blocker and an extra attacker to trigger Karazikar.

Loyal Apprentice

a hasty Thopter each combat while your commander is out.

Life of the Party

gets +X for every creature that attacked this turn — after a mass goad, that number is enormous.

Jeska's Will

with a commander out, both halves: a mana burst and three impulse cards.

Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

copies a creature for another attack trigger, another goad, and another Grim Hireling payout.

Closing the game
Insurrection

take every creature on the board, untapped and hasted. After a turn of goading, most of them are already damaged and their controllers have no blockers. This usually ends the game outright.

Karazikar, the Eye Tyrant

with Blackblade Reforged attached, he is a fifteen-power trampler while everyone else is busy.

Life of the Party

the other finisher — one enormous first-striking trampler after a table-wide attack.

Grenzo, Havoc Raiser

steals the cards you need while chipping in.

Interaction and mana
Chaos Warp

Dauthi Voidwalker

unblockable, blanks their graveyards, and steals their best card.

Boggart Trawler // Boggart Bog

graveyard hate on a body, and a land when you need one.

Hexing Squelcher

Demonic Tutor

any card.

Liquimetal Torque

Mind Stone

ramp early, a card later.

Fellwar Stone

usually taps for what you need.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep a rock and a defensive artifact. Crawlspace on turn three means the goaded board genuinely cannot reach you, which is what makes the whole plan safe rather than merely chaotic.

Attack every turn, with everything, at every opponent you can. Karazikar's trigger is per player attacked, so spreading one creature each at three players is three goads — far better than sending everything at one person.

Bothersome Quasit

find it. Goad alone makes them attack; Quasit makes them unable to block, which converts the table's forced attacks into a board wipe they perform on each other.

Insurrection

hold it until two opponents are low and their boards are still large. Taking a goaded, damaged table's creatures is usually lethal to more than one player at once.
