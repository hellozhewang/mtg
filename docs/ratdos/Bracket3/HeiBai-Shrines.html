Hei Bai's Spirit tokens are not Shrines. Grow the Shrine count with Go-Shintai of Life's Origin and repeated Hei Bai entries; spend WUBRG on Spirits once the engine is established and you can protect it.

Bracket 3 for ratdos: 100 cards, 35 lands and 15 Shrine cards. The three Game Changers are Aura Shards, Rhystic Study and Serra's Sanctum. This is a Shrine-and-blink engine with table-wide drain and token combat finishes, rather than a dedicated infinite-combo list.

Turn Hei Bai into a Shrine engine
Hei Bai, Forest Guardian

costs only 3G despite having five-color identity. Each entry reveals until the first Shrine, which you may put directly onto the battlefield, then shuffles. This is not a choice among every Shrine in the library. The token activation counts legendary enchantments, including your non-Shrine legendary enchantments; it does not count every enchantment or every legendary permanent.

+
Ephemerate + Hei Bai, Forest Guardian

one white mana buys a new entry now and, if cast from hand and it resolves, another cast through rebound next upkeep. Hold it as targeted-removal protection when appropriate. An immediate blink returns Hei Bai before an unresolved sweeper, so it will not save him from that sweeper.

+
Essence Flux + Hei Bai, Forest Guardian

a second one-mana instant blink for another Shrine or protection from a targeted spell. Hei Bai is a Spirit, so he also returns with a +1/+1 counter. Blinking resets summoning sickness for his tap ability.

+
Daydream + Hei Bai, Forest Guardian

a one-mana sorcery-speed entry with flashback for later. Use it proactively when you have mana to develop, rather than counting it as instant-speed protection.

+
Displacer Kitten + Hei Bai, Forest Guardian

each noncreature spell can blink Hei Bai before that spell resolves, finding another Shrine. Creature spells do not trigger Kitten, including enchantment creature spells. Kitten can also reset a noncreature Shrine, but Sterling Grove may prevent targeting that enchantment.

+
Thassa, Deep-Dwelling + Hei Bai, Forest Guardian

one free entry at your end step, even when Thassa's devotion is too low for her to be a creature. Once you need a specific enchantment more than a random Shrine, blink Pia or Moon-Blessed Cleric instead.

+
Teleportation Circle + Hei Bai, Forest Guardian

another persistent end-step blink that contributes to enchantment count and can also reset Soul-Guide Lantern. It targets artifacts or creatures, not ordinary noncreature Shrines.

Airbender Ascension

a two-mana enchantment that becomes a repeatable end-step blink after four creature entries. Tokens count toward its quest counters. Its initial airbend exiles a creature and gives its owner permission to cast it for two generic mana; airbending Hei Bai requires paying that recast cost, whereas the later end-step blink returns him immediately.

Starfield Vocalist

doubles Hei Bai's entry, constellation triggers, Avatar Shrine entry triggers and Life's Origin token triggers. It does not double upkeep, first-main-phase or end-step triggers merely because they belong to a Shrine. Warp is an inexpensive burst of value; blinking the warped Vocalist makes it a new object that escapes the original delayed exile.

Flicker of Fate

can blink an enchantment as well as a creature. Reset Northern Air Temple for a larger entry drain or Southern Air Temple for counters across the team when that is stronger than another random Shrine. Grove's shroud prevents targeting your other enchantments, so check legality first.

Get the Shrine count working for you
Go-Shintai of Life's Origin

each nontoken Shrine entry, including its own, makes a nonlegendary Shrine enchantment creature token. Those tokens increase Shrine and enchantment counts and trigger the Avatar Shrines. They are not Spirit tokens, do not increase Hei Bai's legendary-enchantment count, and do not trigger Life's Origin again. Its WUBRG tap ability returns an enchantment from your graveyard directly to the battlefield.

→
Sanctum of All → Go-Shintai of Life's Origin

use the upkeep search to establish the token multiplier when the board is stable. All can search either the library or graveyard, so destroyed Shrines remain available. With six or more Shrines, another Shrine's triggered ability triggers an additional time; All never doubles its own search.

→
Sanctum of All → Northern Air Temple

fetch the drain engine when Shrine entries are about to snowball. If you need immediate resources, search The Spirit Oasis or Sanctum of Fruitful Harvest instead. A Shrine found during upkeep can trigger at the later first main phase; an upkeep Shrine arriving after that upkeep began will wait until the next upkeep for its own upkeep trigger.

Sanctum of All

resolve its search before your existing Honden triggers when you want the larger Shrine count to increase their effects. The six-Shrine check for additional triggers happens when an ability triggers, so finding the sixth Shrine later does not retroactively duplicate triggers already on the stack. Starfield Vocalist and All each add one extra instance to eligible entry triggers; together they produce three instances, not four.

The Spirit Oasis

its entry draws for your current Shrine count, then each later Shrine entry draws another card. Life's Origin tokens are Shrines and qualify. These draws are mandatory; avoid unnecessary Shrine entries when your library is nearly empty.

Sanctum of Calm Waters

offers a large first-main-phase refill followed by one discard. The draw is optional, which matters when Oasis or Seeing Winds has already filled your hand. Discarding an enchantment can set up Replenish or Life's Origin rather than simply losing a card.

Honden of Seeing Winds

recurring upkeep draw scales with every Shrine, including tokens, and is mandatory. This is strong early but can become dangerous with a huge Shrine count; count the library before doubling its trigger with Weaver of Harmony.

Sanctum of Fruitful Harvest

supplies Shrine-count mana at your first main phase. Each trigger produces one chosen color, not a mix; if doubled, choose separately for each resolution. Spend this mana in that phase unless another effect says otherwise. It does not by itself supply WUBRG.

Kyoshi Island Plaza

turns its entry and later Shrine entries into basic lands entering tapped. The list has six basics total, so this is a finite burst of ramp rather than an endless source. Use it to supply missing colors before additional Shrines create more search triggers; once the basics are gone, it still counts as a Shrine.

Weaver of Harmony

for G and a tap, copies an activated or triggered ability from an enchantment source. Copy All's search, a drain trigger or a useful entry trigger. It cannot copy Hei Bai's ability because Hei Bai is not an enchantment, and it cannot copy Sanctum Weaver's mana ability. Its anthem improves your enchantment creatures, including Shrine tokens.

Finish the table
+
Northern Air Temple + Go-Shintai of Life's Origin

Temple drains each opponent for your Shrine count on its own entry, then for one on each later Shrine entry. Life's Origin adds token Shrine entries to each nontoken Shrine, accelerating the drain. Order triggers thoughtfully when the current Shrine count matters.

Sanctum of Stone Fangs

drains each opponent at the start of your first main phase for your Shrine count, while gaining you that amount once. Sanctum of All can double this trigger. Protecting this engine through a turn cycle is often more valuable than spending five mana to make extra Spirits.

Honden of Infinite Rage

deals Shrine-count damage to any target on upkeep. Kill an important creature, remove a planeswalker or finish a low-life opponent. It targets only one recipient per trigger, unlike Stone Fangs and Northern Air Temple.

Purphoros, God of the Forge

each other creature entering deals two damage to every opponent, including Spirit, Monk and Shrine tokens. Hei Bai's activation can therefore become a direct finisher. Purphoros works while devotion leaves him a noncreature enchantment; indestructible does not protect him from exile.

+
Crescent Island Temple + Purphoros, God of the Forge

Temple's entry creates a Monk for every Shrine, and each later Shrine entry makes another Monk. Each individual creature entry triggers Purphoros, turning a large Temple entry into substantial table-wide damage. The Monks have prowess but are not Shrines or enchantments.

Southern Air Temple

places Shrine-count counters on every creature when it enters, then adds a counter across your creatures for each later Shrine entry. Build the army first, then resolve the large entry when possible. Blinking your creatures removes their counters, so move to attacking once their accumulated size matters more than another entry.

Go-Shintai of Shared Purpose

at end step, pay one for a Spirit per Shrine. Each extra trigger from Sanctum of All requires a separate payment. These Spirits have ordinary blocking rules, unlike the restricted Spirits made by Hei Bai.

Honden of Life's Web

makes a Shrine-count Spirit army every upkeep without an activation payment. Those tokens support Purphoros, Aura Shards and Southern Air Temple, and can defend you while the drain engines work.

+
Hei Bai, Forest Guardian + Southern Air Temple

create Hei Bai's Spirits on the preceding opponent's end step, then use Shrine entries to grow them before attacking. They cannot block or be blocked by non-Spirit creatures, so they are strong attackers but cannot defend you against most creatures. Keep real blockers instead of assuming every token army protects you.

Destiny Spinner

makes your creature and enchantment spells uncounterable, though opponents may still answer abilities or remove permanents. Its four-mana activation turns a land into an enchantment-count attacker with haste and trample, providing a mana sink when Shrine drain is not enough. Prefer an untapped expendable land; animating a crucial land exposes it to creature removal.

Draw, tutor and develop
+
Eidolon of Blossoms + Setessan Champion

draw from enchantments entering, including Shrines cheated out by Hei Bai and token Shrines from Life's Origin. Champion also grows. These draw triggers are mandatory, so a mass return with Replenish needs a library-size check.

Sythis, Harvest's Hand

draws and gains life when you cast an enchantment spell. Cheating a Shrine onto the battlefield or creating a Shrine token does not trigger Sythis. She is an efficient cast engine and one more legendary enchantment for Hei Bai's token count.

+
Rhystic Study + Esper Sentinel

independent draw prevents the deck from relying entirely on the commander. Southern Air Temple's counters increase Sentinel's power and therefore the tax opponents must pay to deny its draw.

→
Pia, Aether Ascetic → Sanctum of All

discard a spare card on Pia's entry to put the best enchantment directly into your hand. Repeated blinks make her a reusable tutor, but each successful search requires another discard. Discard a recoverable enchantment when that advances your rebuilding plan.

→
Moon-Blessed Cleric → Sanctum of All

puts the Shrine on top of your library. Resolve a Hei Bai entry afterward, without an intervening shuffle, to reveal that exact Shrine and put it onto the battlefield. Fetchlands and other tutors can shuffle away the setup; a draw can move the Shrine to your hand instead.

→
Idyllic Tutor → Aura Shards

find the removal engine when opposing artifacts or enchantments are the main obstacle. If development matters more, find Sanctum of All; against counterspells, find Destiny Spinner. This tutor puts the enchantment in hand, so budget its casting cost too.

→
Eladamri's Call → Go-Shintai of Life's Origin

finds the Shrine-token multiplier because it is also a creature. Other useful targets are Pia for access to any enchantment, Displacer Kitten for repeated blinks, or Destiny Spinner before fighting counterspells.

→
Sterling Grove → Sanctum of All

sacrifice Grove and pay one to put All on top, then draw it or use Hei Bai's entry. While Grove remains, your other enchantments have shroud. That stops your own Flicker of Fate and Kitten from targeting those permanents. Weaver of Harmony can still target their abilities on the stack, because the abilities themselves do not have shroud. Grove does not give itself shroud.

+
Sanctum Weaver + Serra's Sanctum

both scale with your enchantments, including nonlegendary Shrine tokens. Weaver produces a single chosen color per activation; Sanctum produces only white. Neither makes a full spread of five colors on its own, and Weaver cannot tap immediately without haste.

++
Birds of Paradise + Delighted Halfling + Bloom Tender

early creatures accelerate Hei Bai and help assemble colored mana. Halfling's colored mana only casts legendary spells and cannot pay Hei Bai's activation. Bloom Tender counts colors among your permanents; Hei Bai alone is green, not all five colors, despite his color identity.

++
Nature's Lore + Three Visits + Farseek

fetch typed dual lands to cover missing colors. Lore and Visits can find Tropical Island, Savannah, Bayou or Taiga untapped; Farseek puts its find onto the battlefield tapped. The first two search Forest cards, not only basic Forests.

+
Wild Growth + Utopia Sprawl

cheap enchantment ramp also contributes to constellation and enchantment count. Sprawl requires a Forest and makes its chosen color. Prefer stable Forest-typed lands so the Aura is not dependent on Dryad remaining in play.

Dryad of the Ilysian Grove

gives every land all basic land types, fixing WUBRG and letting you deploy an extra land each turn. The lands are still nonbasic unless they actually have the basic supertype, so Kyoshi Island Plaza cannot search for them just because Dryad is active.

+
Sol Ring + Arcane Signet

Ring pays generic setup costs while Signet supplies a missing color. Avoid keeping a hand rich in colorless mana but unable to cast green development spells.

Preserve the engine and rebuild
Aura Shards

each creature entry, including every token, can destroy an artifact or enchantment. Choose only relevant opposing pieces; the effect is optional. Blink Hei Bai for both a new Shrine and a removal trigger, and use mass token entries to remove several obstacles at once.

Go-Shintai of Hidden Cruelty

a repeatable end-step creature answer whose toughness threshold scales with Shrine count. Each paid trigger requires one mana. It destroys rather than exiles, so it cannot remove an indestructible creature by itself.

Touch the Spirit Realm

cast it to exile an artifact or creature until the enchantment leaves, adding to your enchantment count. Channel for a delayed blink that can save Hei Bai from a wipe and return him at the next end step. If delayed exile sends your commander to exile, leave him there rather than moving him to the command zone if you want the return to work.

++
Swords to Plowshares + Anguished Unmaking + Generous Gift

efficient answers to a creature, a nonland permanent, or any permanent respectively. Preserve them for effects that stop entries, enchantment abilities or your finish, rather than trading with every ordinary attacker at a four-player table.

Austere Command

choose the pair of modes that leaves the most of your engine intact. Destroying creatures of both size bands preserves noncreature Shrines but kills your enchantment creatures too. Avoid the enchantment mode unless resetting your own Shrine board is necessary to survive.

+
Heroic Intervention + Clever Concealment

Intervention grants hexproof and indestructible without targeting, so it works through Grove. Concealment phases out targeted nonland permanents and handles exile sweepers, but it cannot target enchantments protected by Grove's shroud. Sacrifice Grove before casting it if you must protect those enchantments. Colorless tokens can convoke generic costs, not the two white symbols.

++
Swan Song + An Offer You Can't Refuse + Arcane Denial

protect the engine with cheap interaction while retaining mana to develop. Song has restricted spell types, Offer gives the opponent Treasures and Denial lets them draw; spend them where that trade is justified.

Deflecting Swat

free while you control Hei Bai and able to redirect a targeted spell or ability. It does not counter an untargeted sweeper or make an illegal new target legal.

Soul-Guide Lantern

provides graveyard interaction without exiling your own recovery resources. Blink it for another targeted graveyard exile, or sacrifice it to clear every opponent's graveyard at the critical moment.

Replenish

restores all your enchantments from the graveyard at once after destruction or discard. Returning Shrines see each other enter for their relevant triggers, as do constellation engines, so count draw, drain and token triggers carefully. It cannot recover exiled cards.

Sevinne's Reclamation

recovers low-cost engines such as Northern Air Temple, Sanctum of Stone Fangs, Sterling Grove or Sanctum Weaver, and can also return a land. Flashback copies the spell for a second recovery. It cannot return the four- and five-mana Shrines.

→
Hall of Heliod's Generosity → Northern Air Temple

put a destroyed Shrine on top at the preceding end step, then draw it or let Hei Bai find it. The same plan recovers another enchantment, though Hei Bai only finds one with the Shrine subtype. Budget 1W plus the Hall's tap and preserve the top card from intervening shuffles.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep two to three lands with green access, early acceleration, and either an independent draw engine or a blink plan for Hei Bai. A hand containing several expensive Shrines but no development is too slow. The mana base has ten fetchlands, twelve typed duals and six basics; the basics are deliberate fuel for Kyoshi Island Plaza. Serra's Sanctum and Hall of Heliod's Generosity are useful utility lands but weak early color fixers.

Develop on turns 1-2, land Hei Bai around turns 3-4 when supported, then prioritize a repeating blink or the Shrine-token multiplier. Spend mana making more Spirits only when the Shrine and draw engines are established, Purphoros converts them into an immediate threat, or you need attackers for the next turn.

When several triggers happen together, choose an order that serves the turn: an All search can raise Shrine count before existing Honden triggers resolve; token creation can supply creatures before a Southern Air Temple entry puts counters on the team. Newly created creatures do not receive counters from an ability that already resolved. Extra triggered abilities must have met their duplication condition when they triggered.

Never confuse legendary enchantments, all enchantments, Shrines and Spirits when counting. Hei Bai's activation, Sanctum Weaver, the Shrine engines and the restricted Spirit blocking ability each care about a different group. Shrine is an enchantment subtype, not a creature type.

This list has no intentional infinite loop or compact two-card win. Its strong draws build a threatening engine around turns 6-8 and finish with layered drain, Purphoros triggers or enlarged tokens; disruption can push the win later. Keep protection available, avoid overextending into exile, and choose recovery over another expensive threat when the table is preparing a reset. This places the finished list in tier A rather than the compact-combo S tier.
