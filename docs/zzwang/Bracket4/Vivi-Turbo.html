Vivi-Turbo is a separate Bracket 4 upgrade of Vivi-Spellslinger. The Bracket 3 original stays intact. Build power before using Vivi's once-per-turn mana burst. The protected finishes are a real mana loop into Ballista, or a fueled Breach spell chain; Curiosity is outstanding draw but does not create an infinite combo by itself.

Make the commander repay the setup
Vivi Ornitier

Every noncreature spell cast adds a +1/+1 counter and deals one to each opponent. Copies are not casts. Activate the zero-cost mana ability only on your turn and only once for that particular ability; untapping Vivi does not reset it.

+++
Ponder + Preordain + Brainstorm + Consider

Cheap noncreature casts grow Vivi before the mana burst. Brainstorm needs a later shuffle to clear unwanted cards, and Consider can place a recursion target in the graveyard.

+
Gitaxian Probe + Mishra's Bauble

Low-cost setup that grows Vivi. Bauble draws at the next upkeep, not immediately, so it cannot draw a just-tutored top card during the same turn.

Wild Ride

On Vivi, one red adds the cast counter and another three power until end of turn. Use it BEFORE the mana activation to increase the burst. Its haste is incidental because Vivi's mana ability does not tap.

Harmonic Prodigy

Doubles Vivi's triggered ability, so each noncreature cast grows it twice and deals two to every opponent. It does not double mana activations or turn copies into casts.

Curiosity

Damage from Vivi to three opponents can give three optional draws per triggered damage ability. Those draws are not spell casts and do not trigger Vivi again. Curiosity is not attached yet when Vivi's trigger from CASTING that Aura resolves.

+
Mystic Remora + Rhystic Study

Start a draw engine before dumping the hand into mana. Remora's upkeep grows and both engines depend on opponents declining to pay the relevant cost.

+
Wheel of Fortune + Timetwister

Refills that also refill opponents. Wheel discards into the graveyard for Breach; Timetwister shuffles graveyards back and can undo a mill plan. Do not treat the two as interchangeable once the kill is underway.

Two mana-loop configurations, with a real outlet
+
Vivi Ornitier + Quicksilver Elemental

During your turn, pay blue for Elemental to gain Vivi's activated abilities. Its copied zero ability uses Elemental's OWN power, normally three, and makes three blue/red mana. Pay blue to gain a fresh instance from Vivi again, then use that fresh instance. Each pass nets mana while Elemental's power is at least two. It does not require tapping or haste.

++
Agatha's Soul Cauldron + Quicksilver Elemental + Vivi Ornitier

Exile Elemental from the graveyard with Cauldron and put its counter on Vivi. A Vivi with a counter gains Elemental's activated copy ability. Pay blue targeting Vivi itself to gain a fresh zero ability. Here the new mana ability uses VIVI'S power, so Vivi needs at least two power for a positive loop. The normal once-per-turn restriction still applies separately to every instance; no plain untap trick bypasses it.

Walking Ballista

Turn the mana bank into an actual win by casting for enough X, then distribute counters as damage across all opponents. Its casting cost is twice X. The mana loop itself causes no spell-cast triggers and is not automatically lethal without this outlet or another funded finish.

→
Imperial Recruiter → Walking Ballista

Ballista's power is zero in the library, so Imperial finds it to HAND. Imperial cannot find the three-power Quicksilver Elemental. Do not claim the same tutor reaches both halves.

→
Gamble → Quicksilver Elemental

Unrestricted hand access to the missing piece, with a real random-discard risk. A discarded Elemental can become a Cauldron target, while Breach can recast a discarded artifact or other missing piece if enough fuel remains.

Breach, fuel and alternative kills
++
Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze

Establish Breach before sacrificing Diamond, since Diamond discards your hand. Escape and resolve Diamond, sacrifice it for three blue, then escape Freeze for one-blue-plus-one. Aim enough storm copies at yourself to replace the six other cards exiled by those two escapes, then aim surplus copies at opponents. Repeat only while the graveyard and library can actually fund it.

Brain Freeze

Milling a player out does not make them lose immediately. They lose when they next try to draw from an empty library, unless another damage finish ends the game first. Keep enough of your own library for mandatory draws and recovery; this is not unlimited fuel without a replenishment plan.

→++
Intuition → Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze + Walking Ballista

With Breach already available, put the two unwanted cards in the graveyard and use the chosen card from hand. Without Breach or another recovery plan, Intuition is NOT a guaranteed one-card assembly: opponents choose what you receive. Count at least the escape costs before pitching the rest of the hand.

Past in Flames

Recovers rituals, cantrips and tutors for another attempt. Flashback exiles each spell and does not let you flash back Ballista or Breach. Its four-mana cost is a recovery expenditure, not an opening hand's only engine.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication

Cast Duplication targeting a permanent you already control, then flash Dualcaster in before it resolves. Each new hasty Dualcaster copies the ORIGINAL Duplication still on the stack, targeting Dualcaster. The token army can attack if combat remains, but copying spells is not casting them and does not trigger Vivi's burn. Do not claim a same-turn noncombat kill from this pair alone.

+
Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty + Storm-Kiln Artist

Birgi refunds red per original cast; Artist also makes Treasures for instant/sorcery copies. They keep a finite spell chain funded, but drawing cards and producing mana alone do not bypass the need to cast a winning spell.

Find and protect the critical window
→
Mystical Tutor → Intuition

Top-deck access to a setup spell, protection or a ritual. Use it after fetches and other shuffling searches, then arrange a real draw this turn if the window will not survive until your next draw step.

→
Personal Tutor → Gamble

Finds a sorcery on top. It can also find a draw/filter spell or a wheel, but cannot directly find an instant, Ballista or Breach.

→
Merchant Scroll → Brain Freeze

Direct-to-hand access to a BLUE instant, including Intuition or a blue counterspell. It cannot find the red Breach enchantment or any arbitrary instant of another color.

→
Spellseeker → Mystical Tutor

Finds a qualifying instant or sorcery to hand, including Freeze, Gamble or protection. It can bridge into another tutor but does not directly find the creature or enchantment combo pieces.

→
Muddle the Mixture → Underworld Breach

Transmute finds mana value two, including Breach, Cauldron or Curiosity's doubled-trigger partner Prodigy. It does not find the zero-mana Ballista or the five-mana Elemental.

→
Dizzy Spell → Curiosity

Transmute at sorcery speed for a mana-value-one spell such as Curiosity, Gamble, Mystical Tutor or a cantrip. Its normal shrink mode is not the reason it is here.

+++
Chrome Mox + Mox Diamond + Mox Opal + Mox Amber

The fast-mana upgrade has real costs. Amber needs a legendary permanent, Opal needs three artifacts, Chrome consumes a colored nonartifact card, and Diamond consumes a land. Do not spend the only blue pitch card casually.

++
Ancient Tomb + Mana Vault + Sol Ring

Burst generic mana for the commander, Elemental, tutors and a large Ballista. Preserve red and blue sources; colorless alone cannot launch Vivi or Breach.

Jeska's Will

With Vivi present, both modes can create the mana and extra cards needed for the final turn. Count the targeted opponent's hand rather than assuming seven mana.

Hexing Squelcher

Makes all your spells uncounterable, including Breach and Ballista. It does not stop removal or activated/triggered interaction, and creature ward costs can still be paid.

Samut, Tyrant of Naktamun

Gives instant and sorcery spells split second. While one is on the stack opponents cannot cast responses or activate nonmana abilities, but triggers still happen. It does not protect your enchantment or creature spells in the same way, and players can act between spells.

++
Force of Will + Fierce Guardianship + Pact of Negation

Preserve mana for the actual combo. Guardianship needs a commander; Force needs a blue pitch card. Pact's next-upkeep payment matters after a failed attempt.

++
Force of Negation + Flusterstorm + Swan Song

Negation is free only on other players' turns. These are selective answers rather than universal protection against creatures or abilities. Keep removal for resolved hate.

++
Deflecting Swat + Redirect Lightning + Chain of Vapor

Redirect targeted interaction or bounce the hate piece before committing. Swat cannot stop a sweeper. Chain's controller may sacrifice a land to copy it, potentially returning one of your permanents too.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep functional blue-red sources, acceleration that really works, and a cheap draw plan. A hand with only colorless rocks and a five-mana Elemental is a mulligan. With Vivi active, cast growth and draw spells before using the mana ability unless an immediate protection payment requires it first.

Secure a protection window, assemble the mana engine or Breach with sufficient fuel, and keep the outlet available. Tutor and fetch before placing a card on top. Against graveyard hate, the Elemental/Ballista route avoids Breach; against creature-activation hate it needs the hate removed first. This list targets protected turn-four-or-later Bracket 4 opportunities, with no measured win-rate claim; the original Bracket 3 version retains its own table expectations.
