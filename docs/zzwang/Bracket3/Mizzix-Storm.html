Mizzix reduces only GENERIC costs, one per experience counter, and never the coloured pips. At four experience Reiterate with buyback costs RR and Crackle with Power costs RR plus whatever X exceeds the discount. Count your coloured mana, not your total, before you start a chain.

A Goblin storm deck by zzwang. Bracket 3: the three Game Changers are Lion's Eye Diamond, Mystical Tutor and Underworld Breach. No mass land denial.

The commander
Mizzix of the Izmagnus

casting an instant or sorcery with mana value greater than your experience gives another counter, and each counter makes them one cheaper. Climb it with a 1, then 2, then 3, then 4-drop; after that most of the deck costs only its pips.

Goblin Electromancer

another one-generic discount for the turns Mizzix is not out, and a second Goblin for the deck.

Big Score

costs R at three experience: two cards and two Treasures for one red.

The Reiterate kills
+
Mizzix of the Izmagnus + Reiterate

the core engine. At four experience, Reiterate with buyback costs RR and comes back to hand every time.

+
Reiterate + Desperate Ritual

cast the ritual, hold it on the stack, buy back Reiterate for RR copying it: RRR back, net +R and +1 storm each lap. Stop, resolve the original, then cast the finisher.

+
Reiterate + Pyretic Ritual

the same loop with the second ritual.

+
Reiterate + Seething Song

net +3 red and +1 storm per lap.

+
Reiterate + Mana Geyser

three tapped opposing lands make it mana-positive at four experience. Without Mizzix, six tapped lands sustain it and seven profit, but you need eleven mana to start.

+
Reiterate + Turnabout

copy Turnabout to untap your lands each lap. Three lands are enough once the first UU and RR are paid.

Crackle with Power

the finish: at X=8 it deals 40 to each opponent. With experience its 24 generic shrinks, the RR does not.

Lightning Bolt

the backup finish: buy back Reiterate on Bolt as many times as you have mana.

Archmage Emeritus

draws on every cast and copy and the draw is mandatory. Before an unbounded Reiterate loop, bounce it with Snap or Into the Roil, or you deck yourself.

The Breach kill
++
Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Brain Freeze

start with Breach out, an empty hand, and LED, Freeze and six other cards in the graveyard. Escape LED for free, crack it for UUU, escape Freeze for 1U: each lap is two casts, a floating U and six cards exiled. Aim the early Freezes at yourself to fill the graveyard, but keep seven cards in your library. Once storm is high enough, aim every copy at the opponents until their libraries are empty, crack the last LED for red and escape Wheel of Fortune: they draw from empty libraries and lose. Mizzix is not needed. Crackle is not the finish here: X=8 costs 26 mana, far more than the laps make.

Lion's Eye Diamond

also a free ritual on the Reiterate turn: crack it with the chain spell on the stack after your hand is empty.

Brain Freeze

on its own, a mill finish once storm is high: three cards per copy.

Stroke of Genius

with unlimited mana, aim it at an opponent and Reiterate copies it for the others.

Past in Flames

flashback all the rituals and cantrips for a second lap; also cheaper with experience.

Mizzix's Mastery

overloaded at high experience: cast every instant and sorcery in your graveyard for free.

Finding the pieces
Mystical Tutor

Reiterate, Brain Freeze or the missing ritual. It cannot find Underworld Breach, an enchantment: Muddle the Mixture does that.

Personal Tutor

a sorcery to the top: Mana Geyser, Past in Flames, Mizzix's Mastery or Crackle with Power.

Firemind's Foresight

Reiterate, Desperate Ritual and Lightning Bolt in one card: a whole kill.

Muddle the Mixture

transmute for Underworld Breach or Brain Freeze; the transmute cost is not discounted.

Spellseeker

finds Brain Freeze, Desperate Ritual or Mystical Tutor.

Merchant Scroll

finds a blue instant: Mystical Tutor (which then finds Reiterate), Muddle the Mixture or a counterspell for the kill turn.

Solve the Equation

any instant or sorcery: Reiterate, Turnabout or Crackle with Power.

Tezzeret's Gambit

draw two and proliferate experience.

Card flow
Frantic Search

free after a discount; untaps three lands.

Snap

untaps two lands and returns Archmage Emeritus before a loop.

High Tide

doubles Islands for the chain turn, and Turnabout or Frantic Search untaps them for more.

Windfall

a full refill for the chain turn, and cheap once Mizzix has a few counters.

Wheel of Fortune

the second wheel.

Dig Through Time

delve makes it two blue late.

Treasure Cruise

the same.

Mystic Remora

early draw; let it go once the chain starts.

Storm-Kiln Artist

a Treasure per instant or sorcery cast or copied: Reiterate loops make Treasures too.

Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

a red for every spell, which pays the RR on Reiterate.

Protection
Hexing Squelcher

makes your spells uncounterable for the chain turn.

Pact of Negation

free on the turn you win.

Force of Negation

free on an opponent's turn.

Mana Drain

counter, and next turn's mana funds the chain.

Flusterstorm

wins counter wars during the chain.

Swan Song

one blue for a counterspell or a sweeper.

Into the Flood Maw

one blue to bounce a Rule of Law or Damping Sphere that stops the engine.

Deflecting Swat

free with Mizzix out.

Opening hand and sequencing

Keep lands of both colours, a cantrip and one ramp piece. Mizzix on turn 3 or 4, then climb experience with a 2-, 3- and 4-drop instant or sorcery on successive turns.

The Reiterate and Breach loops are played from turn 6 by table agreement. Before then, build experience, sculpt the hand and hold protection.

On the kill turn: Squelcher or a free counter up first, rituals into Reiterate, Archmage Emeritus out of the way, then Crackle with Power.

Sol Ring

early speed into Mizzix.

Lotus Petal

the last coloured mana on the chain turn.

Simian Spirit Guide

a free red for Reiterate.
