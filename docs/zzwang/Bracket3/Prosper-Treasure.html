Prosper gives you a second card every turn and a Treasure every time you use one. That is the whole engine, and it compounds: the deck is packed with other impulse effects, and every single card played off exile makes another Treasure. Then the Treasures stop being mana and start being a win condition — ten of them under Revel in Riches, or ten sacrificed to Bolas's Citadel.

The commander
Prosper, Tome-Bound

exiles a card at your end step that you may play until the end of your NEXT turn — two turns of access, not one — and every card you play from exile makes a Treasure. Deathtouch on a 1/4 also means he blocks anything.

Watch the expiry. Cards exiled by Prosper vanish at the end of your next turn. Plan the mana so the expensive ones get cast; a wasted impulse is a wasted card and a missed Treasure.

Lightning Greaves

haste and shroud, because Prosper is the deck.

More impulse, more Treasures

Every one of these makes a Treasure per card played, because Prosper does not care whose effect exiled it.

Reckless Impulse

two cards for two mana, and two Treasures when you play them.

Jeska's Will

with your commander out you take both halves — a huge mana burst AND three impulse cards.

Outpost Siege

a card every upkeep, forever.

Chandra, Torch of Defiance

an impulse card or two damage to each opponent, plus a ritual on the other plus.

Etali, Primal Storm

a free spell off every player's library on every attack — and every one of them is a Treasure.

Professional Face-Breaker

Treasures on combat damage, and it converts Treasures back into impulse draws.

Ob Nixilis, Captive Kingpin

whenever opponents each lose EXACTLY one life, he grows and exiles a card you may play. Reckless Fireweaver and Mirkwood Bats both deal exactly one, so he fires constantly.

Bolas's Citadel

plays off the top of your LIBRARY rather than from exile, so it makes no Treasures — but it casts your whole deck for life, and then sacrifices ten Treasures to deal ten to each opponent.

The Treasure multiplier
Academy Manufactor

every Treasure you would make becomes a Clue, a Food AND a Treasure. Three artifacts instead of one, on every single Prosper trigger.

Goldspan Dragon

your Treasures tap for two mana each, and it makes more on attack and whenever anyone targets it.

Pitiless Plunderer

a Treasure for every creature that dies.

Grim Hireling

two per combat damage step, and it converts them into removal.

Ruthless Technomancer

turns a creature into Treasures equal to its power, then reanimates with them.

Revel in Riches

Treasures whenever THEIR creatures die, and ten Treasures at your upkeep simply wins the game. With Academy Manufactor, ten is two or three turns away.

Turning artifacts into damage
Reckless Fireweaver

one damage to each opponent for every artifact that enters. With Academy Manufactor that is three per Prosper trigger.

Marionette Apprentice

a drain for every artifact or creature you lose — every Treasure you crack.

Marionette Master

the same, scaled to its power, which fabricate makes four.

Mirkwood Bats

a drain when you CREATE a token and again when you SACRIFICE it. Academy Manufactor turns one Treasure trigger into six points of drain.

Mayhem Devil

a damage every time any player sacrifices anything, yours included.

Crime Novelist

grows and makes red mana on every artifact you sacrifice.

Hellrider

one damage per attacking creature, which closes games the old-fashioned way.

Rakdos Charm

graveyard hate, artifact removal, or a symmetric ping that finishes a token board.

Sacrifice outlets and cards
Deadly Dispute

two cards and a Treasure for a spare artifact, at instant speed. It is the best card in the deck alongside a board of Treasures.

Big Score

two cards and two Treasures.

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

feed it a Treasure or a Clue each end step and they either give up a permanent or you draw and they lose two.

Skullclamp

on a Servo or Faerie, two cards.

Bitterblossom

a free body every turn — Skullclamp fodder, Braids fodder, and a Mirkwood Bats trigger.

Commander's Sphere

mana now, a card later.

Mind Stone

the same.

Necropotence

as many cards as your life total allows.

Phyrexian Arena

a card a turn.

Read the Bones

two cards and a scry.

Sign in Blood

two cards, or two damage to a player at four life.

Night's Whisper

the cheap one.

Removal
Bedevil

artifact, creature or planeswalker, at instant speed.

Dreadbore

a creature or planeswalker for two.

Infernal Grasp

the cleanest black removal spell.

Deadly Rollick

free with your commander out.

Feed the Swarm

the only enchantment answer in Rakdos.

Chaos Warp

the second one, and it handles planeswalkers too.

Damnation

a wrath in a deck whose value lives on artifacts and in exile.

Blasphemous Act

cheap, and your Treasures survive it.

Light Up the Night

scalable reach at any target, with a flashback for a second helping.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep mana and one impulse effect. Prosper on turn four with Reckless Impulse or Outpost Siege behind him starts the Treasure engine immediately; a hand of expensive payoffs does not.

Academy Manufactor is the single biggest upgrade. Every Treasure effect triples, so every drain payoff, every Fireweaver ping, and every Revel in Riches counter triples with it.

Do not spend Treasures freely once Revel in Riches is out. Ten is the number and it wins on your upkeep with no combat, no targeting and nothing they can respond to except artifact removal.

Ob Nixilis, Captive Kingpin

play him before Reckless Fireweaver or Mirkwood Bats, not after. Every batch of exactly-one damage gives you a counter and another impulse card.
