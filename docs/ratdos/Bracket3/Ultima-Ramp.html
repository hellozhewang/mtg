Ultima is not merely an evasive land-hosing attacker; it is a mana doubler in the command zone. Its static ability adds an additional {C} whenever you tap a land for {C}. Because every single land in this pure colorless ninety-nine produces colorless mana, resolving Ultima turns five lands into ten mana, allowing you to cast colossal Eldrazi titans, activate Planar Bridge, or execute infinite mana finishes turns ahead of normal timing. Meanwhile, Ultima's attack trigger permanently neutralizes opponents' most threatening utility lands (Cabal Coffers, Gaea's Cradle, Nykthos) and color-fixing by converting them into colorless-only Wastes.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Ancient Tomb, Mishra's Workshop, and The One Ring. No mass land denial. Infinite mana combos (Basalt Monolith + Forsaken Monument / Rings of Brighthearth into Walking Ballista) wait until turn 6 under house rules.

The engine: Ultima's double mana
Ultima, Origin of Oblivion

a 4/4 flying God that doubles the mana of all your lands whenever tapped for {C}, and attacks to put a blight counter on target land, permanently stripping all types and abilities to make it a colorless mana tap.

Candelabra of Tawnos

pay {X} and tap to untap X target lands. Because Ultima causes each land to produce two mana, untapping your lands generates massive net mana bursts.

Deserted Temple

pays {1} and taps to untap target land, untapping a high-yield land like Ancient Tomb, Eldrazi Temple, or Shrine of the Forsaken Gods for extra mana.

Forsaken Monument

adds an additional {C} whenever you tap ANY permanent for {C}, stacking with Ultima to turn ordinary lands into triple-mana powerhouses while buffing colorless creatures by +2/+2.

Omnath, Locus of the Void

colorless creature whose unspent mana never empties across phases and turns, turning your massive mana surpluses into floating reserves and growing Omnath into a lethal beatdown threat.

Under Bracket 3 rules, infinite mana and Ballista kills must wait until turn 6 or later. Spend turns 1 through 5 establishing your manabase, casting Ultima, and dropping mid-game ramp engines.

Opening hand and sequencing

Look for an opening hand with two to three lands, early mana acceleration (Sol Ring, Mox Opal, Everflowing Chalice, Mind Stone, Expedition Map), and a card draw or mid-game ramp piece (The One Ring, Chimil, Worn Powerstone).

Turn 1-2: Deploy fast mana rocks and utility lands. Use Expedition Map to find Ancient Tomb, Mishra's Workshop, or Urza lands.

Turn 3-4: Cast Ultima, Origin of Oblivion on turn 3 or 4. Once Ultima resolves, your lands immediately produce double mana.

Turn 5: Attack with Ultima to put a blight counter on an opponent's most dangerous land (Cabal Coffers, Cradle, or a triome). Use your doubled mana pool (ten or more mana) to drop an Eldrazi titan (Ulamog, Kozilek, Sire of Seven Deaths) or an engine (Echoes of Eternity, Darksteel Monolith, Portal to Phyrexia).

Turn 6+: Under Bracket 3 rules, tutor or deploy Basalt Monolith + Forsaken Monument/Rings to generate infinite colorless mana into Walking Ballista, or wipe opposing boards with All Is Dust and overwhelm the table with annihilator attacks.
