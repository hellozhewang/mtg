The Doctor's extra card is an offer, not a shield you can assume will hold. Opponents can decline and attack you normally. Build your own draw engines, token army, and interaction so that accepting the offer benefits you and declining it does not leave you helpless.

The Second Doctor and Romana II are both commanders. Doctor's companion makes this a legal Azorius pairing: begin with both in the command zone and shuffle the other 98 cards into your library. Track their commander taxes and commander damage separately. This Bracket 3 build uses Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, and Consecrated Sphinx as its three Game Changers, with no planned infinite combo, mass land denial, or extra-turn chain.

Make the Doctor's offer work for you
The Second Doctor

Your normal draw plus the optional draw at your end step can turn on second-card payoffs every turn. Opponents who take the offered card cannot attack you or your permanents during their next turn, but can still cast spells, activate abilities, and attack other players. The restriction already created persists even if the Doctor leaves. His unlimited hand size applies to everyone, not just you.

Romana II

Copy a token that entered this turn for one mana and a tap, creating your copy tapped. The best targets are a new Homunculus Horde copy or an 8/8 Kraken; Drakes, Illusions, and Treasures also work. Romana has summoning sickness, the original token must still be a legal target on resolution, and she cannot copy an old token merely because it would be useful now.

Tataru Taru

Supplies a card on entry and converts an opponent drawing outside their own turn into a tapped Treasure, once each turn. The Doctor's offer happens on your turn, so an opponent accepting can trigger her. Three opponents accepting still produce at most one Treasure from Tataru that turn.

Gleaming Splendor

Rewards an opponent's second draw in any turn, and its activated ability can give you and a chosen opponent a card. The Doctor giving someone their first card of your turn does not trigger it by itself. Use the activation when it crosses a useful threshold for you, rather than automatically helping an opponent.

Loran of the Third Path

Removes an artifact or enchantment on entry and later gives you and a chosen opponent a card. Her draw ability is especially useful on an opponent's turn to reach your second draw, or after an opponent has drawn once to trigger Faerie Mastermind and Gleaming Splendor.

Smuggler's Share

Checks opponents' draws and land entries at each end step. If it triggers alongside the Doctor on your turn, put Share's trigger on the stack first so the Doctor resolves before Share checks the counts. It needs two draws per opponent, so one Doctor draw alone is insufficient.

Develop mana before playing several four-drops
Sol Ring

Pays the generic portion of either commander's cost and accelerates engines. It cannot provide white or blue for interaction, so keep colored sources rather than assuming Ring fixes an otherwise awkward hand.

Talisman of Progress

An untapped two-mana accelerator that helps deploy a four-mana commander on turn three after three land drops. Arcane Signet and Fellwar Stone provide similar development, although Fellwar Stone's available colors depend on opposing lands.

Azorius Signet

Fixes both colors but requires another mana to activate. Count that input cost when deciding whether you can cast the Doctor and still hold up a response.

Mind Stone

Early acceleration that converts to a card later. Its draw can also cross a second-card threshold at instant speed when an extra Drake, Illusion, or set of Homunculi matters.

Archaeomancer's Map

Finds two of the three basic Plains and helps catch up when opponents have more lands. Its triggered ability can put any land from your hand onto the battlefield, not only a basic Plains, but it does not ramp you when nobody is ahead on lands.

Smothering Tithe

Converts opposing draws into Treasures unless their controllers pay. It helps finance protection, token copies, and large finishing spells. Romana can copy a newly entered Treasure when there is no better token available, but her copy enters tapped and cannot immediately tap for mana.

Minamo, School at Water's Edge

Untaps Romana for another token copy or Shorikai for another activation. Budget the blue mana and Minamo's tap as well as the cost of the ability you want to use again; this is finite value, not an untap loop.

Draw on several turns, not only your own
Rhystic Study

The first independent draw engine and a way to draw during opposing turns without spending more mana. Each extra turn on which you draw a second card is another opportunity for your token payoffs.

Mystic Remora

Provides cheap early cards while opponents develop their noncreature spells. Pay cumulative upkeep only while the expected cards justify delaying your board; keeping it forever is not the objective.

Esper Sentinel

Adds an inexpensive draw threat without giving opponents cards. Its tax scales with its power, so Elspeth's counters or a Mirror Entity activation can make the payment harder during the turns those effects apply.

Consecrated Sphinx

Turns every opposing draw into an optional draw-two for you. One ordinary opposing draw step can enable your second-card payoffs on that player's turn. Opponents may decline the Doctor's offer because of this, but their other draws still feed the Sphinx. Decline draws when your library is running low.

Faerie Mastermind

Rewards an opponent drawing their second card in a turn; it does not trigger on every extra card after that. Its activated ability can start several draw and Treasure triggers, but gives everyone a card, so use it with a clear payoff.

Teferi's Ageless Insight

Doubles your draws except the first draw in each of your draw steps. A Doctor end-step draw becomes two, while a draw-two effect outside the exception becomes four individual draws. It increases both token thresholds and Psychosis Crawler triggers.

Shorikai, Genesis Engine

Draws two, discards one, and creates a Pilot for one mana and a tap. As an uncrewed Vehicle it can activate immediately after entering and survives creature-only wipes. Its activation crosses the second-card threshold on an opponent's turn without depending on opponents casting spells.

Skullclamp

Turns an unboosted Pilot, Soldier, Squid, or Illusion with one toughness into two cards. Illusions can have large power but still one toughness, making this especially good with Minn. Tokens with two or more toughness survive the equipment's reduction, and Elspeth's counters can stop the immediate death.

Mister Fantastic, Reed Richards

Turns each separate token-entry event into an optional card. Romana, Shorikai, and your draw payoffs all supply events, and separate Smothering Tithe triggers can produce separate draws. Multiple tokens entering simultaneously produce only one trigger. This can start another second-card payoff, but does not make those payoffs trigger again after your second draw has already passed.

Brainstorm

Draws three individual cards before putting two back, so it can cross both Alandra thresholds despite not increasing hand size overall. Use a fetchland afterward when you want to shuffle away the returned cards.

Frantic Search

Draws two, discards two, and untaps up to three lands. It supplies draw triggers on another player's turn while restoring mana for interaction, but normally costs a card in net resources.

Fact or Fiction

Finds several useful cards without giving opponents cards. It puts cards into your hand rather than drawing them, so it does not trigger your draw payoffs. The opponent choosing the piles cannot deny you access to every useful card if both piles contain something you need.

Turn the extra cards into a real board
Alandra, Sky Dreamer

Your second card each turn creates a flying Drake; your fifth gives Alandra and your Drakes a power-and-toughness boost based on your hand size when that trigger resolves. The fifth-card trigger during your end step comes too late to enhance that turn's combat. Arrange the fifth draw before combat when it is your finishing plan.

Minn, Wily Illusionist

Makes a scaling Illusion on your second draw each turn. When an Illusion dies, its power determines the maximum mana value of a permanent you may put from hand onto the battlefield. This includes a land, and does not use a normal land play. A token dying while equipped with Skullclamp has the equipment's power bonus when checking its last power.

Homunculus Horde

Every existing Horde makes a copy when you draw your second card that turn. Newly created copies did not see that second draw and do not immediately copy themselves from the same event. Drawing twice on successive players' turns is how the army multiplies; Romana can add another copy of a freshly made token.

Ominous Seas

Stores a counter per draw and converts eight counters into an 8/8 Kraken at instant speed. Romana can copy the Kraken the turn it enters. Creating the tokens during the end step before your turn lets them untap and attack on your next turn; making them in your own main phase does not grant haste.

Chasm Skulker

Grows with your draws and leaves Squids behind when it dies. The death trigger counts its +1/+1 counters rather than its total power. Exile does not create the Squids; islandwalk makes them difficult to block against opponents who control an Island.

Elspeth, Storm Slayer

Doubles your tokens, including Treasures and Romana's copies, while also providing bodies herself. Her zero ability adds counters and gives your existing creatures flying until your next turn, enabling attacks and better blocks. Counters increase toughness and may prevent Skullclamp from killing a small token immediately.

Finish through combat or through the draw engine
Mirror Entity

Converts available mana into an army of large creatures and gives them all creature types. Activate it before Alandra's fifth-card trigger if you want that trigger to boost the whole existing team as Drakes. Minn's Illusions retain their additional power from other Illusions, making this interaction especially dangerous. The ability grants neither haste nor evasion, and creatures entering after it resolves are not included in that activation.

Akroma's Will

With either commander present as you cast it, choose both modes for flying, double strike, vigilance, lifelink, indestructible, and protection from all colors. It turns an established token army into a lethal attack and can protect that army when necessary. It does not let newly created creatures attack without haste.

Psychosis Crawler

Converts every card you draw into one life loss for every opponent. A large draw spell can finish through combat restrictions. Windfall briefly empties your hand during its resolution, but state-based actions are not checked between its discard and draw instructions, so that moment alone does not kill the Crawler.

Windfall

Refills a depleted hand, produces many draw triggers, and can generate substantial Treasures with Smothering Tithe. It also refills all opponents. Cast it when the payoff matters immediately, preferably with Grand Abolisher or Voice of Victory protecting your turn. The Doctor's unlimited hand size can make the largest discarded hand substantial.

Blue Sun's Zenith

Turns accumulated Treasures into cards at instant speed, reaching Alandra's fifth draw before combat or draining the table with Psychosis Crawler. Do not draw more cards than remain in your library; Teferi's Ageless Insight can double the requested draws. There is no infinite-mana engine here, so its size depends on resources you actually accumulated.

Sea Gate Restoration // Sea Gate, Reborn

An early untapped blue land for three life when needed, or a late draw burst that scales with the cards left in your hand as it resolves. It can supply a Crawler finish or dig back to Approach. Playing it as a land spends that copy's spell option unless you later recover it to hand.

Approach of the Second Sun

Resolving the first cast puts it seventh from the top and gains seven life. Seven subsequent draws retrieve it if you do not shuffle or otherwise change the order. The winning cast must be from your hand and must resolve after you have cast another Approach this game. A copied spell is not a second cast. Save protection or a counter for the winning attempt.

One combat example: start your turn with four Drakes that can attack. With Alandra present, draw your fifth card before combat and have ten cards in hand when her trigger resolves. The Drakes become 12/12 for the turn. Akroma's Will gives each double strike, making each worth 24 damage if it connects. Distribute attackers according to actual opposing life totals and blockers; that example is not automatically enough to kill three opponents at 40 life.

Protect the board while keeping opponents' wins in check
Grand Abolisher

Protects your turn from opposing spells and activated abilities of their artifacts, creatures, and enchantments. It does not stop land abilities or triggered abilities. Resolve it before committing to Windfall, an Approach finish, or an important combat.

Voice of Victory

Stops opposing spells during your turn and creates two attacking Warriors when it attacks. Those tokens feed Mister Fantastic and can be clamped during your second main phase before their end-step sacrifice. A copy Romana makes of one of those tokens is not subject to the original mobilize trigger's delayed sacrifice, but enters tapped and cannot join that combat.

Clever Concealment

Phases out selected nonland permanents, protecting the token army and its engines from many exile and destruction wipes. Newly created creatures can convoke it even when they cannot attack. Blue or colorless tokens cannot pay its white mana symbols, so keep white mana or white creatures available.

March of Swirling Mist

Phases out your important creatures for protection or opposing blockers before a finishing attack. Exiling blue cards can reduce its generic cost, which makes the Doctor's large hand useful defensively. It does not protect enchantments or uncrewed Shorikai.

Flawless Maneuver

Free to cast when either commander is on the battlefield and protects your creatures from destruction. It does not stop exile, sacrifice, or lethal toughness reduction, and it does not protect noncreature engines.

Your Temple Is Under Attack

Either protects your creatures from destruction or gives you and one opponent two cards. The draw mode can enable second-card payoffs on an opponent's turn; choose the opponent whose refill is least dangerous. It cannot perform both modes on the same cast.

Selfless Spirit

A visible source of creature protection that needs no mana to activate. Do not expect it to stop exile or toughness reduction; leave a different answer available when those are likely.

Mana Drain

Counters a threat and funds generic costs in your next main phase. Spend the resulting colorless mana on an engine, Mirror Entity, or the generic portion of a large Blue Sun's Zenith; it cannot supply the Zenith's three blue symbols.

Dovin's Veto

An uncounterable answer to a noncreature spell. Counterspell and Arcane Denial cover creature spells too; Swan Song and An Offer You Can't Refuse provide cheaper but narrower protection with resources given to the opponent.

Arcane Denial

Stops a spell and replaces itself at the next upkeep, while offering the spell's controller up to two cards. Those opposing draws can feed Sphinx, Tithe, Tataru, Mastermind, or Gleaming Splendor. The card you draw on an opponent's upkeep can be your first draw toward a second-card payoff that turn.

Force of Negation

Covers a dangerous noncreature spell without available mana on an opponent's turn by exiling another blue card. During your own finishing turn, its alternative cost is unavailable; reserve three mana or another answer.

Winds of Abandon

Overload exiles opposing creatures while leaving your army intact, opening a finishing attack. It gives those opponents basic lands for the creatures exiled, so do not use it for minor value when you cannot capitalize.

Supreme Verdict

Resets a creature board and cannot be countered, but normally kills your own creatures too. Use phasing or indestructibility when preserving your team is worth the additional resources. Uncrewed Shorikai and your enchantment engines remain available afterward.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Interacts with graveyards without shutting down your own Hall of Heliod's Generosity or Mystic Sanctuary. Its mass exile activation costs no extra mana once it is on the battlefield; the draw activation is an alternative use, not an additional effect.

Find and recover the missing function
Muddle the Mixture

Counters an instant or sorcery, or transmutes at sorcery speed for a two-mana card. Find Ominous Seas for a threat engine, Gleaming Splendor for mana and draw access, Grand Abolisher for protection, or Winds of Abandon for removal. Transmute puts the card into your hand, so you must still pay to cast it.

Idyllic Tutor

Finds one of your enchantment engines, including Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Teferi's Ageless Insight, or Ominous Seas. Choose the function you are missing; tutoring another expensive engine while already behind on board can be too slow.

Hall of Heliod's Generosity

Returns a destroyed enchantment to the top of your library, where the Doctor or another draw effect can recover it. It produces only colorless mana normally, and repeatedly using it replaces fresh draws with known cards.

Mystic Sanctuary

Returns an instant or sorcery to the top only when it enters untapped, normally requiring three other Islands. Typed dual lands count as Islands. Fetch it for protection or a finisher after establishing the requirement, and avoid shuffling away an Approach you were about to draw.

Witch Enchanter // Witch-Blessed Meadow

Counts as a potential white land in your opening hand and an artifact-or-enchantment answer later. Loran and Generous Gift provide additional answers, while Swords to Plowshares and Path to Exile efficiently remove individual creatures. These tools keep a single hostile permanent from shutting off the entire draw plan.

What to keep and how to sequence

Keep three functional lands, a two-mana accelerator or cheap draw engine, and either interaction or a token payoff. The deck has 36 lands plus Sea Gate Restoration and Witch Enchanter as two additional land options. The basic Plains support Archaeomancer's Map; basic Islands and typed duals support Mystic Sanctuary. Mulligan hands with several four-to-seven-mana engines but no early development.

An example keep is Flooded Strand, Sea of Clouds, Island, Arcane Signet, Minn, Wily Illusionist, Swords to Plowshares, and Brainstorm. Develop Signet on turn two, then choose Minn with interaction available or a commander according to the board. Do not put both commanders ahead of a useful engine simply because they are available from the command zone.

Prefer the Doctor when you need continued cards or the attack bargain. Prefer Romana when you already have a strong token to copy and can expect her to survive to untap. A turn-three Doctor after a turn-two rock is a development option, not a requirement to tap out into a threatening table.

Track how many cards you have drawn separately on every player's turn. A second draw can create a Drake, an Illusion, and a copy from each existing Homunculus Horde. A third draw does not repeat those second-card triggers. Use instant-speed draw and Shorikai across turns instead of spending every source during your own main phase.

Keep protection for the engine that currently matters most. If you already have a threatening army, resolve Grand Abolisher or preserve a counter before looking for more tokens. If creatures cannot safely attack, work toward Approach or a Crawler draw burst. A large hand is a resource to spend on winning and defending, not a reason to leave opponents drawing indefinitely.
