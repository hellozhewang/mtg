The single most important rule to understand is that Mortarion does not just require life loss—he requires mana to convert that lost life into Astartes tokens on your end step. You cannot simply dump 20 life and expect an army for free; you must pay {X} mana where X is capped by the life you lost this turn. Consequently, the key to piloting Mortarion is pacing: balance early life payment for cards and ramp, cultivate explosive black mana engines like Cabal Coffers, Crypt Ghast, and Phyrexian Altar, and then unleash massive life-payment engines like Bolas's Citadel, Necrodominance, or Wall of Blood to turn your life total directly into a lethal swarm of 2/2 menace Warriors.

The engine: Life loss and Astartes deployment
Mortarion, Daemon Primarch

a 5/6 flying commander that turns life lost this turn into 2/2 black Astartes Warrior creature tokens with menace on your end step for {X} mana

Wall of Blood

pays 1 life at instant speed with zero mana cost as many times as you choose, instantly unlocking any size of X for Mortarion's end step

Unspeakable Symbol

pays 3 life with no mana cost to put +1/+1 counters on Mortarion or your creatures, pumping your board while enabling huge Astartes swarms

Doom Whisperer

surveils 2 for 2 life repeatedly with no mana cost, sculpting your draws and fueling your graveyard while racking up life loss for Mortarion

Necrodominance

draws cards equal to life paid on your end step; paying 5 to 10 life refills your hand and immediately sets the ceiling for Mortarion's token creation

Peer into the Abyss

draws half your deck and loses half your life in a single spell, drawing 35+ cards and letting Mortarion spawn a massive army of menace Astartes

Vilis, Broker of Blood

turns every point of life lost into a fresh card draw, drawing 10 cards whenever you lose 10 life to feed Mortarion

Black Market Connections

chooses all three modes on your upkeep to create a 3/2 creature, create a Treasure, and draw a card for 6 life, guaranteeing 6 Astartes every turn

Blood Celebrant

filters mana for 1 life per activation, providing flexible mana fixing while accumulating life loss on demand

Greed

pays 2 life and {B} to draw cards at instant speed, converting extra black mana into hand advantage and Astartes tokens

Aristocrats drain and token payoffs
Ayara, First of Locthwain

whenever a black creature enters under your control, drains each opponent for 1 and gains 1 life; when Mortarion creates X black Astartes, Ayara immediately drains the table for X and refunds your spent life

Mirkwood Bats

drains each opponent for 1 life whenever you create or sacrifice a token; triggers on every Astartes token created and on every Treasure or token sacrificed

Bloodletter of Aclazotz

doubles all life loss opponents suffer during your turn, turning modest combat swings and aristocrats drain into fatal bursts

Bastion of Remembrance

passive enchantment that drains each opponent for 1 and gains 1 life whenever a creature you control dies

Species Specialist

Braids, Arisen Nightmare

sacrifices a spare Astartes token on end step to force opponents to sacrifice or take 2 damage and draw you up to three cards

Pitiless Plunderer

converts dying Astartes tokens into Treasure tokens, generating mana to fuel Mortarion's {X} cost on future turns

Morbid Opportunist

draws a card on the first creature death each turn cycle

Devotion, lifegain, and life stabilization
Gray Merchant of Asphodel

massive devotion drain and lifegain burst that instantly replenishes 10 to 18 life after paying life for Mortarion

Whip of Erebos

grants lifelink to your entire creature army, letting your 2/2 menace Astartes gain back 10 to 20 life whenever they connect in combat

Aetherflux Reservoir

gains compounding life on every spell cast, providing an ocean of life points to pay for card draw and Mortarion triggers before firing 50-damage laser blasts

The compact finish: Infinite drain loops
+
Exquisite Blood + Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose

whenever an opponent loses life, you gain that much life; whenever you gain life, Vito drains target opponent, creating an instant infinite drain loop

+
Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond

the classic enchantment counterpart; any life gain or loss triggers an unstoppable infinite loop that drains all opponents to zero

++
Bolas's Citadel + Sensei's Divining Top + Aetherflux Reservoir

with Citadel and Reservoir in play, tap Top to draw a card, then cast Top from the top of your library for 1 life; Reservoir gains increasing life on each cast, netting infinite life and letting you activate Reservoir to deal 50 damage to every opponent

Under Bracket 3 rules, these compact two-card combos are reserved for turn 6 or later. Spend turns 1 through 5 developing mana, paying life for cards, and swarming the board with Astartes tokens, then assemble the loop if the table stabilizes.

Opening hand and sequencing

Look for opening hands with three mana sources (lands and rocks like Sol Ring or Jet Medallion) and at least one life-payment or card-advantage engine (Black Market Connections, Necrodominance, or Greed).

Spend turns 1 through 3 deploying mana rocks and establishing utility enchantments. Deploy Mortarion on turn 4 or 5 once you have excess mana to activate his end-step ability.

Begin losing life deliberately through fetchlands, Snuff Out, Dismember, or Wall of Blood, and pump your spare mana into Mortarion's trigger to spawn 4 to 8 menace Astartes.

Use Ayara and Mirkwood Bats to drain opponents as tokens enter, and swing with menace Warriors. On turn 6 or later, execute an Exquisite Blood drain loop or cast Peer into the Abyss to overwhelm the table in a single turn.
