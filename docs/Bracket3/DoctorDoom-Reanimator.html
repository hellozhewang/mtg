Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Doom connives at the beginning of combat, which is already too late to cast a sorcery-speed reanimation spell before that combat's attackers. Set up your graveyard in the first main phase when you want an immediate attack, or use Doom's connive to prepare your second main phase and next turn.

This is a Bracket 3 Villain combat deck with a reanimation package. Develop real creatures, grow the team with Iron Monger, copy your strongest threats, and bring back what the table removes. Doom's land-discard drain is incidental reach. The three Game Changers are Rhystic Study, Fierce Guardianship, and Cyclonic Rift.

Establish the Villain board

Doctor Doom, King of Latveria

Each combat, give one Villain menace and have it connive. Pitch a creature you intend to recover or an excess card; discarding a nonland card adds a counter to the conniving creature. His drain triggers once per discard event containing one or more lands, not once per land.

Baron Strucker, HYDRA Overlord

Discounts Villain spells and supplies one optional connive from another Villain entering each turn. The discount helps actual creature casts, while the enter trigger also works when a Villain is reanimated.

Tombstone, Career Criminal

Another Villain discount, plus recovery to hand on entry. It can retrieve a dead engine even when you lack a reanimation spell, and is a useful creature to copy when rebuilding.

Iron Monger, Sadistic Tycoon

Turns each completed connive into a counter on every Villain you control. Even a connive that discards a land triggers the team counters; it need not put a counter on its own creature to count.

Prowler, Clawed Thief

Connives when another Villain enters, turning creature deployment into selection and team growth through Iron Monger. It also sees Villain tokens, so Doctor Doom and Mysterio can create several triggers. Resolve them carefully without throwing away lands you still need.

Kid Loki

Creatures you have put counters on this turn have hexproof, so Iron Monger can protect your Villain board during a push. The protection expires when the turn ends even though the counters remain; it does not stop untargeted wipes.

Leader, Super-Genius

Adds a card before each connive, turning filtering into net card advantage, and supplies another combat connive itself. It supports the attack plan without needing a separate land-discard engine.

Loki, God of Mischief

Doom's targeted combat ability supplies a reliable draw trigger. Puppet Master and other targeted abilities add opportunities on other turns. It triggers only once each turn, and targeted spells such as Reanimate do not count as abilities.

Red Ghost, Intangible Genius

Your normal draw plus a connive or another draw effect produces a hasty 3/3 Villain. Its unblockable body is a reliable way to connect with Loki's combat-draw ability. Each turn has only one second draw, even if you draw many more cards.

Puppet Master, String Puller

Each attack can stop an opposing creature from blocking and goad it into another player. That opens menace attacks and can later produce Treasures. It need not attack itself to trigger.

Baleful Strix

A cheap card and flying deathtouch blocker that buys time while you build. It is not a Villain, so it does not receive tribal buffs, but is expendable material for Victimize after replacing itself.

Put the right creatures in the graveyard

Faithless Looting

Fixes an opening hand and deposits a reanimation target before combat, when you still have time to return it and attack with haste. It costs a card on its own; the advantage comes from using the discarded creatures.

Frantic Search

Filters two cards into your graveyard and untaps three lands. Use it before combat if you want a sorcery-speed reanimation follow-up, or late in an opponent's turn while leaving mana available.

Entomb

Find the creature that solves the current problem. The Squadron Sinister supplies an airborne haste finish, Doctor Octopus refills a depleted hand, and Doctor Doom supplies bodies. Anger is a useful target when you already have a board and a Mountain.

Buried Alive

Sets up a finite value package rather than an infinite combo. Doctor Octopus, The Squadron Sinister, and Doctor Doom are a strong recovery pile when you already hold reanimation; do not expose all three to known graveyard hate without a follow-up.

Anger

Usually belongs in the graveyard. With a Mountain under your control, including a typed dual, it gives the whole team haste. Living Death returns it to the battlefield, which switches off this graveyard ability; plan another haste source for that line.

Bring them back efficiently

Reanimate

One mana returns a creature from any graveyard, but you lose life equal to its mana value. Return a useful three-drop when that stabilizes the board instead of automatically paying seven life for the largest target.

Animate Dead

Efficient permanent reanimation with a -1/-0 penalty. Destroying or bouncing the Aura makes you sacrifice the returned creature, even if that creature is indestructible.

Necromancy

Normally a lasting three-mana reanimation effect. You can cast it outside sorcery timing for an emergency blocker or enter trigger, but then it is sacrificed at the next cleanup step and takes the creature with it. That temporary mode can return The Squadron Sinister during beginning of combat to grant haste before attacks.

Victimize

Exchange an expendable token or Baleful Strix for two creature cards in your graveyard. You need two targets when casting it, and sacrifice a creature during resolution. Both returned creatures enter tapped, so haste alone does not let them attack that turn.

Living Death

A reset and rebuild after creatures have died or been discarded. It first exiles the creatures already in graveyards, then sacrifices the battlefield, then returns the exiled cards. Creatures sacrificed by this spell do not immediately return, and opponents get their graveyards back too.

Agadeem's Awakening // Agadeem, the Undercrypt

A land early and a selective rebuild late. Returned creatures must have different mana values: Kid Loki at one, Loki, God of Mischief at two, and Iron Monger at three are one compact legal group. Several four-drops cannot all be returned together by one cast.

Chainer, Nightmare Adept

One discard each turn permits one creature cast from your graveyard at its normal or otherwise available cost; it does not cast the creature for free or override timing. Nontoken creatures entering without being cast from your hand gain haste, including reanimated creatures.

Carnage, Crimson Chaos

Returns a creature with mana value three or less, such as Iron Monger, Baron Strucker, or Tombstone. That returned creature must attack when able and is sacrificed if it connects with a player; take the value before losing it. Mayhem is available only during the turn Carnage was discarded and still follows normal timing.

The Soul Stone

Early black mana with a late repeatable recovery mode. Harnessing requires seven mana, tapping it, and exiling one of your creatures; a spare token is a useful payment. Once harnessed it returns a creature only at the beginning of your own upkeep.

Takenuma, Abandoned Mire

Creature recovery from a land slot, often discounted by several legends. Its channel ability can return a creature to hand when direct reanimation is unavailable, but cannot return Living Death or another sorcery.

Make every returned threat matter

Doctor Doom

The six-mana Doom makes two 3/3 Doombot Villains on entry and draws a card at your end step for one life. Those bodies feed Iron Monger, Shared Animosity, and Victimize. An artifact creature, including a Doombot, gives this Doom indestructible but does not protect it from exile or sacrifice.

Doctor Octopus, Master Planner

Pumps your other Villains and refills your hand toward eight at your end step. It is often the best reanimation target when low on resources. Its end-step ability checks whether you have fewer than eight cards, so it does not always draw.

The Squadron Sinister

The main reanimation finisher: its other-Villain bonus grants +2/+2, flying, and haste. Return it before attacks when possible. Discarding it to Doom enables its five-mana mayhem cast later that turn, but a normal sorcery-speed cast will be in the second main phase.

Tri-Sentinel, Act of Vengeance

A large evasive attacker that can deal three damage to one creature per opponent on entry. Copy or reanimate it to repeat that removal. Unearth is a seven-mana fallback that exiles it afterward; ordinary reanimation does not carry that exile condition.

Mysterio, Master of Illusion

Adds a 3/3 Villain for every nontoken Villain you control when its enter trigger resolves, including itself. It rewards rebuilding several creatures together. The tokens are exiled when that Mysterio leaves, so protecting it matters.

Black Cat, Cunning Thief

An enter trigger that steals access to two selected cards from an opponent's top nine. You still pay to cast them and follow timing rules, but can use mana of any type. Reanimation and copies provide additional selections.

Ultron the Annihilator

Supplies a Robot Villain on entry and on each attack. Sacrificed Treasures and artifact creatures can also drain opponents, but this is backup reach rather than the deck's main engine.

Morbius the Living Vampire

Flying and lifelink convert your tribal pumps into meaningful life recovery after Reanimate or Black Market Connections. Use its graveyard exile ability for selection only when recovering its body is less valuable.

Kardur, Doomscourge

Reanimation can buy a multiplayer combat cycle by forcing opponents to attack elsewhere when able. Its attack requirement also affects creatures they deploy after it enters. It does not prevent noncombat wins, and in a duel there is no other opponent for their creatures to attack.

Copy threats and finish through combat

Loki, the Deceiver

Attack to make a temporary nonlegendary copy of another Villain. Copy the six-mana Doctor Doom for permanent Doombots, Tri-Sentinel for damage, or The Squadron Sinister to pump the current attack. The copy enters attacking, so its own attack triggers do not trigger.

A copied Doctor Doom's new Doombots enter after attackers were declared and are not attacking in that combat. A copied Mysterio's tokens disappear when that temporary Mysterio leaves. A copied Doctor Octopus can refill your hand at the end step: put its sacrifice trigger below its draw trigger so the draw resolves first.

Loki, Lord of Misrule

Turn an established army into nonlegendary copies of The Squadron Sinister. Each copy buffs the other Villains, grants flying and haste, and creates an immediate combat threat. This changes existing creatures rather than making them enter again, so it does not create a new set of enter triggers.

Chameleon, Master of Disguise

Copies a creature you control while keeping its own name, so it can coexist with the original legend. Copy an anthem, a card engine, or a creature with a useful enter ability. It remains legendary; copying Chameleon again can still cause a legend-rule conflict.

Spark Double

Creates a nonlegendary copy of an existing creature with an additional counter. It can copy Doom or a tribal payoff without losing the original. It cannot copy a creature entering simultaneously with it from Living Death; after that spell sacrifices the board, it normally has nothing available to copy and dies.

Roaming Throne

Choose Villain to double triggered abilities on your other Villains, including Doom's combat trigger, Iron Monger's team counters, and Loki's attack-copy trigger. It does not double activated abilities or Leader's replacement effect that adds a card before conniving.

Shared Animosity

Makes a broad Villain attack lethal without pumping opponents. Resolve Loki's attack-copy trigger before the original attackers' Animosity triggers to count the new attacking Villain. The token itself was not declared as an attacker, so it gets no Animosity trigger of its own.

Cyclonic Rift

Your third Game Changer is a flexible answer early and an overloaded opening for a finishing attack later. Use the seven-mana mode when you can convert the cleared board into meaningful damage before opponents rebuild.

Keep enough resources and answers

Black Market Connections

Choose the modes you can afford in life: Treasure for mana, a card for resources, or a changeling that counts as a Villain. Its token can be sacrificed to Victimize or exiled to harness The Soul Stone.

Rhystic Study

The dedicated draw Game Changer gives you resources through opponents' turns. It supports normal creature deployment when your graveyard is shut off.

Mystic Remora

Cheap early draw that helps find lands and setup. Let it go when its increasing upkeep cost interferes with developing creatures and holding interaction.

Arcane Signet

Together with Fellwar Stone and the three Talismans, fixes the three colors needed for Doom and your multicolor Villains. Favor sources that leave black available for reanimation and blue for protection.

Patchwork Banner

Choose Villain for mana fixing plus a team bonus. It helps ordinary creature casts and makes the token bodies from your reanimated threats more dangerous.

Fierce Guardianship

Protects a resolved commander, a reanimation spell, or a developed board from noncreature interaction. It is free only when you already control a commander; it does not protect Doom for free while he is still on the stack.

Force of Negation

Free noncreature interaction on opponents' turns at the cost of another blue card. On your own reanimation or attack turn, you need to pay its mana cost.

Mana Drain

An unconditional counter whose colorless refund can fund a large creature or generic reanimation cost on your next main phase. Swan Song and An Offer You Can't Refuse provide cheaper, narrower protection.

Deflecting Swat

Redirect targeted removal or a counterspell aimed at your critical play, often for free with Doom present. It cannot redirect an untargeted wipe.

Withering Torment

Creature or enchantment removal that can clear an obstacle to reanimation. If an opposing permanent prevents the graveyard plan, answer it before putting more creatures into the graveyard.

Chaos Warp

Broad permanent interaction when narrower removal cannot answer the problem. The controller may reveal and put a different permanent onto the battlefield, so it has a real downside.

Deadly Rollick

Exiles a creature without mana while Doom is present. Save it for threats that are difficult to destroy or would benefit too much from dying.

Toxic Deluge

A scalable reset that can clear indestructible creatures, at a life cost and usually at the expense of your own small creatures. Reanimation gives you a recovery plan, but does not refund the life.

Vandalblast

Single-artifact removal early or a one-sided artifact wipe later. Overload preserves your own mana rocks and Robot bodies.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Exile opposing graveyards before Living Death so that your rebuild is more favorable. It leaves your own graveyard intact. Holding it open can matter more than cashing it in for a card.

Rakdos Charm

Instant-speed graveyard exile, artifact destruction, or a way to punish an opposing token army. Its damage mode affects your own creatures' controller too, so count your board and life total before using it.

Otawara, Soaring City

A land or emergency bounce spell-like effect from channel, discounted by your legends. Channel is an ability, and it can also trigger Loki, God of Mischief by targeting a permanent.

What to keep and how to sequence it

Keep three lands covering all three colors, early mana or a draw engine, and a meaningful creature or reanimation setup. A hand of reanimation spells without any target or setup is a mulligan, as is a hand of giant creatures without enough mana.

A strong example is Polluted Delta, Badlands, Island, Arcane Signet, Faithless Looting, Animate Dead, and The Squadron Sinister. You have fixing, acceleration, a way to put the large creature in the graveyard before combat, and a cheap way to return it. Draws determine whether to deploy it for pressure immediately or build a wider board first.

There are 36 dedicated lands plus Agadeem's Awakening as a spell/land. Nine fetchlands find original duals, shocks, the surveil duals, or Xander's Lounge. The three basics provide basic-land search targets; a typed dual with Mountain also turns on Anger. Fetch tapped surveil lands when the tempo cost is manageable, not when you need to hold an answer.

Cavern of Souls normally names Villain, but its colored mana cannot pay for reanimation spells or counters. Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth helps supply black mana for Agadeem's Awakening, while also helping opponents produce black. Mana Confluence, City of Brass, shocks, fetches, and your life-paying engines all add up, so preserve painless mana and look for safe Morbius attacks.

In a normal game, establish two- and three-mana support, then Doom, then the threat or reanimation that fits your hand. Put Iron Monger onto the battlefield before a combat connive when you want the whole Villain board to grow. Move to combat with an untapped Villain that can benefit from menace.

For a same-turn reanimation attack, discard with Faithless Looting or Chainer before combat, or use Entomb, then return your threat while a haste source is available. Doom's combat discard usually sets up the second main phase. Victimize's creatures enter tapped, so plan those as a rebuild rather than immediate attackers.

Keep a recovery card or a creature in reserve once the board already threatens enough damage. Against graveyard hate, cast your creatures normally and use connive for selection; do not automatically discard your only threat. The deck is built to play a fair creature game before the graveyard package becomes available.
