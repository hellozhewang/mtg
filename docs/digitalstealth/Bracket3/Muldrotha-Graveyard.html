The single biggest mistake a new pilot makes with Muldrotha is treating her as an expensive six-mana beater rather than an engine that resets upon re-entering. Muldrotha allows you to play one land and cast one permanent of *each* permanent type from your graveyard during each of your turns. However, if Muldrotha is blinked or recast (via Displacer Kitten, Phantasmal Image, or Kaya's Ghostform), she enters as a new object, immediately refreshing your permissions for every permanent type that turn. Furthermore, Command Beacon bypasses commander tax entirely: you can sacrifice Command Beacon to put Muldrotha into your hand, cast her for her base six mana, and immediately play Command Beacon right back out of your graveyard as your land drop.

The Gravetide avatar and permanent recursion
Muldrotha, the Gravetide

Cast for {3}{B}{G}{U}. Allows you to play a land and cast a permanent of each permanent type (creature, artifact, enchantment, planeswalker, battle) from your graveyard during each of your turns.

Command Beacon

Taps and sacrifices to put Muldrotha into your hand from the command zone, bypassing commander tax completely. Muldrotha then replays Command Beacon from your graveyard every turn.

Secrets of the Dead

Two-mana enchantment that draws a card whenever you cast a spell from your graveyard, turning Muldrotha's recursive plays into an avalanche of card advantage.

River Kelpie

Draws a card whenever a spell or card is cast from a graveyard or enters the battlefield from a graveyard.

Six

Creature that attacks to mill three cards and returns a nonland permanent to your hand, while granting all nonland permanents in your graveyard retrace.

Phantasmal Image

Two-mana clone that copies Muldrotha; legend rule sends the original to the graveyard, allowing you to recast Muldrotha and reset all permanent cast permissions.

Self-mill engines and graveyard tutors
Survival of the Fittest

The premier creature toolbox. Discard any creature for {G} to search for another creature; Muldrotha can immediately cast the discarded creature from your graveyard.

Hermit Druid

Activates for {G} to reveal cards until hitting a basic land, dumping massive chunks of your library directly into your graveyard.

Stitcher's Supplier

Mills three cards on entry and three on death, providing six cards in the graveyard for just one black mana; recastable every turn.

Hedron Crab

Mills three cards on each land drop, rapidly stocking your graveyard off fetchlands and exploration plays.

Mesmeric Orb

Forces each player to mill a card whenever a permanent untaps, filling your graveyard at blazing speed while disrupting opponents.

Ripples of Undeath

Enchantment that mills three cards during each upkeep and allows you to pay 1 life and {1} to put one milled card into your hand.

Altar of Dementia

Sacrifice outlet that mills target player equal to the sacrificed creature's power, fueling your own graveyard or milling opponents out.

Entomb

One-mana instant putting any card from your library directly into your graveyard.

Buried Alive

Three-mana sorcery tutoring up to three creature cards directly into your graveyard.

Unmarked Grave

Two-mana sorcery searching for any nonlegendary card and putting it into your graveyard.

Gravebreaker Lamia

Puts any card from your library into your graveyard on entry, and reduces the mana cost of spells you cast from your graveyard by {1}.

The lockdown suite and recursive removal
Spore Frog

The iconic Muldrotha fog lock. Sacrifice Spore Frog to prevent all combat damage this turn, then recast it from your graveyard on your turn for {G}.

Haywire Mite

Artifact creature that sacrifices for {1} to exile target noncreature artifact or enchantment and gain 2 life; recastable every turn.

Accursed Marauder

Forces each player to sacrifice a nontoken creature on entry; recast each turn to relentlessly grind down opposing boards.

Plaguecrafter

Forces each player to sacrifice a creature or planeswalker, or discard a card; recast every turn.

Glen Elendra Archmage

Negates noncreature spells for {U}; persist brings her back, and Muldrotha recasts her fresh when she dies.

Siren Stormtamer

One-mana flyer that sacrifices to counter any spell or ability targeting you or a permanent you control.

Seal of Primordium

Enchantment that sits on the battlefield and sacrifices at instant speed to destroy an artifact or enchantment; recast each turn.

Seal of Removal

Enchantment that sacrifices at instant speed to bounce any creature; recast each turn to protect Muldrotha or disrupt opponents.

Pernicious Deed

Scalable enchantment sweeper that blows up all artifacts, creatures, and enchantments with mana value X or less; recast each turn.

Oko, Thief of Crowns

Three-mana planeswalker that neutralizes opposing commanders by turning them into vanilla 3/3 Elks; recastable from your graveyard.

Compact loops and infinite finishers
++
Displacer Kitten + Lotus Petal + Muldrotha, the Gravetide

Cast Lotus Petal from your graveyard with Muldrotha. Displacer Kitten triggers, blinking Muldrotha and resetting her permanent-type cast permissions. Lotus Petal enters and is sacrificed for one mana. Repeat infinitely for infinite mana of all colors, then cast every permanent in your graveyard repeatedly to win with Syr Konrad or Marionette Apprentice.

++
Kaya's Ghostform + Phyrexian Altar + Muldrotha, the Gravetide

Enchant Muldrotha with Kaya's Ghostform and sacrifice Muldrotha to Phyrexian Altar for {B}. Ghostform returns Muldrotha to the battlefield as a new object, resetting her cast permissions. Spend the {B} to recast Kaya's Ghostform from your graveyard onto Muldrotha. Repeat infinitely for infinite death and ETB triggers, draining the table with Syr Konrad, Marionette Apprentice, or The Meathook Massacre.

In accordance with Bracket 3 rules, two-card combos are reserved for turn 6 or later. Early turns focus on ramping, filling the graveyard, and establishing recursive control.

Syr Konrad, the Grim

Deals 1 damage to each opponent whenever another creature dies, is milled from a library, or leaves your graveyard, rapidly burning the table.

Marionette Apprentice

Pings each opponent for 1 life whenever an artifact or creature you control dies, converting infinite sacrifice loops into lethal table drain.

The Meathook Massacre

Acts as an asymmetric creature wipe on entry and drains each opponent for 1 life whenever your creatures die in sacrifice loops.

Opening hand and sequencing

Look for opening hands with 2 to 3 lands, cheap mana acceleration (Birds of Paradise, Delighted Halfling, Sol Ring, Sakura-Tribe Elder), and an early self-mill engine or tutor (Stitcher's Supplier, Hedron Crab, Hermit Druid, Entomb, Survival of the Fittest).

On turns 1 through 3, prioritize mana ramp and stocking your graveyard with diverse permanent types.

On turn 4 or 5, cast Muldrotha, the Gravetide. If you have open mana or 0-mana permanents (Lotus Petal, Spore Frog, Kaya's Ghostform), cast them immediately to protect Muldrotha and lock out opposing combat.

Throughout the midgame, loop Spore Frog to nullify enemy combat, use Haywire Mite and Plaguecrafter to grind away opposing boards, and draw cards with Secrets of the Dead.

On turn 6 or later, execute your finish using the Displacer Kitten + Lotus Petal loop, the Kaya's Ghostform + Phyrexian Altar loop, or an Altar of Dementia table mill.
