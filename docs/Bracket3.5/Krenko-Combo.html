Krenko doubles your Goblin count every time he taps, so the deck's only real question is how many times you can untap him. Everything else — the sacrifice outlets, the mana Goblins, the two damage enchantments — exists to convert that pile of 1/1s into a kill that does not need combat. And Goblin Recruiter turns the top of your library into a scripted sequence, which is where most of the skill lives.

The commander and the untaps

Krenko, Mob Boss

{T}: create a token for every Goblin you control. Four Goblins becomes eight, then sixteen. He needs haste or a turn, and then he needs to untap.

Thornbite Staff

equip it to Krenko. Now every Goblin that dies untaps him — so tap for Goblins, sacrifice one to any outlet, untap, tap again. This goes as far as you want.

Umbral Mantle

{3} and untap. With Skirk Prospector converting the new Goblins into red mana, each cycle pays for itself and then some.

Thousand-Year Elixir

haste for the activation and a separate untap.

Marvin, Murderous Mimic

has all activated abilities of your creatures, so Marvin taps for Krenko's ability as well.

Lightning Greaves

haste the turn he lands, and shroud against the removal that is absolutely coming.

Swiftfoot Boots

the version that still lets you equip him yourself.

Arena of Glory

the 21 Mountains help this enter untapped. Pay {R}, tap and exert it to make {R}{R}; spending any of that mana on Krenko, Conspicuous Snoop or Warren Instigator gives that creature haste for the turn. It skips your next untap after exerting, so use the ordinary red-mana ability when haste gains nothing. It does not give haste to Goblins put onto the battlefield by Instigator or Muxus, since you did not cast them with its mana.

Hanweir Battlements

enters untapped and gives one creature haste for {R} and a tap. Use it on a fresh Krenko or Conspicuous Snoop to activate immediately, including after Warren Instigator or Muxus puts that Goblin onto the battlefield. Giving Instigator haste before combat also enables a same-turn attack. The land produces only colourless mana, and you must leave it untapped plus have a separate red source for the haste ability; it cannot help pay Instigator's {R}{R}. Its meld ability has no partner in this deck.

The combos

Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

with Zealous Conscripts, Kiki copies the Conscripts, the copy untaps Kiki, and you repeat. Unlimited hasty 3/3s, and with Impact Tremors or Purphoros out it is unlimited damage without ever attacking.

Zealous Conscripts

the other half. It also steals a blocker or untaps your own Krenko when you just need one big turn.

Conspicuous Snoop

with Kiki-Jiki on top of your library, Snoop gains Kiki's ability, taps to copy ITSELF, and every token Snoop can do it again. Same unlimited result, and Goblin Recruiter sets it up on purpose.

Thornbite Staff

on Krenko with any free sacrifice outlet — Skirk Prospector, Goblin Bombardment, Ashnod's Altar — the Goblin you sacrifice untaps him, so the doubling never stops.

Goblin Sharpshooter

it untaps whenever ANY creature dies, so feeding Goblins to Goblin Bombardment untaps it for another ping each time. On an unlimited Goblin board that is unlimited damage.

Turning Goblins into damage

Impact Tremors

one damage to each opponent per creature entering. This is the cheapest way to convert a doubling into a kill.

Purphoros, God of the Forge

two damage to each opponent per creature entering, on an indestructible body.

Boggart Shenanigans

one damage per Goblin that DIES, which pairs with the sacrifice outlets rather than the token makers.

Pashalik Mons

the same trigger, and it converts a Goblin into two.

Goblin Bombardment

free sacrifice outlet that points at faces.

Siege-Gang Commander

three bodies and a repeatable two-damage cannon.

Massive Raid

damage equal to your creature count, at instant speed.

Goblin Grenade

five damage for one mana and a Goblin.

Hobgoblin Bandit Lord

damage equal to the Goblins that entered this turn — after a Krenko activation that number is enormous.

Shared Animosity

every attacking Goblin gets +1/+0 per other attacking Goblin. On a board of twenty it is simply lethal.

Goblin Chieftain

haste for the team and an anthem, which is what makes a fresh Krenko batch able to attack.

Goblin Warchief

haste plus a discount on every Goblin spell.

Combat Celebrant

exert for an extra combat, so the whole swarm swings twice.

Mana from Goblins

Skirk Prospector

sacrifice a Goblin for {R}. It is the outlet, the mana engine and half the combos.

Ashnod's Altar

two colourless per Goblin, and it is not a Goblin so it survives Goblin-specific hate.

Brightstone Ritual

{R} for every Goblin ON THE BATTLEFIELD, including your opponents'. After one Krenko activation this is ten or more.

Battle Hymn

the same idea counting all your creatures.

Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

red mana on every spell, and it does not empty between phases.

Ruby Medallion

everything costs one less.

Herald's Horn

name Goblin — a discount and a free Goblin off the top most upkeeps.

Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx

your devotion to red is high; this is often five or six mana.

Three Tree City

choose Goblin as it enters. Pay {2} and tap it to make red mana equal to your Goblin creature count, including tokens. Activate Krenko first when possible so the larger swarm pays for Muxus, equipment costs or Umbral Mantle activations without sacrificing Goblins. With eight Goblins it produces eight red mana after you pay the two-mana activation cost. Keep a separate way to pay that cost; its ordinary tap ability makes only colourless mana, so it is not an unconditional early red source.

Mana Vault

three mana on turn one.

Lotus Petal

free acceleration.

Simian Spirit Guide

a red mana from your hand for nothing.

Stacking with Goblin Recruiter

Goblin Recruiter

puts ANY number of Goblins from your library on top, in an order you choose. It is a tutor for your next several turns at once — but only if you have a way to reach the stack. There are three.

Conspicuous Snoop

casts Goblins straight off the top of your library. With Snoop out, a Recruiter stack is effectively your hand. This is the best access tool by a distance.

Goblin Ringleader

reveals the top four and puts every Goblin among them into your hand. Stack exactly four castable Goblins and it is a four-card draw.

Muxus, Goblin Grandee

reveals the top six and puts every Goblin creature with mana value five or less onto the BATTLEFIELD. Stack five cheap Goblins under whatever you draw and Muxus deploys all of them at once.

With Snoop already out, a good stack is Skirk Prospector, then Goblin Warchief, then Krenko, then Muxus — Prospector makes mana from the Goblins you already have, Warchief discounts and hastes the rest, and each one is cast off the top for free card advantage.

Without Snoop, stack for Ringleader: four Goblins you can actually pay for this turn or next, cheapest first.

Going for the combo, stack Kiki-Jiki on top and cast Conspicuous Snoop underneath it.

Tutors and refills

Goblin Matron

any Goblin to hand. Usually Skirk Prospector, Krenko or Muxus.

Imperial Recruiter

any creature with power 2 or less — Goblin Recruiter, Conspicuous Snoop, Skirk Prospector.

Gamble

finds anything, and the random discard is usually survivable. Priority is roughly Thornbite Staff, then Goblin Recruiter, then Kiki-Jiki, then Umbral Mantle — the pieces with no redundancy.

Goblin Lackey

connects once and puts Muxus or Krenko onto the battlefield for free.

Warren Instigator

an unblocked double-strike attack can put two Goblin creatures from your hand onto the battlefield. Put Goblin Matron in with the first damage trigger, tutor Muxus, then put Muxus in with the second. Arena of Glory can give Instigator haste when you cast it; blockers still stop the damage triggers, and it needs Goblins in hand, not the command zone.

Goblin Ringleader

four cards deep in a deck that is mostly Goblins.

Wheel of Fortune

a fresh seven when you have emptied your hand, which this deck does by turn four.

Skullclamp

your tokens are 1/1s, so every clamp is two cards.

Goblin Engineer

tutors an artifact into the graveyard and recurs cheap ones — Thornbite Staff and Skullclamp both qualify.

Interaction

Hexing Squelcher

an uncounterable Goblin that makes all your spells uncounterable while it stays on the battlefield, protecting Krenko and your combo pieces as you cast them. Goblin Matron and Goblin Recruiter can find it, and Ringleader or Muxus can pick it up. It and your other creatures have ward—pay 2 life; opponents can still pay to target them, and it does not protect activated abilities or stop untargeted board wipes.

Deflecting Swat

free with your commander out, and it saves Krenko mid-combo.

Abrade

three damage or an artifact.

Chaos Warp

mono-red's only answer to an enchantment.

Vandalblast

overloaded, a one-sided artifact wrath.

Pyroblast

one mana against the blue deck holding a counterspell.

Red Elemental Blast

the second copy.

Goblin Trashmaster

an anthem that also eats artifacts.

Goblin Chirurgeon

regenerates Krenko through a targeted kill spell for the cost of one token.

Cavern of Souls

name Goblin and Krenko cannot be countered.

What to keep in an opening hand

Keep fast mana and a Goblin. Sol Ring, Mana Vault or Lotus Petal into a turn-two or three Krenko is the start that wins; a hand of five-drops is not.

At 35 lands, keep reliable early mana rather than counting on Warren Instigator connecting. It costs {R}{R}, so check your red sources. A Mountain into Arena of Glory can cast it with haste on turn two, but exerting Arena leaves it tapped through your next untap step; take that line when an open opponent and Goblins in hand make the attack worthwhile.

Set up protection before committing Krenko. Hexing Squelcher makes your spells uncounterable, and Cavern of Souls protects Goblin spells cast with its restricted mana; neither stops an opponent countering Krenko's activated ability. Lightning Greaves or Swiftfoot Boots helps against targeted removal, while Squelcher's ward only makes opponents pay life. You have less hand filtering now, so keep enough mana and a real route to an engine or refill.

Count the sacrifice outlet before you go off. Thornbite Staff on Krenko does nothing without something to kill a Goblin, and Skirk Prospector is both the outlet and the mana.

Goblin Recruiter

cast it with a plan, not on curve. It is the best card in the deck when you know which four or five cards you want and how you are going to reach them, and close to a blank when you do not.
