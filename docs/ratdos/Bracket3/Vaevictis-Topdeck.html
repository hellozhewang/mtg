Vaevictis Asmadi's attack trigger is not a chaotic coin flip. A new pilot assumes you are giving opponents free permanents at random, but this deck rigs the odds completely. You sacrifice cheap tokens, utility elves, or expendable fodder while targeting opponents' commanders, key combo pieces, or essential lands. More importantly, topdeck manipulation ensures you always know and control what you flip: use Sylvan Library, Scroll Rack, Cream of the Crop, or Vampiric Tutor before combat so you cheat an Eldrazi, Dragon, or bomb onto the battlefield every swing, while Lantern of Insight lets you inspect opposing topdecks before choosing targets.

Bracket 3: the Game Changer cap is three, filled by Demonic Tutor, Tergrid, God of Fright, and Vampiric Tutor. No mass land denial. Savage Ventmaw plus Aggravated Assault forms an infinite combat loop that waits until turn 6 under house rules.

The engine: Vaevictis and topdeck manipulation
Vaevictis Asmadi, the Dire

whenever it attacks, each player sacrifices a chosen permanent and reveals their top card, putting it onto the battlefield if it is a permanent. You choose all targets, so destroy their best engines while sacrificing your own fodder.

Lantern of Insight

reveals the top card of every player's library at all times. You know exactly what each opponent will reveal before selecting their sacrificed permanent, and you can tap and sacrifice Lantern to force an opponent to shuffle if they reveal a dangerous bomb.

Scroll Rack

pay {1} and tap to exchange cards in your hand with the top of your library. Tuck high-cost behemoths like It That Betrays, Portal to Phyrexia, or Terror of the Peaks on top right before declaring attackers so Vaevictis cheats them into play.

Sensei's Divining Top

inspects and reorders the top three cards for {1}, guaranteeing you flip a permanent. If needed, tap it to draw a card and put Top on top of your library so Vaevictis immediately puts Top back onto the battlefield.

Sylvan Library

draws two extra cards each upkeep and lets you choose cards from hand to place on top of your library, seamlessly stacking your next Vaevictis flip.

Cream of the Crop

whenever a creature enters, look at the top X cards where X is its power. Vaevictis entering digs six cards deep; flipping an 8/8 or 11/11 digs eleven cards deep, permanently locking in consecutive powerhouse hits.

Mirri's Guile

reorders the top three cards on your upkeep for zero mana.

Hua Tuo, Honored Physician

taps during your turn before attackers are declared to put any dead creature from your graveyard on top of your library, letting Vaevictis resurrect it directly into combat.

Haunted Crossroads

pay {B} to put a creature card from your graveyard on top of your library at instant speed.

Volrath's Stronghold

land that pays {1}{B} and taps to return any creature from graveyard to top of library.

Mortuary Mire

enters tapped and puts target creature card from your graveyard on top of your library.

Stealing and sacrifice payoffs
Tergrid, God of Fright // Tergrid's Lantern

whenever an opponent sacrifices a nontoken permanent, put it onto the battlefield under your control. Every attack with Vaevictis steals up to three opposing permanents while you flip another from your own deck.

It That Betrays

an 11/11 annihilator Eldrazi that steals every nontoken permanent an opponent sacrifices. Flipping this into play alongside Vaevictis's attack immediately takes over the table.

Mayhem Devil

deals 1 damage to any target whenever ANY player sacrifices a permanent. Four permanents sacrificed each attack triggers four pings to burn life totals or clear small creatures.

Mazirek, Kraul Death Priest

puts a +1/+1 counter on all your creatures whenever any player sacrifices a permanent, pumping your entire board by four counters on every swing.

Korvold, Fae-Cursed King

draws a card and gains +1/+1 whenever you sacrifice a permanent to Vaevictis, fetchlands, or Treasures.

Grave Betrayal

steals every opposing creature that dies, returning them to your side with +1/+1 counters at each end step.

Riveteers Ascendancy

whenever you sacrifice a creature, return a creature with lesser mana value from your graveyard to the battlefield.

Infinite combat loop and finishers
+
Aggravated Assault + Savage Ventmaw

Savage Ventmaw attacks and adds {R}{R}{R}{G}{G}{G}; in the postcombat main phase, pay {3}{R}{R} to activate Aggravated Assault, untapping all creatures for infinite combat phases, infinite green mana, and infinite Vaevictis sacrifice triggers

+
Aggravated Assault + Old Gnawbone

connecting with creatures creates massive Treasure pools that fund infinite activations of Aggravated Assault

Wulfgar of Icewind Dale

doubles all attack triggers, causing Vaevictis to force two rounds of sacrifices and two topdeck flips per attack.

Roaming Throne

name Dragon or Elder. Doubles Vaevictis's attack trigger and ward {2} makes it difficult to remove.

Moraug, Fury of Akoum

extra combats on landfall let Vaevictis attack multiple times per turn.

Rhythm of the Wild

makes creature spells uncounterable and gives entering creatures Riot for immediate haste.

Anger

gives all creatures haste from your graveyard while you control a Mountain.

Arena of Glory

land that taps to grant haste to two creatures.

Under Bracket 3 rules, the Savage Ventmaw and Aggravated Assault combo must wait until turn 6 or later. Use turns 1 through 5 to deploy mana dorks, cast Vaevictis, and establish board control.

Opening hand and sequencing

A solid opening hand contains two to three lands, early ramp (Sol Ring, Birds of Paradise, Ignoble Hierarch, Nature's Lore, or Farseek), and a topdeck setup piece (Sensei's Divining Top, Scroll Rack, Sylvan Library, or Cream of the Crop).

Turn 1-2: Establish mana acceleration and color fixing across Black, Red, and Green. Deploy early dorks or topdeck manipulation artifacts.

Turn 3-4: Deploy a haste enabler (Rhythm of the Wild, Arena of Glory, Greaves) or sacrifice engine (Black Market Connections, Awakening Zone). Cast Vaevictis Asmadi on turn 4 or 5 with haste.

Turn 5+: Declare attacks with Vaevictis. Sacrifice an expendable dork or token and target the most threatening permanent each opponent controls. Use Top, Scroll Rack, or Vampiric Tutor before the trigger resolves to flip a massive bomb into play.

Turn 6+: If opponents stabilize or mount a defense, tutor and assemble Savage Ventmaw + Aggravated Assault to take infinite combats, sacrifice all opposing permanents, and close out the table.
