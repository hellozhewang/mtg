A fully optimized Bracket 4 extra combat and combo deck led by Gogo, Mysterious Mime. Gogo bypasses the legend rule by retaining his own name when copying a creature, while granting both himself and the target creature +2/+0 and haste at the beginning of combat. This turns any bomb cast in your precombat main phase into an immediate double threat. With Bracket 4 fully uncapping Game Changers and legalizing mass land denial, the deck pairs full cEDH fast mana and stax locks (Blood Moon, Magus of the Moon, Ruination) with multiple redundant, compact win conditions.

The copy engine and extra combats
Gogo, Mysterious Mime

At beginning of combat, copies any creature you control until end of turn without triggering the legend rule, giving both Gogo and that creature +2/+0 and haste. Cast your biggest threat and swing with two copies immediately.

+
Combat Celebrant + Gogo, Mysterious Mime

Cast Celebrant; at combat, Gogo copies Celebrant and both gain haste and +2/+0. Exert Gogo in combat 1 to untap all creatures and take combat 2. In combat 2, exert the real Celebrant to untap all creatures and take combat 3. Yields three devastating combat phases in a single turn.

Port Razer

Dealing combat damage untaps each creature you control and grants an additional combat phase. Gogo copies Port Razer to create two independent extra combat triggers.

Bloodthirster

Flying, trample 6/6 that untaps and grants an additional combat phase whenever it deals combat damage to a player. Copying it with Gogo creates two 8/6 flying tramplers that chain extra combats across all opponents.

Karlach, Fury of Avernus

Untaps all attacking creatures, gives first strike, and adds an additional combat on your first attack. Gogo copies Karlach to double the threat.

Moraug, Fury of Akoum

Landfall during your main phase grants an additional combat phase and untaps all creatures. Each attack stacks +1/+0 across your entire board.

Infinite combos
+
Godo, Bandit Warlord + Helm of the Host

Godo enters and puts Helm onto the battlefield. Equip Helm (paying {5} or {R} with Magnetic Theft). At beginning of combat, Helm creates a nonlegendary hasty copy of Godo. That token attacks, untaps itself, and creates an additional combat phase where Helm triggers again, generating infinite combats and hasty Godos.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Zealous Conscripts

Tap Kiki-Jiki to create a hasty token copy of Conscripts; the token's ETB untaps Kiki-Jiki. Repeat for infinite hasty Conscripts.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Coercive Recruiter

Tap Kiki-Jiki to copy Recruiter; the copy's ETB untaps Kiki-Jiki and grants haste. Repeat for infinite hasty Pirates and gain control of every opposing creature on the board.

+
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Combat Celebrant

Tap Kiki-Jiki to create a copy of Celebrant. The token attacks and exerts, untapping Kiki-Jiki and granting an additional combat. In the next combat, Kiki-Jiki creates another token Celebrant to repeat infinitely.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Twinflame

Cast Twinflame targeting any creature; hold priority and cast Dualcaster Mage. Dualcaster ETB copies Twinflame targeting Dualcaster. The copy resolves, making a hasty Dualcaster that copies Twinflame again, looping for infinite hasty Dualcaster Mages.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Molten Duplication

Identical infinite hasty token loop using Molten Duplication.

+
Dualcaster Mage + Heat Shimmer

Third redundant instant-speed copy loop with Dualcaster Mage.

+
Aggravated Assault + Neheb, the Eternal

Attack with Gogo copying Neheb for 12+ combat damage. In your postcombat main phase, both Nehebs add {R} for each life lost by opponents (24+ mana). Pay {3}{R}{R} into Aggravated Assault to untap all creatures and take another combat, generating infinite combats and mana.

++
Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond + Wheel of Fortune

Escape LED for three red mana, sacrifice LED discarding hand, escape Wheel of Fortune to draw seven fresh cards and mill seven cards. Generates infinite red mana and sculpts the exact winning combo.

++
Underworld Breach + Grinding Station + Sol Ring

Tap Station sacrificing Sol Ring to mill three cards; escape Sol Ring from graveyard by exiling three cards. Sol Ring enters, untapping Station. Repeat to mill your entire library, escape infinite mana, and assemble your finish.

Mass land denial and stax
Blood Moon

Turns all nonbasic lands into basic Mountains. Shuts down multicolored mana bases completely while our mono-red manabase functions untouched.

Magus of the Moon

Creature version of Blood Moon that can be tutored with Imperial Recruiter and copied by Gogo with haste.

Ruination

Four-mana sorcery that destroys all nonbasic lands on the table, crippling three opponents simultaneously.

Strip Mine

Land-slot uncounterable land destruction to remove problematic utility lands.

Acceleration and card engines
+++
Chrome Mox + Mox Diamond + Mox Opal + Lotus Petal

Zero-mana mana acceleration powering out turn-one and turn-two plays.

Lion's Eye Diamond

Burst three-mana artifact engine that fuels Underworld Breach loops or accelerates high-cost payoffs.

+
Mana Vault + Grim Monolith

Burst colorless acceleration to cast five- and six-mana threats ahead of curve.

+
Ancient Tomb + City of Traitors

Fast lands producing two colorless mana each for explosive early mana development.

Birgi, God of Storytelling // Harnfel, Horn of Bounty

Generates {R} on every spell cast to fuel storm turns, or plays as Harnfel, Horn of Bounty for runaway card advantage.

Treasonous Ogre

Converts life into burst red mana to deploy Godo, Kiki-Jiki, or multiple extra combat threats in one turn.

Jeska's Will

Adds massive red mana based on opponent hand size and exiles three cards to play.

The One Ring

Protection from everything for a full turn cycle, buying an untouchable turn while drawing an escalating stream of cards.

Wheel of Fortune

Refills an empty hand with seven fresh cards while disrupting opponents' sculpted hands.

Fable of the Mirror-Breaker // Reflection of Kiki-Jiki

Creates Treasure ramp, filters cards, and transforms into Reflection of Kiki-Jiki as a backup combo piece.

Goblin Engineer

Tutors Grinding Station, Defense Grid, Cursed Mirror, or Sensei's Divining Top directly to the graveyard and reanimates them.

Inventors' Fair

Tutors Helm of the Host, Defense Grid, The One Ring, or Grinding Station directly to hand.

Protection and interaction
Defense Grid

Taxes all spells cast on your turn by three generic mana, shutting down opposing counterspells and instant-speed removal during your combo.

Deflecting Swat

Free redirection spell with Gogo on the battlefield to protect combos from countermagic and spot removal.

Bolt Bend

One-mana redirection when controlling a creature with power 4 or greater (consistently enabled by Gogo's +2/+0 pump).

Untimely Malfunction

Versatile modal answer that destroys an artifact, redirects a spell, or prevents two creatures from blocking.

Tibalt's Trickery

Hard counter in mono-red to stop a game-winning spell.

+
Pyroblast + Red Elemental Blast

One-mana hard counters against blue interaction, Rhystic Study, and Cyclonic Rift.

Chaos Warp

Instant-speed answer to any permanent, including enchantments red normally cannot answer.

Blasphemous Act

Cheap board wipe to clear opposing boards before deploying your haste threats.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep hands with fast mana (Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond, Sol Ring, Mana Vault, Ancient Tomb, City of Traitors) and either a tutor (Gamble, Imperial Recruiter), a lock piece (Blood Moon), or an extra combat threat.

Deploy Blood Moon or Magus of the Moon on turn 1 or 2 whenever possible. Against 3-color and 4-color decks, an early Blood Moon ends the game on the spot.

Always prioritize casting Defense Grid before committing to Godo, Kiki-Jiki, or Underworld Breach lines to completely lock opponents out of countermagic.

Godo, Bandit Warlord with Magnetic Theft or 11 mana is a one-card win condition; protect Godo with Deflecting Swat, Bolt Bend, or Red Elemental Blast when casting him.
