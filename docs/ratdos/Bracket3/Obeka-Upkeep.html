Plain text ↗ On GitHub ↗

Build a payoff before chasing a huge hit. Obeka's damage produces extra upkeeps after combat, not extra turns, untaps, or ordinary draw steps. A protected connection with one useful engine is better than an enormous Obeka with nothing to trigger.

Make the first connection count
Obeka, Splitter of Seconds

Her baseline two combat damage gives two additional upkeeps. Menace requires two blockers but is not reliable evasion against a developed table. Removing her after damage does not erase a trigger already on the stack.

Brotherhood Regalia

Three total mana to cast and equip your legendary commander gives unblockability and ward two. Usually the best first equipment because a modest hit already advances the engine.

My Precious // Allure of Power

Hexproof plus unblockability protects both the connection and your investment. The Adventure can turn a spare token into two cards first; the equipment remains available afterward if the Adventure resolves.

Aqueous Form

One mana buys unblockability and an attack scry. It is efficient but exposes you to losing the Aura when the creature is removed, so keep protection available.

+
Silent Arbiter + Obeka, Splitter of Seconds

Arbiter permits at most one blocker, while menace requires at least two, so Obeka cannot be legally blocked while both effects apply. This also slows incoming armies. You can only attack with one creature, so your Dragons become defenders or Court fuel until Arbiter leaves.

Shizo, Death's Storehouse

A land-slot route through blockers. Fear still permits black and artifact creatures to block; combined with menace, the defender needs at least two eligible blockers.

Lightning Greaves

Haste and shroud protect an immediate attack. Move the boots off before equipping other equipment or casting an Aura on their wearer; shroud prevents your targeting too.

Swiftfoot Boots

A slightly more expensive haste option that leaves your own targeting available. Hold the equip mana when recasting Obeka through commander tax.

Ring of Valkas

Gives haste and adds a counter at each upkeep while attached to the red commander. Counters added after combat enlarge the next attack; they do not retroactively increase damage already dealt or create more upkeeps this turn.

Wrecking Ball Arm

Makes Obeka a base 7/7 for five total mana to cast and equip. Small creatures cannot block her, but two creatures with power three or greater still can; add actual unblockability against them.

Blackblade Reforged

Converts normal land development into more damage and therefore more upkeeps. Its bonus and +1/+1 counters apply on top of Wrecking Ball Arm's base 7/7.

Hard-Won Jitte

Doubles combat-damage opportunities. An unblocked seven-power Obeka can produce fourteen extra upkeeps, but all occur after the entire combat phase, not between first-strike and regular damage.

Win from the upkeep triggers
Court of Embereth

The primary table-wide finisher. Every upkeep adds a Knight, then, if you are monarch, damages each opponent for your creature count. The damage grows even without an attacking token army.

++
Obeka, Splitter of Seconds + Wrecking Ball Arm + Court of Embereth

A seven-damage connection gives seven extra upkeeps. Starting those upkeeps with Obeka and one other creature, the Court alone deals 3+4+5+6+7+8+9 = 42 to each opponent, provided you stay monarch and the creatures survive.

Court of Ire

While monarch, every upkeep supplies seven damage to any target. Remove a blocker or key creature when necessary; otherwise concentrate enough triggers on the opponent most able to stop the engine. Seven damage to one opponent is not seven damage to each opponent.

Court of Cunning

While monarch, each upkeep can mill every opponent for ten. Several enhanced hits or copied Courts can empty libraries. Players lose for failing to draw, not merely for reaching zero cards; graveyard decks and shuffle effects need special care.

Court of Ambition

Forces each opponent to choose between losing six life and discarding two cards while you are monarch. Excellent pressure and disruption, but it is not guaranteed damage while opponents have cards to discard.

Twilight Prophet

With the city's blessing, each upkeep adds a card to your hand and drains each opponent for its mana value while gaining you that much life. Lands reveal for zero. This puts the card into hand rather than drawing it.

Creeping Bloodsucker

A cheap early payoff that damages every opponent and normally gains three life per upkeep at a four-player table. It offsets repeated Arena and Bitterblossom life costs, but damage prevention reduces the life gained.

Dragonmaster Outcast

Once you have six lands, each upkeep makes a 5/5 flyer. The condition must be true as the upkeep begins and when the trigger resolves. This is a turn-six-and-later threat; it does little in an early hand.

Bitterblossom

Protects the monarch with flying blockers, helps reach ten permanents for ascend, and increases Court of Embereth damage. The life loss is mandatory on every upkeep, so count it before committing to a huge hit.

+
Court of Vantress + Court of Embereth

Copy the damage engine while monarch. A new Court becomes monarch on entry and starts triggering on the next upkeep, not the upkeep in which it entered.

+
Court of Vantress + Court of Cunning

Copy the mill engine to turn later upkeeps into much larger library reductions. You may copy an opponent's artifact or enchantment too; the target need not belong to you.

Extravagant Replication

The six-mana top end copies another nonland permanent you control each upkeep, including creatures. It is a snowball engine, not an immediate rescue spell. Avoid copying your legendary commander or legendary equipment because of the legend rule.

The list has no extra-turn effects, mass land denial, or built-in infinite loops. Its main wins require combat access, developed mana, and a surviving payoff. Treat turns six through eight as a construction target, not a measured or guaranteed kill turn.

Draw enough cards to keep playing
Super Intelligence

A one-mana upkeep draw engine attached to your creature. Putting it on a spare creature can spread your investment away from Obeka. It gives cards to the enchanted creature's controller, so do not casually put it on an opponent's creature.

Phyrexian Arena

Every extra upkeep draws a card for one life. Particularly strong here, but mandatory draw and life loss can become liabilities with a low life total or nearly empty library.

Rhystic Study

The independent draw engine for games where Obeka is repeatedly answered. It helps assemble protection and payoffs without requiring combat or multiple upkeeps.

+
Ancestral Vision + As Foretold

Suspend Vision early, then accelerate its time counters with Obeka. Alternatively, As Foretold can cast the zero-mana-value spell from your hand without waiting for suspend.

Inspiring Refrain

Each resolution draws two and suspends itself with three counters again. A large batch of upkeeps can cast it more than once, but each casting needs another three counters removed; the cycle is finite.

Bonehoard Dracosaur

Each upkeep provides two exiled cards plus a Dinosaur if any land was exiled and a Treasure if any nonland was exiled. Two lands do not make two Dinosaurs. Cards expire at the end of the turn, and normal land-play limits still apply.

Plargg and Nassari

An opponent denies you one revealed nonland card, then you may cast up to two of the others immediately without paying their mana costs. Repeated upkeeps produce substantial resources even without your own hand.

Court of Locthwain

Banks opposing cards in exile with ongoing permission to play them. Each upkeep that resolves while you are monarch also grants one free cast until end of turn from that Court's exiled cards. Normal timing still applies, so save sorceries for the postcombat main phase.

Becoming monarch does not draw a card immediately, and extra upkeeps do not create extra monarch draws. The usual monarch card arrives at your end step. Hitting the monarch with Obeka can retake the crown before the added upkeeps start.

Supply mana during the long turn
+
Arcane Signet + Fellwar Stone

Low-cost fixing to deploy the commander and keep interaction available. The three Talismans serve the same early-development role; prioritize colored mana over a hand full of slow engines.

Replicating Ring

Every upkeep advances the eight-counter threshold. Once it creates eight Replicated Rings, those new noncreature artifacts can tap immediately. The original Ring itself does not untap during Obeka's extra upkeeps.

Generous Plunderer

Gives you an untapped Treasure each upkeep if you choose, while one opponent gets a tapped Treasure. Their gift is a real resource for their next turn; distribute it deliberately rather than feeding the strongest opponent.

Rousing Refrain

Suspend early to convert later upkeeps into red mana based on an opponent's hand size. Its mana explicitly lasts through step and phase changes until end of turn. Ordinary floated mana does not.

As Foretold

Extra upkeeps increase the size of spell it can cast, but the allowance is once per turn, not once per upkeep. It can supply a free instant on each opponent's turn as well when you retain a suitable card.

The Soul Stone

Primarily an indestructible two-mana black rock. Harnessing it costs seven mana, a tap, and an exiled creature, so reserve that mode for a long game with useful creatures already in the graveyard.

+
The Soul Stone + Twilight Prophet

Once harnessed, the Stone can restore a dead Prophet during an upkeep. The returned Prophet will not trigger until a later upkeep; sequence the recovery before expecting more drain.

Exploit the initiative
Sarevok's Tome

Takes the initiative and normally taps for two colorless while you hold it. After completing a dungeon it can cast a revealed nonland card, but its tap abilities still need an untap before reuse.

Caves of Chaos Adventurer

Adds the initiative while supplying a substantial body to defend the crown. Attack with it for another card when doing so does not compromise Obeka's connection; Silent Arbiter limits you to one attacker.

Rilsa Rael, Kingpin

Takes the initiative and, after a completed dungeon, grants an attacking creature +5/+0, first strike, and menace along with deathtouch. Target Obeka for more damage and more upkeeps; Rilsa does not need to attack herself.

While you have the initiative, each upkeep advances one room in the Undercity. Taking the initiative also advances it, even if you already held it. Finishing the dungeon does not discard the initiative; the next venture begins another run.

The common route is Secret Entrance, Forge, Trap!, Archives, then Throne of the Dead Three. Forge puts two counters on Obeka, Trap! makes a chosen player lose five life, and Archives draws a card. The final room can deploy a creature from the top ten, but this list is not creature-heavy enough to guarantee a hit.

The three basics give Secret Entrance real targets; the original duals and shocks are not basic land cards. Running out of basics later does not prevent progressing through that room.

Finishing a dungeon after combat turns on Rilsa's bonus for a later attack. Neither the new bonus nor postcombat Forge counters increase upkeeps that have already been generated.

Spend tutors on what the hand lacks
→
Demonic Tutor → Court of Embereth

Find the main finisher when you already have a protected, substantial connection ready. Without evasion or a live commander, find the missing support first.

→
Profane Tutor → Court of Vantress

Suspend before combat so Obeka's added upkeeps can finish the two-counter wait. Find the copy engine when another useful enchantment is already present.

→
Fabricate → Brotherhood Regalia

Fetch efficient unblockability when opponents have enough blockers to defeat menace.

→
Fabricate → Wrecking Ball Arm

Fetch power when Obeka can already connect. Damage and upkeep payoffs should both be ready before investing five mana in casting and equipping it.

→
Fabricate → Crystal Chimes

Recover after a destroy effect has put several Courts or draw enchantments in your graveyard. Chimes returns them to hand and you still have to recast them.

Keep the engine alive
Mithril Coat

Flash it in to save a legendary creature from destruction; the entry trigger attaches it without an equip payment. It does not stop exile, sacrifice, or enough toughness reduction.

+
Deflecting Swat + Deadly Rollick

Free interaction while you control Obeka. Swat can redirect targeted removal and Rollick removes a blocker or dangerous creature, but neither answers every kind of spell.

+
Swan Song + An Offer You Can't Refuse

Keep one blue mana available to protect the connection or stop a noncreature win. The gifts they leave behind matter, particularly if the opponent can use the Treasures immediately.

+
Mana Drain + Counterspell

Unconditional spell counters that remain useful without your commander. Drain's mana arrives at your next main phase, not during the intervening added upkeeps.

Force of Negation

Free only on an opponent's turn. During your own attack and added upkeeps you must pay its normal cost unless another effect supplies an alternative.

March of Swirling Mist

Phase out blockers before blocks, or phase out your important creatures through a wipe. Phasing your own Obeka protects her but removes her from combat, so that attack will not generate upkeeps.

Cyclonic Rift

Clears opposing boards while preserving the engines, setting up a connection and buying time against a threatening table. Its overloaded mode does not remove lands.

Toxic Deluge

An inexpensive emergency reset. A small X may preserve Obeka's five toughness while killing small creatures; it also removes your own tokens and can kill indestructible creatures.

++
Feed the Swarm + Chaos Warp + Resculpt

Cover troublesome enchantments, artifacts, creatures, and other permanents across the three colors. Warp can reveal a replacement threat, Feed costs life, and Resculpt leaves a blocker, so choose the answer that fits the board.

+
Soul-Guide Lantern + Court of Cunning

Do not feed an opposing graveyard engine without a plan. Keep Lantern untapped to exile opponents' graveyards, including in response to a shuffle-back trigger after milling its source.

Malakir Rebirth // Malakir Mire

Save a critical creature from dying or play the land face when needed. To return Obeka this way, leave her in the graveyard rather than moving her to the command zone.

Do not expose every Court at once when opponents still have a sweeping answer. Your enchantment recovery handles destruction, not exile.

Takenuma, Abandoned Mire

Channel to recover a creature when rebuilding matters more than another land. It cannot recover your destroyed Courts or equipment.

Opening hands and sequencing

Keep three lands with access to blue, black, and red, a two-mana accelerator, and either an evasion piece or a cheap engine. Two-land hands need both acceleration and a credible draw plan. Mulligan hands full of five- and six-mana payoffs.

A typical start is land development, a turn-two rock, then Obeka on turn three if safe. Use turn four to establish a payoff or protected connection. This is an example curve, not a reason to cast the commander naked into obvious removal.

The mana base has 36 lands plus Malakir Rebirth as a land option. Fetch untapped typed duals for the missing colors, and use the tapped surveil land or triome when it does not delay your curve.

Before combat, prepare evasion, power, and protection. After damage, count the exact added upkeeps and resolve them one at a time. Opponents receive priority; the batch is not an uninterruptible action.

Order simultaneous upkeep triggers deliberately. Creating extra creatures before Court of Embereth resolves increases its damage. A copied engine created during an upkeep only begins triggering on a subsequent upkeep.

Paradox Haze

Gives one additional upkeep after your first upkeep each turn. It supplies redundancy without Obeka, but it does not trigger again from every extra upkeep.

Track separate pools for ordinary mana and Rousing Refrain's persistent red mana. Extra upkeeps do not untap lands, do not refresh As Foretold, and do not allow ordinary sorcery casting or equipping.

If Obeka is removed repeatedly, build around the Courts, Paradox Haze, and independent draw instead of paying commander tax immediately. Recover the engine, hold protection, and recast when you can get a useful hit.
