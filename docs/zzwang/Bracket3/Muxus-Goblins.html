Author: zzwang. Bracket 3 for four-player games: 100 cards, three Game Changers, no mass land denial; its combo wins wait for turn 6.

Commander: Muxus, Goblin Grandee. Krenko, Mob Boss is deliberately included in the 99. The list has 35 actual lands plus 2 spell/land MDFCs; choose the land face when the opening hand needs mana.

Your engine and opening hand
Muxus, Goblin Grandee

The commander is a six-mana deployment engine, not just a large attacker. This list has 37 Goblin creature cards costing five or less in the 99. An unarranged six-card reveal can still miss; Goblin Recruiter is how you turn it into a specific board. Land the entry trigger after developing mana, then use the bodies for a combo or a hasty attack.

Krenko, Mob Boss

Included in the 99 and a legal Muxus hit. Muxus often supplies several Goblins at once, so a hasty Krenko activation immediately converts that board into a much larger army. Hold some bodies back as blockers when you do not have a lethal line.

Keep three usable lands and either a cheap rock or a credible Goblin mana plan, ideally with a tutor or card engine. Two lands need real early acceleration, not just Mana Geyser. Do not keep an opening hand full of four- and five-mana Goblins simply because Muxus could put them into play from the library.

Mana Vault

Together with Sol Ring, Arcane Signet and Mind Stone, this gets Muxus onto the battlefield without needing a developed army first. Vault supplies colorless mana and does not normally untap; preserve two red sources for the commander.

Skirk Prospector

Converts spare Goblins into red mana. Mogg War Marshal and Impulsive Pilferer leave value when sacrificed. Keep enough bodies for Krenko or a combo rather than sacrificing the whole board just to cast Muxus blindly.

Brightstone Ritual

Counts every Goblin on the battlefield. This is explosive once bodies exist; it is not an opening-hand mana source on an empty board. Mana Geyser is a later burst based on opponents' tapped lands.

Howlsquad Heavy

Gives other Goblins haste immediately and makes a Goblin at your combat. Its huge mana ability is available only at max speed; do not count that ability as early ramp. The Fire Crystal also grants haste and reduces red spell costs.

Win one — arrange a Muxus reveal
Goblin Recruiter

For a combo board with a full hand as backup, arrange these ten cards from TOP to BOTTOM: Kiki-Jiki, Lightning Crafter, Skirk Prospector, Goblin Warchief, Hexing Squelcher, Goblin Ringleader, Krenko, Mob Boss, Goblin Matron, Pashalik Mons, General Kreat, the Boltbringer. The first six are all eligible Muxus hits; the next four are the Ringleader refill. Keep the Recruiter that arranged the pile on the battlefield as champion fodder. Avoid intervening draws, shuffles or library exile; if your next draw step comes before Muxus, put an extra useful Goblin ABOVE this pile to absorb that draw. Adjust the pile for cards already outside your library.

Goblin Ringleader

After Muxus finishes putting the first six cards into play, Ringleader reveals positions seven through ten and puts those four Goblins into your hand. Put its entry trigger above Lightning Crafter's champion trigger so the refill resolves first. This supplies Krenko, Matron, Pashalik and Kreat as backup if opponents disrupt the combo. It requires both Muxus' and Ringleader's entry abilities to resolve.

Goblin Warchief

Belongs in the first six here, so the Goblins already on your battlefield have haste and the backup Goblins in hand cost one less generic mana. Krenko costs one generic plus two red and can tap immediately after you cast him while Warchief remains. Kiki-Jiki and its copies already have haste for the combo; Warchief improves the broader board and fallback plan. Hexing Squelcher arriving with Muxus does not retroactively protect the Muxus spell from being countered.

Lightning Crafter

Resolve the original Crafter's champion trigger by exiling the Goblin Recruiter that arranged the pile. If Recruiter was removed, Ringleader is expendable AFTER its refill trigger resolves. Tap Kiki-Jiki to copy Crafter. The hasty token's champion trigger exiles Kiki-Jiki; tap the token to deal three damage, then sacrifice it to Skirk Prospector. Its leave trigger returns Kiki-Jiki untapped, with its own haste. Repeat the copy, champion, shoot and sacrifice sequence for lethal damage to all opponents.

Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker

This loop needs the original Crafter to remain on the battlefield, a free sacrifice outlet and successful champion/return triggers. Kiki-Jiki cannot target legendary creatures, including Krenko or Muxus. It can copy Matron, Ringleader and Siege-Gang Commander for value when a win is not ready.

Win two — Krenko and an untap engine
Thornbite Staff

Equip Krenko and give him haste or wait until he can tap. Krenko is a Warrior, so the Staff does not automatically attach to him; budget the four-mana equip cost. With Skirk Prospector and at least one other Goblin, tap Krenko for tokens, sacrifice one token for red, and let its death untap Krenko. Each cycle adds mana and grows the board.

Pashalik Mons

Every sacrificed Goblin deals one damage to any target, so the Staff loop wins without combat. General Kreat or Impact Tremors instead turn repeated token entries into damage to all opponents. Without a damage payoff, make a sufficiently large army and use haste if attacking is possible.

Win three — Snoop and Recruiter
Conspicuous Snoop

With Snoop able to tap, use Recruiter to put Kiki-Jiki on top. Snoop gains Kiki-Jiki's activated ability and can copy itself because Snoop is not legendary. Each new copy has haste and the same borrowed ability; make enough copies for a lethal finish. The last copy copies the Recruiter already on your battlefield, whose entry trigger puts Mogg Fanatic on top. The Snoops now gain Fanatic's sacrifice ability; sacrifice them for one damage each. Keeping Kiki-Jiki on top during the copying stage is essential.

Infinite combos — from turn 6
++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Lightning Crafter + Skirk Prospector

Kiki copies the Crafter. The copy's champion trigger exiles Kiki; the hasty copy taps for 3 damage; sacrifice it to Skirk Prospector; Kiki returns untapped — it has haste — and copies the Crafter again. Unlimited 3-damage shots, so this one kills on its own. Goblin Bombardment work as the outlet too. The original Crafter must stay on the battlefield; when it enters you must exile another Goblin or Shaman (champion) or sacrifice it.

++
Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Thornbite Staff + Goblin Bombardment

the Staff attaches itself to Kiki when Kiki enters after it (otherwise equip costs {4}). Kiki copies any nonlegendary creature, you sacrifice a creature to Goblin Bombardment, and the death untaps Kiki. Unlimited loops.

→+
Goblin Recruiter → Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Conspicuous Snoop

Recruiter puts Kiki on TOP of your library. Snoop has every activated ability of the Goblin on top, so it copies itself with Kiki's ability — and every hasty copy does the same. Snoop needs haste or a turn in play to start, and most copies end up tapped, so finish with Mogg Fanatic's sacrifice ability — stack Fanatic next so the Snoops borrow it — or Goblin Bombardment.

++
Krenko, Mob Boss + Thornbite Staff + Skirk Prospector

equip the Staff to Krenko ({4} — he's a Warrior, so it won't attach itself). Krenko taps for a Goblin per Goblin; sacrifice one to Skirk Prospector; the death untaps Krenko; tap again. The army doubles every lap. Win with Pashalik Mons, General Kreat or Impact Tremors.

+
Goblin Sharpshooter + Goblin Bombardment

every Goblin you sacrifice untaps Sharpshooter for another ping — two damage per Goblin.

The three numbered wins below walk through each line card by card.

Anything that searches your library shuffles it and wrecks the stack — Goblin Matron, Imperial Recruiter and Pyre of Heroes. Use those before Recruiter, never after.

Cards, removal and recovery
Goblin Ringleader

Refills your hand from a Goblin-heavy library. Matron finds the creature you need, and Pyre of Heroes upgrades a spare Goblin into another costing exactly one more; Pyre cannot skip mana values.

Skullclamp

Excellent with unboosted 1/1 tokens. Chieftain and Hordemaster can keep a token alive after equipping; use a sacrifice outlet to get the draw rather than assuming the equipment always kills it.

Gempalm Incinerator

Cycling draws a card and can remove a creature based on the Goblins on the battlefield. Goblin Chainwhirler and Village Pillagers clear small opposing creatures without hitting your own army. Pillagers' Treasures enter tapped.

Goblin Trashmaster

Sacrifice Goblins to remove artifacts that would stop the engine. Chaos Warp is the broad answer to other troublesome permanents; save it for effects your creature removal cannot touch. Blasphemous Act is the reset when opponents' boards are larger.

Goblin Chirurgeon

Sacrifice a Goblin to regenerate a key creature against destruction. Regeneration does not stop exile or toughness being reduced to zero, and using a regeneration shield taps the creature.

Molten Duplication

Can copy legendary Muxus: the new entry trigger still happens, then the legend rule leaves one Muxus. This costs a real card and two mana but can rebuild or complete an arranged reveal. Kiki-Jiki cannot make this copy himself.

Soul-Guide Lantern

Answers a threatening graveyard without interfering with your own library-based win. Graveyard exile replacements can still stop death-trigger untap lines, so answer them before committing to Staff.

Game Changers and turn protection
Mana Vault

GAME CHANGER 1/3. Early acceleration for the six-mana commander. It is not a normally untapping mana rock.

Jeska's Will

GAME CHANGER 2/3. Usually produces a large red burst; if you control your commander as you cast it, choose both modes. Cards exiled this way must be played this turn with normal costs and timing.

Ancient Tomb

GAME CHANGER 3/3. Two colorless accelerates rocks, equipment and generic costs; it cannot pay red pips and costs two life each use.

Hexing Squelcher

Protects your spells from being countered while it remains in play. It does not stop opponents from removing creatures, exiling graveyards, countering abilities or preventing damage.

Deflecting Swat

Redirects a targeted spell or ability, and is free only while you control a commander on the battlefield. Your commander sitting in the command zone does not enable the alternate cost. It cannot redirect a spell with no targets.

Before committing to a win

Check for effects that stop searching, creature-entry triggers, activated abilities or graveyard returns, then choose the line that avoids them or remove them first. Stage mana and protection before exposing all pieces. After a stopped attempt, use your card engines and the remaining Goblins to rebuild instead of repeatedly casting the commander into the same answer. No line is guaranteed through interaction.

Complete Snoop package — compact wins from turn 6
+++
Conspicuous Snoop + Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Goblin Recruiter + Mogg Fanatic

With Snoop able to tap, resolve Recruiter and put Kiki on top with Fanatic below it. Snoop gains Kiki's activated ability and can target itself because it is nonlegendary. Make more than 120 hasty Snoop copies, leaving the newest one untapped. Use that copy to copy Recruiter instead of Snoop. The new Recruiter puts Fanatic on top, so every surviving Snoop gains Fanatic's sacrifice ability. Sacrifice at least 40 Snoops for 1 damage each to each of three opponents. Most Snoops being tapped does not prevent the sacrifice ability. Kiki and Fanatic must still be in the library when searched; Recruiter cannot put a card from your hand back on top.

+
Torch Courier + Conspicuous Snoop

Cast Courier for {R} and sacrifice it targeting Snoop for haste this turn. Courier's ability has no tap cost, so it works immediately. Do this before starting the Kiki-top sequence. If Courier is your only haste source and still in the library, Recruiter can stack Courier, then Kiki, then Fanatic: cast Courier off the top through Snoop, sacrifice it to give Snoop haste, and Kiki is now revealed. Avoid extra draws, mills, exiles and shuffles that move Kiki away from the top before the copying ends. Shroud prevents Courier or Snoop's copied Kiki ability from targeting Snoop.

→
Goblin Recruiter → Goblin Ringleader

For a refill, put Ringleader on top followed by Torch Courier, Conspicuous Snoop, Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker and Mogg Fanatic. Draw Ringleader, then pay {3}{R}; its entry trigger puts the next four Goblins into your hand. If Ringleader is already in hand, omit it from the pile and cast it before another draw. This is a four-card hand refill, not an immediate Snoop kill: Kiki in hand cannot grant Snoop its ability from the library. Choose the Kiki-top pile instead when Snoop is ready to win. Additional draws require padding or a revised pile; use fetches and other shuffling searches before Recruiter.

Mogg Fanatic

The library-top finisher lets Snoop tokens sacrifice themselves for damage without another permanent payoff. An ordinary Fanatic on the battlefield can also sacrifice itself for one damage; that single card by itself is not an unlimited damage engine. Keep compact wins for turn six or later under the house rules.
